You can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to gain one of the following benefits:
- You and your allies within 15 feet of you ignore [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] from Snow or Ice.
- You can see through areas [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]] by snow to a distance of 60 feet.
- You are [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immune]] to the effects of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]].

Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.
