This Charm allows you to cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Raulothim's Psychic Lance\|Raulothim's Psychic Lance]], on a failure, the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition instead of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition. Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.
