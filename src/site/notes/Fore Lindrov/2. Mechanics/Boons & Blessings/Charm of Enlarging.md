This Charm allows you to cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Enlarge-Reduce\|Enlarge/Reduce]] (Enlarge Only). Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.

If you cast the spell on yourself, it requires no [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]].
