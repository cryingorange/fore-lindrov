You can cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Mage Hand\|Mage Hand]], requiring no spell components and using your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as the spellcasting ability (your choice). 

When you cast the spell in this way, it lasts its full duration with no [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] required, and the hand is invisible. Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you
