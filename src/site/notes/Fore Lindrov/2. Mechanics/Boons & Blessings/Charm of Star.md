You can cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Enhance Ability\|Enhance Ability]], requiring no spell components and using your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as the spellcasting ability (your choice). 

When you cast the spell in this way, it lasts its full duration with no [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] required. Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you
