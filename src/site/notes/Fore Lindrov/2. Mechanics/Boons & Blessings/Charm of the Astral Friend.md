**Summoning.** You call forth a [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Aberration/Gazer\|Gazer]]. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet.

**Ally.** The [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Aberration/Gazer\|Gazer]] is an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Ally\|Ally]] to you and your allies. In combat, the [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Aberration/Gazer\|Gazer]] shares your Initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don't issue any, it takes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Dodge\|Dodge]] action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

Once used five times, the Charm vanishes from you.
