You can expend the Charm as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] to gain a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Fly Speed\|Fly Speed]] equal to your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] for 1 minute, growing bat-like wings that sprout from your back, you also have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on saving throws against the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition.

Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.
