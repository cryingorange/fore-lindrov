You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

Whenever you suffer a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] or are reduced to 0 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]], you spray acid; each creature within 5 feet of you takes Acid damage equal to your number of Hit Dice.
