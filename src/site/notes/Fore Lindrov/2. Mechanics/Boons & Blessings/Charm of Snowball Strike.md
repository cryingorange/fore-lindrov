**Bonus Action.** As a Bonus Action, you create a magical snowball in your hand and throw it. You must have a hand free to hold the snowball, or the use is wasted. 

**Details.** The snowball is a magic ranged weapon with which you are proficient. It has a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet, it deals 1d4 Cold damage on a hit, and it scores a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] on a **19** or **20**. If you score a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] it with the snowball, the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn. 

The snowball vanishes immediately after you hit or miss with it, or at the end of your turn if you don't throw it.

Once used five times, the Charm vanishes from you.
