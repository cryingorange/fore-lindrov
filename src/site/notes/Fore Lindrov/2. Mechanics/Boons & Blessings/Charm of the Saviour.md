When a creature you can see within 60 feet of you is reduced to 0 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] as a result of taking damage, you can use your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to cause the creature to drop to 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]]. Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.
