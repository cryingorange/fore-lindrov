During your move, you can pass through the space of an ally, a creature that has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition, a Tiny creature, or a creature that is two sizes larger or smaller than you.

Another creature's space is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] for you unless that creature is Tiny, 2 sizes smaller or larger than you, or your ally.
