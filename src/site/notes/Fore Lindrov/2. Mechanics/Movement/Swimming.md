While you're swimming, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (2 extra feet in difficult terrain). You ignore this extra cost if you have a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Swim Speed\|Swim Speed]] and use it to swim. At the DM's option, moving any distance in rough water might require a successful DC 15 STR (Athletics) check.
