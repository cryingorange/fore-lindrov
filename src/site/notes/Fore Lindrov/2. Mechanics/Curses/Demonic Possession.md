Demonic Possession arises from the chaos and evil of the Abyss and commonly besets creatures that interact with demonic objects or linger in desecrated locations, where demonic spirits await victims.

**CHA Saving Throw.** A creature that becomes the target of Demonic [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Possession\|Possession]] must succeed on a DC 15 CHA saving throw or be [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Possession\|Possessed]] by a bodiless demonic entity. 

**Taken Over.** Whenever the possessed creature rolls a 1 on a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Test]], the demonic entity takes control of the creature and determines the creature's behavior thereafter. 

**Regaining Control.** At the end of each of the possessed creature's later turns, the creature makes a DC 15 CHA saving throw, regaining control of itself on a success.

**Temporary Possession.** After finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]], a creature with Demonic [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Possession\|Possession]] makes a DC 15 CHA saving throw. On a successful save, the effect ends on the creature. A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Dispel Evil and Good\|Dispel Evil and Good]] spell or any magic that removes a Curse also ends the effect on it.
