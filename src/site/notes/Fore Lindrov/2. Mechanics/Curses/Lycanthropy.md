# Overview
There are various methods of contracting the Lycanthropy such as  being bitten by a lycanthrope and not curing the Curse before it takes hold.

Other known methods are esoteric rituals such as the Lunar Sacrament. A violent and bloody rite that involves the sacrifice of an innocent person, underscoring the ruthless and uncompromising nature of someone willing to do evil in exchange for power.

Other means of gaining lycanthropy are possible. The curse of a powerful being, a side effect of a powerful artifact, or eating the raw flesh of a lycanthropic creature: all of these are potential triggers for the Lycanthrope Transformation.

## Achieving a New Stage

To move from one stage to the next higher one in this Transformation, an event or some other notable occurrence tied to the story of the character should take place. The following events are suggestions for ones that might trigger a move to the next stage of the Transformation:
- Making a further bargain with the Fey creature who gifted you with Lycanthropy originally
- Killing a powerful creature under the light of a full moon in your hybrid form
- Failing two Death Saving Throws
- Killing an innocent creature in your hybrid form after failing your saving throw against Lust for the Hunt

## Flaw: Lust For The Hunt

>[!info|bg-c-red c-p-sm]+ Details
>> The savage nature of your curse sometimes causes you to lose control. These ferocious tendencies are a constant struggle between you and the beast within.
> ### WIS Saving Throw
>-  **Bloodied.** The first time you become [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Bloodied\|Bloodied]] after finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]], you must succeed on a DC 10 WIS Saving Throw. 
> 	- If you are in the light of a full moon, you automatically fail this saving throw.
> 	- On a failure, you immediately enter your Hybrid Form for 1 hour, [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] is casted on you, or you start your start at full [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]], repeating the save and returning to your normal form on a success.
> ### Failed Saving Throw
> **Hybrid Form.** While in your Hybrid Form from failing the WIS Saving Throw, you suffer from the following effects:
> - **Feral.** You go completely feral, and you cannot use any of your equipment or class features.
> - **Kill Bloodied Creature.** On your turn while in Hybrid Form, you must move toward the closest creature you can see, smell, or hear, prioritizing [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Bloodied\|Bloodied]] creatures. 
> 	- If you end your movement and no creature is within 5 feet of you, you must use the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Dash\|Dash]] Action to move toward the closest one.
> 	- If there is a creature within 5 feet of you and you have not used your action, you must make an Attack Action using your Claw or Bite attack.
> 	- When you damage a creature on your turn and it is not reduced to 0 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]], you must use all your attacks that turn against the target.

# Lycanthrope: Stage 2

> You select one Stage 2 Boon and gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Lycanthropy#Flaw Silver Sensitivity\|Flaw: Silver Sensitivity]]

## Boons
>[!tip|bg-c-blue c-p-sm]- Boon: Hunter's Focus
> ### Mark Creature
> - **In Hybrid Form.** While in hybrid form, you can use a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] to Mark one creature within 60 feet as your prey. 
> 	- A creature remains marked this way for 1 hour, or until it dies. You may only have 1 creature marked at a time.
>
>### Benefits
> - **Marked Effects.** While a creature is marked as your prey, you gain the following benefits:
> 	- Whenever you hit the Marked Creature with a melee attack, you deal an additional 1d6 damage of that attack's type.
> 	- You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on any WIS (Perception or Survival) check you make to find it.

>[!tip|bg-gray c-black c-p-sm]- Boon: Iron Pelt
> ### Resistance
> - **In Hybrid Form.** While in hybrid form, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
> 	- Magical and Silvered weapons ignore this [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]].

>[!tip|c-p-sm]- Boon: Kindred Form
> ### Beast Form
> - **Transforming.** When you enter your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Lycanthropy#Lycanthrope Stage 1\|Hybrid Form]], you can choose to Shape-Shift into a Beast depending on your form of Lycanthropy.
> 	- As a Bonus Action, you can change between your Hybrid and Beast Forms.
>
>> **Werebear:** [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Beast/Black Bear\|Black Bear]]
>> **Wererat:** [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Beast/Giant Rat\|Giant Rat]]
>> **Werewolf:** [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Beast/Wolf\|Wolf]]
> - **Indistinguishable.** You are indistinguishable from other creatures of that type, and you can communicate with those creatures.
> ### Rules While Shapeshifted
> - **Statistics.** Your game statistics are replaced by the Beast's stat block except for the following traits: 
> 	- Your creature type.
> 	- Your Hit Points.
> 	- Your Hit Point Dice.
> 	- Your INT, WIS, and CHA Scores 
> 	- Your Class Features; Languages; and Feats. 
> 	- You also retain your skill and saving throw proficiencies and use your Proficiency Bonus for them, in addition to gaining the proficiencies of the creature. 
> 	- If a skill or saving throw modifier in the Beast's stat block is higher than yours, use the one in the stat block.
> - **Limitations.** You are limited in the actions you can perform by the anatomy of your new form.
> 	- You cannot speak, but you can understand languages you know.
> 	- All equipment you wear and carry falls off you when transforming.
> 	- You can't cast spells or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentrate]] on spells.

# Curing Lycanthropy
>[!warning]- For DMs ONLY! (PLAYERS DO NOT CLICK)
> **Remove Curse.** A creature can be cured by of Lycanthropy if a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] is casted on it. The target must then succeed on a DC 28 CON Saving Throw, each spell level above third reduces this DC by 2. 
> 
> **Full Moon.** This saving throw is made with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] unless casted on the cursed creature during a Full Moon. 
> 
> **Success.** On a success, the curse is broken, the creature is cured of its Lycanthropy, returns to its normal form and gain 3 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Levels.
> 
> **Failure.** On a failure, the curse remains, and the target automatically fails any saving throw made to break this curse for 1 month.
