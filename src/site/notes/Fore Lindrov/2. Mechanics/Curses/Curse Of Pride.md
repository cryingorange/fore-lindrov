# Overview
## General
- **Roleplaying Guidelines.** The failure of the Pride Saving Throw is meant to represent a character's deeper progression into arrogance and pride.
	- The player should aim to roleplay in such a way that accurately represents the character's stage of progression.

<br>

## Progression
- **Pride Saving Throw.** If the character expended at least one Pride Die, they must make a WIS Saving Throw at the end of their next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].
	- On a successful save, the character suffers no effects.
	- On a failure, they suffer the effects of the next level in a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Curse Of Pride#Curse Stages\|Stage]].
	- The base DC increase based on the Character's Level (see [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Curse Of Pride#Pride DC Table\|#Pride DC Table]]).
- **Stages.** The character begins their journey of Pride at [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Curse Of Pride#Stage 1\|#Stage 1]], for each Pride Saving Throw they fail, they suffer the effects of the next level in a Stage.
	- Once the character reaches the end of a Stage, the next time they fail a Pride Saving Throw, they go to the next stage.

## Stage 2: Arrogance

> **Note.** The character suffers the penalties of each level below the one they are in. For example, if a character is at Level 3 of Stage 2, they also suffer the effects of Levels 1 and 2.

<br>

| Level | Effect |
|:-----:| ------ |
| **1**      | If the character fails an Ability Check with a bonus from a spent Pride Die, they gain a 1d6 penalty to Ability Checks until the end of their next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]]. |
| **2**      | If the character misses an attack roll with a bonus from a spent Pride Die, they gain a 1d6 penalty to Attack Rolls until the end of their next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]]. |
| **3**      | If a target succeeds on a saving throw induced by spent Pride Die, the character gain a 1d6 penalty to Saving Throws until the end of their next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]]. |
| **4**      | The character must spend one Pride Die on an Ability Check or gain one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level at the end of their next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].|
| **5**      | The character must spend one Pride Die on an Attack Roll or gain one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level at the end of their next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].|
| **6**      | The character must spend one Pride Die to force a target to make a saving throw or gain one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level at the end of their next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].|
