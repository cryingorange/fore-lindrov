**Special Lycanthropy.** A Humanoid who succumbs to a Loup Garou's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Lycanthropy\|Lycanthropy]] becomes a Lycanthrope. This form of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Lycanthropy\|Lycanthropy]] can't be removed while the Loup Garou that inflicted the curse lives.

**New Loup Garou.** If a werewolf who was turned by the Loup Garou slays it and remains Cursed with Lycanthropy for 1 year, it transforms into a Loup Garou.  

# Curing Loup Garou Lycanthropy

>[!warning]- For DMs ONLY! (PLAYERS DO NOT CLICK)
> **Remove Curse.** Once the Loup Garou that turned a cursed creature is slain, that creature can be cured of its Lycanthropy across three separate Full Moons. On the night of a Full Moon, [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] must be cast on the cursed creature.
> 
> Immediately after [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] is casted on the creature, it must make a DC 28 CON Saving Throw. The DC is reduced by 2 for each spell slot level above 3.
> 
> **Success.** The creature records one success toward breaking the curse. If the creature accumulates three successes total across three separate Full Moons, the curse is broken, and it is cured of its Lycanthropy.
> 
> Once cured, the creature returns to its normal form and gains 3 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Levels.
> 
> **Failure.** On a failure, the curse remains, the target's current tally of successes resets to 0, and the target automatically fails any saving throw made to break this curse for 1 month.
