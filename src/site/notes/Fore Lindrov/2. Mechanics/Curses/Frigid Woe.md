# Overview

Frigid Woe manifests with bluing veins and a thin coating of ice that grows over a victim's body. As the Frigid Woe covers the victim, the victim eventually petrifies into a type of creature known as a [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Undead/Coldlight Walker\|Coldlight Walker]].

# Effects

Any creature that finishes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] in Icewind Dale makes a DC 10 CON Saving Throw. Constructs automatically succeed on this save. On a failed save, the creature is afflicted by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Curses/Frigid Woe\|Frigid Woe]] and suffers the following effects:

**Curse.** The creature gains 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level and is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Cursed\|Cursed]]. While the creature is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Cursed\|Cursed]], finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] doesn't reduce the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level.

**Frozen Joints**. The creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] is reduced by 5 feet.

**Coldlight Fate.** When a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Cursed\|Cursed]] creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level reaches 6, it doesn't die. Instead, the creature transforms into a Coldlight version of its former self; its creature type becomes Undead, and it gains [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immunity]] to Cold and Poison damage, and the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Petrified\|Petrified]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] conditions, and 60 feet of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]] if it didn't have it in life. The Curse then ends for that creature.

**Fighting the Curse.** Daily at Dawn, an afflicted creature makes a DC 10 CON Saving Throw. On a failed save, the creature gains 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level as more ice coats its skin. On a successful save, the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level doesn't increase. 

If the creature makes the save outside of Icewind Dale, it's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] decreases by 1. If the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level is reduced to 0, the Curse ends on the creature.

**Remove Curse.** Removing the Curse immediately ends it for a creature.
