# Effects
If you start your turn within 10 feet of anything metal, you must make a CON Saving Throw against the spell save DC of the creature that Cursed you. On a failed save, you begin sneezing uncontrollably, have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition and are [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Suffocation\|Suffocating]]. 

You repeat the save at the end of each of your turns, ending the effect on yourself on a success. The effect also ends on you if you're targeted by a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]] spell.
