# Overview
Intense, magical Cold such as Blizzards of Icewind Dale, or Alchemical Mishaps can leave a creature with a lingering Cold Injury. The DM may impose this injury if a creature takes a significant amount of Cold damage from a single source or environment, or from prolonged exposure and failures to saving throws against [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]]. The injury's mechanics are generalised to allow the DM to roleplay the injury such as Frostbite.

# Effects

- **Exhaustion.** The creature gains one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level.

- **Necrotising Wound.** The creature loses 3d6 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] daily at Dawn and its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] lost. 
	- This reduction lasts until the injury ends. If the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] maximum is reduced to 0, it dies.
	- Whenever the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] maximum is reduced in this way, it gains 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level.

- **Shivering Rest.** While the creature has any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels, finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] neither restores the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] maximum nor reduces its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels. 

# Fighting The Injury
A creature can attempt to treat the wound throughout the afflicted creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] by making a DC 15 Medicine (Wisdom) check or by using a Healer’s Kit. On a success, the creature does not lose [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] at the following dawn and reduces its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels by 1 at the end of that [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

The injury ends only when the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels are reduced to 0, or it is subjected to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]] spell or other similar magic.
