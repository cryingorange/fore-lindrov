# Overview
Intense, magical heat such as Dragonfire, or Alchemical Mishaps can leave a creature with a lingering Burn Injury. The DM may impose this injury if a creature takes a significant amount of Fire damage from a single source or environment.

# Effects

- **Exhaustion.** The creature gains one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level.

- **Exuding Wound.** The creature loses 3d6 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] daily at Dawn, and its HP maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] lost. 
	- This reduction lasts until the injury ends. If the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] maximum is reduced to 0, it dies.
	- Whenever the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] maximum is reduced in this way, it must succeed on a DC 15 CON Saving Throw or gain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level.

- **Painful Rest.** While the creature has any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels, finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] neither restores the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] maximum nor reduces its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels.

# Fighting The Injury

A creature can attempt to treat the wound throughout the afflicted creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] by making a DC 15 Medicine (Wisdom) check or by using a Healer’s Kit. On a success, the creature does not lose [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] at the following dawn and reduces its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels by 1 at the end of that [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

The injury ends only when the creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels are reduced to 0, after a spell or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Magical Effects\|magical effect]] restores at least 5 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] to the afflicted creature, a nonmagical effect restores its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] to max or it is subjected to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]] spell or other similar magic.
