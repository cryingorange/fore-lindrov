When you make a melee attack, you can also mark the target. 

Until the end of your next turn, any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attack]] you make against the marked target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]]. The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attack]] doesn't expend your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]], but you can't make the attack if anything, such as the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition is preventing you from taking [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reactions]]. You are limited to one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attack]] against each Marked creature per turn.
