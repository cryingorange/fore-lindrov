# Creature

**Requirements.** When you take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] Action, you can use one of your attacks to throw a creature you are [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappling]]. To do so, the creature must be equal to or smaller than your Size.

**Unwilling Creature.** The target must succeed on a STR or DEX saving throw (target's choice) against your STR Save DC.

The target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on the saving throw if it is the same size as you, and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] if you are at least two sizes larger than it. 

On a failure, you throw the target a number of feet up to your STR Score to a space you can see within range of the target and the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition. On a success, the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] condition ends on the target.

The target takes Bludgeoning damage equal to 1 + your STR modifier (minimum 1). 

**Thrown At A Creature.** If the creature is thrown at another creature, that creature must succeed on a DEX saving throw against the same DC or take the same damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.

<br>

# Object

**Requirements.** When you take the Attack action, you use one of your attacks to throw an object you are [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Carrying or Lifting a Heavy Object\|Carrying]] a number of feet up to your STR Score.

**Thrown At A Creature.** If the object is thrown into a space occupied by a creature, that creature must succeed on a DEX saving throw against your STR Save DC.

On a failure, the creature takes an amount of damage (usually a number of d4s equal to the object's size) of a type the DM thinks is appropriate for the object (usually Bludgeoning).

If the object is large enough, the creature may have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] conditions at the DM's discretion.

<br>

# Throwing Beyond STR Score

**Athletics Check.** If you attempt to make a Throw beyond your ability to do so, you must make a STR (Athletics) check of DC (10 + Total Distance minus the Distance you can Throw). On a failure, you throw the creature up to your STR Score.

A failure on this check counts toward your limited number of failures as seen in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] action.
