When you take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Dash\|Dash]] Action, you gain extra movement for the current turn. The increase equals your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] after applying any modifiers. With a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] of 30 feet, for example, you can move up to 60 feet on your turn if you Dash. If your Speed of 30 feet is reduced to 15 feet, you can move up to 30 feet this turn if you Dash.

If you have a special speed, such as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Fly Speed\|Fly Speed]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Swim Speed\|Swim Speed]], you can use that speed instead of your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] when you take this action. You choose which speed to use each time you take it.
