You can try to tumble through a hostile creature's space, ducking and weaving past the opponent. 

As an Action or a Bonus Action, the tumbler makes a DEX (Acrobatics) check contested by the hostile creature's DEX save DC. On a success, it can move through the hostile creature's space once this turn without considering [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]].
