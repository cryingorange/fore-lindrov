Winching a portcullis up or down requires the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action. If a creature can't reach the winch (usually because it's on the other side of the portcullis), lifting the portcullis requires the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action and a successful STR (Athletics) check. The DC of the check depends on the type of portcullis, as shown in the Portcullises table.
