When you take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] action on your turn and attack with a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light]] weapon, you can make one extra attack as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] later on the same turn. That extra attack must be made with a different Light weapon, and you don't add your ability modifier to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative. 

>For example, you can attack with a Shortsword in one hand and a Dagger in the other using the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] Action and a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]], but you don't add your STR or DEX modifier to the damage roll of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] unless that modifier is negative.
