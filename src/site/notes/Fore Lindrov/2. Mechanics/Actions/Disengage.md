If you take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Disengage\|Disengage]] action, your movement doesn't provoke [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attack]] for the rest of the current turn.
