A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] creature can use its Action to make a STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) check contested by the grappler's STR (Athletics) check.

The condition also ends if the grappler has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition or if the distance between the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] target and the grappler exceeds the grapple's range.

## Help Creature
You can take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Help\|Help]] Action to try to free a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] creature within 5 feet of you, which requires a successful STR (Athletics) check contested by the grappler's STR (Athletics) check or the Grapple's escape DC.
