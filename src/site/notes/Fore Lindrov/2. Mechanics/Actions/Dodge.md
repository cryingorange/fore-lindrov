Until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] if you can see the attacker, and you make DEX Saving Throws with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]].

You lose these benefits if you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition or if your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] is 0.
