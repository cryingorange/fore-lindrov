**Ability Check.** You make a STR (Athletics) or DEX (Athletics) check contested by the target's STR (Athletics) or DEX (Athletics) check (it chooses which). On a success, the attack causes no damage or other ill effect and the target drops one object of your choice that it's holding.
 
**Disadvantage.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on your STR (Athletics) or DEX (Athletics) check if the target is holding the item with two or more hands or is at least one size Larger.
