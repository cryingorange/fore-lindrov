# Details

**How To Use.** A Legendary Action is an action that a monster can take immediately after another creature's turn. Only one of these actions can be taken at a time and only after another creature's turn ends. The monster can't take a Legendary Action if it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition or is otherwise unable to take Actions.

**Limitations & Regaining Legendary Actions.** A monster has a limited number of Legendary Action uses, and that number is specified in the stat block. The monster expends one use whenever it takes a Legendary Action, and it regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.

# Multiple Legendary Creatures

If there are multiple creatures with Legendary Actions fielded at once, they each share the same pool of Legendary Action uses. Only one monster can take a Legendary Action at a time, and after another creature's turn.
