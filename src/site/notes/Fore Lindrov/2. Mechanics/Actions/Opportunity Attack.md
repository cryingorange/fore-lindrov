You can make an Opportunity Attack when a creature that you can see leaves your reach using its Action, its Bonus Action, its Reaction, one of its Speeds, or if it takes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to use [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Rune Magic\|Rune Magic]]. 

To make the Opportunity Attack, take a Reaction to make one melee attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike against the provoking creature. The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach.
