When you are first infected with this Disease, you gain one level of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]].

Every 24 Hours, the target must succeed on a DC 10 CON Saving Throw or gain one level of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]].
