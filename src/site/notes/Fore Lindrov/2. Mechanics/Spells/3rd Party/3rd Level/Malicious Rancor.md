> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a skull that belonged to a creature that succumbed to a curse, worth at least 1 sp, which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Round\|Round]]

You hurl a deformed skull covered in scratches and holes and strewn in curses at a creature within range, where the skull explodes with violent hatred.

**Ranges Spell Attack.** Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Necrotic damage. 

**Detonation.** Hit or miss, the skull then detonates, emitting a psychic wailing from that point. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on the target must make a WIS saving throw.

**WIS Saving Throw.** On a failure, a creature takes 5d4 Psychic damage, or half as much damage on a successful one. Until the start of your next turn, if a creature enters the area for the first time or ends its turn there, it must make the saving throw against the detonation.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The Psychic damage increases by 2d4 for each slot level above 3.
