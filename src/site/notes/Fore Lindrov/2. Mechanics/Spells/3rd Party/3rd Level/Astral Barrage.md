> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a portal to the cosmos and send asteroids hurtling through it toward a creature within range. 

**DEX Saving Throw.** The target must succeed on a DEX saving throw or take 7d6 Bludgeoning damage. 

**Maintain Spell.** You can take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action on each of your subsequent turns to keep the portal open and target another or the same creature. The spell ends if you use your Action to do anything else. While [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentrating]] on this spell, your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] is halved.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 2d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
