> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a calcified bamboo stick)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

**DEX Saving Throw.** Massive bone spikes sprout under up to four creatures on the ground of your choice you can see within range. They must succeed on a DEX saving throw or take 3d10 Piercing damage and be impaled by the spike, becoming [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] and held aloft 5 feet in the air for the duration. 

**Restrained.** A creature [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] in this way takes 1d10 piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can use its Action to make a STR (Athletics) check contested by your spell save DC, breaking the bone spike and freeing itself on a success.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage (both initial and ongoing) increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 3rd.
