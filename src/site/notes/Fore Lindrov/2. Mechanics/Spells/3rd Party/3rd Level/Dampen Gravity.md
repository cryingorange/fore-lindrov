> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self 
> **Components:** V, S, M (a feather)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You focus your powers to lessen the call of gravity in a 60-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from you. 

Each creature of your choice in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] gain the following benefits:
- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Jumping\|Jump]] distance doubled 
- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] increased by 10 feet. 
- Advantage on Acrobatics checks
- Ignore falling damage if they end their fall within the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]].
