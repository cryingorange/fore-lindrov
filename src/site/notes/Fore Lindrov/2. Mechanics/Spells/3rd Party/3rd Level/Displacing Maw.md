> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 10 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

Your rib cage opens up violently, forming a maw that attempts to devour a creature within range. 

**STR Saving Throw.** The creature must make a STR saving throw. On a failure, it takes 4d8 Piercing damage and is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleported]] through magic to a point of your choice on the ground within 60 feet of you, where the maw reappears and regurgitates them. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not transported.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8, and the teleportation range increases by 10 feet, for each spell slot level above 3.

**Classes:** #Warlock, #Wizard
