> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You touch a willing creature. Its bones become soft and rubbery, and it gains the following benefits:
- It can spend 5 feet of movement to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Escape A Grapple\|Escape A Grapple]] without requiring an ability check and can squeeze through a space that is large enough for a creature two sizes smaller than it.
- It has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on DEX saving throws.
- It has Resistance to Bludgeoning damage.
- Its reach with melee attacks increases by 5 feet.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 3.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
