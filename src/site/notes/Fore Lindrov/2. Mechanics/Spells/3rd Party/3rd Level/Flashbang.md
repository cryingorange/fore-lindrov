> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a handful of metal powder)
> **Duration:** 1 minute

A tiny spark at a point you choose within range erupts with a bright flash. 

**CON Saving Throw.** Each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on that point makes a CON Saving Throw, taking 2d8 Thunder damage and gaining the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Deafened\|Deafened]] conditions for the duration on a failed save or only half as much damage on a successful one.

At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8, and the radius increases by 5 feet, for each spell slot level above 3.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Bender, #Ranger, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
