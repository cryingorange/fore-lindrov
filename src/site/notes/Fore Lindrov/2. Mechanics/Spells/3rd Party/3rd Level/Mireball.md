> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a tiny ball of guano and mud)
> **Duration:** 1 minute

A blob of gloopy mud splunks from your pointed finger to a point you choose within range, then splooges with a wet burp into an explosion of sludge. 

**STR Saving Throw.** Each creature in a 15-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centred on that point makes a STR Saving Throw. On a failure, the target 3d10 Bludgeoning damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition. On a successful one, a target takes half as much damage only.

**Changed Terrain.** A creature that enters the area or ends its turn there must succeed on a DEX Saving Throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition. A creature that chooses to spend one additional foot of movement for every foot of movement in the area doesn't need to make this save.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 3.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Bender, #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Warlock
