> **Casting Time:** 1 hour
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (100 gp of alchemical fluids or holy water, which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You apply alchemical fluids or holy water to a contaminated Humanoid creature while reciting an exacting magical chant that expels eldritch contaminants from the target's body. 

When you finish casting the spell, all [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]] levels and mutations are removed from the creature. It then gains [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level for each contamination level removed with this spell.
