> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Hit Point Dice.** As part of casting this spell, you must expend one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]] or the spell automatically fails. 

A willing target takes Fire damage equal to a roll of the expended [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]]. If the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition, that condition immediately ends.
