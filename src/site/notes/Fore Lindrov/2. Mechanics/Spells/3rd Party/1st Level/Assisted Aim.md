> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** 1 minute

You invigorate up to three creatures within range with improved accuracy. Each target gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls it makes with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Range\|Ranged]] weapons. Additionally, the normal and long range of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Range\|Ranged]] weapons wielded by a target are doubled for the spell's duration.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
