**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a tiger's claw)
**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Choose a creature you can see within range. You infuse them with osteomantic energy, growing sharp claws from their carpal bones. 

**Unarmed Strikes.** For the duration, the target's unarmed attacks deal an extra 1d4 Slashing damage on a hit.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases to 1d6 at 2nd level, 2d4 at 3rd Level, 2d6 at 4th Level, and 3d4 at 5th Level.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
