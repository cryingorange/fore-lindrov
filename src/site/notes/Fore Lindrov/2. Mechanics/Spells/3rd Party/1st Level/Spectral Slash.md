> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** 20 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Attack.** You send forth a spectral copy of yourself to strike down your foe. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within 20 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 damage of your weapon's damage type.

**Optional Action.** You can then use an Action to move up to 20 feet in a straight line towards the target, streaking through a spectral trail, and take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] Action against it. To use this action, you must attack with a melee weapon.

**Using a Higher Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 and the range and distance you can move increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 1.

**Classes:** #Paladin, #Ranger
