> **Range:** Self

**Hit Point Dice.** As part of casting this spell, you must expend one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]] or the spell automatically fails.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can expend one additional [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] for each spell slot level above 1.
