> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You enhance the gravitational field around your body, allowing you to jump higher and farther. For the duration, your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Jumping\|Jump]] distance is tripled. 

**STR Saving Throw.** Additionally, once per turn immediately after you jump, a wave of gravity is unleashed, and each creature in a 5-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from your origin space must make a STR saving throw. On a failed save, a creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Ranger, #Sorcerer
