> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You command the body of a creature to twist and warp. If the target has 125 HP or fewer, it takes 8d10 Necrotic damage, and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition. 

Otherwise, its Speed is 0 until the start of your next turn and the target makes a CON saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
