>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  60 feet
>**Components:** V, S, M (a silver scroll describing the spell’s target worth at least 1,000 gp, which the spell consumes)
>**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

**Cast As Circle Spell.** This spell can only be cast as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Circle Spellcasting\|Circle Spell]], Casting this as a Circle spell requires a minimum of two Secondary Casters. For each Secondary Caster, the DC increases by 1.

When the spell is cast, each Secondary Caster must expend a level 7+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.

**Target With Legendary Actions.** You tap into the life force of a creature that is capable of performing Legendary Actions. 

**CON Saving Throw.** When you cast the spell, the target must make a successful Constitution saving throw or lose the ability to take Legendary Actions for the spell’s duration. 

**No Legendary Resistance.** A creature can’t use Legendary Resistance to automatically succeed on the saving throw against this spell. 

**Repeat Save.** An affected creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, regaining 1 Legendary Action on a successful save. The target continues repeating the saving throw until the spell ends or it regains all its Legendary Actions.
