> **Casting Time:** 24 hours
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (enchanted bones, incense, and herbs worth at least 1000 gp, which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** Until dispelled

**Conditions.** You touch a point and infuse an area around it with Osteomancy. The area is a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] with a radius of up to 120 feet, and the spell fails if the radius includes an area already under the effect of a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/3rd Party/7th Level/Boneyard\|Boneyard]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Hallow\|Hallow]] spell.

**Benefits.** The affected area is subject to the following effects:
- You know the exact location and hear the thoughts (if any) of any Undead creature within the area, and you know if they are under someone's control.
- All creatures within the area lose Resistance and Immunity to Necrotic and Piercing damage.
-  As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]], whenever a non-Undead creature dies within the area, you can choose to rip its skeleton from its flesh and force it to rise again under your control. 
	- If you do, the creature is resurrected with 1 HP, its creature type becomes Undead, it becomes Immune to all damage, loses access to any Legendary Actions it had (if any), and you can mentally command its actions (no action required). At the end of its next turn, it dies and cannot be resurrected by any means short of a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/Wish\|Wish]] spell.

**Classes:** #Cleric, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
