> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**CON Saving Throw.** You coat your hand with vicious osteomancy. A creature you touch makes a CON Saving Throw, taking 10d8 Slashing damage on a failed save. Additionally, you rip off one of the creature's limbs (leg, arm, or other similar appendage). On a successful save, the target takes only half as much damage.

**Limb Loss Immunity.** A creature is Immune to losing its limbs if it is Immune to Slashing damage, has Legendary Actions, or the GM decides that the creature is too big for its limbs to be cut off with this spell. Such a creature takes an extra 5d8 Slashing damage instead.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 4d8, and the extra damage by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 7.
