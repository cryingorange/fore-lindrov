> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (bone powder)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You attempt to trap one creature you can see within range in a field of gravity that compresses and folds in on itself. 

**STR Saving Throw.** The target must succeed on a STR saving throw or take 12d10 Bludgeoning damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition until the start of your next turn as it reels from the pain.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.

At Higher Levels. You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 7.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
