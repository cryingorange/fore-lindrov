>**Casting Time:** Action 
>**Range:**  30 feet
>**Components:** V
>**Duration:** Instantaneous

You utter an ancient word of Power crafted by the Whispered One. 

### Power Word Blind Effects

|Hit Points |Effect|
| --- | --- |
| 0 – 49 | The target permanently has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition as its eyes are ripped from its body. |
|50 – 99 | The target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition for 1 minute. |
|100+| The target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition, and makes a CON saving throw at the end of each of its turns to end the effect. After 1 minute, it automatically succeeds.  |
