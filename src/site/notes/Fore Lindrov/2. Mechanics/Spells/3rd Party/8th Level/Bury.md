> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

Until the spell ends, the pull of gravity increases in a 300-foot-tall, 30-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cylinder\|Cylinder]] centered on a point on the ground within range. 

**Prone.** Each creature in this area has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition and cannot lose this condition while there. 

**Damage.** A creature that enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there gains the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition and takes 4d6 Bludgeoning damage. 

**Flying Creature.** A flying creature in the area immediately falls to the ground and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition. 

**STR Saving Throw.** A creature must succeed on a STR Saving Throw at the start of each of its turns to move, or take any Action. A creature moving through that area must spend 3 feet of movement for every 1 foot it moves.

**Start Turn while Prone.** A creature with the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition that ends its turn in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cylinder\|Cylinder]] must make a STR Saving Throw. On a failure, the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] conditions from the crushing pressure. 

The target is under [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Three-Quarters Cover]], as only the upper part of their body is visible. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns to end this effect.

**Full Duration.** If the spell lasts for its full duration, the ground cracks under the tremendous weight generated and becomes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]. Each 5-foot-square area requires at least 1 minute to clear by hand to remove the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]].

**Casting as a Circle Spell.** Casting this as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Circle Spellcasting\|Circle spell]] requires a minimum of five secondary casters capable of casting Dunamancy Magic. In addition to the spell's usual components, you must provide a special component (a black, obsidian shard from the Far Realm), which the spell consumes.

The spell's casting time increases to 24 hours, its range increases to 1 mile, the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cylinder\|Cylinder's]] radius and height increase to 1 mile, and its duration increases to until dispelled (no Concentration required). The spell ends early if any caster who participated in this casting contributes to another casting of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/3rd Party/8th Level/Bury\|Bury]] as a Circle spell.

When the spell is cast, each secondary caster must expend a level 4+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.
