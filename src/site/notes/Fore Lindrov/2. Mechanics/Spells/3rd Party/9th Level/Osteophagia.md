> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

The most lethal osteomantic power. You can cause the skeleton of a creature to liquefy in a matter of seconds, causing the body to collapse in on itself. 

**CON Saving Throw.** A creature you touch makes a CON saving throw. On a failure, it dies. On a successful save, the targets takes 10d6 Necrotic damage. 

**Immune.** Creatures without bones and creatures whose CR or character level is equal to or higher than the caster's CR or character level are Immune to this spell.

**Casting as a Circle Spell.** Casting this as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Circle Spellcasting\|Circle spell]] requires a minimum of five secondary casters, and the caster does not need to be able to cast 9th Level spells. The combined CRs and Character Levels of the involved casters must be equal to or higher than 23. 

In addition to the spell's usual components, you must provide a special component (Five bones of a creature of CR 23 or higher), which the spell consumes.

When the spell is cast, each secondary caster must expend a level 5+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.

Additionally, when the spell is cast, each secondary caster dies, and instead of the usual effects, the primary caster instead gains the ability to cast this spell At Will.
