> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, it gains a Fly Speed equal to its Walking Speed and can hover.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 6.
