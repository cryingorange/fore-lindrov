> **Casting Time:** 10 minutes or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Ritual\|Ritual]]
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** 8 hours

Using a ritual of ancient magic, you distort the gravitational balance of an enclosed space of your choice for the duration. 

**Dimensions.** The chamber can be of any shape up to 200 feet wide, 200 feet tall, and 200 feet long. While in the chamber, a creature doesn't take falling damage, has its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Jumping\|Jump]] distance tripled, has a Fly Speed equal to its Walking Speed, and can hover.

Casting this spell on the same spot every day for a year makes this effect permanent.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
