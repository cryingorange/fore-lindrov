> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You fire out the bones of your forearm through your hand, leaving a severe wound that immediately regrows and heals. 

**Ranged Spell Attack.** Make a ranged spell attack against the target within range. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 Piercing damage and must succeed on a STR saving throw or be pushed back 15 feet. If pushed into an obstacle, the target is impaled on it and is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] by the bone until the end of its next turn. 

**Large or larger.** Creatures that are Large or larger have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on this saving throw.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
