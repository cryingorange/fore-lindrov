> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You cause a wave of gravity to erupt at a point within range.

**DEX Saving Throw.** Each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on that point makes a DEX Saving Throw. On a failure, the creature takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition or only half as much damage on a successful one. 

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6, and the radius increases by 5 feet for each spell slot above 2nd.
