> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self (20-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]])
> **Components:** V, S, M (a scorpion's tail)
> **Duration:** 1 minute

**Emanation & Reaction.** You grow a long, scorpion-like tail made of sharpened bones. Whenever a creature moves while within a 20-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from you, you can use your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to try and pierce them with your tail. 

**DEX Saving Throw.** The target must succeed on a DEX saving throw or take 2d4 Piercing damage and be pulled to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you as your tail drags them in.

**Using Higher-Level Spell Slots.** The damage increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Ranger
