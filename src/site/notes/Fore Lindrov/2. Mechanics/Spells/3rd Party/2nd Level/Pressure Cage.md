> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a shard of ball and chain)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] of powerful gravity centered on a point within range. 

**STR Saving Throw.** The area becomes Difficult Terrain for the duration. Each creature in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] makes a STR Saving Throw. On a failure, the creature's Speed is reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. 

**Repeated Save.** A creature must also make this save when the Sphere moves into its space and when it enters the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] or starts its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

**Bonus Action.** On subsequent turns, as a Bonus Action, you can move the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] up to 20 feet in any direction.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The sphere's radius increases by 5 feet for each spell slot level above 2.
