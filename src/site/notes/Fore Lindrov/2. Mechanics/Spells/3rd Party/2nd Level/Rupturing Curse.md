**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You focus your magic to shatter the body of one creature you can see within range. 

**CON Saving Throw.** The target must succeed on a CON Saving Throw or take 3d6 Piercing damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.

**Bludgeoning Damage.** In addition, if the target takes Bludgeoning damage equal to 5 times its CON modifier (minimum of 5) or more in a single turn, its skeleton shatters, reducing its Speed to 0 for the duration of the spell. 

**Ends Early.** If the target does not take such damage before the start of its next turn, the spell ends. A creature without bones is Immune to this effect.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 2d6 for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Warlock, #Wizard
