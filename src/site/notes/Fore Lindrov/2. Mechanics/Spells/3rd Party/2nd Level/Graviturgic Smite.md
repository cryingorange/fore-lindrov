> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]]
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

The target hit by the strike takes an extra 2d6 Bludgeoning damage from the attack and it must make a STR Saving Throw.

**STR Saving Throw.** On a failure the target's Speed is reduced **to** 10 feet until the spell ends. On a success, the target's Speed is reduced **by** 10 feet until the spell ends.

**Limit Reduction.** A creature's Speed cannot be reduced to lower than 10 feet from this effect.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
