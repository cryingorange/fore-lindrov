> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 15 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (the body part of a Great One)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You summon a large number of tentacles and slugs from your palm to strike a creature within range. 

**Melee Spell Attack.** Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 3d6 Bludgeoning damage. If you score a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]], the target also has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition until the start of your next turn.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 and range increases by 5 feet for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
