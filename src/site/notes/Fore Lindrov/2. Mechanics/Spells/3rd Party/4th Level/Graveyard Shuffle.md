> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of spinal bone and an opal worth at least 300 gp, which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You drain the bone marrow of a creature, attempting to steal its life force and transfer it to another creature of your choice. 

**Ranged Spell Attack.** Make a ranged spell attack against a target within range. On a hit, the target takes 4d6 Necrotic damage. Choose another creature within range; it regains a number of HP equal to half the damage dealt, or if it was dead for less than 1 minute, the creature returns to life with HP equal to the number regained. If any of the targets do not have bones, the spell fails. This spell can't return to life a creature that has died of old age, nor can it restore any missing body parts.

**Classes:** #Cleric, #Druid
