> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]]
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Damage & Concentration.** Your strike cause ambient magic surrounded a creature to collapse and detonate. The target hit by the strike takes an extra 4d6 Force damage from the attack. If the creature is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentrating]] on a spell, that [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] is broken.

**Dispel Magic.** In addition, each ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. For each ongoing spell of level 4 or higher on the target, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (DC 10 plus that spell's level). On a successful check, the spell ends.
