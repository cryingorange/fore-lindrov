> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a strip of magnesium)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

**Emanation.** Bright light erupts from your form and dazzles those around you in a 10-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]]. 

**Bright Light.** You emit [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] in a 50-foot radius and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 50 feet. 

**CON Saving Throw.** Whenever the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] enters a creature's space and whenever a creature enters the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] or ends its turn there, the creature must make a CON Saving Throw. 

On a failed save, the creature takes 3d10 Radiant damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 4.
