> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a virgin's carpal bone)
> **Duration:** 1 minute

**STR Saving Throw.** A creature within range must succeed on a STR saving throw or be trapped inside an iron maiden formed of bone that appears in its space. The creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] conditions, has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Total Cover]] and can't be damaged by attacks or effects originating from outside the iron maiden.

**Piercing Damage.** A trapped creature takes 3d6 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns as spikes of bone pierce its body. At the end of each of its turns, a trapped creature can repeat the saving throw, escaping and ending the spell on a success. 

**Stats.** The bone maiden has AC 14, 80 HP, Immunity to Psychic and Poison damage, and Vulnerability to Bludgeoning damage. If the maiden is destroyed, the creature trapped inside is freed and the spell ends.

**Classes:** #Warlock, #Wizard
