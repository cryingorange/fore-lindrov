> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You flick your finger against a creature. The target is impacted by a devastating gravitational force and must make a STR saving throw. 

**STR Saving Throw.** On a failed save, it takes 6d10 Bludgeoning damage and is pushed 60 feet away from you. The push stops early if the creature hits a solid surface or is pushed into a creature of its size or larger. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.

**Pushed Into Another Creature.** If the target is pushed into or through the space of a creature, that creature must make a DEX saving throw, taking 1d10 Bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. This damage is increased by 1d10 per size smaller the creature is than the target.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage the original target takes increases by 1d10, and the distance it's pushed by 30 feet for each spell slot level above 4.
