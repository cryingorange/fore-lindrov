> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a flute made of a femur worth 50 gp)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You magically create the horrifying illusion of all your bones breaking and your body bending in impossible ways, accompanied by the sound of a shattered skull. 

**WIS Saving Throw.** Each creature of your choice in a 30-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from you must make a WIS saving throw. On a failure, the target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition for the duration. 

**Frightened.** A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] creature's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] becomes 0 and it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition from its legs giving out under the fear. Whenever the target takes damage, it repeats the save at the end of its turn, ending the spell on itself on a success.
