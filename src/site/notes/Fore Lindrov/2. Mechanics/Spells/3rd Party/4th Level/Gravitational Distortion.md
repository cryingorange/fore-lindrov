> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You cause a gravitational distortion at a point you can see within range.

**STR Saving Throw.** Each creature in a 30-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] makes a STR Saving Throw, taking 6d6 Bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

**Pushed.** Additionally, any creature that fails this saving throw is pushed 30 feet in a straight line in a direction of your choice.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
