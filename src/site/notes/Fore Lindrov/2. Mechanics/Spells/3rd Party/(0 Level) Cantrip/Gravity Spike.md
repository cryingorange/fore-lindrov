> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a localized gravitational field that temporarily shifts the landscape to impale a creature. Choose a creature on the ground you can see within range. 

**DEX Saving Throw.** The creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 1d4 Piercing damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** This spell's damage increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
