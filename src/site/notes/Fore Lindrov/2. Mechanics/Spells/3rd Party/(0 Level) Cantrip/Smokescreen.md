> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a puffball mushroom)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Round\|Round]]

**Sphere.** You spew a thick cloud of dense black smoke that forms a 5-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point within range or as a 5-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from you that lasts until the start of your next turn. 

The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]].

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The radius increases by 5 feet when you reach levels 5 (10-foot), 11, (15-foot), and 17 (20-foot).
