> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** M (an eldritch parasite)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

Your eye darkens under the influence of the eldritch madness, appearing as an endless starry sky. 

**Ranged Spell Attack.** Make a ranged spell attack against a creature within range as you fire a projectile of eldritch energy from your eye. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Necrotic damage. If you score a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]], you can reroll any roll of 4 or lower on the damage die until you get a higher result.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
