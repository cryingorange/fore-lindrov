> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You release a loud, monstrous roar mimicking those of ravenous beasts, causing shockwaves around you. 

**STR Saving Throw.** Each creature in a 10-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from you must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from you and knocked prone.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** This spell's radius increases by 5 feet when you reach 5th level (15 feet), 11th level (20 feet), and 17th level (25 feet).

**Classes:** #Bard, #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Warlock
