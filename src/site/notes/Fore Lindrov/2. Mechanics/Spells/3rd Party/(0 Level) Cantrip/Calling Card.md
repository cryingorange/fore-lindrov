> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** S, M (a stamp carved like the mark left by your calling card)
> **Duration:** Until dispelled

You touch a corpse and leave a magical mark. You can choose to make it a visible mark that appears as a rune or word written on the flesh, or you can choose to make the mark [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]]. A creature that takes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Study\|Study]] Action to specifically examine a corpse automatically detects it, whether visible or Invisible.
