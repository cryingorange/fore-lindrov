> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

**DEX Saving Throw.** You touch the ground under you and 20-foot-tall treelike bones sprout from the ground in a 30-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on you. Each creature of your choice in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] makes a DEX saving throw, taking 4d8 Piercing damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

**Area Change.** For the duration, the area becomes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]], and when a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d8 Piercing damage for every 5 feet it travels; you are Immune to this effect. The forest of dread provides [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Half Cover]] to any creatures inside the area. The bones disintegrate when the spell ends.
