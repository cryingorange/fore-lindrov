> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Charisma Saving Throw.** You attempt to gaze upon the soul of a creature you can see within range. The target makes a CHA Saving Throw. On a failure, its origins are revealed to you, and you learn all of the creature's Ability Scores, Skill Proficiencies, Vulnerabilities, Resistances, Immunities (Damage / Conditions), Saving Throw, Weapon and Armour Proficiencies, Alignment, and Traits.

If a creature succeeds on this saving throw, it is Immune to this spell for 24 hours.
