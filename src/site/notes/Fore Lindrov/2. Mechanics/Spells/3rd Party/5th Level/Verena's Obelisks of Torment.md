>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  120 feet
>**Components:** V, S, M (a dagger with an obsidian blade worth at least 300 gp)
>**Duration:** 10 Minutes

**Obelisks.** Up to five obelisks burst from points on the ground that you can see within range. Each obelisk has a diameter of 5 feet and a height of 10 feet. The ground where a pillar appears must be wide enough for its diameter. The obelisks remain until the spell ends or when you cast this spell again.

**INT Saving Throw.** When the obelisks first appear, each creature within a 5-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from each obelisk makes an INT Saving Throw, taking 4d6 Psychic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Restrain.** Whenever a creature tries to moves out of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]], psionic chains lash out to restrain it. The target must make a STR Saving Throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition until the start of its next turn. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

**End Turn.** Any creature that ends its turn within the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] or has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition from the obelisk's chains takes 2d6 Psychic damage.   

**Using a Higher Level Spell Slot.** The initial damage increases by 1d6 and you create one additional obelisk for each spell slot level above 5.
