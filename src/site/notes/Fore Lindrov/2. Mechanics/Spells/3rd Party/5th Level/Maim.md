>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  Self
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous

Your hands become claws bathed in necrotic energy. Make a melee spell attack against a creature you can reach. On a hit, the target takes (4d6) Necrotic damage and a section of its body of your choosing withers:

- **Upper Limb.** The target has disadvantage on STR checks, and, if it has the Multiattack action, it has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on its first attack roll each [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Round\|Round]].
- **Lower Limb.** The target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet, and it has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on DEX checks.
- **Body.** Choose one damage type: Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing. The target loses its Resistance to that damage type. If the target doesn't have Resistance to the chosen damage type, it is Vulnerable to that damage type instead. 

The effect is permanent until removed by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Greater Restoration\|Greater Restoration]], or similar magic.
