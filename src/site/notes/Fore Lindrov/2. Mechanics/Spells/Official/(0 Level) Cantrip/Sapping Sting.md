>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  30 feet
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous

You sap the vitality of one creature you can see in range. The target must succeed on a CON saving throw or take 1d4 Necrotic damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.

**Cantrip Upgrade** This spell's damage increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
