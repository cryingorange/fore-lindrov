>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  Self (5-foot radius)
>**Components:** V
>**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a momentary circle of spectral blades that sweep around you. All other creatures within 5 feet of you must succeed on a DEX saving throw or take 1d6 Force damage.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** This spell's damage increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).

**Classes:** #Artificer, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
