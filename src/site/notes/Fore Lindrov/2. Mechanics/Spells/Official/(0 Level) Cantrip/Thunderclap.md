>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  Self
>**Components:** S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous

Each creature in a 5-foot ~={blue}Emanation=~ originating from you must succeed on a CON saving throw or take 1d6 Thunder damage. The spell's thunderous sound can be heard up to 100 feet away.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).

**Classes:** #Artificer, #Bard, #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
