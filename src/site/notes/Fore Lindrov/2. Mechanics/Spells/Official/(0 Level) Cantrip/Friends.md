>**Casting Time:** Action 
>**Range:**  10 feet
>**Components:** S, M (some makeup)
>**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You magically emanate a sense of friendship toward one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] condition for the duration. The target succeeds automatically if it isn't a Humanoid, if you're fighting it, or if you have cast this spell on it within the past 24 hours.

The spell ends early if the target takes damage or if you make an attack roll, deal damage, or force anyone to make a saving throw. When the spell ends, the target knows it was [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] by you.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
