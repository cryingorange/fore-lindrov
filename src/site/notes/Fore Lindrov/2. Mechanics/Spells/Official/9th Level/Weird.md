> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You try to create illusory terrors in others' minds. Each creature of your choice in a 30-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point within range makes a WIS saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 10d10 Psychic damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition for the duration. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] target makes a WIS saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a failed save, it takes 5d10 Psychic damage. On a successful save, the spell ends on that target.

**Classes:** #Warlock, #Wizard
