**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a caterpillar cocoon)
**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You transform up to ten creatures of your choice that you can see within range. An unwilling target must succeed on a WIS saving throw to resist the transformation. An unwilling Shapechanger automatically succeeds on the save.

**Forms.** Each target assumes a Beast form of your choice, and you can choose the same form or different ones for each target. The new form can be any Beast you have seen whose Challenge Rating is equal to or less than the target's (or half the target's level, if the target doesn't have a Challenge Rating). The target's game statistics, including mental ability scores, are replaced by the statistics of the chosen Beast, but the target retains its HP, alignment, and personality.

**Temporary HP.** Each target gains a number of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] equal to the HP of its new form. These [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] can't be replaced by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] from another source. A target reverts to its normal form when it has no more [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] or it dies. If the spell ends before then, the creature loses all its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] and reverts to its normal form.

**Limited Actions.** The creature is limited in the Actions it can perform by the nature of its new form. It can't speak, cast spells, or do anything else that requires hands or speech.

**Gear.** The target's gear melds into the new form. The target can't activate, use, wield, or otherwise benefit from any of its equipment.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
