> **Casting Time:** 1 minute
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (a hummingbird feather)
> **Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature and bestow a limited ability to see into the immediate future. For the duration, the target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Tests]], and other creatures have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against it. The spell ends early if you cast it again.
