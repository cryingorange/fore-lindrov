> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a jade circlet worth 1,500+ GP)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You shape-shift into another creature for the duration or until you take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to shape-shift into a different eligible form. The new form must be of a creature that has a Challenge Rating no higher than your level or Challenge Rating. You must have seen the sort of creature before, and it can't be a Construct or an Undead.

**Temporary HP.** When you cast the spell, you gain a number of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] equal to the HP of the first form into which you shape-shift. These [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] vanish if any remain when the spell ends.

**Stats.** Your game statistics are replaced by the stat block of the chosen form, but you retain your creature type; alignment; personality; INT, WIS, and CHA scores; HP; Hit Point Dice; proficiencies; and ability to communicate. If you have the Spellcasting feature, you retain it too.

**Gear.** Upon shape-shifting, you determine whether your equipment drops to the ground or changes in size and shape to fit the new form while you're in it.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Wizard
