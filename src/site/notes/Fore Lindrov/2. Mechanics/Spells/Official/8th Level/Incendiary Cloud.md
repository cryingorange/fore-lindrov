> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

A swirling cloud of embers and smoke fills a 20-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point within range. The cloud's area is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (like that created by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Gust of Wind\|Gust of Wind]]) disperses it.

When the cloud appears, each creature in it makes a DEX saving throw, taking 10d8 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature must also make this save when the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] moves into its space and when it enters the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The cloud moves 10 feet away from you in a direction you choose at the start of each of your turns.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
