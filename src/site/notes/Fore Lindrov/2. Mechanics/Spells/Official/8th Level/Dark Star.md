> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a shard of onyx and a drop of the caster's blood, both of which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

This spell creates a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point you choose within range. The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] can have a radius of up to 40 feet. The area within this [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] is filled with magical [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness]] and crushing gravitational force.

For the duration, the spell's area is difficult terrain. A creature with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision]] can't see through the magical [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness]], and nonmagical light can't illuminate it. No sound can be created within or pass through the area. Any creature or object entirely inside the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] is Immune to Thunder damage, and creatures are [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Deafened\|Deafened]] while entirely inside it. Casting a spell that includes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Spell Components\|Verbal ]]component is impossible there.

Any creature that enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must make a CON saving throw. The creature takes 8d10 force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature reduced to 0 HP by this damage is disintegrated. A disintegrated creature and everything it is wearing and carrying, except magic items, are reduced to a pile of fine gray dust.
