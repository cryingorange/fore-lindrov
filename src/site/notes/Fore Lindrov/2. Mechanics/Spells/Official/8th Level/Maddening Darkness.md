> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** V, M (a drop of pitch mixed with a drop of mercury)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

Magical [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness]] spreads from a point you choose within range to fill a 60-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] until the spell ends. The darkness spreads around corners. A creature with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision]] can't see through this [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness]]. Non-magical light, as well as light created by spells of 8th level or lower, can't illuminate the area.

**WIS Saving Throw.** Shrieks, gibbering, and mad laughter can be heard within the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]]. Whenever a creature starts its turn in the sphere, it must make a WIS saving throw, taking 8d8 Psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Classes:** #Warlock, #Wizard
