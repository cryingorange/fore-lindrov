> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You utter a word imbued with power from the Upper Planes. Each creature of your choice in range makes a CHA saving throw. On a failed save, a target that has 50 HP or fewer suffers an effect based on its current Hit Points, as shown in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/7th Level/Divine Word#Divine Word Effects Table\|#Divine Word Effects Table]]. Regardless of its HP, a Celestial, an Elemental, a Fey, or a Fiend target that fails its save is forced back to its plane of origin (if it isn't there already) and can't return to the current plane for 24 hours by any means short of a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/Wish\|Wish]] spell.
