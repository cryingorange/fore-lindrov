> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** 1 hour

You imbue one creature you touch with magical healing energy for the duration. Whenever the target casts a spell using a spell slot, the target can immediately roll a number of unexpended Hit Point Dice equal to the spell slot's level and regain HP equal to the roll's total plus your spellcasting ability modifier; those dice are then expended.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
