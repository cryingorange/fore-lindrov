> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You conjure spirits from the Elemental Planes that flit around you in a 15-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] for the duration. Until the spell ends, any attack you make deals an extra 2d8 damage when you hit a creature in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]]. This damage is Acid, Cold, Fire, or Lightning (your choice when you make the attack).

In addition, the ground in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] for your enemies.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Wizard
