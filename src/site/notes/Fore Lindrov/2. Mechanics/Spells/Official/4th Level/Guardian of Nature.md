> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

A nature spirit answers your call and transforms you into a powerful guardian. The transformation lasts until the spell ends. You choose one of the following forms to assume: **Primal Beast** or **Great Tree.**

**Primal Beast.** Bestial fur covers your body, your facial features become feral, and you gain the following benefits:
- Your walking Speed increases by 10 feet.
- You gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision]] with a range of 120 feet.
- You make STR-based attack rolls with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]].
- Your melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 Force damage on a hit.

**Great Tree.** Your skin appears barky, leaves sprout from your hair, and you gain the following benefits:
- You gain 10 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]].
- You make CON saving throws with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]].
- You make DEX and WIS-based attack rolls with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]].
- While you are on the ground, the ground within 15 feet of you is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] for your enemies.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Ranger
