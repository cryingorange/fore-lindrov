> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (an undead eyeball encased in a gem worth at least 150 gp)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Flame-like shadows wreathe your body until the spell ends, causing you to become heavily obscured to others. The shadows turn [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] within 10 feet of you into [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness]], and bright light in the same area to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]].

Until the spell ends, you have Resistance to Radiant damage. In addition, whenever a creature within 10 feet of you hits you with an attack, the shadows lash out at that creature, dealing it 2d8 Necrotic damage.
