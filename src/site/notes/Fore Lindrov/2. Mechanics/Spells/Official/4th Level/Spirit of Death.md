> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a gilded playing card worth at least 400 gp and depicting an avatar of death)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

**Summoning.** You call forth a spirit that embodies death. The spirit manifests in an unoccupied space you can see within range and uses the [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Summon Spells/Reaper Spirit\|Reaper Spirit]] stat block. The spirit disappears when it is reduced to 0 HP or when the spell ends.

**Ally.** The spirit is an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Ally\|Ally]] to you and your companions. In combat, the spirit shares your Initiative count and takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don't issue the spirit any commands, it takes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Dodge\|Dodge]] action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the spell's level appears in the [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Summon Spells/Reaper Spirit\|Reaper Spirit]] stat block.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
