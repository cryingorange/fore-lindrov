> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (a leather strap)
> **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target's movement is unaffected by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]], and spells and other [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Magical Effects\|magical effects]] can neither reduce the target's Speed nor cause the target to have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] conditions. The target also has a Swim Speed equal to its Speed.

In addition, the target can spend 5 feet of movement to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints, such as [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Adventuring Gear/Manacles\|Manacles]] or a creature imposing the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] condition on it.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.

**Classes:** #Artificer, #Bard, #Cleric, #Druid, #Ranger
