> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

Dim, greenish light spreads within a 30-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point you choose within range. The light spreads around corners, and it lasts until the spell ends.

When a creature moves into the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, that creature must succeed on a CON saving throw or take 4d10 Radiant damage, and it suffers one level of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] and emits a dim, greenish light in a 5-foot radius. This light makes it impossible for the creature to benefit from the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition. The light and any levels of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] caused by this spell go away when the spell ends.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
