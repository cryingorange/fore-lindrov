> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and your allies have Resistance to Necrotic damage, and your HP maximums can't be reduced. If an ally with 0 HP starts its turn in the aura, that ally regains 1 HP

**Classes:** #Cleric, #Paladin
