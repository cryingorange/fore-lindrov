> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

A cool light wreathes your body for the duration, emitting [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet.

Until the spell ends, you have Resistance to Radiant damage, and your melee attacks deal an extra 2d6 Radiant damage on a hit.

In addition, immediately after you take damage from a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to force the creature to make a CON saving throw. On a failed save, the creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Druid
