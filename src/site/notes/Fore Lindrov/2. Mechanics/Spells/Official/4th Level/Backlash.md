> **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take in response to taking damage
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Reduced Damage.** You ward yourself against destructive energy, reducing the damage taken by 4d6 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

**CON Saving Throw.** If the triggering damage was from a creature within range, you can force the creature to make a CON saving throw. The creature takes 4d6 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage reduction and Force damage from this spell both increase by 1d6 for every spell slot level above 4.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
