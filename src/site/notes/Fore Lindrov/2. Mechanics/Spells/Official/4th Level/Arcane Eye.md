> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of bat fur)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You create an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]], invulnerable eye within range that hovers for the duration. You mentally receive visual information from the eye, which can see in every direction. It also has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision]] with a range of 30 feet.

As a Bonus Action, you can move the eye up to 30 feet in any direction. A solid barrier blocks the eye's movement, but the eye can pass through an opening as small as 1 inch in diameter.

**Classes:** #Artificer, #Wizard
