> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, sunlight spreads from a point within range and fills a 60-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]]. The sunlight's area is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] and sheds [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet.

Alternatively, you cast the spell on an object that isn't being worn or carried, causing the sunlight to fill a 60-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from that object. Covering that object with something opaque, such as a bowl or helm, blocks the sunlight.

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Darkness]] created by a spell of level 3 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.

**Classes:** #Cleric, #Druid, #Paladin, #Ranger, #Sorcerer
