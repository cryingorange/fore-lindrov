> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components**: V, S, M (a bit of fur and a crystal rod)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

A stroke of lightning forming a 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]] blasts out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]] makes a DEX saving throw, taking 8d6 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
