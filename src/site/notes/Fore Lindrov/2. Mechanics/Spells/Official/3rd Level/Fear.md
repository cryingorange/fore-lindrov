> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a white feather)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Each creature in a 30-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cone\|Cone]] must succeed on a WIS saving throw or drop whatever it is holding and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition for the duration.

A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] creature takes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Dash\|Dash]] action and moves away from you by the safest route on each of its turns unless there is nowhere to move. If the creature ends its turn in a space where it doesn't have line of sight to you, the creature makes a WIS saving throw. On a successful save, the spell ends on that creature.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
