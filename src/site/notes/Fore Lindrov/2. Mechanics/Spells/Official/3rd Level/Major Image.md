> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of fleece)
>**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other visible phenomenon that is no larger than a 20-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]]. The image appears at a spot that you can see within range and lasts for the duration. It seems real, including sounds, smells, and temperature appropriate to the thing depicted, but it can't deal damage or cause conditions.

**Move Image.** If you are within range of the illusion, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] action to cause the image to move to any other spot within range. As the image changes location, you can alter its appearance so that its movements appear natural for the image. 
>**Example**
>If you create an image of a creature and move it, you can alter the image so that it appears to be walking. Similarly, you can cause the illusion to make different sounds at different times, even making it carry on a conversation, for example.

**Illusion.** Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, for things can pass through it. A creature that takes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Study\|Study]] action to examine the image can determine that it is an illusion with a successful INT (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the image, and its other sensory qualities become faint to the creature.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The spell lasts until dispelled, without requiring Concentration, if cast with a level 4+ spell slot.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
