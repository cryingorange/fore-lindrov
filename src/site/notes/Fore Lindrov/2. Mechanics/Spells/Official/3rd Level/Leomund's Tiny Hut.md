> **Casting Time:** 1 minute or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Ritual\|Ritual]]
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a crystal bead)
> **Duration:** 8 hours

A 10-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] springs into existence around you and remains stationary for the duration. The spell fails when you cast it if the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] isn't big enough to fully encapsulate all creatures in its area.

Creatures and objects within the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] when you cast the spell can move through it freely. All other creatures and objects are barred from passing through it. Spells of level 3 or lower can't be cast through it, and the effects of such spells can't extend into it.

The atmosphere inside the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] is comfortable and dry, regardless of the weather outside. Until the spell ends, you can command the interior to have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness]] (no action required). The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] is opaque from the outside and of any color you choose, but it's transparent from the inside.

The spell ends early if you leave the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] or if you cast it again.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Wizard
