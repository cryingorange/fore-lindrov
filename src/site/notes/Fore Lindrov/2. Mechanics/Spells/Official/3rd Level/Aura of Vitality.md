> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] for the duration. When you create the aura and at the start of each of your turns while it persists, you can restore 2d6 HP to one creature in it.
