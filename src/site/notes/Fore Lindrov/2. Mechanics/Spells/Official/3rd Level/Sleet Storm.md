> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature umbrella)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Until the spell ends, sleet falls in a 40-foot-tall, 20-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cylinder\|Cylinder]] centered on a point you choose within range. The area is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]], and exposed flames in the area are doused.

Ground in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cylinder\|Cylinder]] is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]. When a creature enters the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cylinder\|Cylinder]] for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a DEX saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition and lose [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]].

**Classes:** #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
