> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]]
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** 1 minute

The target hit by the strike takes an extra 3d8 Radiant damage from the attack, and the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition until the spell ends. At the end of each of its turns, the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] target makes a CON saving throw, ending the spell on itself on a success.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The extra damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3.
