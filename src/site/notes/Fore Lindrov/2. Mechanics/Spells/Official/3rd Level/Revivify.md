> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (a diamond worth 300+ GP, which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. That creature revives with 1 HP. This spell can't revive a creature that has died of old age, nor does it restore any missing body parts.

**Classes:** #Artificer, #Cleric, #Druid, #Paladin, #Ranger
