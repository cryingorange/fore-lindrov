> **Casting Time:** 1 minute or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Ritual\|Ritual]]
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** 1 hour

A Large, quasi-real, horselike creature appears on the ground in an unoccupied space of your choice within range. You decide the creature's appearance, and it is equipped with a saddle, bit, and bridle. Any of the equipment created by the spell vanishes in a puff of smoke if it is carried more than 10 feet away from the steed.

For the duration, you or a creature you choose can ride the steed. The steed uses the [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Beast/Riding Horse\|Riding Horse]] stat block, except it has a Speed of 100 feet and can travel 13 miles in an hour. When the spell ends, the steed gradually fades, giving the rider 1 minute to dismount. The spell ends early if the steed takes any damage.
