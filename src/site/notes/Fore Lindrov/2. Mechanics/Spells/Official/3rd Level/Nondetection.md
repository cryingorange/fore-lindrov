> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of diamond dust worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** 8 hours

For the duration, you hide a target that you touch from [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Schools of Magic\|Divination]] spells. The target can be a willing creature, or it can be a place or an object no larger than 10 feet in any dimension. The target can't be targeted by any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Schools of Magic\|Divination]] spell or perceived through magical scrying sensors.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Ranger, #Wizard
