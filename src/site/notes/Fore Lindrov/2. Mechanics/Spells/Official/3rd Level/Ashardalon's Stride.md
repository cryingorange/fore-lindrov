**Casting Time:** Bonus Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

The billowing flames of a dragon blast from your feet, granting you explosive speed. For the duration, your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] increases by 20 feet and moving doesn't provoke [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attacks]].

When you move within 5 feet of a creature or an object that isn't being worn or carried, it takes 1d6 Fire damage from your trail of heat. A creature or object can take this damage only once during a turn.

**Using Higher-Level Spell Slots.** Your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] increases by 5 feet for each spell slot level above 3 and the damage increases by 1d6 Fire damage for each spell slot level above 3.

**Classes:** #Artificer, #Ranger, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
