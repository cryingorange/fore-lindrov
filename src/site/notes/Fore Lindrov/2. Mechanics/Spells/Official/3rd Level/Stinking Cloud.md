> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 90 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a rotten egg)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] of yellow, nauseating gas centered on a point within range. The cloud is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]]. The cloud lingers in the air for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Gust of Wind\|Gust of Wind]]) disperses it.

Each creature that starts its turn in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] must succeed on a CON saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition until the end of the current turn. While [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] in this way, the creature can't take an Action or a Bonus Action.
