> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** S, M (a pinch of confetti)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a twisting pattern of colors in a 30-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] within range. The pattern appears for a moment and vanishes. Each creature in the area who can see the pattern must succeed on a WIS saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] condition for the duration. While [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]], the creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition and a Speed of 0.

The spell ends for an affected creature if it takes any damage or if someone else uses an Action to shake the creature out of its stupor.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
