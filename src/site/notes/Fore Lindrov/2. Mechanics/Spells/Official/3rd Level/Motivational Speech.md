> **Casting Time:** 1 minute
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** 1 hour

You address allies, staff, or innocent bystanders to exhort and inspire them to greatness, whether they have anything to get excited about or not. Choose up to five creatures within range that can hear you. For the duration, each affected creature gains 5 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] and has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on WIS saving throws. If an affected creature is hit by an attack, it has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on the next attack roll it makes. Once an affected creature loses the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] granted by this spell, the spell ends for that creature.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] increase by 5 for each slot level above 3rd.
