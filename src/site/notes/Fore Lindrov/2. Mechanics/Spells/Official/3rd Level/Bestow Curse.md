**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute
You touch a creature, which must succeed on a WIS saving throw or become Cursed for the duration. Until the curse ends, the target suffers one of the following effects of your choice:
- The target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against you.
- Choose one ability, the target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on ability checks and saving throws made with that ability.
- In combat, the target must succeed on a WIS saving throw at the start of each of its turns or be forced to take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Dodge\|Dodge]] action on that turn.
- If you deal damage to the target with an attack roll or a spell, the target takes an extra 1d8 Necrotic damage.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** If you cast this spell using a level 4 spell slot, you can maintain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] on it for up to 10 minutes. If you use a level 5+ spell slot, the spell doesn't require [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], and the duration becomes 8 hours (level 5-6 slot) or 24 hours (level 7-8 slot). If you use a level 9 spell slot, the spell lasts until dispelled.
