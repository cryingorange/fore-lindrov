> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a cockatrice feather)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

**CON Saving Throw.** You attempt to turn one creature that you can see within range into stone. The target makes a CON saving throw. On a failed save, it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition for the duration. On a successful save, its Speed is 0 until the start of your next turn. Constructs automatically succeed on the save.

**Subsequent Saving Throw.** A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] target makes another CON saving throw at the end of each of its turns. If it successfully saves against this spell three times, the spell ends. If it fails its saves three times, it is turned to stone and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Petrified\|Petrified]] condition for the duration. The successes and failures needn't be consecutive; keep track of both until the target collects three of a kind.

If you maintain your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] on this spell for the entire possible duration, the target is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Petrified\|Petrified]] until the condition is ended by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Greater Restoration\|Greater Restoration]] or similar magic.
