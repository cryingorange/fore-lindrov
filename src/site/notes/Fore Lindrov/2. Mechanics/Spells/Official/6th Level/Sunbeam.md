> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a magnifying glass)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You launch a sunbeam in a 5-foot-wide, 60-foot-long [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]]. Each creature in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]] makes a CON saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d8 Radiant damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

Until the spell ends, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to create a new [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]] of radiance.

For the duration, a mote of brilliant radiance shines above you. It sheds [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. This light is sunlight.
