> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 5 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You and up to five willing creatures within 5 feet of you instantly [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] to a previously designated sanctuary. You and any creatures that [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] with you appear in the nearest unoccupied space to the spot you designated when you prepared your sanctuary (see below). If you cast this spell without first preparing a sanctuary, the spell has no effect.

You must designate a location, such as a temple, as a sanctuary by casting this spell there.
