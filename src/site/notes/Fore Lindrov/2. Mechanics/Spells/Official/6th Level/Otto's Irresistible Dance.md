> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

One creature that you can see within range must make a WIS saving throw. On a successful save, the target dances comically until the end of its next turn, during which it must spend all its movement to dance in place.

On a failed save, the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] condition for the duration. While [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]], the target dances comically, must use all its movement to dance in place, and has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on DEX saving throws and attack rolls, and other creatures have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on attack rolls against it. On each of its turns, the target can take an action to collect itself and repeat the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Wizard
