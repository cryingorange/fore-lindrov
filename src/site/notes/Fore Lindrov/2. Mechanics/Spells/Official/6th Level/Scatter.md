> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

The air quivers around up to five creatures of your choice that you can see within range. An unwilling creature must succeed on a WIS saving throw to resist this spell. You [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] each affected target to an unoccupied space that you can see within 120 feet of you. That space must be on the ground or on a floor.
