> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You weave together threads of shadow to create a sword of solidified gloom in your hand. This magic sword lasts until the spell ends. It counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. It deals 2d8 Psychic damage on a hit and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Finesse\|Finesse]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light]], and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Thrown\|Thrown]] properties (range 20/60). In addition, when you use the sword to attack a target that is in [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Darkness\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Darkness]], you make the attack roll with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]].

If you drop the weapon or throw it, it dissipates at the end of the turn. Thereafter, while the spell persists, you can use a Bonus Action to cause the sword to reappear in your hand.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a 3rd- or 4th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 3d8. When you cast it using a 5th- or 6th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 4d8. When you cast it using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases to 5d8.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
