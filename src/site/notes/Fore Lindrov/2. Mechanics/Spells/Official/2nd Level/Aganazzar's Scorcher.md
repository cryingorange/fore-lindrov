> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a red dragon's scale)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]] of roaring flame 30 feet long and 5 feet wide emanates from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a DEX saving throw. A creature takes 3d8 Fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
