> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (an eyelash in gum arabic)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour
A creature you touch has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition until the spell ends. The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
