>Casting Time: Action
>Range: Self
>Components: V
>Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Your body becomes blurred. For the duration, any creature has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against you. An attacker is immune to this effect if it perceives you with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Blindsight\|Blindsight]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Truesight\|Truesight]].

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
