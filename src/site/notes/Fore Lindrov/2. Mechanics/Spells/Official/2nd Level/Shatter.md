> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a chip of mica)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

A loud noise erupts from a point of your choice within range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered there makes a CON saving throw, taking 3d8 Thunder damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A Construct has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on the save.

A nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried also takes the damage if it's in the spell's area.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
