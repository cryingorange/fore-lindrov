**Casting Time:** Action or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Ritual\|Ritual]]
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

For the duration, no sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] has Immunity to Thunder damage, and creatures have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Deafened\|Deafened]] condition while entirely inside it. Casting a spell that includes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Spell Components\|Verbal]] component is impossible there.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Cleric, #Ranger
