> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (a hot pepper)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You touch one willing creature, and choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. Until the spell ends, the target can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] action to exhale a 15-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cone\|Cone]]. Each creature in that area makes a DEX saving throw, taking 3d6 damage of the chosen type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
