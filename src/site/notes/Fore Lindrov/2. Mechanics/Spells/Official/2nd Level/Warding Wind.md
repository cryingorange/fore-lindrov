> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

A strong wind (20 miles per hour) blows around you in a 10-foot radius and moves with you, remaining centered on you. The wind lasts for the spell's duration.

The wind has the following effects:
- It [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Deafened\|Deafens]] you and other creatures in its area.
- It extinguishes unprotected flames in its area that are torch-sized or smaller.
- It hedges out vapor, gas, and fog that can be dispersed by strong wind.
- The area is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] for creatures other than you.
- The attack rolls of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Range\|Ranged]] weapon attacks have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] if the attacks pass in or out of the wind.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
