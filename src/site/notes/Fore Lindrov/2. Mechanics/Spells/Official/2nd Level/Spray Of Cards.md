> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self (15-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cone\|Cone]])
> **Components:** V, S, M (a deck of cards)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You spray a 15-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cone\|Cone]] of spectral cards. Each creature in that area must make a DEX saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d10 Force damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
