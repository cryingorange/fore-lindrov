> **Casting Time:** 10 minutes
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

Up to five creatures of your choice who remain within range for the spell's entire casting gain the benefits of a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]] and also regain 2d8 HP. A creature can't be affected by this spell again until that creature finishes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The healing increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** #Cleric, #Paladin
