> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** S, M (a coin), M (2 gp)
> **Duration:** 1 minute

When you cast the spell, you hurl the coin that is the spell's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Spell Components\|Material]] component to any spot within range. The coin lights up as if under the effect of a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light]] spell. Each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of the coin must succeed on a WIS saving throw or be distracted for the duration. While distracted, a creature has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on WIS (Perception) checks and initiative rolls.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
