> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You magically empower your movement with dance-like steps, giving yourself the following benefits for the duration:
- Your walking Speed increases by 10 feet.
- You don't provoke [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attacks]].
- You can move through the space of another creature, and it doesn't count as [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]. If you end your turn in another creature's space, you are shunted to the last unoccupied space you occupied, and you take 1d8 Force damage.

**Classes:** #Artificer, #Bard, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
