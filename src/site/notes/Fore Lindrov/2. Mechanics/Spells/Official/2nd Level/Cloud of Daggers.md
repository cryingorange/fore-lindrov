> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a sliver of glass)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You conjure spinning daggers in a 5-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] centered on a point within range. Each creature in that area takes 4d4 Slashing damage. A creature also takes this damage if it enters the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] or ends its turn there or if the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] moves into its space. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.

On your later turns, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] action to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] up to 30 feet.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 2.
