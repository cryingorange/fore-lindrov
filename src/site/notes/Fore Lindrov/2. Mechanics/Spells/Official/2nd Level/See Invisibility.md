> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of talc)
> **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, you see creatures and objects that have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane. Creatures and objects there appear ghostly.
