> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, M (bat fur and a piece of coal)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

For the duration, magical [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Darkness\|Darkness]] spreads from a point within range and fills a 15-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]]. [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]] can't see through it, and nonmagical light can't illuminate it.

Alternatively, you cast the spell on an object that isn't being worn or carried, causing the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Darkness\|Darkness]] to fill a 15-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from that object. Covering that object with something opaque, such as a bowl or helm, blocks the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Darkness\|Darkness]].

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] created by a spell of level 2 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Warlock, #Wizard
