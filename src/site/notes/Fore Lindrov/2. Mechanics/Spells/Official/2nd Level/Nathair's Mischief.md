> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** S, M (a piece of crust from an apple pie)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You fill a 20-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] you can see within range with fey and draconic magic. Roll on the Mischievous Surge table to determine the magical effect produced, and roll again at the start of each of your turns until the spell ends. You can move the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] up to 10 feet before you roll.

>[!info|bg-plain]+ DM's Note
> - The effect occurs when the spell is cast, as well as at the start of the Player's turn after the roll for the effect once the Player concludes the movement of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]].
