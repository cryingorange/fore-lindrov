> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (ashes from burned mistletoe)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You radiate a concealing aura in a 30-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to DEX (Stealth) checks and leave no tracks.
