>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  120 feet
>**Components:** V
>**Duration:** 1 minute

One creature that you can see within range must succeed on a CON saving throw, or it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Deafened\|Deafened]] condition (your choice) for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success. 

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2
