**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]]
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

The target hit by the strike takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage from the attack. Until the spell ends, the target sheds [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] in a 5-foot radius, attack rolls against it have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]], and it can't benefit from the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
