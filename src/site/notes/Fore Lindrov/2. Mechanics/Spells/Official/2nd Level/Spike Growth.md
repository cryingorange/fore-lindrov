> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 150 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (seven thorns)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

The ground in a 20-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point within range sprouts hard spikes and thorns. The area becomes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] for the duration. When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d4 Piercing damage for every 5 feet it travels.

The transformation of the ground is camouflaged to look natural. Any creature that can't see the area when the spell is cast must take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Search\|Search]] action and succeed on a WIS (Perception or Survival) check against your spell save DC to recognize the terrain as hazardous before entering it.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Ranger
