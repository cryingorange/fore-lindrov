> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (1 gp)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You create three twisting, whistling, hypoallergenic, gluten-free darts of magical force. Each dart targets a creature of your choice that you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack for each missile. On a hit, a missile deals 2d4 Force damage to its target.

If the attack roll scores a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]], the target of that missile takes 5d4 Force damage instead of you rolling damage twice for a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]]. If the attack roll for any missile is a 1, all missiles miss their targets and blow up in your face, dealing 1 Force damage per missile to you.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the spell creates one more dart, and the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Spell Components\|Material]] component increases by 1 gp, for each slot level above 1st.
