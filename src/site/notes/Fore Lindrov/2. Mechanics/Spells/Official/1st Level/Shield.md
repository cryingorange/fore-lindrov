**Casting Time:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]], which you take when you are hit by an attack roll or targeted by the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Magic Missile\|Magic Missile]] spell
