> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components**: V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

A creature you touch regains a number of HP equal to 2d8 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The healing increases by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
