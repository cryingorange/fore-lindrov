> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

A creature you touch makes a CON saving throw, taking 2d10 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.
