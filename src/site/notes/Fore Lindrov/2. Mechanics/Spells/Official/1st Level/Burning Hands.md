> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

A thin sheet of flames shoots forth from you. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cone\|Cone]] makes a DEX saving throw, taking 3d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Set Ablaze.** Flammable objects in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cone\|Cone]] that aren't being worn or carried start burning.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
