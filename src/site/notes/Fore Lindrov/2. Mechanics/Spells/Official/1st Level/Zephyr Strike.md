> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You move like the wind. Until the spell ends, your movement doesn't provoke [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attacks]].

Once before the spell ends, you can give yourself [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on one weapon attack roll on your turn. That attack deals an extra 1d8 Force damage on a hit. Whether you hit or miss, your walking Speed increases by 30 feet until the end of that turn.
