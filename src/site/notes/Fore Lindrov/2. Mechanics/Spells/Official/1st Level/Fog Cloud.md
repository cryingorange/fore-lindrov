> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] of fog centered on a point within range. The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by[[ Gust of Wind\| Gust of Wind]]) disperses it.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The fog's radius increases by 20 feet for each spell slot level above 1.
