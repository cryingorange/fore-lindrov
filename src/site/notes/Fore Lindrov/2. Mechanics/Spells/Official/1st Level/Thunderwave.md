> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a wave of thunderous energy. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] originating from you makes a CON saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 Thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

In addition, unsecured objects that are entirely within the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] are pushed 10 feet away from you, and a thunderous boom is audible within 300 feet.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
