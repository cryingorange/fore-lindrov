**Casting Time:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]], which you take in response to taking damage from a creature that you can see within 60 feet of yourself
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

The creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by green flames. It makes a DEX y saving throw, taking 2d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.
