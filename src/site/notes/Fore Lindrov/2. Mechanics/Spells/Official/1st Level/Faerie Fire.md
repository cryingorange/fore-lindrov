> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Objects in a 20-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] within range are outlined in blue, green, or violet light (your choice). Each creature in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] is also outlined if it fails a DEX saving throw. For the duration, objects and affected creatures shed Dim Light in a 10-foot radius and can't benefit from the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition.

Attack rolls against an affected creature or object have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] if the attacker can see it.
