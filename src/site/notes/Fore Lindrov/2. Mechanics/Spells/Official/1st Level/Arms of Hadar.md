>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  Self
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous

Invoking Hadar, you cause tendrils to erupt from yourself. Each creature in a 10-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from you makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 2d6 Necrotic damage and can't take [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reactions]] until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
