>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  30 feet
>**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of blood)
>**Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Up to three creatures of your choice that you can see within range must each make a CHA saving throw. Whenever a target that fails this save makes an attack roll or a saving throw before the spell ends, the target must subtract ~={blue}1d4=~ from the attack roll or save.

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
