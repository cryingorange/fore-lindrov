> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** 90 feet
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You magically mark one creature you can see within range as your quarry. Until the spell ends, you deal an extra 1d6 Force damage to the target whenever you hit it with an attack roll. You also have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on any WIS (Perception or Survival) check you make to find it.

If the target drops to 0 HP before this spell ends, you can take a Bonus Action to move the mark to a new creature you can see within range.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** Your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] can last longer with a spell slot of level 3-4 (up to 8 hours) or 5+ (up to 24 hours).
