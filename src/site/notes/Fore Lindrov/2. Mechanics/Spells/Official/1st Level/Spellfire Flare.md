> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 60 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a blast of brilliant fire. Make a ranged spell attack against a target within range; a target gains no benefit from [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Half Cover]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Three-Quarters Cover]] for this attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d10 Radiant damage.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You create an additional blast for each spell slot level above 1. You can direct the blasts at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each blast.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
