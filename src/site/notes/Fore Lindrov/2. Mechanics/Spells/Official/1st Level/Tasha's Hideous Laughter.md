> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 30 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (a tart and a feather)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

One creature of your choice that you can see within range makes a WIS saving throw. On a failed save, it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] conditions for the duration. During that time, it laughs uncontrollably if it's capable of laughter, and it can't end the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition on itself.

At the end of each of its turns and each time it takes damage, it makes another WIS saving throw. The target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on the save if the save is triggered by damage. On a successful save, the spell ends.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Warlock, #Wizard
