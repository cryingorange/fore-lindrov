**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Round\|Round]]

**Sphere.** The gravity in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point you can see within range increases for a moment. Each creature in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] on the turn when you cast the spell must make a CON saving throw. 

**Failed CON Save.** On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 Force damage, and its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] is halved until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and suffers no reduction to its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]].

Until the start of your next turn, any object that isn't being worn or carried in the sphere requires a successful STR (Athletics) check against your spell save DC to pick up or move.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
