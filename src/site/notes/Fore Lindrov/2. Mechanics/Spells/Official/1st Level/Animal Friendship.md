>**Casting Time:** Action
>**Range:**  30 feet
>**Components:** V, S, M (a morsel of food)
>**Duration:** 24 hours

Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spells ends.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Druid, #Ranger
