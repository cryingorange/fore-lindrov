> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a weapon
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

As you hit the target, grasping vines appear on it, and it makes a STR saving throw. A Large or larger creature has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on this save. On a failed save, the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition until the spell ends. On a successful save, the vines shrivel away, and the spell ends.

While [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]], the target takes 1d6 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an action to make a STR (Athletics) check against your spell save DC. On a success, the spell ends.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** #Ranger
