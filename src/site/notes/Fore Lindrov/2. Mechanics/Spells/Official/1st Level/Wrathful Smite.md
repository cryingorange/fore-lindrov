**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]]
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** 1 minute

The target takes an extra 1d6 Necrotic damage from the attack, and it must succeed on a WIS saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition until the spell ends. At the end of each of its turns, the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
