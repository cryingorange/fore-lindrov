> **Casting Time:** 8 hours
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S, M (an agate worth 1,000+ GP, which the spell consumes)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You spend the casting time tracing magical pathways within a precious gemstone, and then touch the target. The target must be either a Beast or Plant creature with an INT of 3 or less or a natural plant that isn't a creature. The target gains an INT of 10 and the ability to speak one language you know. If the target is a natural plant, it becomes a Plant creature and gains the ability to move its limbs, roots, vines, creepers, and so forth, and it gains senses similar to a human's. The DM chooses statistics appropriate for the awakened Plant, such as the statistics for the [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Plant/Awakened Shrub\|Awakened Shrub]] or [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Plant/Awakened Tree\|Awakened Tree]] in the Monster Manual.

The awakened target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] condition for 30 days or until you or your allies deal damage to it. When that condition ends, the awakened creature chooses its attitude toward you.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Druid
