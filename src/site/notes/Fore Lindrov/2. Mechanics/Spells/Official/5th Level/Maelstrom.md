> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S, M (paper or leaf in the shape of a funnel)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

A swirling mass of 5-foot-deep water appears in a 30-foot radius centered on a point you can see within range. The point must be on the ground or in a body of water. Until the spell ends, that area is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]], and any creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a STR saving throw or take 6d6 Bludgeoning damage and be pulled 10 feet toward the center.
