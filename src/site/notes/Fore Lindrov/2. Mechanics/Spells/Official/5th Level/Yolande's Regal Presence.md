> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature tiara)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You surround yourself with unearthly majesty in a 10-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]]. Whenever the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] enters the space of a creature you can see and whenever a creature you can see enters the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] or ends its turn there, you can force that creature to make a WIS saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 4d6 Psychic damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition, and you can push it up to 10 feet away. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Wizard
