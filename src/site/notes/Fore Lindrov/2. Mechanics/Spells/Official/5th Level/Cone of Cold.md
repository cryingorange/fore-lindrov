> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a small crystal or glass cone)
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a blast of cold air. Each creature in a 60-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cone\|Cone]] originating from you makes a CON saving throw, taking 8d8 Cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Sorcerer, #Wizard
