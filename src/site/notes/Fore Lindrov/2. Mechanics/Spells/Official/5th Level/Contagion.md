> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** 7 days

Your touch inflicts a magical contagion. The target must succeed on a CON saving throw or take 11d8 Necrotic damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition. Also, choose one ability when you cast the spell. While [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]], the target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on saving throws made with the chosen ability.

The target must repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns until it gets three successes or failures. If the target succeeds on three of these saves, the spell ends on the target. If the target fails three of the saves, the spell lasts for 7 days on it.

Whenever the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] target receives an effect that would end the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition, the target must succeed on a CON saving throw, or the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition doesn't end on it.

**Classes:** #Cleric, #Druid
