> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a scrap of sailcloth)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

Wind wraps around your body, tugging at your hair and clothing as your feet lift off the ground. You gain a Fly Speed of 60 feet. Additionally, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on ability checks to avoid being [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]], and on saving throws against being [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]].

When you are targeted by a spell or attack while this spell is in effect, you can use a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. If this movement takes you out of range of the triggering spell or attack, you are unaffected by it. This spell then ends when you reappear.

**Classes:** #Druid, #Ranger, #Sorcerer
