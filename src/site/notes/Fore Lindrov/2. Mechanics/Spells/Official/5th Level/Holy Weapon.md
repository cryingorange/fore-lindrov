> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
> **Range:** Touch
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You imbue a weapon you touch with holy power. Until the spell ends, the weapon emits bright light in a 30-foot radius and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. In addition, weapon attacks made with it deal an extra 2d8 Radiant damage on a hit. If the weapon isn't already a magic weapon, it becomes one for the duration.

As a Bonus Action on your turn, you can dismiss this spell and cause the weapon to emit a burst of radiance. Each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of the weapon must make a CON saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d8 Radiant damage, and it is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] for 1 minute. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]]. At the end of each of its turns, a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] creature can make a CON saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Classes:** #Cleric, #Paladin
