>**Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** 120 feet
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] of yellow-green fog centered on a point within range. The fog lasts for the duration or until strong wind (such as the one created by Gust of Wind) disperses it, ending the spell. Its area is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]].

Each creature in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] makes a CON saving throw, taking 5d8 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature must also make this save when the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] moves into its space and when it enters the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] moves 10 feet away from you at the start of each of your turns.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.

**Classes:** #Sorcerer, #Wizard
