> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

Until the spell ends, your veins bulge and become visibly green beneath your skin, your eyes weep constantly with liquid poison, and you gain the following benefits:
- You are Immune to Poison damage and the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition.
- You can know if creatures you can see within 30 feet of you have Resistance or are Immune to Poison damage or the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition.
- As a Bonus Action, choose a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. That creature makes a CON saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the creature loses its Resistance or Immunity to Poison damage or its Immunity to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition until the end of your next turn.
- As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, venom blasts from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in a 15-foot-Long, 5-foot-Wide [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]] must make a CON saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 4d6 Poison damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.
