> **Casting Time:** Action
> **Range:** Self
> **Components:** V, S, M (a moonstone worth 50+ GP)
> **Duration:** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 minute

For the duration, moonlight fills a 20-foot [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from you with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]]. While in that area, you and your allies have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Half Cover]] and Resistance to Cold, Lightning, and Radiant damage.

While the spell lasts, you can use one of the following options, ending the spell immediately:
- **Liberation.** When you fail a saving throw to avoid or end the Frightened, Grappled, or Restrained condition, you can take a Reaction to succeed on the save instead.
- **Respite.** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, you or an ally within the area regains HP equal to 4d10 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

**Classes:** #Bard, #Druid, #Ranger, #Wizard
