**Exhaustion Levels.** This condition is cumulative. Each time you receive it, you gain 1 Exhaustion level. You die if your Exhaustion level is 6.

**D20 Tests Affected.** When you make a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Test]] the roll is reduced by 2 x your Exhaustion level.

**Speed Reduced.** Your Speed is reduced by a number of feet equal to 5 x your Exhaustion level.

**Removing Exhaustion Levels.** Finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] removes 1 of your Exhaustion levels. When your Exhaustion level reaches 0, the condition ends.
