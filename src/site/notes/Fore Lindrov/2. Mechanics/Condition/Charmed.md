**Can't Harm the Charmer.** You can't attack the charmer or target the charmer with damaging abilities or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Magical Effects\|Magical Effects]].

**Social Advantage.** The charmer has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on any ability check to interact with you socially.
