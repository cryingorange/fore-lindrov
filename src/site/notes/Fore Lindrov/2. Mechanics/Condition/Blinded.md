**Can't See.** You can't see and automatically fail any ability check that requires sight.

**Attacks Affected.** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] rolls against you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]], and your attack rolls have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]].
