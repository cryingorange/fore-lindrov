**Ability Checks and Attacks Affected.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of fear is within line of sight.

**Can't Approach.** You can't willingly move closer to the source of fear.
