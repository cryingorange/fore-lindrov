**Contamination Levels.** This condition is cumulative. Each time you receive it, you gain 1 Contamination level. You [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Monstrous Transformation\|Transform]] if your Contamination level is 6.

**Saving Throws Affected.** If you are reduced to 0 HP with Contamination levels, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Death Saving Throw\|Death Saving Throws]].

**Dying.** If you die with Contamination Levels, it is treated as if you died from having 6 levels of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]].

**Finishing a Long Rest or 24 Hours.** If a creature with levels of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]] finishes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]], or it has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]] levels for 24 hours, it must succeed on a DC (5 x Contamination Levels, Minimum 10) CON Saving Throw or gain one level of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]].
