Some effects cause a creature to be possessed by another creature or entity. A possessing effect defines how the possession operates. Possession can be prevented by the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Protection from Evil and Good\|Protection from Evil and Good]] spell and ended by the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Dispel Evil and Good\|Dispel Evil and Good]] spell.
