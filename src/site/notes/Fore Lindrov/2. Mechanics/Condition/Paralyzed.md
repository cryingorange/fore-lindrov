**Incapacitated.** You have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition.

**Speed 0.** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

**Saving Throws Affected.** You automatically fail STR and DEX saving throws.

**Attacks Affected.** Attack rolls against you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]].

**Automatic Critical Hits.** Any attack roll that hits you is a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.
