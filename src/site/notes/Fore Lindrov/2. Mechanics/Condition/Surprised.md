If a creature is caught unawares by the start of combat, that creature is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Surprised\|Surprised]].

A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Surprised\|Surprised]] creature can't move or take an Action on your first turn of the combat, and can't take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] until that turn ends. A member of a group can be surprised even if the other members aren't.
