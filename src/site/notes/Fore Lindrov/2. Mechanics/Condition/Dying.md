>**Reduced to 0 HP.** If you reach 0 HP and don't die instantly, you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Dying\|Dying]] condition until you regain any HP, and you now face making [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Death Saving Throw\|Death Saving Throws]].

**Knocked Down.** You have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition and cannot end it unless you regain at least 1 HP, and if you were [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentrating]], you make the Concentration Check when you gained this condition with Disadvantage. 

**Ability Checks and Saving Throws Affected.** You have Disadvantage on STR and DEX ability checks and saving throws.

**Turn Options.** During your turn, you can only move and take one of the following options which cause you to gain levels of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]].
1. Action (Gain 2 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Levels)
2. Bonus Action (Gain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Level)
3. Reaction (Gain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Level)

**Free Object Interaction.** If you want to make a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Free Object Interaction\|Free Object Interaction]], you must succeed on a DC 15 CON Saving Throw, or fail to do so.

**Stabilised.** When you become [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Stable\|Stable]], you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition and gain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Level. A creature can remove the Exhaustion levels it gained from becoming [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Stable\|Stable]] by finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].
