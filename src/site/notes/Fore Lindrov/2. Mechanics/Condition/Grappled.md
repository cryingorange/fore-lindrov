**Speed 0.** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

**Attacks Affected.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against any target other than the grappler.

**Movable.** The grappler can drag or carry you when it moves, but every foot of movement costs it 1 extra foot unless you are Tiny or two or more sizes smaller than it.

**Two Grapplers.** If two creatures are trying to give the same target the Grappled condition, the two grapplers make a STR (Athletics) contest to determine who imposes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] condition.
