**Speed 0.** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

**Attacks Affected.** Attack rolls against you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]], and your attack rolls have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]].

**Ability Checks and Saving Throws Affected.** You have Disadvantage on DEX ability checks and saving throws.

**No Somatic Components.** You cannot cast spells that require Somatic Components.
