**Turned to Inanimate Substance.** You are transformed, along with any nonmagical objects you are wearing and carrying, into a solid inanimate substance (usually stone). Your weight increases by a factor of ten, and you cease aging.

**Incapacitated**. You have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition.

**Speed 0.** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.

**Attacks Affected.** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] rolls against you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]].

**Saving Throws Affected.** You automatically fail STR and DEX saving throws.

**Resist Damage.** You have Resistance to all damage.

**Poison Immunity.** You have Immunity to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition.
