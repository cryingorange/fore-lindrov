# Overview
Madness can be Short-Term, Long-Term, or Indefinite. More mundane effects, like the ones presented in the guidelines below, impose Short-Term Madness, which lasts for just a few minutes. 

More horrific effects or cumulative effects can result in Long-Term or Indefinite Madness. A creature’s intellect can help it understand eldritch knowledge without breaking its mind and a creature can add its INT modifier to saving throws it makes against Madness. 

<br>

# Gaining Madness & Duration
- **Stunned Condition.** When a creature becomes afflicted by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Madness\|Madness]], be it [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Short-Term Madness Table\|Short-Term]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Long-Term Madness Table\|Long-Term]], or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Indefinite Madness Table\|Indefinite]], the creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition until the start of its next turn, and then the Madness takes effect.

- **Short-Term Madness Duration.** A creature afflicted with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Short-Term Madness Table\|Short-Term]] is subjected to an effect from the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Short-Term Madness Table\|Short-Term Madness Table]] for 1d4 minutes. 

- **Long-Term Madness Duration.** A creature afflicted with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Long-Term Madness Table\|Long-Term]] is subjected to an effect from the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Long-Term Madness Table\|Long-Term Madness Table]] for 1d4 × Eldritch Moons (or 1d4 x 10 days if you aren’t using Eldritch Moons in your world).

# Curing Madness
> **Cure Tiers.** Cures for madness work top-down: whatever cures a higher tier of madness automatically cures all lower tiers, but lower-tier cures cannot cure higher tiers.

## Permanent Cures 
- **Short-Term Madness:** The creature is subjected to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]] spell.
- **Long-Term Madness:** The creature is subjected to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Greater Restoration\|Greater Restoration]] spell or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Modify Memory\|Modify Memory]] is used to remove the memory of the traumatic event.
- **Indefinite Madness:** The creature is cured with a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/Wish\|Wish]] spell, or killed and brought back to life with a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/True Resurrection\|True Resurrection]] spell.

## Temporary Cures
- **Short-Term Madness:** The creature is subjected [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Calm Emotions\|Calm Emotions]] spell or has a Momentum Die (Jaegar Class).
- **Long-Term Madness:** The creature is subjected to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/8th Level/Mind Blank\|Mind Blank]] spell or is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness#3. Inebriated\|Inebriated]].
- **Indefinite Madness:** The creature is in the area of a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Hallow\|Hallow]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/8th Level/Holy Aura\|Holy Aura]] spell, or a location that wards off Eldritch Influences.
