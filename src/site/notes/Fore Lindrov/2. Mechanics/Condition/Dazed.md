**Only One Option.** You can only Move, use an Action, or use a Bonus Action on your turn. 

**Hasted.** If you are affected by a spell or effect that gives you an extra Action on your turn (such as the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Haste\|Haste]] spell or the fighter's Action Surge feature), you can still take this extra Action, in addition to the Movement, Action, or Bonus Action from [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Dazed\|Dazed]] condition.
