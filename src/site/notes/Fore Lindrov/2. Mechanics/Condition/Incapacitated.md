**Inactive.** You can't take any Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction.

**No Concentration.** Your Concentration is broken.

**Speechless.** You can't speak.

**Surprised.** If you're Incapacitated when you roll Initiative, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on the roll.
