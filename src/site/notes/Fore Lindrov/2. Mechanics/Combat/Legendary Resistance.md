## Monster
When a Monster uses its Legendary Resistance, it loses HP equal to half its Hit Point Die.

## Player
When a Player Character uses its Legendary Resistance, it must succeed on a DC 15 CON saving throw or gain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level. A creature can remove the Exhaustion levels it gained from using its Legendary Resistance by finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].
