## a. Impeded Weapons
When making a melee attack roll with a weapon underwater, a creature that lacks a Swim Speed has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on the attack roll unless the weapon deals Piercing damage.

A ranged attack roll with a weapon underwater automatically misses a target beyond the weapon's normal range, and the attack roll has Disadvantage against a target within normal range.

## b. Fire Resistance
Anything underwater has Resistance to Fire damage.
