# Base
When you hit with a ranged attack roll using a weapon that has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Thrown\|Thrown]] property, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage roll.

# Lesser
When you roll a 1 or a 2 on an attack roll made using a weapon that qualifies for this style’s extra damage, you can reroll the d20 and keep the new result.

# Greater
The +2 bonus to damage rolls becomes equal to your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Proficiency Bonus Table\|Proficiency Bonus]] instead.
