# Base

While you're wearing Light, Medium, or Heavy armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

# Lesser Advancement

The Armor Class bonus increases to +2.

# Greater Advancement

While you're wearing Light, Medium, or Heavy armor, Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage you receive is reduced by an amount equal to your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Proficiency Bonus Table\|Proficiency Bonus]].
