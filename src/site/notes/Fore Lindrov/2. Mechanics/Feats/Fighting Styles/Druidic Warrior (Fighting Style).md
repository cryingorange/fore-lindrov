# Base

You learn two Druid cantrips of your choice. [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Guidance\|Guidance]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Starry Wisp\|Starry Wisp]] are recommended. 

The chosen cantrips count as Ranger spells for you, and WIS is your spellcasting ability for them. Whenever you gain a Ranger level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another Druid cantrip.

# Lesser Advancement

You learn one additional Druid cantrip of your choice. Additionally, choose one of your known Druid cantrips, you can add your spellcasting ability modifier to that spell's damage rolls.

# Greater Advancement

Choose one level 1 spell from the Druid or Ranger spell list. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast this spell a number of times per [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] equal to your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Proficiency Bonus Table\|Proficiency Bonus]], requiring no [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Spell Components\|Material]] components.

You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
