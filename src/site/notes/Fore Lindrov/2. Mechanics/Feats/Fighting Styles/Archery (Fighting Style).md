# Base

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with Ranged weapons.

# Lesser Advancement

You gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls you make with Ranged weapons.

# Greater Advancement

Once per turn, you can ignore [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Half Cover]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Three-Quarters Cover]], and gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on the attack roll.
