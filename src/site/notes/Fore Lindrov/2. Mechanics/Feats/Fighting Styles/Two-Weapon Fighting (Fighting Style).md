# Base
When you make an extra attack as a result of using a weapon that has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light]] property, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of that attack if you aren't already adding it to the damage.

# Lesser
Attack rolls made using your off-hand weapon gain a +2 bonus.

# Greater
Attacks made using your off-hand weapon have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] if your primary [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Two-Weapon Fighting\|weapon attack]] that preceded it missed.
