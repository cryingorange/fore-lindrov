# Base

When a creature you can see hits another creature within 5 feet of you with an attack roll, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to reduce the damage dealt to the target by 1d10 plus your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Proficiency Bonus Table\|Proficiency Bonus]]. You must be holding a Shield or a Simple or Martial weapon to use this [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]].

# Lesser Advancement

If you reduce the damage to 0, you can make one attack against the attacker if they are within your weapon's range.


# Greater Advancement

If the damage is not reduced to 0, you can choose to redirect the remaining damage to yourself, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] against this instance of damage.
