# Base

When you hit with your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]] and deal damage, you can deal Bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 plus your STR modifier instead of the normal damage of an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]]. 

If you aren't holding any weapons or a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Armor/Shield\|Shield]] when you make the attack roll, the d6 becomes a d8.

At the start of each of your turns, you can deal 1d4 Bludgeoning damage to one creature [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] by you.

# Lesser Advancement

Your Unarmed Strikes count as weapons with the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Finesse\|Finesse]] property, allowing you to enchant them as if they were weapons.

# Greater Advancement

The damage of your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]] increases to Bludgeoning damage equal to 1d8 plus your STR modifier instead of the normal damage of an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]] or 1d10 plus your STR modifier if you aren't holding any weapons or a Shield.

Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strikes]].
