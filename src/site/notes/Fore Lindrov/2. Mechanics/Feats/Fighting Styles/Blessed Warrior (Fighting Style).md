# Base

You learn two Cleric cantrips of your choice. [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Guidance\|Guidance]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Sacred Flame\|Sacred Flame]] are recommended. 

The chosen cantrips count as Paladin spells for you, and CHA is your spellcasting ability for them. Whenever you gain a Paladin level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another Cleric cantrip.

# Lesser Advancement

You learn one additional Cleric cantrip of your choice. Additionally, choose one of your known Cleric cantrips, you can add your spellcasting ability modifier to that spell's damage rolls.

# Greater Advancement

Choose one level 1 spell from the Cleric or Paladin spell list. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast this spell a number of times per [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] equal to your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Proficiency Bonus Table\|Proficiency Bonus]], requiring no [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Spell Components\|Material]] components.

You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
