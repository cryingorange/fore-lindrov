# Base

When you roll damage for an attack you make with a Melee weapon that you are holding with two hands, you can treat any 1 or 2 on a damage die as a 3. The weapon must have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Two-Handed\|Two-Handed]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Versatile\|Versatile]] property to gain this benefit.

# Lesser Advancement

You gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls made with a Melee weapon that you are holding with two hands. 

The weapon must have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Two-Handed\|Two-Handed]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Versatile\|Versatile]] property to gain this benefit.


# Greater Advancement

When you roll damage for an attack you make with a Melee weapon that you are holding with two hands, you can reroll any 1 or 2 on a damage die, taking the result only if it is higher than a 3.
