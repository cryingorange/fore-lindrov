# Base

When you're holding a Melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.

# Lesser Advancement

When you roll a 1 or 2 on an attack roll made using a Melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you can reroll the attack roll. You must use the new result.


# Greater Advancement

The +2 bonus to damage rolls becomes equal to your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Proficiency Bonus Table\|Proficiency Bonus]] instead.
