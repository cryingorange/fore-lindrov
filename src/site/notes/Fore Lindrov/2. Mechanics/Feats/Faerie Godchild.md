The protection of your faerie guardian is often unpredictable. As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] when you are targeted by an attack or when you roll a saving throw against a harmful spell or effect, you can invoke your guardians protection. Each time this protection is invoked, roll on the table below to determine the form this protection takes; You may use this feature a number of times equal to half your Proficiency Bonus (rounded down), regaining all expended uses after a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]]:


| D4  | Effect |
|:---:|:------:|
|  1  | **Flying Flurry.** You gain a Fly speed equal to your walking Speed as birds or insects swarm around you until the end of your next turn, and you can immediately move up to half your Speed as part of this Reaction.        |
|  2  | **Obscure From Sight.** You have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition until the end of your next turn.       |
|  3  | **Guardian Fyre.** Glittering sparkles fall in a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on you. All creatures and objects within the area, except for you, fall under the effects of the *[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Faerie Fire\|Faerie Fire]]* spell until the end of your next turn.       |
|  4  | **Stoneheart.** You gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] equal to five times your level, and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Petrified\|Petrified]] condition. While [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Petrified\|Petrified]] in this way you cannot be targeted by spells or effects that cannot target objects and your AC becomes 17 if it isn't already higher. These effects last until the start of your next turn.      |
