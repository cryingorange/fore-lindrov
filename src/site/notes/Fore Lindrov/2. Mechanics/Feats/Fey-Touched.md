Your exposure to the Feywild's magic grants you the following benefits:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your INT, WIS or CHA by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Fey Magic.** Choose one level 1 spell from the Divination or Enchantment school of magic. You always have that spell and the *[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Misty Step\|Misty Step]]* spell prepared. 

You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either spell in this way, you can't cast that spell in this way again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]]. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells' spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
