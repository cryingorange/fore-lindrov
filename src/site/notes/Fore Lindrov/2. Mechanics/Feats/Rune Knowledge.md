You gain access to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Rune Magic\|Rune Magic]], granting you the following properties:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your INT, WIS, or CHA by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Learn Rune.** You know two Runes of your choice, chosen from the Runes Table and are granted the Benefits of each [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Rune Magic\|Rune]] you know.

**Rune Casting.** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, you can invoke the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Rune Magic\|Powers]] of any Runes you know. The Runes' invocation ability is the ability increased by this feat.

**Repeatable.** You can take this feat more than once.
