**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your CON or CHA by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Damage Resistance.** When you gain this feat, choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to the chosen damage type.

**Fearsome Power.** When you deal damage to a creature as part of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action on your turn, you can use the Dragon's Terror benefit of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Feats/Cult of the Dragon Initiate\|Cult of the Dragon Initiate]] Feat as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] this turn.
