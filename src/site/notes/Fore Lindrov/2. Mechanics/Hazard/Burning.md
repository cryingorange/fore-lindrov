A burning creature or object takes 1d4 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. As an Action, you can extinguish fire on yourself by giving yourself the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated.
