# Creature Effects

- **Perception Checks Affected.** All creatures under the Moon's Light have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on Perception Checks.
	- Passive Perception Scores gain a bonus of +5
- **Halved Magical Healing.** All creatures under the Moon's Light recover half as much [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] from [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Magical Effects\|Magical]] Healing (minimum 1).

<br>

# Travel Effects

## Lunar Shower

- **Roll a D4.** Fragments of the moon frequently fall down to the world. For each hour that passes, roll a d4. On a roll of 1, a Lunar Shower that lasts for 1d6 × 10 minutes begins. 

- **Effects.** During the lunar shower, a rain of ephemeral light falls upon the world, although it looks like solid fragments the shards are weightless figments of light.
	- This rain dispels all spells of 5th level or lower upon contact.
	- Ends [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]].
	- Halves the range of all [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Magical Effects\|magic]] (minimum 5 feet)

**Passive Perception.** Creatures with a passive Perception of 15 or higher can see these fragments falling towards them 1 minute before the lunar shower begins.

<br>

# Madness Effects

## Short-Term Madness

- **Short-Term Madness.** When a character gains a Short-Term Madness under the Shattered Moon, the moon tries to repair itself by eating part of the character’s life.
	- For the duration of the Madness, the character takes 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage at the start of each of their turns. 
	- Any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Magical Effects\|magical]] healing drives the moon's influence away and stops the damage from recurring.

<br>

## Long-Term Madness

- **Long-Term Madness.** Whenever a character gains a long-term madness under the Shattered Moon, one of the creature's limbs shatters like the moon. 
	- The character takes 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage and must roll a d4 to determine which limb breaks, using the following table:



| D4  | Limb                                                                                                                                                                | D4  | Limb                                                                                                                                                                 |
| :---: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | **Right Arm:** You can no longer hold anything with two hands, and you can hold only a single object at a time.                                                         | 3   | **Left Arm:** You can no longer hold anything with two hands, and you can hold only a single object at a time.                                                         |
| 2   | **Right Leg:** Your base walking speed is reduced to 5 feet. If you still have at least one leg, you can use a crutch or cane to move at half your original base speed. | 4   | **Left Leg:** Your base walking speed is reduced to 5 feet. If you still have at least one leg, you can use a crutch or cane to move at half your original base speed. |

<br>

## Indefinite Madness
- **Indefinite Madness.** A character that gains an Indefinite Madness under the Shattered Moon becomes its prey, a meal to invigorate it.


- **33 and Below.** The player must roll a d100; on a result of 33 or lower, the moon's powers fail before devouring the character, in fact invigorating them.
	- The character is cured of all madness effects afflicting them, and they fall under the effects of the Enlarge Spell for 24 Hours.
- **34 and Above.** The character's body is consumed by the moon as it attempts to use their life to repair itself.
	- The creature takes 75 (10d6 + 40) Necrotic Damage. 
	- If this damage reduces the character to 0 HP, they disintegrate into particles of lights that float up to the Moon.
