**Disadvantage.** Any creatures in the Haze have Disadvantage on saving throws against [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]]. 

**Constitution Saving Throw.** Non-Undead and Construct creatures that finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] or spend 24 hours in the Contamination Haze must succeed on a DC 20 CON Saving Throw or gain one level of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Contamination\|Contamination]]. 

**Food & Drinks Spoilt.** All exposed Food and Drink spoils after 24 hours in the Contamination Haze.

**Corpses Affected.** Any corpses in the Contamination Haze rises as a [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Monstrosity/The Consumption/Consumption Combat Form (Medium)\|Consumption Combat Form (Medium)]] after 24 hours in the Haze. 

**Outside Teleportation.** Any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleportation]] spell cast by a creature outside the Contamination Haze targeting a creature or location within the haze automatically fails and the spell slot is wasted.

**Extradimensional Travel.** Any creature in the Haze is unable to use any method of extradimensional movement, including [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleportation]] or travel to a different plane of existence. They don't prevent the creature from passing through an interdimensional portal.
