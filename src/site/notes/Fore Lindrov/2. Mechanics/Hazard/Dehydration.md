A creature requires an amount of water per day based on its size, as shown in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Dehydration#Water Needs per Day\|#Water Needs per Day]] table. A creature that drinks less than half the required water for a day gains 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level at the day's end. 

**Remove Exhaustion.** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] caused by dehydration can't be removed until the creature drinks the full amount of water required for a day.

# Water Needs per Day

| Size   | Water      | Size       | Water |
| ------ | ---------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Tiny   | 1/4 Gallon | Large      | 4 Gallons      |
| Small  | 1 Gallon   | Huge       | 16 Gallons      |
| Medium | 1 Gallon   | Gargantuan | 64 Gallons      |
