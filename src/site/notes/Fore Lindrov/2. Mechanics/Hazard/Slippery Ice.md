Slippery ice is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]. A creature that moves onto slippery ice for the first time on a turn, or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 10 DEX saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.
