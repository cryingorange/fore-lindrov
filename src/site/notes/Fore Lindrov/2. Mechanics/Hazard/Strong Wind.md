# Overview
**Ranged Attacks & Fog/Flame.** Strong wind imposes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on ranged attack rolls with weapons. It also extinguishes open flames and disperses fog. 

**Difficult Terrain & Flying.** The area in the air is considered [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]], and a flying creature in a strong wind must land at the end of its turn or fall.

**Speed.** Typically a Strong Wind moves at 20 miles per hour, or 30 feet per round.

**Vision.** A strong wind in a desert can create a sandstorm that imposes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on WIS (Perception) checks.
