A creature can takes damage from lava when it comes into contact with it for the first time or when it starts its turn still being in contact with the lava.


| Situation                                                   | Damage            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | :-----------------: |
| Small body part such as a hand or foot is submerged in Lava | 3d10 Fire Damage  |
| Large body part such as an arm or leg is submerged in Lava  | 6d10 Fire Damage  |
| Half Body Submerged in Lava / Wading through Lava           | 10d10 Fire Damage |
| Fully Submerged in Lava                                     | 18d10 Fire Damage                  |
