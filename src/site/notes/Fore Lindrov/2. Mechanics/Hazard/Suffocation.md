A creature can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to 1 +  its CON modifier (minimum of 30 seconds) before suffocation begins. 

When a creature runs out of breath or is choking, it gains 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Level at the end of each of its turns. When a creature can breathe again, it removes all levels of Exhaustion it gained from suffocating.
