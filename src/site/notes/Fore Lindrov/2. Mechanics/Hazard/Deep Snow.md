In Deep Snow, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (2 extra feet in Difficult Terrain). You ignore this extra cost if you have a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Burrow Speed\|Burrow Speed]] and use it to burrow or have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Cold damage.
