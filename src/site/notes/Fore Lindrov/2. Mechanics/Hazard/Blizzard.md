# Overview
Bone-chilling blizzards drive a wall of wind and snow like a living glacier.  Blizzards typically lasts 2d10 hours.

# Effects
## Snowstorm

**Extreme Cold.** The storm projects [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]]. 

**Obscuration.** Due to the howling wind and dense blue-white ice particles, the area in the storm is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]]. 

**Immunity.** Creatures that are [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immune]] to Cold damage can see normally within it.

## Strong Wind
**Strong Wind.** Ranged Attack Rolls with weapons have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] and exposed flames in the area are doused. 

**Difficult Terrain & Flying Creature.** The area in the air is considered [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]], and a flying creature in the area must land at the end of its turn or fall.

**Speed.** Typically a Strong Wind moves at 20 miles per hour, or 30 feet per round.

## Howling Wind
**Deafening.** The winds can reach incredible pitch and volume, making it impossible to hear. Creatures in the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Blizzard\|Blizzard]] have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Deafened\|Deafened]] condition.

**Communication.** A creature that wants to communicate with another creature in a Blizzard must take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Influence\|Influence]] Action on their turn or use a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to make a DC 15 Athletics (CON) Check to shout at the creature.
