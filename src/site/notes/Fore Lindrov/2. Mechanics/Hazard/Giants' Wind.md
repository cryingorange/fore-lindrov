A Flying Creature that starts its turn or enters for the first time on a turn in a space with Giants' Wind must succeed on a DC 15 STR or DEX Saving Throw (creature's choice) or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition. 

A flying creature in a Giants' Wind must also land at the end of its turn or fall.
