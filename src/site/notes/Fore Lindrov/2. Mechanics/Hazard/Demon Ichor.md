# Overview

Demon ichor is what remains behind after a demon dies or the tarry substance left behind when it is sent back to The Abyss. It's a reduction of demonic blood, viscera, and bodily fluids with the consistency and odor of bile.

# Effects

- **CON Saving Throw.** When a creature comes in direct contact with demon ichor or enters an area with it on the first time on a turn or starts its turn in the area, it must make a DC (10 + 1d12) CON Saving Throw. 
	- Fiends automatically succeed on the saving throw. 
- **Cursed.** On a failed save, the target becomes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Cursed\|Cursed]], and has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on Saving Throws made against Demons.
	- In addition, a non-Demon, Ooze, Plant or Undead creature is magically warped by the ichor, as determined by rolling on the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Demon Ichor#Flesh Warping Table\|#Flesh Warping Table]] while [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Cursed\|Cursed]].
