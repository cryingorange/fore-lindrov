**Difficult Terrain.** Whirlpools are considered [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]]. 

**STR Saving Throw.** A creature that moves into a Whirlpool for the first time on a turn or starts its there must succeed on a DC 15 STR Saving Throw.

Creatures without Swim Speed have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on this Saving Throw.
Creatures with Swim Speed can use DEX instead of STR.

**Failure.** On a failure, the target takes 9 (2d8) Bludgeoning damage, has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition.

A creature with the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition can end the condition by taking an Action to make a DC 15 STR (Athletics) Check.

**Athletics Check.** In order to move in a Whirlpool, a creature without Swim Speed must succeed on a DC 15 STR (Athletics) Check to do so, on a failure they are unable to do so.
