# During Travel
**Conditions.** A creature exposed to the Extreme Cold must succeed on a DC 10 CON saving throw at the end of each hour or gain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level. 
A creature that fails the saving throw by 5 or more gains a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Injuries/Freeze Injury\|Freeze Injury]].

**Resting In Extreme Cold.** A creature that takes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]] while exposed to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]] cannot recover [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] and cannot expend Hit Dice to restore their [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]].

# During Combat
**Taking Cold Damage In Combat.** Whenever a creature takes Cold damage while exposed to Extreme Cold, it must make a saving throw against [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]], this save can only happen once per turn. 

# Other Details

**Remove Exhaustion Levels.** A creature can remove one Exhaustion level it gained from being exposed to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]] by finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]] and succeeding on a DC 10 CON Saving Throw at the end of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]]. A creature has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on this saving throw if it spent the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]] near a source of heat.  


**Automatic Success.** Creatures with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immunity]] to Cold damage automatically succeed on the save, as do creatures that are naturally adapted to living in the arctic.
