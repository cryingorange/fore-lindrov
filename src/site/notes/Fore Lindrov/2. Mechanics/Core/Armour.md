# Proficiency
Armor training allows you to use armor of a certain category without the following drawbacks. If you wear Light, Medium, or Heavy armor and lack training with it, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Test]] that involves STR or DEX, and you can't cast spells. If you use a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Armor/Shield\|Shield]] and lack training with it, you don't gain its AC bonus.
