**Extended Travel or Forced March.** Characters can push themselves to travel for more than 8 hours per day, at the risk of tiring. 

At the end of each additional hour of travel beyond 8 hours, each character must succeed on a CON saving throw or gain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level. The DC is 10 plus 1 for each hour past 8 hours.
