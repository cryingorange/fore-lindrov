**One Spell with a Spell Slot per Turn.** On a turn, you can expend only one spell slot to cast a spell. This rule means you can't, for example, cast a spell with a spell slot using the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action and another one using a Bonus Action on the same turn.
