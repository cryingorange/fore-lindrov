An area might be [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Lightly Obscured\|Lightly Obscured]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]]. In a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Lightly Obscured\|Lightly Obscured]] area—such as an area with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]], patchy fog, or moderate foliage—you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on WIS (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

A [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]] area—such as an area with Darkness, heavy fog, or dense foliage—is opaque. You have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition when trying to see something there.
