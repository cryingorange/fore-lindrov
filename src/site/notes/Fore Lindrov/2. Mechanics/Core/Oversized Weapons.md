A creature has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls with a weapon that is sized for a larger attacker. 

The DM can rule that a weapon sized for an attacker two or more sizes larger is too big for the creature to use at all.
