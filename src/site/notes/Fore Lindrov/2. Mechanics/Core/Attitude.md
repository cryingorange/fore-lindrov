>**General.** A monster has a starting attitude toward a player character: [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Attitude#Friendly\|#Friendly]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Attitude#Hostile\|#Hostile]], or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Attitude#Indifferent\|#Indifferent]].

# Friendly
A Friendly creature views a character favorably. A character has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on an ability check to influence a Friendly creature.

<br>

# Hostile
A Hostile creature views a character unfavorably. A character has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on an ability check to influence a Hostile creature.

<br>

# Indifferent

An Indifferent creature has no desire to help or hinder a character. Indifferent is the default attitude of a monster.
