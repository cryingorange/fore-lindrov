Some game effects curse a creature or an object. The effect that confers a curse defines what the curse does. Curses can be removed by the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Greater Restoration\|Greater Restoration]] spells or other magic that explicitly ends curses.
