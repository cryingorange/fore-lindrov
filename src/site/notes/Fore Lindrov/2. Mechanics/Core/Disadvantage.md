If you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on a [[D20\|D20]] Test, roll two d20s and use the lower roll. A roll can't be affected by more than one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]], and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on the same roll cancel each other.
