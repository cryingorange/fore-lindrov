Characters without water and Rations can stave off [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Dehydration\|Dehydration]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Malnutrition\|Malnutrition]] by gathering food and water as they travel. 

**Survival Check.** A foraging character makes a WIS (Survival) check once per journey stage (or once per day if a stage is shorter than a day). 

**DC.** The DC is determined by the abundance of food and water in the region, as shown in the Foraging DC column of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Travel Terrain\|Travel Terrain]] Table. If multiple characters forage, each character makes a separate check.

**Determining Amount Found.** A foraging character finds nothing on a failed check. On a successful check, roll 1d6 and add the character's Wisdom modifier to determine how much food (in pounds) the character finds per day of the journey stage, then repeat the roll for water (in gallons).
