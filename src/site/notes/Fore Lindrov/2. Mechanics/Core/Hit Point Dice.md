Hit Point Dice, or Hit Dice for short, help determine a player character's HP maximum. Most monsters also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]] to regain Hit Points.
