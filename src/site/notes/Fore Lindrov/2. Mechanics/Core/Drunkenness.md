# Overview

## Free Drinks
A character can have a number of alcoholic drinks equal to their Constitution Modifier per Long Rest. Each additional drink it takes requires the creature to succeed on a CON saving throw against [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poison]] or move up one stage of Drunkenness.

## Drunkenness Saving Throws
If a character succeeds on the CON Saving Throw, they remain at the current stage of Drunkenness. 

## Removing Drunkenness
**Free Drinks.** The number of drinks a character can have before making saving throws against Drunkenness is reset if the character falls under any effect that removes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition. 

**Poisoned Condition.** Any effect that removes the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition removes the character's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]].

**Short Rest.** During a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]], a character can spend Hit Point Dice equal to the stage of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]] they are at to suppress the effects of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]] except for the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition, for 1 hour after their Short Rest. This feature can only be used for up to the 4th Stage of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]] (Blacked Out).

**Long Rest.** All levels of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]], the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] conditions gained from [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]] are removed once a creature finishes a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

# 1. Tipsy
> **CON Saving Throw:** DC 10
> **Description:** The character is tipsy, feels good about themselves and is more sociable.

On a failure, the character has the following traits:
- Advantage on Charisma Checks. 

<br>

# 2. Buzzed
> **CON Saving Throw:** DC 15
> **Description:** The character is slowing, their inhibitions are lowering and they feel stronger.

On a failure, the character has the following traits: 
- Advantage on Charisma Checks. 
- Advantage on Strength Checks and Saving Throws
- Disadvantage on DEX Checks and Saving Throws

<br>

# 3. Inebriated
> **CON Saving Throw:** DC 18
> **Description:** The character is drunk, but believes that they are in peak physical condition and can achieve anything.

On a failure, the character has the following traits: 
- Gain the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition.
- Gain one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Level.
- Advantage on saving throws against [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]].
- If the character finishes a Long Rest while in this stage of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]], they must succeed on a DC 15 CON Saving Throw at the end of the Long Rest or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] Condition for 24 hours.

<br>

# 4. Blacked Out
> **CON Saving Throw:** DC 20
> **Description:** The character is very drunk, and all its social inhibitions have been lifted, time passes like a blur.

On a failure, the character has the following traits: 
- Gain one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Level.
- The character will not remember anything from when it reaches this stage of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]] until the end of its next Long Rest.
- **Hangover.** If the character finishes a Long Rest while in this stage of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]], they must succeed on a DC 20 CON Saving Throw at the end of the Long Rest or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] Condition for 24 hours.

<br>

# 5. Passed Out
> **CON Saving Throw:** DC 25
> **Description:** The character is completely wiped out.

- Gain one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] Level.
- Gain the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition.
- **Hangover.** If the character finishes a Long Rest while in this stage of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Drunkenness\|Drunkenness]], they must succeed on a DC 20 CON Saving Throw at the end of the Long Rest or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] Condition for 24 hours.
