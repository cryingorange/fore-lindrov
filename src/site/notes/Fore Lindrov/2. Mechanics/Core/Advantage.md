If you have Advantage on a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Test]], roll two d20s, and use the higher roll. A roll can't be affected by more than one Advantage, and Advantage and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on the same roll cancel each other.
