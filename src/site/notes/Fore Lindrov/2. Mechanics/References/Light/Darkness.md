Darkness creates a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Heavily Obscured\|Heavily Obscured]] area. Characters face Darkness outdoors at night (even most moonlit nights), within the confines of an unlit dungeon, or in an area of magical Darkness.
