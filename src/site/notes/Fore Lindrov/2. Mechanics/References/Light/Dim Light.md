Dim Light, also called shadows, creates a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Lightly Obscured\|Lightly Obscured]] area. An area of Dim Light is usually a boundary between Bright Light and surrounding Darkness. The soft light of twilight and dawn also counts as Dim Light. A full moon might bathe the land in [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]].
