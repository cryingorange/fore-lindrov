> - **Stunned Condition.** When a creature becomes afflicted by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Madness\|Madness]], be it [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Short-Term Madness Table\|Short-Term]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Long-Term Madness Table\|Long-Term]], or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Madness/Indefinite Madness Table\|Indefinite]], the creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition until the start of its next turn, and then the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Madness\|Madness]] takes effect.
