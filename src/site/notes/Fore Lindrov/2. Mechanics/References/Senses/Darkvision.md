If you have Darkvision, you can see in [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] within a specified range as if it were [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] and in [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Darkness]] within that range as if it were [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]]. You discern colors in that [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Darkness]] only as shades of gray.
