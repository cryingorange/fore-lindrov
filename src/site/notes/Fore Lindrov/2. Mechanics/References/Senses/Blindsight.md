If you have Blindsight, you can see within a specific range without relying on physical sight. Within that range, you can see anything that isn't behind Total [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Cover]] even if you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition or are in [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Darkness]]. Moreover, in that range, you can see something that has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition.
