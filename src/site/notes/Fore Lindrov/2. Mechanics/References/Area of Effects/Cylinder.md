**Definition.** A Cylinder is an area of effect that extends in straight lines from a point of origin located at the center of the circular top or bottom of the Cylinder. 

**Size.** The effect that creates a Cylinder specifies the radius of the Cylinder's base and the Cylinder's height.

**Point of Origin.** A Cylinder's point of origin is included in the area of effect.
