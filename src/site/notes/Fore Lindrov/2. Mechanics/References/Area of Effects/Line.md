**Definition.** A Line is an area of effect that extends from a point of origin in a straight path along its length and covers an area defined by its width. 

**Size.** The effect that creates a Line specifies its length and width.

**Point of Origin.** A Line's point of origin isn't included in the area of effect unless its creator decides otherwise.
