**Definition.** A Sphere is an area of effect that extends in straight lines from a point of origin outward in all directions. 

**Size.** The effect that creates a Sphere specifies the distance it extends as the radius of the Sphere.

**Point of Origin.** A Sphere's point of origin is included in the Sphere's area of effect.
