**Definition.** A Cube is an area of effect that extends in straight lines from a point of origin located anywhere on a face of the Cube. 

**Size.** The effect that creates a Cube specifies its size, which is the length of each side. 

**Point of Origin.** A Cube's point of origin isn't included in the area of effect unless its creator decides otherwise.
