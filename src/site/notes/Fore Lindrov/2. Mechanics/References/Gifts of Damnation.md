# Stage 1

## Gift of Joyous Life

**Regain HP.** If you are [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Bloodied\|Bloodied]] at the start of your turn, you can choose to roll a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]] (no action required) and regain a number of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] equal to the roll. 

**Rolled a 1.** If you roll a 1 on the die, you regain no Hit Points and take 1 point of Force damage instead. 

**(1/SR).** You regain this ability when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

<br>
<br>

## Gift of Joyous Youth

**Regain HP.** If you have no remaining [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] at the start of your turn, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to make a CHA Saving Throw against your Transformation DC. 

**Success.** On a success, you regain 1 expended [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]].

<br>
<br>

## Gift of Prodigious Talent

**Gain Proficiency or Expertise.** Choose two Skills. If you lack proficiency in it, you gain proficiency. If you have proficiency but lack Expertise, you gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Expertise\|Expertise]] with that skill.

**Penalty.** If you roll a 1 or 2 on an Ability Check using one of the chosen skills, you immediately lose half your maximum number of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]], rounded down. You do not regain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] lost this way until you finish two [[Long Rests\|Long Rests]].

At Transformation Level 2 or higher, this penalty doesn't apply.

<br>
<br>

## Gift of Unsurpassed Fortune

**Attack Roll / Saving Throw.** When an enemy you can see within 10 feet succeeds on an attack roll or saving throw, you can use your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to roll a d20. 

**Success.** On a 10 or higher, the triggering attack or save misses or fails, and you regain the use of your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]]. 

**Failure.** On a 9 or lower, if you are lower than Transformation Level 4, you take 1d6 Psychic damage per Transformation Stage, which cannot be reduced.  

**(1/LR).** You regain your use of this ability when you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

# Stage 2

## Gift of Liberating Freedom

**Wings.** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] while aren't wearing Heavy Armor, you can manifest a pair of leathery wings, if the Armor or clothing you are wearing accommodates them. You gain a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Fly Speed\|Fly Speed]] equal to half your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]].

**Dismissal.** These wings recede if you dismiss them as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]], if you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition, or if you become [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Bloodied\|Bloodied]] while they are manifested.

**Duration.** When you've manifested your wings for a total of 1 hour, you cannot do so again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>

### Transformation Level 2+

**Wings.** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] while aren't wearing Heavy Armor, you can manifest a pair of leathery wings, if the Armor or clothing you are wearing accommodates them. You gain a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Fly Speed\|Fly Speed]] equal to your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]].

**Dismissal.** These wings recede if you dismiss them as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] or if you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition.

**Duration.** When you've manifested your wings for a total of 1 hour, you cannot do so again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>
<br>

## Gift of Unfettered Glory

**Additional Damage.** Each time you damage a creature with a melee attack or Cantrip, you can do an additional 2 Force Damage per Transformation Stage.

**Lower Recovery.** Each time you roll a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]] to regain HP during a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]], you regain 2 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] fewer per [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]]. At Transformation Level 3 or higher, this penalty doesn't apply.

<br>
<br>

# Stage 3

## Gift of Second Chances

**Roll Hit Point Die.** When you are reduced to 0 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]], you can use your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to roll a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]]. 

**Set HP.** If you roll a 2 or better, set your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] to the result of the roll. 

**Failure.** If not, you suffer a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Death Saving Throw\|Death Saving Throw]] failure.

**(1/LR).** You regain the use of this gift when you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>

### Transformation Level 3+

**Roll Hit Point Die.** When you are reduced to 0 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]], you can use your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to roll a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]]. 

**Set HP.** You set your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] equal to the result of the roll. 

**(1/LR).** You regain the use of this gift when you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>
<br>

## Gift of Unconditional Love

**Failed Saving Throw.** When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you fails a Saving Throw, you can use your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] equal to 1d10 plus your Transformation Stage. 

**Rolled a 1.** If you roll a 1 on the d10, you gain no [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition. At Transformation Level 3 or higher, this penalty doesn't apply.

**(1/SR).** You regain the use of this gift when you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>
<br>

# Stage 4

## Gift of Martial Prowess

**Missed Attack.** Once per turn, when you miss with a weapon attack or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Unarmed Strike\|Unarmed Strike]], you can reroll the attack roll. If you have fewer than 3 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] available, you cannot use this gift.

**Success.** If the reroll hits, you must expend 3 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] and add the result to the attack's normal damage as Force damage. 

**Failure.** If the reroll misses, you must expend 3 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] and take Psychic damage equal to the result. At Transformation Level 4 or higher, this penalty doesn't apply.

**(1/LR).** Once you hit a target with an attack using a rerolled attack, you cannot use it again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>
<br>

## Gift of Unbridled Power

**Regain Spell Slots.** Upon finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]], you can spend 2 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Dice]] and regain a number of spell slot levels equal to the highest number rolled. 

**Take Damage.** You take Psychic damage equal to twice the total that you rolled. 

**(1/LR).** You regain the reuse of this gift when you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>

### Transformation Level 4

**Regain Spell Slots.** Upon finishing a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short Rest]], you can spend 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Hit Point Dice\|Hit Point Die]] and regain a number of spell slot levels equal to the number rolled. 

**Take Damage.** You take Psychic damage equal to the roll. 

**(1/LR).** You regain the reuse of this gift when you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].
