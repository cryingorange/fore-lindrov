When you take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] action on your turn and attack with a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light\|Light]] weapon, you can make one extra attack as a Bonus Action later on the same turn. 

That extra attack must be made with a different [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Light\|Light]] weapon, and you don't add your ability modifier to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative. 

>**Example.** You can attack with a Shortsword in one hand and a Dagger in the other using the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] Action and a Bonus Action, but you don't add your STR or DEX modifier to the damage roll of the Bonus Action unless that modifier is negative.
