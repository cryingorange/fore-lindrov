**Forcebreaker.** Striking a Large or smaller structure of magical force (such as those made with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/7th Level/Forcecage\|Forcecage]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Wall of Force\|Wall of Force]]) with this weapon automatically shatters that structure. 

**Huge or Larger.** If the target is a Huge or larger structure of force, this weapon shatters a 20-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Cube\|Cube]] portion of it.
