You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your STR score isn't at least 13 or if it's a Ranged weapon and your DEX score isn't at least 13.
