# Overview

>Doors are commonly considered Medium objects.

**Forcing Open.** With the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] action, a creature can try to force open a door that is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Objects/Doors#Barred Door\|Barred]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Objects/Doors#Locked Door\|Locked]], doing so with a successful STR (Athletics) check. The table provides the DC of the check. 

**Guidelines.** For bigger doors, double or triple the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] and increase the DC of the check by 5.

<br>

# Common Door Table
| Door        | AC  | HP   | DC to Force Open | 
| ----------- |:---:|:---:| :----------------: |
| Glass door  | 13  |  4  |        10          |
| Wooden door | 15  | 18  |         15         |
| Stone door  | 17  | 40  |         20         |
| Metal door  | 19  | 72   |        25          |

<br>

# Barred Door
A barred door has no lock. A creature on the barred side of the door can take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action to lift the bar from its braces, allowing the door to be opened.

<br>

# Locked Door

Characters who don't have the key to a locked door can try to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Picking A Lock\|pick the lock]] using Thieves' Tools.
