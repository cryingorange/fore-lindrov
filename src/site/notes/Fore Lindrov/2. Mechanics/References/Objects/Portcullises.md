# Overview
Typically made of Iron or Wood, a portcullis blocks a passage or an archway until it is raised into the ceiling by a Winch and Chain. 



## Cover & Statistics

**Attacking & Cover.** Creatures within 5 feet of a lowered portcullis can make ranged attacks or cast spells through it, and they have Three-Quarters [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Cover\|Three-Quarters Cover]] against attacks, spells, and other effects originating from the opposite side. 

**Destroy Portcullis.** A portcullis can also be attacked and destroyed, use the AC and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] of a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Objects/Doors\|Metal Door]] (if iron) or a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Objects/Doors\|Wooden Door]] (if wood).
