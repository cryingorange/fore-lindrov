If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a CON saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used to make the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.
