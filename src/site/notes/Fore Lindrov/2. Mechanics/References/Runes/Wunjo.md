> Wunjo Rune

*A Rune meaning Happiness, or Joy*

# Benefit
After using Heroic Inspiration or gifting it to another player, you can choose to roll a d100, on a 1–20, you keep the Heroic Inspiration. Once you keep a Heroic Inspiration in this way, you cannot do so again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 24 hours

When this Rune is drawn on the ground or on a boulder, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Animal Friendship\|Animal Friendship]] originating from the Rune.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

After this Rune is drawn on an object and you hold it aloft, your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Ally\|Allies]] that can see the Rune gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on saving throws against the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] conditions.
