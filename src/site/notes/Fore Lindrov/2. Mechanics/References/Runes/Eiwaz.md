> Ehwaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Yew Tree, or World Tree / Yggdrasil*

# Benefit
You gain Proficiency in Arcana.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 1 minute

When you draw this Rune on a suitably sized piece of wood, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Shillelagh\|Shillelagh]]. This Power can be invoked At Will.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a piece of [[Fore Lindrov/Items/Mundane Items/Adventuring Gear/Paper\|Paper]] or [[Parchment\|Parchment]] with three questions on it, and sleep for at least 10 minutes while holding the  [[Fore Lindrov/Items/Mundane Items/Adventuring Gear/Paper\|Paper]] or [[Parchment\|Parchment]], you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Contact Other Plane\|Contact Other Plane]], receiving an answer to each question when you awaken, but only after you succeed on the spell's INT Saving Throw.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 11)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Until Erased

You carve this Rune into the bark of a living tree. As part of the Action to carve a Rune, you can cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Tree Stride\|Tree Stride]], travelling between trees previously marked with this Rune.
