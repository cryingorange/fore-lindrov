> Sowilo Rune

*A Rune meaning The Sun*

# Benefit
You are Immune to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 1 minute

When you draw this Rune on a creature, that creature can cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Enthrall\|Enthrall]] once within the next minute. 

<br>

# Power (Level 7)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on an object, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Daylight\|Daylight]] on the object. 

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 13)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

When you draw this Rune on the brow of a creature, the target casts [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/6th Level/Sunbeam\|Sunbeam]] (no Concentration) and becomes Immune to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition. The target's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/6th Level/Sunbeam\|Sunbeam]] and Immunity to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition end when your Concentration ends.
