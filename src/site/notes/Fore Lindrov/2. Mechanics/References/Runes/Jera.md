> Jera Rune

*A Rune meaning Abundant Harvest and Fertility*

# Benefit
You have Proficiency in Nature.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action 
> **Duration:** 24 hours

**Conception.** If you draw this Rune on the belly of a living creature, it guarantees that any sexual union involving that creature in the next 24 hours results in conception.

**Successful Birth.** If the rune is drawn on a pregnant creature at least once per month during its pregnancy up to , the act guarantees the successful birth of a healthy offspring.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune in the earth among living plants, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Plant Growth\|Plant Growth]] (Overgrowth), centered on the Rune.
