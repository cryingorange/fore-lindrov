> Ehwaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Horses, Freedom or a Nithing Pole*

# Benefit
**Animal Handling.** You gain a +1 bonus to WIS (Animal Handling) checks you make to control a mount.

**Grappled & Restrained.** You gain a +1 bonus to DEX (Acrobatics) checks you make to end the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] conditions.

<br>

# Power
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a lock or set of shackles, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Knock\|Knock]], but without making any sound.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** 5 minutes
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Phantom Steed\|Phantom Steed]].

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 9)
> **Scribing Time:** 12 Hours
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Scarification.** When you carve this Rune into your left hand, bloodletting and scarring it during a new moon, and pouring it over a staff-like object of your choice, you create a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Nithing Pole\|Nithing Pole]].

**Additional Scars.** Each new use of this power creates a new scar; those after the first don’t need to be on the left hand, but they must be on the
hands or arms.
