> Raido Rune

*A Rune meaning Travel*

# Benefit
Your Speed(s) from your Species increases by 5 feet and you always know which way is North.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

After you draw this Rune on an object, a creature holding it has Advantage on DEX (Acrobatics) checks to skit, skate, or control a sled.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on your skin, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Water Walk\|Water Walk]].

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 7)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** 12 hours 

When you draw this Rune on the sole of your foot or shoes, you and five other creatures of your choice that you can see automatically succeed on the first four CON Saving Throws made against Exhaustion during [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Travel Pace\|Extended Travel]], the other creatures can only benefit from this if they are travelling with you.
