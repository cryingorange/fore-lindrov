> Otalan Rune

*A Rune meaning Property, Inheritance or Family*

# Benefit
You have Proficiency in Persuasion.

<br>

# Power
> **Scribing Time:** 10 minutes
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

When you draw this Rune on a reflective surface such as a polished shield, mirror or still, clear water. You cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Scrying\|Scrying]] targeting an object that was in your possession for at least one week, or any member of your immediate family within three generations of you.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 13)
> **Scribing Time:** 10 minutes
> **Duration:** Until Dispelled or Triggered

**Teleportation.** After you draw this Rune on an object, when the object's owner dies it [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleports]] into the hands of another creature whose identity is specified when the Rune was drawn. 

**Beneficiary.** This beneficiary can be described by name, title or relationship (such as "The King of Fore Lindrov" or "My Firstborn Son"). 

**Range.** This Power functions over any range, as long as the object and beneficiary are on the same plane of existence.

**Another Creature.** If another creature is holding the object when this Power is triggered, that creature can choose to prevent the object from Teleporting to the beneficiary by making a CHA Saving Throw. On a success, the object is not Teleported to the beneficiary, and the creature knows there was an attempt to Teleport the object. 

**Only One Object.** Only one object can benefit from this Power at any given time. If this Power is invoked on a different object, the previous object loses this Power.
