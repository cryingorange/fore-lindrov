> Kaunen Rune

*A Rune meaning Flaming Torch, Enlightenment or Ulcers*

# Benefit
You gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]] out to a range of 30 feet. If you already have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]], you gain Proficiency in Perception.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
## Bonfire
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Create Bonfire\|Create Bonfire]] (no Concentration, 8 hour Duration) in the space the Rune was drawn.

## Continual Flame
When you draw this Rune on an object or brazier, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Continual Flame\|Continual Flame]] in the space the Rune was drawn.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

When you draw this Rune onto a weapon, the weapon sheds [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from the object and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 10 feet. Additionally, the weapon deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a Non-Undead, Non-Construct creature, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]] on the target.
