> Dagaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Day and Laying the Dead To Rest*

# Benefit
By taking the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Study\|Study]] Action to examine a creature you can see within 10 feet of you, you can determine whether it is alive or dead with absolute certainty.

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:**  Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a creature, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]].

# Power (Level 6)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:**  Concentration, up to 1 hour

When you draw this Rune on an object, [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] fills a 60-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from the object and sheds [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet. 

**Dispels Magical Darkness.** If any of this Light's area overlaps with an area of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Darkness\|Darkness]] created by a spell of level 3 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.

# Mastery Power (Level 13)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Turn Undead.** When you draw this Rune on the ground or a wall, each Undead of your choice within 30 feet of the Rune that can see it must make a WIS saving throw. If the creature fails its save, it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] conditions for 1 minute. For that duration, it tries to move as far from you as it can on its turns. This effect ends early on the creature if it takes any damage, if you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] condition, or if you die.

You can only invoke this Mastery Power once per week.
