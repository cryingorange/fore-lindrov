> Perto Rune

*A Rune meaning Hidden Truth or Trickster*

# Benefit
You gain a +1 bonus to INT ability checks to solve riddles or puzzles, and INT saving throws against Illusions.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a creature, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Disguise Self\|Disguise Self]] on the target. If the creature has an INT score of 3 or higher, it decides it's appearance.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 8 hours

When you draw this Rune on the ground, all objects in a 10-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from the rune can't be targeted by any Divination spell or perceived through magical scrying sensors.
