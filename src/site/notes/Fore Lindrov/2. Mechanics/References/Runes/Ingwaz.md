> Ingwaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Ancestors or Greater Deities*

# Benefit
You have Proficiency in History.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 8 hours (Before Activation), 1 minute (After Activation) 

When you draw this Rune on a Spear, the Spear deals an extra 1d4 Radiant damage on a hit, this benefit is activated once a creature uses the Spear to make an attack roll within 8 hours of the Rune being drawn. Once this benefit is activated, it lasts for 1 minute.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Benefits.** When you draw this Rune on the face of a willing creature, the target gains the following benefits for 1 minute:
- **Resistance.** Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
- **Bonus Damage.** The target gains a bonus to the damage of STR-Based attacks based on your Level. (+2 for Levels 1–8, +3 for Levels 9–15, +4 for Levels 16–20)
- **Advantage.** Advantage on STR checks and STR saving throws.

**No Concentration.** The target can't maintain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], and can't cast spells. 

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When the Rune is drawn on the flesh of a corpse, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Speak with Dead\|Speak with Dead]] (Concentration required) on the corpse.
