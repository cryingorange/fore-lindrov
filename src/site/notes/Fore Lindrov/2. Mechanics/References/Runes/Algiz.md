> Algiz Rune

*A Rune meaning Elk and Reindeer or Evading Danger*

# Benefit
You gain a +1 bonus on Initiative rolls.

<br>

# Power
> **Scribing Time:** 15 minutes
> **Duration:** 8 hours

When this Rune is drawn on the ground or a wall, each Non-Undead and Non-Construct creature in a 10-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from the Rune gains a +2 bonus to WIS (Perception) checks and have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on saving throws against the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition.

<br>

# Mastery  Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When this rune is drawn on a willing creature, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Polymorph\|Polymorph]] on the target, turning it into an [[Elk\|Elk]].
