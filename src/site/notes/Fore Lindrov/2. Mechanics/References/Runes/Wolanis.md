> Wolanis Rune

*A Rune meaning Cloud, or Gathering Storm*

# Benefit
 When you make a High Jump or Long Jump, you can jump up to your maximum distance if you move only 5 feet on foot immediately before the jump rather than 10 feet.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)

## Drawn on Ground

> **Scribing Time:** 1 hour
> **Duration:** 8 hours

When this Rune is drawn on the ground you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Leomund's Tiny Hut\|Leomund's Tiny Hut]] originating from the Rune.

## Drawn on Creature

> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 1 hour

When you draw this Rune on a willing creature, the target has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Lightning damage.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)

## Drawn on Object

> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** 1 hour

After this Rune is drawn on an object, it gains a Fly Speed of 30 feet at which time it can be ridden in the air and can carry up to 400 pounds.

## Drawn on Creature

> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

When you draw this Rune on a willing creature other than yourself, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Fly\|Fly]] on the creature.
