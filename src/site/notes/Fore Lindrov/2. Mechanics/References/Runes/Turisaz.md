> Turisaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Giants, Thunder and Lightning*

# Benefit
You can speak and understand Giant. If you already know this
language, you gain Advantage on WIS (Insight) checks you make on Giants.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 8 hours (Before Activation), 1 minute (After Activation)

When this Rune is drawn on a weapon, the weapon deals an extra 1d4 Lightning damage on a hit, this benefit is activated once a creature uses the weapon to make an attack roll within 8 hours of the Rune being drawn. Once this benefit is activated, it lasts for 1 minute.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

When this Rune is drawn on armor, it grants it's wearer Resistance to Lightning damage and a +2 bonus to the wearer's STR Score.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 hour
> **Duration:** 24 hours or until Triggered

When this Rune is drawn on a surface, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Glyph of Warding\|Glyph of Warding]] (Explosive Rune only, Lightning damage), the Rune acts as the Glyph in the spell.
