> Laukaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Water or The Sea*

# Benefit
You can speak and understand Aquan. If you already know this
language, you gain Advantage on CHA (Persuasion) checks on creatures from the Elemental Plane of Water.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 24 hours

When you draw this Rune on an object, it becomes more buoyant. Any creature grasping the object has Advantage on STR (Athletics) checks to swim or stay afloat.

<br>

# Power (Level 7)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 24 hours

When you draw this Rune on a Non-Undead, Non-Construct creature's chest, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Water Breathing\|Water Breathing]], targeting only that creature.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 11)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the surface of a lakebed, riverbed, or the sea bottom, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Control Water\|Control Water]] centered on the Rune.
