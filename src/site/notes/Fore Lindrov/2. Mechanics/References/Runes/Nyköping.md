> Nyköping Rune

*A Rune meaning Passage of Time, The Seasons or the Phases of the Moon*

# Benefit
You have Proficiency in Survival.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** 10 minutes
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Cure Wounds\|Cure Wounds]] on up to six creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you.

<br>

# Power (Level 6)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground under an open sky, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Gust of Wind\|Gust of Wind]], originating from the Rune.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 9)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a creature, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Haste\|Haste]] on the target.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 13)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 hour
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground beneath an open sky, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/8th Level/Control Weather\|Control Weather]] (no Concentration, Precipitation only, Lower Stage only).
