> Hagalaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Hail, or Winter Weather*

# Benefit
You have Advantage on WIS (Survival) checks to predict weather conditions for the next 24 hours in your vicinity.

<br>

# Power
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a willing creature, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Protection From Energy\|Protection From Energy]] (Cold damage) on the target.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 9)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground under the open sky, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Sleet Storm\|Sleet Storm]].

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 14)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground inside the area of an effect caused by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Sleet Storm\|Sleet Storm]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Ice Storm\|Ice Storm]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/8th Level/Control Weather\|Control Weather]] (Hail or Blizzard only) or other similar spells, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Dispel Magic\|Dispel Magic]] (7th Level) on the effect.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 14)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 hour
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground inside the area of a natural storm. You cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/8th Level/Control Weather\|Control Weather]].
