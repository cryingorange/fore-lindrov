> Naudiz Rune

*A Rune meaning Necessity, or Filling Needs*

# Benefit
You can cast the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/(0 Level) Cantrip/Spare The Dying\|Spare The Dying]] cantrip once per [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

When you draw this Rune on an object, a creature can touch the object and gain a 1d4 bonus to the next ability check or saving throw it makes within the duration of the Rune's Power. A number of creatures equal to your Proficiency Bonus can benefit from this effect at any given time.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 15)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 24 hours

After you draw this Rune on an object weighing no more than 20 pounds. As an Action, you can [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] the object into your hand if it is within 400 feet of you, regardless of barriers. 

**CHA Saving Throw.** If another creature is holding the object when you try to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] it, the creature can choose to prevent the object from Teleporting to you by making a CHA Saving Throw. On a success, the object is not Teleported to you, and the creature knows there was an attempt to Teleport the object.
