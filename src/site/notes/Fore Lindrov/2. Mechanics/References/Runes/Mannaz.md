> Mannaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Humanoids, Dwarves or Humans*

# Benefit
You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Petrified\|Petrified]] condition and effects that would alter your form, such as the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Polymorph\|Polymorph]] spell or Lycanthropy.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground, a boulder or a wall, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Calm Emotions\|Calm Emotions]], centered on the Rune.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the flesh of a creature, the target must succeed on a CHA Saving Throw. On a failure, if the target is a Lycanthrope it is forced into its Humanoid form. If the target is transformed by a spell such as [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Polymorph\|Polymorph]], the spell ends on it. 

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 9)
> **Scribing Time:** 8 hours
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on a plant or animal, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Awaken\|Awaken]] on the target.
