> Tewaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Fearlessness, Assemblies or Councils*

# Benefit
You have Advantage on Saving Throws against the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 1 hour

**Present Object.** After this Rune is drawn on an object, a creature holding it can take an Action to present the object.

**Lupines.** Wolves, Worgs, Werwolves and other Lupine creatures within 60 feet of the Rune that can see it must succeed on a CHA Saving Throw.

**Failed Saving Throw.** On a failure, the target must stay at least 5 feet away from the rune and cannot touch or make melee attacks against the creature presenting it until the end of the presenting creature's next turn.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When this Rune is drawn on the ground, a floor, or a wall, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Calm Emotions\|Calm Emotions]] (Any Creature Type, Must be Willing) centered on the Rune.
