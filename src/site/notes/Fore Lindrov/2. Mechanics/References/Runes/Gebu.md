> Gebu Rune

*A Rune meaning Generosity, Hospitality or Gifts*

# Benefit
You have Advantage on Intelligence checks to estimate an item’s value.

<br>

# Power
## Drawn on Item
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:**  Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on an item while examining it, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Identify\|Identify]] on it.



## Drawn on Creature
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:**  Concentration, up to 1 minute

When you draw this Rune on a willing creature other than yourself, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Sanctuary\|Sanctuary]] on the creature.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** 1 minute

**Drawing.** You draw this Rune on a wall, floor, or other surface of an enclosed space, in a place where it is clearly visible. 

**Command Word.** For up to 1 minute thereafter, you can activate the Rune by saying a command word with a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, provided you are within 20 feet of the Rune. 

**Calm Emotions.** When you do so, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Calm Emotions\|Calm Emotions]] (Any Creature Type) centered on the Rune.
