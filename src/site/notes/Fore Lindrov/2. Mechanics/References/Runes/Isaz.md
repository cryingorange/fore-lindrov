> Isaz Rune

*A Rune meaning Ice, Imprisonment, Paralysis*

# Benefit
Whenever you take Cold damage, that damage is reduced by 1.

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When you draw this Rune on the ground, slick ice covers the ground up to a 30-foot-square centered on the Rune. You can cause the ice to form into any shape you want so long as it is one contiguous, connected area of ice. The area is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Slippery Ice\|Slippery Ice]]. The ice is considered nonmagical and melts normally.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

When you draw this Rune on a weapon, the weapon sheds [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 10 feet. Additionally, the weapon deals extra 1d4 Cold damage on a hit.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

When you draw this Rune on a shield, a creature holding the shield gains the following benefits:
- **Reflect Sunlight.** As an Action, a creature holding the shield in direct [[Sunlight\|Sunlight]] can use it to focus reflected light onto one creature it can see within 30 feet. 
- **CON Saving Throw.** The target must make a CON Saving Throw, on a failure, it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Blinded\|Blinded]] condition.
