> Fehu Rune

*A Rune meaning Cattle, Livestock or Wealth*

# Benefit
You have Advantage on WIS (Animal Handling) checks you make about Beasts who are domesticated (Cattle, Riding Horses).

<br>

# Power
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute 
> **Duration:**  Concentration, up to 1 hour

When you draw this Rune on an object or collection of objects bundled together such as a large chest stuffed with coins, it becomes easier to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Carrying Capacity\|Lift or Drag]] as if it were half its actual weight.

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** 10 minutes

When you draw this Rune on your skin, you can cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Dominate Beast\|Dominate Beast]] (6th Level). This effect must be used within 10 minutes of completing the Rune, or the Rune dissipates.
