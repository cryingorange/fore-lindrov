> Uruz Rune

*A Rune meaning Wild Oxen, a Sacrifice to the Gods or Strength*

# Benefit
You count as one size larger when determining your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Carrying Capacity\|Carrying Weight]].

<br>

# Power (Level 4)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 minute
> **Duration:** 1 hour

**Shield Bash.** After this Rune is drawn on a shield, a creature wielding the shield can use a Bonus Action to make a melee attack with the shield. 

**Attack.** On a hit, the shield deals 1d4 + STR modifier Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is no more than one size larger than the wielder, it is pushed back up to 10 feet away from the wielder.

<br>

# Power (Level 5)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

When this Rune is drawn on the flesh of a Non-Undead, Non-Construct creature, the creature counts as one size larger when determining your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Carrying Capacity\|Carrying Capacity]] and Advantage on STR checks. 

<br>

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** 1 hour
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Sacrifice.** You draw this Rune on an appropriate sacrifice (such as food, a symbolic weapon, an animal). 

**Living Sacrifice.** If you draw this Rune on a living creature, the creature must be killed within 1 hour after drawing this Rune before you can invoke this Power. 

**Object Sacrifice.** If the sacrifice is an object, the object is destroyed when the Rune is drawn.

**Remove Curse.** When the Rune's Power is invoked, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] on the sacrifice-giver. The level of the spell is determined by the significance of the sacrifice, as determined by the DM.
