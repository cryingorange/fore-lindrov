> Berkanan Rune

*A Rune meaning Birch Tree, Love, New Beginnings or The Bear Maiden*

# Benefit
You gain a +1 bonus on CHA (Persuasion) checks.

# Power

## Drawn on Flesh & Blood
> **Scribing Time:** 10 minutes
> **Duration:**  1 hour

**Flesh & Blood.** When you draw this Rune on a Non-Undead, Non-Construct, Non-Plant creature, the target gains [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on one CHA (Persuasion) check of its choice that it makes within the next hour.

## Drawn on Plant
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:**  Instantaneous

**Plant.** When you draw this Rune on a living plant, you cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Spike Growth\|Spike Growth]], centered on the Rune.

# Mastery Power (Level 8)
> **Scribing Time:** Action
> **Duration:** Instantaneous

When this Rune is drawn on a container with liquid that has no other magical effects, it becomes a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Consumables/Potions/Philter Of Love\|Philter Of Love]]. You can only invoke this Mastery Power once per week.
