# Overview 
**Drow House.** House Duskryn was a drow house of [[Menzoberranzan\|Menzoberranzan]]. They won the charter from [[House Baenre\|House Baenre]] that allowed them to loot the city of [[Blingdenstone\|Blingdenstone]].

**Cruelty.** The nobles of the house were known for their cruelty which they enacted seemingly for their own perverse amusement. 

**Best Armies.** Duskryn consistently trained some of the best armies in Menzoberranzan for the 3,000 years since the house's founding. 

**Performance.** Advancement was awarded for performance, not noble stature as was the norm in most other houses. However, all of the most famous military victories of the house were defensive rather than offensive in nature.

<br>

# Notable Activities
1. **Siege of Blingdenstone.** The armies of House Duskryn, led by [[Neheedra Duskryn - Profile\|Neheedra Duskryn]] successfully conquered and looted the Deep Gnome City of Blingdenstone.
	- Neheedra stayed behind with a small contingent to occupy Blingdenstone, and she went mad, turning into a Drider in Rockblight.
