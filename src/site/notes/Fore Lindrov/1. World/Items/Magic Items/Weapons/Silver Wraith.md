# Overview

**History.** Silver Wraith is a Moonblade created by Charrana Mizzrym, and was meant to be the first blade of their Cadet-House.
It was used in the [[Charrana Mizzrym - Profile\|Charrana’ Mizzrym's]] assassination attempt on [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Lolth-Sworn Drow/Shriala Mizzrym\|Shriala Mizzrym]], and witnessed the death of its creator.
It has refused to serve Shriala, bearing its creator's resentment and jealousy of Shriala, and hates her for killing its previous wielder.

**Details.** Silverwraith is an Adamantine Longsword with the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Finesse\|Finesse]] Property. 

<br>

## Sentience
**Statistics.** Silver Wraith is a sentient weapon with an INT of 12, a WIS of 10, and a CHA of 12. It has hearing and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision]] out to 120 feet. Its alignment is Neutral Evil.

**Communication.** The weapon communicates by transmitting emotions, sending a tingling sensation through the wielder's hand when it wants to communicate something it has sensed. It can communicate through visions or dreams when the wielder is either in a trance or asleep.

**Personality.** Silver Wraith is cold, cruel, cunning, deceptive, vengeful and domineering, and appreciates creatures who display such features, however is not a Drow-Supremacist Purist. It also deeply hates [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Lolth-Sworn Drow/Shriala Mizzrym\|Shriala Mizzrym]], and wishes to see her suffer or be killed.

<br>

## Attunement
**Curse.** A creature that Silver Wraith rejects as its bearer will have a curse placed on it, causing it to make [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Tests]] with [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] for 24 hours, or until the Curse is ended by a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] spell or similar magic.

**Instant.** A creature accepted by Silver Wraith that tries to attune to it will become attuned instantly, causing a new rune to appear on it. That creature remains attuned to the weapon until it dies or the weapon is destroyed.

**Other Creatures.** Silver Wraith functions like a nonmagical Longsword for anyone other than its chosen bearer. 

<br>

# Description
- The blade glows with an ethereal shine and you hear a soft hum that rings through the air.
- Its entire length is coloured a bright teal like welcoming moonlight, and has a magical gleam to it.

# Damage
**Damage.** 1d8 Slashing damage or 1d10 Slashing damage (Versatile)

# Runes
Silver Wraith has only ever served one other Master, Charrana and has only one Rune on it, gaining one if attuned to.

1. **+1 Bonus.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with Silver Wraith.

2. **Critical Hit on 19.** An attack roll made with Silver Wraith scores a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] on a 19 or 20.
