# Effects

## General

- **Bonus (+1).** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
	- Additionally, this weapon deals an extra 1d6 Radiant damage on a hit. 
- **Thrown.** It has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Thrown\|Thrown]] with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. Immediately after hitting or missing, the weapon flies back to your hand.
- **Extraplanar Abjurer.** When you hit an Aberration, Celestial, Elemental, Fey, or Fiend with it, that creature takes an extra 1d6 Radiant damage.

<br>

## Spellcasting

- **Spells.** While holding the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/Mysterious Weapon\|Mysterious Weapon]], you can cast the following spells (save DC 13) from it: 
	- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Protection from Evil and Good\|Protection from Evil and Good]]
	- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Revivify\|Revivify]]
	- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Aura Of Life\|Aura Of Life]]

Once you use the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/Mysterious Weapon\|Mysterious Weapon]] to cast a spell, you can't cast a spell again from it until the next Dawn.
