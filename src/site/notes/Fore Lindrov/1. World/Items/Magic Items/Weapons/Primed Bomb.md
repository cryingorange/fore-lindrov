# Overview

When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a Primed Bomb. 

**DEX Saving Throw.** Each creature in a 5-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on a point within 90 feet that you can see must succeed on a DEX Saving Throw (DC 8 plus your STR or DEX modifier and Proficiency Bonus).

**Success or Failure.** On a failure, the target takes 3d12 Fire damage. On a success, the target takes half damage. 

This damage ignores [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]]. If the target is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immune]], it's considered [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistant]] instead.
