# Overview
**Attunement Requirement.** When [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]] infects a creature that is a Parasite Patron Warlock, it becomes a weapon requiring Attunement.

# Properties
- **Aversion To Fire.** If you take Fire damage, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of your next turn and start [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Burning\|Burning]].
- **Random Properties.** The [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]] has the following random properties:
	- 2 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables#Minor Beneficial Properties\|Minor Beneficial Properties]]
	- 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables#Minor Detrimental Properties\|Minor Detrimental Property]]

# Effects
## Fungal Form
**Choose Form.** Whenever you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]], you can choose what form the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]] takes.

### Chest Tendril Form
> The [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]] manifests as a squashy, elastic tendril that emerges from your chest and grants you the following benefits:
- **Improved Speed.** Your Speed increases by 10 feet.
-  **Simple Weapon.** The tendril counts as a Simple Melee Weapon with which you are proficient.
	- The tendril deals 1d8 Bludgeoning damage and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Reach\|Reach]] Property.
	- You can use a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] to make a melee attack with the tendril. 
- **Spellcasting Focus (+1).** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]]
- **Siphoning Counterspell.**  You always have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Counterspell\|Counterspell]] spell prepared. 
	- When you cast it, you can change the type of saving throw to a CHA save instead, if the target fails the save, the spell is wasted. 
	- Additionally, the target is subjected to your Spell Siphon feature.

<br>

### Tendril Arms
> The [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]] erupts down your shoulders, mutating both of your arms into grotesque, elastic appendages of raw flesh granting you the following benefits:

- **Simple Weapons.** Your arms counts as Simple Melee Weapons with which you are proficient and in this form, you cannot wield any weapon or shield in your hands.
	- Your arms deals 1d10 Bludgeoning damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Reach\|Reach]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Versatile\|Versatile]] (2d6) Properties.
- **Bonus (+1).** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
- **Siphoning Life.** Whenever you attack with your arms, you can cause them to deal Necrotic damage and you regain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] equal to half the Necrotic damage dealt.

<br>

## Special Actions
### Regain Spell Slots
- **Feed Cordyceps.** You can feed your own organic matter you've consumed to the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]] to regain Spell Slots as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]].
	- You can expend and roll a number of Hit Die equal to the level of the spell slot or spell from Mystic Arcanum you wish to regain. 
	- When you do so, you lose [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] equal to the total rolled.

### Mycelial Filament
- **Extrude Filament.** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]], you can extrude a Small Mycelial Filament into an unoccupied space within 5 feet, you determine its appearance.
	- You roll a number of your Hit Point Dice up to your character level, the Filament's HP is equal to that roll.
	- You lose [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]], and your HP maximum decreases by an amount equal to the roll.
	- You have one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level for each Mycelial Filament you have extruded at a time.
- **Statistics.** The Filament is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, it shares your Initiative count and lacks the Extrude Filament Action. 
	- It shares your game statistics and resources other than its Size and [HP](#[^filament-ac]).
- **Attack Action.** The filament can take the Attack action, and mirrors the current form of your [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]]:
	- **Chest Tendril:** Its melee attacks deal 1d8 Bludgeoning damage and have the Reach property.
	- **Tendril Arms Form.** Its melee attacks deal 1d10 Bludgeoning damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Reach\|Reach]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Versatile\|Versatile]] (2d6) properties.
- **Reduced to 0 HP.** When the Filament is reduced to 0 HP, it is destroyed. 
	- Your [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] Maximum is restored by the amount it was reduced during its creation. 
	- You remove the Exhaustion level you gained during its creation.
- **Cannot Die.** You do not die unless your primary body and your extruded filaments are destroyed. 
	- If your primary body dies while the extrusion lives, your consciousness immediately transfers to one extrusion of your choice. 
	- At the end of your next Long Rest, extrusion grows into your original body.
