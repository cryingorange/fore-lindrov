## Description
This weapon is decorated with dragon heads, claws, wings, scales, or Draconic letters.

## Effect

This weapon deals either Acid, Fire, Cold, Lightning or Poison damage depending on what type of Dragon's Breath it has absorbed.

### Slumbering
Whenever you roll a 20 on your attack roll with this weapon, each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 5 damage of the type dealt by the dragon's breath weapon.

### Stirring

**Bonus.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made using the weapon. On a hit, the weapon deals an extra 1d6 damage of the type dealt by the dragon's breath weapon.

### Wakened

**Bonus.** Your bonus increases to a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made using the weapon. On a hit, the weapon deals an extra 2d6 damage of the type dealt by the dragon's breath weapon instead of 1d6.

**Action (1/Dawn).** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, you can unleash a 30-foot Cone of destructive energy from the weapon. Each creature in that area must make a DC 16 DEX saving throw, taking 8d6 damage of the type dealt by the dragon's breath weapon on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. 

Once this action is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
