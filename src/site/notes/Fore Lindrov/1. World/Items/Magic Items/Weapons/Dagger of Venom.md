# Details
**+1 Bonus.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Bonus Action (1/Dawn).** You can take a Bonus Action to magically coat the blade with poison. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature. That creature must succeed on a DC 15 CON saving throw or take 2d10 Poison damage and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. The weapon can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
