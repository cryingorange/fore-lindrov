# Description
This weapon is fashioned from infernal iron and traced with veins of hellfire that shed dim light in a 5-foot-radius.

<br>

# Effects
**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. 


## Activation

**Activation.** While holding this magic weapon, you can take a Bonus Action and use a command word to cause flames to engulf the damage-dealing part of the weapon. 

These flames shed [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Bright Light\|Bright Light]] in a 40-foot radius and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Light/Dim Light\|Dim Light]] for an additional 40 feet. While the weapon is ablaze, it deals Fire damage only. The flames last until you take a Bonus Action to issue the command again or until you drop, stow, or sheathe the weapon.

**Bonus Damage.** While the weapon is ablaze, it deals extra Fire damage on a hit:
- Uncommon (1d4)
- Rare (1d6)
- Very Rare (2d6)

**Hellfire.** Fire Damage dealt by this weapon ignores Fire Resistance and Immunity against Non-Fiends.
