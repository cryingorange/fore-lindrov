> The Bloody End

# Description
This greatsword boasts a brutal and jagged silhouette. The heavy blade is split down the center, revealing a core of pulsing, molten fire that bleeds outward to illuminate its razor-sharp edges. 

The crossguard is cast in the grim visage of a horned fiend, while the thick hilt is scarred with glowing, infernal runes that burn with an inner light.The weapon radiates an unnatural, blistering heat and carries the faint, ever-present scent of sulfur and ash.

# Effects

## General
- **Intimidation.** While holding [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/The Bloody End (Dormant)\|The Bloody End]], you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on CHA (Intimidation) checks. 
- **Bonus (+1).** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
	- Additionally, this weapon deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit. 
- **Potent Fire.** Fire Damage dealt by this weapon ignores [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immunity]]. 

<br>

## Spellcasting

- **Spells.** While holding the[[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/The Bloody End (Dormant)\|The Bloody End]], you can cast the following spells (save DC 13) from it: 
	- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Charm Person\|Charm Person]]
	- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Dominate Person\|Dominate Person]]
	- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Fear\|Fear]]

Once you use the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/The Bloody End (Dormant)\|The Bloody End]] to cast a spell, you can't cast a spell again from it until the next Dawn.

<br>

## Adamantine
- **Adamantine Weapon.** The Bloody End is considered an [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Weapons/Adamantine Weapon\|Adamantine Weapon]].
- **Non Magical Bonus (+1).** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, this bonus cannot be suppressed by effects such as [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/8th Level/Antimagic Field\|Antimagic Field]].
	- You may choose to use either this Bonus or the weapon's innate magical bonus when attacking with it.

<br>

## Damage Threshold & Objects

**Ignores Damage Threshold.** Damage from this weapon ignores [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Damage Threshold\|Damage Threshold]].

**Siege Weapon.** Whenever this weapon or piece of ammunition hits an object, the hit is a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]].
