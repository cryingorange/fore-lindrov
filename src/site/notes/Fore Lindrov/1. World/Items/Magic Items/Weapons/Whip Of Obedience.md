# Overview
> **Attunement:** No
> **Rarity:** Uncommon 
> **Weight:** 3 lb 

# Effects
**Slaver's Weapon.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on attack rolls against creatures with the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition.

When you deal damage to creatures with the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] with this weapon, the target takes an extra 1d6 Psychic damage. 

**Painful Strike (1/Dawn).** When you hit a creature with the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition with this weapon, you can force it to make a CON Saving Throw against the Ability Score DC of the ability used in the attack.

On a failure, the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]] condition until the end of its next turn and this property can't be used again until the next Dawn.
