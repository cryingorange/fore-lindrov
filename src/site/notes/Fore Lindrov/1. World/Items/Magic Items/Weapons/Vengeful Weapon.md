# Effects
**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. 


## Vengeful Curse

**Charges.** The weapon has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at Dawn. While holding the weapon, you can expend 1 charge to cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Hunter's Mark\|Hunter's Mark]] (5th Level).

**Advantage.** When attacking a target with this weapon that is affected by [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Hunter's Mark\|Hunter's Mark]] casted from this weapon, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on the attack roll. You can only benefit from this once per turn.
