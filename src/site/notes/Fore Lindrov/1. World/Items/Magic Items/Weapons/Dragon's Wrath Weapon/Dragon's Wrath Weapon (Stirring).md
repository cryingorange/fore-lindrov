## Description
This weapon is decorated with dragon heads, claws, wings, scales, or Draconic letters.

## Effect

This weapon deals either Acid, Fire, Cold, Lightning or Poison damage depending on what type of Dragon's Breath it has absorbed.

### Slumbering
Whenever you roll a 20 on your attack roll with this weapon, each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 5 damage of the type dealt by the dragon's breath weapon.

### Stirring

**Bonus.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made using the weapon. On a hit, the weapon deals an extra 1d6 damage of the type dealt by the dragon's breath weapon.
