> Bane Of The First Men

# Description
This ancient stone axe is etched with ancient runes, that glow red, with strange, unknown symbols along its haft. A Carnelian with a Giant [Rune](#[^giant-rune]) is fitted into the hilt.

<br>

# Effects
## General
**Bonus.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Constitution Modifier.** You can add your CON modifier (minimum of +1) to the damage rolls of attacks made with this weapon.

**Giant's Slayer.** When you hit a Giant with this weapon, the Giant takes an extra 2d6 Slashing damage and must succeed on a DC 15 STR saving throw or have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition.

## Special Actions
**Invoking the Rune (1/Dawn).** When you target a creature with an attack using this weapon, you can invoke the sword's rune, causing it to flare with crimson light and infusing your attack with bloodthirsty precision, and activate one or more of the following properties:
1.  **Hit Dice.** You spend and roll one of your unspent Hit Dice and add the number rolled to the attack roll. You can choose to invoke the rune after rolling the d20.
	- If this attack hits, you can also spend and roll any number of your unspent Hit Dice and add the total rolled to the weapon's damage. 
2.  **Giant's Bane.** If the target is a Giant, it must also make a DC 15 CHA saving throw or subtract 1d4 from its attack rolls and saves. 
	- The Giant can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns to end the effect. After 1 minute, it automatically succeeds.

Once the rune has been invoked, it can't be invoked again until the next Dawn.
