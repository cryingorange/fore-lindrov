# Drawn

## Not Infected
**Bonus.** If your body is not infected with the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]], you gain a 1d4 bonus to any Arcana, Insight, or Religion checks you make.

## Infected
**Warlock Cantrips.** If your body is infected with the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]], your symbiotic relationship with the fungus begins. You learn 2 Warlock Cantrips of your choice, using INT, CHA, or WIS (choose one when you first receive this carving) as your spellcasting ability for these spells.

# Tattooed
## Not Infected
**Bonus To Checks.** If your body is not infected with the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/Cordyceps Sinensis (Weapon)/Cordyceps Sinensis (Weapon, Dormant)\|Cordyceps Sinensis (Weapon, Dormant)]], you gain a 1d8 bonus to any INT (Arcana), WIS (Insight), or INT (Religion) checks you make. 

**Bonus to Saving Throws.** In addition, you gain a 1d4 bonus to all WIS, INT and CHA Saving Throws you make. This bonus increases to 1d8 on Saving Throws against [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Madness\|Madness]].

## Infected
If your body is infected with the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]], your symbiotic relationship with the fungus begins.

**Warlock Cantrips.** You learn 2 Warlock Cantrips of your choice, using INT, WIS or CHA (choose one when you first receive this carving) as your spellcasting ability for these spells. 

**Warlock Invocation.** In addition, you learn a Warlock Invocation of your choice for which you meet the prerequisites (you can treat invocations that require a certain Warlock Level as if they instead require a certain character level). When you gain a level, you can replace this invocation with another that you meet the prerequisites for.

# Scarified
**Death.** When this carving is completed, you die. 
## Infected
If your body is infected with the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]], it wipes this understanding away from your brain.

**Weapon.** The Cordyceps Sinensis becomes a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/Cordyceps Sinensis (Weapon)/Cordyceps Sinensis (Weapon, Dormant)\|Weapon]] for you. 

**Resurrection.** If you were not dead for longer than 100 years, you come back to life over the course of an hour. 

The Carving closes all wounds, neutralizes any Poison, cures all diseases, and lifts any curses that were affecting you when you died. The fungus replaces damaged or missing organs or limbs. The more tissue you were missing, the more fungal your appearance.

**Warlock Cantrips.** You learn 2 Warlock Cantrips of your choice, using INT, CHA, or WIS (choose one when you first receive this carving) as your spellcasting ability for these spells. 

**Warlock Invocation.** You learn a Warlock Invocation of your choice for which you meet the prerequisites (you can treat invocations that require a certain warlock level as if they instead require a certain character level). When you gain a level, you can replace this invocation with another that you meet the prerequisites for.

**Pseudo-Immortality.** If you are reduced to 0 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]], you do not fall [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Unconscious\|Unconscious]]. You still make [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Death Saving Throw\|Death Saving Throws]] and can be stabilized as normal. If you are stable for 1 minute, you regain 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]].

If you die and haven't taken Fire or Radiant damage within the last minute, you come back to life with 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] after an hour. If you are exposed to Fire or Radiant damage during that period, you do not resurrect.
