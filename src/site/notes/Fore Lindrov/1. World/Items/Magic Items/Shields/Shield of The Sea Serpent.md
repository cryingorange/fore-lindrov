> Shield of the Sea Serpent

# Description

Crafted from the iridescent hide of a deep-sea predator, this shield smells of salt and brine. Its surface is studded with rock-hard barnacles, and the rim is lined with stiff, crimson dorsal spines which seem to flare when danger approaches.

<br>

# Effects

**Resistance.** While holding this Shield, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Cold damage.


**Absorb Cold (1/Dawn).** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]], when you take Cold damage, you gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immunity]] against that instance of damage, and you (2d8 + 2) [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]]. Once this [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] is used, it can't be used again until the next Dawn.

**Interpose Shield.** While holding this Shield, if you're subjected to an effect that deals Cold damage that allows you to make a saving throw to take only half damage, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw.
