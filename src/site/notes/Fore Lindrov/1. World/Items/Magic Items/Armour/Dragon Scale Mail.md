> Dragon Scale Mail

# Description
A set of scale mail made of shimmering Dragon Scales of one kind of dragon.

# Effects
**AC Bonus.** While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

**Breath Attack.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on saving throws against the breath weapons of Dragons.

**Detect Dragon.** You can focus your senses as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to discern the distance and direction to the closest dragon whose type matches the scales this armour is made of, within 30 miles of yourself. This Action can’t be used again until the next Dawn.

**Resistance.** You have Resistance to a damage type determined by the scales that this armour is made of:

|       Dragon        | Damage Type |
|:-------------------:|:-----------:|
|   Black or Copper   |    Acid     |
|   Blue or Bronze    |    Lightning     | 
|        Green        |   Poison    |
| Red, Brass, or Gold |    Fire     |
|    White, Silver    |    Cold     |
