> Erasmus' Staff

# Description
Forged from dark, weathered metal with a wrapped grip, this staff features an angular, sunburst headpiece etched with glowing emerald runes. 

Suspended weightlessly within the open frame is a cluster of faceted green crystals, humming with contained arcane power around a brilliant central shard.

# Effect

**Charges.** This staff has 20 charges and can be wielded as a magic Quarterstaff that grants a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with it. While holding this staff, you can expend charges to produce one of the following effects:

- **Magic Absorption (1 Charge).** If you succeed on a saving throw against a spell or magical effect that deals half damage on a success, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]] to expend 1 charge to instead take no damage.

- **Magical Empowerment.** Immediately after casting a spell or producing a magical ability, you can expend charges and give the spell or ability an additional effect:
	- **Twinned (3 Charges).** If the effect targets only one creature, it targets a second creature within range.
	- **Increase Duration (4 Charges).** If the effect lasts until the end or start of a creature's turn, it becomes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] (up to 1 minute).

- **Arcane Blast (9 Charges).** You can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to expend 6 charges to release a burst of arcane magic. Each creature in a 60-foot-Long, 5-foot-Wide [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Line\|Line]] must make a DEX Saving Throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature takes 26 (4d12) Force damage.

- **Powerful Strike (Up to 5 Charges).** When you hit with a melee attack using the staff, you can expend up to 5 charges. For each charge you expend, the target takes an extra 1d6 Force damage.

- **Regain Spell Slot (Up to 18 Charges).** While holding the staff, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] to regain a Spell Slot, expending a number of charges equal to twice the Level of the spell slot you regain. 

**Regaining Charges.** The staff regains 2d8 + 4 expended charges daily at Dawn.
