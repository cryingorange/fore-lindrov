# Description
This amulet bears the symbol of a deity inlaid with precious stones or metals.

# Effect
**Bonus.** While you wear the [[Holy Symbol\|Holy Symbol]], you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and the saving throw DCs of your spells:
- Uncommon (+1)
- Rare (+2)
- Very Rare (+3)

**Channel Divinity.** While you wear this amulet, you can use your Channel Divinity feature without expending one of the feature's uses. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
