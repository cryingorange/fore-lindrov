## Description
An ancient, headless canopic jar carved from sandstone and inlaid with faded gold sigils. The jar is sealed tight with pitch and wax.

## Effect
As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action, you can sprinkle a handful of dust on a corpse within 5 feet of you. Doing so causes the corpse to fall under the effects of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/7th Level/Resurrection\|Resurrection]] Spell.

The jar contains 3 handfuls of dust.
