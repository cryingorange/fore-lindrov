# Description
A Spell Gem contains arcane spells stored within its body. 


# Details

## Casting

Casting the spell by using the Spell Gem requires the spell's normal casting time. Once the spell is cast, the Gem crumbles to dust. If the casting is interrupted, the Gem is lost.

## DC

If the spell requires a saving throw or an attack roll, the spell save DC uses the same DC and attack bonus as your Spellcasting Feature.
