> Yeti Eye

# Description
This blue, baseball sized glowing eyeball is cold to the touch, and has a strange, wet membrane encased around it.

<br>

# Effect
While holding the eyeball, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to point the eyeball at a creature you can see within 30 feet. On a failure, the target has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]] condition until the start of your next turn. On a success, the target is [[Immune\|Immune]] to the effects of a Yeti Eye (but not abominable yetis) for 1 hour.

Once a creature fails the saving throw against this effect, the eye permanently loses all magical properties.
