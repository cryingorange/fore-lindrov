## Effect
This magic item replaces a lost limb—a hand, an arm, a foot, a leg, or a similar body part. While the prosthetic is attached, it functions identically to the part it replaces. You can detach or reattach it as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, and it can't be removed against your will while you are alive.

> **Multiple Attunement**
> Multiple Prosthetic Limbs take up only one Attunement Slot.
