## Description
This cylindrical object, roughly 3 feet long and 1 foot wide, is forged from interlocking plates of dark adamantine alloy. Faint runes spiral along its surface, pulsing with a slow blue-white light. It hums with barely restrained energy, vibrating faintly in your hands and radiating warmth and a faint smell of ozone. Though heavy, it is manageable for one person to carry.

## Effect
As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action while holding the core, you can activate it and choose one of the following effects: 

**1. Regain Spell Slots.** You recover any number of expended spell slots of any level of your choice. When you do so, you must succeed on a CON Saving Throw of DC (15 + the combined level of spell slots recovered).  On a failure, you gain levels of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] equal to half the combined level of spell slots recovered.

**2. Supercharge Self.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls, and your attacks deal an additional 2d8 Force damage for 1 minute. At the start of each of your turns, you must succeed on a DC 15 CON Saving Throw or gain one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level.

**3. Shatter Core.** The core is destroyed and releases its magic in an explosion that fills a 30-foot-~={blue}**Emanation**=~ originating from itself. Each creature in the emanation takes 10d8 × 6 Force damage. If this damage reduces it to 0 HP, it and everything nonmagical it is wearing and carrying are disintegrated into gray dust. 

This damage can destroy creations of magical force.
