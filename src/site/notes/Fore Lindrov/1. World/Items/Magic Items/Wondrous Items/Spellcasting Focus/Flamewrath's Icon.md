# Effect
**Bonus.** You can use the flamewrath's icon as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Spellcasting Focus\|Spellcasting Focus]] for your spells while wearing or holding it, and you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your spells. 

**Ignore Resistance.** If you use the icon as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Spellcasting Focus\|Spellcasting Focus]], spells you cast ignore Fire Resistance.
