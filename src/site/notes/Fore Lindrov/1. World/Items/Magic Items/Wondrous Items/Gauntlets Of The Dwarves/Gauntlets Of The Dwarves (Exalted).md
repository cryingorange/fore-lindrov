> Gauntlets Of The Dwarves

# Description
These magnificent, heavy plate gauntlets are crafted from dark iron and banded in intricate, glowing dwarven brass, vibrating with raw smith-magic. Each forearm features a massive, polished obsidian orb inlaid with an illuminating gold rune.

<br>

# Properties
- **Random Properties.** The Gauntlets have the following random properties:
	- 2 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables#Minor Beneficial Properties\|Minor Beneficial Properties]]
	- 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables#Major Beneficial Properties\|Major Beneficial Properties]]
	- 2 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables#Minor Detrimental Properties\|Minor Detrimental Properties]]

The detrimental properties of the Gauntlets are suppressed while they are attuned to a Dwarf.

<br>

# Effects
## General
### Metal
*A Dwarf is hard-edged, tempered, and sharp. Like a forged Blade.*
- **Moradin's Metalwork.** You gain Proficiency in [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Tools/Smith's Tools\|Smith's Tools]], if you are already Proficient, you gain Expertise. 
- **Smith's Touch.** A Battleaxe, Greataxe, Warhammer or Maul wielded by you gains a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it.
	- Additionally, it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Thrown\|Thrown]] property with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet while being wielded by you. 
	- Immediately after hitting or missing, the weapon flies back to your hand.

<br>

### Stone
*A Dwarf is legendarily stubborn, unyielding and unrelenting. Like a Stone.* 
- **Moradin's Stonemasonry.** You gain Proficiency in [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Tools/Mason's Tools\|Mason's Tools]], if you are already Proficient, you gain Expertise.
- **Rock-like Grip.** You can add your CON Modifier to STR Ability Checks and Saving Throws.
	- Additionally, the gauntlets can't be unwillingly removed from you while you're attuned to it. 

<br>

### Ale
*A Dwarf is  jolly, festive, and fiercely uplifting. Like a strong Ale.
- **Moradin's Brewery.** You gain Proficiency in [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Tools/Brewer's Supplies\|Brewer's Supplies]], if you are already Proficient, you gain Expertise.
- **Reveler's Hold.** A pint of ale, beer, or mead held by you gains magical potency while it is in your hand.
	- As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]], you can drink from a pint or administer it to another creature within 5 feet of yourself.
	- The creature that drinks the alcohol gains [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
  
<br>
