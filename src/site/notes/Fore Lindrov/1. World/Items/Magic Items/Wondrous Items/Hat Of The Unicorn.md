> Hat Of The Unicorn


# Description
A woolly white cowl complete with a golden spiral horn and a multicolored mane. 

# Effect

- **Protection.** While wearing this cowl, you are under the effects of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Protection from Evil and Good\|Protection from Evil and Good]] (Fiends) Spell.
- **Charges.** This hat has 10 Charges, while wearing the hat , you can cast one of the spells on the following table from it, using your spell save DC. The table indicates how many charges you must expend to cast the spell.

| Spell                    | Cost |
|:------------------------ | :----: |
| [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Cure Wounds\|Cure Wounds]]          | 1    |
|[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Protection from Evil and Good\|Protection from Evil and Good]]|2|
| [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Dispel Evil and Good\|Dispel Evil and Good]] | 3    |

- **Expended Charges.** If you expend the last charge, the hat becomes a Mundane item and loses all its magical properties.
