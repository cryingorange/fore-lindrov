> Shard of the Ise Rune

# Description
This shard of ice is long and slender, roughly the size of a dagger. The [Ise](/img/user/Z_Attachments/Pictures/Ise%20Rune%20(Pic).png) (ice) Rune glows within it.

<br>

# Effects
The shard has the following properties, which work only while it's on your person:

**Frost Friend.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance]] to Fire damage.

**Frigid Touch (1/SR).** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, you can touch a body of water and freeze the water in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] around the spot you touched. Once you use this property, you can't use it again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

**Icy Mantle (1/SR).** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, you can touch yourself or another creature with water on your finger. The water creates an icy mantle of protection. 

The next time within the next minute that the target takes Bludgeoning, Slashing, or Piercing damage, that damage is reduced to 0, and the mantle is destroyed. 

Once you use this property, you can't use it again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

**Winters Howl (1/SR).** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, you can cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Sleet Storm\|Sleet Storm]] (spell save DC 17 or your Spellcasting DC). You regain this ability after you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].

<br>

# Transfer Magic

**Gift of Frost.** You can transfer the shard's magic to a nonmagical item—a cloak or a pair of boots-by tracing the Ise rune there with your finger. 

The transfer takes 8 hours of work that requires the two items to be within 5 feet of each other. At the end of the 8 hours, the shard is destroyed and the rune appears in blue on the chosen item, which gains a benefit based on its form:

- **Cloak.** The cloak is now a Rare magic item that requires Attunement. While wearing it, you have Resistance to Fire damage, and you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on DEX (Stealth) checks made while in snowy terrain.
    
- **Boots.** The pair of boots is now a Rare magic item that requires Attunement. While wearing it, you ignore [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] while walking, and you can walk on water.
