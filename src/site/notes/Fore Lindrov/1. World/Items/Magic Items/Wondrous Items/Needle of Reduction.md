# Effect
- **Charges.** This needle has 6 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at Dawn.

- **Reducing Stab.** When you take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] Action, you can make one of the attack rolls using the needle as an [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Improvised Weapons\|Improvised Weapon]].
	- You can use your STR or DEX modifier for this attack.
	- On a hit, you can expend one charge to cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Enlarge-Reduce\|Enlarge-Reduce]] (Reduce Only), and its duration increases to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]], up to 1 Hour.
