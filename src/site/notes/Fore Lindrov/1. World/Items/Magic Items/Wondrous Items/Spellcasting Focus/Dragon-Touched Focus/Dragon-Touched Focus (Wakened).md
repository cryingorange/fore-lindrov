## Description
This wondrous item can be a scepter, an orb, an amulet, a crystal, or another finely crafted object. It typically incorporates imagery of dragons' wings, claws, teeth, or scales.

## Effect
### Slumbering
You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on Initiative rolls. While you are holding the focus, it can function as a spellcasting focus for all your spells.

### Stirring
The focus gains an additional property determined by the family of the dragon in whose hoard it became Stirring:

- **Chromatic.** Whenever you use a spell slot to cast a spell that deals Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison damage, roll a d6, and you gain a bonus equal to the number rolled to one of the spell's damage rolls.

- **Gem.** Whenever you use a spell slot to cast a spell, you can immediately [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Teleportation\|Teleport]] to an unoccupied space you can see within 15 feet of you.

- **Metallic.** When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes a saving throw, you can use your Reaction to give that creature Advantage on the saving throw.

### Wakened
While you are holding a Wakened focus, you can use it to cast certain spells. Once the item is used to cast a given spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn. The spells are determined by the family of the dragon in whose hoard it became Wakened.

- **Chromatic.** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Hold Monster\|Hold Monster]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Rime's Binding Ice\|Rime's Binding Ice]]

- **Gem.** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Rary's Telepathic Bond\|Rary's Telepathic Bond]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/4th Level/Raulothim's Psychic Lance\|Raulothim's Psychic Lance]]

- **Metallic.** [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/6th Level/Fizban's Platinum Shield\|Fizban's Platinum Shield]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Legend Lore\|Legend Lore]]
