> Black Dragon Mask

# Description
This horned mask of glossy ebony has horns and a skull-like mien. The mask reshapes to fit a wearer attuned to it.

# Effect

## Exalted

**Random Properties.** The Black Dragon Mask has the following random properties:
- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables\|2 Minor Beneficial Properties]]
- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables\|2 Minor Detrimental Properties]] 
- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables\|1 Major Beneficial Property]]

The detrimental properties of the Dragon Mask are suppressed while the Mask is attuned to a Chromatic Dragon of the same colour.

**Water Breathing.** You can breathe underwater.

**Dragon Sight.** You gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]] out to 60 feet. If you already have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]], its range increases by 60 feet.

**Damage Absorption.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immunity]] to Acid damage and you regain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] equal to half of any Acid damage you are dealt.

**Dragon Tongue.** You can speak and understand Draconic. You also have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on any Charisma check you make against Black Dragons.

**Draconic Majesty.** While you are wearing no armor, you can add your Charisma bonus to your Armor Class.

**Dragon Breath.** If you have a breath weapon that requires rest to recharge, it gains a recharge of 6. Additionally, you gain a +3 bonus the Saving Throw DC of your breath weapon, Frightening Presence and Acrid Rage where applicable.

**[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Combat/Legendary Resistance\|Legendary Resistance]] (1/Day).** If you fail a saving throw, you can choose to succeed instead.
