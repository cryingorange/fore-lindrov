> Black Dragon Mask

# Description
This horned mask of glossy ebony has horns and a skull-like mien. The mask reshapes to fit a wearer attuned to it.

# Effect

## Dormant

**Random Properties.** The Black Dragon Mask has the following random properties:
- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables\|1 Minor Beneficial Property]]
- [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Artifact Properties Tables\|1 Minor Detrimental Property]]

The detrimental properties of the Dragon Mask are suppressed while the Mask is attuned to a Chromatic Dragon of the same colour.

**Water Breathing.** You can breathe underwater.

**Dragon Sight.** You gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]] out to 60 feet. If you already have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Senses/Darkvision\|Darkvision]], its range increases by 60 feet.

**Damage Absorption.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Acid damage. If you already have damage [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Acid from another source, you gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immunity]] to Acid damage.

**Dragon Tongue.** You can speak and understand Draconic.

**Dragon Breath.** If you have a breath weapon that requires rest to recharge, it gains a recharge of 6. Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus the Saving Throw DC of your breath weapon, Frightening Presence and Acrid Rage where applicable.
