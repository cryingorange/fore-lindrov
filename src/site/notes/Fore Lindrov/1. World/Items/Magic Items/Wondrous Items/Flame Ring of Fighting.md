# Description
This ring is crafted from a dark, volcanic obsidian, polished to a mirror sheen. Embedded within its band is a single, perfectly cut [Rune](#[^ild-rune]) that glows. 

The metal around the rune is warm to the touch, and it carries the faint scent of a dying campfire.

# Effect
You can take a Bonus Action to cause a stream of molten fire to spew from the Rune, forming into a +1 Melee or Ranged weapon of your choice. The weapon uses the same statistics of a typical weapon of its type, but deals Fire damage instead.

[^ild-rune]: **DC 15 History Check**
	Identify that this is the Giant Rune for "Fire".
