# Effect
**Roll.** This heavy cloth bag contains 2d4 flat runestones when found, you can determine the runes by rolling on the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Bag of Runestones#Runestone Table\|#Runestone Table]].

**Bonus Action.** You can take a Bonus Action to pull out one of the Runestones and trigger its effect.
