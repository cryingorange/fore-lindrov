# Overview

[Theme Song](https://youtu.be/gzf-64N6wcI?si=Mq4roEiWElUhmW30)

Also known as, "乾坤之心石" (Qián Kūn Zhī Xīn Shí)

##  Description
This stone heart is chiselled to a strange almost life-like perfection, to the point that it looks almost like it would start beating and smells deeply of earth and clay.

About five feet across, the heart looms nearly as large as a person. Faint channels of gold and cerulean light pulse within its veins, like the echo of long-forgotten life. Drawn across it are unintelligible Giant Runes that emit a blue glow. 

<br>

## Sentience

**Statistics.** The Heart of Heaven is a sentient Lawful Neutral artifact with an Intelligence of 16, a Wisdom of 22, and a Charisma of 24. It has hearing and Darkvision out to a range of 120 feet.

The weapon communicates telepathically with its wielder and can speak, read, and understand Common and Giant.

**Personality.** The heart pushes its wielder to break restraints, defy authority, and reject any force that would limit choice, movement, or joy. 

It encourages mischief, harmless pranks, bold risks, and juvenile mockery of anyone who takes themselves too seriously. Authority figures, rigid hierarchies, and joyless tyrants draw its immediate scorn.
