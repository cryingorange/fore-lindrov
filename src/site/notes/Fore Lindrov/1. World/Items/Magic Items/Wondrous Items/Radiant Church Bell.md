> [!infobox]+
> # Radiant Church Bell
>   ![Radiant Church Bell.jpg\|200](/img/user/Z_Attachments/Pictures/Radiant%20Church%20Bell.jpg)



# Description
This beautiful bell is encrusted with Moongold and shimmers unusually in the moonlight and blessed by the hand of the [[Radiant Church\|Radiant Church]].

# Effect
As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action, you ring the bell. Each creature in a 30-foot-[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Emanation\|Emanation]] originating from the bell has one of the following conditions ended on it, roll a d6 if more than one of the possible conditions are affecting the target:

| d6  | Condition Ended |
| --- | --------------- |
| 1   | [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]]     |
| 2   | [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]]  |
| 3   | [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]]    |
| 4   | [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Petrified\|Petrified]]   |
| 5   | [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Stunned\|Stunned]]     |
| 6   | Your Choice     |

Once this property is used, the bell becomes a nonmagical [[Bell\|Bell]] and must be blessed by a Priest of the Radiant Church casting [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Ceremony\|Ceremony]] to become magical.
