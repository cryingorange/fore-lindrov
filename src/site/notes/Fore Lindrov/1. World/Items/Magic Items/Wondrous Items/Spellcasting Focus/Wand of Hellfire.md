# Description
This wicked wand is crafted from dark, gnarled wood and wrapped in scorched leather. Its head is a fearsome, stylized skull of a ram with large, curved horns whose eyes glow an infernal orange.

Etched into the leather wrapping of the shaft are glowing etchings that pulse with heat.

# Effect

## Bonus

**Bonus.** While holding this wand, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and the saving throw DCs of your spells:

- Uncommon (+1)
- Rare (+2)
- Very Rare (+3)

**Hellfire.**  If you use the wand as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Spellcasting Focus\|Spellcasting Focus]], spells you cast ignore Fire Resistance and Immunity against Non-Fiends.

## Charges

**Charges.** This wand has a number of charges:
- Uncommon (3 Charges)
- Rare (5 Charges)
- Very Rare (7 Charges)

While holding it, if you cast a spell that deals damage, you spend one charge to change the damage to Fire damage, and deal an additional 1d6 Fire damage per charge used.

**Regaining Charges.** The wand regains expended charges daily at Dawn:
- Uncommon (d3)
- Rare (1d4 + 1)
- Very Rare (1d6 + 1)
