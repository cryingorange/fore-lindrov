## Description
A brass bottle painted with gold trimmings.

## Effect
When you take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to remove the stopper of this painted brass bottle, a cloud of thick smoke flows out of it. At the end of your turn, the smoke disappears with a flash of harmless fire, and an [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Elemental/Efreeti\|Efreeti]] appears in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you.

The first time the bottle is opened, the DM rolls on the following table to determine what happens.

| 1d10 | Effect                                                                                                                |
|:----:| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1   | The [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Elemental/Efreeti\|Efreeti]] attacks you. After fighting for 5 rounds, the [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Elemental/Efreeti\|Efreeti]] disappears, and the bottle loses its magic. |
| 2–9  |  The [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Elemental/Efreeti\|Efreeti]] understands your languages and obeys your commands for 1 hour, after which it returns to the bottle, and a new stopper contains it. The stopper can't be removed for 24 hours. The next two times the bottle is opened, the same effect occurs. If the bottle is opened a fourth time, the [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Elemental/Efreeti\|Efreeti]] escapes and disappears, and the bottle loses its magic.                                                                                                                     |
|  10  |   	The [[Fore Lindrov/3. Stat Blocks/Elemental/Efreeti\|Efreeti]] understands your languages and can cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/Wish\|Wish]] once for you. It disappears when it grants the wish or after 1 hour, and the bottle loses its magic.                                                                                                                    |
