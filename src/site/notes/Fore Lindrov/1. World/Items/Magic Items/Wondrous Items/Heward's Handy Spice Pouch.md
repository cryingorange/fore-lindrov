## Description
A plain leather belt pouch that appears to be empty.

## Effect
- **Charges.** This magic items has 10 Charges and regains ~={blue}1d6 + 4=~ expended charges daily at Dawn.
- **Magic Action.** You can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to expend 1 charge while naming any nonmagical food seasoning (such as salt, pepper, saffron or cilantro) and remove a pinch of the desired seasoning from the pouch.
- **Uses.** A pinch is enouhg to season a single meal.
