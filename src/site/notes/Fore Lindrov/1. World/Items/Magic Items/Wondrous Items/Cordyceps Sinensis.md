# Effect
**Enduring Life.** While infected with the Cordyceps Sinensis, when you are reduced to 0 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] or killed outright, you can drop to 1 [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/HP\|HP]] instead. Once this property has been used, it can't be used again until the next Dawn.

**Parasite Warlock.** If you are a Parasite Patron Warlock, it becomes a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Weapons/Cordyceps Sinensis (Weapon)/Cordyceps Sinensis (Weapon, Dormant)\|Weapon]] for you. You can use your choice of CON or CHA instead of STR or DEX for the attack and damage rolls. 

# Acquisition
- **Methods.** To gain the benefits of the Cordyceps Sinensis, you must ingest it, or graft it underneath your skin. 
	- For 24 hours, the fungus will incubate within you; during that time, if you swallow liquid flames or light the grafted area ablaze, you will kill the parasite. 

- **24 Hours.** After 24 hours have passed, the only way to remove the parasite is with a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Remove Curse\|Remove Curse]] spell cast at 6th level or higher. If you receive [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Eldritch Carvings/Nawre's Blessing\|Nawre's Blessing]], only a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/Wish\|Wish]] spell or other similar magic will remove the fungus.

# Curse
**Death.** If you die or use Enduring Life when killed outright while infected with the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Cordyceps Sinensis\|Cordyceps Sinensis]], the parasite gains more control over your body; if you die three times, you become an NPC under the GM's control.
