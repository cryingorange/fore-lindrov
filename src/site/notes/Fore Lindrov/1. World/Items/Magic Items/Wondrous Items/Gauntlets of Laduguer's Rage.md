## Description
These gauntlets are carved from seamless, featureless gray stone, its plates are so finely fitted they move like living muscle and the gauntlets feel warm to the touch, with a vague smell of soot around it.

## Effect
**Fury.** You gain the power of never-ending fury. After you make a melee attack, you automatically enter a Special Fury for 1 minute. While furious, you can't cast spells, can't verbally communicate, and are Immune to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] conditions.

**Healing From Damage.** While in this fury, Each time you deal damage, you regain 2d8 HP and may immediately remove a condition you currently suffer from or end this fury. 

**Additional Damage.** While in this fury, you may choose one of the following features before you make a melee attack:
	1. You may spend a spell slot before you make a melee attack. Doing so causes the attack to deal an extra 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage per level of spell slot expended if the attack hits. 
	2. You may forgo the Healing from dealing damage before you make a melee attack instead losing 2d8 HP. Doing so causes the attack to deal an extra 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage, you may choose lose an additional 2d8 HP, dealing an additional 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage per 2d8 HP lost if the attack hits. 

**Duration.** Once used you may not use this fury again until you finish a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].
