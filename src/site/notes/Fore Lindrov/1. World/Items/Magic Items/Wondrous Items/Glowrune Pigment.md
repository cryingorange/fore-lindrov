## Description
This set of 1d4 + 2 small paint pots contains pigments mixed from crushed luminescent gemstones.

## Effect
>This magical paint bestows temporary magical gifts on creatures with runes drawn on their skin with this paint. One paint pot contains enough pigment to paint one rune. A creature can spend 10 minutes to paint one of the following runes onto itself or another creature:

**Life Rune.** The painted creature gains 10 Temporary Hit Points and has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Death Saving Throw\|Death Saving Throws]].

**Light Rune.** The painted creature gains Darkvision to a range of 30 feet. If the painted creature already has Darkvision from another source, the range of its Darkvision increases by 30 feet.

**Mountain Rune.** The painted creature is Immune to the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Prone\|Prone]] condition and has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on STR and CON saving throws.

**Shield Rune.** The painted creature has Advantage on DEX saving throws against effects that deal damage.

>A creature can benefit from only one painted rune at a time, so a new rune painted on a creature has no effect unless the old one is removed first. The rune's benefits last for 8 hours or until the painted creature uses its action to wipe away the rune.
