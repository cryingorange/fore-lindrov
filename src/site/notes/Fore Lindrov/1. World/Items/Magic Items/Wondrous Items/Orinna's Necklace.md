> Orinna's Necklace

# Description
This thick necklace of brass and iron bears a pendant depicting a Lightning Bolt with a dwarf's beard between its borders. Etched in Dwarvish Script is the name, "Orinna".

# Effect
**Charges.** The necklace has 6 charges and regains (1d6) charges daily at Dawn. While wearing it, you can expend a number of charges to create one of the following effects:

- **Shield (2 Charges):** You cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Shield\|Shield]]. 
- **Thunderwave (2 Charges): **You cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Thunderwave\|Thunderwave]].
- **Spell Reflection (3 Charges):** If you are targeted by a Magic Missile spell, or a spell that requires a Ranged Attack Roll, roll a 1d6. On a 1–5, you are unaffected. On a 6, you are unaffected and reflect the spell, turning the caster into the target.
