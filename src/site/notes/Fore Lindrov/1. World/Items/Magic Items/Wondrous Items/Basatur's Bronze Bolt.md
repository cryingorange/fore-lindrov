## Description
A thick, barbed crossbow bolt forged of blackened infernal iron, its head faintly glowing as if still hot from battle. The shaft is etched with the name of a demon slain in the battle that earned Basatur his title as the Hundred Demon Slayer.

## Effect
As part of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Attack\|Attack]] Action, you fire the Bolt at either an unoccupied space of your choice or at a creature to cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/6th Level/Summon Fiend\|Summon Fiend]] (7th Level, Demon Only) or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/1st Level/Hunter's Mark\|Hunter's Mark]] (7th Level). Once used, this property can't be used again until the next Dawn.
