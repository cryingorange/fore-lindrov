## Description
This Humanoid skull has the name "Shriala Mizzrym" carved unto its forehead, the emeralds in its eye sockets fit perfectly, and glow with a magical hue.

## Effect
**Charges.** The skull has 6 charges and regains ~={blue}1d6=~ expended charges daily at Dawn.

**Cast Spell.** While holding the skull, you can expend 1 charge to cast *[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/7th Level/Power Word Pain\|Power Word Pain]]* (DC 18).
