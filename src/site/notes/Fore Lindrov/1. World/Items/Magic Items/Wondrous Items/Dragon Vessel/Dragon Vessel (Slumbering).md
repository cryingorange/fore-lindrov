# Description
This vessel can be a potion bottle, drinking horn, or other container meant to hold a liquid.

# Effect
**Fill Vessel.** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]], if the vessel is empty, you can speak the command word to fill the vessel with one of the following (your choice): 

## Mundane Liquids
- Ale 
- Olive Oil 

## Healing Potions
 - [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Consumables/Potions/Potions of Healing/Potion of Healing\|Potion of Healing]]
 
## Magic Potions
 - [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Consumables/Potions/Potion of Climbing\|Potion of Climbing]]

**Time Limit.** A potion you create in this way loses its magical properties if it isn't imbibed within 24 hours.

**Once Per Dawn.** Once this property is used, it can't be used until the next Dawn.
