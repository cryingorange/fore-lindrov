> Zenora's Cradle of Creation

# Description

This organic, calcified orb pulses with a rhythmic, faint warmth, its surface a seamless fusion of bleached bone, twisted driftwood, and petrified flora. 

It is the crystallized essence and Value of the Primordial Spirit once known as Zenora.

<br>

# Effect

While holding the cradle, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action to use the essence of creation that was once Zenora to cast [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/Wish\|Wish]] from it and suffer none of the spell's stress.

Once you do so, the Cradle is destroyed and turns to dust.

<br>

>[!warning]- For DMs ONLY! (PLAYERS DO NOT CLICK)
> - **Theme.** The nature of whatever the PCs use the cradle for fit Zenora's aesthetic, that being a Forest spirit of bone, bark and moss.  
> 	- The general theme of a Cradle of Creation is that it creates things anew, it doesn't bring back what once was.
> - **True Desires.** The Wish spell only works in response to a creature's deepest, most honest emotional need.
> 	- It responds only to pure emotion, without true desire and emotionality behind the Wish, the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Magic\|Magic]] Action is wasted and the spell isn't cast.
> 
>> [!list|c-p-sm bg-plain c-black]- Example Guidelines
>> - **Resurrecting a Character.** A character could try to use this to bring a creature back to life, although not necessarily it is the 'same person'. 
>> 	- The Cradle dissolves into the ground. Roots burst from the soil and wrap around the dead body like a cocoon.
>> 	- The companion comes back, but forever changed, skin smooth, white birch, hair made of weeping willow leaves.
>> - **Empower Self.** A PC tries to empower themselves, they will be imbued with a relentless amount of Primordial Power.
>> 	- If they are a Humanoid, they could begin to turn into a Plant / Tree Person, potentially changing their race.
>> - **Advance Weapon / Item.** The Wish can also be used to enhance or push items into their next stage or to become stronger, albeit it needs to be Primordial in nature such as the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Briarheart/Briarheart (Exalted)\|Briarheart]].
