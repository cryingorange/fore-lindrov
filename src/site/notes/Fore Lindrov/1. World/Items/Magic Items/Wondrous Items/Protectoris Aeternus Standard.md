## Description
This Standard bears the Golden Cross of King [[Baldwin of Lupra\|Baldwin of Lupra]]. It acts as an inspiring symbol to all of [[Radhantia\|Radhantia]], and a banner that heralds the arrival of the [[Protectoris Aeternus\|Protectoris Aeternus]]. 

## Effect
You can spend 10 minutes inspiring your companions, shoring up their resolve to fight. When you do so, choose up to six Friendly creatures (which can include yourself) within 30 feet of you that can see or hear you and can understand you.

Each creature gains [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] equal to your Level + your CHA modifier. A creature can't gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Temporary HP\|Temporary HP]] from this item again until it has finished a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Short Rest\|Short]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Long Rest\|Long Rest]].
