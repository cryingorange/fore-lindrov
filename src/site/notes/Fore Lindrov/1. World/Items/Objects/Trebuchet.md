# Overview
A Trebuchet is a catapult that throws its payload in a high arc so it can hit targets behind walls. Loading a Trebuchet requires two [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Actions, and aiming it requires two more [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Actions. Then a crew member can take the Trebuchet Stone action.
