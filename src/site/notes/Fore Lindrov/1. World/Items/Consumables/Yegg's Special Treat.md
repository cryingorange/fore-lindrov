## Description
A chunk of dried meat with a dark, delicious looking sear.

## Effect
You can use a Bonus Action to consume this treat, choosing one of the following effects:

| d4  | Effect |
|:---:|------|
|  1  | **Cold Resistance.** You gain Resistance to the next instance of Cold damage you take within the next hour.        |
|  2  | **Advantage.** You have Advantage on the next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Test]] you make within the next hour.       |
|  3  | **Temporary HP.** You gain 5 Temporary HP that lasts until the end of your next turn.      |
|  4  | **Bonus.** You gain a 1d4 bonus on the next [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/D20 Test\|D20 Test]] you make within the next hour.       |
