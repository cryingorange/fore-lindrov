## Description
A chunk of dried meat with a dark, delicious looking sear.

## Effect
You can use a Bonus Action to consume this treat, choosing or rolling a d4 for one of the following effects:

| d4  | Effect |
|:---:|------|
|  1  | **Resistance.** You become Immune to one [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Consumables/Yegg's Magnum Opus#Damage Type Table\|damage type]] for 1 hour.        |
|  2  | **Inspiration.** You gain one Heroic Inspiration.       |
|  3  | **Aid.** You fall under the effects of *[[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Aid\|Aid]]* (3rd Level) for 1 hour.      |
|  4  | **Remove Exhaustion.** You remove one [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] level.       |
