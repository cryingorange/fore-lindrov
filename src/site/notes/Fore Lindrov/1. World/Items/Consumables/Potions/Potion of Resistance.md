## Description
The liquid is thick and viscous, swirling with a slow, hypnotic churn.

## Effect
When you drink this potion, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance]] to one type of damage for 1 hour. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table:

|  d10  | Damage Type |  d10   | Damage Type |
|:-----:|:----------- |:------:|:----------- |
| **1** | Acid        | **6**  | Necrotic    |
| **2** | Cold        | **7**  | Poison      |
| **3** | Fire        | **8**  | Psychic     |
| **4** | Force       | **9**  | Radiant     |
| **5** | Lightning   | **10** | Thunder     |
