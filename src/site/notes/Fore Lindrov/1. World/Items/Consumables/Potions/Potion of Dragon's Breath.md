# Description
The liquid within this heavy glass vial roils with a violent, shifting hue. A faint, low rumble vibrates against your palm, and a sharp, elemental tang stings your nostrils whenever the stopper is loosened.

# Effect
When you drink this potion, roll a d10 to determine the draconic essence contained within:

| d10 | Damage Type | d10 | Damage Type|
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---| 
| **1–2** | Acid | **7–8** | Lightning |
| **3–4** | Cold | **9–10** | Poison |
| **5–6** | Fire |


After drinking this potion, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] to exhale at a target within 30 feet of yourself. The target makes a DC 13 (Or you CON Save DC) DEX saving throw, taking 4d6 damage of the type rolled on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. 

The effect ends after you exhale three times or when 1 hour has passed.
