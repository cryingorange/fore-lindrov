# Description
This potion looks like liquid gold, with a single scale from a Chromatic, Gem, or Metallic Dragon suspended in it.

# Effect
 **Transformed.** When you drink this potion, you transform into an Adult Dragon of the same kind as the Dragon the scale came from. 
 
The transformation lasts for 1 hour. Any equipment you are wearing or carrying melds into your new form or falls to the ground (your choice). 
 
 **Statistics.** For the duration, you use the game statistics of the adult dragon instead of your own, but you retain your Languages, Personality, and ,emories. You can't use a dragon's Change Shape or its Legendary or Lair Actions.
