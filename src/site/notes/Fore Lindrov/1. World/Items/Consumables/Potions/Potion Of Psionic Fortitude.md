## Description
The liquid is a cold, dense mercury that refuses to splash.

## Effect
When you drink this potion, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] for 1 hour on saving throws you make to avoid or end the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Stunned\|Stunned]] condition on yourself.
