## Description
This liquid constantly shifts color and form, mimicking a miniature, roiling storm trapped inside the vial.

## Effect
When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/9th Level/Shapechange\|Shapechange]] spell for 1 minute (no [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]] required).
