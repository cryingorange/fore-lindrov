# Description
This potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colors.

# Effect
When you drink this potion, you gain a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Climb Speed\|Climb Speed]] equal to your Speed for 1 hour. During this time, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on STR (Athletics) checks to [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Climbing\|Climb]].

# Identification
DC 13 Arcana, Investigation or History Check (Herbalism Kit or Alchemist's Supplies)
