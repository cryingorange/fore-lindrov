# Description
This potion is a clear, brilliant ruby color, yet it possesses the weight of heavy mercury. A single, bright spark of light drifts slowly through the center.

# Effect
When you drink this potion, it removes any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels or Diseases you have, ends the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition on you and any severed body parts immediately regrow.

Additionally, you remove any of the following effects: 
- Any reduction to one of the target's ability scores.
- Any reduction to the target's HP maximum.
