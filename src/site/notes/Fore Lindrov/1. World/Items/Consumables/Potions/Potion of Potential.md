# Description
This potion shimmers like liquid silver, thick and warm to the touch.

# Effect
**Strong & Charismatic.** When you drink this potion, your Strength and Charisma scores both become 22 for 1 minute.

**Bodily Perfection.** You fall under the effects of Greater Restoration, remove any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels you have and end the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition on yourself. For the next 1 minute, you regain the maximum number of HP from any form of Hit Point Recovery.

**Spellcasting Components.** For the duration, all spells you cast require only Somatic Components. 

**Body Changed.** Your physique shifts to an idealized peak form, with a sharper jawline and defined musculature.

**Doesn't Affect Everyone.** This potion doesn't work on creatures that do not believe in themselves.
