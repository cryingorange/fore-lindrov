## Description
The potion's orange liquid flickers, and smoke fills the top of the container and wafts out whenever it is opened.

## Effect
After drinking this potion, you can take a Bonus Action to exhale fire at a target within 30 feet of yourself. The target makes a DC 13 (or your CON Save DC) DEX Saving Throw, taking 4d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The effect ends after you exhale the fire three times or when 1 hour has passed.
