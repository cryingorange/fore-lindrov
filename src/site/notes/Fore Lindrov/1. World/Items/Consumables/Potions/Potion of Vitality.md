## Description
This potion's crimson liquid regularly pulses with dull light, calling to mind a heartbeat.

## Effect
When you drink this potion, it removes any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Exhaustion\|Exhaustion]] levels you have and ends the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition on you. For the next 24 hours, you regain the maximum number of HP for any Hit Point Die you spend.
