## Description
A bottle with a strong blue liquid within that glimmers when agitated.

## Effect
As a Bonus Action, you can drink it or administer it to another creature within 5 feet of yourself. The creature that drinks the magical blue fluid in this vial chooses expended spell slots to recover. The spell slots can have a combined level equal to no more than 4.

For example, if you drink a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Consumables/Potions/Potions of Spell Recovery/Potion of Greater Spell Recovery\|Potion of Greater Spell Recovery]], you can recover up to four levels' worth of spell slots, regaining either two level 2 spell slot or four level 1 spell slots.
