## Description
This potion's container looks empty but feels as though it holds liquid.

## Effect
When you drink the potion, you have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Invisible\|Invisible]] condition for 1 hour. The effect ends early if you make an attack roll, deal damage, or cast a spell.
