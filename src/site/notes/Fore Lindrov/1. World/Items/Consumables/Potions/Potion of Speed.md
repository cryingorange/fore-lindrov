## Description
This potion's yellow fluid is streaked with black and swirls on its own.

## Effect
When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/3rd Level/Haste\|Haste]] spell for 3 Rounds (no Concentration required). The spell ends at the end of the third Round.

## Identification
DC 20 Arcana, History Check (Herbalism Kit or Alchemist's Supplies)
