## Description
A bottle with a strong red liquid within that glimmers when agitated.

## Effect
As a Bonus Action, you can drink it or administer it to another creature within 5 feet of yourself. The creature that drinks the magical red fluid in this vial regains 4d4 + 4 Hit Points.

## Identification
DC 15 Arcana, Nature, History  Check (Herbalism Kit or Alchemist's Supplies)
