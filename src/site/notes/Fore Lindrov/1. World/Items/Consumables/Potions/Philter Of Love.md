## Description
A bottle with a rose-hued, effervescent liquid contains one [easy-to-miss](#[^passive-perception]) bubble shaped like a heart.

## Effect
The next time you see a creature within 10 minutes after drinking this philter, you are charmed by that creature and have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] condition for 1 hour.

## Identification
DC 15 Arcana, History Check (Herbalism Kit or Alchemist's Supplies)
