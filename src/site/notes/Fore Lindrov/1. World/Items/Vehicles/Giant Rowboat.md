## Move (Oars)
**Move (Requires Oars).** The rowboat moves up to its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]], each one foot turn costs one foot of movement. If the creature is **Large** or smaller, it must succeed on a STR (Athletics) check or have the appropriate STR Score to move the boat:
- Medium Creature: DC 15 STR (Athletics) Check or STR Score 20
-  Large Creature: DC 10 STR (Athletics) Check or STR Score 15.
