# Actions
## Move (Oars)
**Move (Utilize Action, requires Oars).** The punt moves up to its Speed, each one foot turn costs one foot of movement. If the creature is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a STR (Athletics) check or have the appropriate STR Score to move the boat:  
- Medium Creature: DC 15 STR (Athletics) Check or STR Score 21  
- Large Creature: DC 10 STR (Athletics) Check or STR Score 16.

<br>

## Move (Sails)
**Move (Utilize Action, requires Sails).** The punt moves up to 40 feet while sailing against the wind or 60 feet while sailing with the wind. If the creature is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a STR (Athletics) check or have the appropriate STR Score to move the boat:
 - Medium Creature: DC 15 STR (Athletics) Check or STR Score 21
 - Large Creature: DC 10 STR (Athletics) Check or STR Score 16.
