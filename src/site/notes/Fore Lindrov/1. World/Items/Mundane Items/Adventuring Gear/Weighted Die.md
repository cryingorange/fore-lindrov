# Effect
When you obtain this 6-sided die, roll on the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Adventuring Gear/Weighted Die#Weighted Face Table\|#Weighted Face Table]] to determine the side the die is weighted towards. Whenever you roll on the Heavy Face, it is treated as the Target Face.

**Change Weight.** Using [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Tools/Woodcarver's Tools\|Woodcarver's Tools]], [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Tools/Mason's Tools\|Mason's Tools]], or [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Tools/Potter's Tools\|Potter's Tools]] depending on the die's material, you can spend one hour to change the weighted side with a successful DC 15 Check.

**Identify.** A creature can take the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Study\|Study]] Action to make a a DC 15 DEX, INT or WIS (Investigation) or WIS (Perception) Check to identify the die is weighted.
