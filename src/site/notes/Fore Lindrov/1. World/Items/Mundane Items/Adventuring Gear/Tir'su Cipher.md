# Description
This series of interlocking obsidian rings is etched with the circular, needle-like script of the Githyanki. 

# Effect
As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Study\|Study]] Action, you can use this cipher to decipher any Tir'su writing of the Githyanki if you succeed on a DC 15 Investigation Check.

The amount of time you spend on examining the writing determines what you decipher:

| Time       | Result                                                                                           |
| :----------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1 Minute**   | You recognize single words or names.                                                              |
| **10 Minutes** | You understand the general intent of the writing (e.g., a warning, a hit list, or a map legend). |
| **1 Hour**    | You understand the overall specific meaning and message of the writing.                                                                                                 |
