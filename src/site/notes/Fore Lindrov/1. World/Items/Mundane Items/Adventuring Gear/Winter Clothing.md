# Details
When you are wearing Winter Clothing, and not wearing Heavy armor, you automatically succeed on saving throws against the effects of [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]] as long as the clothing remains dry.
