## Details
When you are wearing Snow Shoes, moving through [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Deep Snow\|Deep Snow]] doesn't cost you extra movement, but moving through any other type of terrain is considered [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]].

You need at least 1 minute to put on or remove your snow shoes.
