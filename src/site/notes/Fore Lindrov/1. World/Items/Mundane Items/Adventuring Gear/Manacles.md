# Details
## General
As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] action, you can use [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Adventuring Gear/Manacles\|Manacles]] to bind an unwilling Small or Medium creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]], or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. 

While bound, a creature has [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls, and the creature is [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] if the Manacles are attached to a chain or hook that is fixed in place. Otherwise it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition but its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] is not 0.

## Escape
Escaping the Manacles requires a successful DC 20 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action. Bursting them requires a successful DC 25 Strength (Athletics) check as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action.

Each set of Manacles comes with a key. Without the key, a creature can use Thieves' Tools to pick the Manacles' lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check.
