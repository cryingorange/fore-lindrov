## Details
A Bedroll sleeps one Small or Medium creature. While in a Bedroll, you automatically succeed on saving throws against [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Hazard/Extreme Cold\|Extreme Cold]].

**Wrap Around Self.** A creature can wrap a bedroll around itself to use as a makeshift [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Adventuring Gear/Blanket\|Blanket]], but it's [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] will be halved.
