# Details

As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action, you can wrap a [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Mundane Items/Adventuring Gear/Chain\|Chain]] around an unwilling creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Grappled\|Grappled]], [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]], or [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 STR (Athletics) check.

If the creature's legs are bound, the creature has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition until it escapes. Otherwise it has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] condition but its [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Movement/Speed\|Speed]] is not 0.

Escaping the Chain requires the creature to make a successful DC 18 DEX (Acrobatics) check as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action. The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Restrained\|Restrained]] creature or another creature within 5 feet of it can burst the Chain with a successful DC 20 STR (Athletics) check as a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Utilize\|Utilize]] Action.

Destroying the Chain also frees the target, ending the effect.
- **AC:** 19
- **HP:** 10
- **Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
