## Effect
An an Action, you can light a Dynamite Stick and throw it at a point up to 60 feet away, where it explodes. Each creature in a 5-foot-radius ~={blue}Sphere=~ centered on that point makes a DC 12 DEX saving throw, taking 3d6 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The Dynamite Stick does double damage to objects and structures.

> **Binding Multiple Sticks.** 
> It takes 1 minute to bind two or more Dynamite Sticks together so they explode at the same time. Each stick after the first increases the damage by 1d6 (to a maximum of 10d6) and the effect's radius by 5 feet (to a maximum of 20 feet).

> **Longer Fuse**
>It takes 1 minute to rig dynamite with a longer fuse so it explodes after a longer period of time, such as 1 minute or 10 minutes.
