## Details
Setting fire to a keg full of Gunpowder causes it to explode. When a keg explodes, each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Area of Effects/Sphere\|Sphere]] centered on the keg makes a DC 12 DEX saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
