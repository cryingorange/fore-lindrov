# Overview
This suit of armor is reinforced with Adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. The metal is dark, almost black, with a unique green-gold sheen that sets it apart from iron or steel. 

It feels unnaturally heavy and appears completely seamless, devoid of the scratches or dents found on common equipment.

## Requirements

**Additional STR Requirement.** If the armour has a STR score Requirement such as with Heavy Armour, that requirement increases by +2.

**Double Weight.** Adamantine Armour weighs twice as much as its normal counterpart.

# Effects

**No Critical Hits.** While you're wearing it, any [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]] against you becomes a normal hit.

**Magic Resistance.** While you're wearing it, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Magical Effects\|Magical Effects]].

**Magical Avoidance.** If you are is subjected to a spell or magical effect that allows you to make a saving throw to take only half damage, you instead takes no damage if you succeed on the save. 

**Damage Threshold.** You gain a Damage Threshold of 5, if you already have a Damage Threshold. This Damage Threshold is increased by other existing [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Damage Threshold\|Damage Thresholds]] or Damage Reduction such as from the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Feats/Heavy Armor Master\|Heavy Armor Master]] feat.

**Resistance To Spells.** While wearing Adamantine Armour, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to damage from Spells.
