> Dung Hauler's Mask

# Description
This thick mask made of dark-brown boiled leather and reinforced with brass fittings and iron rivets has a blunt, boxy breathing chamber at the snout.

Two holes on the sides allow for herbs and well-smelling flora to stave off dangerous miasmas. It straps tightly against the face to form an air-tight seal.


# Effect
**Advantage.** While wearing this mask, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on saving throws against the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition from breathing.

**Poison Avoidance.** If you are subjected to an effect that requires you to breathe (such as [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Cloudkill\|Cloudkill]]) that allows you to make a CON Saving Throw to take only half damage Poison damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the Saving Throw.
