# Overview
This suit of armor is woven with Mithral, a rare, semi-organic ore that naturally resonates with the flow of arcane energy. The metal possesses an iridescent, silver-white sheen that seems to shift and shimmer in the light. 

It is impossibly light, appearing more like a second skin or fine silk than metal, and is completely devoid of the weight and friction of standard equipment.

## Requirements

**No Disadvantage & STR Requirement.** If the armor normally imposes [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on DEX (Stealth) checks or has a STR requirement, the mithral version of the armor doesn't.

**Half Weight.** Mithral Armour weighs half as much as its normal counterpart.

**Trained.** You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium armor.

<br>

# Effects

**AC Bonus (+1).** You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor.

**DEX Bonus.** You can add your full DEX modifier to your AC, regardless of armor type. 

**Resonant Stability.** While wearing this armour, you have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on CON Saving Throws made to maintain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Concentration\|Concentration]]. 

**Reflect Spell.** As a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Reaction\|Reaction]], when you succeed on a saving throw against a spell, choose one creature you can see within 120 feet. The target must succeed on a DEX Saving Throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, they take a number of d6s equal to the level of the spell in Force damage.
