# Overview
An alchemical process has bonded Moongold to this weapon or piece of ammunition.

<br>

## Effects

A Moongilded Weapon has 10 charges. 

While holding the weapon, you can take a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Actions/Bonus Action\|Bonus Action]] to activate it, connecting to the material and expend 1 charge to give yourself one of the following benefits for 1 minute:
- **Burning Radiance.** You deal an additional 2d6 Radiant damage to Non-Humanoids and non-Celestials using the weapon.
- **Fortified Mind.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on INT, WIS or CHA Saving Throws (you choose when you activate this effect) against Aberrations.
- **Steadfast Psyche.** You have [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Advantage\|Advantage]] on Saving Throws against the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Frightened\|Frightened]] condition and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Madness\|Madness]]. 
- **Psychic Shield.** You gain [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] to Psychic damage. 

**Recoating.** Moongold loses its effectiveness over time; once a Moongilded weapon loses all its charges, it must be recoated with Moongold.
