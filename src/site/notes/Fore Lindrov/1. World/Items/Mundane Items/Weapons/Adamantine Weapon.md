# Overview
This weapon or piece of ammunition is made of Adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. The metal is a dark black with a distinct, matte-grey finish and feels unnaturally dense in the hand.

<br>

## Requirements

**Additional Score Requirements.** If the weapon has a STR or DEX score Requirement such as the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Heavy\|Heavy]] property, that requirement increases by +2.

**Double Weight.** Adamantine Weapons weigh twice as much as its normal counterpart.

<br>

## Effects

### +1 Weapon

**+1 Weapon.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon or piece of ammunition, this bonus cannot be suppressed by effects such as [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/8th Level/Antimagic Field\|Antimagic Field]].

If the weapon has a magical bonus to attack and damage rolls, you may use that bonus instead or whichever is higher.

### Damage Threshold & Objects

**Ignores Damage Threshold.** Damage from this weapon ignores [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Damage Threshold\|Damage Threshold]].

**Siege Weapon.** Whenever this weapon or piece of ammunition hits an object, the hit is a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Critical Hit\|Critical Hit]].
