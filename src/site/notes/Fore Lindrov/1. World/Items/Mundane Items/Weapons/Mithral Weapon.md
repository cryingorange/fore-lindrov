# Overview
This weapon or piece of ammunition is made of Mithral, a rare, iridescent ore that naturally resonates with the flow of arcane energy. It is semi-organic and near-weightless, appearing as a shimmering, silver-white metal that is famously difficult to forge, requiring harmonic resonance rather than brute force.

<br>

## Requirements

**Weightless.** A weapon made of Mithral cannot have the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Heavy\|Heavy]] Property, and has the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Weapon Properties/Finesse\|Finesse]] Property.

**Half Weight.** Mithral weapons weigh half as much as its normal counterpart.

<br>

## Effects

### Bonus

**Weapon (+1).** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon or piece of ammunition.

**Arcane Focus (+1).** This weapon also functions as an Arcane Focus, while holding this weapon, you have a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC increases by 1.

### Magic Well

**Charges (8/Dawn).** The weapon has 8 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

**Cast Spell.** While holding the weapon, you can expend these charges to cast a spell you have prepared. The number of charges expended is equal to the level of the spell cast.
