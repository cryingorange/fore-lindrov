# Overview
An alchemical process has bonded silver to this weapon or piece of ammunition.

<br>

## Effects

**Additional Damage.** When you hit a Fey or Undead with this weapon, you deal an extra 1d4 damage of the weapon's type.

**Ignore Resistance.** Additionally, damage from this weapon ignores [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Resistance\|Resistance]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Core/Immunity\|Immunity]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage if the target is a Fey or Undead.
