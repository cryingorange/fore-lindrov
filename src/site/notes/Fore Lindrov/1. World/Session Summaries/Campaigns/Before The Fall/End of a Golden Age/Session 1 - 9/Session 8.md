# Gnoll Cave

The party enters the cave, met with a dark space that even those with darkvision can’t see through. Grimrid rushes in, resisting the effects of the void as Lu follows in after, alongside the Githyanki who strides in, unaffected by the void. Pork rushes in as well, and is also unaffected. Most who enter the void experience a strange effect as they try to speak, except for Lu who has somehow managed to project his thoughts to all others. Verena and Spoon take a moment to investigate the void, both realising that they could scale the walls instead to avoid it. Verena tries first, touching the slimy wall and stops to clean her hands. Meanwhile, Spoon takes out his skewers, trying to use them to scale the wall. He fails to stab into the hard wall, as Derrick points out that it could have damaged the tools.  
  
Derrick suggests using their claws instead, he tries, though he quickly slips off the wall. Still projecting his thoughts, the others hear Lu comment that he has the letter before a familiar silence that follows when Lu and the letter interact. Continuing on, Grimrid manages to forcefully pry open the door. He is met with a man, dressed in black, ragged clothing, with a trench coat and hat that covers most of his features. As Grimrid holds the door by himself, the man asks Grimrid's name. Pork comes out of the void, helping Grimrid with the door as Grimrid does not answer the man's, instead telling the lad to help.  
  
The man does as the two proceed to endlessly inquire about each other's identity. Meanwhile, back at the entrance, Verena learns from Spoon, taking out a hammer and a crowbar to aid her climb across the wall, keeping her hands clean. Spoon and Derrick try once more, this time successfully getting a grip on the wall as they all begin to climb across the room. The Githyanki also emerges from the void, immediately hostile towards the man, who responds fluently in Gith. The man, Ghoul, secures employment under the Gith. Meanwhile, inside the room, Derrick slips, bringing both himself and Spoon into the void as the kobold leaps to protect his ward. Verena leaves the room, landing without an issue. She informs the others of Derrick and Spoon's plight, but soon the two emerge and are safe. Verena checks her belongings and finds the letter on her person once more. The party agrees that Lu got back into the letter somehow and leaves him in there whilst they continue.

  

# Birthing Pool

The party finds a cave filled with water of some sort. Grimrid, Spoon, Derrick, and Vael Zereth wade into the water and approach a clutch of eggs. Vael Zereth tells the party that the eggs are how the mindflayers spread and commands the party to destroy the eggs. In the water, the party also sees movement as some fish like creatures swim away from them. Still on shore are Ghoul, Verena, and Pork, bothered by the unclean water. But they follow the rest into the water. Verena, Grimrid, and Pork move to the alcove, looking through the items and a giant crystal. Meanwhile, Spoon, Derrick, Vael Zereth, and Ghoul kill the eggs and fish like creatures. With Ghoul shooting his gun at the creatures, causing a loud sound as the fish attacks the party. The party manages to take care of the fish, with Vael Zereth paranoid of any potential infection. He explains ceremorphosis, how quickly it infects and the many symptoms the infected experience before they inevitably become a mind flayer. Now safe, the party continues to explore the alcove. 

# Mind Crystal

In particular, they look at the giant purple glowing crystal. Derrick, Verena, and Spoon wish to harvest the crystal, enlisting the help of Grimrid who refuses to carry the entire crystal, instead offering to chip off a piece. Meanwhile, Pork is staring intently at the crystal, sensing the power in it. With Verena’s encouragement, he stares and connects with the crystal just as Grimrid swings his hammer at it. A big surge of power courses through the room, assaulting the minds of those around the crystal before coalescing on the weakest one, Pork. But Verena steps in, taking on the effects herself as her body begins to melt into sludge. Grimrid rushes to help, shouting at Verena to steel herself. He places a hand on her shoulder and watches as her form collapses from the light touch. Spoon also wants to help but does not understand what is happening to Verena. He takes a moment to look, to try to make sense of it when he finds himself in a different space. Floating in a pool of sorts, his body feels different, unlike his own. The body has tentacles where his mouth would have been. He moves through, being birthed. He touches the ground for what seems to be the first time, the sensation is overstimulating as he does not know what to feel, what to expect. He finds himself needing to breathe manually, to taste. And as he blinks his eyes open, his mind takes in the new stimulus. He finds himself in a new world, a different realm, and he is a mind flayer. But not just any kind, a different, pure mind flayer. Both Spoon and Verena steel themselves, returning back to their bodies, no longer any weird melting sludge. Yet in the back of their minds, they know that if they were to lose focus, they would lose hold of themselves. Now fine, they carry on. The party realises the supplies to be that of Phandalin’s. Grimrid refrains from taking anything, not wanting to steal from the already impoverished town. Verena cracks open the barrel of wine, taking some for herself and suggests that Spoon do the same, recommending that the chef try out some new stews with alcohol, assuring the kobold that the alcohol would all burn off, making it suitable for young ones like Derrick. Spoon also takes some salt, a key ingredient for their meals to come. 

Prisoners

Continuing past the alcove, the party sees a hole into the next room, blocked by a weird bubble. Grimrid takes out his axe, repeatedly driving it into the bubble in hopes of breaking it when Vael Zereth opens it, causing Grimrid to fall through 20 ft of the levitate effect. Spoon and Derrick jump onto Grimrid, using him as a platform. Reaching the lower level, cages with humans line the right side of the cave whilst tables with a console and jars of brains line the left. Grimrid is preoccupied with the ground, revelling in the solid, sturdy floor compared to the horrors of floating he had suffered. Spoon, Derrick, Ghoul head to the humans. Spoon checks their heads for signs of surgery, only agreeing to help them when he is sure that they do not have any marks. Though Vael Zereth speaks his mind, voicing his disagreement as he reminds the party of the tadpoles upstairs, a different way to infect the minds of their victims. The party disagrees, not caring for Vael Zereth’s concerns as they refuse to kill anyone until it is necessary. Spoon tries to argue that they could spot symptoms first before anyone has to be killed but Vael Zereth rebuts, stating that the Mind Flayers could have found a way to hasten the infection. From the hostages, the party also finds the parents of ayla, the young girl now safe with Garaele. The party also learns about the individuals present, some wolves and one that is particularly large. 

Brain Jars

Meanwhile, Verena heads over to the desk. She makes sense of the console, and grabs the nearest brain jar, placing it into the machine when she hears the voice of a little girl. When she tries to speak to it, Verena realises that the jar is not a recording but rather the actual brain of a young child named “Xu Huang”, who still seems to think they are alive. The child cries that he’s sorry for crossing the river, that he’ll never disobey his mama ever again.  
  
Verena tries best as she can to calm the brain and resorts to disconnecting it rather quickly when the child would not calm down. But before more can be done, a fight breaks out behind. As their disagreement with Vael Zereth grows, Spoon makes a threat for the party to stop helping Vael Zereth. And as Ghoul swoops in to smoothen matters, he tells the party that they need Vael Zereth to open the doors, and reminds Vael Zereth that he too needs the party. Much to their annoyances, both parties set aside their distaste for one another whilst they work to fight a common enemy.  
  
Finishing their conversation, the party joins Verena and are also interested in the brain jars. Grimrid suggests smashing the jars, continuing on the habit the party had picked up since their first run in with an intellect devourer. Verena agrees, walking over to the same jar she knows houses the child’s mind and is about to blast it when the party steps in to take the jar. But Verena insists, stating that she had already spoken to that individual and that they’d have no information. Vael Zereth does not care about taking away the jar when Grimrid and Verena intercepts, Verena blasts the jar, destroying it as she urges for the others to pick another one.  
  
The second one they try is a frightened man named Garren who was hired to escort some nobles of House Kai to Urshu, though he does not remember much and provides not much insight into the place they are now. The jar is seized by Vael Zereth for now as the party puts the last jar on. As the last jar is placed, a calm voice speaks, one who immediately rushes to reassure the party members, to tell them the information he already has and possible ways to escape. The party is surprised to find such a calm individual and learn that he is Sergeant Sorren of the Golden Army who served under Lady General Marina. Verena first lies to the Sergeant, telling him that they could not light a torch due to not wanting to be found and have been sent by higher authorities to help. Though seeing the Sergeant’s calmness and diligence, Grimrid feels it only right to tell the Sergeant the truth, as he does, the Sergeant responds calmly, only requesting the party tell the steward to pass on a message, that it was an honour to serve her Lady General Marina. As he gives them the final piece of information, he requests that the party kill him, and Grimrid does. Though he finds it a shame for the Sergeant to end up in such a place. Verena suggests that Grimrid pick up what is left of the Sergeant and bury it elsewhere as he sees fit. The idea of honouring one’s sacrifice inspires Verena to pick up the brain of the child she had spoken to, wrapping it up in a handkerchief. 

Blood Debt

Continuing on, the party finds a set of wolf pups who can speak a word of common. Spoon approaches first, trying to befriend the pups who begin saying the word “bad” and nothing else. Verena joins in, speaking to the pups in their tongue as she and Spoon manage to converse with the pups. They don’t learn much from the two pups but the two manage to extort most of the meat Spoon has, though they do seem to like Spoon at least a little bit. 

  

Entering the final room, the party are met with the sight of their friend Sildar, tied up and gagged before the body of a dead wolf. In the room is also a much bigger wolf, with tentacles and no eyes. The wolf manipulates his tentacles to speak as both he and his people call the party murderers, hunters, and bad people. He explains how his tribe, the Sawplee tribe once lived in the forests when the tentacled ones came, carved into them and made them think, disrupting their lives. The Gnolls then mistook their words for prophecy and purpose, following the leader’s commands. The leader demands for the party to pay the blood debt for slaughtering their kin and mutilating the body for no reason. The only ways to pay off the debt are either death or a limb. None of the party wishes to pay up, so Verena tells a lie. She claims that the blood debt, losing a limb, would hurt their own families too, that the wolves would only perpetuate the cycle of vengeance that never ends. She suggests for them to find another way, a new direction for the wolves to take. Grimrid approaches with a more practical explanation, that this would only seek to continue the war between the humans and the wolves, that there would be other places where the wolves can be accepted. The leader speaks up once more, sharing his desire for his pact to live on, for them to have a new life, one free from destitution. Grimrid states that he seeks no war with the wolves and offers to help find a place far from the humans, such as the forest. Yet, for the blood debt, Grimrid has no answer to fix that. Ghoul steps up and as someone who understands how it is to live a life in which they had no choice in, he offers his hand to make the wolves whole again. 

Killing Vael Zereth

This is where Vael Zereth speaks up, calling the party fools for falling for the wolves' words. Halting his words, Ghoul raises his gun, handing Vael Zereth his resignation in the form of a bullet. The party rushes to fight Vael Zereth, as Ghoul warns of the danger of letting Vael Zereth run free, which the party never intended to. Vael Zereth has his summon attack the hostages, killing one of them before his spell breaks. The party does manage to catch up to him, with Spoon and Derrick taking the final hit, killing the man. The party then return back to the wolves, hearing out their wants for a life where their pups do not starve, where their elderly do not need to travel or hunt. Speaking to Sildar, the party also learns of a druid friend he had, one who might be the master Biscuit serves as the party assures the wolves that they would find a place for them in this world. The leader wolf had also explained how two cloaked figures had approached their cave. They brought about a cold air with them, and one of them had two glowing eyes under their hood. The other was shorter with fur all over their body and smelt of earth, wearing a white cloak. They demanded them to attack the Giants as the leader wolf had no choice but to give some of his Flinds to the white dragon. With the last piece of information given, the party leaves the cave with Sildar and the other hostages. As they leave, Grimrid has the villagers take as much of the supplies as they can.

Sildar

The party also takes the chance to ask Sildar a series of questions. Grimrid takes the first shot, channeling his frustrations towards the man as he asks how Sildar had failed to help his master. Though in Sildar’s defence, they were up against 24 gnolls when a dragon showed up. Sharing what the party knows of demons and their relation to the gnolls, Sildar suggests his friend Alara might be able to help. When it is mentioned that Alara is a demonologist, Ghoul’s interest is piqued. According to Sildar, Alara was a demonologist who liked demons a lot, maybe too much, and had made a pact with an imp called “Shtick”. Though he knows little of her whereabouts, not having seen most of his old party in some time. 

  

The party also inquires of his old age when Sildar reveals that somewhere along his travels, he and his party had changed upon some waters that were said to grant a long life. Not immortality but a long life. They drank it and now, at about 150 years old, Sildar still lives. Sildar also mentions Thell, his old friend who he does not know is dead. The party breaks that news very awkwardly, and Sildar voices his need for some alcohol. Still wanting to learn more, Verena offers a waterskin of the wine she had taken as they move on to the next subject. 

  

On the topic of Elowen, Sildar shares that she was part of their first mission. Unfortunately she had died and refused to leave. Sildar, feeling guilty for failing to save her, visited for a month. Which resulted in Elowen falling for him. She had confessed, professing her passions and desire for Sildar which caused him to run away. The party then shares a second set of bad news, that Elowen had acquired a doll that would trap Sildar’s soul inside if he ever died. His response is to continue running from her. 

Then, they speak of his blade, and his tendency for misplacing it. He shares about the time he fell in love with a girl, who turned out to be a thief who stole his family’s sword. 

  

Lastly, the party informs Sildar of the current state of Phandalin, how the mayor is a weakling and how the red brands run the place, now housed in an old manor that Sildar surmises belonged to the Tresserdor family, whose crypts were undoubtedly broken into.
