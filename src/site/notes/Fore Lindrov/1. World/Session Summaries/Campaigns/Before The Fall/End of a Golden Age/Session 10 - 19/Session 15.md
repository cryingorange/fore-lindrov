# Glasstaff’s Throne

The party pushes past Glasstaff’s lair, finding a corridor with displays of magic items. Tormund, Spoon, and Verena take a moment to look at the items whilst Grimrid approaches the guard. Grimrid throws his hammer at the, pushing the guard into the room and announcing the party’s arrival. To the party’s surprise, Glasstaff begins monologuing, treating the encounter more like a theatrical performance than proper combat. The party leans into the game, with Grimrid taking on the role of a hero, here to put an end to the evil mage’s plans. 
  

# The White Dragon


The party puts up a good fight, besting Glassstaff’s minions easily enough. Unable to keep up the facade for long, Glassstaff drops his human form, revealing an adult white dragon. Combat ensues but not for long as Tormund falls. Spoon has a spell scroll of revivify, rushing to help when Derrick proclaims that he would cast the spell. In the midst of it all, Glassstaff grabs Derrick, proclaiming that he would sacrifice him as he punches through the ice floor, plunging into the cold waters with Derrick in hand. Spoon dives in without hesitation, ready to save his bitey baby. Grimrid follows, halting Spoon to not go on a suicide mission himself. 

  

# Help Asked, Help Given

  

He manages to convince the Spoon who returns to where the party is. The time for revivify expires. But Vulcan steps up, pulling out the canopic jar from before as he uses the remains of his people to bring Tormund back to life. Panpan approaches the party, wanting to help save Derrick, although the party refuses, not wanting to put Panpan in danger after just saving him. Instead Panpan offers the party help, he blesses them with some much needed rest and also blesses Verena’s draconic focus, just as Paper said that baby dragons were able to do. The party also tries to steal from the shelves with Verena being petrified when Yegg storms in. He chastises Spoon for going along with the plan to attack Glassstaff and hands him a bag of treats. Yegg also drags in Zafir al-Harid who helps unpetrify Verena. 

  

# Into Glasstaff’s Lair

  

The party, knowing that they are about to enter the freezing water, rush around to find means to help themselves with most of them stuffing blankets in their armour to protect themselves. They dive in, with Spoon and Verena leading the party and eventually surface in a different room. The cold weakens some of the party members, namely Tormund, Vulcan, and Zyraxes. 

The room they arrive in is mostly empty, save for a big door and two frozen adventurers before it. The party tries to open the door, and Spoon hears a voice. The voice turns out to be a mephit, who is strangely obsessed with violence and people falling down. He demands that the party slap Grimrid and tell him jokes. Rushed for time, Grimrid takes one for the team, getting backhanded and mocked at by the Mephit. The party does somehow manage to leave, entering a second vast room where Glassstaff greets them, continuing his game as he flies down to the final chamber. It is a room that the party is somewhat familiar with, having seen different parts of it from the mirror. 

  

# Final Battle

  

Derrick is trapped, held in place by both ice and magic that tethers him to the statue of the Dragon Queen. Glassstaff boldly declares his intentions to sacrifice Derrick, relishing in the game as combat ensues once more. 

  

During which, the party learns to avoid the dragon and its breath, splitting up and hiding behind walls. Tormund and Zyraxes stand their ground, taking the brunt of the Dragon’s attacks. Tormund reaches death’s door once more, still weakened from his recent reincarnation. Just as the situation looks dire, Lu emerges from the letter, claiming to have seen everything from within as he summons his tiger, Horse and rides bravely into battle after picking up the dragon slaying sword. Grimrid gathers the attention of the dragon, putting on false bravado as he proclaims that the dragon would be an easy fight, being only a white dragon that has not the intelligence of a blue dragon, the cunning of a green dragon, might of a red dragon, nor the cruelty of a black dragon. A mere white dragon that the dwarves used to kill in droves. Glassstaff proceeds to chase after Grimrid, the two partaking in a game of cat and mouse whilst the party handles Glassstaff’s minions. The game seems to have gotten to Grimrid’s head as he relentlessly throws insult after insult. Glassstaff grapples him, picking him up as he sends a concentrated cold breath right at Grimrid, then unceremoniously drops the dwarf. The party closes in, with Spoon rushing in, revivify scroll in hand as he attempts to cast magic beyond his usual capabilities. He succeeds, bringing Grimrid back to life as Verena boldly approaches Glassstaff, blasting at his scales for having killed her friend. Glassstaff stalks over to Verena, swiping at her only to miss as he crushes the icicle before her. She smiles, looking at Glassstaff before uttering a simple retort that has the already insulted Dragon more angry than before as Verena now becomes the focus of his attention. Surrounding the Dragon, the party works together, each attempting to cut the dragon with the blade. Spoon succeeds, slashing at the dragon as Derrick spews fire at the ice dragon. Lastly, as the icy mist clears, Verena walks up to Glassstaff, telling him that in the end, he is nothing more than a mere worm as her magic blasts through his eye, killing him. 

  

# Conclusion

In the concluding battle, the party is left with the White Mask and a strange fortelling by Glasstaff, which they find on a piece of paper on his table, the strange prophecy fortelling that the White Dragon Mask would be used to become something, and that these were words heard from the Exarch of Tiamat, Arauthator, which spurred Glasstaff into action. Glasstaff writes that his dream from which he gleaned the prophecy showed him the visage of a great ivory dragon that would ascend into an endless sky, with wings of white that would blot out the moon. Accompanied by the crushed statue of a one-eyed man, and that left behind from the Dragon’s rise was the dead corpse of a Brass Dragon. 

  

As the party attempt to decide what to do with the potent Artifact that is the White Dragon Mask, Vulcan suggests that it might be time to return home, so that they can hide the White Dragon Mask away. There is a small degree of revelation from him that he is a Prince of sorts from the Kingdom of Urshu, and that the tombs of Urshu would be well-protected enough to ensure that the White Dragon Mask never falls into the wrong hands. As a sort of gift, the party suggests that Vulcan take the Dragon Slayer Sword and the Dragon Armour to ensure that if any Draconic Minions that might try to reacquire the White Dragon Mask can be much more easily fought off. The party and the Vulcan Force promise each other that they will see each other again, once Grimrid and the party seek out Jarchigudai, and once Vulcan and Zyraxes hide the White Dragon Mask.

  

The party inquire how Vulcan and Zyraxes will get back, with Zyraxes citing that they will fly, before transforming into his true form, a Young Brass Dragon and flying away with Vulcan, much to the party’s surprise, with some of them yelling, “He could have done that the whole time?”

  

The party help Yegg acquire a Wyvern’s egg, allowing him to complete his Magnum Opus of food, and him leaving each of the party members with some as a goodbye. The party ask Panpan what he and his two Kobolds will do now that the danger has passed, with Panpan saying he would like to return to his forest to help more people. 

  

Derrick also is seemingly unaware of the sheer size difference between himself and Panpan, despite them both being of the same age. He’s surprised to learn that he’s supposed to be able to grant Boons just like Panpan, trying his hardest, he only farts, although Spoon, Pen and Paper applaud him. Pen and Paper whisper quietly to Spoon that Derrick needs only more practice. 

  

As the party rests in Cragmaw Castle, the curse that came with the Potion of Strength that Tormund took begins to show itself, overtime his muscles grow bigger and bigger, granting him great strength, but their freakish size slow him down, to the point where he can no longer even see from out of his deltoids.
