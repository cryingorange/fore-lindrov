# Grimrid’s talk

As the party heads to the Shrine of Luck, Grimrid’s demeanour gets brought up. Grimrid expresses his opinions, believing the town of Phandalin to be of little help in his quest to find his master, that they should head back to the last known location and try searching for more clues there. The party is confused, explaining that Phandalin suffers from Gnoll attacks, Gnolls who also attacked Jarchigudai. And with that as their only clue, the party is now in Phandalin to hopefully find more clues that would lead them to Jarchigudai. Realising the miscommunication they had, the party arrives at the Shrine of Luck.

# Shrine of Luck

Before the statue, Spoon and Lu begin to muse aloud, wanting to interact with the figurine of the goddess. Verena steps away, not remotely interested in the gods nor the potential mischief the rest seem to be up to. Playing a prank on the others, she uses her magic to make an ominous sound, successfully scaring Grimrid. Meanwhile, Spoon and Lu pay to the figurine, giving coppers and gold as the figurine glows, both receiving the goddess’ blessing. Verena approaches the house, about to knock on the door as a scream comes from the inside. The others rush ahead, breaking a window as they see Nars Dendrar thrashing about, opening his wounds. The party helps subdue Thel, knocking him out as Garaele and Spoon dress the man’s wounds. Garaele questions why the party is here, to which Grimrid quickly responds that Verena has been cursed, again. Verena is quick to correct that this is her first time being cursed by the hag. Verena does try to suggest that it was Elmina who did it but Garaele doesn’t believe that the sweet old lady could ever do such a thing. Still Garaele helps, requesting for the party to retrieve the figurine for her to break the curse. Spoon and Lu do so, as Garaele explains that they all have to hold hands in a circle, praying to Tymora to break the curse. Fortunately for Verena, she does not have to speak and is able to avoid praying to a goddess. Before the curse is lifted, Garaele holds Verena’s hands and requests for Grimrid’s help, stating that the ritual only works if a dwarf helps. She admits her lie, taking the chance to apologise to the two, admitting her mistakes and flaws which Verena agrees with. Grimrid accepts the apology, offering one of his own as well. With the curse lifted, Verena is now able to speak freely once more. Garaele shares with the party what had happened to Nars and advises that they reach out to Linene Graywind for some work to help the town. The party also inquires of Garaele’s outing before the goblins had captured her and she reveals that she had attempted to exorcise the lady of the lake, to no avail as the undead is stronger than what she can presently handle. 

  

Before they leave, Garaele requests that the party pray with her. Spoon and Lu pray sincerely, both having witnessed and felt the goddess’ blessing. Grimrid prays as well, though to Moradin instead of Tymora. Meanwhile Verena pretends to agree, praying insincerely. Yet as luck would have it, the goddess’ blessing is bestowed upon her, a strange divine power graces the abyssal tiefling. 

# Linene Graywind

The party seeks out Linene, taking up her quest to help lead the incoming shipment to town. Linene offers the party 300 gold and also informs them of some other supposedly famous adventurer she had hired called “Nastings” or something. The party does get some other information from Linene, finally meeting someone who has heard of Sildar, who was apparently Thel Dendrar’s friend. Before they leave, Verena does attempt to trick Linene into giving them more pay, though it does not go according to plan. 

# Securing the Shipment

Following the path, the party comes across the shipment. Before the cart stands an adventurer in clean plate armour. The adventurer laughs, a little too loudly, with his foot pressed against a dead gnoll. The legendary monster hunter Linene had hired is a youthful man who looks skinny compared to the heavy armour and weapon he wields. He introduces himself as Hastings, the renown adventurer as he begins recounting tales of his adventures. Spoon and Derrick are entranced, excited to meet a famous monster hunter. Grimrid does not care for the man, walking past him to speak with the merchants. Lu finds the man odd, having heard of the legend of Hastings yet the person before them does not seem to match the legends. Verena leaves as soon as the man begins his self absorbed rant, joining Grimrid’s side. Spoon pulls out a piece of paper, asking Hastings to sign the pamphlet, which he does. 

  

Grimrid approaches the merchants, learning of their suspicions of Hastings who apparently screamed like a child when the Gnoll appeared and that the Gnoll had smashed the wheel of the cart, leaving them stranded in the middle of the road. Grimrid accesses the damage, declaring that they are done for and that there is no way to fix the wheel. Just then, Verena shows up, glancing at the damage before declaring that Grimrid could fix it. Though Grimrid does require materials as he begins looking for a good tree to chop down. Verena speaks with the merchants, learning from them the same information they told Grimrid. Though some things do not add up, the Gnoll attacked the cart rather than the people, yet the wheel was bludgeoned, not slashed. That and the Gnoll dying to “Hastings” when he is clearly an incompetent adventurer. Leaving the merchants alone, Verena speaks with the horse, unhooking it from the cart and telling it to move away if required but to not run away and to return. 

  

Meanwhile, Lu continues staring at Hastings, confidently deducing that this man, who knows not what a kobold is, is clearly pretending to be the famous monster hunter, taking someone else’s glory as his own. Lu begins shouting at the man for his immoral actions. Verena does try to intervene but as neither man is willing to listen, she lets them be. Before they can do anything, multiple explosions go off. 

# Gnoll Ambush

The party is ambushed by Gnolls and two wolves. The wolves behave weirdly, staring at the party, observing them. The merchants and the horse are heavily wounded. In an attempt to save everyone, Grimrid summons Ruinora Schiffer who heals the horse, giving it a chance to run away. With Ruu’s appearance, the wolves join in combat. One approaches Grimrid, using psionics to incapacitate the dwarf, causing Ruu to vanish as Grimrid loses concentration on the spell. Spoon manages to eliminate most of the gnolls, whilst Grimrid helps the merchants. Verena feels a presence take over as a deep rage towards the Gnolls boil up within her. She teleports over to the Gnoll leader as a voice that isn’t her own shouts. The voice chastises the Gnolls for serving a fake as Verena summons a massive mace, smashing down on the Gnoll leader. Lu manages to take the final blow on one of the wolves. Somehow the party manages to save the people, horse, and cart. 

# Laymon

As combat ends, “Hastings” begins laughing, acting as if he had done anything but scream and swing randomly during the combat. Lu manages to hold back his anger, threatening to hurt the man if he continues to tarnish Hasting’s reputation. Disappointed, Spoon chimes in as well, reminding the man that all he did was scream. “Hastings” continues, feigning ignorance until Lu punches the man, hurting him enough to have him break character. The man, Laymon, is apparently an actor of sorts and is method acting to try and play Hastings as best he can. He hurls insults at Lu, calling him a mindless brute. During this Grimrid walks up behind Laymon, using his shield to knock the man out. Grimrid reiterates that the tree isn’t going to chop itself, and lends Lu the little handaxe he has. Lu starts to confide in Grimrid, concerned that his family would hear about Laymon’s incorrect portrayal of himself. Grimrid scoffs, saying that true honour lies in stories passed down within the clan and begins to share about his family. 

  

Meanwhile, Verena approaches Spoon, who is confused at Laymon’s actions. Verena explains that people lie. Spoon begins being unsure of how to spot actual adventurers from fakes, to which Verena offers a simple set of rules; Fake adventurers talk too much, are too clean, and scream a lot. Derrick comes to terms with the fake adventurers as well, demanding that they begrime him so that he is a real adventurer. Which Spoon refuses, stating that it is important for Derrick to take his baths and be clean. Instead Derrick attempts to be cool and stoic, deepening his voice and answering only in single words and grunts. Verena also takes the chance to bring up the discomfort Spoon might have had around Verena since the Thugs incident. Which Spoon does share and list his rules to determine if someone can be killed. Verena does partially explain her actions, sharing that where she comes from everyone can be killed for any reason. And that the Thugs clearly had bad intentions with Garaele. Still she relents, compromising that the next time they aren’t to kill someone, it would be good if Spoon vocalised it. Verena looks through Laymon’s pockets, taking the hundred gold he had on him and splitting it with Spoon as Laymon’s punishment for lying to them. Spoon immediately hands the gold over to Derrick, telling him that is it for his hoard. 

# Pork is not Food

The two then speak with Pork, who seemed confused and lost during the combat when one of the wolves kept staring at him. Pork shares that the wolves wanted to take him away, that they told him that people are evil and would only want to hurt him, feeding him lies that the party intends to slaughter Pork for food. The two reassure him that they would not, with Verena explaining Pork’s name as “Spoon naming him after something that he loves”. Pork is easily convinced and shrugs off the bad feelings, trusting the party who have been nothing but good to him. 

# Gnoll Interrogation

Spoon heals the Gnoll they kept alive for interrogation. The Gnoll begins to speak in a series of clicks, in a way that the party cannot understand. They try asking questions but are mostly met with not much of a response as Gnolls aren’t particularly known speaking. Meanwhile as the others interrogate the Gnoll, Verena asks Spoon for a pound of raw oxen flesh, using her scalpel to carve slices into it as she eats the entire thing. The party are confused but Verena replies that it is of no concern, certain powers just come with a cost. 

# Wolf Autopsy

Curious about the wolves, Spoon and Verena cut one open, looking at its brain that had been experimented with, reminding Verena of the wolf brain she saw when they found Baron Moneybags. The two find nothing more and leave the wolf’s corpse where it is. 

# Special Pass

Grimrid and Lu chop down a tree, managing to fix the cart back well enough before they head back to town with three unconscious people. Before they enter the town, a group of “guards” approach, demanding payment from the party. Verena does try to lie but the Thugs do not care for much other than gold. Channeling his anger, Lu storms over to a nearby tree, shaking it vehemently as he uproots a portion of it, tossing it aside. He then walks back over to the Thugs, telling them that they have a special pass. The Thugs, not wanting to mess with Lu, let the party through without issue. As they head back, Derrick, inspired by Lu, proceeds to smack trees on their way back, muttering about a “special pass” as he does. 

# Back to Phandalin

The party returns to town as the three unconscious humans wake up. Laymon runs off, not wanting to deal with the party again. The two merchants are confused that they are alive but nonetheless head off to the inn for some much needed rest. The party brings the supplies to Linene who is also surprised to learn that the merchants survived. The party does tell her of the Fake Hastings she had hired. As reward for returning with both the supplies and her people, she pays them the 300 gold as promised and a crossbow. The crossbow gets handed to Spoon and Derrick who intend to strap the weapon on Derrick’s back, making the dragon a walking ballista. 

# Nars Dendrar

The party reports back to Garaele as well, and Nars is now awake and calm of sorts. He begins to share what had happened. That his father, Thel, was the best hunter and used to be an adventurer a long time ago, travelling in a party with a demonologist and a knight. And the recent Gnoll attacks had inspired his father to come out of retirement. Nars and his sibling, Nilsa, could not leave their father alone, thus the three headed out to find the Gnoll’s lair. But it seemed as though the Gnolls knew they were coming, they had been ambushed, his father and sister grabbed. The wolves approached, walking on twos. Nars was forced to watch as they cut into his family, eating their flesh alive. As he relives the memory, he begins to panic, the trauma hitting him once more. Still, the information he has is important as Verena casts a spell, calming Nar’s emotions, calming him down with her words, making him detached from the moment as he numbly recounts the incident. He was afraid that they would eat him too, yet they didn't, instead he was attacked and left bleeding out. He felt his blood pool around him, his body go cold. They ripped his father apart, the wolves are some kind of punishment, sent to punish them all. Then the spell fades and Nars begins panicking once more, screaming. Lu grabs Nars, choking the man to knock him out when Verena waves her hand once more, casting a spell to make Nars sleep. 

# Night at Garaele’s

With that settled, Garaele offers the party a room to rest in for the night. They all accept, with Spoon cooking dinner whilst Grimrid begins his crafting. Lu takes a bath, exiting barely clothed as the Garaele, Spoon, and Verena suggest that he get properly dressed. Though Lu does not understand. Verena covers Lu in a box, saving the eyes of the others whilst Lu begrudgingly puts on an acceptable amount of clothing. Garaele takes out some tea, about to make everyone a cup when Verena steps in, pulling out the leaves she had harvested instead, offering a better quality tea. Verena mage hands a cup to Grimrid, knowing that the dwarf would be too busy working on his creations to even take a sip. Lu takes out his paisho set, asking Verena if she would fancy a match. Lu explains the rules well, and Verena, not wanting to lose, suggests that they play a timed match, just a minute to see who wins. She tells Lu to set up the pieces as she goes to the lavatory. During which she casts guidance on herself, bettering her odds. Verena does end up winning the match despite it being her first time playing the game against an experienced player. Lu is surprised but compliments Verena’s intelligence. The party then takes their rest, with Verena and Derrick each taking a bed whilst the others sleep on the floor. 

  

During their rest, Spoon hears a noise in the middle of the night. Concerned, he sneaks downstairs to find Nars beheaded, his head missing. Following the sound, Spoon sees two gnome-like creatures, one of which holds Gregor's disembodied head and a ball shaped bag that is stained red. Spoon screams for the party’s help, waking them up as they rush down. Lu runs down, only seeing Nars without his head and Spoon with a cleaver in his hand. Derrick joins Spoon’s side, biting at one of the gnome-like creatures. Just as Verena enters, one of them casts silence, preventing any noise from escaping. Grimrid is confused, a deep sleeper who initially doesn’t acknowledge any sound but later does with Pork’s insistence. Verena throws her dagger at the remaining redcap, cutting it as the silence spell is dropped. Grimrid shows up to finish the job, knocking out the remaining redcap. The party attempts to interrogate one of the redcap. From him, they learn that he serves a mistress, likely the hag. But before they are able to get more information from the Redcap, thorns emerge from its eyes and mouth, killing the redcap instantly. Verena guesses that the hag might have some measures to ensure that none of her minions tell on her. With the remaining redcap, the party are unsure of what to do as Verena suggests killing it. And most of them want to, except for Lu, despite how evil it is. Grimrid then suggests that Lu fights it. To which Lu does want to. They wait until the Redcap wakes up, and Lu fights it, killing the Redcap on his own terms.

  

Grimrid helps out Garaele, knowing how shaken the poor woman is. Spoon helps out as well, speaking to the cleric who insists on sleeping in her home, which Spoon can see is Garaele’s way of punishing herself, of reminding what it is she fights to stop. 

  

Verena shares with the party that the redcap likely works for the hag and also hints that the hag eye belongs to the hag. The party decides to visit the hag that night. Knocking thrice at the front door, it opens and Verena tries but fails to confront the hag. Meanwhile, Spoon and Lu break into the shop, finding and stealing papers from the shop that have the routes of the shipments, all the ones that had been ambushed. As they leave and watch from the outside, Verena tries a more direct approach to confronting the hag, though it fails too. Verena rejects the deal and leaves.
