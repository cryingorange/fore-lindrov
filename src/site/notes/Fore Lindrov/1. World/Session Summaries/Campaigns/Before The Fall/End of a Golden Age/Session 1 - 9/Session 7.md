# Hag’s Hut Cont’d

Lu re-emerges from the letter, seeing Elmina for the first time and learns of her plight. Meanwhile, the rest of the party head upstairs, looking through the potions and items. Verena speak to Spoon, suggesting that he help check if she has any illness washing over her as she describes the weird pity she feels towards Elmina. Verena does posit a way to help Elmina, though does emphasise that the party has not much of any way to pay for it. Derrick tries to encourage Verena to help, calling her good when she immediately expresses her distaste for the word. Derrick changes his tone, claiming Verena to be the most bad person ever and bad people definitely help. This does not convince Verena. Grimrid and Verena head to the potions, where Grimrid finds a brown potion. Just as he raises a hand to smash the potion for its foul magic, Verena stops Grimrid, taking the potion instead. Relying on her knowledge of potions, Verena discerns that the potion is a greater healing potion, though one tastes disgusting. She hands the potion back to Grimrid, informing him of its benefits. Verena also finds a second potion, though does not discern its properties. Spoon meanwhile finds a stick, an apple skewer of sorts that he pockets with his other cooking utensil weapons. 

# Arlin and Biscuit

With no other way to help Elmina for the time being, the party leaves the hut, continuing on to the  destination given. Along the way, the party comes across a piece of black cloth before soon they hear a scream as Spoon, Grimrid, and Lu rushes to help whilst Verena opts to stroll. The ones who reach first find a man stuck in the tree with an owlbear and a rabbit-like creature with a horn at the base. The man cries for their help. Though Spoon notices the odd rabbit creature and casts speak with animals, understanding it as it commands the owlbear to attack the man. Grimrid and Lu rush forward, trying to intimidate the animals into leaving but to no avail. The animals turn their attention to the two before them with the rabbit readily stabbing its horn at Grimrid. Lu and Grimrid knock out the two animals, whilst Verena takes her time to examine the rabbit, likening it to an Almiraj, a beast that uses its horn to spear its enemies if driven to battle. It can also be used as the target of a find familiar spell. Verena tries to communicate this to the party, although forgetting that she’s speaking to a group of people who do not study magic, they instead misunderstand that Verena finds the area familiar. Derrick, wanting to help and explore, goes off and comes back with a small sack of gold, which Verena is quick to pretend is hers, lying that one never forgets where they lost their money. Spoon checks up on the thin spectacled man and helps with his injuries.  
  
The man, however, rushes up to Derrick pelting him with questions about dragons. His incessant questions annoy most of the party, with Grimrid and Lu taking a more physical approach to snap the man out of his ramblings. As they slap the book out of his hands, Verena picks it up, reading through to find nothing much other than poorly written self inserts of a man who longs for the knowledge of dragons too much. At least from the horrible writing, Verena is able to find the man’s name, Arlin. Arlin tries a more reasonable approach, offering Derrick and Spoon owlbear meat which does easily tempt them. Derrick begins speaking of hypothetically helping Arlin if he did know of a dragon who he could hypothetically ask these questions to and hypothetically give Arlin some samples of stools that he so desires. Arlin gives in, also showing the party an owlbear egg that he had found. Arlin claims to have slain the mother owlbear and taken the egg, an act that enrages Grimrid who roughouses Arlin more before telling him to scram. Arlin does leave, telling the party that they should come visit him when they can.  
  
With Arlin gone, Spoon ties up the Almiraj, poking its soft belly as Derrick and Verena joins in. Eventually, they opt to heal it, waking it up as Verena casts speak with animals to communicate with it. They try to learn of the Almiraj’s master, although they do not manage much. Spoon tries to tempt the Almiraj, Biscuit, with treats but the Almiraj remains resilient. They do learn that the egg and mother owlbear were family of the now unconscious owlbear, that they had been enacting revenge on the man who so cold heartedly slaughtered his mate and took their child. The party pauses, trying to surmise how the scrawny man could have completed such a feat himself. Placing aside their suspicions of Arlin for the time being, the party instead tries to focus on their mission. Using Biscuit’s proclamation of being the guardian of the forest, Verena points out the other forces that have been taking wolves from the forest and experimenting with them, asking if helping these wolves would not be part of his duty. Eventually, Biscuit agrees to meet with the party again the next day at noon. Spoon leaves a small bit of treat for Biscuit, witnessing as the Almiraj takes the treat when they leave. 

  

Meanwhile, Lu and Grimrid, unable to speak with animals, engage in a bit of conversation that eventually leads to them lifting rocks and bonding over their perceived manliness. 

# Grimrid’s Deal

Continuing on, the party comes across a group of people. Men huddled around a broken carriage that sits both a girl and a black panther. The girl wears very dirty and ragged clothing compared to the men. Sensing that the girl might be in danger, Lu and Grimrid simply walk up to the group, pretending to be adventurers kindly lending a helping hand. Grimrid manages to ascertain the girl’s name, Ayla, although the men seem very cagey with information. Meanwhile as the two pretend to help and try to figure a way to help the girl, Spoon, Verena, and Garaele sneak around the other side, getting closer to the girl as they watch the group. They agree that the men are suspicious, though have not much proof to act on at the moment other than a sneaking suspicion.  
  
The three plan on backups, with Verena disguising herself as a cleric of Tymora, which Garaele comments is a nice look on the Tiefling. Whilst Spoon and Verena discuss their plans, agreeing to follow the men and attack the moment they see them doing anything untoward, Grimrid and Lu have other plans springing an attack on the men. Who turn out to be redcaps. The party takes down the redcaps swiftly, whilst protecting the girl. All except for Garaele who foolishly rushes in to greet death a second time that day. Once the combat ends, Lu steps over to Garaele, chastising her for her hastiness. Verena goes up to the girl, very clumsily wiping her tears, telling the child that she had survived once and will continue to survive more. The words do not comfort the girl. Instead, the others manage it better, calming the child enough where she shares that they were attacked by dogs who then gave her to the little men who said that an old lady promised not to hurt her. She cries for her parents and Garaele offers to take the child back to town, although the party are not optimistic about those odds given Garaele's penchant for danger.  
  
Just as they are lost, a voice speaks up, Cat appears before the party once more, offering to help lead the girl and Garaele back to town. Spoon and Verena are willing to accept Cat’s help with Verena asking for Cat’s aid with the thugs the two would face trying to enter town, which Cat readily agrees to. But Grimrid is more skeptical, unsure if he can wholeheartedly trust the emaciated looking Cat. He insists on them escorting the two back when a familiar voice calls out to him. Ru offers to help, reaching out to Grimrid in his mind. Ru suggests a contract telling Grimrid that payment of some kind is required for Ru’s services. They eventually settle on a mutual exchange of help, so that for each time Grimrid summons Ru, Ru can summon him in turn.  
  
With the contract signed, the two cement Ru’s devilry. Grimrid senses this, and tells Ru to never give up, assuring him that he is indeed deserving of the trust and faith Grimrid puts in him. So, without any visible action from Grimrid, Ru shows up, dressed like a butler as he greets Grimrid as “Master Grimrid”, who does the same to Ru before the two share a handshake.  Spoon cringes at the sight, not believing the incredibly suspicious show Grimrid and Ru have put on. Verena does not hide her disgust at this more “cleaned up” version of Ru. Meanwhile, Lu, who has never met Cat nor Ru is confused. He trusts his friends enough but also does his due diligence as he inquires about Ru. Though seeing Grimrid’s unwavering trust for the man is enough for Lu.  
  
Ru conjures a plate of cookies for the young girl, doing his best to ease the child. Which only makes Spoon and Verena sigh disapprovingly. With that, Ru will act as the child’s and Garaele’s physical body guard enlisted by Grimrid whilst Cat helps with his magic, smoothing over the more difficult parts of their journey as requested by both Spoon and Verena. To be polite, Grimrid expresses his gratitude to Cat, thanking him for his help when Cat politely mentions that he prefers the term “appreciative” instead. When they leave, Verena does point out to Spoon that it is odd that Ru just showed up like that, teasing the usual corny one liners Grimrid says to summon his newfound best friend. The two shrug and leave the matter, assuming that the Devil and Grimrid somehow got closer. 

# Gnoll and Githyanki corpses

With the girl and Garaele safe, the party continues on to find a battlefield. Corpses litter the ground, mostly Gnolls but two are of some kind of green skinned humanoids. The party looks at the items near the humanoids, noting a special sword and a whip. Grimrid leaves the weapon alone, seeing the humanoid that died with her blade as an honourable warrior who deserves to rest with the blade that she fought with til the end. Lu picks up the blade, making a promise to the corpse, hoping to honour her death by making good use of the blade to defeat the Gnolls as she did. Meanwhile, Verena analyses the other corpse, finding a tablet which she can’t read and nothing else as she turns her attention to the three headed flail and the larger Gnoll that lays before it. She realises that the creature is a Flind, an exceptionally strong and vicious gnoll whose parts can be used to craft potions and the like. Spoon analyses the green humanoid as well, finding the skull of an aberration that is gilded in silver and gems for eyes. He manages to ascertain that the skull can be used to summon undead. Spoon and Grimrid then turn to help Verena who wishes to carve the Flind’s body for its parts. As they leave the humanoid corpses, a presence sneaks up behind them. They turn around to see another green humanoid, this one alive as it demands for the weapon Lu had taken, calling the party names. Lu does see that the individual finds his wielding of their weapon to be extremely offensive, driving the weapon into the soil as he makes his point. Though the individual does not care, instead going on about his people, and someone called Vlaakith. At this point, Grimrid and Verena stop listening, walking off to leave the man who seems to enjoy his own voice. 

# Stealing Yeenoghu’s Power

Grimrid takes one look at the hyena bodies, then at the ritualistic circle behind them before making the executive decision that this was all magic related and therefore not his job as he kindly lets Verena take charge on this one. Verena studies the circle, recognising the power it connects to. A small portal into a much grander ocean of power that she could sneakily steal from. Or better yet, trick her way into. She calls over to Grimrid, telling her to bring him the Flind’s body as they place it on the circle, on top of the pile of rotting meat. She slits the Flind’s wrist, using its blood and body to mask her presence as she sneaks in, taking a portion of power for herself, strengthening the boon she had received from the Lord of Savagery, Yeenoghu.
