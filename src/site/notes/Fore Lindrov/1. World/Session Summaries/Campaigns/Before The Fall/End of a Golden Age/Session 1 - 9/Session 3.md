# Greyview

The party returns to Greyview, finding the place on fire. Grimrid, Derrick , and Spoon rush in to help with Verena walking right to the town hall to ensure the Baron lives to pay them for their efforts. During which, the party comes across a family escaping their house as a tree is about to fall on them. Grimrid jumps ahead, attempting to chop down the tree though it falls short as Derrick and Spoon jump in to help, alongside some magical guidance from Verena. They manage to save the civilians and soon arrive at the town hall. They’re told that others are inside and rush to help. Grimrid uses his stone cunning to identify where the survivors are, leading the rest to help.  
  
Grimrid, Derrick, and Spoon push through the smoke, saving everyone within the building whilst Verena trails behind, suffering from smoke inhalation and having an overall bad experience with helping people. They find the Baron’s office and see a trail of two sets of prints that morph into one as they follow it. From this, they deduce the Baron had been kidnapped. Having saved everyone, the party leaves the burning building and sees before them a female Fire Giant. Grimrid recognises the Fire Giant as “Jarchigudai”, the master smith woman who taught him the art of forging. Grimrid is notably quiet, an awkward air around as Grimrid tries to answer his mentor’s questions. Jarchigudai shares her reasons for travel, crafting a special divining rod for the Duke Zalto, though she does slip up on the name for a second.  
  
As the party mentions that the Baron might have been kidnapped, Jarchigudai theorizes that the baron could have been kidnapped for his noble blood. She urges the party to find the Baron, handing the party an item she had been crafting. Grimrid takes the Giant tuning fork from his master, focusing on the item as he feels arcane magic course through him. Grimrid casts the spell locate creature with a range of 10 miles. Grimrid turns to his party members in panic, needing the name and description of the Baron. Verena chips in, referring to the Baron as “Moneybags” whilst Spoon and Grimrid manage to recall the man’s name. With the spell active, the party has a trail to follow. 

# Sildar Hallwinter

Alongside Grimrid’s mentor is a man named Sildar with a scarred left eye. Sildar mentions the Townsfolk told him the Baron was in the Town Hall, asking if he was lost to the fire. The party reveals the same information as they did to Jarchigudai, that the Baron was kidnapped by somebody. Sildar expands on his purpose here, revealing that he is a Sellsword, and was hired by Jarchigudai to escort her, his expertise in that when he was a much younger man, he used to travel through these lands, with quite a fair number of friends here. Spoon manages to pick up that Sildar seems.. Unsettled, when he mentions the word friends, to which the young Kobold misunderstands that Sildar has many mates in the nearby area. Spoon proceeds to pester the man, asking him about his love life. Sildar then makes a false promise to revisit the conversation later, focusing on the still missing Baron. The party leaves, with Jarchigudai calling for Lu to stay behind and defend the Town with her, Grimrid, concentrating on the spell, leads the party towards where the Baron is. 

# The Forest

The magic leads them to a forest. Where Spoon begins hears a soft cacophony of whispers and noises. Deciding to inform Derrick, the two draconians wander off towards the noise, staying close to the ground, they crawl up to a bush where the source of cacophony is just behind and above the bush, and as soon as Spoon and Derrick peek out to see the source of the noise, Spoon suddenly finds himself back in the forest with his Party. He questions if they heard the noise, asking Derrick what he saw. However, the party and Derrick reveal that Spoon and them have been here this whole time, trudging through the forest following Grimrid. 

  

The party, suspicious of what has happened, tells Spoon to stay close, and they remain on guard as they make their way through the Forest, eventually arriving near a cave’s entrance. Before they step any closer, Spoon hears movement coming from behind a grouping of rocks and warns of a potential ambush, spotting snare traps next to the trees. However during Spoon’s attempt to disarm one of the traps, the Psi Goblins spring their attack on the party. But two of the five Goblins are strangely unarmed, and the party learns that they are supercharged with psionic powers, exploding with psychic energy upon death.

# Pork

After defeating the Psi Goblins, the party enters the cave, where above a series of natural stone bridges, Spoon hears noises coming from the caverns below. Grimrid provides some rope that Spoon uses to lower himself down and chances upon a corpse that he does not recognise, but the creature was clearly in pain, its arms warped into weird hands that clearly could not support its weight, and its beaked mouth having human-like teeth implanted into the bone, and its throat so mangled that it clearly couldn’t have been able to breathe properly. 

  

During this investigation, Spoon hears soft cries coming from a partially collapsed patch of soil on his left, detaching the rope he used to descend, which prompts Verena to follow after the Kobold. Behind the wall is a secret tunnel, where Spoon finds a terrified Baby Bulette alongside Verena, who inadvertently scares the Bulette, however thanks to soft babying by Spoon and an offer of food, the Bulette calms, and reveals to the Party that it feared it’s thoughts, that sharp things touched its head. And upon coming out of his hiding spot, revealed the mangled creature that Spoon found was in fact its mother, who the Bulette believes was sleeping. Not wanting to reveal its mother’s death, Verena lies to the little Bulette, saying its Mother had made them in charge of it while its mother sleeps, gaining the little Bulette’s trust. 

  

Spoon and Verena return with the Bulette, who Spoon has decided to name “Pork”, Grimrid gruffly dismisses the little Bulette, seemingly uncaring of the now newly crowned party pet.

# Bruma, Thoom, Ru

  

The party continues on when Grimrid is able to use his stone cunning to see two figures. The party enters the room to find Bruma who begins monologuing and gets interrupted by Grimrid. The party fights alongside with a new ally who’s joined them in combat. The ally is none other than the tall adventurer they had met earlier who the party believes to be Thoom. With Bruma dead, the party questions Thoom, forcing her to reveal her identity. The party fights Thoom who transforms into a fire elemental. The situation is bad, horrible, as Spoon is dying and Thoom is about to attack, Grimrid jumps forward, trying to grab the fire elemental as he fails and falls over the ledge himself. As Grimrid falls, he hears a voice that offers help. Grimrid accepts as magic surges through Grimrid once more, casting yet another spell that summons an angel. With the stranger’s help the party manages to survive, killing Thoom in the process. The angel introduces himself as Ruinora Schiffer, angel of the Morninglord. He explains that Grimrid drew his gaze and that he could not turn a blind eye, thus deciding to ignore the edict to help the party. Verena is skeptical of the Angel, mainly for his celestial nature but as she observes him she realises that he is lying. She calls him out for it, causing guilt to wash over his face as he drops the disguise, revealing his true form. In his place stands dripping corruption, a blacked winged angel with infernal red horns, his fur dishevelled, hagged, and his feet hooved. Ru is no angel but a devil. Ru defends that he did once serve the Morninglord but fell when Archangel Zariel did. Regardless, Grimrid and Spoon are unphased, still happy to befriend the person who helped them in their time of need. Verena dislikes the liar but offers that Ru looks better in his current form than his angelic one. Grimrid offers Ru a hand, accepting the blessing from the ex-angel. With that, Ru leaves. Before the party can do much, Thoom’s scalp splits open, an intellect devourer emerging from her scalp. 

  

The intellect devourer tries to run but the party gives chase, forcing it to attack them instead. It goes for Grimrid, intending to take over his body but fortunately the rest manages to kill the intellect devourer, saving Grimrid. With all obstacles cleared, the party heads downstairs to find the Baron. They first assess him, and once he doesn’t turn into a controlled husk like Thoom was, Verena leaves the whining Baron, examining the different items. She breaks all the jars of brains and stabs the wolf brain that was left open just to be sure. Lastly, Verena takes the very sharp metal scalpel, made from an unknown source. With that settled, they leave. Pork comes out of hiding, noticing the very dirtied Grimrid as he wades over, trying to nudge the dirt off the dwarf. And as Grimrid asks for Verena’s help, the tiefling prestidigitates the dwarf. 

# Back to the Village

  

The party returns back to the village where Jarchigudai and Sildar had been helping, alongside Lu. At Jarchigudai’s prodding the party shares their initial reasons for being in the village, the letter that they were tasked to deliver. Jarchigudai suggests that she might be able to help them if they were to help her, she tells the party to rest for the time being and find her later as she has a mission for the four of them. At around this time, the party approaches Sildar, with Spoon asking Sildar of his preferences in mates and prodding for more information when the man relents, agreeing to introduce his mate to the party when they’re in Phandalin. 

# Lu and the Letter

  

Lu returns to the party, sharing that Jarchigudai has a mission for them. But Spoon and Verena notice a discrepancy in the numbers, as Jarchigudai has specified a mission for the four of them, Spoon, Derrick, Verena, and Grimrid. Just as the two are counting and figuring it out, Lu takes out the letter from his pocket, disappearing into it before Grimrid’s eyes. Grimrid is stunned, bringing the incident to the other’s attention as he very hesitantly picks up the letter. Spoon however, takes it over fearlessly. Nothing happens. Verena suggests that they place the letter in her bag, using mage hand to do so as they leave the probably cursed letter in her bag. 

  

The Baron approaches the party, handing them a thousand gold that Grimrid surprisingly suggests that the Baron keep the money for the village as they would require the funds to rebuild themselves. Fortunately, the Baron rejects the offer, saying that they have other means to get the funds and Verena takes the money before more can be said. The party splits the money evenly, leaving 9 silvers worth for groceries and 1 silver for snacks. 

# Maplehusk

Before the day ends, the party figures it best that they visit Maplehusk once more and eliminate the remaining two arsonist druids. At the entrance of the grove, the party notices a lack of Maplehusk. They enter the grove, only to find the druids dead, and Maplehusk torn from branch to branch. The party surmises that more aberratic creatures must have shown up, killing the druids and Maplehusk in the process. Derrick is upset, expressing his feelings towards having his friend killed. Grimrid tells Derrick of the harsh realities of the world, whilst Verena takes a different approach, suggesting that Derrick find the ones who did this, make them suffer the same and relish in their pain. With that, the party returns to the village to help the ones that are still alive. 

# Jarchigudai’s Mission

  

The party approaches Jarchigudai and learns of a place called the Forge of spells. Grimrid recognises the name, sharing that the forge is said to enable whoever works it to access the powers of the Anvil of Moradin himself. The Forge is said to be able to craft spells and magic that doesn’t exist with true proper dwarven magic. Jarchigudai’s mission for the party is that they would help her locate the forge, and in exchange she would be able to use her connections to send the party to where they desire. The party agrees, but Jarchigudai recommends they rest before embarking on their next journey. Verena takes out the scalpel she had taken before, asking Jarchigudai if she knows what sort of material it is. Jarchigudai turns the question to Grimrid, who is able to answer to some extent, sharing that they have found similar metals before. At her student’s response, Jarchigudai remarks the Grimrid is not as incompetent as he thinks himself to be. Having enough of Jarchigudai’s manner of speech, Verena points out to Jarchigudai that her methods of teaching may not align with Grimrid, suggesting that just as how different blades and weapons require different techniques, so too does her student. Jarchigudai doesn’t take offence at this, instead telling Grimrid that he has made some friends along the way. 

# Downtime

Spoon spends his days cleaning the houses. Verena steps out to forage for tea and gets dragged by Grimrid to help with his metalworks, which Verena denies. She instead heads to the administrators, complimenting them on their work to avoid having to do manual labour and boost morale. She returns to brag to Grimrid of her efforts, which the dwarf can see through the prideful facade she puts up. Grimrid gives Verena a hair stick, one with a flower at the end with death motifs. As Verena receives the gift, Grimrid comments that it is not his best work. At that, Verena simply replies that she does not use any subpar products whilst placing the pin in her hair. The party rests for 10 days, helping out the village best they can before leaving for Phandalin to catch up with Sildar and Jarchigudai. 

  

# The Ox

As they travel, they come across a mound on the horizon. Getting close, the mound is blue, a blue ox to be precise. The Ox is dead, clear signs of battle about, and worst of all its master is missing.
