Finishing up their short rest, Spoon attunes to the dragon dagger taken from the dead cultist member, sensing the word “greed” from it. With that settled, the party leaves, heading back to Loog to hand him the food and set off into the direction of the cultists. They have a general plan to disguise themselves as cultists. 

# Kobold Cultists at Wizard’s Camp

Along the way, they come across a group of kobolds looting from a camp site. Spoon approaches the kobolds, speaking to their leader, a kobold with dragon wings. The leader, Scrimp, demands to know why the party is there, his conversation with Spoon quickly turns to insults as he calls metallic dragons stinky, which Spoon refutes and the two continue bickering whilst the party continues through using the title of the “blessed”. Tormund enters the tent, finding kobolds who are taking a bunch of papers. He intimidates them into letting him have them instead and find a series of magic scrolls. Verena and Grimrid head to the chest, the former curious about the magic that seems to come from it. Etched onto the box is a very arrogant warning, signed by a self proclaimed prodigy of magic. Curious as to what the magic does, Verena proceeds to cast detect magic. Meanwhile back with Spoon and Scrimp, Spoon asks Scrimp how he got his wings, which Scrimp reveals was granted to him by his master. Spoon then challenges Scrimp to fly, pushing the kobold to show off and proclaim that he’s better than Spoon. Scrimp also reveals certain information about Glassstaff, calling him the great dragon. That spider who used to be a drow wizard also serves Glassstaff in Kragmaw castle. 

  

Back at the chest, as Grimrid’s impatience kicks in he opens the chest, triggering the trap within that summons a spectre. The spectre is easily dealt with and afterwards they find a Dwarven splint armour in the chest, a boyhood armour to be specific. The armour belongs to someone from the Rockseeker clan but it is heavily damaged. Alongside the armour is also a note written in Elvish, a reminder to look for someone to repair said armour. 

  

Meanwhile, the Kobolds are enamoured by Derrick, who greatly enjoys the attention and demands the kobolds give him tribute, which they immediately obey as they scamper off to take stuff. Verena also finds a canopic jar when one of the kobolds comes over, demanding the item. She refuses, and the kobold insists. But before she is able to do much, Vulcan steps in, successfully getting the kobold to step back. The jar is then left in Vulcans’ care. As the kobolds continue to take everything, they anger Grimrid when they disrespect the Dwavern armour. Grimrid swings his hammer, letting it loose as he kills the Kobold, starting a fight between the kobolds and the party. When the fight ends, Grimrid gets a stern talking to as Derrick suggests Grimrid say something to the kobold if he is indeed guilty, which Grimrid does, praying that the kobold finds his peace, suggesting that the first armour that he makes will be for kobolds. Spoon heals the wounded kobolds, waking them up as Derrick provides them with new instructions, telling them to rest for two days. 

  

The rest of the party look around the area of the wizard’s camp, finding his spell scrolls and a Wight creation ritual which Vulcan comments is an unpleasant form of magic. Looking through their items, Verena takes out a potion she took from before, casually mentioning its benefits and potential side effects as it did come from a hag. Which Tormund takes and as part of his “identify” spells, drinks. He becomes stronger, as the potion was supposed to trigger and seems to have no adverse effects. 

# Helembriar

The party comes across a magic circle that was used to activate multiple scrolls of “animate dead” simultaneously. The same botched scrolls of animate dead that lack the component granting the caster control of the undead. Following the path, the party encounters the swarms of undead, easily killing the undead as they finally find the self proclaimed genius wizard Helembriar. 

  

Alongside Helembriar is a dwarf. Grimrid addresses the Dwarf who does not respond. Upon inspection, Spoon realises that the Dwarf does not breathe and informs Grimrid of this fact. Grimrid then attacks the Elf, demanding that he explain what has happened to the Dwarf. The Elf does not desire to cooperate, seeing as he was attacked and continues to berate and belittle the party. 

  

Grimrid is somewhat subdued, brought to the bell and tied up there whilst Pork accompanies him. Spoon manages to deescalate the situation, getting Helembriar to talk. Helembriar shares that Glassstaff knows little magic that he is a fool despite the high levels of magic he displayed. In exchange for his life Helembriar offers the party a scroll of his own making, taking back his other spell scrolls before he is allowed to leave. Just as he does, Zyraxes takes hold of the undead dwarf, ending its unlife swiftly as Vulcan drops a bag of 50 000 gp as compensation for taking the Elf’s minion. 

  

Zyraxes provides an insight into their actions, sharing that necromancy is normal where they come from, although their version of it involved explicit consent from the people before their death to be raised. The party frees Grimrid and Vulcan offers Grimrid a chance to speak to the Dwarf. Speaking with him, Grimrid learns the Dwarf’s, Dermek’s story, of a young dwarf who was eager to forge a life worthy of a story. 

  

That he wishes to be remembered as the man who found the robber that had stolen a block of adamantine from his family’s crypt, that he did not abandon his clan. How he wishes for them to know that he was no coward and had fought the elf til he died. To this, Grimrid swears a blood debt to be paid the next time he comes across Helembriar. As for the final question, seeing as Grimrid had nothing left to ask, Zyraxes jumps in, asking Dermek for his favourite colour as Zyraxes vows to wear a dark blue ribbon for the fallen dwarf. Verena gentle reposes the body, ensuring that it won’t be raised anytime soon as the party leaves once more. 

# Infiltrating the Dragon Cult

They eventually arrive at the cultist lair and are able to trick the guards into letting them in using the title of the “blessed” whilst the others pretend to be new initiates. Continuing past the trapped bit of forest, the party discusses their plans and Verena decides that she would indeed pretend to be the blessed, trusting herself to leverage the position for their gain. 

  

Committed to the plan, the party appears before an ice wizard with a whip of dragonscale, alongside him is a drow who seems to bear resentment towards the dragon cultists. The Chosen starts asking Verena questions, to which she tries her best to respond with the same zealotry as the cultist they had met. However as the Chosen begins speaking ill of metallic dragons, Spoon can’t help but speak up which causes the Chosen to force Verena to enact some kind of punishment against Spoon. Fortunately, the Chosen is easily pleased and the party manages to trick the Chosen, though for how much longer is unclear.
