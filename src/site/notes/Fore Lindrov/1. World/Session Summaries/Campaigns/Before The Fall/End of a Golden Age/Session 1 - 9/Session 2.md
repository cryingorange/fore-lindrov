# Greyview

The party soon reaches Greyview, arriving at a town of simple yet sturdy buildings. Two structures are of note: an Inn and a modest but dignified town hall. From within the town hall comes commotion. The party enters, following Baron Cedric Valen. The townsfolk stare at the new arrivals, the odd bunch made up of a dwarf, tiefling, kobold, and dragon, the farmers seem surprised at the Baron having decided to show up in person, and an individual catches the eye of the party.. A 7 foot tall, muscled, Human Woman dressed in leather armour and a scurrying rat on her shoulder, her jet black hair tied in a ponytail. 

  

The party ignores her for now, following the Baron as he begins to address the complainant farmers. He attempts to appease them, promising them that he would tend to their worries, heralding the party as the individuals who saved him and his guards, that with such capable adventurers at his side, their problems will certainly be solved. The townsfolk, assessing the party find them extremely unimpressive, telling the Baron and the party of the burning of their crops, the Human farmers accuse the Fire Giants as the culprits, citing their obvious affiliation with Fire as evidence. They even go so far as to accuse the Goliaths of trying to outcompete them, something that fractures the faction of farmers as racial tensions begin to brew. The shouting and fighting overpowers the Baron’s voice of reason as it seems like the town is about to erupt into chaos.

  

Luckily, Verena mutters an incantation in an ancient language, using a spell to calm the emotions of the crowd, giving a moment for the Baron to speak. The Baron assures his people that he has enlisted the help of the party, promising to get to the bottom of the situation. He says that he will personally look through every request, that he will use his own resources to provide support to those who need it. Ushering the farmers to the administrators of the Hall to take their requests so that he may review them. The farmers are clearly still unhappy, but this satiates their whining for a little while as the Baron gives them a channel to voice their issues. 

  

# Post Meeting

  

With the meeting adjourned, Spoon heads towards the tall woman from before. He approaches with his usual positive disposition but is immediately dismissed by the woman as she insists that she works alone and has no need for anyone else. The adventurer begins walking away, and Verena casts speak with animals. With Spoon as a distraction to hinder the woman’s exit, Verena is able to pick up on what the rat is muttering. The rat speaks of destruction, swearing to burn down the village. The party does find this rather concerning, but are interrupted by the Baron, requesting that they approach a farmer to gather more information and find out who exactly is burning the crops of the farmers. He gives the party an amulet he received from Chieftess Guh, a reminder of his powerful connections, and the ease of travel he could provide the party should they help him. The only inkling of the clearly magical amulet’s function being that Chieftess Guh told the Baron is “Keep Man Alive”, however Grimrid, with his experience in Giant Runes is able to identify that the “Life” Rune is carved into the amulet’s surface. By the time the party finishes their conversation with the Baron, they realise the strange, tall woman has already disappeared with her fire-hungry rat.

  

# Old Man Herbert

The party ask around for more information, and are directed to a farmers who claims his farm was recently burnt down, a man they all refer to as Old Man Herbert. The party finds themselves in conversation with a grumpy, whiny old man, who makes outlandish claims that he saw the Giants stomping on his crops, that they burned not just his farm, but his home and his farm. He laments that the party are freeloaders, who drain the money from hard working folk like him and pay themselves fat salaries from their tax coffers. Verena, gently, and somewhat threateningly reminds him to watch his tongue and how they speak to them, something that Herbert tries to oblige begrudgingly, telling them that he will bring them to his farm.

  

# Herbert’s Farm

  

Approaching Herbert’s farm, the party sees that only the crops have been burnt and all his claims of damages to his home and animals are false. Spoon, following the complaints, approaches the house and begins cleaning the house and notices some trails. The trail leads to a massive footprint. However upon closer inspection, the Kobold notices that the footprint appears artificial. Spoon theorises that whoever dug it must have been intending to frame the Giants and that they must have acquired a Giant’s foot to stamp into the ground. 

  

Meanwhile, Grimrid heads over to a fallen tree, who Herbert claims is clear evidence of a Giant’s presence, citing that no other creature could fell such a massive tree and attempts to confirm Herbert’s suspicions. His investigation instead yields a strange pattern that he can’t decipher. Meanwhile, Verena spends some time speaking to Herbert, noticing the inconsistency in his complaints. She questions if he had felt any tremors of sorts to prove a Giant’s presence, yet Herbert begrudgingly says that he did not, coming up with excuses and appearing unable to admit that his suspicions might be wrong and his prejudice unjustified. 

  

Verena then approaches Grimrid, observing the tree alongside the Dwarf as she realises that the tree has been felled with the use of a thornwhip spell as she notices strange holes in the great tree’s trunk. Verena also points out a small grouping of vegetation that stands out from the local flora pointing it out to Grimrid and Spoon. Grimrid, despite looking RIGHT AT the vegetation, is unable to notice anything, seeing just a pile of grass with no remarkable difference, however Spoon is able to identify this differentiation and upon investigation identifies this vegetation as plantlife that would grow near a pond. The party realises they would have to ask the grumpy old man, Herbert for more information, and push the responsibility upon Grimrid, citing the Dwarf’s general stubbornness and grumpiness a fair match to the old man’s. Grimrid goes and somehow, with a fair bit of frustration, filibustering and enduring sarcastic insults manages to acquire the information they need, and are led towards a forest where such plant life would be growing. 

  

Druid’s Forest

  

The party strides off and soon arrives at the beginning of the forest. Grimrid notices some druidic markings and footpaths that seem to follow said markings, the carvings nearly imperceptible, carvings that the rest of the party are completely unable to spot. Though erring on the side of caution, the party follows Grimrid closely and soon arrive at a cliff with markings for them to head up. Grimrid climbs up first, then lets down a rope which Verena tries to climb but fails miserably, slipping down multiple times. 

  

Derrick, Spoon’s Gold Dragon Wyrmling Ward, gives it a shot, easily climbing up with the help of the rope. Then Spoon tries, succeeding easily as well. All that is left is Verena, who attempts for the third and final time. She slips, and in an effort to save what’s left of her pride, she teleports twice, making it up the cliff. 

  

The party comes across a ridge, boldly crossing it as a group of wolves, who were stalking them since they entered the forest, come from behind, intending to hunt and kill the party. The wolves soon realise that the party are no easy target as Grimrid transforms, becoming large as he takes on several wolves himself. Spoon and Derrick handle some of the wolves themselves, showcasing their combat prowess. Verena does not, getting incapacitated by the pollen from the flowers. Though she recovers just in time to take out the last wolf. 

  

Finally at the end of the markings, the party find themselves before a giant grove of vines, protected by a Giant, long-winded Tree named Maplehusk, who introduces himself to the party as they approach the Grove’s entrance. Maplehusk kindly heals the party of their injuries, a far cry from the expected hostility, he reveals that he is a close personal friend of the Wildfire Druids that protect this forest, and goes into multiple tangents and long-winded stories of his time growing in this forest, with one of particularly amusing story of squirrels stuffing his ears full of acorns, as a corroborative piece of evidence to the party that they should NEVER trust a Squirrel. The party uses this story as a springboard to convince Maplehusk to allow them to seek out the Druids citing that they are hunting a sort of “squirrel” who has been burning and destroying nearby crops and trees, an act that Maplehusk denies that his friends would ever commit. 

  

Maplehusk denies entry to the party due to Druids being deep in meeting, the old tree tells the party of the leader of the Druid Grove, Thoom, a Goliath Druid that he has been friends with for a long time, lamenting that Thoom has since stopped saying hello to him ever since she found a strange Black Seed in the forest. The party use this as yet another springboard to say that perhaps the Druids are being misguided, or perhaps they could help the party with these burnings, promising Maplehusk that if it was indeed them that committed this horrible act, that the party would give them a stern talking to, thus gaining entry into the Grove.

  

# The Grove

Entering the grove, the party find themselves meeting with the acting leader of the Druids, Hurlga, with Thoom nowhere in sight. The druids claim that Man has brought nothing but death and destruction to the forest, that nature screams in pain at their industry, their metallurgy, that only Fire can cleanse this land to return it back to the green. The druids are manic, enraged, and the party in no mood to speak to people who clearly cannot be talked down, attack the Druids. They kill the entire circle, including Hurlga, leaving two of the Druids alive. An interrogation and investigation of the Druid’s bodies and that of those left alive reveal that the Druids appear to have been altered, stitch marks line their foreheads as the party knocks the still living Druids out to figure out what steps to take next. 

  

The party find journals, items and realise that there is another Druid who is also not present, Hurlga’s sister, Bruma, via a dagger that was gifted to Hurlga etched with Bruma’s name has joined Thoom to complete some kind of ritual that will burn all of Greyview into ash. In the druid’s rooms, they find journal entries that suggest the Druids valued cooperation, and did not hold these beliefs until very recently. The journals also reveal that one of the Druids was found dead by Thoom, that Thoom told them the Druid was killed by people of Greyview, sparking this supposed war against all. 

  

Within Thoom’s locked room the party find papers, books and metal items, a strange fact especially with the Druids’ rhetoric of being against industry. They also find a black canister, clearly the “seed” that Maplehusk had mentioned prior containing fluid inside, a series of notes reveal that Thoom has been trying to identify this canister and has spent countless days trying to open it, with her last entry indicating that she may have found a way to do so.  
  
Impressed upon a map on the table above the canister are markings that reveal burn sites throughout the surrounding area of Greyview, and etched into it is an image of the Baron, and the Town of Greyview circled. Verena finds a series of books of no particular import, but Spoon finds strange crystals and gems holding some kind of power within them that would be able to affect spells as well as various carved Spell Gems that Grimrid poorly explains enable someone to cast Disguise Self, Invisibility and Dispel Magic. It is here that the party realise that the tall Adventurer must have been Thoom in Disguise, and her rat a Wildshaped Bruma and that they must be looking to initiate whatever Ritual it is that will destroy Greyview at the town itself, and that tens will lose their lives, and potentially this fire will rage throughout all the lands, and hundreds more will die. As the party rushes out to return to Town, they lie once more to Maplehusk as the tree asks them how their talks with the Druids went, saying the Druids are busy, and that the party has business to attend to. The party rush off to Greyview, not knowing what they might find.
