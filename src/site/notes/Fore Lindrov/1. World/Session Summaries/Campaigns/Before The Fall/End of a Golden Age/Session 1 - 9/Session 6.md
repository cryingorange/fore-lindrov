# Mayor Harbin

The party gets up the next morning, barely done with breakfast when they hear a  commotion outside. The Mayor tells them to come out and they are greeted by a big group of people. An angry Goliath glares at the party, accusing them of the murder of Gregor. Despite the party’s rebuttal the goliath pays no mind, blinded with rage. A dwarf speaks out, dressed in fine armour and a flowing white cape which Grimrid realises is made of dragonscale. Grimrid also realises that the dwarf’s hammer has runes of renown and is made of ancient oak with dwarven patterns adorning it. The dwarf introduces himself as Thrain Thunderaxe, who Grimrid only knows is a dwarven lord, found guilty of treason. Grimrid speaks to the Dwarf in Dwarvish, revealing his knowledge of the dragonscale cape, which Thrain does acknowledge but refuses to give anymore information. From what little he shares, they learn that he is the leader of the redbrands and that Kira, the angry Goliath, was Gregor’s partner. She seeks revenge on those who have killed her lover, despite the party’s insistence of their innocence. Thrain tells them that the matter will not cease until they accept Kira’s challenge of combat. Which the party does, easily beating Kira and her companions before Thrain calls the battle to an end, not wanting more of his people dead. With that settled, the party prepares to leave for the day. Garaele insists on following the party, which the party agrees but tells her that she is to follow them and not rush head in to danger. 

# Lady of the Lake

The party arrives near the lake, noticing an odd red light about the area. As they approach, they see the lake filled with a red liquid. Upon investigating, Verena finds a journal detailing the horrid acts of a serial killer. Garaele begins to read but stops due to the grotesque nature of the journal. Spoon finds a tombstone nearby, with the name “Elowen” carved into it. They manage to surmise that “Elowen” is the lady of the lake. Garaele shares with the party the passage to summon the lady of the lake, which the party repeats and successfully summons her. The spirit was confrontational at first but as the party notices her obsession with Sildar, they play into it, positioning themselves as friends of Sildar and people who can help her get Sildar back to her side. This is when she reveals the doll that she has is a gift from her friend, one that would trap Sildar’s soul within if he died. Elowen shares the history between her and Sildar, how she had stayed in the lodge and was murdered by the person who lived there, tossed into the lake when Sildar arrived, heroically attempting to save her. But she also shares that Sildar had fallen for another, someone who had stolen his sword, the very same sword that he has a penchant for misplacing. From Elowen, the party learns that Sildar had allegedly been in Phandalin a hundred years ago. As much as the party would like to stop Elowen at the moment, they are also aware that the red lake means her powers are stronger than usual. Ultimately, they decide to leave fighting her to another day, when she isn’t empowered and hope that Sildar does not die in the time being. 

# Cat’s Game

Using the directions provided by Elowen towards her friend’s house, the party soon enters a swamp-like area. As they walk in, Pork and Spoon sense a presence behind them, though Verena and Grimrid fail to spot anything. The figure does make itself known, a cat-like spirit appears before the party, offering to act as their guide through the swamp, so long as they play a game. The first part of the game is a riddle, which the party does manage to answer correctly. The second party is a game of chase, the party is to capture some mice. During which a group of weird creatures that repeatedly introduce themselves as Mr Frundles. The party focuses on capturing the mice, ignoring the weird Frundles. Though eventually are forced to confront them as Grimrid fights them off single handedly, getting charmed by the Frundles for a bit before the rest of the party steps in to help. Finishing the task set out by Cat, the party arrives at the final game, a set of two riddles. They answer the first after some hesitation, and the second they struggle with. As they provide the wrong answers, the party becomes more rat-like with each wrong answer, sprouting whiskers and tails. They eventually get it right as Cat leaves them with one final gift, a stone of polymorph and inscribed upon it, a final hint, “Your knife is necessary, but not yet sufficient. Collect what's useful first.. Reject only your ignorance, and you may survive, unless you're ready to deal. Perhaps both is the way.” 

# Deal with Hag

  

The party is mostly clueless but picks up the stone nonetheless. As they knock on the hut’s door, there is no response and the party decides to enter. The owner of the hut is Elmina who bluntly states her annoyance and tells the party that they are not welcomed for the lying and allegedly stealing Verena committed against the Hag. Regardless, Grimrid tries to negotiate with the hag, seeking the gnoll’s camp. Grimrid plays right into Elmina’s hand, eventually offering up his craftsmanship for the information. Elmina drags it out, dismissing Grimrid’s craftsmanship which Verena does speak up for. Elmina brings up the eye, although quickly refuses and mentions that she has already procured more, showing the party a pair of wolf eyes. Spoon and Verena both notice Elmina's reaction towards the wolf eyes. The two, piecing together the hints provided by Cat, surmise that Elmina is allergic to canines. They withhold themselves, agreeing that a deal should be struck first before the party ambushes Elmina. They eventually settle on the price being Grimrid's craftsmanship and the party's labour. Elmina tells the party to find the being within the swamp that has been annoying her, and demands for the party to bring them before her. The party agrees and as part of Elmina's ritual, Grimrid drinks out of the cat's bowl, fulfilling his end of the deal. 

# Mr Frundles

The party then heads out, back to the area where cat brought them as they ponder the last hint given by Cat. They surmise that the enemy in this case must refer to the Frundles, that they are to somehow work with the creature to battle Elmina. As they approach, they hear the horrid noises of Mr Frundles. By party vote, Grimrid casts comprehend languages to converse with Mr Frundles. As Mr Frundles longs to see the world, he takes interest in Spoon and Derrick, asking if they would help him. Without a moment's hesitation, Derrick agrees and Mr Frundles joins with Derrick. Derrick still retains most of himself with Mr Frundles inhibiting a part of his mind. Derrick is also able to speak Mr Frundles’ language, commanding the army of Frundles easily. From Derrick, the party learns that Mr Frundles is on the run from Maglubiyet for offending the goblinoid deity. 

# Fight with Hag

The party returns to Elmina, barely presenting Frundles to her before they strike. During which, Garaele uses her divine powers to support the party, proving to be quite an eyesore for Elmina. Elmina chases after the cleric, and takes Garaele hostage in an attempt to negotiate with the party. Spoon and Grimrid step forward, sincere in their desire to save Garaele whilst Verena takes it as an opportunity to get closer, the latter managing to hide her intentions from the hag. They approach and Elmina demands for her freedom in exchange for Garaele’s life. Grimrid takes initiative to bargain, demanding that Elmina leave the village as well to which Elmina demands for more, wanting the heads that her redcaps had failed to acquire. During this, Spoon and Verena communicate, agreeing to strike the second they have the chance to. When the opportunity arises, Spoon uses the polymorph stone from Cat on Verena, transforming the tiefling into a Wolf as Elmina begins to have a sneezing fit. The party defeats Elmina easily this time with Pork taking the killing blow as he enjoys his meal for the day. Just as they realise that they had not yet acquired the information from Elmina, the magic of the deal animates Elmina's body as she utters the information. With that, Cat shows up, speaking about the death of Elmina. On cat’s body are some letters written in Sylvan which Verena manages to notice. Based off what cat has shared, it claims that the source of the hag’s immortality is in his name, the letters on his body might spell just that. At the party's insistence, he undoes the ratkin transformation on the party and provides the party with one last cryptic hint, telling them to check the basement for the real Elmina.

# The Real Elmina

The party do so, taking their time to investigate the hut. As they enter, they're greeted by Frundlings repeating their rather annoying catchphrase. Derrick commands the Frundlings to leave, telling them to head in a direction. The party then heads to the basement and finds a tree leaking sap into a bowl. Spoon notices sticks placed before the tree and breaks one, casting the spell to speak with plants. Now able to understand the tree, he learns that she is the real Elmina, who had approached the hag when she was pregnant. As part of her deal with the hag, she had been turned into a tree, forced to stay here whilst the hag tortured her with the lack of information of her family. She begs for Spoon’s help, who shares this with the party and assures Elmina that he would try, though limited by what he is able to do as Elmina begs and cries for her family and for the party to stay with them despite Spoon’s efforts to convince Elmina that they would not be able to stay. Grimrid asks Garaele if she is able to do anything, having heard of the miracles performed by clerics. Unfortunately, Garaele admits that she is not able to do much at the moment, unable to perform such high level magic. Seeing the helpless situation, Verena breaks a stick as well, speaking to Elmina who once more pleads for the party's help, getting her to at least agree to let the party leave so that they could possibly try to help her situation. 

  

The party returns back to the surface, ransacking through the hag's stuff. Grimrid breaks the hag's cauldron, destroying her craft as she did his. Spoon and Verena pilfer from the garden, the former finding spices to use in his cooking and the latter finding a small magical plant. Exhausted from their fight with the hag, the party takes a short rest in the hag's hut.
