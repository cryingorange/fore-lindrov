# Tormund

Just as the party is about to continue on their mission, they hear a rumbling approach. A very tall minotaur shows up, with arrows sticking out of his body. He proclaims that he is here to help, referring to himself as the great Arch Magus Tormund. Grimrid recognises the individual, tossing a pouch of 50 gold pieces to the Minotaur, telling him to return the axe he had taken. Though Tormund refuses, claiming that he had taken the item fair and square. Spoon and Derrick speak up, recognising Tormund from the time they had entered his maze and were rewarded with a spell scroll that could cast any spell they wanted. Spoon shares that they have kept the prized scroll at home for safekeeping. Tormund also recognises Verena, referring to her as a fellow Arch Magus. Sildar reacts as any other human would, raising his weapon at Tormund. Used to this, Tormund retrieves a letter he has written by Lady General Marina. The letter attests for Tormund's good nature and with the backing of the Lady General, is enough to persuade most people.  

# Magnanimous

Continuing on with their investigation, the party revisits the mirror. Spoon and Verena look into it as the vague appearance of a man shows up. He claims to be trapped in the mirror and introduces himself as Arch Magus Magnanimous. Magnanimous claims to have travelled to the Abyss, specifically Tanatos, as part of his passionate studies and in doing so accidentally got himself stuck in the mirror. He asks for the party’s help, pleading at them to help him leave. But despite his best efforts, Grimrid and Spoon sense something amiss. Magnanimous cries that he can no longer live like that, that the Abyss does things to a person. Grimrid points out that Magnanimous could be a demon and continues to sense that something is off. Tormund feels a weird sense of familiarity, of kinship with Magnanimous, smelling copper rotten smell that reminds him of home. Initially, Tormund is ready to help a fellow Arch Magus, but Verena stops him, explaining that a proper Arch Magus would have figured out how to escape on their own. Grimrid also spots writing on the back of the mirror, “Death” in Abyssal as recognised by Verena. Eventually, with his sob story, Grimrid relents, beginning the very ominous chants as instructed by Magnanimous. Though sensing that something is still wrong, Verena steps in at the last moment, casting a spell at the circle to disrupt it. But Magnanimous manages to escape, revealing his true form of a demon. The demon possesses Grimrid, and combat ensues as the rest panic about what to do. They try to fight the demon, though only seem to be hurting Grimrid physically before Grimrid snaps out of it, regaining control of his body from the Demon. In his mind however, the demon continues to taunt Grimrid. He brings up the craft that Grimrid lost, taking on the form of Grimrid’s father as he tells him what a disappointment he is, going as far to say that he is glad Grimrid’s mother had died before she could see what Grimrid has become. Grimrid attacks the demon, “killing” his father as the demon resumes his taunting, telling Grimrid that this is what he wants, to kill his father and take his place, that he is a useless thing, undeserving of the name of “Embersmith”, but not to worry for they will be there for him. 

# Thrain’s Room

Continuing past, the party enters what seems to be Thrain’s room. Grimrid heads to the forge that doesn’t seem to spew any smoke. Beside the forge is a battleaxe. Grimrid analyses the item, sensing the sadness of its creator, the longing and abandonment. It’s clear that Thrain had made the weapon and had left it there. Grimrid opts not to take the weapon himself, wanting to respect Thrain. Sildar however, takes the weapon, not wanting to waste the resources available. Meanwhile, Spoon and Tormund see a beast. They try talking to it and learn that it can speak. Spoon manages to get information out of the beast, Eater. Eater shares that the short meat had brought a nice and plump meal for him, that he is now washing the round meat. Spoon does try to bribe Eater with food, though not enough for Eater. When Spoon asks if Eater can bring them to the round meat, Eater agrees to help if the party approaches, though he does look like he wants to eat the party as they decide not to approach. 

  

In the bedroom, the party finds a chest with a mithril lock. Grimrid does not recognise the type of metal and Tormund tries to open the chest but fails, Verena gives it a shot as well but also fails. Spoon makes an improvised lockpick and attempts to pick the lock, succeeding. Inside lies a Dwarven Plate armour, and his family axe. The Dwarven plate tells of Thrain’s accomplishments. Derrick initially wanted to take all the items but was eventually convinced not to by Spoon when he likened it to someone taking Derrick’s hoard. Spoon finds a secret hatch, following it leads the party through the underground. The party defeats Thrain’s men, leaving most of them alive. They follow the path to where Thrain is, seeing Harbin in the bath alongside Thrain’s generals. Before Thrain can open the black canister, the party rushes in and combat ensues as Grimrid tells Thrain that this fight is no longer about honour as Thrain has crossed the line, whilst Thrain states that he is merely following the way the stone is cut. During combat, Thrain continuously taunts Grimrid for his naivety, telling the younger Dwarf of how everyone will abandon him, that hope is lost. But Grimrid doesn’t listen, instead believing in his allies, trusting that Dwarves do not fall because they do not fight alone, that perhaps Thrain is where he is because his brothers do not walk by his side as he summons Ruu to his aid. The party comes together, with Verena stepping up to attack Thrain, the rest joining in as Grimrid lands the final blow, knocking the Dwarven lord unconscious. 

# Thrain’s Interrogation

They then wake Thrain up, as Grimrid questions him for the location of Jarchigudai. Thrain shares that she is with Glassstaff, who is not a dragon but brings mist wherever they go, a wizard of some kind. The party also learns Thrain’s and Glassstaff’s usual meeting spot. When asking why Thrain did all of this, he shares that he had been banished for treason, framed by his lieutenant. So he travelled, crawled out of the dirt and realised that the surface was similar to his home with people like the useless mayor Harbin. Grimrid insists that a hero like Thrain has much to live for, believing that he should have sought restitution rather than just accepting the falsehoods. He iterates that a dwarf’s honour is never lost until he gives up, that Thrain's contempt towards traitors means that he is a better man, that at the very least, there still remains one dwarf that believes in him. With that, the party leaves. 

# Reidoth and Biscuit

The party arrives at the meeting spot with Biscuit. Sildar recognises the Al Miraj, and Biscuit tells the party about the Echo Four, Sildar old party. He also explains the origin of the Al Miraj, that overtime, the bunnies in Reidoth’s forest become Al Mirajes. On their way back to where Reidoth is, the party comes across two bickering Hill Giants who are arguing about who had taken their supplies. At first, the two blame the party, with one chasing Grimrid about. Eventually, Spoon manages to convince the giants that they mean no harm and allow them to help investigate the situation. The Giants agree, both leaving to continue their game of rolling down the mountain in the meantime. Verena investigates the animals’ corpses, realising that the holes on their chest were made by some kind of ice weapon of sorts. Grimrid spots a squirrel on the cart, wanting to chase it away when Spoon stops him, casting speak with animals to talk to the squirrel. From the squirrel, Spoon learns that there is some creature they call the “white devil” who allegedly roams the area with skin white as spider’s silk, whose breath leaks ice, and shoots out claws. The party doesn’t find any more information and leaves, continuing on to meet Reidoth. 

  

Arriving at the meeting spot, the party sees a group of trolls harassing Reidoth for the owlbear by her side. To the party’s surprise, Reidoth proceeds to cuss at the Trolls, threatening major bodily harm upon them in a very crass manner. Spoon asks Sildar if he had taught Reidoth those words and despite his denial, Spoon and Grimrid see that he has. Sildar tells the party that they must help as Reidoth cannot take those Trolls alone. The party does, attacking the Trolls as Grimrid discovers the power of the Dwarven Thrower. The party quickly kills the leader and as the others run away, Verena does not stop, killing two more because she can and because one had hurt Pork. 

  

The party then speaks with Reidoth who acts grumpy and upset but still helps the party with the wolves. Though she does take Sildar, demanding that he help with the logistics. Spoon takes the opportunity to ask if Reidoth is Sildar’s mate and though she denies it, he sees through her deception. When asked about their old age, Reidoth grabs Sildar, chastising him for telling the party about the fey, even though he did not. She does however, inform the party that it had been about 400-500 years since. The party also informs Reidoth of Sildar’s curse of undeath. As for matters of Alara, Reidoth warns them to not trust anything Alara says, and tells them of the tale of Imp, the poor imp imprisoned by Alara that she refuses to let go. Sildar also requests that Reidoth share the resources she has with the party, and though begrudging, she gives them the items. With that, the party leaves Reidoth, Sildar, and Biscuit, continuing on with their quest to find Jarchigudai.
