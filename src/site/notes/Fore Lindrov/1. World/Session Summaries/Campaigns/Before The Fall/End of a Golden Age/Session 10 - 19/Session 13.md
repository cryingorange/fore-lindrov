# Stories

The Chosen asks the acolytes to introduce themselves, that only by shedding their old selves can they embrace the new. The first to speak is Hal, a 35 year old man who initially stayed silent but was convinced by Grimrid. He shares that he was a cobbler’s son and had taken over his father's business when a con man came to town, selling promises of a gold mine. Hal sold his father's store, investing in the scheme when the man took off with his money. In his desolate state, Hal was found by the group and they spoke of a place far better than where he came from, so with nothing else left he followed. 

  

Next is Zyraxes, who begins telling a tale of his hometown. Where all the people he met and spoke to would always extend an invite to meet at an oasis. Yet, when Zyraxes showed up, the place would be empty. Until one day, he met a kid, an urchin who claimed to be a prince. And the kid spoke to Zyraxes, shared stories. To Zyraxes’ surprise, the kid showed up time and time again, providing Zyraxes the friendship he greatly needed and so now he's here, following friends. 

  

When it's Tormund’s turn, he gives his usual introduction and speaks of the school he wishes to start some day. But his tale lacks a sob story, a reason for him to have sought out the dragon cult as others did. 

  

Next, a lady from the group speaks up, Serel, she used to be a servant of Illmater where the temple was a place of peace, but with time more began to lose faith. She believes that the dragons are gods and will put everything right again. 

  

Vulcan speaks up, a tale of a desert prince who learnt everything to be a perfect King. Yet despite his merits and achievements the prince felt empty, alone, forced to live a life he didn't choose. So he vanishes, and it would follow a dragon to wherever it needs be. 

  

Tarn shares his plight. He claims to be a guard who didn't do anything when one day he was wrongfully accused of harassing an elf. Fired from his job and unable to do anything about it, he seeks for the dragon to make this stupid place better.

  

And an old voice pipes up, Verrick shares that he knows the truth. That dragons exist and that dragons are the true power, god. 

  

Lastly, Grimrid, who speaks of his exile, and of his lost craft. He shares his desire to seek out the dragon's knowledge to regain what he had lost.

  

With the stories over, the chosen tells the acolytes to present their tributes to the scale.

  

# Skills

  

Hal offers a pair of leather boots, Serel offers her clerical powers. Grimrid offers his strength, attempting to show his might. But he fails and the Chosen isn't impressed. The chosen vocalises this, asking Grimrid if he knows how to wake the dragon. Which allows Grimrid a second chance to prove himself as his hammer smashes through a tree. He gains the Chosen’s approval who remarks that Grimrid strikes like one born to serve the dragon. 

  

Next, Tormund tries to offer a spellscroll but scribbles something absolutely horrid. He tries again, failing. Then again. Another failure. Unable to handle the failure he smashes down a wall, losing himself as Verena gets called forward to discipline Tormund. Which she somewhat manages, eventually having Tormund on his knees as Verena mutters for him to prostrate himself. In response, Tormund turns around and walks away. This draws the suspicion of the Chosen, who secretly asks Tormund why he is here, then posing the same question to Verena who manages to give a satisfactory answer. 

  

The chosen has Verena step forth, to be an example of the cleansing that the initiates would soon partake. He shoots a cone of cold right at Verena, who manages to take it, playing off the pain as religious fervour. 

  

Tormund is then scolded by the Chosen for being bad at even grovelling. The Chosen then asks for the old man to present his gift. And when the old man comes up empty, the Chosen suggests a combat between Tormund and the old man, much to Tormund's dismay. 

  

# Death Of An Old Man

  

Meanwhile, the drow has been looking at Spoon. And Spoon responds with a growl so convincing that the Drow gives up on collaborating with Spoon, believing Spoon to have been a captured Kobold. Spoon realises this in hindsight and asks Derrick to attempt to get the Drow’s attention, which Derrick tries by whistling in morse code. During the mess, Spoon rushes into the house, preparing to sneak up on the Chosen. 

  

Back outside, Tormund and Verrick have to fight. The old man charges without hesitation, not wanting to lose his place in the cult, whilst Tormund attempts to dissuade the old man. But as the old man continuously lunges at Tormund, the minotaur pushes the man, hoping to halt his actions. Tormund loses control of his strength, pushing the man too far as the audible crack of his neck is heard. 

  

With the death of the old man, Grimrid strikes, hurling his hammer at the Chosen, knocking him back into the room. Combat ensues as Spoon, Grimrid and Verena all rush to attack the chosen, leaving Derrick, Zyraxes, and Vulcan back to hold off the others. The Drow takes a step back, casting one spell that ends up benefitting the party, then a second to leave, staying in a safe space as he watches to decide who he should side with. During which, Tormund is in disbelief, holding onto the lifeless body of the old man, he asks him to wake up, shaking the man, checking his wounds. Eventually, Tormund comes to the conclusion that the man is dead, an old man, killed by Tormund's own hands. Tormund turns to one of the cultists, vowing to rip their head off and lick their skull. Derrick hears this, panicking as he starts whistling louder, hoping that Spoon would hear his morse code. It does not work. The rest do emerge, helping to end the cultists before Tormund could unleash his anger upon them. 

  

# Vizeran

  

With the enemies dealt with, Grimrid walks up to Tormund, shovel in hand as he is ready to help. But Tormund requests for a moment alone, accepting the shovel as he walks off to bury the old man's body. The rest speak with the Drow, who is Vizeran, the owner of Cragmaw castle before Glassstaff came and overwhelmed his castle. The party learns a fair bit of information from Vizeran, that Glassstaff had brought the fire giant to the castle in chains. And that Vizeran was initially working with some goblins, namely Ruxithid, the same Goblin Mr Poopybutthole had mentioned. He also knows that the cultists are creating something that would bring about absolute draconic energy. In return what Vizeran wants is his spellbook back. The party agrees, needing Vizeran’s powers to send them to the castle in the first place. 

  

It's about here when Tormund returns, riddled with guilt. The party notices his demeanor and speaks to him as Tormund muses that he might truly be a bad guy. Verena interjects, indirectly nudging Tormund to see himself in a better light. Playing into his thinking, she drafts him a spell scroll called “Better Self” insisting that he study this and that he is capable, being an archmagus and all. Derrick chimes in, adding that Verena is from the Abyss and isn't evil, whilst Spoon agrees that even Verena is changing for the better which Verena disagrees with. Tormund hears the words of his friends and though it might take some time before he can truly believe in himself, at least for now, his party does. 

  

# Vizeran’s Cruelty

  

Vizeran also intends to kill the acolytes left alive but Spoon steps in, reminding Vizeran about the fate of his spell book. Vizeran relents and suggests that the party travel appear as acolytes, that pretending to be any chosen or blessed would run a greater risk. Verena takes one of the spell scrolls of disguise self, using it to create the illusion of a black cloak. Grimrid picks up one of the cloaks from the ground, cutting off the access and handing it to Spoon who dons the cloak. Meanwhile for Derrick, he is immediately interested in the idea of playing a spy. With that, the party heads off.

# Cragmaw Castle

  

In a flash, they arrive at the side of a mountain, snowy and cold. In the front they see two guards, huddled near a flame as the party approaches. The guards signal for the door to be opened as new acolytes have arrived but it does not. After a little bit one of the other guards peeks through, shouting that the one meant to open the gate is nowhere to be found and they open the gates instead. After passing through, a bugbear appears, chastising the guards for opening the door when it is his job. The bugbear then gets the leader as the party looks around for a bit. As the Chosen approaches the party give their respects. As for Derrick, he is promptly offered up as Verena takes the credit for capturing the dragon. The Chosen does remark that Derrick is a runt and gives Verena a dragon eye amulet which Verena does sense is filled with magic potential. The Chosen takes Derrick away, putting on manacles made specifically for dragons on Derrick before dragging him away. The party is given free reign to explore, only informed to show up when it is time for initiation. 

  

Grimrid explores the area with his stone cunning, gathering a rough idea of the castle. Meanwhile the rest of the party head to where the other acolytes are and meet a familiar face, Arlin. Arlin is excited to see the party, stating that he didn't know that they were headed in the same direction. Arlin claims that some one called Resmir has brought him to a cave with a storm giant, a maid with no arms who was very kind. They knew that he felt lonely and assured him that there was a place with kindred spirits where he could be nurtured and gave him a dragon scale dagger. The party leaves, with Vulcan and Zyraxes staying behind. 

  

# Goblin’s Room

  

As they explore, the party comes across a room with many beds and an effigy. Most of them hurl at the wretched smell, except for Verena who lets her curiosity get the better of her as she makes up an excuse to be in the room alone. Unbeknownst to her, the party are still outside as they hear Verena's attempts at dancing and chaotic chanting. The first attempt goes poorly, too clean, too systematic. The second doesn't either. It is only when Verena channels the most chaotic person she knows, Derrick Mr Poopybutthole Frundles Draconius Maximus that it works. Right as she's ending the ritual, the party steps in. Verena pulls herself up rather quickly, pretending that whatever the party saw to be a figment of their imagination as they continue to question and tease her for her actions. The next room they step into is also a bedroom, this time with no effigy but Grimrid insists that they all leave Verena alone. Frustrated, Verena steals from the chests and returns with supplies for the party, handing them off before they can say much more. The party circles back to a prayer room they had seen before, this time unoccupied as Verena takes a look at the Draconic statue before them. Verena discerns that the statue is that of the ancient Tiamat, known as the Queen of Dragons who embodies all facets of the Chromatic dragons. 

  

# Paper The Kobold

  

Meanwhile, Grimrid and Spoon continue up ahead, finding a cell containing Derrick and a gold Kobold, also from a nanny service. The kobold is Paper, whose bitey baby Palarandus was taken away by a weird goblin with a big head. Her brother Pen, is also a nanny but Pen was not captured, only Paper. Paper seeks the party's help to help PanPan which the party agrees. When describing PanPan, Paper does remark that PanPan is much bigger than Derrick, that Derrick is small for his age. 

  

Out from one of the other rooms is the same Blessed previously using the prayer room. He warns the party not to enter any of the locked rooms, and for them to be careful getting close to Paper, for she bites. The blessed does acknowledge the pendant Verena received, remarking that the others should follow suit. Once he leaves, the party members immediately head to the locked doors attempting to unlock them. Verena breaks the lock in one of the rooms but leaves it be. Meanwhile, Spoon and Grimrid both try to unlock one of the other rooms with Spoon eventually succeeding. They enter a room with a mirror, an altar, and a chest. Spoon dives for the chest, shifting through to find stuff. Meanwhile, Verena is at the altar, the expensive draconic statuette catching her eye. As those two are busy, footsteps begin walking towards the room. Grimrid walks out of the room, allowing himself to be found as he pretends to be apologetic, reaching out for the man as he grabs onto him.  
  
The party springs into action, grabbing the important items and attacking the cultist, swiftly ending his life before shoving the man under the bed to hide his body. As for the magic mirror, both Verena and Tormund approach it, with Tormund taking the leap of faith to activate it. The mirror shows a room, much like this one but empty. Then upon another activation, the party begins to see many different rooms of the castle. They assume the mirror works both ways. They do see parts of the dungeon of the castle, where glassstaff and PanPan are. Armed with more knowledge on the place, the party continues their exploration with Grimrid's master still missing.
