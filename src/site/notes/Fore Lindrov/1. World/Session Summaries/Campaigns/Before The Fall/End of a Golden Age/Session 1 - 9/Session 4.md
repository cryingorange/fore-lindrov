# The Ox Continued

Scanning the area, Spoon investigates the Oxen corpse, finding no signs of wounds on the ox. Cutting into it, Spoon, Verena, and Lu get hit with a wave of cold air. The inside of the ox is cold, its bone dark and frostbitten. Whatever killed the Ox did so from the inside out. Just a small distance away, Grimrid finds a circular area of stone. He recognises it to be his mentor’s attack, something that she would have used only if the situation was dire. Spoon collects some of the Ox meat and then heads to the stone, finding a set of paw prints belonging to a medium sized creature. Verena deduces that the prints could belong to a gnoll, though she is unsure why gnolls have attacked Jarchigudai. Spoon also finds some dark fur, and Grimrid finds a canine skull amongst the stones, cementing the party’s deduction. Grimrid spots some tracks, wanting to follow it immediately when the others suggest taking a good look at the area before leaving. Lu looks at a displaced tree, finding that it has several marks on it. Amongst the blood spots, Spoon finds a stomach that he tries to feed Pork but Pork rejects it revealing that the bulette only eats live prey. 

# Goblins and Garaele

Having searched everything, the party follows the trails that Grimrid found only for it to be a dead end. As Grimrid curses at the fact, Spoon hears a noise in the distance. The party barges in on the goblins, noticing that they had a hostage. They also notice the weapons the goblins carry are from Sildar and Jarchigudai. Enraged, Grimrid fights the goblins with the party following in tow. The party captures the leader of goblins who wields a staff and has a varnished eye pendant. The party knocks out the Goblin, allowing Pork to have the kill and his first meal in a while. Grimrid picks up the weapons, hoisting it up on his back. Verena picks up the varnished eye pendant attuning to it as the party speaks with the hostage, Sister Garaele. Spoon helps bandage her wounds, noticing that parts of her skin are necrosed. Spoon does try to ask a little about the injuries though Sister Garaele changes the topic, introducing herself as a cleric of Tymora. As it turns out Sister Garaele hails from Phandalin and vaguely mentions that she was out doing something for the town before the goblins ambushed her and she willingly followed them to their next location, though she was unsure where. Spoon senses some lies in her words and tries to push to no avail. 

  

As the party takes a short rest, Garaele approaches the members individually first speaking with Grimrid as she tries to learn from where they hail. Grimrid shares the name of his hometown, to which Garaele says that she knows of the legendary craftsmanship from the area, flattering Grimrid as he swells with pride. Though that is short lived when she comments on Grimrid’s craftsmanship, realising that the Dwarf harbours a great shame for his lack of skill. She comforts him, telling him that not all roads lead down the same paths as that of one’s ancestors. Hearing the word “road”, Grimrid leans in, telling Garaele that he needs to know which road his master, the giant, went. She reassures him that they will find his master. Grimrid then softens and asks her about her own hometown, learning that Garaele hailed from a small village and travelled to Phandalin. Grimrid then asks about Lady Luck, to which she shared her parents were devout believers, and Grimrid nods saying that Lady Luck is not as good as Moradin, but probably the best for a human, to which she chuckles and humours him.

# Suspicious Garaele

Finishing up her conversation with Grimrid, Garaele approaches the rest of the party flattering Lu, Spoon, and Derrick with her niceness that grosses Verena out. She asks them the same questions, learning that Derrick lives in a floating house in the sky. When she directs the question to Verena, the tiefling lies, claiming to hail from somewhere in the north, sprinkling in bits of truths in her lie. Though everyone sees through her words, as Garaele does not push the matter. Verena changes the subject, asking Garaele about Phandalin as the cleric reluctantly shares that the town is in a bad situation right now with the recent Gnoll attacks and the lack of supplies. As they proceed to the road, Garaele suggests that they do not, instead urging them to go to the path by the forest. When pressed about why, Garaele reveals that the Mayor has hired some unsavoury characters and appointed them as the town’s guards. Most of the party want to head to the road, desiring a more direct approach with the “guards”, all except Verena. Verena agrees that there might be benefits to going the forest route, suggesting that they can keep the element of surprise if they do. Grimrid agrees, trusting his friend to have a plan. Lu takes more persuasion, positing that Garaele might have ulterior motives for leading them down that path, though Verena suggests that if that were the case, they could take her on alone, especially since she lost to the Goblin. With Lu convinced, Verena heads to Spoon, convincing him similarly. With that the party sets off to the route led by Garaele. 

# First Redbrand Encounter

As luck would have it, the party still encounters a group of “guards” in the forest. As they try to threaten the party for some gold to pay the toll. Grimrid doesn’t take the threat, trying to threaten the thugs in turn. Though it fails to work as the Thugs do not believe that the party would do anything to them. Spoon chimes up, stating that they have a letter from the Baron. He shows the letter to the Thugs, with Grimrid holding onto it, not trusting the Baron with the letter. Verena notices that the Thugs do not know how to read, taking a stab in the dark as she tries to fib about the letter’s contents. The lie does not work as the Thugs then scoff at the party, demanding Garaele as payment instead. At the insinuation, Verena casts a spell, causing two of the Thugs to go unconscious. The Thugs charge into battle, though Lu and Spoon remain uncertain, both panicking at the consequences of killing guards. Lu tries to convince the party to stop, realising he is unable to separate himself from it when he requests for the party to not kill the Thugs, just knock them out. Spoon meanwhile, tries to discourage Derrick who seems very much into violence as he speaks of someone, not himself, knocking people out and running away before the people wake up. In an effort to stop Derrick, Spoon grapples the Dragon, wrestling him away from the conflict to keep both their hands clean. Verena aims for the leader, shooting right at his foul tongue as punishment for the words he’s uttered whilst Lu and Grimrid handle the others. Though halfway through the combat, laughter surrounds the party as a Gnoll drags the sleeping Thug away. The party then faces the Gnolls, managing to kill all of them. Though on the side of the Thugs, their leader is killed by the Gnolls. 

  

Garaele, Spoon, and Lu help out the remaining Thugs whilst Grimrid and Verena loom over the Thugs. Garaele snaps at them, telling them to back off if they do not wish to help. Grimrid meets Garaele’s harshness, mocking her naivety and beliefs. Still Garaele pushes, selfishly preaching at Grimrid who has been under a fair bit of distress since the disappearance of his master. Verena chimes in, stating a matter of factly that some people cannot be redeemed. Though Garaele does not care, instead focusing on the Thugs and muttering a prayer for the fallen leader. Grimrid leaves whilst Lu, Spoon, and Garaele all pat themselves on the back for helping the Thugs that had treated them rudely. As they head back to Phandalin, the remaining Thugs cry, whining of their fallen leader and how he was lost too soon. Back at Phandalin, Garaele leads them to the Shrine of Luck, telling the party to revisit her if they are serious about helping the town. 

# Phandalin

The party then takes a short break, exploring the town despite its depressive aura. Verena uses her magic to turn into a human version of herself to lower the guard of the people they would next speak to. At Grimrid’s request, they head to the borders of the town, though Grimrid realises that the clear lack of walls around the place doesn't help them locate the Gnoll attacks. Spoon and Lu do notice the abundance of wolf pelts around the town. 

# Barthen’s provisions

Next, they head to Barthen’s provisions. They meet the sleeping shopkeeper, Elmina Barthen, an old woman dressed in green with wispy white hair that used to be red, and cloudy hazel eyes. From Elmina, Spoon learns several rumours about the town. First, the lack of supplies in town are due to the recent Gnoll attacks of caravans about the area, as a result the people are scared, afraid of starving, and are steadily running out of money. This also leads to Elmina’s concerns for her business, since fewer people have visited her shops after clearing it out for food and with the lack of new shipments, she fears that she may have to close down her shop. Second, the Redbrands were hired by the mayor due to the Gnoll attacks. Though instead of protecting the town, they’ve turned to extorting it, imposing unfair taxes and tolls as they wish. As for the Mayor, he is a useless man, a coward who doesn’t care about the people. He’s more than happy to pay the Redbrands, their payment not at all affecting his wealth. Third, the Hunters, Thel, Nilsa, and Nars Dendrar are a family of hunters who live in Phandalin who went out to find the Gnoll Lair. Nars returned half-dead, covered in blood and pieces of his Father and Brother in a sack. They suspect that he might have murdered his family but it’s not as if the Redbrands would do anything about it, given how useless they are as guards. From those rumours, there are concerns of Nars becoming a demon or a cultist. Fourth, Elmina speaks of the Lady of the Lake, a spirit said to reside in the lake and if you answer her riddles, she would grant any request. Fifth, the people of Phandalin despise wolves, see them as pests that snatch children and kill sheep. They used to have a dense wolf population, but thanks to the hunters, the wolves are not much of a problem anymore. Sixth, Linene Graywind is the person in charge of arranging imports and exports and is looking for people to go check on their latest predicted shipment. 

  

Spoon also realises Elmina’s interest towards the pendant Verena is wearing, though she seems angry at the sight of it. Elmina extends an offer for trade to Verena, interested in the necklace as Verena lies about it being a family heirloom. Elmina sees through the lie, suggesting to Verena that she knows that it was misplaced. Verena bargains for 500 gold but Elmina is not willing to pay the price, negotiating for 350 gold, and a book on demons. As Elmina hands the book over, Spoon notices an oddity, that there were marks in the dust showing a pair of hands that were much different from Elmina, a pair of long and gnarly fingers. Verena delays the offer, looking around as she secretly speaks to Lu and Grimrid about the matter. Her party members leave it up to Verena to handle the situation, which ultimately she does not accept the offer, politely rejecting before leaving. Lu and Spoon notice the angry expression on Elmina’s face, though she does nothing as Verena leaves. 

# Stonehill Inn

Leaving the shop, the party heads to the Inn. As they enter, the more perceptive of the party, Lu and Spoon, notice the difference between the nicer tables that were all taken up by the Redbrands. The party speaks with the barkeeper, with Grimrid ordering a mug of ale only to learn that it would cost 10 gold. Verena tries to sympathise with the shopkeeper, lying to the shopkeeper and as she does, a magic evokes, causing her to be unable to speak.

  

Verena leaves the shop, allowing Spoon and Lu to uncover more information as she attempts to figure out what the varnished eye pendant is. She tries to identify it, letting Grimrid know of her curse through the minor illusion. Just as Grimrid readies his axe, Verena stops him, telling him to wait as they would need to head to the Shrine to fix the issue.
