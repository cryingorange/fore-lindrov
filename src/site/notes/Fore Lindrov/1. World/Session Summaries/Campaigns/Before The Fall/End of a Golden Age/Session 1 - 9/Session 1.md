# The Gathering

The four adventurers gather at the meeting point, answering to a job that allegedly is related to the royal family, potentially dangerous but it pays well. Spoon and Derrick arrive first, both admiring their foray into the material plane.  
  
Next are two voices, whining and bickering as a tall grey skinned tiefling adorned with fineries arrives alongside a short dwarf dressed in armour adorned with Giant runes. The fancy tiefling is Verena, and her grumpy companion is Grimrid.   
  

Last to join the group is a man with a long ponytail dressed in traditional eastern style clothing, Lu mutters a line of the importance of trees speaking in poetic one liners throughout the adventure. 

# Meet Halloway The Wise

Eventually, an old wizard shows up, dressed in grey ragged robes. He introduces himself as Halloway the Wise and hands the party a letter. Grimrid accepts immediately and attempts to open, sending him hurling back towards the trees. Lu and Verena raise their hands, ready to attack the wizard should they have to but he assures them that he’s no threat. He explains the quest, to deliver the letter to Titan Steward, Hekaton and promises 2500 gold upon the successful delivery.  
  
Verena tries to get Halloway’s promise of the price in writing, not trusting the rather suspicious wizard. She manages to see through part of his facade, through an Investigation Check, learning that the Wizard is disguised in some way. During her questioning of the mysterious wizard, Spoon offers everyone jerky, with Verena imparting a lesson to never say thank you, but rather saying one is Grateful when expressing gratitude. As the party takes a look at the letter, they recognize the symbol of the Royal Family, a fact that even Halloway himself is surprised by, which triggers a lot more suspicion from the party. Spoon offers some jerky to Halloway to suss him out, who swiftly ate the jerky, but shortly after a thud, like something landing on the ground was heard. As Halloway steps backwards, Spoon realises that his jerky fell right through Halloway beneath his robes. The party, through a series of harsh interrogation and a potential refusal to accept Halloway’s quest, forces him to reveal that he is a Skeleton, working for a Lich who lives in the tower in the forest. 

  

# More Like Halloway The Skeleton

  
Halloway reveals that he remembers little of his mortal life, but when he was raised as an Undead, he found his mind much sharper than the other guardians of the tower, he took to reading and learned magic. His Master, named Halftkin, took this as an opportunity to use Halloway as his Administrator, to tend to tasks that the immortal Lich is too lazy to attend to himself. The party tries to force Halloway to escort them to his Master, but the Undead Wizard warns them not to do so, and assures them Halftkin works for the good of the realm, but his master is fickle, and easily irritable.

  

# Halloway’s Quest

  

The party eventually relent as Halloway also emphasises that on their journey they should help people along the way, noting to them their first pit stop should be the Town of Grayview, when questioned on his insistence, he reveals that his Master gives him full autonomy to carry out his duties, but Halloway himself wishes to give his tasks to Adventurers that care not just for coin. He recalls a previous adventuring party that allowed an entire village to burn in service of a task he gave, which is not how he wishes to conduct business on behalf of his Master.

  

# Journey to Grayview

  

On their three day journey, the party forages for food for Spoon to cook, all admiring his culinary skills. The first day’s meals were delicious but sadly poisoned by some berries Verena might have found. Ironically, the only one affected was said Tiefling who insists that all is fine. The second night, Grimrid successfully hunts a boar once more and Verena successfully forages for some tea, offering each party member a cup. The third night is when Grimrid’s luck runs out, finding apples instead of the usual boar. Verena heads out to hunt, returning with a rabbit for dinner. 

  
  

# An Injured Man

  

Continuing on their journey, the party stumbles upon a person lying in the middle of the road, blood pooling around them. Most of them rush forward, with Verena strolling behind. The man is alive, though badly wounded by an arrow to the chest. They heal the man and from him learn that a bunch of goblins have attacked a caravan. The party rushes to help and find a group of goblins who look changed, their minds bigger than they should be. Battle ensues and most of the party rush to help. 

  

# Goblin Ambush

  
Lu fights with his calligrapher’s brush, painting strokes of inky animals that come to life to the civilian’s aid. Grimrid runs forth, swinging his axe wildly at the enemies, activating the runes on his armour when appropriate. Spoon and Derrick actively help as well, picking off the goblins at higher ground that the party would otherwise have had issues with. Lastly is Verena, who though is confused by her party’s enthusiasm, helps as best she can, incapacitating two enemies and finishing off the last goblin. 

#   Something Sinister

As her magic reaches into the mind of the goblin, showing it horrible visages of the Abyss, for a moment it feels as if another entity has taken over her magic, and as if the goblin sees something other than what Verena showed before it falls over dead. Grimrid and Lu approach the civilians, helping ease their nerves as the Baron offers them a 100 gold to be split amongst the group. The Baron reveals that he has come to Grayview due to some trouble that has befallen the local region, but that he will expand more upon it when they reach the Logistical Town.  
  

# Baron Cedric Valen

  
He hires the party to lead the convoy to Greyview for protection, expressing a deep regret at his guards’ deaths, and grateful that the party saved at least one of his men. The party, upon speaking to the Commoners, reveal a little more about the issues that plague the region, saying their crops have been affected recently, and that they ventured far to seek out the Baron directly, and instead of being driven away, the Baron allowed them into his home to hear them out, and even went so far as to personally bring them and himself to Grayview for a meeting of Farmers that is occurring at the Town. 

  

Verena does tell Grimrid that this potential trouble would be a great chance to get more money, which he points out the money they had already received. Verena counters this by saying that the money was for saving them not for the journey. Verena then offers her services for hire which after some convincing the Baron offers them more gold when they arrive in Greyview but also the promise of introducing the party to their contacts. 

  

The party also takes the time to dissect a goblin, trying to analyse what has affected its state. Spoon expertly cuts the goblin open only to find that everything inside the goblin seems “normal” in a way, with no deformations or signs of mutation present on the clearly abominable and abnormal Goblin. They harvest some materials and then head off on their new quest.
