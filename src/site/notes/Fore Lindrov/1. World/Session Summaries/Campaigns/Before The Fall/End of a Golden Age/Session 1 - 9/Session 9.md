# Back at Phandalin

The party returns to Phandalin, sending more people to Garaele as they are now left without a place to rest for the night. They lead the drunk Sildar to the inn and upon entering receive an unpleasant welcome from the innkeeper who blames the party for the new wave of unrest they brought about the place. Sildar reveals the party’s intentions to kick out the red brands from the town. And though sceptical, the innkeeper points out to Kiera, drunk in the corner crying about her dead lover. Near where she is lies an unconscious man, bleeding from a head wound. The innkeeper explains that the unconscious man had tried speaking to Kiera, asking her to leave but was attacked and now none of them dare approach, offering the party a free night’s stay if they manage to get Kiera to leave. They agree as Spoon and Derrick head to the unconscious man to help him out. Spoon manages to tend to the man’s wounds as he and Derrick sit the man upright. Ghoul approaches Kiera, having never met her before as he tries to speak with her.  
  
During which, Grimrid and Verena stay at the bar, watching the situation unfold. Verena suggests a wager, betting one gold that Ghoul would get slapped, Grimrid accepts, doubling the wager to two should Ghoul land on his behind from being hit. Continuing his conversation with the innkeeper, Grimrid also shares that they had solved the wolf situation the town had been facing, and in return the innkeeper is thankful, offering them free boarding for their stay. The innkeeper also asks Grimrid about Elmina, wanting to know where the old lady went. Grimrid shifts the responsibility to Verena, telling the innkeeper that she would know. When the innkeeper poses his question, Verena makes up a lie that Elmina had moved out of town, since it wasn’t safe and that she had grandchildren in other places to be with. The innkeeper seems surprised and maybe disappointed as he shares that Elmina had helped him and his wife during the rough patch of their marriage. Verena simply remarks that maybe she has gone on to help other couples and turns her attention back to the other’s handling Kiera. Ghoul tries to speak to Kiera who has no interest in speaking as she continues to cry about her dead lover and all the wonderful memories they shared. When it looks like the situation is about to escalate to more than just a mere bar fight, Verena steps in casting a spell on Kiera as she suggests that Kiera return to her home and tuck herself in bed. With that, much to Grimrid’s disappointment of a lack of a bar fight, the matter is resolved. They get the keys to their rooms from the innkeeper and also learn that laymon currently resides at the inn. Spoon leads Derrick to take this shower, despite the dragon’s resistance. Ghoul retreats to his room, preparing a kettle of medicine which he imbibes.

# Failed attempts at breaking in

Grimrid and Verena head out to the graveyard of the town, both burying the minds of the knight and child they met in the wolves’ cave. Grimrid does a carving for both, marking the graves of the two who lost their lives to mind flayers. As they finish up, Verena spots an individual staring in their direction, a man dressed in a dark cloak. She suggests that they confront the man and they do. The man turns out to be some sort of dragon fanatic, as he rambles incessantly about some dragon god of the stars and how he would be the god’s great receiver. Regretting their decisions, Verena walks away first, leaving the man to his fanaticism. Grimrid soon follows after, and the two soon return to the inn where Spoon is tucking Derrick into bed. On the way back, Verena brings up hers and Spoon’s plans to enter the hag’s shop. Grimrid refuses to partake in the idea, saying that he won’t be a part of it. Once Derrick is asleep, Spoon sneaks out to join Grimrid and Verena, all intending to pull a prank on Laymon. Before they arrive at Laymon’s room, Ghoul stumbles out from his room, behaving oddly, seeming out of sorts. The party tries to convince Ghoul to return to his room, although he does not agree, making his way downstairs and back up, insisting that he wasn’t drunk as the party suspected he was. Spoon checks up on Ghoul, realising that the pitcher of tea he had to be some kind of drug. Grimrid provides some rope to Spoon, as they all agree that tying up Ghoul would be the best course of action to ensure that he didn’t wander off in the night. With that done, the party heads to Laymon’s room. They try the door knob, realising that it is closed. They also realise that they have no way to break into the room, which is when Verena brings up Barthen’s Provisions once more suggesting that the shop would have some kind of lock picking tools. So they leave, arriving at the shop only to realise the exact same issue, that they had no way of breaking in. One of the neighbours notices their presence and lets them know that Elmina isn’t in. Realising that they’re drawing quite a fair bit of attention, the party gives up on the provisions shop and on pranking Laymon for the night. 

  

The next morning, Spoon wakes up first to prepare the snacks for the road, followed by the rest. Ghoul approaches the party, asking where they are headed off to as the party slowly comes to the realisation that Ghoul intends to follow them. Still new to Ghoul, they are apprehensive about him joining. Spoon and Grimrid let it slide, whilst Verena is more cautious, telling Ghoul that his addiction makes him a risk, and that she’s willing to let him follow them on the condition that he doesn't stand behind her. He agrees and they head out for the day. 

# Tresendar Manor Crypt

Following Sildar’s advice, they enter the manor through a back entrance, reaching the crypts. As they enter, they see large corpses strewn about. The closest corpse has a sword accompanying it, one that is beautifully crafted, its silver hilt shaped into that of a bird of prey mid-flight. Consulting with Sildar, the party learns that the Tresendars were Goliaths and that the sword belonged to Sir Aldith Tresendar, the one who had hired Sildar and his party. Verena is curious as to why the red brands had not taken the obviously precious blade; both she and Spoon decide to leave the blade alone, believing that it would not result in anything good. Ghoul touches the blade, and the body of Sir Aldith reanimates, grabbing onto Ghoul’s arm. As do the other corpses, puppeted by some undead magic as combat ensues. During which Ghoul activates a certain magic of his, the figure of a woman reaches out from behind him, stabbing a dagger into his heart. As this happens, chants fill the air, in Abyssal. Verena understands the chants, taking in the tale of a mother who sacrificed her child in exchange for something. The figure also pulls apart Ghoul’s wraps, revealing his emaciated form as the rest of the party begin to panic, wondering if Ghoul was also a bad person. The party defeats the corpses and Grimrid takes measures to push the corpse of Sir Aldith into the pit. They turn to Ghoul, weapons raised as they demand him to explain. Ghoul hesitates, causing the others to worry more as Verena speaks up, revealing that Ghoul was a sacrifice. The others press, learning the type of ritual that Ghoul was a part of and how he isn’t supposed to be alive. Grimrid turns to Verena, asking for her opinion as the Tiefling responds that she doubts Ghoul would betray them, and if he did, they could just kill him. Grimrid is satisfied with the answer and drops the matter. Spoon is still suspicious, unable to fully trust the very evil looking party member. 

# Sir Aldith

From the body, his spirit rises. Sir Aldith appears before the party as a spectre. He recognises Sildar, as the old knight kneels before the man. Aldith inquires about the state of Phandalin and receives the party’s incredibly honest opinions on the poor state of the town. Aldith tells the party what he knows, that one of the dark robed figures had arrived days before, carrying a container like egg thing. And that there is a beast that lurks in the halls, cries. He suggests that they do nothing to draw the beast’s attention and asks for the party’s help to rid those that disrespected his family’s crypt and to help Phandalin. He offers his family’s ancestral blade, Talon, sharing that it was forged by dwarves. Grimrid takes the blade as the party continues into the next room, stealthing to where Grimrid says more people are. 

# Fighting the Redbrands

The party witnesses a group of red brands, gambling amongst themselves. They see a masked individual in a black cloak who speaks to the subordinates. From them, the party learns the name of a “Master Glassstaff” and how the mayor had also been dragged here, as well as the other gift bestowed. The other gift being the beast Aldrith had warned them about. Before they can enjoy their game, the party springs into attack, hurting the masked man as combat ensues. The party also fights against Kiera, who had as per Verena’s spell, returned to her home and went to bed. The battle is close, though the party manages to be victorious. Amidst the fighting, Sildar and Ghoul had killed some of the red brands, much to Spoon’s displeasure. He chastises the two men for their actions, stating that they shouldn’t have killed the red brands who were merely trying to protect themselves and who had been working for the wrong people. He also emphasises against not killing the leader so that they could have interrogated him. 

# Looting the Redbrands

During that all, Verena swipes the sack of gold the masked man had put down and checks the man’s body for any valuables. She finds none but takes the man’s mask, just in case they would need to disguise themselves as him. Ghoul noticed that Kiera was using some sort of substance and thus enters her room in search of the substance. He finds a pouch with some drugs, pockets it as the others enter. They open the chest in the room, finding more drugs that Verena takes. Ghoul asks for the item, piquing Verena’s interest in it as she asks him what it is. When he reveals that it is drugs, she turns the conversation to a business opportunity, declaring that she would sell it for 500 gold. Ghoul tells Verena to keep the items for now, not having the funds to afford them. The rest of the items in the chest are mundane, but Spoon takes a look through, throwing all the unimportant items behind him as he realises that there is a false bottom to the chest. He finds a tankard, a couple’s memento between Gregor and Kiera, though there was someone’s name scratched off, replaced by Gregor’s name. Spoon also finds a set of rings, probably for 

  

Grimrid heads to the desk, where he finds excerpts of stories that might have been the “writings” Kiera spoke of. He reads them, only to find that they are incredibly raunchy stories. Grimrid regrets his decision, turning to Verena for a fire to burn the items. She does not, but suggests that they let Ghoul read it. Grimrid hands the paper off to Ghoul, who proceeds to lament having read it. Spoon is curious and snatches the paper away despite all the protest. He is unphased, confused about the other’s reactions as he innocently points out his perception of the sheet, not understanding the innuendoes. The party continues, entering one of the other rooms to find a journal from someone who made a deal with the hag and got a potion that would give him heightened strength. They move on to the last room. As they enter, they see a magic circle. Grimrid declares that the room is magic related and leaves, standing outside with Sildar, as he leaves the situation for his more magic inclined friends. Sildar then shares about his old party member, Alara, who would instantly touch and use every magic item she came upon without hesitation. Grimrid remarks that Verena is slightly more sensible, right as she walks up to the magic circle to conduct her own investigations. Spoon heads to the book on the floor, seeing a journal/spell book sort of thing that speaks of a mirror, one that houses a person who spoke to its previous owners, luring them in with sweet words. Meanwhile, Verena is at the magic circle, trying to ascertain its nature though she is unable to. She does sense that whatever ritual this circle was intended for, it is complete and all that remains is to activate it. Though very tempted, she does not activate it for now. 

# Ghoul’s situation

Last is Ghoul, who goes to the chest and notices that it has no locks. He borrows equipment from Grimrid to open the chest and as he tries a spell triggers. Under the effects of the spell, Ghoul sees the figure of his mother approaching him. He shoots. To the rest, Ghoul has shot at them for no reason. And to make matters worse, he shot at Derrick. The first bullet misses, but the subsequent ones don’t. Ghoul mutters something before running away. Verena tries to discern what might have caused the sudden change and realises that Ghoul is under the effects of a spell. She tries to explain it to the others, telling them to stop Spoon from killing Ghoul. Though it does not work, as Spoon keeps Derrick in his safe space before casting zephyr strike on himself as he races after Ghoul whilst the rest are unable to stop him.  
  
Grimrid is in shock, turning to Verena for guidance who tells him to stop Spoon before rushing off, leaving Grimrid alone. As Spoon walks up to Ghoul, he sees his mother, here to finish the job. He questions her, asks what was so bad about him that she had to sacrifice him. Spoon hears this, and ignores it, telling Ghoul that he does not care, that he isn’t his mother and that Ghoul has to pay for hurting his baby. To which what Ghoul hears is his mother telling him the worst things a child could ever hear, even as Ghoul pleads for an answer, the illusion of his mother affirms that he was a component, a mere thing to be used for her ritual, he was not special, he was not anything, and in fact, it didn’t even have to be him. Ghoul falls to his knees, submitting to fate as he gives up, allowing his mother to end his life that shouldn’t have started.  
  
The others catch up, as Spoon activates his aberrant powers. Verena casts magic missile at Ghoul, snapping him out of the spell’s effects. Whilst Sildar and Grimrid try to grapple Spoon, but with his aberrant transformation, he easily slips past their grasp. Verena tries to speak to Spoon, attempting to get him to see reason, to prevent the typically very pacifist Kobold from hurting a technically innocent person. Spoon doesn’t stand down completely, but offers Ghoul a chance to explain himself and provide proof, or else Spoon will kill him. Ghoul channels his form of dread once more, as it shows the ritual that occurred, a pregnant woman cuts open her belly, pulling out the fetus of her unborn child, sacrificing it in her ritual, stabbing the child’s chest as its flesh melts away. She has a manic look in her eyes, as if she had achieved her life’s goal. During the entire ritual, she doesn’t spare a glance at her child, not realising when the child reaches a hand out, trying to touch its mother. Once the ritual is complete, another figure shows up, steals the child away and manages to keep it alive whilst pulling out the dagger from its heart. The man does not raise the baby, keeping it around and letting it take care of itself. The child is seen around ghouls, eating the same rotten flesh as they did. That child grows up in the astral sea, knowing only slavers, mind flayers, and pirates. Ghoul takes out the dagger, the same one from the ritual and offers it to Spoon. He asks for a chance, sharing that he admires the party’s closeness, something that he never knew of, and should Spoon not be satisfied, Spoon can take his life anytime he pleases. Spoon takes the dagger, and agrees to it on one condition; that Ghoul has to stop whining about his mother. In Draconic, he says, “Even an egg must crack to hatch, do not wait for mercy when you have claws”. Which Verena translates, though proceeds to add a bit as she too is frustrated at Ghoul’s whining. She scoffs that a mother’s love is unnecessary before turning away. Ghoul sits with his feelings, muttering an embarrassed apology before leaving.
