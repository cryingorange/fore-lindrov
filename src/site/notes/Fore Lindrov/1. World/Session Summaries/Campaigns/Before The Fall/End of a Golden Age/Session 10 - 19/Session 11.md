# Thundertree Ruin

The party arrives at the meeting point, Thundertree Ruin. As they step in, Spoon feels that the air is colder. The party spots a corpse on the ground, their wounds bleeding. Grimrid steps around the dead body, surveying the place as Tormund and Spoon examine it. Tormund deduces that the body has been dead for about two hours. Spoon  notices some shards of wood in the wounds, deducing that the cause of death was both being crushed and stabbed in the face. On the body, Spoon finds a dragonscale dagger and Tormund finds a cultist mask, similar though less ostentatious than the mask they found earlier. As Grimrid steps forward, hidden plant creatures lunge forward to attack him. Combat ensues and in the midst of the chaos steps out two flamboyant looking men, a Brass Dragonborn and a sun elf. The two step in to help with the combat, with the sun elf putting out the fire that Verena had set with her fireball. As the last of the creatures die, the party speaks with the two weirdly dressed men. They are Vulcan and Zyraxes of the Vulcan force. They pull out a pamphlet, wanting to recruit the party as they share the various benefits and high costume budget they offer members. Derrick is immediately drawn in, wanting to join and wants Spoon to join as well, though Spoon deflects instead managing to be an honorary member. Meanwhile, as the two workshop names for the entire party, Grimrid stalks off, exploring one of the many houses. He finds what seemed to be an old tavern and a barrel of very well aged wine. Grimrid drinks from the barrel, tasting what is the best wine he has tasted in a long while. As the party enters, some of them drink the wine as well. Grimrid then seals the top of the wine, carrying the barrel with him. 

  

The party then enters a second building, finding an altar to Gruumsh that they surmise requires some kind of a sacrifice. Tormund tries first, slicing his arm to drip some blood onto the altar but that is insufficient. Spoon then suggests shanking Tormund to which he agrees and is then shanked by Spoon who knocks him out cold. As Tormund falls unconscious, Spoon feels a blessing from Gruumsh. Tempted by the thrill, Spoon does consider shanking Tormund once more but holds back. The party then destroys the altar and sees that the vines are still active, indicating that the protection over the area has yet to be erased. Entering another hut, Spoon finds a hidden hatch and most of them enter, save for Verena and Grimrid, who are attempting to protect the wine before they join the others downstairs. 

# Gruumsh’s Trial

Underground, Spoon and Tormund come across a trial of some kind, one requiring a proof of strength. Tormund tries to pull the vine, trying to do it alone but it does not work. Spoon then runs upstairs to inform the others of what they had found, still unwilling to part with the barrel of fine wine just yet, Verena tells Spoon to scream if they need help. Derrick helps out, pulling the other vine as Vulcan performs a dance of inspiration and Zyraxes shouts encouragement at Tormund. The two succeed and pull the doors apart to reveal the second part of the puzzle. Figuring it out, Spoon casts thaumaturgy, screaming as he solves the other part of it, triggering Verena to run down, abandoning the barrel. Verena reaches downstairs, finds no combat but analyses the door, with Pork’s help they solve the puzzle, and are allowed to enter through. Inside is a ghost, Gudan, the prophet of Gruumsh. And he declares that though the party is not the orcish chief, they are still allowed to take one item for passing the trial. Tormund takes one of the items and once safe, Verena tries to destroy the statue. Though it does not work as combat ensues. Tormund smashes the statue and is briefly processed as he rushes forward to kill whoever is nearest. The party destroys Gudan, and helps Tormund with the possession as they end the protective vines around town. Drained of their resources, the party rests for the night. As they rest, they hear soft sounds of crying. 

# Loog, Arsenic

The next morning, the party is awoken by Vulcan and Zyraxes who drag the party up for morning drills. Spoon manages to get out of the morning drills as he is an honorary member. Meanwhile Tormund and Verena struggle to stay awake, both poorly posing. As for Grimrid, he is able to stay asleep sitting down with his arms crossed as a barrel, tricking Zyraxes who fully believes that Grimrid is just that committed to the pose. They then set off to the tower, finding inside a crying Hill Giant who is injured. Spoon and Verena manage to comfort the Hill Giant who shares that he was attacked by an ice giant who took the food he had stolen from the two other hill giants the party had met before for Guh. 

  

# Arsenic

Following the directions from Loog, the party finds a set of tracks and follows it to a cart in the middle of nowhere. Spoon and Derrick cautiously approach with Verena trailing behind. Meanwhile Grimrid places the barrel down in a safe space, taking a wide berth around, only to be met with an ogre. The leader of the party is Arscenic from the Monochrome Kingdom that was a cage and that they wish to go to the ice lands. AS the party tries to figure out if they can trust the Hobglobin, the demon in Grimrid’s mind speaks to him, telling him to kill them all. Seeing as Arscenic and his crew wish to seek out the Frost Giant as well, the party agrees to a truce, to work with Arscenic. Arriving at the Frost Giant’s camp, the party speaks to the Frost Giant who is missing an arm and has a half scarred face. The Giant dismisses the party yet allows them to stay around as they talk to him. Arscenic tries to flatter the Giant, to no avail. The more they speak to the Frost Giant, they learn a little bit from his outbursts, in particular his pain when it comes to Svardbog his home. In an attempt to prove to the Frost Giant of his might, Arscenic begins to brag about the time he killed a Treant guarding a druid grove.  
  
The party realises that Arscenic is the one who killed Maplehusk and springs an attack on him. Derrick, remembering what Verena had told him lunges at Arscenic, telling him how he desires to torture the man and have him watch his loved ones die. During combat, Derrick also realises that Arscenic has a Nilbog shard in him as well. Spoon and Tormund turn their attention to Derrick, with Spoon calling Derrick by his full name “Veriton Sunflare the Third”. Spoon talks down Derrick, telling him that he was tasked to raise Derrick to be a dignified gold dragon, and not for him to act like some kind of rabid animal, that if he continues this they would be going home. The party kills most of Arscenic’s group and knocks him out. They then speak to Derrick who reveals that because Arscenic has a Mr Frundle, it feels like he did it, that and Verena had said to torture the one who killed Maplehusk. Spoon explains that what Derrick is feeling is guilt, and that he should not take it out on others, that it’s good that he is guilty and that Derrick should instead try to do something else to repent, that Maplehusk would not want him to beat himself up. But Derrick still insists that Arscenic has to die as that is the only way to get the shard of Mr Frundle out of him. Verena steps in questioning if death is the only solution given that they did not have to kill the Nilbog the first time Derrick had taken over Mr Frundle. Derrick explains what Arscenic had experienced, trapped in his own body and forced to watch as Mr Poopybutthole did not help when he lost his people, constantly puppeted by other beings. Verena does suggest that they ask if the shard would like to merge with Derrick willingly, though it does not. Ultimately, the party is left with not much choice and as Derrick decides to kill Arscenic, Verena uses her magic to end his life in what looks to be the least painful way. 

# Frost Giant, Ragnavald

The party then continues their conversation with the Frost Giant, learning that he is the brother to Jarl Storvald as he mockingly asks if they would kill his brother. Verena is in full support of fratricide, stating that people like them should die. But the Frost Giant reiterates that good killers do not die, knowing the power his brother holds. The party then asks if he is here to seek out strength, to overthrow his brother. In response the Frost Giant reveals that his brother had given him his injuries and now all giants within 10 miles are hunting him. Grimrid attempts to bargain, asking for the supplies in exchange for their silence, though the Frost Giant does not take it. Tormund feels a sense of kinship towards the Frost Giant, and expresses his intent to help when Grimrid pushes back, asking why they should help a raider.  
  
Tormund expresses that he feels the same, understanding where the Frost Giant is coming from and Spoon jumps in, reminding Grimrid that the Frost Giant though strong, has not actually used his strength on the party, only talking to them so far. As conversations continue with the Frost Giant, eventually combat ensues as the only way for the party to prove their strength, to even have the Frost Giant hear them out. Ultimately they do beat the Frost Giant up and learn his name, Ragnavald. He does not understand the party’s desire to help him but still takes it, giving them the bag of food they were here for and also provides them with some information on the cloaked figures, that the ice wizard smells like a dragon. Grimrid pays the Giant 100 gold, which he initially rejects but ultimately accepts when Tormund iterates that it is only fair to pay for information. The Frost Giant leaves, the last words from the party being advice for him to become an adventurer.
