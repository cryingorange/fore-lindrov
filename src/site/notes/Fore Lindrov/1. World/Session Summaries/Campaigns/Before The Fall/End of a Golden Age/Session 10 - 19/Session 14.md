# The Chosen

Tormund and Spoon continue their attempts with the mirror, eventually ending up at the last remaining room where potions line the walls. Meanwhile, Grimrid and Verena head over to the other room, followed by Derrick who asks them for the information they found, curious and wanting to join in. At the door, Grimrid tries to lock pick it, breaking the lock pick as it joins the initial broken lock pick from Verena’s failed attempts. Grimrid then breaks the lock instead and Spoon enters, rushing into the chest and rummaging through the items. He finds an array of potions and spell gems. Verena steps in as well, taking the draconic statuette for herself as they leave. 

  

They exit the prayer room, coming face to face with a Chosen who inquires about their experience with Tiamat. Grimrid keeps his head down, trying not to gather any attention and merely remarking that she is fearsome. Whilst Tormund fibs, claiming that he found her majestic. The Chosen sees through his lack of conviction, commanding the party to reenter the prayer room. He forces Tormund to kneel, whipping and lashing the minotaur’s back as he tells him to prostrate himself, which Tormund does not do well. Tormund’s reaction does not satisfy the Chosen as the Chosen suggests that Tormund should be taught by his fellow betters, referring to Verena who had been bestowed the draconic amulet for “catching a golden dragon”. Left with not much choice, Verena steps in, pulling out her draconic dagger to stab Tormund in the shoulder. Still not satisfied, the Chosen suggests that Tormund should make an offering to the Dragon Queen and instructs him to pull out his dagger. Verena steps in, suggesting that she be the one to do it instead, not wanting to reveal that Tormund did not have a draconic dagger of his own. The Chosen accepts and though unwilling, Verena grabs Tormund’s arm, slicing his wrist into the sacrificial bowl as she feels a gift bestowed, granting her access to the draconic amulet’s powers. 

  

# Zafir al-Harid

  

With that, the party are left alone and Spoon mends Tormund’s wounds before they head down to the mess hall. Grimrid notices a smithy nearby, heading towards it himself. He sees a double bladed scimitar, a weapon unfamiliar to him. The smithy, an elven man with golden skin, black hair, and red eyes that glow with a supernatural hue, wearing a necklace with a red symbol of the unique blade welcomes Grimrid, inviting him to the seating area. Grimrid beckons, entering a space with no chairs and carpeted floors. The man sits on the ground, as Grimrid does the same. The two break bread together and share a conversation. From which, the man, Zafir Al Harid, reveals that he is not a part of the dragon cult, instead praying to a god of strength and duty, a veil of red eyes worshipped in the lands of fate. Vataqatal is his name, known to some as the god of blood and of the double bladed scimitar, but also the god of strength and duty that strength comes from trial and service. Grimrid questions why the Elf would craft blades for the goblins and dragon cult and learns that Zafir is not beholden to the dragon but rather to Glassstaff who had captured Zafir one night. 

  

Grimrid also inquires the location of his master and as Zafir pushes back, asking what the Fire Giant means to him, Grimrid surmises that Zafir is loyal, though more so to duty, to upholding the virtues of his god. Zafir believes that his current situation is as his god intends, that this discomfort is a test. When Grimrid asks what Zafrir thinks about gaining freedom, Zafir shares a tale of a gladiator from long ago, forced to fight and though he was beaten and scarred, he never once asked for his freedom. The gladiator believed in endurance and in doing so earned recognition. His fame and triumph is known to all. He was commanded to fight in a thousand battles without rest. Then at the final battle, he fell and rose again. 

  

All witnessed as his mortal blood spilled onto his double bladed scimitar, as he rose, a new divinity. That all is to say that Zafir believes in his god, immensely so, that if he were to be freed, it would be by his god’s will. Grimrid then questions what of justice and fighting for the oppressed. At which Zafir expresses his disapproval for the people, claiming that the savages follow the wrong gods, speaking of justice when they lack perspective. Grimrid then reveals that Jarchigudai is his master. Hearing that Grimrid is not of the dragon tribe, Zafir reveals more information, sharing that the bugbears are foolish, far removed from enlightenment. He suggests bringing the bugbears and goblins to overthrow Glassstaff, sharing that the Fire Giant had been brought into the throne room and was never seen again. Grimrid requests for Zafir’s help but Zafir reveals that he had taken an oath to never raise his blade against Glassstaff, though instead could offer them his masterpiece, the double bladed scimitar. But his help comes at a price, Grimrid must prove that they are sent from Vataqatal and Zafir asks if Grimrid is an artist. He is however, disappointed to learn that Grimrid had sold his craft to save his master, claiming that the dwarf spits on the face of his master, ending the conversation. 

  

# Hobgoblin Leader

  

Grimrid heads back to the mess hall, approaching the well mannered Hobgoblin and manages to negotiate for some deal with the hobgoblin after settling aside their differences for the battle the hobgoblins had with Jarchigudai. From him, Grimrid learns that the hobgoblins had made it out of the warlands, where they met Groll. They initially joined in, trusting the strength in numbers to be a big aid for their situation. But now Groll forces them to listen to the dragon. Grimrid offers to kill the hobgoblins, telling the Hobgoblins that it is for their future. Eventually, the Hobgoblin is convinced, telling Grimrid that he wants the bugbears dead, and an ear from each as proof. He also tells Grimrid that the battle would be best won with the Goblins by their side. Grimrid accepts the offer and advice, entering the kitchen to join his party. 

  

# Yegg The Chef

  

Meanwhile, the rest of the party heads to the kitchen, witnessing a group of goblins commanded by another that seems to be the head chef of the place. The head chef initially approaches the party, chastising them for entering his kitchen. The party asks for food and is met with more resistance. Though Tormund’s sadness and Verena’s flattery are met with a serving of soup. The soup does not look appetising, much like randomly put together slop. Yet, when Tormund drinks, the soup is phenomenal. Spoon takes the lead, speaking with the head chef Yeg, understanding the great effort and thought Yeg puts into his meals. Spoon also notices the spice bag Yeg has, inquiring about it at first before suggesting a trade of recipe knowledge. Yeg has Spoon prove himself, allowing the use of the space and eventually ingredients as well, though any animals required would have to be wrangled by the party themselves. 

  

# The Boar

  

Tormund joins Spoon in the boar pen, as Spoon shares that they would have to physically dominate the boar and prevent spooking any of them. Tormund tries, having a rough time at first before managing to pick up the boar, hurling it right at the gate where Verena is. Verena is met with a flying boar that hits her, causing her to fall onto the ground right as Grimrid steps in commenting that now is not the time for naps. With the ingredients prepared, Spoon begins to cook. He too makes a stew, one that is served to all the guests of the mess hall. Yeg tries a mouth, and does not dislike it, going as far as to comment that it is not bad. Grimrid and Verena also take their hand at trying to convince Yeg to help the conflict but Yeg remains stubborn, scolding Verena for her lack of understanding of the mastery of cooking, viewing it as nothing more than a task. They get kicked out of the kitchen and Spoon then has his moment to speak with Yeg. 

  

# Yegg’s Info

  

He relates to Yeg, sharing that cooking is like an art that not many appreciate, still, it is something they continue to do. Yeg admits that his art has been stagnating, mainly due to Glassstaff’s preference for icy meats. Yeg takes a skewer of meat, placing it over the icy mist vents as it freezes the meat instantaneously, an affront to the ingredient. He also shares that something is in the storeroom, scurrying around though Yeg has paid it no mind. Overall, Yeg’s professional recommendation is for Spoon to reconsider the plan, that he had witnessed many who try to overthrow Glassstaff, all to no avail. The last to attempt was some two legged big sized man with beige skin and a flat ass, who wielded some fancy magic that did not work against Glassstaff. Spoon does ask how he can help Yeg regain his spark and Yeg requests for an egg to be brought in from the cold outside, that they would not have to worry about any parent as no other creatures come near the castle. 

# Pen The Kobold

  

With that, the party heads to the storeroom where amongst the stuff is Jarchigudai’s gear. Spoon approaches one of the bags, noticing some movement as a head pops out from the bag, Arlin. However, Arlin behaves very weirdly, as he is actually Pen, doing a rather bad impression of the human. Spoon toys with Pen for a bit before the party eventually reveals that they are not in fact dragon cultists. Pen is concerned for the safety of both PanPan and Paper, wanting to help his sister but also trusting the party thanks to Spoon. 

  

Pen also reveals that dragons are supposed to be able to bless things, like the Draconic amulet that Verena has. This is news to the party and to Spoon. Before the party can do much however, they hear sounds from upstairs as they head out, noticing the weird urgency as they all pile into the prayer room where Paper and Derrick are. 

  

# Death To The Cult

  

The party stands near the door, blocking the exit as the Chosen threatens Paper before the party and they attack. Grimrid throws his hammer at the Chosen, as Tormund rushes in to attack his screams silenced by Verena’s well placed spell. Spoon uses the spell gems provided by Reidoff to summon a bird that attacks alongside him. Grimrid also summons Ruu, calling his brother to aid in their battle against the corrupt Dragon cultists who Ruu also seems to have a grudge against. The party ends the enemies quickly before rushing down to kill the remaining chosen. Spoon jumps down first, landing on the guard before slashing at the man. Grimrid joins after but lacks the energy to stand up before Tormund does the same, landing on the two. Verena steps out from the prayer room to the sight of Tormund, the Chosen, and Grimrid on the ground where she teasingly mocks Grimrid for picking such an inopportune time to rest, then blasting the Chosen. The party ends the Chosen’s life swiftly alongside the goblins who spring to attack. Grimrid then gives a speech, holding onto the dead Chosen’s head. Grimrid claims that dwarves have long memories and this would not be the first time their kind have allied together against a common enemy. The Goblins cower, following Grol’s decision as they are clearly scared of the bugbear. Meanwhile, Grimrid discerns that Grol is acting entirely out of self interest, nothing bad but leaves them with an ally that could turn on them at any moment. Lastly, the hobgoblin who Grimrid had initially made a deal with now sees Grimrid’s true colours, losing any ounce of trust they had before.
