# Return To Velkynvelve

## Zipzap

The party returns to Velkynvelve, greeted by sleeping Kua Toa. Zipzap rushes out, clinging on to Elisa as he realises that she has once more changed her form. He blames her transformation on their separation and claims that he will never leave her again. Zipzap is suspicious of the new party members, bemoaning their weakness calling Ivy a pile of sticks and Roast a weak bone lady. He demands payment from his subjects, telling Grimrid to hold his things despite Elisa’s protest. The party also learns from Zipzap that Wystram and Crimson had run off. 

## Monk Kame

In the midst of chaos, Roast takes the chance to teleport past Zipzap with Ivy, the two gaining entry into Velkynvelve. Grimrid heads to sleep, finding a comfortable spot to rest. The rest of the party heads to speak with the other remaining groups. They first speak with Monk Kame who shares perplexing foreign food word related metaphors that completely fly over Roast’s head. Kame asks Lu how he’s been, who shares that he has lost a limb and friend but not his resolve. He shares that he plans to, like a noodle in soup, wander Fore Lindrov with his Kuo-Toa and see where the tide takes them, mentioning to Lu after seeing the [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Items/Magic Items/Wondrous Items/Heart of Heaven & Earth\|Heart of Heaven & Earth]] that he believes he will see the party again. Then having shared his vast wisdom, Monk Kame and his disciples leave. 

## Dragon Cult

Next are the Dragon Cultists, Cyanwrath and Neronvain. Neronvain is suspicious of the party, only trusting Elisa and believing the others to be spies of The Cabal. Cyanwrath is eager to fight, as always, which Roast agrees with. But ultimately Cyanwrath laments to hold off the battle till the next time they meet. In this usual crazed state, Neronvain rambles at the party, speaking words that sound illogical but contain truths. When Neronvain learns that the party has the stone, he asks for it. But right as Zipzap is busy insulting Lu, telling him that he has no children because they wouldn’t want to be his. Which crosses a line as Lu tells Zipzap that he’s gone too far, handing the stone to Elisa before leaving. Elisa warns Zipzap that children are a soft spot for Lu. 

They hand the Heart of Heaven & Earth to Neronvain who begins deciphering the rock immediately, saying something along the lines of the pillar that shakes the Earth lies in a slag of iron. Which Zephyros points out to be the Fire Giant’s seat, also known as the Ironslag. Neronvain continues his rambles, muttering something about a nail which Roast interjects is true, vaguely alluding that she knows something of said nail.   Cyanwrath then hands a rock of teleportation circle to Elisa, proclaiming that he shall escort Neronvain back on foot. The two leave as well. 

## Derendil

The party then speaks to the Quaggoth King, Elisa rejects the King’s offer to join his group as they too leave as well. 

## Anintra Baenre

Which leaves the party with one last matter, Anintra, still trapped in the prison the party had left her in, they now get to decide her fate. Elisa suggests killing the drow, seeing no reason to leave one alive when they have no use for her. Zipzap agrees, wanting to cut off her head when Roast interjects that they should not waste meat. The three are in agreement as Grimrid steps in, he suggests leaving the drow be, seeing not much sense in spending more time on the drow. 

But Roast and Elisa disagree, citing that they do not want to leave another evil being about to bring more pain to the others in the Underdark. Grimrid however, is confident that the drow would sooner die to a mindflayer than be able to cause any more harm. But Grimrid defers his vote to Lu, not wanting either of the two “childlike Eparty members to bear the responsibility of taking the drow’s life. Lu agrees with the murder. The party heads to the cell, unlocking it as they work together to drag the Drow out, with Grimrid swinging his axe right into her chest, ending her painfully. 

## Nyktos

Before they leave, Elisa receives two Sendings. The first from a familiar voice, who apprehensively calls out, it turns out Nyktos is alive and fine in her home. Elisa shares word of Crimson and that he’s with Wystram. Though both agree that Crimson is likely the more responsible one between the two. But Nyktos confirms that she has sent people out to retrieve her dog. She apologises to the party and shares word that there’s trouble in the tower of the Eternal Flame, pushing the party to go to the Tower to see what's going on.

## Iymrith

The second Sending is from Iymrith, who checks in to see if the party has found the spelunker. The party updates Iymrith on their current mission and she apologises for not being much help as Uthor has become more brazen recently, making unilateral decisions on his own, despite earlier on being locked down by Iymrith's manipulation of Nym and Mirran. Whilst Nym is still under her control, Mirran, seeing Uthor's actions has become spurred to also begin taking action on her own.

She mentions that Uthor has been in communication with Tritons, and that she must lie low for now in the Maelstrom, lest she be found out and killed by the Storm Giants. She will continue actively trying to continue with keeping the Storm Giants inactive, with the old way of proposing a two-queen solution, and any action taken by the Storm Giants would require an agreement of the two, and thus knowing that Mirran and Nym would naturally argue with one another effectively crippling the Storm Giant's now no longer working, she must find a new solution.

Ilymrith also mentions her conversation with Vaghar and the mention of the mask, which Elisa awkwardly explains that she had to give up the mask to Ardor Mor, that it was the only way. Iymrith does not blame Elisa, understanding and placing her trust in her, and after Iymrith asks for the condition of the party, with Elisa citing that they aren't doing too well. Iymrith tells them to find a gargoyle of hers to aid them on their journey. 

The party does so, finding a gargoyle 20 feet up. Roast climbs up first, waving and greeting the statue before gaining three potions from it, patting the statue as she leaves. Elisa goes up next, greeting Iymrith as well and thanking her for the items. Then Lu, who receives a single copper and bemoans for second before getting chastised by the others for his lack of manners. Ivy and Grimrid get their items as well, with Grimrid clinging onto Ivy as she scales the walls. 

# Elizar's Funeral

## Mourning Cultists

### Vrolda

With that, the party heads to the Tower of the Eternal Flame. At the gate, Mimin, the Magma Elemental that guards the way into the tower seems oddly sad to Elisa. Upon entering the Tower, the party finds out why, the body of Elizar Dryflagon, Administrator to the Eternal Flame and the first Cultist to have found Vanifer lays in the middle, with his friends surrounding his corpse. 

The party learns that Vrolda was the one who who found his corpse, that they had been out on a mission to retrieve a piece of the Vonindod. With Elizar desperately wanting to leave the Tower to do a mission so that he could prove to Vanifer that he could do things without her, and that he wanted to improve his anxiety so that he could serve her better. 

Angry, Elisa blames Vrolda for allowing Elizar to be alone, claiming that he should know that Elizar needed Vanifer. The others stay silent, knowing it's not their place to interject but as they watch, Ivy realises the wounds on Elizar’s corpse tell a story, how in his last moments he was brutalised, and the final blow completely disfiguring him. His fingers on one of his hands were gone, implying he had lifted his hand to cover his face, and his other arm is mangled, implying that whoever killed him disabled him and forced him into a kneeling position, and the final blow ripped through his head. 

Vrolda expresses that his honour is stained forever, and that he will live the rest of his wretched life trying to make up for this incompetency. A notion that Elisa at the time doesn't seem to have any sympathy for, but Lu and Grimrid do.

### Lyzandra

Lysandra, Elizar's "Rival" mourns very deeply and angrily laments that her lack of eyes makes her unable to cry, unable to release her feelings as others can, forced to keep it in. Elisa tries to comfort Lysandra and so does Roast although the words of a stranger mean nothing when a closed one has passed. Roast approaches the body, quietly praying under her breath, hoping that Elizar’s death is useful for those who come after. 

### Gilli

Lu does a small tea ceremony, paying his respects to the dead. Elisa then turns to Gili, who seems regretful and unsure, the child has never seen Vanifer in such an emotional state and does not know what to do. The party also realises as the mushroom on Gilli’s head grows. Elisa suggests a way to mourn one’s death is to continue paintings, to make those in honour of those that have passed. 

### Xaxan

The party leaves the main hall, continuing upstairs when Xazan interrupts them. He asks for the party’s help to place a dagger by Zegdar’s side. Xaxan shares he had been the one to open a scroll they found on Elizar's person, and would have been blasted to ash like his changelings had it not been for Zegdar who had pushed Xaxan out of the way and shielded Xaxan from the blast. Xaxan shares that Zegdar is now in critical condition since sustaining the injury. 

Elisa clarifies about the proper way to place the dagger, revealing Xaxan's superstition, he instructs Elisa to place it on Zegdar's right, and facing downward, saying that the dagger is the first weapon he had ever used to take a life, and that surely it's luck will heal Zegdar. 

## Upper Levels

### Emberhorn Tribe

The party heads upstairs to do just that. Coming up to the fifth floor, the party sees a group of Minotaurs huffing and puffing, upset that their leader is hurt. The Minotaurs refuse to let the party in, blaming Elisa for not being here to help the Tower of Eternal Flame. They cite that they hear Zhae Tsung relentlessly complaining that Elisa is not present to assist, and they have concluded that had Elisa been here, then their leader would not have been harmed in the first place. The party, faced with no choice, beat the Emberhorn Minotaurs into submission, illiciting a renewed respect. All of the party members participated in the combat except for Grimrid, who sees no need to place the dagger if they are not allowed in. After beating up the Minotaurs, the party are allowed in and they meet both Zegdar and the Nothics. 

### Zegdar's Condition

Zegdar is shown to have become horrifically burnt, and apologises to the party for not being able to stand to greet them, in their conversation Zegdar reveals his motivation to marry Vanifer and have children with her, so that their Bulls of Fire may coat the world in fire. Wyx announces to Elisa loudly that many believe Elisa is to blame for Zegdar's condition and thus the Prophet Vanifer's sorry state. He does however mention that Zegdar is one of the few in the Tower most closest to Imix, and that he is Immune to Fire, so it is strange that the Scroll's flames actually burnt him.

Roast tries to heal Zegdar, enlisting Grimrid’s help to pour a potion on Zegdar only for it to have no effect. Unable to help Zegdar, Elisa places the dagger, promising to find whoever did this as they wish him a smooth recovery and leave. 

## Zhae Tsung

### The Scroll

They head to the library where they find Zhae Tsung who as usual is annoying and has apparently been complaining enough about Elisa’s lack of appearance that some think she could be linked to all the issues the Eternal Flame have been experiencing. When questioned about the Scroll, it's discovered that he had taken it, and he calls Xaxan and Zegdar fools, that such things should have been brought to him first, and it is their stupidity that brought Zegdar his ills. Zhae Tsung also espouses that Vanifer is a child with no inkling on what needs to be done, he says that Destiny is to be grasped in one's hand, not something one simply bumbles into.

He also shows no remorse in regards to Elizar's death, citing everyone as emotion-addled fools and timewasters. In the meantime, Elisa teaches Roast the best way to get revenge on an enemy, which is to infiltrate their mind, as shown when she had pushed Zhae Tsung’s books only for him to have honed his reflexes to catch them. The party takes the scroll, looking at it as Roast uses identify on it to find that it is a scroll set to explode should it be opened by the wrong individual. Roast guesses that Elisa would be the right person, handing her the scroll as the party turns back to their spat with Zhae Tsung. 

### Ambush

An ambush occurs because Elisa forgets about the shaking item in her bag and being more curious about the scroll. Just when they least expect it, Aurelion’s insignia shoots out from Elisa’s bag, causing the scroll to open as a black hand shoots out, grabbing Zhae Tsung's face specifically. 

 Springing out of the letters are comes Daegra, the Werewolf Companion of Aurelion's freedom fighting MLA Leader rival, Ozul, whom they had watched die at the hands of The Flood. Daegra had in that time gone mad, and in order to cope with the horrors she saw, began to think that SHE was Ozul. But no longer, spikes and metals have been driven into her head, driving her mad, and the spikes pull and tug at her brain, forcing her transformation into her Werewolf Form (Loup Garou), alongside her is Damien Wyne, Aurelion's mentor and very good friend, now twisted alongside Daegra, with metal spikes also pinned into his head by Aurelion. Both of them having been tormented by Aurelion, under the control of the infernal machineries in their head attack the party. It's clear their target being to assassinate Zhae Tsung. The scroll also creates Walls of Force that block off any escape from the floor of the Tower they're on.

Combat ensues and in the midst of it, Grimrid is infected with Lycanthropy and lands the killing blow on Daegra, unknowingly taking on the unique curse of Lycanthropy Daegra had, with both Daegra and Damien turning into dust from exploding upon their deaths. Elisa heads over to Daegra’s corpse, promising her that when she next sees Aurelion, she would teach him a lesson for what he’s done.

In the meantime, Zhae Tsung spits upon the dust that was Damien, speaking about himself in third, and saying that "Zhae Tsung is not so easily killed.", declaring to the PCs that now that he has a measure of the enemy, he will orchestrate the method of their demise. The PCs also read the opened scroll, a clear messsage from Aurelion, and surprisingly addressed to Elisa with just the words, Elisa what do you see?", the second line being "Who do you think the enemy is?"

## Vizeran

Vizeran breaks through the Walls of Force with an adamantine weapon, accompanied by Kleve and his Undead Drow, he reveals that the Wall of Force also came with a silencing element to it, which ensured that no one would have come up to help the PCs. Elisa shoves Zhae Tsung, encouraging him to go to the funeral, who reluctantly does but not before dissing the party to find Grimrid who ran away in shame. 

The party follows the wallowing Grimrid who barks at the party to leave. Alas they do not, with Kleve stepping in to catch the tables that Grimrid threw at them, placing them back. The party tries to talk sense into Grimrid and when it does not work. Vizeran steps in, having his undead servant fetch a potion that he tells Grimrid to drink. Grimrid drinks the potion but Vizeran realises that the lycanthropy is not yet removed. He offers to help figure out Grimrid’s situation. In exchange, Grimrid offers that if Vizeran seeks to revolutionise his race, the party had just met Lolth that same day. Vizeran turns to Elisa who awkwardly shares that they had met his dad. 

He pretends to not care, calling his Father a fool, and that he'd prefer his father be dead. But softly asks Elisa if he is well, at which point they share that he seems to be doing alright. Vizeran shares more information, hinting to Grimrid that Gauntlgrym has been taken over by devils. Vizeran also speaks to Ivy, indirectly informing her to be careful, citing that there are many who seek to use Primordials as spell components due to their nearly limitless potential of Arcane Energies because of their connection to the primal energies.

He tells the PCs to fetch Vanifer, and mentions that one must say their goodbyes, even when it is difficult, and that people will so often regret the goodbyes they do not say, a slight hint at Vizeran's feelings about his own Father.

## Vanifer

The party proceeds to Vanifer’s room, knocking at first then entering when they hear no response. Inside, they are greeted by paintings. The first is a self portrait, in which Lu senses Vanifer’s feelings of pain, that half of her does not belong to her. Next is a Dark malevolent flaming entity that the party deduces is Imix. From it, Lu senses that Vanifer feels haunted by this entity. Then a painting of a bazaar, one that feels like the past. Then a curved dagger portrait sculpted from obsidian that feels extraplanar from the elemental plane. It bears Vanifer’s hesitation to pick up the dagger, to follow her destiny. Then a painting of Nyssa, and the volcano she presently resides in. 

On the smaller desk are paintings created by Gili, portraits of all the cult members, even Vizeran. Moving past the living quarters, the party finds Vanifer in the altar room, upset. She begins blaming herself for Elizar’s death, saying that she should have stopped him, that her actions were killing everyone. She rambles about Arale’s death, how she had died in her arms and had vowed to never experience that again yet now she let Elizar die. Vanifer begins questioning her abilities as leader, if she should even be one. Elisa steps in, sitting beside Vanifer as she comforts her, assuring her that these insecurities were normal, listing examples of Nyktos and herself. Lu does as well, approaching the situation from a general’s point of view that only serves to remind Vanifer of her duty. 

As Vanifer begins to pour her emotions out, the others reach out to offer comfort, Roast helps comb Vanifer’s hair whilst Ivy sits beside Vanifer, wrapping her tail around for comfort. When Vanifer refers to herself as fire, implying that she burns everything, Roast reminds her that fire can be useful, that it is warm, Vanifer is warm. Vanifer also shares her birth place, the land of fate where there was nothing but desert and everything was for sale. As a slave with no last name, Vanifer became a consort. 

But the woman who owned her was kind, always telling her that she’d get out, and how she always treated everyone the same regardless of the wealth they bring or do not, and said that no one was born bad. When the dame sold Vanifer to a noble Jami, she told her to make something of herself. That’s when Vanifer met Arale, who was abused by the Jami. From then Vanifer promised herself to keep people safe, to ensure that no one by her side suffers from the same pain she did. Yet when everyone turns to her for help, she’s at a loss of what to do. Ivy reminds Vanifer to say goodbye, to be with her family, as she wished she could have. With the party’s encouragement, Vanifer stands up, as Elisa helps tie up her hair once more with a piece of scrap fabric provided by Roast. 

The party knows that Vanifer is having a hard time because no one here at the Tower treats her like a normal person, they all see her as a Divine Creature, a Prophet, rather than "Vanifer". As they exit the room, they find Grimrid with Zipzap, moving about the items in outside Vanifer's Room. When asked why, Grimrid claims that they were cleaning up, though their true intent was to escape the emotional moment. 

# The Funeral

As Vanifer makes her way down, she looks to the party for assurance before stepping out to see her Cultists, who immediately begin to call out for their leader, reaching out for her like she's some kind of divine being to be touched. They clearly do not see her as Human, but something MORE, despite her VERY young age.

Vrolda immediately begins apologising to Vanifer, but is shut up by Ivy who glares at him. Then Lysandra approaches, pleading for Vanifer to carry Elizar’s corpse with her sacred touch so that he might go to the afterlife. Elisa steps in, offering the party’s help as they all lift the corpse in Vanifer’s stead. 

Then Vanifer gives a speech, sharing that Elizar was her first friend and that she would miss him for the rest of her life. One by one, they all approach the body, offering him a final word. Vrolda apologises once more, vowing to carry this dishonour for the rest of his life. 

Then Lysandra, who kisses his cheek as a farewell. Elisa thanks him for his welcomes every time she visited, and his hospitality, always ensuring that she’d have her buckets ready for baths, and lastly for all the help he provided teaching her different animal forms. Ivy tells him that he is loved, and has a big family who cares for him in life and death. 

Roast, seeing a kind funeral for the first time, realises that funerals are for the living as each individual before her mourns Elizar’s death. She offers him a prayer, hoping that he might end up in the right afterlife despite what they know of dead souls, but if he is not, she will help ensure that he is. As does Lu, hoping to help right this wrong. 

And Grimrid, who laments that this flame has sputtered out. Vanifer pushes Elizar’s body into the lava, letting out a yell as she feels his body turn to ash before vowing to continue Elizar’s mission. She comes out of the lava with a pipe, Elizar’s. She asks the party for their help to stop the fire giants, and as they agree she hands them the pipe, telling them to make sure he’s there when they stop the fire giants.
