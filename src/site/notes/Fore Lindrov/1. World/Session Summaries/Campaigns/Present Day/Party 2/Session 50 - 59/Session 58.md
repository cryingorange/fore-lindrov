The party deals with the clean up as Lucatiel deals with the awakened book that continuously screams in abject horror. Elisa and Nyktos take the opportunity to tease Lucatiel about their chad phase, reciting their heroics back to them. To put an end to the teasing, Lucatiel offers Elisa 50 gold, which the sorcerer accepts. Lucatiel then tries the same tactic with Nyktos but offers her 30 instead, however, with the increase of price to match 50 gold, both parties agree to forget Chad-Lucatiel’s existence. Tiny and Blade speak with one another as Tiny learns of Blade’s mission to purge the land of chaos in preparation for something called “The March E  
  
When asked where he would go next, Blade plays a recording. The voice of Reggie rings through, “Wherever The Winds Take!! E Excited to hear their dad’s voice, Tiny wants a way to record it and Lucatiel produces a magic item capable of doing so, allowing Tiny to record their dad’s voice twice. 

  

Elisa’s friend, Duncan, awakens. Elisa greets him fondly, calling him “Ding Dong Eaffectionately as her friend instantly remarks on Elisa’s lack of eyes. Duncan clarifies how he was healed and learnt that The Star Mistress had cured him of his affliction. Elisa checks in to see how her friend is feeling, as Duncan shares that he had seen the Elder Brain, that something terrible is going to happen.  
  
He explains that the mind flyers had enthralled everyone, trapping them in some kind of army and inserting a tadpole into their eyes. But their goddess had protected everyone, keeping them hidden in a part of their minds where the mind flyers couldn’t find them. Duncan shares that the moon isn’t just a far realm artifact or some kind of influence exerted by the Unnatural Old One, but rather an egg, that the Old One is gestating within the moon.  
  
That all of the multiverse will be destroyed if it hatches. Elisa changes the topic, asking Duncan about his brother, though he unfortunately knows not of what’s happened to the others. All he knows is that they had moved Solaris, that the Purple Tiefling and her friends they had once helped had sold them to the mind flayers. Explaining that the three had wanted to kill The Star Mistress and seized the opportunity, using the mind flayers to rid the city of Solaris. This enrages Elisa, outraged that the people they had helped repaid their kindness with betrayal. The party asks for Blade’s help, requesting that he help lead Duncan back to where their home is. Blade readily agrees, and kindly provides some metal shavings for future scrying purposes should the party need to find him. 

  

The party heads down, picking up Vinnie and Elisa convinces him to join their friend Dolores, stating that she’s a chef who would be able to cook him delicious foods. They then head to Sandy, who requires some convincing but with flattery from the party and Elisa’s persuasive words, Sandy agrees to follow the party down the beanstalk. Near the door, the party makes one last detour to the kitchen.  
  
Lucatiel spends their time using a combination of spells to freeze and cool the soup pot down to a manageable temperature, whilst Nyktos frantically embroiders a kitchen towel for Dolores. Finishing her work, Nyktos showcases the towel to the party, in their exhaustion Lucatiel comments that the towel doesn’t look nice, disheartening the cleric. Though Elisa is quick to reassure Nyktos that the embroidery is nice and Lucatiel takes back their harsh remarks. Tiny brings the heavy pot of soup to the door, offering it to the two mimics who happily enjoy their meal, blissfully unaware that their master is no longer alive. The party makes their way past the doors, stealing the rug as intended before heading back down to where Jack is. 

  

Having completed their mission with the Golden, the party has to fulfil their promise with Jack.  
  
Not wanting to give Jack false hope, Nyktos secretly prays to Tenebrous, asking him to give Jack a second chance at life, that a man like Jack who has lost everything would no doubt dedicate his life to Tenebrous if he helped. Her prayers are answered as a crimson light envelops the space, wrapping around Jack’s body, rotting the parts of his body that had turned to plant. Jack screams from the immense pain as his body decays and reforms his human limbs.  
  
The party watches as Jack is given a second chance at life, reborn by Tenebrous Ewill. Jack falls to his knees, barely coming to grips with what has just happened. Nyktos approaches Jack, placing a gentle warm hand on his cheek as she lifts his face to meet her gaze.  
  
As Jack looks up, his eyes, once empty and devoid of life, are reinvigorated as he sees his raison d'etre, the Aimorragikos. Nyktos explains that the Lord of Blood has given him a second chance at life, that he is now to be the Iron. She has him rise, taking his hand and breaking off his middle finger. Though he does not scream this time. The blood drips from his fingers, forming a blade of blood and his lost finger.  
  
Having told him his mission, Nyktos tells Jack to return home, channelling her magic as he combusts. Blood splatters on everyone nearby as Jack crumbles into a puddle on the ground, absorbed and creating a network of connections that Tenebrous Emost loyal followers can tap into. Using the connection, Nyktos senses Callahan communicating that the Black Hand welcomes its second finger.  
  
With that settled, Nyktos turns back to the party who are reasonably a little shocked, requesting less of a mess for when that happens next time. Tiny comments on Nyktos Espeech, complimenting that she’s improved since last and immediately attributing it to the notes they had given to Nyktos. Seeing as they are about to part ways soon, Nyktos offers Elisa to request for Duncan’s blood, just so that she can check in on him during his journey back. Elisa asks Duncan if he wishes to give Nyktos some of his blood which freaks out the Solarian.  
  
Though a quick explanation later has Duncan understanding the situation and teasing Elisa, calling her “Buttlisa E The party enjoys the banter, especially Lucatiel who takes the opportunity to ask for more embarrassing stories about Elisa. Duncan shares some and divulges Elisa’s hero name “Elisa the Eminent E which the party immediately uses to tease their friend. 

  

With that, Lucatiel teleports them back to Parsley’s cave. Duncan and Blade take their leave, heading for the party’s home. Sandy speaks with Parsley, revealing the party’s trickery that led her back here. Parsley confronts the party about the matter, learning that the party had to take down The Golden in order to retrieve Sandy and casually asks for The Golden’s spellbook.  
  
The party rejects and reiterates that they did what was demanded of them according to their deal. Seeing as the party has decided to play coy, Parsley agrees, releasing them of their contract as they feel the gift of immortality they had faded. What remains, however, is Tiny, still in their fey boar form.  
  
Naturally, Tiny kicks up a fuss, unhappy that they had not reverted back to their draconic self. Tiny rushes up to Parsley, offering a deal for their original self back. Unsure of what to offer in exchange, Tiny offers an IOU, which does intrigue the fey though Lucatiel is quick to suggest otherwise, warning Tiny that it is not good to owe a fey anything. Instead Tiny offers the staff they had recently acquired, the staff of fate.  
  
Elisa steps in, flattering Parsley for his good sense of style and taste, instead offering him the rug from the Golden’s house. Parsley takes the deal, letting Tiny keep the staff of fate and transforms them back into their original form, the transformation leaving a mark of fey magic on Tiny. Having settled that, Parsley and Sandy take their leave, with Parsley telling the party to give the ring to King Raula. 

  

The party turns in for the night, and Nyktos decides to check in on the six individuals outside of New Phandalin. Instead of a peaceful night, she sees their fear and shock, screams and chaos amok.  
  
Nyktos rushes out, seeing the moon in a different phase. The once yellow moon is now black, with a wide grin upon its face as laughter fills the air. The grin splits, as black ichor like tar pours from its mouth, inking the world. Rushing back in, Nyktos warns the party of the situation outside which Tiny heads out to see, rushing back in with a horrified expression. Lucatiel seems the most unaffected, too tired to entertain the changes of the moon.  
  
Before the party rests, Nyktos and Tiny have a conversation with Nyktos handing Tiny the bag of holding they had desired. However, the bag comes with at a cost with Tiny taking over responsibility of Dame Aylin’s body. Tiny grumbles about Pyrite but accepts. The next morning, the party sets out, some of them seeing the black moon for the first time. They realise the darkness takes a toll out of them, though light provides comfort and ease of travel. Lucatiel uses their magic to create a stone that emits just enough light for the road. 

  

Along their way back, the party comes across a patch of land covered in the black tar from the moon. Nyktos, Zipzap, Crimson begin sinking into the tar. With the party’s help, they manage to pull themselves out, though the black tar sticks to Crimson and Zipzap who sank in deeper. Lucatiel notices that the tar is flammable and tests it on a small, isolated portion.  
  
The tar bursts into flames, creating a small explosion. Seeing as Crimson is unaffected by fire, the party tests it out, setting the tar on fire and safely removing the tar from him. Not seeing much harm to the tar, Zipzap leaves it on. The party returns to New Phandalin where the atmosphere is heavy. Finding the party as soon as they return is King Raula.  
  
He remarks that the moon did something, attributing the moon’s change to the Perpetuals, frantically requesting for the item that the party found in the cave. Nyktos requests for a more private place to speak and the King leads them to a house guarded by two men. They’re allowed in and come face to face with what seems to be several statues. Except they’re not.  
  
Instead what stands before them are people, men, women, and children alike who are petrified, their faces morphed into an unsettlingly wide smile. King Raula explains that these individuals looked at the moon and were suddenly transformed back. Nyktos looks at the individuals and kneels before a small child, casting a spell to cure them of the moon’s effects. It works and the child is unpetrified, now sent back to their parents.  
  
Knowing that they can’t fix everyone today, Nyktos hands King Raula the ring, informing him of the power it has and the dangers of wielding such immense power. Raula expresses that they do not have time and shares his concerns that the Perpetuals are here to kill them. He leads the party to an empty town hall, bringing them down to where two captives are kept. Upon entering the party can smell the stench of blood and see the various tools set out that were used on the two scouts Raula suspects are individuals from the Perpetuals.  
  
They look through the scouts items, finding two letters and learning more about the individuals. Tiny walks up to one of the men, soon realising that the man wasn’t looking down but rather dead in his chair. Looking at the wounds, they realise the extent of torture the man was put through as Nyktos realises that the other man is less hurt and was likely forced to watch his friend suffer. The party tries to speak with the remaining scout, with Elisa reading his mind and calling his bluff. Though the party fails to gather any information from the man.  
  
The man spits in Tiny’s face, aggravating Tiny’s other self, as their hand is raised, ready to kill the man for his disrespect. Though Elisa manages to stop it, allowing Tiny to calm their other self down. The torture has both the party and King Raula uncomfortable as he proposes that they leave to find another method. Before they do, the scout calls out that the party and Raula are already dead, that they should prepare to face the order’s wrath. 

  

King Raula suggests that the party find other scouts and follow them to their camp instead. The party agrees and sets out to do so. Before they leave, Nyktos stops by the six refugees, now allowed within the walls of New Phandalin. They are fine, although disturbed by the appearance of the moon as is everyone else.  
  
Concerned for their safety, Nyktos offers them gold, enough to cover three nights worth of stay for the six people at the local tavern. She apologises for the delay in their departure and assures them that they will be back soon as she blesses for their blood to be strong and carry them through this endeavour. 

  

The party leaves and Lucatiel spots a location not too far where scouts might spy on New Phandalin from. Leading the party there, Nyktos notices some tracks and Tiny follows those same tracks, bringing the party close to where a campsite is. Conjuring an arcane eye, Elisa scouts the area, noticing the two men at the campsite and how the tar has completely covered their bodies. Tiny and Nyktos walk up to investigate the two sleeping bodies only to realise that the two are not waking up despite the loud noises the party is making. Elisa warns Tiny of some individuals within the lake, whilst Nyktos investigates the bodies.  
  
Before they can react, two tentacle-like creatures jump out as the bodies come to life. The party engages in combat, dealing with the creatures as best as they can. Lucatiel distracts one of them, luring the creature in with their bright light and handling it themselves as the creature remarks how delicious Lucatiel’s mind seems.  
  
Nyktos puppets the two bodies, hearing a voice cry out as she takes over control. Nyktos lands a hit on one of the creatures covered in tar, only to realise that radiant energy ignites tar similar to how fire does. With the situation under control, Crimson takes the final bite on the two tentacle creatures, ending their lives. 

  

With that settled, the party investigates what is left of the area when the blue skinned individuals Elisa spotted surface. Turns out, they are merfolk merchants from Neroswim, Tynee and Wavia. The party purchases some wares, with Lucatiel finally getting the ink they wanted and Tiny buys the two weapons they have for sale whilst Elisa buys them out of all the fish they have.  
  
The merfolk also happen to witness the situation at the campsite, informing the party that they saw a mind flayer take one of the scouts when a purple tiefling then came by, tracking the mind flayer. Looking around the area, Tiny spots a trail of blood, unrelated to the fight they just had. They point it out to the party and Nyktos is able to bond with the individual whose blood it is. Seeing from their senses, she witnesses as a mind flayer stares at the individual, scalpel in hand as he is prepared to remove their brain like they did with the other. But before he does, something piques the mind flayers attention, causing him to turn around when the connection cuts.  
  

Nyktos explains what she saw as they ran, telling the party about how odd it was for the connection to suddenly sever. They rush to a cave entrance, entering to find dead intellect devourers. Tiny hangs back, investigating one of the dead creatures as the others rush forward, only to activate a trap and be incapacitated. Tiny learns that the intellect devourers were killed by a mere cantrip of toll the dead.  
  
They share this with the party and Nyktos discovers that whoever came here before they did took a single path, stopping in the centre of the room and easily killing all the intellect devourers before moving forward. Elisa blasts the lights out, deactivating the trap as the party moves on forward. They rush into the next chamber only to find a purple tiefling with green eyes that crackle with power. 

  

Lucatiel studies the tiefling, realising that her eyes hold no emotions. And as the tiefling spots Elisa, there is a single moment of emotion before it flickers away as the tiefling resolves her emotions in a matter of seconds. Recognising the tiefling, Elisa confronts her, shouting that they helped her, helped cure whatever was inside of her only for her to betray them, sell them to the mind flayers for no good reason.  
  

The tiefling simply remarks that Elisa wouldn’t understand, to which the sorcerer responds, commenting that Fool’s words mimic Goetia’s. Though Fool pays no mind to anything and battle begins. The party is able to break Fool’s hold on the mind flayer, annoying her a tad as they stand in their way. Elisa and Lucatiel constantly block her spells from activating. Elisa successfully prevents Fool from casting invulnerability on herself, turning the tide of combat. Fool attempts to kill the mind flayer first, wanting to reduce him to nothing but ash, though Crimson is able to stop that, protecting the mind flayer from death. Though sadly the mind flayer still dies later in battle.  
  
With the mind flayer dead, Fool’s attention turns to Elisa. But before she’s able to do anything, a spell from Nyktos kills her. And for a second it’s almost unreal. Elisa, Zipzap, Tiny, Nyktos, and Crimson all gather around the body. Zipzap remarks if they can eat her, whilst Nyktos asks for the heart. Though before they do that, Fool’s eyes shoot open as combat continues. Tiny activates the fey magic that resides within them, triggering an unstable flux of energy that causes random bursts of magic. This disrupts Fool’s plans. Fool rushes to Elisa, attempting to set up a wall of force that is counterspelled by Elisa. The wild magic however, grants her a boost, allowing her to cast a Prismatic Wall around Elisa, thinking that she’s successfully trapped her inside alone.  
  
Though in an instant, Nyktos teleports before Fool, antagonising the wizard with a coy smile. Tiny teleports in with Zipzap as well, both attacking the wizard. Fool walks out of the wall, summoning a dragon and letting it deal with the party. Lucatiel conjures a wall of stone around the prismatic wall, preventing Fool’s escape. Whilst Nyktos continues to mess with the wizard, teleporting to the wall above them. Tiny uses the Pincer Staff they just bought, hitting Fool with it and dragging Fool back into the prismatic wall right before Elisa. Pulling out her dagger, Elisa stabs Fool, ending her life.  
  
Though unlike most that die from Elisa’s dagger, she can feel that the soul doesn’t get absorbed but instead goes some place else as Fool comments that she would return. Though for now the party rejoices, picking up the wizard’s items. They look around at the dead bodies around, all the Solarians who had been killed. Elisa looks at all of the corpses, checking if any of them are anyone she knows, fortunately there are none that Elisa knows personally, though the symbols around their neck denmark them as Solarians all the same. Searching through Fool's belongings, Elisa finds a map of Fore Lindrov, with indicators of several locations, including Larryton, the place Vendrin mentioned before. With the battle now over, Elisa now has a big decision to make, a decision that decides the fates of the fallen Solarians.
