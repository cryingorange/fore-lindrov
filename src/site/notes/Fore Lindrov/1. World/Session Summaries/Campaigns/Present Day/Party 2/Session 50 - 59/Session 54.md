As they make their way North, the party comes across a commotion, 4 large shapes looming up ahead where a bridge lies. Stealthily approaching, the party sees a man dressed in a kimono alongside a halfling attempting to negotiate with the four trolls for passage for them and their group. Elisa recognises them both having met Shizuka and Alvar before. Tiny and Lucatiel remain hidden, each hiding behind a rock. Lucatiel notices a child by themselves and waves them over to protect them. Meanwhile, Elisa and Nyktos approach to better understand the situation and help. There they meet the leader of the Trolls, Hack, who demands to know Elisa’s name and proceeds to mock her for having a long name, claiming that long is bad.  
  
He also demands for 50 gold and 50 pounds of food for every creature that wishes to pass their bridge. Elisa first tries to trick Hack using his own logic, saying that “fifty Eis very long and therefore bad, unlike “one Ethat is short. Though that logic fails to work as the Trolls can’t count but more so recognise numbers of coins by their relative weights. Elisa and Nyktos attempt to lie to Hack, using silver and copper pieces to come up with a fake currency called “Gak E  
  
Amidst this, Tiny springs up from behind the rock, attempting to scare the trolls, though their landing falls short as they instead get mocked by Hack for their failed attempt. Elisa and Nyktos use the distraction to fill the bag of “Gak Ewith more stones, creating the impression of more “Gak E They hand the bag over and Hack is satisfied, even thinking the party stupid for giving him more than 50 “Gak E Though that’s where the schemes end as the party realises that they do not have sufficient amounts to provide all the other “Gak Eand food that the trolls want.  
  
Nyktos instead takes a different approach, tired of these Trolls trying to bully the innocent folk. She pulls out her Rod of the Blood Ruler, its heart pulsing as she points it at Hack. Hack laughs, finding it amusing as the little elf points a stick at him. Nyktos offers him a choice, to let them pass or else. And as Hack doesn’t see them as a threat, he opens his mouth to mock. But as the laughter passes his lips, Nyktos utters an ancient word causing Hack to explode as blood splatters from where he once stood. Nyktos looks at the rest of the Trolls, telling them that she’s killed their leader, so she is the leader now and they will be passing through. The atmosphere is tense, both sides having witnessed a person combust just from a single word. They all pass without issue, as the halfling speaks to the party, throwing nervous glances towards Nyktos who offers to sit in front of the man if it means that it would ease his nerves. The party rests for the night and Nyktos, in hopes of making things better, offers a casting of spells to improve their chances of survival to which they accept. 

  

The party leaves and heads towards the Northern Kingdom, soon arriving at a tall wall made of wood and stone. The guards call out to them as they approach, alarmed by Zipzap. Their behaviour leads to the party not entering town yet, instead choosing to speak with the group of people outside the Kingdom’s walls. The group is wary, initially mistaking the party to be sent to chase them away. But as the party interacts with them, the group soon realises that the party is aligned with them in their general dislike of high taxation, at least Elisa and Nyktos are. Learning that the group has someone sick, Nyktos offers her services as a cleric and heals the ill, as an added precaution she asks for their blood under the guise of wanting to check for traces of illnesses. Elisa offers them some goodberries, allowing their wounds to heal instantly. The party gains the goodwill of the group and also offers to guide them to the safeguard they had heard from Shizuka. The group willingly accepts, now staying outside the kingdom, awaiting for the party to be done with their business before they can leave. 

The party approaches the gates once more, now aware of the high taxation that the city charges. They speak to the guards once more and all attempt to prove their status as artisans. Elisa and Lucatiel both attempt to be recognised as herbalists and alchemists though the guards don’t seem to believe them. Tiny markets themselves as an inventor, boasting the suit that they wear as their creation. Having not seen much machinery like that before, the guards believe them. Lastly, Nyktos uses her weavers skill to enter, gesturing to the party who all wear a piece of clothing created by her. The guards allow Nyktos and Tiny in for free but the remaining four would still require to pay a fee of 120 gold per creature, suspiciously 80 gold less than what the group outside had told the party the fee was. Not wanting to pay the insanely steep prices, Elisa, Lucatiel, Zipzap, and Crimson pretend to stay outside, whilst secretly sneaking into the kingdom, successfully avoiding taxations. However, as they pass through the gates, Nyktos overhears the guards commenting on having nothing to pocket this time around. The party all enter the Kingdom to see houses and shophouses all made of dark wood, the area is crowded but decent for a new Kingdom. The party is approached by a man with two guards who wears a jewelled ring. The man introduces himself as King Raula Rex, sharing with the party how he had found a vast treasury of gold and wanted to create a Kingdom of humans to return them to their former glory. He asks if the party are adventurers and requests for their help with a certain individual in town. There lives a man in New Phandalin, an eccentric inventor by the name of Moliére who refuses to contribute to the Kingdom, refusing to pay over 2000 gold in taxes. The party agrees to help and the king provides free entry to all of the party. 

  

Now all entering the Kingdom legally, the party heads towards Moliére’s house. They note the covered dirt in front of the man’s house, all confused and curious as Elisa lifts the tarp and investigates the dirt. Wary of any traps, Lucatiel stays at the back of the group. Suddenly from behind Lucatiel, they hear a voice call out, claiming that they have disturbed the dirt. Moliére, now enraged by the party’s actions, activates his creations as four void creatures burst out from the dirt. Moliére claims to be a sovereign citizen and attacks the party as battle breaks out. The party has no choice but to fight back, easily destroying Moliére’s creations one by one. In the heat of battle, Moliére ends up teleporting both himself and Tiny away from the battle. Though eventually the party is victorious. Lucatiel helps themself to Moliére’s house, grabbing the sack of gold, ready to leave. Though the party stops them, only taking the two thousand gold that Moliére owes before returning to King Raula. The King rewards them for their efforts and the party takes the chance to learn exactly how much the entrance fee to New Phandalin should be and informs the King of his knights Eless than honest behaviour. The party also learns of a group called the Perpeptuals, a murder order of knights that bear a symbol of a silver mask. 

  

With time to spare, the party explores the area, remembering their original intentions for coming to New Phandalin. They first reach the Blacksmith’s, meeting a man called Damien who expressed interest in interesting materials such as Dragon scales. Elisa pulls out the scale that Iymrith had given her and Damien pays her 1000 gold for the scale as well as the promise to build an armour of it for her. Nyktos also has a scale from the Sea Serpents and requests for a shield to which Damien is more than happy to make. As part of the deal, Nyktos bargains for a collar for Crimson. The party then head to the Tavern where they are met with the barkeep who seems to have a gloomy demeanour. The party tries to get the man to open up, though to little success. Instead, he offers them a job, informing them of the shipments of alcohol that should have arrived but haven’t. The party accepts and heads out to check on the shipment. 

  

Along the way, the party comes across a man who's running away from a pack of hellhounds. The party rushes in to save the man, with Lucatiel wrapping a protective wall around the man, keeping him safe from the hellhounds whilst the party makes quick work of them. The man begs for help, informing the party of the situation in his hometown up ahead. The party then rushes into town, wanting to help the innocents who are currently held hostage by some Hill Giants. As they arrive, they see a half elf with spiky black hair, a chiselled body with scars that form constellations flexing his muscles at the Giants. The party approaches carefully, inquiring the man about the situation and learning more of what happened. Lucatiel attempts to help, doing a show of strength with their magic, pretending to lift a rock, tossing it up in the air before dropping it back down. That succeeds in impressing the leader, who agrees to let one person go. From Ken, they learn that he is from the fire cult, and in an attempt to save the people here have already given all his worldly pos## Session s to the Giants. When asked what happened to the unconscious hellhounds, Ken replies that he is a pacifist, he kills only men.  
  
The statement puzzles the party but they are willing to let it slide for now. The leader of the Giants, Chief Mog, demands more entertainment from the party and Lucatiel does yet another show that impresses the Giants, allowing one more civilian to be free. Chief Mog makes a comment about someone named chieftess Guh, though the party doesn’t get more information than that. As part of the negotiation, Chief Mog also demands that the party not give him papers like the reddies did. To bargain for the release of more innocents the party is sent to fight Chief Mog’s pets. The party does so, taking out a couple of them before Chief Mog screams that his favourite was killed. This begins the party’s fight against four Giants and their allies. They begin to pick up the cages that housed the innocents, wanting to throw them where the party was. Lucatiel summons a wall of force that blocks the Giants Eaccess from some of the cages. Tiny and Ken both brace themselves, catching the cages as they land in their direction. Elisa summons a large inky black hand to catch the man who had been thrown as well. As the Merregons rush up to attack the man, Nyktos summons her blood elemental who shoves the man out of the way, taking the damage for him. Tiny charges up to fight the Giants only to be picked up and thrown towards the house Lucatiel is on. Using the bead that Lucatiel had given, Zipzap casts slow, holding back some of the enemies. Lucatiel focuses their attacks on one of the giants, killing it with psychic energy. Tiny rushes back to the Giants, slamming their axe into the Giant’s skull, jumping off and using the Giant’s arm to push off from before driving their axe all the way through, cleaving the giant in half. Nyktos works with her elemental and Crimson to free the innocents from their cage, the three also working together to end the giant they were fighting. Lastly, Tiny swings his axe at the remaining Giant, cutting his head clean off. With that, combat ends, the civilians all safe. Ken properly introduces himself as the Fire of Might, sharing his humble beginnings and the moment he found joy in body building, and of his desire to spread goodness to the world, wanting to keep the passionate flame lit in the hearts of youths. The party takes the opportunity to address his statement of “killing only men Eand suggests he change it to “killing only evil men Eto prevent any confusion.  
  
Ken thanks the party for their help and then proceeds to kneel before Nyktos. The others ooh and ah at this, with Lucatiel commenting how it corresponds to storybooks and fairy tales. Nyktos, not used to Ken’s behaviour, pats his shoulder and compliments him on the job well done. Ken then leaves, continuing his mission of recruiting members for the fire cult. The party settles the matter of the shipment. And Lucatiel experiences yet another moment of her greed for money taking over her logical side as she almost pockets some of the money for herself. Concerned by this, Nyktos takes the opportunity to speak to Elisa about the matter, suggesting that maybe she relaxes the joke about healing requiring payment, Elisa agrees informing Lucatiel that healing is now free as part of their employee benefits. She even carves a stone for Lucatiel, naming them employee of the month, which Nyktos helps attach to a ribbon for a low fee. With everyone safe, the party heads back to the tavern, giving the barkeep his shipments and earning enough of his trust to get his name. As promised, Regix provides the party with free lodging. 

  

The party explores the kingdom once more, bumping into King Raula who Nyktos notices seems much more anxious than before. The King requests for their help with regards to the squatters outside and the party informs the King of the good news that they have it already settled, that they will bring them out once they are done with their current business here. The King then asks the party for one last errand, informing them of a great beast two hours away from the Kingdom. He requests for the party to end the beast and bring back any hoards it might have should it benefit the Kingdom. The party agrees and sets out on the quest. 

  

They enter a space where unbeknownst to them, a wave of magic hits. The party senses nothing, continuing on when a group of magical lobsters begin attacking them. They try their best to fight and Elisa realises that it’s all an illusion. Elisa informs Zipzap who informs the rest and soon the party begins to snap out of one by one, realising that they had been attacking each other. Shrugging that off, the party enters the cave. Tiny realises that the magics surrounding the cave are Fey in nature, prompting an impromptu tourist guide to Fey from Nyktos supplemented by Lucatiel who’s able to give more information on the matter. The party explores the cave, with Tiny noticing and pointing out the illusory floors. They become more wary about traps. Lucatiel teleports over the gap, triggering the fey magics in the space as they turn into a potted plant. The party realises the dangers of using magics here, similar to when they first found Holly. They carry on, trying their best to not use magic and to remember the fey rules. They manage to avoid the other traps for the most part, except for Tiny who ends up doused with feathers. The party eventually ends up in the final room, where a Hydra rests alongside a single ring. Expecting the Hydra to be the beast that the King had told them about, combat ensues. During combat, Nyktos picks up the ring, putting it on though nothing happens. Meanwhile Lucatiel casts a spell, ready to use the bead in her hand when the bead instead awakens, screaming in abject horror before dying as it gets used. This frightens Lucatiel, who has never had that happen before. This similarly happens to Nyktos and her new ring, though less terrifying than what Lucatiel went through. The party makes quick work of the Hydra, with Lucatiel dealing the final blow. Nyktos proceeds to identify the ring, becoming very intrigued with it when she learns that the ring would grant great power to its wearer everyday. She’s tempted to take it, though Elisa and Lucatiel remind her of the dangers of taking from a fey. She eventually gives up on the idea when Tiny encourages her to do as she wishes. The party suddenly feels a wave of magic wash over them, power, as they have been granted with the Gift of Immortality. Sensing something wrong, Elisa immediately begins pleading with the fey entity who bestowed them this gift, attempting to politely reject the gift. Soon enough a fey being shows up, carrying himself like a noble would as he explains the situation to them. In killing the Hydra, the party had taken over the Hydra’s contract with him, making him their employer and are now to guard his treasure forever. The party tries to bargain otherwise, and eventually land on a new deal; the party is to bring back their employer’s pet Sandy, a little dog, floppy like being with blue eyes who loves to eat dire boar meat. Elisa requests for an item of Sandy’s so that they may track her easier and receives a large collar. The party are surprised but left with not much choice as they ponder about what Sandy could be.
