# Thullen's Death

The party finds themselves high in the clouds, causing Grimrid to begin shouting and fainting from his debilitating fear of heights. The party witnesses the last exchange between Thullen, Countess Sansuri's brother, and Zephyros. Exposing that Zephyros intentionally left out that he used to be involved with the Giant Court. Contrary to his initial introduction to the party, with him telling them that he was more of a 3rd Party, who did not subscribe to the beliefs of the Ordning and that of the Giants.

Zephyros seems reluctant to act, lamenting that Thullen should not have acted out of turn, that he had given the Storm King the chance to do "this". But Thullen stands by his words, insisting that his defence of his sister against King Hekaton's insult in the royal court in front of all the courtiers; insinuiating that his Sister was a Harlot and that his Best Friend, Zephyros is not worthy of his position and questioning the intimate relationship both Zephyros and Sansuri had because of Zephyros' low-born status. Zephyros commands the party to attack, with them taking the place of the summons he used all those years ago to kill Thullen. Ivy lands the killing blow, destroying Thullen's head as his body falls down from the sky, with Zephyros turning away and wincing, unable to watch his friend fall and die. 

# The Thousand Year War

## Wulfgrim

The vision then changes again and the party find themselves in a camp of sorts, where a short distance away is an Adult Red Dragon, the Dragon is being tended to by multiple Kobolds, with treasures and jewelry being set on him, however the "jewelry" being given to him look primitive, being small bone charms and unrefined gems. 

A tall man approaches the party as he speaks a language the party does not understand, overtime, thanks to the Giant's Magic that brought them here in this vision enables them to slowly begin to understand his words, the realisation comes that he is speaking some kind of incredibly old form of Common. The man introduced himself as Wulfgrim, and Chief of the Uthgardt. He comments that Ivy appears to be a servant of the Gods, and inquires what tribe they are from. The party members give a sort of Non-Answer, and the boastful Chief begins to tell a story about himself in an effort to impress the Party Members.

He reveals his name was given to him when he slew a Great Wolf, and wears its pelt around his body that he claims grants him invulnerability. Grimrid, fits in surprisingly well, and makes quick friends with Wulfgrim when invited for drinks. From context clues the party is able to pick up that they have somehow joined in a warband that they would soon be led by the Adult Red Dragon to fight some Giants, revealing that they are most likely in the past, a time during the Thousand Year War between the Giants and Dragons, before the time of the God-Emperor Godwin. 

## Drinking Games

As Wulfgrim leads the party to where the rest are drinking, a woman approaches the party. She stares at Roast looking unhappy, and puts a drink in the Roast's hand demanding that they drink. Having never tried alcohol before, Roast takes a sip and learns that she does not like the taste, but falls prey to peer pressure and drinks regardless. The woman's name is revealed to be Scara, who then suggests a game of drunk punch handing Roast another drink. As Roast drinks she prepares herself to be punched in the gut, which is exactly what Scara does. 

Roast manages to hold herself and when Scara takes her next drink, Roast continues the game of drunk punch and wins. Soon Roast and Scara become friends as well. One of the tribe members approaches Ivy, inviting her to dance; she declines, confused by the revelry humans are partaking in. Roast is also brought in to dance by Scara, as the two enjoy a merry time of messy dancing. Lu is also approached by another to dance, he accepts and finds himself dancing with a very drunk individual, but who in the midst of all the twists and turns proceeds to hurl over Lu. 

Lu looks for his party members, finding that none of them possess the right spell to clean his soiled clothing as he wanders out in search for another solution. He eventually relents, using his alchemy jug to conjure water to clean himself as best he is able to. He returns to find Grimrid holding a barrel of alcohol, about ready to chug it as the crowd cheers him on. Grimrid chugs about half of the barrel before becoming incredibly drunk and passes out. 

## Dragon Shenanigans

Meanwhile, Elisa has avoided the party, instead sneaking off to where the Dragon is as she disguises herself as a red kobold. She lies very well to the kobolds, and gets saddled with the arduous task of putting a chain on the Dragon. It does not go well as Elisa gets thrown off. Elisa does manage to learn some information, even making friends with a kobold named Dirt who very kindly shared a cloth with Elisa, allowing her to avoid the chain tasks. Elisa detects the Dragon's thoughts, sensing its Rage and Desire to Dominate.

## Continued Drinking

Back with the rest, Grimrid gets shaken awake by Lu and Ivy, immediately asking for ale once more. Which Roast obliges, handing another tankard to Grimrid which Ivy tries to intervene but fails, instead picking up Grimrid, shaking him so he spits out the alcohol. Sensing that she might be pinned for some trouble, Roast sneaks away whilst they're busy with Grimrid as she finds a nice place to have a nap. The shaking doesn't cause Grimrid to spit out the alcohol but rather to puke as Lu finds his shoes covered in the stuff. Having settled Grimrid, Lu and Ivy notice that Roast is missing. They try to find her but ultimately do not. 

After a short rest, the party regroups. Grimrid awakens from his drunken slumber, believing that he drank the entire barrel as he cures himself of the poison and finds Wulfgrim to do the same. Before the battle, the PCs are able to learn the lore of the world at the time of the Thousand Year War. Those who were loyal to the Dragons believed them to be Gods, and that the Giants were "First Men", created by the Dragons who betrayed their Masters, and that the small Humanoids were new men, made better by the Dragons, and the true inheritors of the Dragons' Will. Wulfgrim then picks up his weapon, ready to fight alongside Grimrid. 

# The Battle

## Frost Giants

As both of the sides arrive, the PCs see the relationship between the Giants and Dragons at the time of the Thousand Year War. In this battle, the PCs and their Red Dragon face a Frost Giant Force, with both the Red Dragon and Frost Giant speaking to each other in their respective languages and monologuing at one another despite them not understanding the other. The Red Dragon loses its temper and begins its attack, rushing at the Frost Giant Leader. During the battle, amidst the darkness the Party sees bright lights cutting through the night sky.

The battle goes well, with Grimrid calling out to the heavens for a steed Griffin but instead the call is answered by a very majestic Goat, Umerian Mott. During combat, Grimrid and Lu catch glimpses of something rectangular cutting through the sky accompanied by two red bejewelled eyes. Then just as they kill the Giants, a splash of blood drops from the sky. Then another as the Red Dragon falls down to the ground. 

## The Vonindod

Red Eyes glow in the darkness, revealing a massive construct well over 30 feet tall that reveals itself to be, the Vonindod. The same construct that they know the Fire Giants are trying to repair. The party learns this construct is Resistant to most magic, able to absorb spells and repair itself. 

One by one, the party is knocked out and anyone deemed unimportant is killed. When they come to, a big Frost Giant enters, picking them up. Then he gently nudges Wulfgrim’s arm before breaking it. Jarl Storvald looks to Ivy, telling her to pick one, to choose who else would suffer. Ivy hesitates to answer and the Jarl takes matters into his own hands, telling Wulfgrim to not scream. 

## Jarl Storvald

Grimrid approaches Wulfgrim, healing his wounds, ready to fight Storvald as he mentions seeing Wulfgrim in Valhalla. Wulfgrim holds Grimrid back, commenting that today he has lost everyone and can not lose another as he volunteers himself for Storvald’s games. Roast steps forward, not liking the situation but also respecting Wulfgrim’s choice. She expresses her regret in not saving Scara earlier, had she known what would have happened, she would have brought Scara away. She offers Wulfgrim some help, casting a spell on him to limit his screams, hoping that at least then he might just survive the twisted game. Storvald picks Wulfgrim up, bringing him a distance away yet ensuring that the party had a clear view of what was happening as he breaks Wulfgrim’s other arm, then his ribs, and cracks his hands. 

## Illmirth

Near where the party is, a blue snout emerges from the ground as the individual tells the party that he could help. Elisa speaks with the individual, learning of their plan to use a teleportation spell and agreeing to it. Just as they jump out of the soil, Storvald notices, controlling the Vonindod to react as Elisa fights back, and the party escapes. 

# Iymrith & Her Family

They wake up days later in a cave where a larger individual approaches them. Roast turns her blade to this stranger who speaks only Draconic, though before more words can be exchanged, similar blue snouts appear. Turns out the party was saved by a trio of blue dragon younglings, Loltral, Ilmirth, and Korax who brought the party to their mother Iymrith. Iymrith explains to the party that the War has been ongoing for 600 years, confirming to the party that they are in fact in the past, in the past 600 year since the start of the Thousand Year War. 

She lets the party rest, and the next day, Lu senses that everyone could use a pick me up and conjures a door that leads into a tea house. The party is led into an establishment that serves only rice cakes and the like. During their stay, the following occurs. Elisa enjoys the bath on the first day and overhears Roast muttering to herself, confused about the hot “acidic Ewaters. Elisa's time is interrupted as she walks Roast through the steps of enjoying a bath before finally having a moment of peace. 

Ivy spends a fair bit of her time carving out little wooden figures for each of the kids and Iymrith, she is visited by Ilmirth who finds it interesting and tries his hand at it, only to make an even better figure before running off with Ivy’s tools. Ivy has to chase the child down, using her vines to stop him before picking him up, still wrapped in vines as he is allowed to watch her whittle. Grimrid imbibes in a lot of rice wine. Iymrith notices the painting of Min on the walls and attempts to strike up a conversation with Lu about it despite neither of them sharing a common language. Lu tries his best to explain that Min is his son, comparing him to Korax but Iymrith does not pick up on the meaning, instead misunderstanding that Lu wants to be her child. 

Roast emerges from her bath and despite understanding both perfectly well, decides to play a prank on Lu by confirming Iymrith’s interpretation. As well as Elisa who also notices the strange atmosphere, and through telepathy, adds on to the story about Lu’s loneliness. Iymrith then pulls Lu in for a hug, much like she does with her actual children. Lu also gives some paper to Loltral, seeing as he is the studious one. As a gift, Loltral then provides everyone with the blueprints for his sled, with there being a line that “everyone can do this Edespite the first step requiring some kind of lightning breath. Loltral spends his days trying to figure out how to bring the paper out of the magnificent mansion. 

He tests many hypotheses yet fails to do so. Roast helps out attempting teleportation and all but the papers disappear once they leave the mansion. Grimrid asks Lu for some paper, which he takes and shows to Loltral before confidently bringing this real paper out of the mansion, confusing the kid but also giving him the paper in exchange for the prank he pulled. Korax also goes to find Lu, asking if he would like to join him with digging, which Lu agrees, spending some time in the dirt with Korax. Elisa and Roast help teach Iymrith common as Elisa starts off with some very important vocabulary, “butts Eand “kick your ass E Roast also joins in learning these very important phrases from Elisa but also teaches Iymrith some Common she knowth. With each day of rest, a new wooden carving of each party member appears on the table. Grimrid offers the carving of him to Iymrith, stating that she should have a carving of the best member of the party. 

# Farewell

When it is time to part, Iymrith teaches Roast a rune that she knows. The [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Runes/Gebu\|Gebu]] rune, meaning gift. It is a rune that she’s learnt from a Giant. When Roast brings up an odd question, asking for some way to prove to Iymrith that she once knew her should their paths ever cross again, Iymrith plays along with the odd question, telling Roast that the names of her sons would be proof that they once met. Now finally able to speak Common, Iymrith gets another chance to speak with Lu, who shares that the portrait is that of his late son, who was 17 when he passed. That his son died due to a tactical mistake he had made. Having all said their goodbyes, the party leaves.

# The Storm King

They then see a scene playing out in the throne room when a much younger King Hekaton was doubting his capabilities to be King, consoled by his wife who constantly reminds him that he is great and that he is the one who answers the Allfather’s call for strength. Yet another change of scenery as the party sees the progression of Hekaton’s cruelty.  How he has stormed into the Hill Giant’s home, showed them their place as he steps on Chieftess Guh, demanding that the Hill Giant’s feat now be shared with all as Queen Neri encourages him, telling him that he alone is worthy to decide when lighting strikes. 

Then with the Cloud Giants whose leader suggested a more gentle and diplomatic approach, Hekaton had instead blatantly suggested the Countess of being a harlot who warms the beds of others, earning him the ire of the Countess Ebrother Thullen who is in turn punished for his words against the King. With the Fire Giants, Hekaton killed their Khan’s wife, attempting to force the Khan into submission as Hekaton’s storm giant bodyguard castrated the Khan’s son, Zaltember and implying that further harm would be done to his daughter, Cinderhild if Khan Zalto did not comply. Then with the Stone King, information about him seems to have been lost to time. The party can only see as a blurry figure speaks to Hekaton, no sound coming from his mouth, and Hekaton speaking on the Stone King's weakness being his Baigujing. Hekaton vows to destroy all knowledge of this person, condemning him to live forever to suffer his insolence, that this is the price he pays for attempting to break the heavens. In response to this all, the Stone King simply spits on Hekaton’s feet, laughing at his attempt. 

# Iymrith's Sons' Deaths

The sight then changes and the party hears a loud scream, surrounded by a metallic scent. They find themselves standing on an island in the middle of the ocean, in front of them are three beheaded Young Blue Dragons, their heads strapped to a Young King Hekaton's hip who is accompanied by the Female Storm Giant Bodyguard who has appeared at every vision of Tyranny that the party has seen. 

He is just about to finish off an Adult Blue Dragon, a Dragon that the party recognizes as Iymrith, she is horrifically injured, missing large chunks of her limbs and her wings, yet she looks on at Hekaton with hatred as he proclaims that she shall join her children in death. It's here the party realise that they're watching the moment of Iymrith's Sons' deaths, the moment that changed her entire life, the moment that maimed her in complete permanence, at least until Elisa and her party regenerated Iymrith's limbs.
