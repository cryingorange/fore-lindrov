## Aberrations Continued
### Zil’trisst The Behir
Finishing up the giant, the party gathers what they can from the Brightmires, finding gems that contain the powers to alter spells. They move on, weary of the Behir they are about to face. The party enters into a tall cave, spires leading higher than the eye can see. Cautious, Elisa has Milton scout ahead and he reports back that the Behir is asleep. The party figures that it would be good to sneak up to the Behir and do so. But as they approach, the Behir seems emaciated, likely weak from the lack of food in the Underdark. Elisa recognises the Behir to be the same one that accompanied Neheedra in Blingdenstone. Instead of attacking, Elisa wakes the Behir up and as it stirs it seems sad, defeated, lost. To their surprise, the Behir speaks in Common, seemingly more sentient than when Elisa last met it. 

The Behir, Zil'trisst, tells the party to kill him, bemoaning that he had nothing else to live for, not since his friend Neheedra sacrificed herself to save him after revealing that Blindenstone had been overtaken by some kind of “Dark Creature E that Neheedra’s sanity returned to her just in time to save him, he reveals that he had accompanied Neheedra since he was but a small hatchling, that he was there the day she turned into a Drider and lost her sanity, and was there for when it returned upon the “Dark Creature”’s takeover of Blingdenstone. The party doesn’t. Instead, they try to offer him an alternative. They tell him of a place willing to accept him. Lu continues, telling Zil’trisst the truth, that they had been sent to capture him, however Lu tells Zil’trisst that he has a choice. The party suggests that Zil’trisst live on with the memory of Neheedra as no one else would know of her tales and accomplishments but him, that she only truly dies if he gives up. Zil’trisst asks what he is to do, if he is to cling on to the memory of his dead friend. The party suggests different things to help. 

Nyktos suggests that Zil’trisst could help the Underdark, bring back the glory to the place that was once his dear friend’s home. Others suggest vengeance, to have his revenge on those that stole his friend from him. Nyktos also shares of the Khan’s son, how the boy had his confidence stolen from him, how powerful people had broken the child and suggests that the two could find solace in one another. That healing would take time but it would be better with a friend. Overall, Zil’trisst seems agreeable but just as he is about to speak, he freezes. 
### The Mind Flayers
And as the party turns around they find themselves ambushed by mind flayers. The aberrations are led by a Ulitharid that Elisa recognises is Lux, by his side are two Mind Flayers with wearing pendants that bear the symbol of Solaris, they connect with Elisa’s mind, revealing their identities to her.. Her Parents. They all ask for her to return home as Elisa apologises for the state that her parents are in. Still they pay no mind to their new forms, only focused on convincing Elisa to return home to Solaris where they can be a family again. Elisa’s mother tells her that everything is alright, that everyone back home have had their sentience returned to them, that they once again pray to the One Of The Stars, however Elisa rejects their offer, saying that they cannot be a family, not like this. 

Elisa offers for her parents to follow her, come home with her where she can try to fix them. Instead, her parents urge her to take Lux’s hand as Lux offers to help, saying that they will try to find a cure together. Elisa accepts his hand and Lux tells her that whatever they do, it is for all of them as he does something. Elisa finds herself in a cold and dank space with floating tables and before her is a Giant Brain. The Brain turns to her and demands for Elisa to obey. 

To the rest of the party, Elisa and the Illithids have been having a telepathic conversation as Elisa gets hugged by the two Mind Flayers, then she takes the Ulitharid’s hand and becomes calm, too calm. At this, the party strikes. The Mind Flayers and Thralls easily throw the party off the platform, isolating Elisa from them. Being 200 ft down, the party readies themselves for a difficult fight as Wystram downs a potion of speed, hastening himself and Nyktos drinks a potion of giant size, growing to the size and strength of a Giant. Wystram manages to scale the 200 foot cliff and grabs hold of Elisa, bringing her down to the ground and forcing the Aberrations down. As this happens, Elisa finds herself still at odds against the Elder Brain, resisting its will, refusing to back down nor obey as it chips away at her spirit.

Lux casts a spell, trapping himself and Elisa in a wall of force, preventing the party from saving her. Or so he thinks as Nyktos teleports into the bubble, pointing her heart staff at him as she demands that he let her friend go, casting a power word kill on the Ulitharid. In retaliation, the Ulitharid casts a spell on Nyktos, removing her ability to cast magic via Befuddlement. Then, Lu dimension doors in with Wystram, allowing Wystram to knock Lux unconscious. With Lux out of battle, the Mind Flayers unleash their power, not holding back anymore. The party is soon all at death’s door, as Wystram is before Elisa’s father, his tendrils reaching for Wystram’s brain. 

But before he is able to, a bark sounds out as Crimson uses his sanctum amulet to save Wystram before dragging him away from the Mind Flayer. Lu is also not in a good state, exhausting himself to summon an undead creature to protect Elisa and ZipZap from the Thralls who are trying to drag them away. As Wystram returns to heal Lu, Lu is able to cast greater restoration on Nyktos, clearing her mind of Lux’s magic as she is able to cast spells once more. The party manages to not die, and somehow turn the tides in their favour once more. Elisa’s parents look at her as their minds connect, they do something, leaving a mark on Elisa’s mind before they vanish, failing a Wisdom Saving Throw before they leave.

### Ulitharid’s & Zil’trisst’s Fates

With that, the party is safe. For now. Even if it doesn’t quite feel that way yet. They return to Zil’trisst with Lux still unconscious. Zil’trisst shares that he knew of their arrival, of their ambush, but he was magically compelled to not speak a word of it. He thanks the party for saving his life, and agrees to head to the place they speak of. He also expresses that the Ulitharid should be killed but relents when he learns that Lux is a part of Elisa’s family, understanding the pain of someone who has lost those dear to him. Lu also agrees that Lux should be killed but suggests imprisoning the Ulitharid as an alternative. 

Elisa doesn’t go for this, her imprisonment spell saved for someone much more deserving, not a tool to be used against someone she wants to save. Trying to make matters light, Lu suggests drawing a butt on Lux’s face but Elisa declines, not wanting to disrespect one of the great leaders of her people. Lu, though slightly disappointed, agrees with Elisa, saying that they wouldn’t want everyone calling him ButtLux when her people are all fixed, speaking as if fixing her people is a matter of when not if. Elisa decides to let Lux go, and softly whispers to him that she’s sorry but hopes to have a cure for them the next time they meet. 
## Return to Zalto Khan 
The party returns to the Fire Giant’s camp with the Behir in tow. The Khan is impressed to learn that Zil’trisst speaks and offers him the chance to join their camp, citing that a creature that can speak should be allowed to make a choice, that he is not one to ensnare and enslave sentient creatures against their will. He is honest, sharing that he had initially intended for Zil’trisst to be a pet for his son, but seeing as the Behir is sentient, Zalto treats him with respect, allowing Zil’trisst to stay and see the good of the army before making his decision. As for the party, Zalto Khan pays them their due and lets the Duergar go free for now. The party speaks to Zil’trisst before leaving, learning of the Behir desire for revenge after the party reveals that they may find themselves in Blingdenstone soon enough, with Zil’trisst asking the party to come find him on the day that they choose to storm Blingdenstone. 
## Duergar
The party shares the good news and with their success are given the location they inquired about. They also learn that someone had already broken into the vault. The party surmises that the Neronvain had somehow broken in and acquired the same information. The party rests for the night in the city and the next morning, Wystram pulls out the barrel from his bag of holding. He stands 30 ft back, throwing the crowbar into it and only when it doesn’t cause an explosion, he approaches and manages to open the barrel. Inside is a black metal canister housing a brain. Elisa connects to the brain, realising that it is an intellect devourer as it tells her its plans to find a new body to inhabit. The Intellect Devourer has no name, but informs Elisa that there are more of its kind. It says that the Mind Flayers saw the truth, that all will be consumed. It asks Elisa where it can get a new host, believing her to be a Mind Flayer, but the party just leaves the Intellect Devourer in the container for now as they continue on their journey.
## The Flumph & Child
On the second day of their travel, the party hears the cries of a child. Slightly paranoid from before, they listen to see if it is an illusion before rushing towards it when they realise it is real. Approaching the party sees a tentacle, jellyfish looking thing that floats, reaching towards an overturned cart where the child’s cries originate. Nyktos rushes forward, recognising the creature as a Flumph, a small, peaceful aberration. She informs the party of the information as they approach. The Flumph attempts to scare the party away, wanting to protect the child. The party manages to convince the Flumph as Wystram lifts the cart and Nyktos tends to the child. The party learns that the Flumph’s name is FlimFlam and all take to the Flumph quickly. As they speak with the child, a young tiefling with orange eyes, they learn that his name is Amnon. His parents are merchants, the cart belonging to them but something happened to the Brightmires that were with them. Then some elves showed up and a big fight happened. 
### Aboleth Cultists
Before the party can properly assure the child, a group of aberrations show up demanding for the child. The leader of the group, Krikluxin speaks of their masters of old. Piecing together what they mentioned, Nyktos surmises that these are cultists of an Aboleth, warning her party through their telepathic bond of this fact. But as the cultist continues to demand for the child, even suggesting that they would pay for him, the party puts their foot down and battles ensue. Wystram leads Amnon and FlimFlam to safety, spending his time watching over and comforting the child. Meanwhile the aberrations come under Nyktos Ecommand as she bends their blood to her will. The lead cultist speaks, claiming that they would give her to their master, have her flesh flayed. Not taking kindly to such threats, Nyktos kills the main leader with a single word. The party easily handles the rest of the cultists. Nyktos picks up the bodies, collecting them for both research and sacrificial purposes. 
### Amnon’s Parents
With things settled, Elisa casts scrying to reveal the location of the child’s parents. What she sees is a drow’s camp. His parents were taken by drow, and now the party will have to rush in to save them whilst somehow not getting themselves enslaved. FlimFlam joins in, able to hear their telepathic communication as it offers to help. Nyktos tries to persuade FlimFlam otherwise but it doesn’t quite work. Playing into FlimFlam’s desire to help, Elisa and Lu tell it that it has to stay in the area to look for any other missing children and help them instead. This convinces FlimFlam as he agrees to send any missing children to the party. Nyktos asks FlimFlam for its favourite colour, taking out the bundle of purple cloth she took from the Golden’s house, cutting out a purple cape for FlimFlam as a parting gift. FlimFlam then bids the party farewell, and as it leaves, they hear FlimFlam’s internal monologue, a journal entry on its adventure and the new friends it met. As for Amnon, the party has no choice but to take the child with them. They know they’ll have to protect him whilst surviving the drow’s camp. Not an easy task, but a doable one. They set out, in hopes of reuniting the family.
