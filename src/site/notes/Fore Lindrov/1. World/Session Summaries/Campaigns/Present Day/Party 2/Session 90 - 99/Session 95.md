The party disguises themselves as gnolls with a casting of seeming whilst having ZipZap and the elk be invisible.

Ivy vows to protect the elks even if it costs her life, willing to not let things control her when her vision flashes and she hears chains rumbling around black fur, and sees a gaping maw of dripping saliva, some sort of creature with no eyes and patterns of red. As black ichor leaks out of her briarheart.

Elisa notices the elk calf's apprehension towards Ivy, checking in with it to learn that the elk claims that Ivy smells like the demons do. But Elisa assures the Elk that Ivy will not hurt them, as she has stated before.

The party witnesses the birth of a new Flind, born when a female hill giant force fed a male hill giant, emerging from the remains of it. They move on, hoping to blend past the crowd when the Flind notices them and has them following him. Before the Flind succeeds, the party manages to establish their gnoll dynamics. With Roast as the Gnoll Pack Lord, Grimrid as the odd one out, Morvek as the mutt, and Elisa as the prankster and Ivy as the most normal one. The party passes a pile of meat, knowing that they would have to play their part. As they follow the Flind across a bridge over a pool of ichor, the party sees two adult elks, tied up and transported, presumably to be food. 

Ivy doesn't want to allow that to happen as the party pretends to pick a fight with the bugbear transporting the elks, learning in the process that the ichor can empower demons, granting them new features such as wings. Taking a gamble, the party fights the hobgoblin and also ambushes the Flind, followed by a dramatic performance where Roast pretends to have been "evolved" into a Flind, taking the flail and hitting the ground. This triggers Ivy, who can only think of the numerous animals that had been treated the same way as the Elks were, who had been scared and frightened, who were hurt and killed.

The party saves the two elks and brings them to where they were headed, entering to hear the screams of some goblins as they rush in to help. The party finds some goblins almost being roasted as they jump in to save them, leaving one petrified, two knocked out and one dead.
