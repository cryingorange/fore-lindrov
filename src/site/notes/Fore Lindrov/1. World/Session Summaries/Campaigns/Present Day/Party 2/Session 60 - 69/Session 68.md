Winning the conflict against Vhagar, Tiny reaches out their remaining hand to Chieftess Guh, offering the Hill Giants a fresh start. A life free from the shackles of the Ordning, one where Hill Giants can rule themselves. Tiny convinces Guh to resume the farming she once loved as a way to bring peace to the world. With her acceptance, Tiny bestows the title of “Guh the Restorer Eto the Hill Giant Chieftess. 

  

As talks with Guh come to a close and she returns to her matters, Guh uses the bag of bounty brought by the party and enjoys a meal after the difficult battle. Whilst she’s distracted, Elisa takes the Conch of Teleportation, storing it for later use. Then, Thane Kayalithica enters, dragging along with her the body of Loog. The Thane is surprised to see that Chieftess Guh still lives but Tiny affirms that there will be no killing.  
  
Instead, Tiny assures Thane Kayalithica that the next time they meet, they would find the King and tell him that all Giants are now free, no longer bound by the caste system. Kayalithica is impressed by Tiny’s proclamations, praising their bravery by saying that they are small for a Giant. She then bids them farewell, calling Tiny a “Little King Ebefore she leaves. 

  

The party heads back down, meeting up with Yuk and the Kobolds. Yuk expresses their desire to leave, seeing as all others they knew are dead. But the party is apprehensive of leaving the Goblin to roam on his own. As a precautionary measure, Elisa gives Yuk a spell gem of summon fiend to be used in the most dire of situations. For Yuk’s general protection, Nyktos gives him a +2 shortsword. Now extremely well equipped, Yuk leaves, hopefully to be seen again someday. 

  

With that, Tiny settles their loyal group of Kobold “friends E As they wonder where to lead the Kobolds to, they feel a sinking feeling, a presence that offers help. Tiny accepts their 

mother’s hand and begins casting Infernal Calling. The spell calls for the use of blood, which causes a momentary pause before Vhagar reminds Tiny that the one who’s always helped them is asking something of them, that they should respond as she wishes. Nyktos notices the reverence Vhagar has when speaking of this person, the same that Tiny has when they mention Reggie.  
  
Tiny reaches out to the Kobolds, pricking each and every one of them, giving her the blood she demands. With that, a green snake-like hand reaches out from the earth, as the scaly individual the party met in White Crest surfaces. Rezran, the vinegary smelling individual greets Vhagar and Tiny, happy to serve another of his Queen’s children. Meanwhile, the Kobolds fall to their knees as a mark of a five-headed dragon sears on their neck. Rezran gathers the Kobolds, and Tiny chooses one of them, renaming them as “Buff E  
  
Buff has the special honour of following and directly serving his master whilst the others are tasked to help indirectly, following Rezran’s orders and staying safe for the time being. With matters out of the way, the party rests in Chieftess Guh’s camp for some days. 

  

The first night, Wystram heads over to Tiny, greeting the dragon and complimenting them on their combat prowess. Curious, Wystram asks Tiny what caused them to be so sure of their convictions. Which Tiny responds with how Reggie had raised them, though Vhagar butts in and insists that they should have killed everything instead, going on about domination and what nots. As Vhagar finishes their monologue, Tiny continues, explaining how their father, Reggie the Dwarf had raised them to help others, teaching them the duty of the strong.  
  
Finding a commonality, Wystram shares that he too was raised by dwarves and asks if Vhagar had been raised by Reggie as well. That is when Wystram learns of Vhagar and Tiny’s age difference but decides to leave that topic alone for now. He instead turns the conversation towards their mutual upbringing and shares of the dwarven ale he enjoyed, suggesting that the two should share a drink.  
  
Tiny agrees whilst Vhagar declines, citing a lack of interest in Dwarven ale. Though Wystram eggs Vhagar on, teasing that Vhagar shouldn't let Tiny stop him, and further coaxes by suggesting that Dragons should enjoy the finer things in life. Vhagar huffs but ultimately agrees. During the long rest, Nyktos pulls out the heart of Margos and analyses it, taking detailed notes and writing up the first papers on Oni, a type of Ogres of which Margos was. 

  

The next morning, the party hears heavy footsteps make their way towards them. And in the entrance stands Loog, huffing as he pouts and huffs at the party, upset with them for the lies they had told. The party tries different ways to pacify the Hill Giant. Tiny attempts a more light hearted approach, utilising humour to diffuse the situation.  
  
As that doesn’t get through to Loog, Nyktos offers an apology to the Hill Giant, choosing a more honest route. The party did after all lie to their friend and such a trust broken would not be easy to get back. Though the bigger betrayal is the party convincing the Chieftess to not eat as much as she desires, causing Loog to soon lose the part of Guh he loves the most. Tiny tries to teach Loog a bigger life lesson, urging him to see beyond the surface, that though Chieftess Guh would gain a heart bigger than before.  
  
Loog does not care, making it rather clear that his initial attractions to Guh were mostly for her appearance. Nyktos attempts another route, convincing Loog that farm work would have the Chieftess growing muscles that would only enhance the aspects of Guh that Loog loves. As the rest join in, Loog believes them, eventually willing to forgive the party and returns to his duties as best husband.  
  
The party has a conversation with Hruk as well, telling him of Moog who had sent them to find him, assuring him that she would take him back. Nyktos tries to have Crimson appeal to Hruk’s love of animals but Crimson’s not the best at placating anyone but his best friend. Meanwhile, Elisa summons out a mastiff and has it follow Hruk instead. Hruk is happy to have his own animal companion, vowing to care for the pet as Elisa conveniently forgets to inform Hruk that the mastiff would disappear when the time runs out. 

  

Nyktos also takes the moment of rest they have to contact Houzuki, informing her sister of the dangers outside and that they’re all safe. Houzuki shares the situation at home, everyone is safe but with the expansions, their home runs the risk of being exposed. Houzuki also informs Nyktos of the situation at Lupra, last she knows of, they are under attack by Giants. Nyktos doesn’t share the information with the party, knowing that they are too far to do anything and trusts the other knights of the Leprae Corpus would keep the Kingdom safe. 

  

The party rests up, settling whatever errands they need to before the day of their travel arrives. ZipZap attunes to the conch and with a blow, the magic activates, teleporting the party to the Maelstrom. Upon arrival, they find themselves surrounded by the large hulking figures of the royal family. But statues as compared to the real deal. 

  

King Hekaton’s statue bears a familiar hammer, one wielded by a Half Orc the party knows as Go’l. Next to his statue is that of Queen Neri, her eyes gentle but wrathful. Then Serissa’s similarly gentle eyes to her mother but noticeable lack of malice. Her expression is youthful and atop her lovely hair sits a crown of blades.  
  
Last but not least, are the statues of Serissa’s sisters, Mirran and Nym. Mirran’s statue mirrors her wild and unpredictable nature, a look of defiance in her eyes with a cloak of swirling clouds around her. Nym’s statue has her piercing gaze, cold, calculating, constantly plotting. Yet despite that they hold a bitterness, a scorn, the will to do anything necessary. 

  

But apart from the statues, there are no other figures about the lower levels of the Maelstrom. Zephyros floats out, warning the party to be careful and that they must not kill anyone or take anything within the Maelstrom. The party splits up, each looking in a different direction for some clues. Elisa notes the glowing crystals, rushing over to them as she attempts to acquire some of the stone for herself. Though her first attempt doesn’t work and she tries once more, this time using an eldritch blast but it doesn't even chip the crystal. She proceeds on to the other rooms, exploring what seems to be servants quarters. In three she finds a couple noteworthy items; a golden whisker, a scale, an emerald tipped pen, and half a note that says “Giants are great E  
  
Meanwhile, Wystram heads to the bubbling pool of water. Taking a look, Wystram spots something in the bottom of the pool but also notes the heat as he refrains from entering, walking over to the one who could enter a hot pool without hurting themselves. Though before he can converse with Nyktos and Crimson, he sees Nyktos dive into the other much larger pool. Tiny heads over to the closed door, pushing it open to find the storage areas.  
  

They find a gigantic barrel of wine and take some in their waterskin. They also find a sea anemone and notices something within its grasp. When they reach out for it, the anemone stings them, hurting them lightly in the process. Unable to retrieve the item, Tiny goes to Wystram for help. Wystram asks for help first and has Tiny watch as they dive into the boiling pool, taking the damage and resurfacing with a pyrite statue of a Plesiosaurus. The two head back to the sea anemone and with no other solution, Tiny decides to attack the sea anemone, knocking it out first.  
  

But then they realise that knocking the sea anemone out causes it to close in on itself and then they decide to deal the killing blow. The dead sea anemone collapses to reveal a pearl the size of a grapefruit. Taking their findings, Tiny then realises what their greed had led to. Tiny asks Wystram if they should inform the others of what they had done and Wystram suggests not informing anyone, keeping it a secret between the two. Meanwhile, Nyktos dives down the pool, following the path as she finds the vortex that Bubbles had told them about.  
  
She cautiously threads the path but with her allies far above, she stops herself before returning back to where Crimson is waiting. Continuing on, Nyktos regroups with Elisa who has been trying to make sense of the various items she’s found. Elisa shows off her new pen to Nyktos who recognises it as an emerald pen, a magic item used to write hidden messages. Elisa then uses her true sight to reveal the message that hints at some people’s plans to bring Serissa away.  
  

Putting two and two together, they suspect that Serissa has been kidnapped. Nyktos analyses the scale and whiskers whilst Elisa attempts to scry on them and Serissa, but they both come up empty. They also investigate the remaining rooms but do not find any more clues of Serissa’s possible kidnapping. Then, Wystram and Tiny return. Before Nyktos and Elisa can share their findings, Tiny’s guilt becomes too much to bear as they confess to the murder that occurred.  
  
The two are shocked and immediately question why they would do such a thing despite Zephyros Ewarnings. Tiny shamefully reveals the giant pearl they had acquired and tries to make an argument to keep it but with some friendly reminders of the potential consequences, such as execution, Tiny eventually heads back to the dead sea anemone, returning the pearl before Vhagar can convince them otherwise.  
  

Wystram also takes the time to show off the new statue he found, emphasising that it was not stolen. The party gathers once more and Elisa shares her findings as well as their theory on Serissa’s kidnapping. With the area explored, the party has no choice but to head up the stairs where they hear a melodic voice that is familiar to some. Nyktos recognises it to be the voice of Aslaug, Serissa’s attendant. 

  

Climbing up the tall stairs, the party find themselves intruding on a business proposal between Aslaug and the Cloud Giants. It does not seem to be going well as the Cloud Giants question why they would accept such a proposal, especially when Countess Sansuri has no obligations to the royal family. Aslaug attempts to make an appeal, playing into the shared history of the Cloud and Storm Giants, the undeniable connection between the two as descendants of Nicias and Vilmos. Aslaug affirms her belief in Serissa, that she is the rightful princess, though the Cloud Giants do not seem to agree. Conversations are cut short as the Giants notice the party’s presence.  
  
Aslaug is furious, her carefully crafted business meeting so rudely disrupted by some mere smallfolk who dare enter the Maelstrom. Enraged by these backstabbing smallfolk, Aslaug attacks and battle commences. The party do a good job at standing their ground, mostly allowing their martials to handle the work as the spell casters comfortably shelter themselves from danger.  
  

During combat, Aslaug narrates tales of smallfolk and their tendency for betrayal as she attacks them. ZipZap and Elisa quickly become prime targets, both dealing significant damage and annoyance to the enemies. Elisa also successfully negotiates for 50 gold from Wystram, who heavily relies on ZipZap’s ability to knock his enemies prone, allowing the monk to strike at their vulnerable enemies. But Elisa soon finds herself stuck in a bubble. Unable to contribute to the fight, she instead begins to cook some ham, enjoying the view from the safe place Aslaug had unintentionally provided. As the party beats Aslaug and her companions, the Cloud Giants teleport away, no longer interested in the battle. 

  

The party then surprises Aslaug by not killing her. Instead, they share news of Serissa’s possible kidnapping and Wystram appeals for Aslaug to watch their actions and make a decision for herself as opposed to the stories she’s heard. Nyktos offers to send word of Serissa once they find anything, understanding that though Aslaug cares not for smallfolk, her sincerity to Serissa seems to be true. And for the first time, Aslaug offers some help to the party, informing them that she will open the door to the throne room for them but nothing else. 

  

With Aslaug’s help, the party successfully enters the throne room amidst the discussion of the Queens E The first thing they see is the Wyrmskull Throne bearing the skulls of three dragons that Elisa knows were the sons of Iymrith. The mere sight of the Throne alone is enough to have both Tiny and Vhagar frightened, the cruel visage of the throne impeding their ability to think logically. Besides the arguing Queens stands a tired Uthor, exhausted from what seems to be the neverending arguments of the two juxtaposing Queens. 

  
Mirran is the first to speak to the party, or rather at them as she openly expresses her distaste for smallfolk, musing that her uncle should kill them. But Nym steps in to hold her sister back temporarily, preferring the more methodical approach as she gently questions the party’s appearance. The party tries to speak of Serissa, but both sisters either ignore or are annoyed by the mention of their youngest sister, dismissing Serissa’s disappearance as a rebellious act or as her incapability to hold the throne. 

  

Meanwhile, Elisa secretly connects to Uthor’s mind, asking if anything has been done to find Serissa. There is a long and awkward pause before Elisa continues, explaining what she heard when she last tried to speak with the Princess. Uthor shares that the two Queens only listen to Ythimir, and even he isn’t exactly sure of why the two Queens listen to Ythimir as much as they do. When Elisa shares that Ythimir has been looking for Serissa, Uthor is surprised but believes that Ythimir is indeed looking for Serissa. 

  

The conversation the rest of the party has with the Queens doesn’t go well. The Queens Eattitudes trigger Tiny as they begin actively insulting the Queens, mocking them and very successfully angering them. This results in the guards holding the party down as the party themselves try their best to stop Tiny from signing their deaths but nothing can stop them.  
  

As Tiny rattles on, Mirran has had enough. She summons a bolt of lightning, concentrating the energy in the palm of her hand as she’s about to unleash it at the party, making them regret ever crossing her. And just as she is about to, the figure that had been watching the party through the gap in the doors steps through, grabbing a hold of Mirran’s arm and allowing the lightning to strike themselves instead. 

  

The individual is a figure of poise, her gaze carrying twin lighting strikes dressed in a fish scale dress accessorised with corals and a whaleboat in her hair. With her appearance, Nym’s cold calculative demeanour dies down, as does Mirran’s temper. In that moment, the two Queens behave almost like children who fear disappointing someone they look up to. Ythimir asks the Queens to ponder on why they wanted to kill the party. Their superficial answers disappoint Ythimir as she gives them a disapproving look. Mirran and Nym are visibly upset, not wanting to disappoint Ythimir for some reason.  
  
Uthor is trying his best to figure out exactly what Ythimir’s plan is, why she does what she does. Ythimir turns to the Queens, holding them both gently as she reminds them that a strong hand has to be tempered with a rational mind, that the laws are to be held with reason and agreement, that the two Queens have the important task of divine rulership, to solve Annam’s puzzle. But Wystram sees through Ythimir’s words, understanding that she’s actively lying to the two Queens to save the party’s life. Meanwhile Elisa reaches out to Uthor once more, and learns that whilst Ythimir speaks gently to Mirran and Nym, she barely spoke to Serissa when she was around. 

  

Ythimir takes control of the situation, the guards yielding to her as they release the party and she offers a gentle hand to them. They all follow along as she leads them to their room. Upon entering, she looks at Elisa with concern and asks about her eyes. Though as Elisa shares that her lack of eyes are a result of a deal and that she’s still able to see without eyes, Ythimir expresses an understanding of what Elisa said. She then turns to Tiny and offers them a potion that would return them their arm.  
  
Nyktos is cautious, immediately asking if she could examine the potion, which Ythimir allows. Nyktos finds nothing suspicious about the potion and ultimately allows Tiny to make the decision, informing them of what the potion would do but emphasising that she is unsure if it would have any side effects. Elisa and Nyktos are suspicious of Ythimir, not trusting her despite her obvious trust for the party. They try to probe more as Elisa questions why Ythimir saved them, but Ythimir is also suspiciously careful with her words, not answering more than she’s asked and constantly avoiding the subject of some sensitive questions.  
  

Nyktos asks Ythimir why she does what she does and Ythimir shares that she was given a second chance at life by someone who had no reason to help. When Elisa asks for Ythimir’s thoughts of the shattering, Ythimir merely comments that change can be good and reiterates that the key is Serissa. The party also mentions Storvald and they follow Ythimir to the scrying pool within the castle. There they see Storvald and his son, surrounded by bodies of the summer courts Eknights. They are all reminded of the reasons why they despite Storvald. And Tiny bitterly shares of the torture Storvald enacted on Severin. 

  

Tiny’s words of mutilation triggers Ythimir as she’s unable to control her power, causing a surge of electricity about her that implodes, hiring the party a good amount. Noticing that she’s accidently hurt them, she reels herself in, apologising for her actions as she helps them with the wounds she caused. With that, Ythimir leads the party to a guest quarters they can use for the night.  
  

Along the way, Nyktos takes the chance to ask for the general reception towards Serissa’s pregnancy, learning that people were mostly excited for the new heir but some were put off about the fact that it was one out of wedlock. They both theorise Serissa’s pregnancy as a potential reason for her kidnapping before Ythimir leaves them alone for the night. 

  

Before they rest, Elisa checks in on some of her friends, scrying on both Iymrith and Go’l. The scry on Iymrith yields nothing whilst the one on Go’l shows him sitting on a throne, looking unhappy as before him is a massive war camp with the soldiers he’s gathered all prepared for war. Elisa also communes with her goddess, receiving the following answers. Firstly, her god does not know where Iymrith is. Secondly, her god is unclear if Serissa was kidnapped for her baby. And lastly her god believes that Serissa’s disappearance is connected to the moon cracking. With possibly more questions than answers, the party rests for the night.
