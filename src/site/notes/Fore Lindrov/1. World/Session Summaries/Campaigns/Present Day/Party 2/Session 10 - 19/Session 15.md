As the party takes their first long rest, awakening the next day. They find themselves face to face with a newcomer, Vesper . After going through introductions, they also learn that Fall Damage has been going out on quests for Peebles, with Tappy directing the party to Glyphic, the young Cleric in charge of the Ruby Temple. They head over to the Ruby Temple and meet Glyphic, a Cleric left in charge of all of Blingdenstone's religious ongoings thanks to Yantha's departure to Mantol Derith. He sends the party off on a quest to acquire books in the deep reaches of Rockblight that will arm him with more knowledge on the various rituals and processes of running the Ruby Temple. During the conversation, the party also learns of a Deep Gnome named Nomi, who is apparently too busy with trying to quell the Earth Elemental corruption plaguing the City to help Glyphic.

The party head off to Rockblight via the Standing Area, with the guards revealing to them that should anything be following them back, the gates will not be opened. The party then move through Rockblight and after a swift encounter with a ghost, they meet an Earth Elemental that crazily smashes its head against the rock wall, unresponsive. The party elect to sneak past it, eventually making it into the second chamber where they're met with an abandoned Library with an entryway sealed shut by vibrating, purple crystals. As Vesper and Elisa are busy chipping away at the crystals, Brom moves on to grab the old dusty books from the shelves as Amunkhet does the same.

However the party is suddenly ambushed by 2 Medusa, who seemingly are able to instruct and command the crazed Earth Elemental from the previous chamber alongside a troop of Mud Mephits. Brom and Vesper are almost instantly petrified as Elisa , Amunkhet and Aurelion barely hold the line against the attack.

Elisa learns of the Medusa's allegiance to someone named, "Neheedra". With a smartly placed Spiked Growth, Elisa manages to prevent the Medusas from escaping to inform their mistress of the party's existence. Aurelion finds himself dropped down in the tunnel formed by the burrowing Earth Elemental and is forced to use a Potion of Healing as he Polymorphs the Elemental into a turtle. In the meantime, Elisa and Amunkhet are able to swiftly take down the Mephits and Medusa as Aurelion climbs out of the tunnel but not before Elisa is fully petrified. 

The remaining party members grab the books and dump them into the Bag of Holding, placing the petrified Brom and Vesper into the bag as well. They make it back to Blingdenstone where they meet Glyphic again, who pays them a hefty sum for the books that they acquired. The party is also directed to the Trader's Grotto to buy Diamond Dust to cast Greater Restoration. As Amunkhet is able to get a good deal of Diamond Dust, the grotto is attacked by 2 Gelatinous Cubes, during the fight an unknown mage, who turns out to be Nomi, who manages to restore Vesper to normal and Aurelion does the same to Elisa.

The party fend off the Slimes and rescue a Guard named Mev Flintknapper, who gives them a Spell Gem with Enlarge/Reduce. They also speak to Nomi, who enlists the party to help her capture a corrupted Earth Elemental, although the party chooses to put this off for a while due to the fact that Earth Elementals are mostly immune to unconsciousness and exhaustion. Brom is freed from petrification as payment for their assistance with the Earth Elemental issue. The party then meet Glyphic again, who entrusts them to help curtail the Ghosts plaguing Blingdenstone, directing them to the Gem-Encrusted passageway, the party make their way there, chipping away at it, they find themselves in gem covered hallways and chambers. Only to eventually be ambushed by multiple guards, who seem to speak in their own Crystallite language, and they are escorted and driven out of the area.

The party then go to meet with Dorbo Diggermatook, the security-in-charge of Blingdenstone, as well as his wife Senni Diggermatook, who is in charge of logistics and settling. The party learns that the city is thinking about stepping up efforts to exterminate the wererats, although Deep Gnomes like Senni believe that the wererats can be reasoned with, and at the end of the day are still Deep Gnomes. Although attempts to parley have always been met with aggression. Before the end of the meeting the party decided to help Dorbo arm the rest of the guard with weapons, with him telling them that his miners managed to find an abandoned Drow's Weapons Cache.  The party then head off, stopping for sleep. They are beset by a horrific attack by some kind of crying flesh tree, they watch in horror as its pollen rips people's flesh and bone to turn them into twisted flesh trees. During the fight the party also see the full body of the strange Beast that stalked them previously, with Brom seeing a flash of Untuk's face in that of the tree for just a moment

The party barely manage to take down the Weeping Willow, thanks to Amunkhet realising that Protection from Good and Evil prevents its cries from charming them into approaching it closer and laying their hands on it for it to give them its pollen. The party then continue their Long Rest and head to the Weapons Cache. However Vesper finds her Pirate and Noble personalities traumatised and unable to handle the horrors of Fore Lindrov. With the Seraph taking full control, being more equipped to deal with extraplanar beings. Elisa finds various strands of silver hair that lead up to the cache, with the rest of the party discovering footprints, and Aurelion realising there are 7 sets of footprints leading up. The party enters the cache just barely able to catch someone going behind the door below a large statue of Lolth. Then suddenly, the traps and defences are triggered, with a wall of spears closing in behind them, the party swiftly take down the Drider Apparitions and destroy the gemstone of Lolth. Entering into the next chamber, they find themselves under attack by Demons.

After some difficulty they finally manage to take down the 3 Demons that guarded the room, although with not enough time to loot the place as they hear the sounds of horrible screaming in the next room. Breaking down the door, the party is met with a faction called the MLA. Whose ideology is eerily familiar to Aurelion , that of freedom of all monstrous races, to no longer live under the blight of Humanity. The party attempted to speak reason to Kriegor, the werewolf leader of the small faction. Albeit to no avail, they give him the respect of mutual combat as they refuse to allow him to continue torturing their Drow Prisoner for information. As they realise that Kriegor and the Monstrous Liberation Army's hatred for humanoid races, especially that of the Drow cannot be talked away due to their horrific enslavements.

As combat begins, Elisa manages to let Amunkhet know that Xaela, the Drow Prisoner, has someone named Drexa coming for her. Aurelion , unable to handle more bloodshed, raises a dagger to Xaela halfway through combat, threatening to kill her if no one stands down. But not before Drexa, a House Captain, and his troop arrive. Amunkhet manages to get him to stand down as she runs outside, with Elisa managing to Polymorph Milton into a Giant Ape right as the battle begins. The party is split between Elisa & Vesper who wish to kill the Drow, and Amunkhet , Brom & Aurelion who wish to return Xaela to Drexa and have her life spared. With Aurelion throwing up a wall of force as the MLA begins to attack the trio trying to spare Xaela's life, with them terrified that she will bring the full might of the Drow onto the MLA and that they will be reenslaved, as the MLA decide to kill Xaela, Amunkhet calls on the dark power that assisted her during the fall of her empire for help.

A being speaks to her, asking that she sacrifice her love of her kingdom and her family to save everybody, including freeing the being in the black opal, who she thinks is her brother Geb. As black waves flush out of her body, the Black Opal cracks and the being within is released. Revealed to be Fraz'Ur'bluu, Demon Lord of Deception and Illusions. Vesper immediately recognises him, and he fulfills his deal with Amunkhet , he takes away all the conflict by gifting the MLA and Drow all of his millenia of knowledge, which they view as ancient text that scrolls across their eyes. They become fully invulnerable and catatonic, with Amunkhet quickly losing her memories, she quickly paints an image of Urshu with the words written in Elvish, "Fix This" as she remembers that Fraz'Ur'bluu revealed the same thing he did all those years ago before taking her memories. That Nicoforus The Pale is Amunkhet's father and ruler of the Ghoulish Imperium and immortal Emperor of all Ghouls.
