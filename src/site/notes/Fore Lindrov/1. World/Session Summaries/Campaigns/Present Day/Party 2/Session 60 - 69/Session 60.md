The party is directed to the nearby Valley, where they hear weird ominous sounds echoing throughout. A group of Ettercaps approach, scaling down the walls, curious about a particular member of the group Lucatiel. In response to the sudden appearance of the creatures that look at Lucatiel the way one would look at food, Lucatiel casts a spell to protect themselves. Combat ensues and the party is also ambushed by Phase Spiders, though they deal with the matter quickly. As they continue on, they find a Cloud Giant being attacked by Gargoyles. The Cloud Giant asks for their help and the party does so. Though Tiny has to fight against their other self’s desire to kill the Giant, convincing themself that they would take action at a later moment.  
  
The party deals with the Gargoyles swiftly, setting themselves and the enemies on fire to do so. They free the Giant, receiving Blagothkus Ethanks and learn that he is looking for his home. Blagothkus pays the party, which Tiny forfeits their portion to the party and Lucatiel as well, having previously promised their next two shares of gold to Elisa and Nyktos.  
  
Realising how much they lost, Lucatiel attempts to convince the two to take a silver piece from them, hoping to use fey trickery to lessen their gold losses. Though it unfortunately fails to work as Lucatiel’s inexperience with trickery is evident. During their conversation, Blagothkus is unable to hide his dislike of Tiny, but the two play it civil. That is until the party finds what’s left of the entrance to Blagothkus’s home.  
  
A mere 5 ft wide entrance is all that is left after the house fell. Blagothkus requests for the party’s help, paying them upfront and offering them a pair of Wyrmreaver Gauntlets used for taking down Dragons. Tiny takes offence to this, directly confronting Blagothkus and accusing Giants of all being the same.  
  
This in turn enrages Blagothkus who has been nice to the party thus far. Blagothkus warns Tiny to not assume his experiences and that he would not assume theirs. Tiny’s instinct is to instead ignore the hand extended to them, until the party intervenes, leaving a tenuous relationship between the party and Blagothkus. 

  

Still, they carry on with helping Blagothkus entering the space to find three dead bodies. The bodies have arrows sticking out of them, specifically shot into their legs so as to impede their abilities to run. They explore the room further, finding two reanimated individuals with a scroll each at their necks atop a tall wall.  
  
Along the walls are also two ballistas that look strangely familiar to Elisa. The party is cautious about entering the room, not wanting to trigger any traps as they try to understand the strange layout of things. Lucatiel gets shoved forward by Elisa, before running to hide behind Nyktos for safety. Tiny is the first to enter, followed by Nyktos. The ballistas begin shooting at the party, hooking onto Tiny first, then to Elisa who suddenly remembers the ballistas from the MLA as she gets slowly reeled in by the item.  
  
Nyktos reluctantly blood bends one of the animated individuals, allowing the party to acquire the scroll of passwall. Tiny bravely pulls the chain out of their body, freeing themselves of the ballista to push the door open. The party is then met with the sight of body parts strewn all over the floor. A single man is left alive, barely hanging on by a thread as he grips onto a stalactite. Nyktos summons Jack, as he appears before the man like an angel with blood wings, lifting him out of the dangerous pit and onto the ground, where unfortunately a trap dies.  
  
Fortunately, they both live and Lucatiel helps by teleporting the man out to safety. Before the party can leave, the statue of Goetia comes to life, attacking the party members it can reach. It knocks Tiny down the pit and focuses its attack on Jack. The party deals with the statue quick enough and moves to speak with the individual they saved. Nyktos offers the man a potion of vitality, allowing them to ease the exhaustion he had suffered. The man speaks of a devil, one who was bored or something along the like, who had captured the people and played games with them, though is unable to provide more information. Nyktos has Jack collect the remains of the knight and individuals, cleaning and wrapping up their bodies in white cloth.

  

The party moves on to find a group of devils and their leader. Their leader is a dwarf like man, or rather a devil in the shape of a dwarf. Elisa recognises him as Orik Thunderbeard, the father back in Gauntlgrym who had wanted to save his daughter using the soul anvil. Orik recognises Elisa, commenting on her new appearance as he remarks that a lot has happened for her as well.  
  
He offers Elisa a chance, not wanting her to throw her life away just like his daughter did. Elisa claims that it is through these wars that people are able to find peace. The party learns of Orik’s story, the tale of a father who would stop at nothing to bring his family back, that he died when Bruenor took his daughter from him, and now the soul anvil he seeks is with Goetia along with his soul and his daughter’s name. Tiny speaks with Orik, likening his father Reggie to the man before him.  
  
Taking pity on the child of a fellow dwarf, Orik offers the party many outs, not wanting to create another father like himself. Though the party has no choice to reject his kindness, finding themselves pit against a man they’d much rather help than fight. They then hear claps coming from behind them, an all too familiar, annoying clap. Behind them is Mordicael, sent to “assist Ethe devils. The party rolls their eyes, taking whatever opportunity they can to insult the obnoxious devil as he makes not so subtle threats to end Zhae Tsung. Combat breaks out and most of the party are grappled by barbed devils. Orik and his griffon, Hrimfax are an effective team, appearing, attacking, then disappearing.  
  
During combat, Mordicael sits on his chaise lounge, enjoying the combat from high above, occasionally throwing a counterspell at the party to mess with them. Though with the party’s combined efforts, they manage to take Orik down, relieved when he does not die but instead returns back to hell. They soon clear up the remaining devils and Mordicael finally leaves. 

  

The party continues upwards, finding a singular icy tower and the control tower that Blagothkus mentioned. Strewn across the floor are bodies of kobolds and a half dragon. Tiny pays their respects to the kobolds and half dragon, reading the letters found on their bodies. Before they enter the control tower, the ice piques Elisa’s attention who requests for Tiny’s help to open the door only for them to be hit with some cold damage.  
  
The party enters the control tower and find themselves in a beautiful room surrounded with gems that both Zipzap and Tiny want to steal. Lucatiel and Nyktos speak the passphrase Blagothkus shared and a head of clouds begins to form. The woman accuses the party of being raiders though fortunately the gauntlets that Blagothkus gave proves the party’s innocence.  
  
Esclarotta tells the party of the illusory entrance the devils have set up within their home and asks the party for a favour. She reveals that she and her husband have a son, Eigeiron, who was unfortunately left trapped in the icy tunnels when a white dragon attacked. Esclarotta assumes that her son has passed, requesting the party to retrieve his corpse so that Blagothkus doesn’t have to witness his son’s decayed corpse. The party agrees, though are more optimistic about Eigeiron’s fate as they ask Esclarotta for anything they could tell her son to prove their identity. 

  

With the information at hand, the party first heads to deal with the devils, hoping to save the victims that might still live. They party finds the remaining kidnapped victims, who ask for help, wanting to be freed from their horned captures. Elisa and Nyktos recognise the bombs attached to the cages, piecing the information together to realise just who was behind all of this. As the party steps forward, they see Basatur, sitting upon his throne of bones. The Orthorn is happy to see the party, eager to battle more worthy opponents. He shares that he has an agreement with the archduke to seek out more worthy adversaries.  
  
Though none of the recent ones have put up much of a challenge, save for the Knights of the Protectoris Aeternus. He gestures to their heads, and confesses that he ate them. The party’s focus turns towards protecting the civilians that remain. They begin to bargain their battle with Basatur, with Lucatiel convincing the Orthorn to not set off his bombs remotely. Nyktos notices the glass orb Basatur has. She links it to the cypher Zhae Tsung spoke of, “A source of life is trapped in heated sand E The glass orb that houses a burning flame. Nyktos manages to convince Basatur to part ways with the orb, though he does mention that the Archduke had tasked him to retrieve the item but gives it up nonetheless. 

  

With conversation over, battle begins. The party easily deals with Basatur and his people, saving the civilians who praise The Silvered King. They send the civilians to safety before taking a short rest. Elisa leans against Lucatiel whilst they read, enjoying their softness. Finally having a moment to themselves, Nyktos tries to bring up a serious topic. Though as she struggles to get her words out, Elisa helps, calling out Tiny for their racist behaviour.  
  
Naturally, Tiny denies the accusation, pinning the blame on the Giants once more. Having had enough of Tiny’s behaviour, Nyktos lists examples, likening Tiny’s recent actions with Godric as one of a bully’s. Tiny’s other half interrupts, annoyed at the party’s attempts as they try to lead Tiny down a path of hatred.  
  
Though with the party’s evident disappointment, Tiny begins to relent, willing to take small steps. Lucatiel takes a moment to educate Tiny on how to best make up with Blagothkus by extending an olive branch to the Giant. Tiny understands the lesson in their own way and the party sets out once more to save Eigeiron. 

  

They head down to the icy tunnels, finding the resting place of Esclarotta and then the white dragon and Eigeiron. Though before the party can do much, the ghostly spirit of the adult white dragon appears before the party. Glazhael the Icy Wind commands the party, wanting to be fed. Lucatiel cleans the chamber with prestidigitation, attempting to curry favour and buy time for the party. Meanwhile Nyktos manages to pull out the four fey fishes she’s kept, freezing them with Lucatiel’s help and presenting them to Glazhael.  
  
The Dragon accepts the food, though finds themselves unable to touch it as they are a ghost. When Tiny points that out, Glazhael merely claims that they must have been so great that they have defeated even death. Glazhael commands the party to bring them back to life, which the party pretends to accept. Glazhael returns to their corpse, morphing into a weapon which Tiny is able to wield. 

  

The party then heads to deal with Eigeiron. Eigeiron is mad, muttering and screaming about how “she Ewill arrive soon, that hell would be unleashed. Despite that he immediately notices Nyktos Eless than stealthy approach. Elisa helps, with Zipzap able to knock the Giant prone allowing Nyktos to cure Eigeiron of his madness before things get worse. Fortunately it works and Eigeiron returns. With that Blagothkus and his family can be reunited. They offer the party and the others a night’s rest, and offer to send them back to the village the next day. 

  

That night, as everyone slumbers, Tiny has a dream. He sees a familiar dwarf, Reggie, surrounded by seven motes of light. Reggie beckons his son awake, trying to grab his attention though it falters. Tiny sees the blurry vision of a woman, dressed in a colourful garb. She emits warmth. Her presence offers comfort, like a head rested on a familiar lap. She calls out to Tiny, who recognises her presence but yet cannot remember who she is. She hugs Tiny, telling him that they are loved.  
  
They question who she is, yet there is no response. All they can see is her long gentle hair, her pale skin, and her eyes. They are an ever changing flurry of colours; green, white, black, blue, and red. She reaches out for the weapon of Bane, commenting that it isn’t who Tiny is. She offers help. Her hand reaches out, pulling out the essence of Glazhael as she plays with its life like it’s mere clay. She remoulds it, fashioning Glazhael into the weapon as it glows green, white, black, blue, and red. “Golden fields, Eshe whispers, telling Tiny that that’s where it will all begin. 

Waking up from the dream, Tiny turns to their other self. For once they both agree, revering in the woman’s beauty. She who emits a warmth that feels like home. And before them, the axe is no longer the Fist of Bane, but rather a weapon made of Dragon Bone. The weapon flashes red, burning with the heat of a dragon.
