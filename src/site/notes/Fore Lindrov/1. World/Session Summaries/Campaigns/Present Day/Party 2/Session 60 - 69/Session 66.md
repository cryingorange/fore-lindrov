Burdened by the guilt of Lucatiel’s death, Tiny speaks to one of his friends, expressing regret, thinking that they could have done more, decided faster, somehow or another to prevent Lucatiel’s demise. But Nyktos disagrees, knowing that Tiny was given an impossible choice. To choose between the life of one friend or another is insane. And as Wystram shares, friends protect one another, Lucatiel knew what they were doing. A party is made of a group of equals, no one has more responsibility for the others Elives. Though try as the party may to reason with Tiny, their feelings hinder their ability to listen, the wound still too fresh to begin healing.  
  
Watching Tiny grief the loss of their friend, Wystram is reminded of his. He pulls out the mask and stares at it. When Elisa expresses curiosity towards it, Wystram explains that it is his life, his choices, and his bond with his family.  
  
Bitterly, Elisa confesses that she’s seen something like it before. Her friend, Ilythera, had one just like it. Wystram doesn’t recognise the name but assumes it to be a fellow member of the organisation. Elisa asks what is done with the mask, and Wystram shares that it represents who they are, and that the masks serve as an embodiment of the person, seeing as they could rarely retrieve the bodies of their fallen. Elisa shares that they did bury her body, which Wystram acknowledges as a kind act before sinking deep into thought. Elisa then remarks that she may want to visit Ilythera’s grave eventually before walking off. 

  

Severin tells the party that during his torment, he dreamt of a blue summer's dress, its sleeves filled with sand, set on the ground between two raging storms, but it was not blown away by the winds, in fact it remained calmly between the billowing clouds, its fabric swayed, almost danced in tandem with the winds of the maelstrom, and yet he felt drawn to the sands that fell from the dress' cloth, commenting that he felt drawn to the dress as it billowed in a way, but unaware of what it means.

  

The next morning, the party awakens and speaks with their many companions. They head to Morti first, healing the unconscious necromancer. As he jumps awake, Morti is terrified, desperately attempting to placate, to obey as how the Frost Giants had taught him. Connecting to his mind, Elisa finds a broken man. He stumbles and stammers, repeatedly promising that he would stay silent, that he would be good. As he opens his mouth, the party realises the reason for his lack of speech isn’t just out of fear, but rather that his tongue was severed, silencing him.  
  
Morti rejects any kindness shown, too afraid to do anything. He vows to be a good servant to the cloud giants, unable to free himself of the cruel fate. Helpless, the party simply suggests that he rest. And he does, scrambling off the bed they had placed him on to a corner where he curls up, trying to look as small and as unnoticeable as possible. Elisa takes the blanket, draping it over Morti who flinches at the feel of someone nearby whilst Nyktos places rations near him.  
  
Then they leave, and speak with another friend. They go to Severin. Wounds cover every inch of his body, his wings torn, his limbs broken. The party heals Severin as best they can, though no power they presently have could restore him his body. Tiny tries to remain positive, affirming that there is a way to rebuild what was lost, believing that their dad can help rebuild Severin good as new. 

  

The subject of the god shards comes up, Nyktos looks through her belongings, realising that the Jarl had taken more than just their gold. Elisa notices the same, as it dawns on the party that they had just lost the two most important items they had. Storvald now holds the god shards, the party’s key to entering the castle, to defeating the eldritch horror now rests in the hands of a dangerous man.  
  
As this is revealed, Wystrams learns more about the party. The random adventurers in the icy prison turn out to be more than just random. Common goals surface; the Jarl and the castle, both things that the party and Wystram have in common. Seeing that, Wystram announces his interest to join the party, though is promptly met with ZipZap’s affirmation that he was already his minion. And the topic changes to a lighter one. 

  

Concerned by Crimson’s lack of energy, Nyktos spends some time making him a blanket with Wystram’s help. The two converse as Nyktos checks in on their newest member and his relations with the others. Wystram shares that the teasing and constant sidetracks are something he’s rather familiar with, Tiny, Elisa, and ZipZap’s behaviour reminds him of his old friends. Nyktos assures him that they mean no harm with their joshing, merely a way to pass the time and to cope.  
  
As she finishes up the blanket, she pulls out a pair of Wyrmreaver Gauntlets, a magic item that would no doubt interest someone like Wystram. At Nyktos Erecommendation, he tries them on, the magic swirls empowering his punches. The two switch to Elvish as they begin their negotiations. Though with nothing much to offer Wystram has a slight disadvantage. They eventually settle on a monthly instalment of 100 gold with no end date. Though the agreement can be renegotiated at any time should Wystram chance upon something Nyktos would like to have. 

  

A couple days later, the party eventually arrives at Grudd Haug. They’re dropped off alongside Loog who is excited to reunite with his round goddess. As they head to the camp, a Hill Giant approaches with his group of lackeys. The Giant turns out to be Hruk, Moog’s husband. As Loog states his name and attempts to enter, Hruk stops him, pretending to not know of any “Loog E calling him weak for having failed the important mission of bringing Chieftess Guh her meal.  
  
The party stands up for Loog, with Elisa immediately issuing a challenge against Hruk, who deflects and instead has his people play push with the party. His minions scoff at the party, mocking them. They are foolish enough to taunt the elves, calling them knife ears and threatening to cut them off, they antagonise Tiny, laughing at their lack of an arm, and Elisa for her blindness. The party aren’t happy with the comments and agree to the games. Tiny plays the game as intended, using their sheer strength to push their enemy across the field.  
  
Wystram grapples his enemy, forcefully dragging them across the area. Elisa paralyses hers, then pushes him with her staff. Nyktos blood bends hers and as he breaks out, she summons Planty to grapple him, tossing him over the line. The party won but also greatly insulted the minions who charged into battle. Itching for a fight, the party obliges. The two martials are relatively kind, trading blows with their enemies as they knock them out. The spellcasters, less so.  
  
The first death happens at Elisa’s hand as her magic blasts the enemy off their feet, landing as their skull makes contact with a rock. Nyktos follows up with an attempt to be kind but as they dodge out of Planty’s grasp, a ring in their ears bursts their eardrum, ending their life. With the games settled, the party intends to follow Loog in, but Hruk warns that the Chieftess would consider them food if they did, suggesting the party bring other foods to placate the Chieftess Eappetite. They agree and with Hruk’s directions, the party find themselves approaching a hamlet. 

  

As they do, Elisa sends Milton to scout ahead, checking if the people are alright and if it is safe to approach. With Milton’s confirmation, they walk up. The place is decent, surprisingly so for Fore Lindrov. And the people are at ease, confident with weapons at their side. Something feels amiss. Nyktos sniffs the air, wondering if the place had been taken over by some other beings.  
  
She notes the hint of sulphur in the air, and warns the others of potential devils about. Before they can proceed further, an adult male with brown hair and brown eyes, with a sheathed shortsword by his side approaches them. He introduces himself as Criston, explaining that the party is free to rest in the stables and share the laws of the hamlet that are not to be broken.  
  

The laws are simple, no stealing, no killing, and no harming. All of which the party agrees to as they survey the area. Tiny notices that the weapons are of Dwarven make, and so too are the houses, which contradicts Criston’s tale that the humans have lived there forever. The party tries to probe but is met with some resistance. Elisa has Milton sneak around whilst the party heads to the travelling merchant.  
  
As they approach, the merchant smiles at them, recognising the party as it turns out to be John Smith, the human-loving demon that the party helped by informing him the way out of the Abyss. He’s once again here to sell his wares and live amongst the humans. Wystram is suspicious of the man that desperately claims to be human and also rejects any gifts that the demon offers. The rest teasing reprove Wystram for his rudeness and the party moves on. John Smith shares the fascinating things he has seen humans do and admits to watching them at night and when they change. 

  

Milton returns, sharing what he witnessed to Elisa. He too had watched some of the people change as he notes a symbol on their chest and of the immense wealth they all seem to have. Elisa has Milton take a gem, breaking one of the laws of the place. 

  

The party then speaks with one of the people, sharing their intent to purchase some food, though the townsfolk are guarded, more so than normal. And as Wystram makes a slight display, flexing his muscles, the woman is weirded out and leaves, urging the party to speak to Criston instead. Nyktos takes the opportunity to crimson bond with one of the villagers, noting that they were weirdly healthy and stronger than townsfolk usually were. The party speaks to Criston, learning of a contract the hamlet has that promises most of their food resources to another.  
  
This leaves the party without food but they are not without information as Wystram reveals that some Giants know of the hamlet’s location. To which Criston graciously gifts 2500 gold worth of gems. The party leaves, returning to their friend John Smith who happily offers a curio to solve the party’s problem. In exchange, he asks that they explore a cave he found and bring him back anything they find. The party agrees as the curio he offers would fix Chieftess Guh’s insatiable appetite. He hands them an item to teleport and also points out the strange dirt the land has. 

  

Curious, the party investigates and Nyktos Econnection with blood allows her to sense the sheer amount that is in the ground. No hamlet of such size would have this much. The party confirms their suspicions and knows not to trust the people of the hamlet. They plan to act as bait during the night, hoping that the people would attack them and give them reason to fight back. But as night is still far, the party instead heads out to where John Smith had told them, using the item he gave to teleport there. 

  

The ground beneath them shifts, an inky dark portal growing as it encapsulates them, they find themselves falling through, reaching the other end of the portal as their feet find air rather than solid ground. Most of them avoid falling, catching themselves on surfaces and using other means to stay safe.  
  
The ones that do fall are Wystram, Nyktos, Crimson, and ZipZap. Wystram seems unaffected, using his skills to slow his descent as he falls down safely. Elisa reacts quickly, levitating ZipZap as he dramatically yells for help. Tiny reaches out for Nyktos and misses as she moves out of the way to grab Crimson.  
  
Using the ancient language she summons a sphere of blood that catches both her and Crimson as it slowly lowers them safely. ZipZap sports a hallway in the wall and is levitated there by Elisa, the party joins up and they find both a body and a door. Looking about the area, Elisa recognises that they are in the underdark, a place she does not have fond memories of. 

  

In Dwarven, the door reads “Locksayer E It is a type of door that is locked with a password. Embellishing the door is a carving of a dwarf with a silver hammer, and a line that connects from his mouth to a crown. Nyktos investigates the body, noting how badly charred it is, whilst Elisa investigates from a distance with her mage hand. As she touches the corpse, Elisa feels a familiar connection, tapping into it, she feels the corpse’s final emotions; fear, irritation, and a scorching pain.  
  
Still observing the door are both Tiny and Wystram, though the latter is a distance away. Wystram suggests that the password could be “crown Eand Tiny proceeds to utter the word in Dwarven. In a split second fire that smells of brimstone shoots out from the door, blasting at Tiny, Wystram, Nyktos and Crimson.  
  
The hellhound is excited for the first time in a while as the familiar scent of hellfire spews from the door. Having taken some damage, they proceed to be more cautious, and Elisa shares about the Dwarven kingdom of Gauntlgrym. Tiny tries a couple more passwords before landing on the right one “Battlehammer E the royal family of Dwarves. As they enter, Elisa spots yet another thing that she dislikes, spider webs. She protests, not wanting to enter what could be either a Drow or spider filled place. Though the party tries their best, coaxing her as the web is burnt off.  
  
Wystram rushes forward, climbing the walls as he accidentally sets off one of the traps, causing a poisonous cloud to be shot at him. This makes things worse for Elisa, as she grows certain of a Drow’s presence. Still, she bravely enters the area, with Milton offering a supportive hand. The party notes a trip wire and an immense heat but before any of them can say anything, Crimson leaps ahead, jumping over the wire and landing with a familiar sploosh. Those who had witnessed the pup’s first swim knows exactly what the hellhound swims in  Elava.  
  
Safe to say, Elisa feels extra unhappy, memories of her quick dip in lava surfacing as the faded burn scars across her body remind her of the horrible moment. The party offers more support, assuring Elisa that they would protect her from falling in as she very begrudgingly hugs the wall, slowly making her way in. Wystram rushes in, scouting the area for Elisa. Nyktos follows in closely, looking out for any dangers in the lava that might result in a repeat of last time.  
  
Fortunately, there are no dangers and Crimson seems happier. Nyktos moves on to investigate the bodies, noting that they were very recently killed. Wystram spots one of the corpses moving and asks Nyktos to take a look, though she doesn’t spot anything. She does however, notice scratch marks like that of an Imp and guesses that Wystram might have seen one. 

  

They make their way through the rooms, learning of the people who lived there and of the treasures they discovered. They approach the room where the Imp was hiding, and see a statue of the Archduke of Avernus, a certain devil that Elisa would like dead. She threatens the imp, ready to harm it should it prove to be an enemy. Though the Imp seems to hate Goetia as well, his behaviour has the party lowering their guard.  
  
The Imp explains the situation to the party and how the Drow are close to getting the powerful item. But he offers a way out, a ritual that he knows that could end help them through the doors. The party doesn't question the Imp as Nyktos, Tiny, and Wystram offer up their blood for the ritual. The Imp explains that one person has to conduct the ritual and Nyktos volunteers, seeing as she’s the most familiar with them.  
  
Though as the magic proves hard to wield, Tiny steps in to help, offering his strength to the ritual. And as it succeeds, the two feel their chest burning, an unbearable pain as a mark is seared into their skin and a visage of a man appears. The man has black hair, bits of him are made from metal and wire, one of his arms is nothing but a machine. He calls Elisa by her name and takes in her now blinded form and chained self. He asks what happened to her, ignoring Nyktos as she demands to know what he did to them.  
  
The man or more specifically, devil, is Aurelion, an old friend of Elisa’s. Aurelion explains that when he had Zartaza sign the contract for him she had free reign, and as part of his contract he was sent to hell where he burnt in hellfire for the longest of times. Until he was found by the devils. The acting Archduke, Bel, had taken a particular interest in him.  
  
Each and every time Aurelion died from the Blood War, he was picked back up, his body replaced with machinery, his arm, his skin, and most importantly, his heart. Atlas, the imp, had helped free Aurelion from the neverending war. The sacrifice and duty that devils fulfil to keep the demons, to keep chaos at bay.  

  

Aurelion goes on, sounding quite a bit like the master he now serves as he recounts how the war has changed him. That chaos is the very evil of the world. Chaos corrupts, destroys, and ruins. The treasure beyond the doors is one that is powerful, and in the wrong hands it could obliterate the masses. The Drow are the wrong people to have it. And so is the party. According to Aurelion, the party is chaos, and as chaos is evil and cruel, chaos must not have the artefact. With that, they fight. 

  

Aurelion is strong, using his magic to keep the party in place. Though the party is also a threat. Wystram punches the devil, hurting him enough to be a concern as Nyktos follows up with a power word kill, dealing a great amount of damage and breaking Aurelion’s concentration on his spell.  
  
Before Aurelion can cast more magic at the party, Tiny’s wild magic has Aurelion turned into a sheep, quickly deescalating the situation. The one dangerous battle turns easy, as the party makes quick work of the animated armors around them. Then, Elisa casts a suggestion on Aurelion, causing him to give up the key as the party kills him, sending him back to the blade. 

  

With the key in hand, the party rush into where the Drow are, stopping in their tracks as they see not only Drow, but also three Fire Giants. One of which they recognise as [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Cult of The Eternal Flame/Vrolda\|Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Cult of The Eternal Flame/Vrolda]], from the Fire Cult. The Drow leader speaks, taking an interest in Wystram as she comments her lack of a body to warm her bed. Elisa connects with [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Cult of The Eternal Flame/Vrolda\|Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Cult of The Eternal Flame/Vrolda]], as he prompts her to attack, saying that he’d take on one of the Giants. The other Fire Giant asks the party to leave, but before he can say much, Elisa strikes.  
  
The party makes quick work of the Drow leader, ending her before she can do anything. Meanwhile Wystram has properly gained the ire of the Fire Giant, first insinuating that he would be working for the Drow and secondly prompting him to do better when he misses his attack. The Fire Giant unleashes a rampage against Wystram, bringing him close to death. Fortunately, Wystram survives that, rushing off to the door and keeping his distance from the Fire Giant. Elisa and Nyktos do the same, leaving Tiny to fight the Giant.  
  
Tiny holds their ground well, though likewise also insults the Fire Giant’s honour as Tiny attacks the Drow, disrespecting the one on one battle they were meant to be in with the Fire Giant. As the last of the Drow dies, the Fire Giant scorches the ground, turning it into molten rock as it burns some members of the party. Though they manage to end the Fire Giant before he kills them, [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Cult of The Eternal Flame/Vrolda\|Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Cult of The Eternal Flame/Vrolda]] succeeds in holding his ground. From what he’s gathered. Duke Zalto, the duke of the Fire Giants, seeks the Vonindod  metal in order to reforge the weapon that he has as they believe that they’ll be able to rebuild the Ordning with the Fire Giants at the top with it.
