The party sets out once more, and with Elisa’s navigating, the party quickly makes their way to the frozen waterfall that Callahan mentioned. They walk behind it only to find nothing but a stone wall. Elisa clarifies with Callahan on ways to open the door to which the young child responds that he does not have the ability to do so yet but that his parents would move the blood inside. Everyone then turns to Nyktos who does just that, sensing and manipulating the blood inside the walls to unlock it. The doors open and the party are halted by four guards who question who the party is and why Callahan is alone with them. Not wanting the kid to hear, Nyktos quietly conveys that the child’s parents likely passed. They are then led to the chieftess of the town, walking past their settlement that houses a population of 20-30 people. On one of the walls the party sports a strange mark that none of them can read before passing it. As the Paladins both cast divine sense to note that there is an undead inside where they need to go. They enter a space with icy pews and notably a statue of Tenebrous, similar to what was seen before; of a figure sat atop a throne made of bones. Behind a curtain, a giant finger pokes through with piercing blue eyes that soon follow as a giant with long grey hair, an icy veil, dressed in a dress decorated with skulls of humanoids. The Chieftess of Dulogo, Sigrid , asks the party of Callahan’s wellbeing, though notably refers to the people of Dulogo as “small folk E She also introduces herself as the only Blood Cleric of the town. That piques the interest of Nyktos who too introduces herself as a cleric of the Lord of Blood. Sigrid refers to Nyktos as “elf Eand asks her to prove her powers, to which Rook immediately offers assistance by slicing his skin, allowing Nyktos to prove her sangromancy skills as she manipulates his blood. During this exchange, Elisa whispers to Zepheros asking him about the Giant Chieftess. He responds that she is a Frost Giant, that their kind rank amongst the 3rd from the bottom. He goes on to list the rankings of Giants in ascending order; Giant-kin, Hill, Stone, Frost, Cloud, Storm, and Titans. Nyktos shares the party’s intentions to seek the Amber Temple, requesting for any support that Sigrid could provide. Sigrid shares that the people of Dulogo are able to maintain their body temperatures. Nyktos asks if she’s able to learn this, requesting Sigrid’s help to teach her more about the Lord of Blood. Sigrid responds that blood is the matter of life and to master the body is to also master one’s mind. She shares that despite being the only Blood Cleric in town and the one to have taught the others of the Blood Lord’s ways, she herself does not know the name of the Lord of Blood. Nyktos steps in, informing her of the obelisk they had found as well as the holy symbol she obtained from it, and of the name of the Lord of Blood, “Tenebrous E Sigrid enquires about the obelisk, having never heard of it before. Pyrite then asks Sigrid about the Amber Temple, though the Frost Giant conveniently ignores every question relating to the topic. Ilenti asks if Sigrid has seen another shadar kai in the area, hoping to gain some information about Arala, though Sigrid shares that she does not leave the town but the others might have. She then tasks the party to retrieve the bodies of the young boy’s parents and tells the party to seek the human named Ormir for more information. 

  

The party then leaves Sigrid to find Ormir. They manage to do just that and meet an older gentleman with green eyes. They ask him about the region and learn that there are many deadly fauna and demons that have resided here for generations. And from Ormir they also learn the general location of the creature's nest as well as the names of Callahan’s parents, Sigurd and Brynhilde. The party asks for any coats that the town might have for sale but are only fortunate enough to have Ormir’s coat. With that, they set out to retrieve the bodies. Along the way, the party comes across yet another stack of stones and another note from Arala is found. The note details part of her journey and how she misses her husband, Hayato but feels guilty about doing so, blaming herself for his death. She continues through the mountains, trekking towards the Amber Temple. Pyrite prays for blessings for the road ahead, sensing a dark presence before the snow storm subsides, allowing the party to travel once more. The party then arrives at some ruins with Pyrite immediately noticing a half collapsed archway surrounded with magic. Meanwhile, Elisa follows a blood trail to find three snow wolf bodies that have been ripped apart and left there for some time. Though interestingly enough, she notes that the area is no good place for a den and is curious as to why the bodies would be there. Rook follows some paw prints and eventually the party all end up at the archway that Pyrite is at. Nyktos notices something under the snow atop the archway and Rook helps to remove said snow to expose some mould. They’re mostly confused before the mould starts to move and reveals an eye. Pyrite notes that the archway has traces of conjuration magic. The mould asks the party to keep quiet, annoyed at them for interrupting his rest. Though Elisa continues to speak with the demon, asking him for his name and eventually giving him the name “Moulder Ewhich the demon seems to like. They gather from Moulder that the creature they seek went through the archway and eventually manage to bribe Moulder into both opening the gate for them and letting them out when they have to leave. They convince Moulder with a series of bribes; Elisa offering one of Zipzap’s necklaces, Pyrite cooking up a pound of wereboar flesh that Nyktos provides, gold from Pyrite, Nyktos, Rook, and Ilenti, a glamerweave from Rook, and lastly a polished rock from Pyrite. Moulder has Rook hang the glamerweave of the side of his archway, transforming the illusion from what Rook had set to a nice stone pattern that blends well with the existing archway. Satisfied, Moulder allows the party through. 

  

The party finds themselves separated. Elisa finds herself in one of the safest rooms, as Moulder seems to have taken a shine to her, granting her safe passage. Alongside Elisa is Nyktos and Crimson, unsure of where the others are, they both agree to sit down, confident that it would be better for them to stay in a safe location, trusting that the others would soon stumble upon this room. They light a bonfire and enjoy some snacks whilst waiting. Meanwhile, Pyrite finds himself with Sir Smigginsworth in a room where there lies blood in the shape of a forked tail wrapped around a skull. Entering the room, both Pyrite and Sir Smigginsworth feel a rush of madness wash over them, and as the madness is too much to bear, Pyrite feels a familiar presence that offers him help. Taking the help, the voice speaks out, telling Pyrite to wake up and get out of there. He does so successfully, though Smigginsworth is still paralysed. Not wanting to leave him alone, Pyrite starts to move about the place, dragging the horse along with him. Rook finds himself in a room with Fido where a man is being tortured by a skeleton. He recognises the man as the one that turned innocents into effigies. Rook finds the site amusing, seeing that the man got some form of punishment for what he had done but not wanting to alert the skeleton, he simply makes his way past, heading to another room. Ilenti finds herself in a room filled with mirrors lining the walls and a sword on the centre podium, she takes the blade, usable, fully intact and coated with silver. 

  

Back to Pyrite, his journey with Sir Smigginsworth eventually leads the two of them to a hallway. Casting detect magic, Pyrite notes both fiendish and illusory magics about. He picks up a rock, tossing it into the area and watches as it falls through the ground into what he presumes is a pit. He then turns around, leading them both to the mirror room that Ilenti was just in. Looking into the mirrors, he sees a human male with dark hair and skin in the mirrors, Kiril, the buff hunter they had seen in Kislev. Kiril and his pack flank a bald headed man and at his command, they rush to attack the man. As Pyrite stays in the room to make sense of the visions in the mirror, the creature they were here to seek attacks him. Battle ensues and it does not turn out well for Pyrite as the creature takes him down. It is about 3pm when Rook finds Vorun, learning of the room the boy first entered, a den of sorts with many dead bodies about. Rook senses his connection with Smigginsworth break as they continue on, trying to find the others. Ilenti then stumbles upon Elisa and Nyktos, the two who were blissfully enjoying their afternoon tea. Just as they were about to invite Ilenti to join, Nyktos notices the presence of the wolf, hurriedly asking everyone to leave as Elisa casts pass without trace, allowing them to evade the wolf for now. They enter a small room that resembles that of a resting area though Elisa and Nyktos are suspicious, and the former soon finds an illusory wall, retrieving a parchments with sigils on it. Looking at the paper, Elisa recognises the sigil for the school of Abjuration whilst Nyktos points out the “Remove curse Esymbol. The three think about what the parchment could mean, and remembering Faraine’s warnings from before, they surmise that the wolf might have been cursed into its current form, that mayhaps a remove curse could undo the spell. 

  

Pyrite wakes up, still alive. He notices his blade looks different, bigger than before. He looks into the mirror once more, noting as the bald man speaks with an aged woman with white hair who seems kind and gentle, a teenage boy by her side. The man asks if he can trust her, which the woman nods, seemingly telling the truth. Rook and Pyrite then find each other but unfortunately for them, the wolf finds them as well. The two try their best to hold back the wolf, allowing Vorun to escape with Fido before escaping themselves as well. Ilenti, Nyktos, and Elisa then enter the mirror room, similarly watching the visions in the mirror as the ones before them did, they see Zuleika and her partner, the bald man, now understanding their relationship to one another. They then head to the next room which has a stone head in the centre of it, Ilenti and Nyktos both hear a voice of a child calling for help. They respond to her, asking her where she is and offering assistance as best they can. Meanwhile, Elisa is confused at her two party members speaking to air. She is further confused as Zipzap hears the voice too. Nyktos tries to help the kid but finds herself trapped and soon the wolf appears before them. Nyktos and Ilenti attack the wolf, trying to protect themselves as Elisa remains confused at what her party members are doing, she walks through the creature, shouting at her party to stop with their jokes before she leaves. This happens again the second time they come by the room before Ilenti sees the spell for what it is, an illusion. She calls out to the party, informing them of such allowing Elisa, who was already sceptical of the illusion, and Nyktos, who trusts her sister implicitly to both see past the illusion. The three also stumble upon a room where they hear simian howling, then enter the same madness room that Pyrite had been to before. Nyktos and Crimson are both paralysed with madness, staying that way for 5 minutes whilst Elisa laughs crazily for 7 minutes, and Ilenti cries for a full 10 minutes. During which Zipzap’s nice hallucinations get interrupted by the Griffon attacking him and Zipzap quickly petrifies it. As Nyktos comes back, she feels a pain at the back of her neck, tracing the wound and realising that it’s the same mark Arala had. She looks at Ilenti who also sports the same mark and worries about it before sharing with Elisa, Ilenti and eventually Rook and Pyrite. 

  

The party eventually stumbles upon one another before gathering at the first room, waiting for the creature to show up. It doesn’t. The party then move on, making their way to the den room that Vorun had seen to retrieve the bodies of Sigurd and Brynhilde. Additionally, Nyktos finds a wolf bust with the words “My one beloved, Zuleika E The party eventually heads to the mirror room, watching the last vision; one of two bloodied and young teens being forced to battle to the death by Kiril, where the survivor is then turned into a werewolf, much to the disgust of Emil. The party then gives up waiting for the wolf to show up, having waited for sometime. Rook casts a minor illusion to lure the wolf over whilst the rest prepare their attacks. They make swift work of the wolf, knocking it out before Nyktos casts greater restoration on him, turning him back into Emil. They heal him and Nyktos returns him his wolf bust, trying to gain his trust. Emil speaks of the blood moon clan and Kiril’s pointless fights that he puts the new recruits through, vocalising that their leader should have turned both of the young teens instead. He also asks about his mate, Zuleika, and is pleased to hear that she is fine and well. He surmises that Zuleika likely has some plans of revenge against Kiril, seeing as she is one to hold a grudge. Pyrite emphasises to Emil that he owes them for saving him and Emil agrees, promising to help when the time comes to fight Banecroft. The party eventually agrees on leaving Emil here in Moulder’s place, taking a chance that Banecroft would not care enough to come find Emil. That’s when Rook shares of the torture victim that lives here as well. They head to find the right room, eventually finding it at 8.10pm, they first speak with the skeleton, learning some but not much before eventually attacking and defeating the skeleton easily when Elisa shares that the tortured man knows of Hethrediah. As a sign of good faith, Elisa relaxes the torture device whilst Nyktos lies about agreeing to free the man once he answers their questions. They learn that Hethrediah is at the manor and works with Banecroft. Nyktos asks about Adella’s death, still thinking that it had to do with the beasts that Adella cared for. Though the man informs her otherwise, stating that Hethrediah had shown up for her. Nyktos goes silent for a moment. Ilenti follows up with the questioning, asking why Hethrediah hadn;t come for her or Arala instead but learns that he eventually would have. Nyktos collects herself and asks who Hethrediah is, to which the man responds that he is a demon, a god. That Hethrediah has an invulnerable army. The man pleads for his freedom, trying to convince the party that they’d benefit from his help, that he could navigate the social dynamics with the demons far better than them. Amongst his pleas, Nyktos looks up at the man, stating that they don’t make deals before slashing him in half. As she does, the man changes, revealing his true self before cursing at the party, swearing revenge on them. With that settled, the party settles Emil down in the first chamber, leaving him with enough food to survive down here. 

  

The party returns to Dulogo, bringing the bodies back and joining in the funeral for Sigurd and Brynhilde. During which, the ritual starts with the people collectively using their blood magics on the two corpses, which Nyktos joins in on as they pull the blood from the bodies, bringing it before Callahan. The boy uses his powers, trying to control the big amount of blood but as he slips Sigrid subtly helps the child, placing all the blood into the bottle that she holds. Sigrid then places the bottle in the ceiling of the cave, manipulating the ice before covering it back up. With that the funeral comes to a close as the townsfolk bring the bodies away for burning. Nyktos approaches Callahan, pulling out the flute they had found from before. Callahan expresses that it belonged to his father, and Nyktos affirms that it is now his, and wishes him well before leaving.

  

As they rest for the night, Ilenti finds Elisa, expressing her concerns about the mark on their necks and if it would be safe for them to stay in the settlement for fear of it attracting demons to Dulogo. Elisa examines the mark, noticing that it’s mirrored compared to Arala’s and seeks out Nyktos to confirm that that’s the case for both of them. Nyktos shares her experience with the mark, noting that she felt mentally weaker since obtaining it. Unable to figure much else out, the party rests for the night. 

  

The next day, they set out with Sigrid as their guide. Sigrid casts control weather, taming the snow storm. During their walk, Pyrite asks if Sigrid knows of the Monochrome Kingdom, which she expresses that she does not, having holed up in this place for a long time. Sigrid shares her experience, how she too once sought out the Amber Temple, only to have been defeated before she could reach the place. And in the moment when she was close to death, her blood pooling and freezing around her, Tenebrous reached out to her, giving her the powers that she has today. She goes on about Frost Giant culture and how they were ruled absolutely by Jarl Storevald, who believed that a great Artifact called, “The Ice That Never Melts Ehad the power to bring about a new Ice Age and propel the Frost Giants to the top of the Ordning, the caste system that all Giants adhere to. Pyrite then inquires about the Doom of the Desert, learning that it is the title of a great dragon. Lastly, Nyktos asks about the demons in the vicinity, concerned that their marks would attract many to them. They then sport a familiar stack of stones and Nyktos calls for a rest, immediately searching for the parchment Arala left behind. Sigrid questions the need for a break and when Nyktos shares that it was made by her sister, Sigrid applauds the individual’s ability to hold onto hope as she had made her trek up the mountain. Pyrite, Ilenti, and Nyktos all pray at the stacks, during which they see visions of a giant goat with red eyes that crashes into someone, pushing them off the cliff. As Nyktos prays, she speaks to Arala, informing her that they too now bear the same mark as she does, but they will be strong and so will she. For Ilenti, she directs her prayer towards asking for help, a sign of sorts and is rewarded with a vision of a gate with statues as the vision closes in on the demonic looking statue with a horned head. Rook has a different summon today, changing out Sir Smigginsworth for an Elk. He tries communicating with the Elk mentally, though each attempt is met with regular animal noises as opposed to words. Seeing this failed communication, Elisa casts speak with animals to communicate with the Elk and learns that the Elk is pulling a prank on Rook. Nyktos joins in on the fun, suggesting to Rook that he should speak to the Elk in the Elk’s language rather than his own. Pyrite raises a brow at the suggestion, confused by the entire interaction. Eventually the Elk speaks to Rook, though refuses to give him a name due to his fey nature, and instead agrees to being called “Something E The party then moves on. 

  

Sigrid leads the party up til a distance before she can no longer continue. She turns to Nyktos, calling Nyktos by name before expressing that she once thought that she was meant to reach the Amber Temple, though perhaps now she realises that maybe that’s Nyktos destiny instead. With that, she bestows Nyktos a boon, one that allows her to better protect herself before leaving, with the Frost Giant bestowing upon Nyktos the Blood Aegis ability, enabling her to use her blood to harden her body.

  

The party continue their way up, noticing a pile of rock that Pyrite and Nyktos recognise as salt. Elisa picks up a piece and begins licking it. Nyktos picks up a piece as well and notices an eye on the piece she has, she suggests that Elisa stop licking the piece and surmises that it might have been a person who was petrified into salt after Pyrite identifies the stone to be pre-mined Salt. Elisa gathers the pieces of the person, piecing them together and apologising to the pile that used to be a person for her actions. As they continue on, they reach a point with multiple salt petrified people and upon approaching the figures, Ilenti, Nyktos, and Pyrite recognise this area as the same area from their vision. Ilenti warns the others about a potential goat. Moments later, it shows up. Elisa speaks to Sangzor, calling him by name and in return he speaks to all of them, showcasing the various languages he is able to communicate in. He shares that the Amber Temple holds great secrets, terrible screams, living, dying, crying. Sangzor asks the party for a price, one payment for passage through his area. But as he shows signs of being able to do anything, the party begins asking him for potential deals. Nyktos inquires if, hypothetically, Sangzor could cure some children who are a hybrid of human and animal, which he affirms that he can. Rook asks, his interest piqued when Sangzor expresses that he’s able to reincarnate anyone without cost. He inquires about the cost for an item that would allow him to traverse to the astral plane and readily offers his heart for the price. Pyrite then inquires if Sangzor could end the moon, to which he can’t, or if he can kill Banecroft, to which Sangzor does not respond. Pyrite then quiets down, fully realising the magnitude of Sangzor’s power. Elisa then inquires about a solution for thralldom, offering the price of her saliva. 

  

With those two deals set in stone, Elisa begins to cough, feeling a lump in her stomach before she begins hurling all of the spit out of her body. Her throat hurts immensely, her lips now cracked and bleeding. Sangzor says nothing and simply begins eating the saliva like a beast. As he finishes up the first course, he then rushes at Rook, his horns piercing through Rook’s chest and heart. As Sangzor enjoys the second course, Rook is left dying on the floor, a gaping wound on his chest. Pyrite heals Rook up enough to not die. And as Rook comes back to life, his wings however start to decay, falling off his body as the Celestial light that once resided within him fades. As he comes to terms with what he just did, breaking one of the tenets of his oath, a form appears before Rook. The form is grey, like a shadow from a lost potential. As Rook looks up he sees himself, his celestial self to be specific, looking down at him in disappointment.

  

There’s still a matter of one last payment and Nyktos waits for Sangzor to finish his meal before offering up pus to pass. Pustules begin forming all over her body, excruciating pain coursing through her as she screams, barely able to cling onto consciousness as Sangzor begins to enjoy his third meal, only stopping after a whole 15 minutes of pure agony for Nyktos. Ilenti and Pyrite cautiously approach Nyktos, the former reaches out a hand but stops as Nyktos instinctually flinches away. Still bearing with the pain, Nyktos begins healing herself of the wounds, and with both Pyrite and Elisa’s contributions, she’s able to cure all of the pustules, though there are some aspects that still remain. Ilenti speaks to her sister, immediately apologising. But Nyktos looks at Ilenti, unable to be extremely kind in the moment and simply tells her sister to stop apologising for things that are not her fault. Ilenti speaks to Nyktos trying her best to comfort her older sister, though it’s clear that Nyktos is in a dazed state, her mind not quite remembering the past minutes. Still, their conversations end on a lighter note, with Nyktos able to come back to the present. Pyrite speaks to Nyktos, asking about her well being and thanking her, to which Nyktos weakly makes a joke advice, telling Pyrite to never offer up pus as a price. 

  

Afterwhich, Pyrite approaches Rook, checking up on him and affirming his willingness to help bring back Telantes. Their conversation helps Rook steel himself and affirm his decision as he knows that he is now one step closer to bringing Telantes back, that it is worth the price paid. Meanwhile, Zipzap approaches Elisa, asking if she’s okay. To which she is but she is unable to speak. This seems to spark an idea in Zipzaps mind and Elisa shares that mayhaps not being able to talk isn’t too bad. Zipzap suggests that they could intimidate people by having Elisa make herself bleed from her mouth. Seeing as she’s only able to communicate telepathically, Elisa shares that now people can’t stop her from talking to them. Ilenti then checks up on Elisa next, asking if her wounds hurt. They do but Elisa is quick to quell Ilenti’s concerns, guilt, and sadness by joking that no one can stop her from speaking in their head ever again. To which Ilenti affirms that she wouldn’t want to stop Elisa. The two then share a hug. Ilenti then goes to Rook, inquiring about his plans after Barovia and his plans for Vorun. Rook says that he’ll train Vorun up until he’s ready and he believes that the kid will be. Muttering under her breath, Ilenti states that Vorun would never be ready for Rook to leave, which Rook does hear and only gives a bitter smile in response. Nyktos then approaches Elisa as the druid is quick to express her concern for Nyktos despite what has happened to herself. Nyktos cracks the same joke about not donating pus as before, trying to lighten the mood. Elisa shares that she now knows of the way to cure her people, listing the spells to Nyktos. Realising that she could help, Nyktos tells Elisa that eventually she’ll learn those spells too and between the two of them they can heal her people twice as fast, a sentiment which Elisa appreciates. Elisa then speaks of how others can’t stop her from talking to them now and Nyktos affirms that she would never want Elisa to stop either. The start talking about Rook as Nyktos suggests that the three of them could be part of a special therapy group, jesting about how the two of them could charge Rook for it. Elisa smiles, adding that they should charge him by the hour to be a part of it. Nyktos shares that though she is no one special, that she’s very proud of Elisa for what she’s doing for her people, that she’s very brave. Elisa then looks at Nyktos, expressing that she’s no one special either but she’s happy to be in the same party as all of them, and is happy to have met Nyktos. A sentiment which Nyktos agrees with, responding that she’s happy to know all of them as well. And the two share a hug, their friendship now stronger than before. Ilenti approaches Vorun, offering the kid a smoke which he hastily accepts. When Ilenti attempts to speak to Vorun about his feelings, he instead insists that they have to get to the temple, that they have no time, for once thinking of something bigger than himself. With that, the party sets out for the temple together.
