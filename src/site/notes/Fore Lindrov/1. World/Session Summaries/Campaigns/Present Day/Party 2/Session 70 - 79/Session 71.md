The party teleports back into the pool. As everyone finds their bearings, Nyktos feels the magic around them, as if time has slowed down for her. She sees the threads of magic that link the pool to the Abyss. She reaches out, grasping the magic as she redirects the magic to link the forest with Dulogo. The pool begins to change, a red hue colouring the area as the liquid rushes upwards bringing the party along with it as they are swept gently onto the ground once more, each with a handful of dirt as they complete their final trial. Thalor is confused and shocked, once again questioning the sight before him. Nyktos alludes that it is a higher power who wishes to help the Elves, though holds back on the details as Wystram reminds her of their previous conversation. The party questions how Myrantar came to be in Camlash’s lair, Myrantar reveals that she was a priestess of House Xorlarrin but was cast into the demon web pits for failing Lolth’s order to kill her lover, a priestess of House Baenre. Myrantar claims that her lover’s magic was faster and hence she was banished before she could complete her task though Nyktos picks up on the Drow’s lies. Myrantar then declares her blood debt, vowing to commit what is left of her life to the party. 

With that, the party takes a short rest before heading back to the village. During which, Myrantar speaks to Nyktos inquiring of her Arachnid form and complimenting her for it, a gesture that Nyktos greatly appreciates having not been able to freely be in her true form for a long while. Wystram finds a quiet area and prays to the Raven Queen, saying once more that he deserves no redemption but instead entreats for her help to protect the others Shadar Kai. As he makes his plea, one of the feathers that he took disappears. Behind him is a presence, that of a soft embrace, cold yet warm. She pulls the magic from his Eldritch Claw Tattoo, infusing it with the feather plucked as it turns into a greatsword bestowed by the Raven Queen to her champion. And just as silently as she appears, she leaves. Now armed with a weapon to protect the ravens Wystram takes this as her response to his questions. 

Back at the village, Elder Vaelith is quick to assume the party’s failure, though they all prove him wrong, each producing the feather, bark, and dirt that they require. Myrantar is not kind to the Forest Elves, referring to them as savages and remarking that they should submit to their betters. Nyktos whilst understanding of Myrantar’s upbringing does not take kindly to her words, instead telling the Drow that the elves are spoken for and that she is not to do anything to harm the Forest Elves. 

Unable to dispute their achievements, Elder Vaelith prepares for their admission, a sign that they are now part of the initiated. He produces an ancient bowl, proclaiming that the ingredients are to be added to the sacred bowl and burn a mark into each party member. Those able to read Elvish know that the bowl isn’t some ancient sacred artifact but rather a common salad bowl. Elisa and Tiny are seemingly excited by this, both mistaking that they would get a cool new tattoo. Meanwhile, for their own reasons, neither Nyktos nor Wystram desires to be burnt. Nyktos tries to push the matters aside, telling Vaelith that the matters at hand are more important, lives are at stake. 

But the Elder pushes not willing to give up what little misunderstood culture they have left. Though before they are able to continue, a Zombified Wyvern swoops in, alongside their rider, an elven undead with ravenette hair and inky black eyes. The man speaks in common, addressing the party for their names. The party pushes, suggesting that he share his first. He does, introducing himself as Naergoth, whilst implying that his master has intentions for the land. When the party presses to learn the identity of his master, he implies that they’re not important enough to know such details. Seeing as the party wishes to protect the Forest Elves, Naergoth suggests a game. For every hit the party takes, he’ll kill one of the Forest Elves. With not much choice, the party obliges. 

As the undead calls his army forth, a group of the Forest Elves Edead relatives and friends, made undead by either Naergoth or his masters Ehand. The party launches into battle, doing what they can to both kill the undead and prevent themselves from being hit. Tiny is the first to be hit and in response to that, Naergoth fires his gun as the Elves. Tiny rushes up, intercepting the bullet and taking the hit instead. The party stand their ground, able to end the undead before any other Elves die. 

With that, the game comes to an end as Naergoth comments that he would be back the next day to kill both the party and those the Forest Elves they desire to protect. He leaves the party with a gift that some of them recognise. A monstrosity appears before them, a meat effigy made of individuals that Elisa once knew. The members of Fall Damage, Paldron, Declan, Krista strung up as the same effigy Elisa saw. ZipZap is the only one, unaffected by the grotesque sight, elated in fact as he begins to let out the horrid sounds of his favourite scream. The party fights, desperate to protect their allies and also to end the disgusting sight before them. 

They do so, and as the Forest Elves come back to reality, Vaelith comes clean. He shares that they were invaded a couple days ago, their numbers of hundreds left to a mere twenty. Reality sets in, as the party are made aware of the strength that Naergoth has, a whole undead army of two hundred, maybe more. 

Just as the party prepares themselves for a seemingly unwinnable battle, Tiny hears a voice in their mind. The familiar feminine voice warns them that there are too many enemies, that they will surely die if they went as they were. Instead she asks that he come visit her at the Red Belvedere. Tiny calls out to their mother and feels the ability to cast a spell. The others recognise the magic, identifying it to be a Planar Shift. Thalor steps in, asking for the party to bring them to where they were going. They first reject him, but with his insistence, Nyktos relents, allowing the Elf to make his own decisions. 

She does warn him that they have no idea where they would be going, but the eager Elf doesn’t care. So he follows them, as Nyktos summons two of her cultists to protect Thalor. With that, they leave, travelling to who knows where. As Tiny activates their spell, the party finds themselves in hell. The temperature is hot, unbearably so and the party looks to Tiny for directions, seeing as they were the one to bring them here. 

Tiny is unsure simply heading in the direction they feel is right. And as the heat and discomfort gets to the party, they begin being less kind with one another. They whine, asking Tiny if they have arrived yet and complaining when they have not. When asked why they came to Hell, Tiny doesn’t give a response, instead asking why everyone seems to be blaming them. Vhagar gives some form of a response, stating that their mother called them and assumes that she would give them the army they require or something. As they trek through Hell, the party hears voices in their head. Their own voice in an enticing tone that suggests little acts of deceit, trickery, violence. For the most part, they shrug off the voices, able to withstand these temptations. 

As the party continues on, they come across a group of Elven warriors. The first has a word carved in Infernal on his forehead, “Traitor E The group approaches. They speak with the man, Alflan and learns of what happened. He and his people were Hellriders. Following their leader Angel Zariel into the Hells to fight against the forces that would harm their loved ones should they be allowed to escape. But when faced with the horrors of Hell, Alflan fled, leading his people to run away in fear. Forsaken by her soldiers, Angel Zariel fell, becoming the Archduchess. Before they could do anything, a devil approaches the area, dragging with him another Hellrider. The man pleads for his life, begging for mercy. But the Devil, Commander Haruman does not give in. 

He explains the crimes of these individuals, and how they, supposed warriors who had sworn to protect the weak, gave in to their fears, breaking their oaths and leading to the downfall of their leader, and now Bel has usurped the Archduchess Eposition, and her Lady Zariel remains missing. Nyktos probes, asking why the then Angel Zariel had not called for her people to retreat. But Haruman explains that the Hellriders knew what they were here to do, that they were not fighting for themselves but instead for the lives of the others that remained back home. 

That they had a duty to uphold. When learning of where the party intends to go, the Commander gives them a choice. Should the party prove themselves as upholders of justice by carving the sin of this Hellrider onto his forehead, he would help them get across River Styx. The party is conflicted. Ordinarily, most of them would want to help the tortured souls, yet the situation at hand is much more nuanced than just good or evil. Elisa makes her choice easily, not one to stand for betrayal, she gives in to the temptation of Harm. Evaluating the situation, Nyktos understand the importance and trust a leader has for their people. 


And if their people break that trust, then that leader would have no one behind them. She relents, keeping quiet as she too gives in to the temptation of Harm. Wystram observes the situation, and feels hopeless, unable to help the people, not without risking everyone else’s lives. He takes a step back, knowing he cannot help the Hellrider, Altair, before him. Myrantar steps up, offering to carve the word on the man’s head, agreeing that he should be punished for his crimes. Clinging on to Nyktos Equestion of why Lady Zariel didn’t call for a retreat, Tiny makes their decision, not willing to witness the suffering, they try to reason with the Commander but it doesn’t work. If anything the Commander’s insistence angers Tiny. 

How dare a mere Commander speak up against them like that. How dare he go against what Tiny has demanded. Giving into the temptation of pride twice, Tiny uses their powers and takes on a platinum form, attacking the Commander mercilessly. The small fiends that fly about the area swoop in to attack, hitting Wystram as they bring him close to death. Wystram attempts to stop the situation, pointing out that the actions do not seem just. However, his words do not reach their intended audience, as he stands back, trying to preserve what little he has left of his blood. Myrantar steps up to attack and as Wystram tries to hold her back she instead insists on fighting by Tiny’s side. Elisa doesn’t join the fight, rushing back to protect Thalor as she has ZipZap dismiss one of Nyktos Efollowers who was on the verge of death, for dying in Hell would forever bound their soul to this place. Thalor wants to help, but Elisa holds him back as she and Nyktos try to explain their view to him. 

Striking at the Commander once more, the battle ends before Nyktos is even able to do anything. Tiny gives the Commander one last chance, asking if they have changed their mind. The Commander stands his ground, calling Tiny a fool. But Vhagar joins in on the shouting match, telling the Commander that a Fiend is _nothing_ compared to a **King**. With that Tiny kicks the body of the Commander down the River Styx as the vague shape of Tiny’s mother appears behind them. 
 
When Thalor questions once more why they didn’t help, Nyktos and Elisa’s explanations offer no comfort. For Thalor who knows not of the dangers of the world, the two people before him seem like monsters. Inhumane creatures who choose who lives or dies. He begins to back up, almost running away before he is stopped by Elisa’s suggestion spell. The spell has him following them until they are ready to go back home. Wystram walks up to Alflan, freeing him and the others. 

Alflan points out a bag the Commander seems to have left behind and Wystram picks it up, handing its entirety to Tiny. Vhagar generously splits the loot with the others, playing the role of a generous king. Wystram rejects the amount, whilst Elisa and Nyktos are alright taking money that is offered to them. Alflan is first to thank Tiny and Vhagar, and Tiny is quick to say that they didn’t do it for free, implying that Alflan and the other Hellriders now owe them something. Tiny walks over to the Hellriders and demands that they swear fealty to him, giving in to the temptation of oppression. Most of the Hellriders are frightened, it seems the one who has saved them from their tormentor is a tyrant. 

They beg for their lives once more, pleading for freedom. But Tiny does not relent, insisting that this is what they should do, that they should swear to him to right their wrongs. This catches Elisa and Nyktos off guard as they intervene. They tell Tiny that this isn’t what they do, they don’t save people and then force them to swear oaths. But Tiny doesn’t agree, stating that these individuals should pay for their sins through an oath to do better. The two argue that the decision to make an oath should be on the individual, not by Tiny’s enforcement. 


Then a voice creeps into Tiny’s mind, how dare their teammates talk to them that way. These people who didn’t help in their battle against the general, the cowards who did not have their back? Tiny gives in to the temptation of pride once more and points this out, asking the party where they were during the battle, saying that they had left them alone. But Elisa and Nyktos insist that they were protecting Thalor and their people. 

During which, Wystram communes with his Goddess, seeking her opinion and help. He states that he had claimed to desire to tip the scales, to right wrongs and yet when push comes to shove, he fled. He failed to stand his ground, feeling like a worthless individual. His Queen responds, telling him that he does what he thinks is right, that a man is worthy if he dies for what he believes, that Wystram is worthy, as long as he follows what he believes in. His Goddess Efaith in him causes more turmoil.

 For when Wystram fails, the world seems to give him a second chance, the very same second chance that many others do not get. Pushing aside his own matters, Wystram speaks up, telling Tiny that their spirit of nobility always rang true, especially with how they dealt with the Hill Giants. Yet now he charges these Hellriders with a responsibility that they do not have the capability of handling. 

Tiny turns to the Hellriders, offering them a choice to make up for what they had done. Before they even finish their sentence, all but one of the Hellriders flee. Alflan, genuinely regretful of his actions, takes a knee before Tiny, swearing an oath to Tiny. Tiny claims that Alflan now stands as a redeemer, his new purpose is to give those who oppress no quarter, to punish the wrongdoers. 

He grants the Hellrider a weapon and for now Alflan follows the party until they are able to leave him somewhere safe. Following Alflan’s directions, the party head to a jetty where they see an Amnizu and his master alongside their guards. Eavesdropping, Tiny hears the conversation the Amnizu has, as the Amnizu fails to convince his master to return to the forge. As they approach, the Amnizu turns to address the party, and Tiny reveals what he’s eavesdropped to negotiate with the Amnizu. The Amnizu agrees, hoping that the party could convince his Lord to return to the forge, seeing as their army would require the reinforcements and weapons to deal with the increasingly restless Demons of the Abyss. 

Just then Charon arrives on a boat, much smaller than when they last saw him. He states that he would only take one group. And so Tiny steps forth to speak with Lord Bael, and conversations do not go as the party planned. Lord Bael isn’t insulted but has little to no reason to hear the party no longer. 

Wystram steps in asking the lord directly for what he desires in exchange for them to have the boat. Lord Bael demands for power, to fuel him and aid him in battle. Not wanting to give up power, Nyktos inquires if items would work as well, things to help him and his army in combat. Lord Bael agrees, and they all pay their dues. With that, the Lord leaves and now the party has to bargain with Charon once more. Charon states his price and the party begins offering up items to pay. Wystram pays almost all the money that he has, covering both his and Myrantar’s portion. 

Nyktos pays in spell gems and other goods, covering for her, Crimson’s and Thalor’s portion. Elisa easily affords both hers and ZipZap’s amount. Lastly Tiny struggles with payment slightly, having three people worth. But their pride doesn’t allow them to ask for help and so they offer up their bag of holding, paying the price. The party respectfully burns what’s left of Dame Aylin’s body, letting her rest. Having successfully paid, the party boards the ship. Elisa, Tiny, and Nyktos begin asking Charon about his new venture in Hell as the annoyed Charon answers some of their queries, sharing that he found it a lot of trouble to work with other individuals who talk too much.

Along the way, they come across a woman with red hair, swimming in the River Styx as she begs for help. She grips onto the boat and Charon wants to nudge her off. But Elisa halts him but as he explains that all souls in Hell are meant to be there, she relents. Nyktos, on the other hand, is not able to willingly give up helping a person who begs so pitifully, especially because the woman claims to not remember anything. Nyktos asks Charon for his price, and he proceeds to charge double the amount for this last minute addition. Nyktos pays, giving him a soul coin and other items before helping the lady on board. The woman seems scared, but there’s something about her that strikes some of the party as odd. Still she’s allowed to follow them. 

The party finally makes their way to the Red Belvedere, where outside stands a bouncer of sorts. He verifies that the party is on the guest list and informs them of the charges for uninvited guests as well as a mandatory formal attire. Out of money, Wystram attempts to ask Elisa for some, only to learn that the young warlock charges quite an interest fee. Wystram then turns to the other affluent party member, and Nyktos pays for Myrantar, Thalor, the unnamed lady, and for any fancy clothing they require. Elisa pays for formal clothing and also for a bow tie as ZipZap demands for one. Tiny pulls out their formal clothes and top hat, paying for the people they brought along as well. With that, the party is allowed to enter. 

Turns out the Red Belvedere is an establishment for gambling. The place is nice and luxurious, unlike the horrible Hell outside. The party is welcomed and drinks are offered. Some of them take it while others refrain. A winged Tiefling flies down, and begins introducing herself. Windfall explains that the Red Belvedere is a place free of the battles that occur in Hell, a place of peace and relaxation. She explains that some areas are still in the process of being fixed as a result of a lack of manpower. What Elisa and Nyktos realise is that the place is filled with Abishai, no doubt the ones who keep the peace about the Red Belvedere. With Elisa’s truesight on, she also notices a particular spot near the dragon statue that catches her attention, though before they are able to check anything out, the doors burst open. 

Two simian demons barge in, looking to Nyktos as they comment in Abyssal that they thought they smelt the scent of a traitor. They reveal how Demogorgon has been behaving after Nyktos rejected her mother’s offer to kill the entire world. But the two demons strike a nerve as they speak of cutting off Houzuki’s head and attaching it to Nyktos. Not one to take comments about her sister, battle ensues. The demons are strong, seemingly able to dispel any magic that they can identify. They are even strong enough to wield powerful magics, uttering a word as Nyktos finds herself in excruciating pain. But the party manages to beat the demons, reducing them to a pile of black ichor. Windfall approaches the party once more, apologising for the interference but also curiously asking if they know of why demons had entered the place. 


Nyktos suggests that that's a conversation for a more private place. Meanwhile, Elisa gives in to the temptation of greed and tries to gather more sympathy, in hopes that Windfall would give them more compensation, though 20 more talons than the rest is all she is offered. Windfall then finally explains the games of the Red Belvedere and of the conversation rates from Talon to Gold. As they accept the talons, the party finds themselves cursed, the lure of winning weakens their state of mind. Elisa then explores the area she noticed before, finding a hidden entrance that she’s somehow unable to enter. Wystram, the most disciplined of the bunch, asks Myrantar for her opinion. The Drow shares that gambling is something done by those of the lower class, suggesting that the party refrain from partaking. Though they do not have much of a choice, as when asked Windfall reveals that only members who play their games well, get to enter more private spaces. It seems like the party has to gamble and well should they wish to get the army they were here for.
