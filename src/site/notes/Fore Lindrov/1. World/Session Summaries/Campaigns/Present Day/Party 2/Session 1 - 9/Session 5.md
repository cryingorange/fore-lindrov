As the party speaks to Godmore, Untuk Untuk  drinks continously at the inn. However he realises that he cannot get drunk and demands that the innkeeper give him their strongest drink. They retreat down to the cellar and bring him an entire barrel, which he downs in one go. But once again realising that he cannot get drunk for some reason, he demands to be allowed into the wine cellar. Which the innkeeper strangely obliges to, and he spends the rest of his time drinking every bit of alcohol in the cellar while trying to come to terms with what happened to him previously when he died.

Meanwhile the party is invited to dinner by Godmore and are met with a lavish meal of chicken, beef and pork. Despite being highly suspicious, the party decide that it may be better to just oblige Godmore and investigate the strange happenings in the morning. They are escorted to their rooms by Huntley, with Ilythyrra  realising that she is slowly going blind again. The party are each shown to a room of lavish design, with marble stone columns, a fine dresser and a beautiful bed in the room. It is however noticed that Huntley's room is beside theirs and beside his is an additional room. As Untuk returns, he and Brom decide to explore the castle after Brom Brom discovers various paintings from his homeland. Myrddin  goes to bed, ritual casting Leomund's Tiny Hut for himself.

Meanwhile, Elisa  turns into a spider, infiltrating the extremely tired looking Huntley's room, with Ilythyrra  watching through the keyhole and preparing to pick the lock the moment Elisa makes herself known. Elisa breaks into Huntley's dresser, finding his personal belongings and a journal. Revealing that his settlement was destroyed and he wandered for a few days before discovering the city, as well as a worn ring.

As Elisa breaks out of her transformation, Ilythyrra immediately picks the lock on the door and enters. They both realise that Huntley, despite being asleep, does not look like he is about to receive the benefits of a Long Rest. Drawing more suspicion, Elisa  casts Detect Thoughts and reads Huntley's mind, discovering no ill will. However she hears another voice coming from the other room, it is confused, and talks about how everything in the area is "wrong". 

Elisa then decides to look into the room beside Huntley's after Ilythyrra  tells her that there is a figure that does not breathe in the room. Knocking on the door, they find themselves meeting a regular looking woman. Who calls herself Barbara, however Elisa  hears her true name, "Amunkhet" who remembers nothing of arriving in the Golden City other than herself wrenching herself out of the dirt and suddenly being in the Golden City. Meanwhile, Brom and Untuk Untuk Brom explore the palace, with Untuk finding a plant that grows by river near his homeland, crushing its branches creates water, highly suspicious, Untuk and Brom try this, but realise that their thirst is not quenched when consuming this plant. They explore further for another hour after stripping the plant bear and tearing it from its pot.

Upon returning, they realise the pot has been moved and the plant has regenerated. They head back to their rooms to share their findings with the rest of the party. Finally, Myrddin comes out of his room, meeting Brom. Who immediately tries to break an Egyptian looking vase inside Myrddin's room. With him citing that it's for an "experiment". The party is then introduced to Barbara by Elisa and Ilythyrra and they all retire to their rooms.

The morning brings a solemn note however, as the party discusses their plan for the morning, Untuk realises that Ilythyrra is not out of her room. He knocks on the door

No response.. He knocks again. No response.. He eventually decides to smash the doors open, the entire party see this happening and Elisa  immediately rushes into the room. They pull the covers from Ilythyrra, realising that she is dead. Elisa sends her animals to investigate the area, with Myrddin casting Detect Magic, discovering the same instance of massive magic that Elisa detected prior. Untuk realises that Ilythyrra's body has a bullet wound, meaning that she was shot by someone who couldn't evne see her.

Elisa's bear discovers a spot that appears to have been mended. The party immediately confront Godmore, accusing him of not being able to protect the party. He then reveals that he suspects that it was Party 1. With Elisa immediately interrogating him, Godmore reveals that Party 1 seems to be in the city, sending other worldly creatures to attack the city. He promises to protect Ilythyrra's body, and sends the party off to investigate these strange happenings. Amunkhet disguised as a woman named Barbara tags along to assist the party. The party then meet Curlia, who say that something has stolen all her belongings. 

The party investigate her home, with Elisa's bears finding claw marks in the room, and Untuk Untuk discovering tracks that lead out through the district with Brom Brom. Myrddin  again attempts to cast Detect Magic but this time there is no burst of magic. The party then track the trail to an oddly abandoned shack that is not as immaculately clean as Untuk discovered in the alleyways and what the party has observed so far. Even more strange there are no houses anywhere close to this shack. Untuk decides to just open the door, being hit by poison darts. The party trail in one by one, finding the stolen goods they are ambushed by Goblins. 

The party quickly dispatch the Goblins, with Brom Dominating one of the Goblins to be interrogated while Untuk maniacally slaughters them. One of the Goblins mentions the name "Fool" and Elisa immediately tells Untuk not to kill one of them. As the party drops one of the Goblins from the ramparts, it attempts to run. Only for a Glyph to activate and blow it, along with the house up. The party survive, but take a massive amount of damage. As combat clears, Myrddin pipes up, telling the party not to be hasty. However it is too late, as Brom attempts to interrogate his dominated Goblin while Untuk calms down. A glyph activates, and once again blows the party up. 

The party then make it down to the hatch in the shack, revealing a shrine of Party 1 with Trinkets. As they attempt to investigate, Elisa  transforms into a bear and begins trying to smash the statues. The entire party attempt to hold her down, with Brom and Untuk managing to stop her, and Myrddin calmly tells her to relax, to calm down so that they may find this party and avenge Illythyrra. Elisa grumpily sits down, remaining as a bear and begins scratching something into the floor for Myrddin. 

The party then decide to go to Godmore. Leaving Brom and Elisa behind in the shack. Myrddin also learns of Elisa's story, how Party 1 slaughtered her entire city and why she seeks revenge. Meanwhile, Barbara, Untuk and Myrddin head to Godmore, informing him of the issue and are teleported with him to the shack. However they discover that for some reason there is housing surrounding the shack now. A strange sight, seeing how they questioned Godmore about it just moments ago. Godmore then goes down to the basement, telling the party to rest and explore the city. Citing that he needs time to access his omniscience to learn of Party 1's plans. 

During Untuk's time in the city, he sees 2 women with the same faces, but upon a double take realises that it was just in his own mind. The party also all strangely realise that the merchants seem to know their name, Myrddin also asks Godmore to see his Library, only for Godmore to ask that they wait 4 days.

The party take their Long Rest and awaken the next day, escorted by Huntley, who looks even worse than before, Godmore tells the party to go to a cave nearby. Untuk asks for some kind of protection, to which Godmore grants him a piece of his radiant energies along with the location of the cave. The party purchase a cart and travel along, pulled by Untuk. However suddenly, Elisa drops to her knees, her Earworm wriggling and writhing, Myrddin's blade begins shimmering and shaking, and Untuk hears the cries of his tribesman, while Brom feels oddly at peace, unwatched by his Patron. Amunkhet, having been forced to reveal herself after the party felt through her disguise after healing her, has realised her Friend has been oddly silent this whole time she's been here. Brom also sees The Outsider, for just a moment. Standing there, as if waiting. He also hears Izeelzee, as if speaking through static, demand that he "Kill Him"

This is then interrupted by an encounter with 3 oddly silent Water Elementals, with the party swiftly defeating them. Without much time to process what's happened they head to the cave, clearing its obstacles. The party is met with the Moribund. With Fool seemingly knowing who Amunkhet is . She teleports them away and Brom is met with Felix. The man who slaughtered his squad, Felix mocks Brom's feeble attempts to stop "The Dark Masters" and Untuk sees the Sunstone left behind by his friend. 

As the party engages in combat, they realise that their abilities are not working. As they are about to be destroyed, Felix retreats and Godmore's "Essence" reaches out of Untuk and saves them. Teleporting them back. He then tells them that had he not saved them, they would have surely died. Citing them to be "weak". The party, slowly losing their tempers then decide to attack Godmore. but not before realising that they're extremely exhausted.

During the engagement, Godmore's voice cycles in and out between his demigod voice and that of a child. He loses his tantrum and summons Party 1. The party is almost killed in a turn by Sylvaerys and Fool's Combination. With Elisa immediately going down in one shot from Sylvaerys. As they swiftly defeat Godmore before they are killed by the apparitions of party 1. They realise that it was all an illusion. Sitting on a broken chair is an emaciated child, no older than 10, whimpering for people to "play" and being "hungry" they see Ilythyrra's drained body, the tethers of their essence attached to the child and 30 - 50 bodies of dead wanderers. All consumed by the Child.

Brom immediately prepares to smash its head in with The Bloody End, while Untuk prepares his axe.  Myrddin cites that this is cruel and unnecessary. With Amunkhet and Elisa suggesting that they drain its essence. As the party argue on the most Humane Way to end the creature's life, they decide to put it to sleep and Untuk slits the creature's throat.  Myrddin and  Amunkhet use their Unseen Servants to gather all the bodies and create a pyre while Untuk, Elisa and Brom wrap up the bodies of Huntley and Ilythyrra. The party then travel off, taking a long rest in Leomund's Tiny Hut, before heading home. However on the way they are stopped by Assassins similar to Ilythyrra, who ask for her Scroll, pass them a few potions and blink away. 

Elisa hangs on to Ilythyrra's gear, as a momento to her friend. As they arrive back home, they give the wings to Dave. Who says this is the first step to restoring him, but for now they need to fix their home. The new members are acquainted with the residents, and they bury Ilythyrra with Huntley.

Elisa places Grace's Flowers in Ilythyrra's grave and carves her name on the walls of the cave using her friend's dagger. The party then meet Pulliver who is having an argument with the other faction leaders of the settlement. With Kazarak losing his temper about the Trolls. The party are then sent through the Underdark to travel to a used path that Pulliver found through scouting missions. Citing that they need tools, perhaps establishing trade would be a good idea. The party make their way out, traversing through the deadly underdark, running into strange Gargoyles that drop items, the local fauna, and finally, an Ambush on a long Warrior with a strange Wooden Cart.
