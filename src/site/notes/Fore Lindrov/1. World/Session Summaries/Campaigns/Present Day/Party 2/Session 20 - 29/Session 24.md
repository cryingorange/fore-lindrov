As the party leaves, they hear a voice in their heads, the voice of Micheal calling on them to complete the first of four tasks that they owe him. He sends them to gain the trust of the MLA’s new potential allies and to then break their trust, all the while disguised as the MLA. It sounds simple enough as the party heads out to do just that, coming up with fake names for the MLA personas. They arrive at a temple of Shar where the entrance beckons them to share their greatest loss. Aurelion relives this and the door opens for them. That is where they meet 4 Sharrans, doppelgangers disguised as human males with solid black eyes that seem almost soulless and empty. The Sharrans task the party to kill a whole settlement of people, leaving one to witness and for them to bring back the sole survivor. The party heads to the settlement, but upon arrival they know that they wouldn’t do it. Watching his party members, Aurelion begins his farewell speech before attempting to kill himself. Naturally, the party tries to talk him out of it, though in his stubbornness he doesn’t listen. Aurelion sprouts his new leathery wings and attempts to take flight but finds himself grappled by Vesper as Amunkhet traps the both of them in a wall of force. Vesper casts death ward on the Sorcerer who promptly starts hurting himself again but is then quickly healed by Elisa. It takes a bit but Aurelion eventually comes to his senses and stops. 

The party heads back to the temple of Shar, this time ready to kill the doppelgangers. But first, they are forced to go through Shar’s tests. They come across a room where they have to live through their worst grief.

Vesper goes up first and finds herself holding onto her two kids as their tiny hands cling onto hers, as they seem to plead for help. She tries to pull them up time and time again but fails. The party attempts to help, both physically and magically but it quickly dawns on everyone what has to happen. They each try to talk her out of it, attempting to convince her to let go of her children as the Celestial begs for their help. Eventually, though it pains her to do so, she takes one last look at each of her kids, telling them how much she loves them before letting them go, watching as their faces morph with the realisation of what she did. Elisa goes up next. She sees her parents with daggers and a fearful look in their eyes as the darkness surrounds Elisa forming the outline of a Mind Flayer. Elisa attempts to speak to her parents but all they hear is the Mind Flayer’s voice in their heads calling them mum and dad. She watches as the fear crosses their face, their concern for their daughter showing. The party has no words, as they all place a reassuring hand on Elisa, attempting to give her the courage she needs to complete her task. And so she does, attacking them, and subsequently watching as they become thralls, partaking in the process herself.

Then it’s Amunkhet  E turn. She finds herself in a situation similar to Elisa  E, though before her stands her sister, Neferi with her blonde skin and kind soft eyes. And Amunkhet herself is a ghoul, the very creature that destroyed her kingdom. Neferi valiantly tries to protect herself as Amunkhet attempts to reassure her, writing on the walls in Urshuen “I will not eat my sister E though it appears in Darakhul instead. Steeling herself, Amunkhet murders her sister and the scene before her morphs. In front of Amunkhet is now her brother, Geb who seems to know immediately who she is. He bows his head, not wanting to hurt his sister even if she was a ghoul. Amunkhet does what she can, granting him the least painful death that she is able to. Then before her stands a sun elf, representative of her people. Once more, the elf is dealt with as swiftly and painlessly as possible before the last person, her love. Amunkhet comes face to face with her partner from all those years ago, Ankhta. Ankhta stands, arms stretched out before a familiar body, Amunkhet  E. Even in her last moments, Ankhta stands to protect her. Amunkhet takes the hits, willing to die before she has to submit her partner to such a fate. The party tries to talk to her, with Vesper pleading for Amunkhet to not let her love’s sacrifice be in vain. Amunkhet finds the courage and with one swipe, her grief is complete.

It’s only Aurelion’s grief left as he touches the orb, the call for grief as the shadows surround him and then…nothing. The space is empty, he is alone, for he has nothing left.

With that, the door swings open and the doppelgangers appear before them, heads bowed as they offer themselves to die. And die they did as Vesper ’s spirit guardians cut them down, her way of taking her anger out at them. With their deaths, Shar appears seemingly praising the party for being echoes of pain and spreading sorrow wherever they go. Before the omnipotent god, the party has no choice but to take it, to listen to her words as she bestows them with gifts.

As they finally leave the annoying temple of Shar, they’re met with yet another annoying figure, the devil known as Micheal, here to claim his collateral. They are then forced to re-negotiate their deal with him and they come to the compromise of giving him the souls of every chaotic evil aligned demon soul they meet, tieflings not included to be delivered within the hour in exchange for his help with Aurelion  E situation. This time, he calls for only one collateral, a soul for a soul. The remaining members hesitated, Elisa was just about to put hers up when Amunkhet offered her own instead, this time stating that her collateral would only be collected in 300 years time should they fail. With their new deal in place, the party heads back, their minds and hearts still heavy from their time at Shar’s temple.
