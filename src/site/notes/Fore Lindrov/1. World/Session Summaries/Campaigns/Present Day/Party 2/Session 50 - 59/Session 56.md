They break into the package, finding a large golden egg. Which Dolores shares the history of, she explains that there’s an Archfey named The Golden who always gives a golden egg as tribute to the reigning monarch. She seems hesitant, advising them that this Golden individual is extremely powerful. Though the party has no choice and breaks into the egg to get something useful. They gather their supplies, and Dolores leads them towards where The Golden lives, bringing them to the foot of a giant beanstalk. 

  
Before the party can begin ascending the beanstalk, a boom of magic emanates from the top. Elisa feels her earworm shriek and the party watches as a fissure forms on the surface of the moon and the symbol of Solaris forms on it. The light from the moon turns a sickly yellow, the bright light illuminating all the darkness of the world. The party sees a vision of the purple skinned arm, with golden bangles, belonging to a creature channelling psionic energies. The sheer presence of the moon tests the party’s limits, driving Tiny, Crimson, and Dolores mad.  
  
Tiny feels as if his entire body is riddled with scars and begins pulling at his skin, yelling for the party to make the sensation stop. Meanwhile Crimson’s skin becomes brittle as he starts scratching himself, causing deep wounds. Dolores rushes forth to help Tiny, disregarding herself as she casts spells to try to help, not noticing her feathers falling off. Lucatiel tries to make sense of the madness Tiny is experiencing, falling for it herself as well as she finds that the light burns. In an attempt to protect herself, she pulls out her bedroll, hiding underneath it. Nyktos and Elisa all try to help the rest. With Nyktos first tending to Crimson, managing to stop his madness with a greater restoration. Realising that that’s the cure, she begins drawing on her powers, converting her vitality into magic to heal her friends.  
  
Seeing this, Dolores reaches out to cure Nyktos, breaking her wing in the process. Elisa steps in, mending Dolores Ewing. Dolores offers to bring this news to the Queen, handing the party some treats before rushing off. And just as the party cured themselves of madness, Elisa hears the chanting of mindflayers, all saying “Arrive E She finds herself in front of a mass of eyeballs, power flooding her veins. Elisa is once again able to hide her presence from the other mindflayers and manages to gather that Lux is above where the beanstalk is. Elisa’s armour falls, her druidic markings growing into a more eldritch like mark, her hair having a purple glow to it.  
  
Her blackwood staff cracks and twists, tiny starry specs forming on the staff as Elisa loses her ethereal glow. Lucatiel tries to discern what’s happening to Elisa and sees the powers of her undeath coalesce into her dagger, Atrophy. The party steps towards the beanstalk, with Lucatiel learning of the truth behind the beanstalk, surmising that it is a person placed under a fey curse.  
  
The Golden’s defences activate but the party easily handles that. They speak with the being, Jack, who asks for the party’s help to end his misery. The party agrees and heads down to Jack’s roots. Within the chambers the party explores the area, noting the odd markings all over the place and the presence of three creatures who seem to be following them. That is until most of them, except for Lucatiel, arrive at a level covered in filth and potential treasures. Tiny heads in to dig for treasure and they all accidentally leave Lucatiel alone. It takes a long while before the party realises that Lucatiel is missing. And even then they don’t panic, mostly acting under the assumption that the Myconid had gotten annoyed with them and left. Elisa and Nyktos dig for more treasure.  
  
Meanwhile, Lucatiel has been taken by one of the creatures, a Fomorian Deep Crawler, and dragged off. Fortunately, Lucatiel manages to protect themselves, keeping themselves safe in their resilient sphere. Lucatiel manages to buy enough time until the party themselves draw the attention of the creatures. The party kills one, following the other down to where Lucatiel is when Lucatiel’s defences have been broken and they’re dragged into an antimagic field and promptly swallowed by the giantkin creatures. Fortunately, the party survives the ordeal, saving Lucatiel from being digested. Though their lateness annoys the Myconid who huffs and moves on to the treasure space, hoping to find a treasure like Tiny did with his necklace, but Lucatiel only manages to find a letter. Still the party continues on to Jack’s core, and Elisa slowly undoes the shrubbery that protects it using her newfound inability to touch plants.  
  
The party returns to Jack, who is grateful for their help. Lucatiel naively attempts to dissuade Jack from his other request to the party, offering a chance that they could maybe find a spell to cure him of his condition, but Jack’s mind is made up and he instead offers them safe passage up. During the party’s way up, they witness the many obstacles they would have faced had they scaled the beanstalk instead. Nyktos notices a spark in the air, touching it as it forms a ring of fire, Zhae Tsung greets the party with his usual unhappy demeanour. He boasts of his intellect and tells the party to find the cipher to the Embertome, saying that the source of life is trapped in heated sand. The party makes their way through to a seemingly empty space.  
  
Lucatiel and Nyktos cautiously step out on the clouds, checking the grounds before going past safely. Meanwhile, Tiny jumps with reckless abandon, only to find a lack of ground as they zip past Lucatiel and Nyktos. Thankfully, Planty comes to their rescue, grabbing onto them and placing them on the cloud where it is safe. Elisa ferries the party over, letting them out and as they wade into the hallway, they all end up in different rooms of the maze. Elisa and Zipzap land in the same spot, before them is a field of red flowers. They think of passing through but remembering the harmful effects flowers have, they instead step back.  
  
Just as Elisa does so, she feels a tiny prick on her foot and falls unconscious. Zipzap, not knowing what to do, drags Elisa as best he can to another location. Lucatiel finds themselves in a relatively safe room, alongside Crimson. They cast “speak with animals Eto converse with Crimson, only for the pup to innocently ask where his best friend is. Lucatiel promises to bring Crimson back to Nyktos Eside and checks out the room they are in for clues.  
  
Lucatiel finds a sylvan riddle on the wall as well as a golden egg that acted as a way for them to safely get out of the place, though on the agreement that they would never return to this space. With that, Crimson and Lucatiel set out to find the others. Tiny ends up in a room with a stage and a weird statue. They go up on the stage, playing their pan flute badly to the music in the space as the statue grimaces and casts a magic that makes Tiny dance, unable to stop.  
  
Nyktos finds herself at the exit, panicking slightly when she notices that Crimson isn’t with her. The exit has three keys, and just as Nyktos is about to head out to find her friends, she senses a creature nearby. She attempts to blood bend the creature but it doesn’t work, and just as she’s about to leave, she feels a prick and falls unconscious as Elisa did. Not too long after, Zipzap drags Elisa to where Nyktos is, placing the two near one another before leaving to find the rest himself. Lucatiel and Crimson arrive at where Tiny is, Lucatiel attempts to try to get Tiny to stop and eventually he does before they both move on to try to find the party.  
  
The party eventually gathers at where Elisa and Nyktos are asleep. Lucatiel uses the potion of vitality to cure Nyktos who is in turn able to cure Elisa. The party sets out to look for Zipzap, not liking that he is alone in this space. They first find a room with a Hercules hold set up. Tempted to show off their might, Tiny rushes up to try the mechanic. As they pull the pillars together, a gap in the floor opens up, a small pillar with a box slowly rises up. That is when the party notices the presence of something once more. Attempts are made to attack it and Elisa succeeds, hitting one of them with an eldritch blast. She blasts the tiny fella’s head, hurting it substantially as it rushes off without its hat. The party eventually all gather in a room with a mirror and all attempt to figure out the riddle it poses when all of a sudden, three of the people attacking them show up. They speak in rhyme, throwing disses at the party and further angering them.  
  
Combat breaks out and things are not looking optimistic for the party. The quicklings isolated Tiny, bringing them to another room and targeting them alone, easily taking them down and killing them. Nyktos manages to grab one of the quicklings, holding it in place as it suffers attack after attack and managing to get the final word against the quicklings for once. Though the situation worsens with only Nyktos left awake as the rest of the party are unconscious or dying. Still, they manage to turn the situation around with Lucatiel protecting the party from harm whilst they prepare themselves. Eventually, they manage to defeat the quicklings, with one dying to Lucatiel’s wall of force, the other one to Elisa with her using her telekinesis on the creature, threatening to do horrible bodily harm. And the last dies to Nyktos and Crimson, picked up and kept as a sacrifice for Tenebrous later. The party finds Tiny, bringing them back to life and they walk towards the dead Quickling, picking up his body and snapping his neck clean off, before grounding what remained of them into a fine paste beneath their feet as they swear to kill the Golden and make this place a temple to Bane.  
  
The party then moves on, the obnoxious music still playing in the background as they solve the riddles. Lucatiel solves the mirror riddle, Tiny the strength puzzle, and Nyktos the performance stage. They then double back to the room of red flowers, picking some with Elisa’s help. Nyktos ends up with a vial of powder of choking and sneezing whilst Lucatiel has a bunch of it in her bag of holding, just sitting there, a hazard waiting to happen. They manage to leave the maze, ending up at the front steps of The Golden’s place. Elisa and Lucatiel are immediately interested with the rug as it cleans their feet magically. They promptly steal the rug. Tiny goes to push the door open but finds their hand stuck to the door as it speaks out to them demanding for identification. Elisa manages to trick the door into believing that they are The Golden’s guests, though the door demands payment.  
  
Elisa tries to give them the rug they just stole, but that doesn’t work. Tiny pulls out a bottle of wine, finally managing to meet some of the door’s demands but it wants more. Nyktos pulls out 2 other bottles of the same fine wine, then offers a third, giving the doors two bottles of wine each. Fortunately, they accept and the party is allowed in. Inside, they admire The Golden’s home, and Tiny follows Sandy’s scent once more before immediately getting sidetracked by the smell of good food. Both Tiny, Elisa, and Zipzap are enamoured by the food whilst Lucatiel tries to suggest otherwise.  
  
Nyktos is immediately attracted to the mirrors, attempting to see if it’s anything magical like the other mirrors they’ve seen thus far. Lucatiel crawls up to a spot where the vegetables are kept and through their riffling find Psilofyr there. He speaks to Lucatiel, inviting them to meld, giving them the boon of spore spreading.  
  
Meanwhile, with Elisa’s help, Tiny drinks the soup in the kitchen, finding no ill effects. With that, they head on to find Sandy. Lucatiel and Nyktos are suspicious of the ground, as one of them climbs the walls and the other skirts around the edges of the room, avoiding the marble. Whilst Tiny, Zipzap, and Elisa confidently stride in. As they step on the marble, a trap activates, paralysing both Tiny and Elisa. Fish-like creatures spring up, all seemingly empowered by the light from the yellow moon as a series of explosions occur, hurting the party badly, with Crimson in exceptionally bad shape. Lucatiel and Nyktos try their best to save their companions, managing to save Elisa and eventually Tiny without anyone having to die. It’s a close call but the party manages to defeat the fishes passing through yet another of The Golden’s defences.
