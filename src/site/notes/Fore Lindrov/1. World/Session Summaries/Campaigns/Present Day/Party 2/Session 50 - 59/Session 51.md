As they walk through, they appear before the gate. Tiny, being new to the situation, walks through the unactivated gate and continues on. The rest pause, calling out to Moulder who shares that his master said that the party would be coming back and without hesitation opens the gate for them. They make their way in, getting Emil and quickly rushing back to Kislev. On the way, they come across the familiar waterfall, the entrance to Dulogo. Nyktos pops in for what is intended to be a quick stop. Sigrid confronts her and Nyktos shares what the Lord of Blood has said, angering the Frost Giant who thought that she should have been the one to lead the people of Dulogo.  
  
Nyktos tries the nice approach, though her attempts to reason with Sigrid fall on deaf ears as the Giant roars in anger. From outside, the party hears rumbling, though unsure of what the cause is. Elisa heads in, phasing through the door and seeing Nyktos surrounded by the people of Dulogo and a very angry Sigrid. She phases back out to inform the party, and they all spring into action. As Sigrid makes a swipe at Nyktos, she loses control of her body, her blood bended by the elf before her. Tiny rushes to the door, forcing the stone door open with their bare hands and holding it up to allow the others through. The people of Dulogo attack, attempting to kill the woman who so brazenly entered their home and declared herself their leader. Nyktos tries to scare the people into submission, though the lack of any show of violence doesn’t help. Houzuki and Crimson readily attack the people of Dulogo, both wanting to protect Nyktos.  
  
Upon seeing the giant, Tiny feels a rage, an immense desire to kill her. And as the situation drags on, Nyktos finds herself in a precarious situation with little to no choice. She pulls the blood out of the people of Dulogo, hurting them in the process as she makes an example out of Sigrid. As she does, she hears a voice in her head, Tenebrous, asking her to kill Sigrid. And so she does, killing the frost giant, pulling out all her blood and her heart.  The blood poured from Sigrid begins to take the form of a throne as the Lord of Blood, Tenebrous himself appears before his followers. He declares Nyktos his chosen and will, cementing her legitimacy. The blood of the people of Dulogo begins to boil, searing a mark upon their tongue. They’re told to remember the pain, the consequences of not listening to the Aimorragikos. Nyktos walks over to Sigrid’s body, puppeteering what’s left of her, creating the first Heart of the cult. Following that, she goes over to Callahan, offering the young boy a choice to be the first finger of the Black Hand. The confused and frightened child nods his head as his middle finger corrodes, replacing it with a blood finger through a painful transformation. At the sight of this, both Dremnir and Pyrite leave. Nyktos commands the followers to listen to Callahan, naming him the first Finger of the Black Hand. At this, Tenebrous produces an item, a hand, capable of summoning cultists when she needs them. With the item bestowed, Nyktos commands the followers to bow to their god. With that, he then leaves. Nyktos and Houzuki speak, with Houzuki being relatively calm at all that has happened. Meanwhile, Callahan stares at the Heart longer than one would. When asked about it, he clarifies that the Heart speaks to him, informing him of what Nyktos had just relayed to him about preparing everyone to leave at the drop of a hat. With that, they leave. 

  

Outside, Dremnir and Pyrite share some words, both unsettled by the blood cult they just saw. Dremnir advises Pyrite to not lose his heart, for it seems like his party clearly has before walking away, leaving the party once and for all. On their way back to Kislev, they meet two figures, one of which is a girl who eagerly waves at the party like she knows them. Elisa speaks to the figure in her mind, asking who they are and it is revealed that the two are Holly and Lyod. The two explain that they wouldn’t be able to join the party now, needing to lay low until it’s time for the big fight. Holly passes the party a stone, a means of communication that Banecroft wouldn’t be able to intercept. They also offer to take Viona and Theron for the time being. They leave their allies and head on to Kislev. Back at Kislev, the atmosphere is heavy, a feeling of loss and despair hangs over the people. They rush to the Baron and Baroness Ehouse, who meet them with a fair bit of hostility. The couple is stressed, worried about their missing son and blaming the party for it. Pyrite attempts to have the couple refocus by reminding them of the upcoming war, though that falls on deaf ears as the couple express their willingness to damn the village for their son. Nyktos manages to gather their attention by boldly proclaiming that she has an idea of where their son is. This draws them in enough where they ask if the party would save their son, to which most of them agree. With that, the party aren’t arrested and are allowed to leave the town. 

  

The party must now decide, Ilya or Anastrasya. Pyrite suggests going to Anastrasya first, valuing the lives of the many over the life of one. Meanwhile the rest of the party express their desires to save Ilya, not wanting to risk a child’s life when they could save him. Convincing Pyrite that they could tackle both in one day, the party finds Emil and with his directions, they head to the den of the Blood Moon clan. Along the way, they come across a series of animal corpses, dead but bearing no wounds. Nyktos realises that the corpses look like they have had their strength drained, whilst the others find wolf tracks. Nyktos warns the party of Shadows, a type of undead capable of stealing one’s strength. As they travel, they hear a rustling in the distance, worried it might be wolves but are surprised to see a squirrel launch at them, armed with a needle as it attempts to attack the party. The squirrel speaks, accusing the party of harming Beowulf. But the party is quick to clear up the air, clarifying that they are instead Beowulf’s friends and this calms Sir Rattatasker Julius Hettingworth who then asks where Beowulf is. The party proceeds to tell an extremely dramatised story of Beowulf’s encounter with a dark deity, accompanied with graphics provided by Tiny. They share that Beowulf was alone, fighting a dark deity and his hundreds of thousands of cultists. And how he had decimated them singlehandedly with nothing but a giant boulder and his strength of thirty men. And just when the dark deity attempted to pull a trick, targeting an innocent little girl, Beowulf dashed to the little girl’s side, covering thousands of feet in a single blink of an eye. The sheer force from his steps shook the earth, felling the cultists. Knowing what must be done, Beowulf grabs the dark deity with his bare hands, bringing the foul creature over the edge. It is said that he still fights to this day, locked in an endless battle against evil. The party’s story is extremely convincing, easily captivating both Sir Julius and Emil who believe the tale wholeheartedly. Sir Julius promises his blade to the party’s cause, that he and all the animals will turn against Banecroft and take him down once and for all. Elisa tries her best to convince Sir Julius to return with them, luring him in with snacks and promises of trees. Though the good knight does not agree, he has also yet to reject the offer, leaving it open for now. With that, Sir Julius, with his cheeks filled with snacks, bids the party farewell before dashing off to gather the rest of the army. 

  

They eventually reach the den, a cave carved to resemble a wolf’s maw. As they approach, shadowy wolves come out, looking ready to attack the party. Elisa casts speak with animals, learning that the wolves hunger but not for regular food, for lifeforce instead. She pulls animals from her bag of tricks, gathering them up and explaining the situation to them, apologising for what she’s about to do. She offers the animals to the wolves, allowing them to consume the lifeforce of the poor animals who once again suffer the fate as sacrifices. 

  

With that, they pass through, entering the cave where sounds of bad flute music echo through the halls. They meet Skennis who suggests that they leave but the party isn't there to listen as combat ensues. They knock out three of the werewolves. Continuing on in where they bump into Zuleika and an elderly werewolf is blatant about her dislike for the party. The elderly werewolf points at Pyrite, mocking his weak look as he attempts to assert himself. Zuleika and Emil are reunited, both rejoicing before Zuleika shares that she’s secretly brought a silver dagger into camp, stashing it away with their other storage. The elder lady chastises Zuleika’s actions but the party simply ignores her and continues on. They quickly raid the storage room, with Elisa easily pulling the silver knife out of the deer.

  

The party rush to the sounds of cheers, entering just in time to see Kiril holding up Ilya’s hand, declaring him victorious as they stand before the corpse of a boy about his age. The party halts it just in time, as conversations begin and Emil declares a challenge against Kiril, pack versus pack. The party is tossed into combat. Battle is tough, for the first time in a long while, the party has to refrain from killing their enemy and reserve their strength to fight Anastrasya later. The situation isn’t optimistic, with Zipzap almost killed and Elisa in a worse state than normal. Still the party perseveres, knocking out two of the werewolves before Houzuki is also knocked out. Nyktos channels her magic on Kiril, in an attack that should have been the killing blow but yet he stands. At this point, Emil shouts out for the silver dagger, alerting Kiril to the fact that the party has access to his one weakness. He begins to retreat, backing out towards the entrance. Houzuki uses the wand of winter, casting an ice storm to hinder his escape as Elisa and Zipzap teleport to him, silver dagger in hand as she tries to stab him but he dodges the attack. Pyrite teleports to their side, knocking Kiril out with a sleep spell. Elisa, tosses the useless silver dagger over her shoulder, pulling out her screaming dagger, stabbing Kiril and consuming his soul. With Kiril dead, Emil is now officially the leader of the Blood Moon clan. The party reaches out to Ilya, with Pyrite curing him of his lycanthropy and getting ready to take him away when Emil stops them. Emil clarifies that Ilya is now a part of the pack and not one to be taken away. But the party reminds Emil of the bigger picture and he lets Ilya go. Though the party aren’t happy with that, watching the fearful faces of the other victims, children kidnapped by the werewolves and forced to be turned. The party argues for the lives of the children, and when peaceful talks don’t seem to work, they remind Emil of what they’re capable of. How they can unseat him just as quickly as they put him in charge, of how it wouldn’t be wise to mess with a party that has the backings of gods. They succeed, freeing the unturned children from their cages. The party speaks with the children. Out of the four, there is a little boy and a teenage girl, Gar and Stal who are from Kislev. But there are two others who are not. Rivi is a young girl from Vallaki who seems to have been kidnapped before the festival of destinies, blissfully unaware of her family’s fate. Lastly is a 12 year old boy named Yale who bears a blank expression. The child seems lost, like he had nothing. Elisa reads his thoughts, seeing that Yale had witnessed the death of his family, how the werewolves had killed them and kidnapped him. Both Elisa and Nyktos rush up to hug the child, offering him a place at their home if he wishes. The kid speaks, expressing his intentions to kill them all. They’re about to leave when Ilya steps out of the cave, the moon light shining upon him as he screams in pain. Panic takes hold as they rush Ilya back in. They try examining him, finding no trace of magic or marks left on him from Kiril. Yet, it seems light moonlight hurts Ilya, that the curse Kiril had bestowed on him was still present. The party tries to think of ways to protect Ilya but nothing that they can do would keep him safe. That’s when they begin to hear someone speak. Who else would it be but they devil they know, Mordicael appears out of nowhere, offering the party another deal. Tiny is immediately interested, asking questions as he did before. Nyktos entertains the idea, clarifying terms whilst Elisa desperately attempts to reel her friends back in again. Though Nyktos notices there’s something odd in the way Mordicael looks at Elisa, a smug smile as he seems almost certain that Elisa would seek him out one day. Mordicael offers to provide insight on Ilya’s curse, in exchange for a simple task, to kill a devil. Though it is not any devil but Basatur, the Orthorn that has been working with the party thus far. At this point, Nyktos is over the deal, annoyed with how Mordicael constantly speaks in riddles. She joins Elisa who shares with Tiny who Basatur is, convincing them that Basatur is better than Mordicael as he did not attempt to coerce Elisa and her then party into a deal when they first met. Elisa warns that a devil contract never ends as the devils will constantly add hidden clauses. She then asks Tiny if they want to be a big dragon who serves a devil and not himself. This persuades Tiny to drop the matter. 

  

Meanwhile, Pyrite speaks to the one directly involved in the matter, Ilya. He asks Ilya what he wants, if he desires for the deal to be taken. This puts the kid in a spot, as naturally he would want to be rid of the curse but conversely he doesn’t want to be the reason that someone dies. Pyrite turns to Mordicael, asking instead for a complete cure. Naturally, the devil is delighted, expressing that more must be exchanged for a cure. Mordicael suggests nine good souls, that Pyrite should give him nine good souls, one for each of the layers of hell. Pyrite pushes back, not wanting to kill innocent good people and so instead, an agreement is made for 9 evil souls of Mordicael’s choosing. Once again, Pyrite turns to Ilya telling him that a deal with a devil never ends well. That he is in no position to ask Ilya to sacrifice himself to not take Mordicael’s help. Instead he promises that should Ilya not take the deal, Pyrite will try his best to help find a cure, suggesting to make Ilya sleep and keep him out of the moonlight till a cure can be found. Nyktos does not appreciate the amount of pressure Ilya has, turning to Pyrite and telling him to take the deal if he wishes to, but to not put it on the child, that Beowulf didn’t do that. In the meantime, Mordicael does what he does best, allowing a contract to appear before Ilya, tempting him with a cure to his curse. Just as Ilya picks up the pen, so does Pyrite as he signs the deal without reading through it. With the contract signed, Mordicael smiles and reminds Pyrite to inform Dimitri Kreznov to sign his end of the contract. When it’s pointed out that that wasn’t a part of their deal, Mordicael simply points to the part of the contract that states just that. Ilya finds himself afraid, concerned for what he’s gotten his father into but the party assures him that it’s not his fault and they head back to Kislev. Back at Kislev, the Baron and Baroness are more than happy to have their son back, with the Baron stating that any price is worth it as long as their son is back. The party rests for a bit and Alastair comes to speak with them, casually mentioning how Hayden has been speaking with Beatrice more as of late. But the party pays no mind and soon gets ready to set out for Anastrasya’s camp. Before they head out, Elisa leaves Flipflap to accompany the children, leaving them in the care of the Baron and Baroness. While Nyktos tells Houzuki to stay back. Houzuki speaks up but drops the matter quickly. Nyktos hands her a flute, entertainment whilst she’s gone and also hands her the bag of holding that contains Ilenti, though she neglects to tell Houzuki that. Instead, she simply states that the bag has 2 dead angel corpses and that it would be wise to leave it alone. To ensure that her sister isn’t alone and sad whilst she’s gone, Nyktos suggests that Houzuki find Markovia, an old friend of Beowulf’s. 

  

With that, the party set out once more. They make their way to the camp easily, but along the way spot a group of undead targeting some ravens. Nyktos wants to help, being a shadar kai and all but the urgency of their current mission has her turning her back on the Raven Queen, bearing the guilt that comes with it. As they get close to the camp, Pyrite turns into a bat and does some reconnaissance. There, they learn that there are two Anastrasyas in camp as well as the massive scale of her trained army. The party then plans on how they’d be able to take Anastrasya down, quickly coming to the conclusion that it would be impossible to kill her in the camp. Pyrite shares about the carriage that’s heading into the camp and the party decides to use the carriage to sneak in. Elisa casts invisibility on everyone and they prepare to board the carriage. Nyktos sticks onto the carriage from underneath, whilst Pyrite easily lands on the roof in bat form, Tiny hops onto the side of it, managing to cling onto it as it drives. Meanwhile, Elisa waits by the treeline near the gate, entering the camp easily as well. Elisa splits from the group, taking the opportunity to investigate one of the houses where she finds ghouls eating. Some of the materials around the dead body catches her eye as she pulls out a piece of paper, a poster from the festival of destiny. It seems that the victims of Vallaki have been made food for Anastrasya’s army. The rest follow the carriage till it stops, overhearing the conversation between Anastrasya and Asher. They learn the dynamics between the two, that they both hate each other. But the interaction is useful as the party learns that there are three Anastrasya’s in camp. Before Asher leaves, he speaks in Abyssal, extending his help to the party should they wish to kill Anastrasya, looking at the invisible party members as if he knows that they’re there. The party follows and are soon brought out into the woods, far from Anastrasya’s camp. 

  

Asher suggests laying a trap, using Anastrasya’s love and care for her fellow “sisters Eto lure her in. When the party shares that they’ve met Ludmilla and had to kill her, Asher simply remarks that it’s a shame her corpse isn’t here. He asks the party for something of Ludmilla’s and the plan begins to form. The pets would return to Kislev, dropping off Crimson with Houzuki whilst Wild and Zipzap came back with the bird statuette. Meanwhile, Tiny begins working on a dummy corpse, using the materials available to them to create a very realistic looking replica. Nyktos helps, using the weavers Etools and textiles she got from her mother to create “Ludmilla’s Edress. The result is a convincing corpse that the party hangs from a tree. Behind said tree is Zipzap, who uses his ability to mimic Ludmilla’s voice. As Asher leads Anastrasya in, he shows up with two of them. The first responds as the party expects, whilst the second one is more sceptical. But as they’re all in place, the party springs their trap, walls of stone surround the party and Anastasyas, though one of them manages to escape even with Asher’s attempts to halt her. Worry courses through them as they now have to identify the fake immediately and kill her as soon as possible. Anastasya uses her vampire spawn charm to charm Tiny first, making them see her as their trusted ally to protect, prompting them to attack Pyrite, landing two fatal blows. Elisa uses her truesight to point out the real one but is soon also charmed by Anastrasya. With half of the party charmed and Pyrite severely injured, the situation does not look good for the party. To make matters worse, Anastrasya casts a circle of power, blocking out Nyktos Espirit guardians. It is at this moment where the Beast of Darkness speaks to Pyrite, asking him if he would risk the lives of his allies by not using the Blade of Agony. Finally, Pyrite gives in, drawing the weapon and attacking Anastrasya as the armour grows around him once more, this time the maw full forms as the Beast of Darkness takes over, mercilessly ending the clone, freeing Elisa from her charm before swiftly ending the real Anastrasya as well. As Anastrasya falls, Pyrite throws his head back, creating the imagery of a laughing beast. The party holds up their word, trying their best to reel Pyrite back in just as they said they would. Fortunately, Pyrite manages to hold the beast back, though his connection to the beast seems stronger than ever. 

  

The party head back the Anastrasya’s camp, looting all the items. Elisa heads back to the houses she had previously seen, now witnessing multiple dolls of her friends Nyktos and Ilenti. She ignores them and takes whatever shinies she can find before they all gather at Anastrasya’s tent. They head in and Elisa immediately opens a chest, triggering a trap that hurts the party slightly. On her desk, Nyktos finds what would have been a present of raven feathers from Anastrasya to Ludmilla and pockets the feathers, needing to make up for their deaths later. The party gathers what they can before heading back to Kislev where they see Kashin by himself. This strikes them as odd and they speak to the Death Kiss to learn that apparently Hayden wishes to be turned into a vampire and told Beatrice to go to the church alone as a sign of good faith. This is incredibly out of character for him and Nyktos rushes off to the church at once. The rest stay back, trying to learn more from Kashin and Elisa convinces the Death Kiss to follow them but stay at the steps of the entrance so that he doesn’t disobey what Beatrice told him to do. Meanwhile, Pyrite heads to the Baron, informing him that Ilya had been bitten by Kiril resulting in the devil’s deal that he took. When presented with the contract, Dimitri signs without a moment’s hesitation, anything to save his son. The party all convenes at the entrance of the church where two guards and the Wachter staff stand. When questioned, they immediately try to deflect, fibbing about the structure being dangerous and how the Baron forbids anyone from entering. Their words do not convince the party and to make matters worse, Elisa’s detect thoughts reveal that they are simply stalling for time, attempting to delay the party such that their mission can be successful. The party knocks out some of the people and Nyktos makes the rest sleep as they rush their way into the church in search of Beatrice. Nyktos arrives first, coming to a scene of a paralysed Beatrice and Hayden with his dagger ready to kill her. She tries getting him to talk, buying time for the rest of the party to show up. And as they do, they all try to talk Hayden down but he is insistent. He then takes the plunge, sinking his dagger into her heart, ending her life as the party tries one last time to stop him but fails. Alastair appears, sensing the loss of Beatrice and swears vengeance on all people. Nyktos and Elisa immediately rush to Beatrice’s side, the cleric pulling out a diamond and with Elisa’s approval, casts revivify, the two ready to face the dread realm if it means bringing back their friend. Pyrite calls out, time stopping as he speaks with Mordicael. Pyrite requests for Mordicael to rewind the past thirty seconds. To which Mordicael agrees to rewind time, reverse their greatest mistake, though only Pyrite would have the chance to move. In exchange, Pyrite has to complete a simple task, to tell *her* to take the deal. And for fun, or simply because he could, Mordicael toys with Hayden, breaking his fingers and neck. Mordicael drafts yet another contract before Pyrite, allowing him to sign it and signing it himself as well. Time continues as they all see Pyrite appear before Hayden, ending his life. 

  

Nyktos rushes up to Beatrice, curing her of the paralysis before pulling her in for a hug, relieved that she didn’t die. Beatrice numbly returns the hug before standing up, walking to Andre who curses and swears at Beatrice, calling her names before he can’t anymore as his head is sliced off his neck. Beatrice turns her attention to Molly and Misha, with Molly begging for them to spare her daughter. Nyktos jumps in as well, begging Beatrice to spare them, saying that the mother and daughter are only there because of her and her sister. But Beatrice is tired, tired of being targeted, tired of losing control. She believes that nothing would stop them if they ran, that sooner or later, they would come and try to kill her again. At this moment, Zipzap and Kashin arrive and Beatrice commands Kashin to kill those outside which the Death Kiss agrees without hesitation. But he’s stopped by Elisa who holds onto him, apologising for stopping him. It’s only when Kashin innocently asks why he has to kill those people that Beatrice softens. Elisa reminds Beatrice of the good people they have met, of everyone at home, hoping that this incident won’t change the way she views people as a whole. Meanwhile Pyrite, who knows what'll happen if Beatrice dies tries to talk Beatrice out of it, framing humans as these selfish and horrible creatures, telling Beatrice to be better than people. Though it does not work. Pyrite turns his attention to Molly and Misha, asking them why they had to do it. Molly explains their plight, how her husband was killed because of vampires. Nyktos speaks to them in Elvish, telling them that if they keep fighting, they’ll die. But the two are ready to accept that, after all it’s impossible to simply let go of such a matter. Having tried their hand, Elisa, Nyktos, and Pyrite have no solution for the matter and are mostly okay to allow Beatrice to do as she wishes. The only one left protecting Molly and Misha is Tiny. Tiny stands before Beatrice and states that they will not allow her to kill them. Desperate to find a non violent way to stop this, Tiny does the one thing no one expects of them; pray. Tiny calls out, resolute in their desire to stop this incident, to save the lives of everyone involved. A voice reaches out to them, asking if they wish to impose their will, if they’ll dominate this land, dominate those around and crush their opposition if they do not listen, to speak his name if they agree. And for the first time, both Tiny speak in unison, “Bane E And in that moment, Tiny finds themselves able to dominate all those around them, they demand for Beatrice to let Molly go, and she does. Tiny walks over to the mother and daughter, commanding them to stop hunting Vampires and despite their feelings about the matter, they find themselves agreeing. Tiny asks Beatrice if this much is enough and she agrees, telling him to do the same to those outside. With that, he has Beatrice promise to spare those people, and the situation comes to a close, somewhat peacefully.
