The party takes some downtime, with Elisa making some flowers for Nari while Untuk resigns himself to drinking at the bar. Brom also approaches Nari, apologising for the inconvenience and telling her more about his order and how he was raised. Nari tells him that in this world, doing their best is all that they can do and that he is doing well, something that he tells to her as well. Both Elisa and Brom offer the party's assistance in helping to find the scroll and Nari says she will come look for them once she finds out where it is.

In the meantime, Amunkhet spends some times scribing some scrolls, yelling and being annoyed at the work. Eventually both Brom and Amunkhet meetup with Elisa at the entrance of the city, finding her leading a massive dinosaur with Goodberries, revealing itself to be a telepathic caveman with no name. Someone that the party names Friend-Friend, at Amunkhet chagrin. The party then decide to take on a quest from a Duergar named Durn Cragsheat, who employs them to acquire sandstone from a faraway place in the Underdark. 

The party also learn at this time that Friend-Friend is almost as good a tracker as Elisa. He leads the party to their destination, fighting off Ropers in the process, and arriving near the cave where they find an abandoned cart with 2 Dwarven Bodies, their throats ripped out and their bodies missing chunks of flesh. Friend-Friend notices this looks like some kind of predatory kill and Brom finds footprints leading out. The bodies lurch and lunge at both Friend-Friend and Brom, with Elisa immediately teleporting them back.

The party subdue the 2 Ghouls and knock them unconscious. With Amunkhet recognising these symptoms. Signs of the disease that wiped out her kingdom. She suspects that the disease may be airborne, and during the party's discussion on what to do due to the virality of this disease, executes the 2 Ghouls. She tells the party about the disease and they track the footprints to a nearby tomb. Revealed and recognised by Amunkhet to be one of her homeland's. Sethos posits that during the Fall, the changing landscape may have buried the tombs, and with the footprints leading into the tomb. Amunkhet asks the party to help her quell the intruders that have invaded the sacred place of her people. 

The party agrees and they head inside, solving a variety of Egyptian puzzles and Amunkhet summoning a few Undead Skeletons. Sending Sethos into the final chamber, he triggers combat. Brom manages to jump over a flooring of traps that he spotted, but is stuck due to a lack of a running start. Friend-Friend is teleported by Elisa and opens the door. Revealing Ghouls plundering the tomb, feasting on mummified remains, during battle the Captain of these sentient Ghouls summons a Khopesh as its spiritual weapon, magic that is recognised by Amunkhet to be from her culture. The party swiftly take down the Ghouls, but not before Friend-Friend is bitten multiple times by the Ghouls. The party decide to quarantine him as Amunkhet burns the body of her people and Brom buries the tomb for them. Never to be plundered again. 

The party travel back to Karaz-Andor, with Friend-Friend being afflicted with signs of disease, attempting Lesser Restoration on himself, but to no avail. The party then leave Friend-Friend with Amunkhet outside, as per quaratine guidelines

While Elisa and Brom go to Durn, and receive the reward. Durn leads them on a request from the Quartermaster and they decide to get the quest beforee rejoining their party. Only for a massive explosion to happen at the mines, a terrorist attack by the Cult of Eternal Flame. Friend-Friend and Amunkhet rush out to the site of the explosion, with Friend-Friend turning into a Quetzal and dashing to the danger. Amunkhet meets up with Untuk on the way and dashes along with him. Meanwhile, Elisa and Brom are holding their own against a massive group of cultists. With Brom taking a massive amount of damage, and going down. Elisa barely manages to dodge multiple attacks, despite being heavily injured from 6 cultists. 

With Friend-Friend's arrival, he heals Brom and decides to Polymorph Elisa into a T-Rex, with Amunkhet turning Brom into a Giant Ape. They swiftly crush the cultists, with one escaping into the mines and getting away. The party are instructed by a Cleric to help the people in the mines, as the party rushes through, they save a few Duergar from being crushed by rocks with a creative use of Entangle by Friend-Friend and Web from Amunkhet, repeating this multiple times and saving a Duergar from an attack from a few Cultists. The party also run into a Duergar who has been mutilated by rocks, with Amunkhet realising that removal of the rocks will result in his death. However the party manage to time Mould Earth and healing, saving the Duergar and dragging him out. Before heading to the final chamber where they see a unique cultist, a Flame Wrath, holding a Duergar Hostage. He summons a Fire Elemental, casts Hypnotic Pattern and disables most of the party, with Friend-Friend out of Wildshapes, Amunkhet out of spell slots and Brom barely holding on. Untuk breaks out of his Hypnotic Pattern, exploding into a mass of eyes and flesh, he yells to the party to save the Duergar as his eldritch form destroys the cultists, and he fights back the consumption of his mind and body. The party watch as the Flamewrath escapes, with them barely trying to escape Untuk. Elisa teleports most of the party to the end of the dug out tunnel, with Friend-Friend managing to collapse the mines on Untuk, with him fighting every step of the way, attempting to bring the cave down on himself so as not to hurt his friends. Elisa goes ahead to chase down the cultist, with the Duergar on a Tenser's Floating Disk. However she becomes Exhausted and uses her Wildshape to try to kee up as a panther, watching the cultist leave out of the mines, she tries to push herself to the limit, taking another 3 levels of Exhaustion. The rest of the party arrive outside, realising they've run into a Matron Mother Escort of house Mizzrym. Elisa in her defiance is knocked out by the Matron Mother while the others concede to their fates and the party is enslaved by the Drow.

The party wake up in a caged transport, meeting a young boy named Gili, along with the Duergar that Friend-Friend managed to save. They realise that in this transport is a single, heavily guarded transport holding someone they learn to be named Vanifer. Gili's best friend and "sister". Gili tells them that he was taken from the surface and that a few prisoners named "Topsy and Turvy" help him sneak out of the prison cells at night at the various Drow Outposts to speak to Vanifer. Friend-Friend begins to succumb more and more to his disease as the party split the rations amongst themselves, allowing Elisa to recover from a level of exhaustion. And attempting to sleep in the cramped cell. The party then go into their 2nd day of transport, they meet a Harengon named Tee Wee, who attempts to trick the party into causing a distraction, instead deciding to push down Elisa and accuse her of being armed. He does not manage to escape and is beaten by the Inquisitor into submission and crucified onto the party's transport.

The party then split their rations again, with Elisa staying at 4 levels of Exhaustion, Brom at one and Friend-Friend succumbing more and more to his disease. An angry Drow demands that Friend-Friend get up, as he brings the party out to eat, about to kill him, the Duergar that the party saved causes enough of a distraction that they decide to beat him half to death instead of killing Friend-Friend and the 2 are dumped in the transport. The next day, Friend-Friend dies, having succumbed to his disease. The party realise what might happen to him since he was infected with the disease. Friend-Friend rouses from his death, becoming a Darakhul and attempting to eat the party, Brom keeps him back, however shows his hand by smiting Friend-Friend and is dragged out of the cart as Elisa manages to push Friend-Friend out from attempting to attack Gili, who hides beneath the now dead Duergar's body. With Amunkhet trying to hold him down with her bandages. He eventually begins to feast on the dead Duergar and Gili is dragged out from beneath it. Friend-Friend regains consciousness and realises that he is a Ghoul, swearing to the party that he would never hurt them. The party decide to deal with this later, the Drow have the party clean the shit and piss and blood on the cage from Teewee's Death and have them dump the body over a ledge, which the party begrudgingly do. With a beaten Elisa watching, having being beaten to 1HP for not saying "Yes Mistress" when the Inquisitor was giving her speech. The party then travel for another day.
