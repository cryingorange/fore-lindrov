# Back To Camp 

## Renegotiation

Grimrid asks Jarchigudai to assist them in a renegotiation with Cinderhild, but draws only disappointment from Jarchigudai. Who comments that Grimrid should fight his own battles, and not seek help from her.

The party speaks with Cinderhild once more, with the war general affording them five days time to return with the results from the Hill Giants.

## Orik's Gift

Before leaving, Orik solemnly gives Elisa a necklace that he had forged for his daughter. He pleads with her that if she still decides to go down on this path, that at the very least to take the necklace and be granted the protection that he could not grant his daughter. Elisa takes it and promises to keep it very very safe. 

# Forced March 

## Conversations
During their forced march to the Hill Giants, the party have the following conversations:

- **Moverk & Grimrid.** Morvek and Grimrid talk with Morvek under the impression that the conflict between Dwarf and Duergar is due to the colour of their skin but Grimrid says that it is not.

- **Morvek & Elisa.** Morvek gives Elisa some shoes

- **Elisa & Ivy.** Elisa approaches Ivy when she is carving (it was one of the druids but Ivy failed to remember the face). 
	- Elisa asks if Ivy is saving the face for last, which Ivy responds with sad guilty silence, thinking to herself that what the ring master said is true. 
	- With Elisa's probing, Ivy does open up more about her people, sharing what they did, and no matter how little they had they were happy. 
	- But the physical items she has to remember her people by are left in her forest. 
	- Elisa shares that she used to steal stuff instead of going to her classes or doing her chores. And now the only thing she has left is her pendant. 
	- Elisa assures Ivy that when they return to her forest, she can pick up all of her belongings and with reference to the carving, Elisa says that it is harder to remember as time goes by, but the feelings of the memories, the great joy they all felt, that is harder to forget and that Ivy should focus on that.

- **Morvek & Ivy.** Morvek approaches Ivy, giving her a carving of herself made of the piece of her that he had broken off earlier. 
	- Ivy shares about the original Ivy, and the deal she made with the Devil Mordicael in exchange for stopping a great horror that she had uncovered.
	- She reveals that Mordicael tricked her, and she paid dearly, with Ivy's Soul Latern taken from her in a moment of carelessness. 
	- Morvek understands making rushed decisions, but emphasises that they are a group now, and will watch out for one another, hoping that maybe one day they can save original Ivy. 
	- He espouses that he hopes when that day comes, Ivy would have only the figure he carved to remember these moments by.

- **Grimrid & Morvek & Ivy & Elisa.** Grimrid and Morvek have a beard off, with Morvek revealing that he used beard oils, which Grimrid is affronted by.
	- Grimrid reveals he believes that a good beard requires only a brush and proper beard trinkets to mark important events in a Dwarf's life; the beard oil would corrode the metals. 
	- Ivy, being around the area, gets dragged into the conversation, with them both suggesting that she should grow a moss beard and how that would be great. 
	- Eventually, Elisa steps in to save Ivy, telling Ivy that she would indeed like a carving and asks for a carving of herself and ZipZap. ZipZap does keep the statuette.

- **Roast & Grimrid.** Roast approaches Grimrid, asking him about matters of the heart as she tries to find ways to navigate her feelings for Sir Knight (Mordicael). 
	- Grimrid pretends to know of romance, but his long winded tales are cut short when he realises that Roast is speaking of Mordicael. 
	- He attempts to dissuade her otherwise, telling her that he doesn't love her and that he is a Devil, even mentioning the horrible things he has done to Ivy. 
	- Yet Roast does not see to logic, instead firmly believing that their feelings are mutual, stating that the situation with Ivy and her adoration for Sir Knight are but separate matters. 
	- The others try their best to avoid joining in the conversation, with Elisa and ZipZap pretending to be too busy posing to partake.

- **Elisa's Contact Patron.** As a precaution, Elisa contacts her patron, learning that there will be trouble in their path, some corrupting force, not of aberratic nature, that has already impacted the hill giants.

## Morvek's Corruption

As they continue their travels, Morvek is plagued by a vision, looking at a scene from Serissa's perspective, giving birth as her child claws at her womb, trying to break out of her. Morvek's metal arm falls off, as he feels something inside of him. Morvek transforms into an aberratic creature, his berad turnign to tentacle,s his legs dissolving into flesh and his arm bursting out. 

Morvek holds onto his transformed arm, telling Grimrid to cut it off, who does and helps to reattach the metal arm, staving off the curse for now. Roast checks back with Murram to see if they are safe and learns that they have been locked up as the Giants witnessed their horrific transformation. 

Elisa communicates to her god, learning that all Duergar have transformed again, and they may not all be fully transformed yet but that this corruption is something that the Duergar desire, a desire that many of them like Morvek fight against, that it feels like an addictive euphoria that brings no pain or suffering.

Elisa surmises that this change is somehow related to Serissa's child and its birth, as well as that this birth would endanger Serissa. Elisa asks Morvek about this, focusing on the fact that the Duergar allegedly welcome it. Morvek shares that he's disgusted with himself for enjoying the power that comes with the transformation. 

Elisa assures Morvek that he is still the same and that this does not change how she views him. Morvek accepts that, asking Elisa for a favour for ZipZap to not stare at him like that but Elisa reveals that that is how she is able to see.

# Hill Giant Territory

As the party continues on, they find the plants around them are bigger, signs of the Hill Giants' influence on nature. Elisa tries to have FlipFlap fly above the vegetation but strong winds push them back, additionally, the plants do not die when Elisa touches them despite her curse. 

The party initially wanted to cut down the plants to make a path but this upsets Ivy who is connected to plants in a way and so the party stops that, instead weaving through the plants as best they can. Reaching a part of the Hill Giants' lands, the party follows the signs of blood, finding a large pig that they harvest from. The party does fall for a trap as Morvek hits one of the taller leaves, triggering a venus flytrap that they handle easily enough. 

Continuing on, the party comes across a second group of plants that try to kill them, these ones less kind than before. After the party kills the plants, Roast touches the black ichor from one of them and ends up with a horrible curse which Grimrid realises to be curse from demon ichor. 

He chastises her for touching random stuff, telling her not to do so as he cures her of the curse. With that, the party puts together the corruption Elisa's goddess had warned her about. They continue on and reach Grudd Haug. Morvek tries to enter the place and is immediately hurt by something, a ward of sorts that seems to be aberrations at bay. The party agrees that Morvek can't go in but the rest of them could as Roast enters only to be met with a similar effect. The party decides to rest for the night, settling the issue of the ward the next day.

They also come across a river of ale, which Grimrid flies up to drink from and Morvek makes his way to drink as well. The two also pocket one of the decanters of endless ale, carrying it together, bonding over their love of drinks. 

The party eventually arrives at the outskirts of the familiar Grudd Haug, preparing to speak to Guh in the morning.

As they bunker down to rest, Morvek is struck with a wave of lethargy, falling to the ground as he senses that he needs some time to rest before he is able to join the party once more. Morvek shrinks down into a rock sized object, which is picked up by Roast as they head to rest.
