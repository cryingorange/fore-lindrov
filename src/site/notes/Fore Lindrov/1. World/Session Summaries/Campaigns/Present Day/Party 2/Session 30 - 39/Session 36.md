At the church, the party meets Millivoj, the groundskeeper turned vampire priest by Banecroft. He invites the party in and welcomes the boy though it’s obvious that he’s new to the job. He gives them a tour of the church, and as they move to the grounds, his expertise and passion for the grounds shows through with his in-depth description from the number of graves to the condition of the soil. As they head back in and situate the child in his new temporary room, Millivoj requests for the party’s help with the undercroft, stating that he’s never been down there but wanted help in trying to run the church Lord Banecroft had bestowed to him as best as he could. The party accepts and makes a detour to the study Barcovius had told them about before heading down. There, they find a map of Barovia, with several locations marked out by Barcovius alongside some notes. They pocket the map, alongside the emerald pen that was on the desk. 

  

The party heads down to the undercroft, where they successfully avoid a trap of swinging blades before zombies with radiant energies rise from their coffins. The parties slay the zombies quickly and rest before exploring the rest of the place. They continue forth, finding a shattered statue that Elisa expertly pieces together with her psionic abilities. The party finds an old library of sorts, where Elisa and Zipzap find a Book of Medicine and a journal Barcovius wrote. Meanwhile as Elisa and Nyktos read the journal, Ilenti and Rook are both trying their hand at the locked door. Rook eventually breaks through, unlocking the door after some attempts and are immediately met with vampires. Notably, two of the vampires stand beside a pentagram drawn from blood as they seem to be close to completing a ritual of sorts. The party rushes in. Rook holds off the vampires at the door as Ilenti casts Nathair’s Mischief, successfully preventing the vampire from finishing the ritual for now. Nyktos rushes in, managing to turn the vampire as it runs away, hiding in a corner. The party focuses their efforts on keeping the last vampire from finishing the ritual. Elisa uses her telekinesis to restrain the vampire as she heads into the vault, finding the many items that Barcovius had collected, including the skeletal arm he mentioned in his journal. During the battle, one of the turned vampires flees the room. Zipzap and Crimson give chase, attempting to stop the vampire from returning to the surface and hurting others. Zipzap successfully petrifies the vampire, though not without taking a graze from an arrow. By some luck, the party manages to stop the ritual from happening, successfully murdering all of the vampires. Elisa’s telekinesis opens the vault door and the party collects from the vault. They discover that the skeletal hand is a sunblade, capable of emitting sunlight, one of the vampire’s weaknesses. Afterwhich, Nyktos also learns that the ritual would have summoned a powerful demon if completed, Crimson prestidigitates the floor, cleaning up the pentagram before the leave. The party returns back to the surface, and as Millivoj questions what was below, Ilenti shares about the zombies, with Nyktos backing her up, saying that there were many undead. Rook shares that there were zombies, as Millivoj realises the consequences of what he had done. 

  

The party heads to Herschel’s place and Charlie greets them, inviting them to stay for dinner. The party goes up to Peter/Petunia's room to give her the doll but there is an odd lack of response. Nyktos hears wailing from inside the room and the party rushes into action, concerned for the child. Ilenti rushes downstairs, heading out of the house as she ignores Charlie’s questions. This alerts the mother as she runs up, only to be stopped by Zipzap and Milton who attempt to keep the mother calm. The rest make their way into Peter’s room, finding a winged fiend hunkering over the child, seemingly consuming her soul. The fiend crushes two dolls he had in his hand, dolls resembling Nyktos and Ilenti before attacking the party. Charlie is held by Zipzap whilst the party attempts to deal with the fiend and his summoned shadowspawn. The fiend flees and the party have no chance at chasing him down.  
  
Peter wakes up after some healing from Rook. The party gently questions the child, learning about her “friend E Peter shares that he’s been coming over ever since she stole the dolls of Nyktos and Ilenti, that her friend tells her to be angry at the two shadar kai. She mentions that they needed the money and that they could not risk incurring the Baron’s wrath. The party agrees and heads back to the Baron’s Manor to inform Dalaina and Herschel of their families Ewell wishes. They also learn that the only person who can speak up to the Baron is Lady Wachter, though she does not and her sons are often found drinking at an inn. They learn about the Mad Mage in the mountains and of Bluto, a fisherman who had attempted to kidnap children. From what they know, the fisherman was stopped by a girl with pigtails and is apparently working with her for a trip out to the Lake. Herschel suggests that the party get a drink at the Bluewater inn. Turning in for the day, the party heads to the inn where they meet Urwin Martikov. Ilenti pays for the board and leaves first. Meanwhile, the others stay at the bar, ordering drinks and understanding the local wine situation. Urwin asks for the party’s help to check up on the winery as they have yet to receive their usual shipment. The party agrees to help but turn in for the night. 

  

Nyktos heads up to the rooms, only to hear muffled cries from the room at the end of the hall. She approaches to find her sister, Ilenti, sobbing to herself. Nyktos tries to comfort her sister, encouraging her to speak as the younger shadar kai begins to vocalise her self blame. Ilenti blames herself for all of the misfortunes of her life and opens up about Merlara’s death, how she found Merlara in a chest. Nyktos shares that none of it was Ilenti’s fault, blaming instead the people of their hometown, saying that they were mean long before Ilenti was born. During this, Ilenti brings up Nyktos Edeparture, bringing to attention how that had hurt her as well. The conversation eventually comes to a close, with Ilenti tiring herself to sleep. As Ilenti rests, Nyktos tucks her into bed, brushing her hair out of her face as she whispers an “I love you Ein Elvish to her sister before curling up in her own bed, quietly sobbing herself to sleep. 

  

The next morning, the party finds Alastair, Beatrice, and Kashin in the inn. They bring them up to one of their rooms to talk. Alastair shares what they have learnt about Lady Wachter and his plans to infiltrate the nobles Eranks. Rook shows them the Skeletal arm they had gotten. Alastair reveals a story that he heard of Sylvaerys losing an arm during their visit to the manor of House Nightkin, with Erza, the old Vampire Lord seeing mortals as parts of an art piece than living being. Elisa sighs exasperatedly, her reaction prompting questions from the rest of the party. She shares her backstory with them, about the party that had infiltrated her city and genocided everyone but her, and of how she’s sworn vengeance on them. With things planned, the party sets out to the winery, with Elisa leading them, using the map they had gotten from Barcovius. As they travel, the party spots a Dire Wolf with silver fur. Curious, Elisa walks closer to the wolf, who then speaks in her mind with a man’s voice, “Perhaps you have seen my Broomhilda. EElisa shares this with the party, who are all confused but they let the matter slide for now, seeing as they have no other information. The sisters look around from Rook’s reaction as he pulls out his weapons, wondering if there are enemies nearby. They clarify and learn that rain is not common nor normal in Fore Lindrov, and Nyktos explains the hydrologic cycle to the party. They then continue forth, eventually coming across the family that runs the winery, all stuck in their vineyard for some reason. Adrian introduces the family and explains that evil trees that were once imprisoned people from Lucian’s rule have somehow become alive once more ever since the giant tree in the west grew. The party heads to the winery, and watches as hordes of treants and wood woads approach. Rook heads in to find more treants in the winery, as one of them pours a dark sap into the barrels containing the alcohol. During battle, the ravens that perched atop the winery help the party out. The party deals with the treefolk and finds that the alcohol has been poisoned. Elisa picks up the staff that the druid had, Past that, they search for Davian, the patriarch of the family in hopes that he still lives. Unfortunately, they find that Davian has passed and sees the letter he had written in his final moments asking Broomhilda for help. Nyktos closes Davian’s eyes, and Elisa cleans up the blood, placing Davian on his bed with Rook’s help. In the children’s room, Nyktos and Ilenti find a doll from Blinsky’s shop. The party deals with the doll, cutting it down and removing all parts of it from the children’s room. They then head back to where Adrian and his family were to inform them of the unfortunate news. Upon reaching, the party sees a woman with blue eyes and pale skin seemingly trapped in a cocoon in the tree. The woman greets Elisa and the rest learn that she is Grace, Lucian’s lover. The party learns that the group who had tricked Lucian is the same group that had killed Elisa’s city. Grace shares that Goetia is hiding in the dwarven city, that he had a dagger that could drain the life tree’s energy, resulting in Grace’s current condition. Grace asks Elisa for a favour, telling her to break the branch, to kill all the treefolk and herself, to allow the last bits of their energy to reside within her so that she could see the world she loves through Elisa. Elisa speaks to Grace, sharing how she was the first person to treat her with the kindness and grace that she did, and how she does not want this to be the last way she meets her. Grace shares about an apple tree in the courtside of Lucian’s castle that bears both of their names and asks that Elisa brings her to see the tree someday. As Elisa struggles with what Grace has asked of her, her psionic powers activate, allowing her to see Grace’s memories. She sees Grace’s first memory of her husband and feels Grace’s current emotions, the pain she bears for Elisa. She witnesses Grace’s fight with Goetia, with him saying that he didn’t want to do this before stabbing her. The party offers their help as Elisa has made her decision. Beatrice and Nyktos place a hand on her shoulder, assuring her that she wasn’t doing this alone, that they are supporting her. And instead of the scream of the staff, most of the party are met with silence as they watch the tree withers before them, all surrounding plant life as well. And as Elisa takes in the energy, she begins to change, druidic marks etch themselves on her face, her moon sickle glows and transforms into a wooden staff with a blue gem that glows in the centre, the Spire of Conflux. The broken world tree staff fuses back together as well, forming the Staff of the Woodlands. 

  

With that, the party returns to Vallaki and upon entering they see Millivoj speaking to a nobleman. Then, Rook and Elisa dismiss the nobleman who watches as they head off. The party is soon before the townsquare where Barcovius was held. Eventually, the party is unable to stop it from happening, most of them having been charmed by Vicktor.
