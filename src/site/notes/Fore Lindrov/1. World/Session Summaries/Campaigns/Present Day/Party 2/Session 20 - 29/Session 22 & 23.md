The party returns back to the tavern to rest for the night with Amunkhet swiftly returning to her room as Elisa and Vesper sit for a drink with Aurelion, who actively drowns himself in pints of mead, Zipzap in tow. Elisa breaks the awkward silence, broaching the subject of Aurelion’s shortened lifespan. The sorcerer pulls out a piece of parchment, what seemed to be the start of his bucket list. The party sits through the awkward atmosphere before returning to their rooms. Early the next day, Aurelion places a letter in front of each of his party member’s doors, with instructions for it to only be opened upon his death. Amunkhet is the first to see the letter, noticing the moment it was placed. Elisa and Vesper find their letters as well, and without hesitation Elisa tries to decipher the letter’s content by holding it against the light. She makes out some phrases, and gathers the gist of the message. The rest then make their way down to find Aurelion downstairs, sitting alone at a table with food for his party already paid for. The party joins as the sorcerer puts on a happy facade, smiling as they eat. Aurelion then pulls out his bucket list once more, this time with the first 10 items filled. They awkwardly read it, trying to ease the tension by listing the more easily accomplished items on his list. Their meal is then interrupted as a voice speaks in their mind, urging them to find a person called Lesaya or she’d be dead soon. Confused but concerned, the party leaves to ask around for Lesaya’s location. They manage to do so and learn where Lesaya and her husband, Fedor live. Upon rushing into their home, they find Fedor in a crazed state, feeding on Lesaya as he bites into her neck.

The party does what they can to stop him, though the commotion draws the attention of Alicia Ravenheart to the scene. The party surmises the cause of Fedor’s madness to be his fang necklace and Lady Ravenheart shares that she’d be able to cure him if the party brought back the owner of the fang alive. The voice from before speaks to them once more, offering its help in locating the creature. As time is of the essence, the party accepts the help and they travel on in search of the creature. They come across a caravan with the bodies of a family of four strewn before it. The voice warns them as maw demons appear from under the cart. They swiftly defeat the demons and pay their respects, burying the corpses before continuing on. They soon find the creature, a gnoll who says something about a sacrifice for Scag before the party rushes in to attack them. They take down the gnolls easily and Vesper finds a letter amongst the piles of bodies. The letter informs the party of where Ozul intended to meet and after returning to Lady Ravenheart with the gnoll’s body and fixing Fedor, the party sets out once more to the meeting place where Ozul might have been. 

Along the way, they come across a group of drow whom Amunkhet learns that they wish to find Gauntlgrym and take it back from the dwarves who destroyed Q’xorlarrin. Realising that they’re on a scouting mission, the party are unsure of how to proceed. Elisa and Aurelion believe it best to stop the drow before they can hurt anyone, whilst Amunkhet and Vesper are less eager to attack the drow unprovoked. Eventually, the party picks the diplomatic route and speaks to the drow, fibbing that they were there in search of treasure. During this exchange, Elisa peeks into the minds of the drow who express interest in following and ambushing the party when they find their supposed treasure.

The party then leave, continuing on with the drow behind them. Elisa warns the rest of the drows Eintentions as they finally arrive at a study. The study is similar to one they’ve been to before. Elisa , Amunkhet , and Vesper take their time to carefully search the first room, looking out for interesting items and traps whilst Aurelion, blinded by his desire to find Ozul, rushes into the second room where he is met by two inactive Cadaver Collectors. He pays them no mind, and goes to open the drawer, activating a trap that awakens the two constructs. The party is thrown into battle as the constructs work hard to eliminate the intruders. They manage to pull through, destroying the two constructs before returning to their original search. Elisa finds some pages that are covered in gibberish and Aurelion gathers the journal from the desk with similar looking words. Using truesight, Vesper is able to see through the illusory script, reading the pages out to the party and learning of her husband’s involvement with Godfrey, confirming her family’s arrival to Fore Lindrov years, maybe centuries before she did. She rushes to find the key mentioned in the journal but instead finds a light, an essence that reminds her of her husband and herself. Holding the light close to her heart, she absorbs the essence and before the party stands Vesper in her true form. The party is…visibly weirded out by the transformation, questioning various things like how the Celestial speaks or holds things without mouth or hands. Though they quickly get over their initial concern, moving on to the topic of camels instead.

The party heads back to rest, where they are invited for drinks by Aurelion. They agree and spend the night drinking. By the end of it, Elisa and Aurelion are drunk with Vesper and Amunkhet taking care of them respectively. Elisa cooperates and goes to bed with Zipzap in hand whilst Aurelion puts up a struggle. In his drunken stupor, he starts to see Amunkhet as his fiendish self. Fearful, he runs away from her, casting spells and making a mess of the tavern. Fortunately, they are able to coax him to his room and rest for the night.  Except for Aurelion who proceeds to have a nightmare. He wakes up, his skin red, horns protruding from his head where there weren’t any. In a panic, he summons Atlas to alert the rest of the situation. Amunkhet arrives first, followed by Vesper and Elisa. They’re all confused when the voice speaks to them again, saying that they have a way to help. The party presses for a name, not believing in the voice. The voice introduces himself as Micheal and urges the party to meet him at Dragonsmount quickly before the transformation takes over Aurelion. They polymorph Aurelion into a turtle, and rush off, with Elisa successfully warding off any distractions in their path. They arrive before Themberchaud and his imp, who turns into a cambion, and introduces himself as Mordicael. He offers the party a deal, that they would complete 4 quests for him, and in exchange he’d temporarily halt Aurelion’s transformation. The party outlines every criteria they can think of and have to offer up collateral should they fail to complete the quests. Elisa offers up her god’s bias for her, Vesper offers two days of service, and Amunkhet offers up 50 mummies.

With the deal in place, Micheal holds up his end, seizing Aurelion  E transformation for the time being. Themberchaud then asks the party for their help to uncover the secrets that the clerics of the Iron Tabernacle hold. They set out once more and come across a body of a dead hobgoblin who was clearly tortured before his death, and even had speak with dead casted on him after his death. Following the trail, they find a hideout of the MLA, and Ozul himself in a construct like suit. As luck would have it, Ozul and his men have captured the clerics and are actively torturing them, doing to them what they did to Vokel, the hobgoblin they found before. Aurelion tries to argue with Ozul, urging him to see reason but to no avail. Instead, Ozul offers a trade, he asks the party to find the members of their missing patrol, for their bodies to be brought back. The party agrees and go in search of the patrol. Along the way, Elisa spots some slime that she claims has a psionic ping to it. As they continue, the party finds and defeats some Rust Monsters, though Amunkhet loses her tower shield to them. They manage to find one of the bodies of the patrol alongside several pages of charcoal drawings and a letter. The body rests against a purple metal wall, and Elisa realises that the slime she found earlier was from mindflayers. In a fit, she takes out the slime she had bottled up earlier and stomps it, crushing the bottle and slime beneath her feet.

The party then finds the mindflayer’s base where Elisa interacts and speaks with the Mind Flayer who is prepared to attack the party upon realising that Elisa isn’t a thrall. The party goes in, wiping out the thralls and almost killing the Mind Flayer before he flees the losing battle. The party finds one of the MLA members on a table, his head peeled open and passing on from an infection. They also find two others in pods who seem normal. Elisa and Amunkhet open the pods, freeing the two MLA members who behave suspiciously alright. They claim to not be harmed or infected by the Mind Flayer in any way, though Elisa quickly sees through their lies with her detect thoughts as she learns of their intention to infiltrate the MLA. Realising the situation, the party knows what they have to do and Amunkhet casts hold person on the two of them. One fails and stands rooted there as the party picks up the spiked greatclubs used by the other thralls and bash the man to death. The second starts to run but is turned into a turtle by Elisa. The party then stands before him as the spell drops, begrudgingly murdering the second man as well. With a heavy heart, the party returns with the corpses to Ozul and returns his men in exchange for the dwarves. Ozul shares that the clerics were there to seek a dragon egg that Ozul had stolen, that the dragon egg was how they enslaved the people of Gauntlgrym. With the information, and tremendous guilt, the party returns to Themberchaud, sharing with them what they’ve learnt before heading back to the tavern for their much needed rest.

The party, having spent the last week exhausted, finally takes some much needed time off for themselves and spends two days resting. Of which, Amunkhet spent her time reading, recalling and refamiliarising herself with the ways of the arcana. Elisa spends the days crafting and selling potions, Vesper rests, and Aurelion checks off the 3rd item on his bucket list and makes a meal for the party. 
All rested up, Azran calls in the favour the party owes him for messing up his tavern a couple days ago, he tasks them to seek out books about Maegera, the powerful fire primordial that he named his inn after. Azran also shares a strange rumour he heard that one of the forgemasters, Orik, had returned from an expedition with only one arm. The party doesn’t think too much about the rumour and leaves on their mission in search of the missing religious artefacts of the Iron Tabernacle. Upon their arrival at the outpost however, they are met with the sight of injured and paralysed dwarves and adventurers. Damien spots the party and beckons them over, he tells them how they were attacked and asks for their help which the party readily agrees. Elisa springs into action, healing the injured with her goodberries. Just as they were about to approach the two paralysed dwarves however, a troop of MLA members rushed in, ready for battle. The party tries to help as best they can, killing some MLA in the process.

The MLA quickly realises how outpowered they are as they attempt to retreat. Though the dwarves are not ones to let things slide as they continue their attacks, intent on killing all the MLA members. Not wanting this, Elisa heals the MLA right before the dwarves, as they curse and shout at her for it. Amunkhet then cleverly sets up a fog cloud, allowing the party to secretly aid the MLA as best they can. With the MLA retreated, the party is left to face the consequences as they get cursed out for being dark race sympathisers. Damien speaks up for the party, attempting to excuse Elisa’s actions due to her naivety, though the Dwarves Erage angers Aurelion who proceeds to have a shouting match with one of them. Things eventually die down with Damien’s mediation and the party are allowed to continue on their quest.

Along the way, the party meets a group who demand payment to cross their bridge. This is where Aurelion uses his new found powers to make a deal with them, granting them food and money in exchange for unlimited access to their bridge. In a show of power, he has Brom write up the contract and the deal is swiftly completed. The party then comes across the corpse of an Orc, with their skull and brain empty, a clear sign of Mind Flayers. Elisa feels the weird tablet calls out to her, she connects with it and gathers information of dormant Mind Flayer activity in the mining outpost, all other information has however been erased by someone named Kux’ezak. They take note of the information and carry on. They eventually make their way to the old Temple of Moradin where members of the MLA stand watch, halting the party. They talk their way in, offering to help cure the Duergar of their madness.

To help further assure the MLA, Aurelion offers himself up as hostage. They are convinced and lead the party through, upon which they are immediately attacked by the crazed Duergar. The Duergar are attached to machinery, with metal rods sticking out of their heads as they seem to be controlled by the statue of Laduguer. The party manages to knock out the Duergar and have them treat them before they are ushered out and realise that they forgot to loot any religious artefacts they were sent for. Elisa sneaks back in, cautiously and manages to grab one of the statuette before rushing off. The party makes it back to Targrim where they lie about the situation, not wanting to reveal that they had spared and cured the Duergar. Word travels fast of the return of their religious artefact as the King himself appears to, expressing his desire to hunt and hurt the Duergar for desecrating their religious iconography. In exchange for their efforts, the party is rewarded with a trade agreement with Gauntlgrym. That is when the party learns more of the slaves in Gauntlgrym and specifically a drow slave from house Oblodra, a name that has Amunkhet rushing towards the mining outpost where the slaves reside.

It is there where the party meets Zaraen, a Drow with blue eyes and silver hair and a tattoo of her house’s symbol on her forearm. Amunkhet has an awkward conversation with the drow, inquiring about the drow’s house and if there were any other survivors of her family. The drow asks Amunkhet for a favour to help deliver a letter to someone, but nothing comes out of that for now as they continue their conversation. In the meantime, Aurelion speaks with the foreman, who doesn’t take kindly to his questions. It seems that word of the party’s behaviour at the outpost has spread to the city as well. Elisa then bugs the same foreman, asking him more questions about the slaves and their treatment of them. Finally, Amunkhet inquires about Zaraen as well before the party leaves. They make their way to the Great Forge to meet the Dwarf that Azran spoke about. Upon meeting Orik, they are promptly hired to help him procure something called a Soul Anvil. With detect thoughts, Elisa is able to discern that Orik had made a deal with Goetia, and that Orik wishes to bring back his child, as well as his resentment towards King Bruenor. Orik also shares something about the previous adventurers he had met, a big guy with a sword who claimed that he wanted no part in Orik’s adventure, though not much more was said after that. The party sets out once more, this time with Orik leading them.

During their journey, the party learns from Orik that the Brightmires frequently sell magic items that they’ve found in crypts, a fact that the party, especially Amunkhet, notes for future reference. They then arrive at the sacred temple of Moradin, and see two deactivated Dwarf Golems, destroyed and cleaved from what seems to be a big sword. They step inside and come to find a Cyclops and his band who wish to eat the more edible looking members of the troop. Aurelion plays into the Cyclops' hunger and pride as he writes up his second contract, granting them 30 days worth of food made of human flesh and a crown. In the midst of conversations, Amunkhet asks something of Orik, a question that has him choosing between his city and religion, or his child. And though it is a tough decision to make, Orik does as any parent would, picking his child above all else. With the contract signed between the Cyclops, Eye, and Aurelion, the party is allowed through and they’re now at a door. Vesper sees through the door’s illusion, noticing that it’s been unlocked and sealed with some sort of fire magic. With a quick spell from Amunkhet, the door is open as Orik rushes in to see nothing. The Soul Anvil he was looking for, that he gave up his arm for, is missing. It doesn’t take the party long to know that Goetia must have tricked Orik, that he must have taken the Soul Anvil away. Defeated, Orik heads back, convincing himself that any sacrifice would be worth it if he could have his child back.

Along their way back to Gauntlgrym, the party comes across King Bruenor and Targrim who are promptly cursed at by Orik as he blames them for his daughter’s demise. It is then revealed to the party that the King and Targrim know of what happens when souls pass on in Fore Lindrov, having experienced it themselves. The party follows after Orik, concerned for his well being but the Dwarf puts on a strong front as he hands the party their reward, insisting that they take it. 

The party then heads back to rest for the night, or at least some of them do. Elisa heads back out to the mining outposts, wildshaping into a spider to remain hidden. Unfortunately, she’s quickly found by Zaraen, the drow slave from before who seems quite pleased to meet a spider, believing Elisa to be Lolth. Zaraen goes on about some plans that she has to prove her family’s worth and have their house reinstated. Just as she was about to show Elisa her plans, a voice called out from outside. Amunkhet arrives, interrupting the drow who is quick to rush Amunkhet out, telling her to return the next day. Zaraen then brings Elisa down to a secret lair underground where an abandoned Mind Flayer place resides, complete with a brine pool full of tadpoles and a Goblin captured by Zaraen for her experiments. She shares her intentions to release the Goblin in the city, sowing seeds of unrest amongst the Dwarves before her allies strike and take Gauntlgrym back from them. In her…excitement, the drow takes her time to pray to Lolth, and Elisa escapes from the mining outpost the moment she is able to.

Back in her room, Elisa finds Zipzap running around, unable to sleep without her there. Though she is unable to rest, constantly muttering to herself about what she had witnessed when Amunkhet approaches her room. Upon hearing the mutters, Amunkhet exclaims in shock and the rest of the party wakes up to find Elisa in her traumatised state. They try their best to calm her down, and learn what happened to her. Which, amongst the ramblings and confusion, the party concludes that the drow had hurt Elisa. Aurelion suggests telling the authorities, though Vesper and Amunkhet are much more inclined to take matters into their own hands. Before they are able to commit any murders, Elisa  E god steps in, numbing her emotions towards what had scared her and allowing Elisa to focus more on the important details. With a series of mould earths, Elisa shares a play-by-play of what happened with the drow and the party’s rage quells. They come up with a plan for Amunkhet to retrieve the letter first before planning their next steps. 

The next day rolls around and the party executes their plan perfectly. They learn that Zaraen intended to send the coordinates of Gauntlgrym out to her contacts who would then ambush the city. Not wanting that to happen, the party reaches out to Vizeran for some advice, though upon learning the Drow’s identity, he shows up personally and teleports the party to Zaraen’s tent, capturing her and implying that he’ll torture her himself. With that settled, the party heads to the Mithral Seat to inform the King of the Drow’s plans, lying that she had escaped. The King thanks the party and asks for their help once more as they have an expedition planned to leave in a couple of days to fight the MLA.
