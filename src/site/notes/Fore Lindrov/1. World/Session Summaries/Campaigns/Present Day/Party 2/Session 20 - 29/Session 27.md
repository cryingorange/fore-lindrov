Through a quick discussion, the party quickly concludes that the only way to remove the tadpole was through a complicated brain procedure. They knew that the odds of success were slim but alas it was the only way. They propose the surgery to Daegra, who suggests that Huck should make the decision for himself. To ease any potential concerns, the party lies to Huck, as Vesper pretends to be the one to do the surgery instead of Ankhta. Fortunately, Huck is willing and the MLA leads the party back to their camp, not wanting to stay there any longer. As they arrive at the MLA camp, they’re met with stares. But as Daegra and Ozul reunite, Ozul seems slightly more comfortable with the party than before, seeing as they brought this partner back to him. The party shares about Huck’s surgery as Ozul offers his help and the party readily accepts. Drizzt then approaches the group, greeting Ankhta and asking how the party managed their way there. The party reveals that they had met Catti-brie and Thibbledorf. Drizzt shares some information about the last key to the vault, “Minerva’s key Ethough the party would need to ask Ozul directly for any more information about the vault. They rest up for the night, and the next day proceed on with the surgery as planned. Ankhta sets up a Leomund’s Tiny Hut for the surgery, and Vesper goes to speak with Ozul to reveal some truths. However it seems that he was well aware that Ankhta would be conducting the procedure and seemed alright to learn about Vesper’s true form, stating that he had seen many weird monstrosities in Godfrey’s study.

There are some near misses and the pain does cause Huck to scream and panic as Vesper casts calm emotions to help Huck relax. With a series of careful incisions, Ankhta manages to remove the dead tadpole and fix Huck back to his normal self. With that out of the way, the party then moves on to the location of Minerva’s key. They are met with a weird small tube of sorts. They cautiously test it by sending down a mage hand but eventually, Vesper volunteers to go down first. She enters a room filled with pipes on the walls and flies over the traps on the floor to reach another room. Rook follows down as well and ends up in a different room while Elisa and Ankhta stay upstairs, the party communicating through their telepathic bond. Rook stumbles upon a room with a chalk board that writes “Noire and Ak- Eand as he shares that information, he hears sounds of a construct that starts chasing after him as he runs off to yet another room. Vesper, upon hearing the names of her children, shares with the rest of this fact and tries again to find their rooms and more information about their whereabouts. Ankhta joins them downstairs, quickly linking up with Vesper as Elisa follows suit. The party are soon together and wonder about the rooms, finding more information and useful items as they try to avoid the construct. They quickly come across a room with a desk, bed, and a chest. Atop the desk was a golden reliquary, and inside sat something very familiar to Vesper. It was Chrom’s wedding ring, with the inscription inside that Vesper knew by heart. Beside that was a picture frame that housed a painting of a blue haired lady, it seemed that Chrom had commissioned a painting of his wife during his stay.

They wander around more and come across a room as well as notes of who seemed to be Godfrey’s assistant at the time, they find a time reversal box of sorts. And in a chest, Ankhta finds an array of dunamancy scrolls, most of which she takes. The party

eventually finds one another and arrives at the room Rook had been at before. It quickly becomes apparent that the room belonged to Vesper’s children as the party respectfully stands aside whilst Vesper looks for signs of her children, finding a mechanical teddy bear, doll of Chrom, an emotion mimicry toy, and a healing mushroom toy. The party also comes across a room full of chests, acquiring a bunch of potions and Elisa finds a vial of Dragon’s Blood . As the party makes their way from room to room, they encounter the construct that had been chasing them around the place and are eventually cornered into battle. Left with not much choice, the party splits up, with Elisa and Ankhta safe from the construct as it chases after Rook . Rook manages to lose the construct’s tail as the party regroups and plans their ambush on the construct. As it approaches, Vesper summons her celestial, manifesting a much powerful version of herself. Rook and the celestial focus their might on the construct, quickly beating it down as Rook deals the finishing blow, slicing the construct in half. The party retrieves the orb they needed to unlock the door to the final room. Upon entering the space, the party notices lush greenery and a wave passing over them, they feel revitalised, and an odd sensation of time stopping. Elisa, upon spotting the plants, goes to harvest some of them. It is there that they see a purple skin gnome busy at work, unaware of the party’s presence.

The summoned Celestial approaches, gently alerting the gnome, who surprisingly has no reaction towards the party or Vesper’s true form. She mutters about not being done yet and how she wasn’t expecting Godfrey to call on her so soon. The party reiterates that they were not sent by Godfrey as Vesper introduces herself as Chrom’s partner and mother of the children. The Gnome introduces herself as Minerva and after some quick words with her, it quickly becomes apparent that time works vastly differently

in this room. Minerva shares that she was tasked to create vorpal swords but has only finished one, that Chrom would need them, though she doesn’t know why exactly. Minerva hands off the artificial vorpal blade and her key to Vesper. Elisa and Rook speak with the Gnome, learning of her prowess in building. Whilst Ankhta cautions Vesper that a long time would have passed since Minerva last saw Vesper’s family. Ankhta also informs Minerva of the truth, that it has been millennia since she last started her work. Minerva seems appreciative of the truth, though the party doesn’t wish for her to be left alone, seeing how dangerous the world is. Elisa describes the location of their settlement to Minerva and Vesper sends a quick message to inform others of Minerva’s arrival and the Gnome leaves, safe for now. They return to the MLA campsite as Ozul greets them, curious about the last key. Before handing it over, Vesper probes Ozul, asking a couple questions such as “What if the weapon isn’t the weapon? EOr “What if it’s a person? Ethat weirds Ozul out but once she’s ascertained Ozul’s intentions, she reveals to him that people important to her might be at the weapon’s vault, that they are to her what Daegra is to him.

Ozul stops her, assuring that he understands and that neither him nor his people would harm her family. Ozul brings along 3 other MLA members, Daegra, Jub, and Gobby. The party reunites with the two that they have saved before. They speak with Jub, who is now stronger than before and sports some new tribal markings since they last saw him. Gobby shares about his new life with the MLA and expresses his gratitude for them saving his life twice. Rook, finding the goblin endearing, loans him the magical vorpal blade they had found and Gobby graciously accepts, hoping to make the loan a tad more permanent. The party rests for the night and sets out the next day, the MLA members taking an alternate path so as to not draw too much attention to the group. Along the way the party comes across a bunch of Githyanki surrounding a mind flayer and his thralls. Elisa immediately recognises the thralls, people from her old city whilst Ankhta disguises as an old man. The mind flayer looks at Elisa, asking for her help to stop the Githyanki with Elisa failing a WIS Saving Throw and the Mind Flayer using this distraction to teleport away. The Githyanki start cursing at the party, annoyed that they had aided in the mind flayer’s escape.

Elisa continues to bargain for the thrall’s safety but the Githyanki are disinterested and battle ensues. During which, many Githyanki are shoved down the pits, many dying from the fall. The party tries to save the thralls but to no avail as the Githyanki attack them mercilessly. Ankhta uses her magic to incapacitate some of the enemies, seemingly boring them with a long winded speech of old Urshuen facts. However as the battle continues despite Elisa’s attempts to have the Githyanki surrender, Rook and Ankhta are pushed down the pits as well, with Ankhta cornered by two enemies. They take her hostage, threatening to kill her if the rest do not retreat. Left with no choice, the party retreats, waiting for Ankhta’s return. Fortunately, the Githyanki stayed true to their words and the party is now safe together once more. They reach the vault, where before them are the dead bodies of the dwarves that were meant to ambush them, with several of their heads detached from their bodies. They then arrive before a massive door, and Ozul slides in the keys as they form a disk with the symbol of a cube. Strangely enough, the door doesn’t open and they are left puzzled. Except for Vesper who hears the familiar sounds of chains, as she sees a chain connected to the door, pulling on it causes the door to open as they stand before a lift. Entering, it descends down and as it does the party sees the MLA blip and disappear. The party then finds themself in a room with doors on every side, including the ceiling and floor.

Elisa summons her Griffin and the party prepares themselves for a long journey. They choose the ceiling door at random and cautiously enter it to find a chessboard-like room with Elisa pulling out a ram from her bag of tricks. The ram enters first, testing out the room for the party before they do. They find a puzzle hint. They quickly figure it out and upon finishing it, a stone eyeball appears. Vesper picks up the stone and keeps it for now, assuming it’ll be useful later. They head up once more to find an empty room with a large door that bears a carving of Vesper’s husband in his true form, with four empty holes that would fit the stone eyeballs found earlier. Continuing west, the party finds a room with a singularity in it. The Ram is sent in once more and the party watches as it gets sucked into the singularity and is shred to pieces. Upon further pondering, the party decides that the best way to go in is directly, ideally someone strong enough to grab onto the barbed wires. Rook volunteers and the rest help how they are able to. With everything set, he heads in. The force from the singularity is strong as he finds himself clinging on tight to the painful barbed wires that soon make their way around his arm. He presses on but the gravity is too strong as his right arm fractures. He grits his teeth through the pain, making his way to the stone eyeball and allowing the barbed wires to pierce into his skin as he frees the stone and teleports back to the party, bloodied and bruised but not defeated. Elisa rushes to help, healing Rook up and untangling the barbed wire from his arms. Vesper thanks Rook for his help and the armoured mercenary nods in response. The party starts wandering again, heading up to find an empty, ambush looking room before eventually finding a room with bowls.

The room imbues them with a similar aura with what they felt in Minerva’s lab. After attempting and failing to discern what the liquids in the bowl are, Rook takes the plunge and tastes one of the liquids before drinking it and gains a magical effect. He tries another and another magical effect takes place. Elisa then proceeds to bottle up the remaining untested liquids. They move on to a room filled with blades and just as they enter, they hear the voice of a man singing. The party figures out that the blades move around when sound is made and so they test out their theories and ultimately send Milton in to retrieve the stone eyeball in the room, which he does with relative ease. With one last stone left, the party heads back to the ambush room, mostly making their way past. Elisa enters the next room first to find a tavern brawler like Chrom who speaks of a bet that involves punching one another. Vesper follows but before she can make sense of it, a trap is triggered. The party finds themselves in battle with some constructs but that is soon handled and the party is healed up to face the weird ethereal bet hungry Chrom. Vesper volunteers and the rest of the party steps aside to what looks to be a potentially domestic situation. But, her resolve to hurt the man before him, even if it isn’t the man she married, quickly falters. They decide to try once again as Rook volunteers to accept the bet. It commences and the party all prepare to strike, successfully knocking Chrom down in one blow as the ethereal form vanishes and left behind is the final stone eye they needed. They head back to the room with the door, placing the stone keys to unlock it. And just as they do, the MLA members materialise before them. The MLA is fine and Gobby shares about his time in the blades room.

and Ankhta takes some scrolls. Meanwhile, Rook checks out the living quarters, finding a simple set up for one person. Just as she’s wrapped up in their exchange and his arms, Chrom starts casting a spell, one that Ankhta counterspells to stop it from happening. The party find themselves in battle with Chrom as he rambles on about his need to kill his wife and how she’s to blame for their connection that repeatedly leads to their ends. Vesper is confused and hurt to say the least but is unable to do anything at the moment. Before Ozul dies, he hands something off to Gobby, whispering something that no one else could hear.

Vesper calls out to Chrom, trying to understand and as she does starts to bargain with him. Chrom, in his merciless slaughter, disintegrates Vesper, but the spell that should have turned her to dust doesn’t. Through the mask of hate, Vesper sees his true feelings, that killing her hurts him as much as it does her. Meanwhile, the original seems to be busy at work, touching and interacting with things that no one else could see.
