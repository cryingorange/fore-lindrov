# Travel From Zipzapolis

The party travels for seven days, during which the following happen. 
- Grimrid casts create food and water for the party’s meals, ZipZap did not like said food and scolds Grimrid for creating such poor food
- Elisa then prestidigitates the food to make it taste like chocolate for ZipZap and herself 
- Elisa wakes up one morning to find a couple of rocks by her side and a set of dice made by Roast. The rocks spell out a message “Roast spoke ill of thy fate. Apologies. Repentant."
- Elisa and Roast share a conversation about the dice which Roast shared she had been making many other sets for everyone and they can all play dice games soon
- Roast, after learning sending from Vizeran’s scroll, sends out a couple of sendings to some people. Firstly to Nyssa, where Roast called her Toasted one and tried to convey that Elisa had asked for her whereabouts and current happenings. Nyssa, confused by this sudden sending, blocked Roast’s sendings to her. The second is to Vizeran, where Roast shares the good news with the Wisened One (Vizeran) that she has learnt his spell, hoping to someday learn more magic from him. Roast also gets blocked by Vizeran.
- Roast also gifts Grimrid and Ivy a set of dice each, with the highest surface bearing a carving that represents them
- Ivy spends her time carving out the original Ivy, her four Archdruids, and some other druid members of the party. She covers them in moss, particularly any with great imperfections. 
	- Though she carves out one particular druid and finds her work shoddy as she is unable to picture the face of this one person. 
	- Despite her best efforts to try, she is only able to identify all other details but not this one’s face. 
	- The guilt of not remembering all her druids eats at Ivy as she ponders about what Mr Light had told her. 
- Grimrid begins crafting his armour, as he feels the pride and touch of Moridin’s light upon his craft, he begins working faster, but fails twice to harness the power granted to him. 
	- The others hear his annoyance, displayed through the loudness of his smithing. 
- Ivy connects with the earth, collecting knowledge from the beyond. The first time, she finds herself connecting to Mimin, feeling his sadness that the tower is not as warm as it once was. 
	- Then to the Weirwoods, where she felt the land healing, and more presence about the area. Then to a parental figure of sorts as it encourages a little sapling to grow big and strong. 
	- Then finally she connects to another creature, feeling its pain, hearing it scream, it feels alive yet is unable to die. 

# Panicking Settlement

## Ziggy

The party approaches a settlement and are met with a warning shot that springs most of them into battle. As combat ensues, the party realises that the group of human settlements seem to be following the orders of a manic half elf man who screams about fire and explosions. Ivy’s towering stature causes the settlements to be afraid but they stand their ground, willing to die for their families. Though the party does eventually deescalate the situation, with Elisa casting suggestion on Ziggy, the Half Elf arsonist to get them to stand down and convince the others to do the same. Grimrid steps in, convincing the settlers that if the party had wanted them dead, they would be so evidently they are not here to harm anyone. Once everything settles down, the party speaks with some members of the settlement. From their “leader E Dirrel, the party learns that a stranger had approached their settlement, warning them of the Khan’s tribe before dying soon after. Dirrel tells Grimrid that the Silver King brought them security, trade. 

## Strange Man

Dirrel says that when they fought the party that they thought of what their King would want them to do, to protect the people they care about.
Grimrid tells them that they have courage, a poor dwarf would aspire to have a single fibre of their courage. Grimrid misunderstands that the King himself came here to tell them to protect the people they care about. Grimrid asks Dirrel how long the settlement has been here, and Dirrel shares that they were stragglers that were brought together to set up this settlement, to support and export more to Goldenfields. The party learns that it had been two weeks since the knights had left, that they were called away to somewhere else that needed them more. The party also gains some credibility in the eyes of the settlers by having Elisa, a knight, alongside them. 

## Grimrid & Ziggy

Grimrid and Ziggy share a conversation, with Grimrid clarifying that the current day dwarves are not what they once were, and how he does not stand on the side of slavers. Grimrid learns that Ziggy escaped enslavement and promises Ziggy that when this is all over, they will go to Gauntlgrym. Meanwhile, Roast, upon learning that there was a corpse nearby, heads off to study it in hopes of finding clues. Roast finds a corpse, nothing much of import but it is covered in maggots as Roast begins to kill the creatures one by one, drawing the attention of Grimrid. Grimrid analyses the corpse, finding that its limb was cut off with a weapon and also spots a ring that the body had. Roast takes the ring, seeing as Grimrid has no interest in it, finding that this shiny seems quite valuable as she pockets it. 

## Raula's Corpse

The others approach as well and to further sell the story of her being blind, Elisa pretends to trip into the hole dug by Roast, although it does nothing to trick Ziggy. Elisa recognises the corpse, thinner than he used to be but it is Raula, the ex self proclaimed King of New Phandalin. To try and understand the situation better, Elisa contacts her patron for some answers. She holds her necklace in her hands as psionic energies float in the air. She learns that New Phandalin is not okay, there are survivors, all not enslaved. The Silver King still lives and the Giants have reached New Phandalin. 

## Help Settlers

The party volunteers to lead the settlers away to their next destination, concerned for their survival given the current situation. Roast does share her concerns about bringing such a large group with them, suggesting that they arrange them in the same formation wolves do to ensure the highest chance of survival. Elisa gets notably silent at this, remembering a similar formation used previously where many lives were lost.

## Ivy's Friend

During Travel, Ivy comes to be a little closer to a little girl from Dirrel's settlement. She comes to Ivy while Ivy attempts to carve the face of her druid, staying close to Ivy and pretending to be disinterested in Ivy's carving, eventually falling asleep with Ivy's tail wrapped around her body. Ivy then elects to sleep closer to the girl and her family throughout the journey.

# The Outpost

## Entrance

Heading out, the party soon comes across a blockade with Elisa sending Milton to scout ahead. She sees constructs, one golem laying near the entrance and two more patrolling the area. There is one notably large building but that is about it. Ivy asks Elisa if the metal men look like the people from her vision, but they do not appear to be. The party offers the settlers a choice, asking if they wish to live under the command of the fire giants. They do not want to, instead asking if the party could find another way for them to pass through by living under the rule of the same people who had taken from them. The party attempts to speak with the Fire Giant camp. Rhek, the leader of the Hobgoblins informs them of the price, 200 gold per person. Rhek also treats Grimrid decently, and as conversations continue, it would seem that Rhek assumes Grimrid to be like any other dwarf, a slaver and devil aligned. 

## Lord Heechingu

But Rhek does give Grimrid one piece of advice when they meet Lord Heechingu, to not mention the horns. The party heads in, walking through the town to realise that the commonfolk are not well fed whilst the guards seem better off. They also notice a man hanging from a tree, clearly done as an example to the other workers. Speaking to Lord Heechingu as they find him with large horns on his head and reading a book called the “Tactics of the Blood War E Lord Heechingu also treats Grimrid very candidly, expressing a sense of kinship towards the dwarves. Lord Heechingu seems willing to listen to the party about their situation and offers them a task in exchange for letting them and the settlers pass. Recently, the grain supply has been poor, the Lord tasks the party to search for the thief and the missing grain. Roast does ask about the individual left strung up outside and learns that he was the previous mill master, punished for his failure to protect the grain from theft, now his son, Ewan has been appointed the same role. 

# Investigate The Grain

## The Mill

The Lord gives them free reign to investigate, and to help him find out who is stealing the grain. The party sets out, first understanding the situation from the first person they could find. They are met with some differing views, some are sad whilst others carry a quiet rage. Heading to the Mill, the party speaks with the newly appointed Mill Master, who seems suspiciously tired. Elisa uses her powers to detect his emotions and thoughts. She senses his guilt as Ewan tells the party that Jace is the one who did it, all the while convincing himself that this all would be worth it to save everybody. 

In his deeper thoughts, Ewan wants to save the town, but also has a lot of fear in his heart of being caught and losing everything. As for his feelings on the fire giant, he is angry / disappointed with the Kingdom, believing it to be dead as he can no longer depend on the King. From what the party is able to deduce, the one who stole the grain seems to know the schedule well, something that isn’t that great of a secret but still, they think it might be someone working at the Mill. Speaking to the other Mill workers, Grimrid also finds out that Ewan had once fallen into the water of the mill and what should have been an incident that killed him was actually nothing as Ewan managed to swim back out. Ivy finds the said place that Ewan fell into, looking inside as she finds a piece of seaweed. Roast and Milton also attempt to investigate the area, finding nothing amiss about the place. In the corner of the room is also an idol of sorts, one depicting a weird long limbed, fish like creature. 

The party tries to think what this might be but all draw the conclusion that this isn’t a creature they know of. Ziggy takes the idol, confronting Ewan with it who does not take kindly to Ziggy’s questions. The party eventually heads out. 

## Jace's & Ewan's Homes

They stop another villager, learning that this one is a Baldwin loyalist but does not believe that Elisa is a knight when she tries to show her standard. Eventually, the party heads out to Jace’s home, finding his wife who is crying at the door, worried about her missing husband. She is not optimistic about his fate as she tells the party of his unique gloves, one bearing a small flower on his thumb. The party do not find any clues inside the house and end up leaving for Ewan’s house. Outside of Ewan’s house, Roast finds a set of wheel barrow tracks and also a glove with a small flower on its thumb in the bushes. Meanwhile, Elisa and Ziggy are wondering how to head inside. Elisa spots a similar idol inside the house. Ziggy misty steps in the house, opening the door to see a mob outside, all upset at the invasion of Ewan’s privacy. Grimrid tries his best to quell the situation, convincing the villagers that Ziggy is an insane person and that they would leave Ewan’s home alone, they are simply trying to do as the Lord has commanded. 

# The Dung Hauler

The party leaves town, following the tracks Roast found as she informs them of the gloves as well. Along the way, they meet Reese, the dung hauler who is friendly to the party and tells of Ewan’s kind offer to help Reese with his work in the morning. He also informs the party that he sensed something off with the dung and hence did not complete his second round of dung hauling and requests for the party’s help in exchange for a mask. Roast agrees to help as Reese is initially concerned about her ability to shovel the poop, hoping instead the dwarf would do it but agrees to let Roast help once she proves her strength. Roast also in the meanwhile tries to convince Ziggy to not set fire near the dung, lying that it would result in the flames to be diminished but Ziggy is able to see through the lies.
