As the party stand outside of the Mines, preparing to go inside. Friend-Friend receives an eerie feeling in his chest. A voice of some kind, it tells him that he must return home. The party question his intentions, extremely confused by his new behaviour, Elisa casts Detect Thoughts and all she sees is a dark shape. Friend-Friend promises to find the party as he turns into a Quetzal and flies away.
At this time, a man approaches the party. He introduces himself as Aurelion , someone who was rifted into Fore Lindrov chasing after someone. A sorcerer of Order, he reveals that he is part of law enforcement and is has been entrusted with the same quest as the party, to clear out the mines.

As the party venture through the mines, Brom takes this time to explain to both Bjorn and Aurelion exactly why the party chose this mission. He explains that their friend, Untuk, was changed somehow. Into something incomprehensible, something terrible and that in the ensuing battle with the Cult of Eternal Flame, they were forced to collapse the mine on him, and they're not sure if he survived. Bjorn and Aurelion are unable to really understand exactly what it is that Brom means. But the party press onward, running into various remains of Duergar who were killed during the attack. The party move their bodies from beneath the debris, with major contributions from Elisa 's casting of Mould Earth and the Strength from Brom and Bjorn.

The party also run into a group of Magma Mephits, who heat up Brom 's armour as a prank in order to get a rile out of them. The party knows this, and decide to simply leave the area, seeking another way. Eventually they find themselves in a familiar area, the cave network leading up to where Untuk supposedly lies. Eventually finding themselves in a cave with various corpses. Brom, Elisa and Bjorn investigate the corpses as Aurelion and Amunkhet prepare for the worst. The party is attacked by groups of maggots within the corpses, swiftly dealing with them albeit leaving Bjorn slightly disturbed from having hundreds of maggots all around his body. The party attempt to take a rest, only to hear horrific rumbling coming from behind them. 

They run forward, pushing themselves up against a corner. They watch with their breaths held as a massive Purple Worm pushes past them into the cave chamber. As they attempt to take a rest, they realise that there are spiders that live in this corner of the cave, who grow increasingly aggressive from the party's intrusion. Aurelion swiftly tosses some rations and manage to goad them into become friendly. At this moment, the party come to the realisation that there are 2 Purple Worms as another dashes past them, with Elisa forming a dirt wall to hide herself.

The party then plan their attack after taking a short rest. With Aurelion moving forward to try to cast a spell and catch the worms by surprise. However as soon as he reaches within 60ft. the worms detect him and begin their attack, with one of them burrowing down into the earth and another rushing towards them through the walls.

Aurelion swiftly uses Wall Of Force to create a dome to protect the party from an attack from below as Elisa polymorphs the other into a small turtle as it bursts from the wall. The other Purple Worm rends itself from the ground as Bjorn jumps down and climbs onto its body, attaching himself to its head and taking sting after sting as Brom takes the turtle and turns it on its shell. In the meantime Amunkhet and Aurelion and Elisa blast away at the Worm mounted by Bjorn. Brom grabs the polymorphed tortoise and tosses it into the rampaging worm's mouth, undoing its polymorph the tortoise worm explodes out and begins to descend as Bjorn narrowly misses its mouth as he is launched upwards as the worm explodes. 

Amunkhet feather falls Brom as he trips trying to climb down and jumps down with Aurelion. She polymorphs Bjorn into a Giant Ape and the 2 giants fight witihn the small burrow made by the worm, eventually being wittled down and killed by a polymorphed Bjorn.
Finally the party make their way to the last chamber and crush the Purple Worm eggs that the creatures were protecting. Aurelion takes no part in this cruelty and looks away. Elisa has Bjorn clear out Untuk's chamber, with the party finally finding.. Nothing. Elisa finds tracks with Milton and the party realise that something left this chamber, leaving large prints that slowly morphed into hoof prints and slowly morphed into various human like foot prints.

The party decide that whatever left this cave is not something they can currently deal with and leave the mines to inform the Duergar of the bodies, mephits and burrow that leads out before retiring at the inn. However as they enter the inn they spot Holly and Nari, who certain members of the party give massive hugs to. With Holly apologising for leaving. Amunkhet shows Holly and Nari her paintings with them teasing her about them as Elisa tells them about their home. Bjorn and Aurelion introduce themselves to the pair and Brom takes some time to check on Nari's progress on getting a Scroll of Legend Lore.

Holly then teleports the party and Nari to their home as Elisa suggests that they take a tour. Only for Nari to begin the realisation that all of her friends are Undead, going against the very tenants of her Order. Kleve calls for Vizeran as he sees Holly teleport in. With the entire party realising a fight is about to happen, they stand down as Vizeran appears and Holly and him exchange a tense conversation, blasting spells at one another as Bjorn is forced to grab Elisa for fear of her safety as she tries to stand between them. Vizeran also reveals that Holly smells and feels like a Demon Lord. However the 2 Wizards decide to leave this altercation for another day as Holly warns the party to not pursue this whole Demon Lord angle that Vizeran is trying to insinuate and also says she will edit Nari's memories. Suggesting it might not be a good idea to reveal these things to her and put her in such a position. 

She also warns the party to be careful of Vizeran, saying that he can cast 9th level spells. Vizeran then reveals to the party his intentions as Bjorn and Aurelion introduce themselves to him. An introduction that Vizeran finds useless as he snaps at them with sarcastic annoyance.

He reveals that he has plans to stop the Drow informing the party once more that Lolth's grip on the Drow is as good as taking them hostage, and he has a plan to free them and show them a better way. He tasks the party to go to Mantol-Derith, a neutral tradepost for the races of the Underdark. Suggesting they go through a major faction due to the location's high security. 

He asks they meet an informant named, "Daehrow" and acquire information for him. Giving the party a stone to give to the Informant when they meet them. He then teleports the party to the Tower of Eternal Flame saying that Vanifer also wishes to speak to them. Brom spends some time with Elisa, the fire cult member, checking on her and continuing lessons with Ignan as the rest of the party take their rest while Aurelion and Bjorn get acquainted with everyone. The party visit Vanifer the next day, she gives each of them a hug and is given a formal introduction to Bjorn and Aurelion after Gili  shows Amunkhet somemore drawings and Vanifer updates the party on the status on the Dragon Egg. The egg who Elisa shares a tender moment with with Milton. Elisa attempts to return Vanifer's necklace back to her but she asks Elisa to keep it for their Teleport. Vanifer reveals that there is a child in a nearby settlement that is one of The Flame, that without guidance they could be a danger to those around them. She asks them to retrieve this child and also to find him, as she does not know who it is specifically.

The party agree to this and leave, travelling the Underdark they find Iymrith's Gargoyles, she reveals herself to the party, thanking them for thier help and regretfully requests their assistance again. She asks that the party buy food from a nearby settlement to feed a small chapter of Dragon-folk that have been hiding away. The party then go to the settlement of Dwarves and nearly get into a fight due to Amunkhet looking like an undead, however the conflict is averted as a Obsidian Armored Warrior gives the party his blessing and calms the Dwarves down.

The warrior is.. strange. He reveals his name is Vulcan and is uncomfortably interested in Amunkhet. With Elisa casting Detect Thoughts as he reveals his hatred of Dragons and of the problem that he solved for the Dwarves. She learns something.. Terrible, from her Detect Thoughts. Amunkhet is continuously questioned by Vulcan as the rest of the party go to purchase food, refusing the Dwarves kind offer to carry the food for them they learn a bit more about the Dwarves' xenophobia. Vulcan bids them farewell and the party leaves, finding the Dragonfolk they deliver the food as promised and bid farewell to the Dragonfolk. 

They then prepare to seek out Vanifer's Fire Child.
