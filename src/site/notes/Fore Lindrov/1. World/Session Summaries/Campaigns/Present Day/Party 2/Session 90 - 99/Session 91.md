# Damien The Smith

The party visits the smithy first, encountering Damien the smith who Elisa had previously commissioned. Damien is apprehensive at first, cautious to not speak out of turn until the party assures him that they mean no harm and are on his side with Roast giving him the dung hauler's mask to better put up with the smell of the place. He reveals that he had run back to pick up the commissions, only to be stopped by Amunathep's crew and was hurt by them. Grimrid inspects Damien's work, finding them to be not the best but okay. Morvek also reminds Damien to keep his calm when he begs to be saved, hating the fact that he is basically enslaved, forced to repair the weapons of the Fire Giants' Army and being bullied by the Ogres and Goblins. Morvek tells him to keep his cool lest he lose his life.

# Amunathep

They then head to the "Sun Elf", who mistakes Roast for an Elf when she speaks to him in Elvish. Amunathep pretends to not understand Elvish as a result of the great distance from his heritage. Going along with his act, Roast suggests they be invited in to reminisce about Elven culture, though Amunathep misunderstands the situation greatly. Elisa steps in, casting [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/2nd Level/Suggestion\|Suggestion]] on Amunathep for him to invite the entire party in and to give them all his stuff. He does so, confusing his own underlings with his tastes as the party steps into the tent with him. The party loots the items and before they leave, Elisa casts a [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Modify Memory\|Modify Memory]] on Amunathep, making him believe that he had invited the entire party in but failed to perform, thus in his desperation to buy the party's silence, he had given them all of his stuff. The party leaves the tent, all disappointed and annoyed like with Elisa telling Amunathep to just cry which he does, with many hearing his loud weeping.

# Orik's Backstory

After which they head to the Dwarves. Grimrid looks at Orik's armour, seeing that it is not a Dwarven Lord's armour. Beside Orik is a great Hell-griffon which Roast attempts to befriend but fails to. Orik nods and the guards leave as he asks Elisa why she is here, which Elisa deflects. Orik doesn't see any physical changes with Elisa but does sense her emotional changes. Orik insists that Elisa leaves whilst she still can but that decision is not one she can make. Grimrid cuts in, baiting Orik as he questions why dwarves fight no longer and instead now serve. In High Dwarvish, Grimrid also utters that it is because of him that this has happened but only Morvek understands Grimrid's words. Orik meets Grimrid's frustration with pragmatism, claiming the stories to be false, that there is no glory in being a Dwarf. 

He scoffs at Grimrid's mention of honour, sharing that the Dwarven King, Bruenor Battlehammer, was the one who betrayed and sacrificed not just his people but also Orik's daughter, Orinna. Feeding lies and legends to his daughter, all to sacrifice her in the war for Gauntlgrym, all so that Bruenor could get his crown, at least according to Orik. He shares that Bruenor, he and Targrim were regular dwarves, good friends in fact. With Bruenor regaling them with tales of heroism of the Dwarves, of a future where the Dwarves reclaim their ancient of Gauntlgrym. He shares that Bruenor was no noble, that it was Targrim and Orik, that searched through dangerous dungeons, all in a bid to help Bruenor fulfill his dream of bringing back the Pride of the Dwarves and resurrecting their ancient kingdom.

When Grimrid pushes why the Dwarves have allowed Bruenor to lead them down such a dark path, the path of slavery, of cruelty, Orik tells Grimrid the truth, that the Dwarves want safety. Grimrid doesn't stand for this, insisting that he is here to help and is sent by Moradin himself to right the matters that even his cousin Bolin The Bold could not. The stories of bravery, of heroism might be bullshit at least to Orik, but not the hearts of the Dwarves Grimrid says. Grimrid will not give up hope. Orik tells Grimrid that hope will not bring back his daughter, and thus Moradin means shit to him. 

Orik shares that he saw Bruenor go down in battle, that Targrim casted resurrection magic on him to bring him back to life, and yet when Orik begged Targrim in the midst of battle to bring his daughter back, Targrim cast him aside. Which to Orik he viewed as the greatest form of betrayal, that to Orik confirms his current declaration of Bruenor's intentions. 

He shares that the Devils of Avernus showed him the truth of the Soul Anvil, gave him hope, had him search for it, and convinced him to sign another contract after he failed to find it. His second contract with them forcing him to start a coup against the King, which caused Bruenor to become even more of an isolationist and sign further contracts with the Devils, all in a bid to provide absolute safety to the Dwarves of his Kingdom. 

Grimrid pushes, telling Orik to not blame Moradin for his daughter's death if Orik was the one that let her die. Grimrid tries to help Orik see that he did not kill his own daughter, but in his anger Orik doesn't see to reason, lunging at Grimrid as he begins to pummel him. Morvek tries to break the two apart, telling Grimrid that he doesn't know what it is like to have someone die for him. Orik spits at Grimrid, calling him a baby as the two go back and forth until Elisa steps in, begging Orik to stop. Through telepathy, Morvek tries to connect to Orik, sharing that he has suffered a similar fate, telling Orik that it was not his fault. Orik doesn't listen, drawing his weapon to attack Grimrid when Elisa interposes, taking the blow. 

Grimrid tells Orik that he is here to fight the moon and free all the souls. Orik goes to Elisa, who tells him that she could not let his pain be taken advantage of no longer, Elisa has also made her bed, but she promises to get him out and all her friends out. As Grimrid repeats once more in High Dwarvish that this is all his fault and as Orik walks away, Grimrid makes a promise to end this. Morvek then approaches Grimrid, asking him what the self blame was about when Grimrid learns that Morvek is here to stop the moon as well and suggests leaving that conversation for another time.

# Murram Blackstone & Duergar

The party heads to the Duergar, Elisa finds one of them familiar but doesn't manage to place them when they call out to her first. Elisa recognizes this Duergar as Murram Blackstone, head cleric of Karaz Andor but with all his limbs and none of his old metallic augments and replacements. Murram comments that Morvek smells like an Aberration, while Roast takes the chance to look at Morvek for his previous wrongdoings. 

As Murram continues to speak, Morvek finds his vision blurring. Then he is sent back home, to a room of dark stone. Morvek seems to be seeing not his memory but rather the memory of another Duergar who was present at the Ritual they performed in an attempt to save their people from turning into Aberrations from the corrupting presence of the moon. His visions toggle between two scenes, the ritual done by his Father and a travelling group of Duergar led by Murram, the procession that left Karaz Andor after Elisa and her earlier party saved them from the Khan Zalto's wrath. Morvek watches as their ritual directly impacts the other Duergar, transforming half of them into monsters and regrowing the bodies of the other half only to have them be attacked and slaughtered by their turned kindred. 

Morvek snaps back to, as Murram continues to share that his group had been searching for the City of Blades, knowing it to be somewhere in the Underdark when they all changed, either regrowing lost limbs or transforming into something horrid. Yet all the same they feel a wound in their body, something crawling inside. When the convoy turned, many of them were split up, scattered, while others turned into monsters. Murram and his small group of Duergar were captured by the Fire Giants and enslaved. Now forced to help Jarchigudai create Golems. 

Morvek realises that the timeline of their ritual and this Duergar transformation aligns. Morvek cautiously reveals that the City of Blades is not in the Underdark, but rather from the Castle-Town, although he remains bit cagey about sharing more information. 

Roast steps in, telling Murram about the castle-Town from which they both hail from. Morvek reveals himself to be the heir of the City, transforming larger to prove his strength, and Murram, unimpressed by Morvek, tells him that if he truly is some kind of heir and High King of all Duergar, then he should fix all this. To turn those who have transformed back, or to kill them, so that they will not live the rest of their wretched lives as filth. 

Morvek tells Murram a method to delay the spread, to cut one's limbs and replace them with prosthesis. A cultural habit that many of the Duergar outside of the Castle-Town retained, but were unaware of its true purpose. Elisa takes out the drill she had previously received from the Duergar, as Murram immediately summons one of the Duergar over to replace their limb. Before the party can react, Morvek cuts off the Duergar's limb, heating up the prosthesis as it is jammed against the Duergar's arm, slowing the progression of their curse. 

Roast takes the remaining arm, lying about disposing it when she instead feeds it to her mother as Ivy watches. Elisa asks Morvek why he speaks the way he does, which is very much unlike the typical Duergar that she's met. Morvek shares that he experienced a different point of view as the origins of his strange cowboy-esque manner of speech and attitude. As well as vaguely alluding to the ritual that he performed with his Father, that sent them hurtling int othe Dread Realm of the moon. Elisa connects the dots, realising the ritual had caused a permanent change to the Duergar and Morvek vows to make the situation right.

# Jarchigudai

The party heads to Jarchigudai as Grimrid introduces most of his party, speaking for Ivy and Elisa to bolster their reputation before his master. Roast introduces herself with a knightly salute as an individual of House Duvelier, and Morvek introduces himself as heir to the Rune Forge. Jarchigudai sees the Stormgirdle Roast has, asking how she got it as she reveals the King had given it to her. 

Jarchigudai states that she does not care for the Storm Giants. Declaring that it was their Leadership that caused the Death of the Giants twice over now, that she will no longer stand idly by and allow it to happen ever again, that she stands behind her Warmaster and Khan Zalto. Jarchigudai tells Morvek to show his rune, however Morvek reveals that they are no simple runes, but eldritch markings carved right into the flesh. Luckily, Ivy, Roast, Grimrid, and Jarchigudai are unable to actually see the "Rune", as their minds are unable to comprehend such an Eldrtich Truth, having been shielded from its horror.

Elisa is able to see it thanks to her Eldritch Nature, commenting that it looks cool. As Jarchigudai speaks down at the party, Roast refutes, upset at the Giant before Grimrid interjects, sharing to his master that he and his party's hand at letting the moon here, making an appeal for his master's help. The party turn their attention to Grimrid's words as they begin discussing through the telepathic bond. Elisa blames Grimrid for all the pain she has suffered, the friends lost, telling Grimrid that not only did he fail to stop the moon, the Elder Brain still lives. 

# Grimrid's Revelation

When Grimrid refutes that it was a God that he killed.. And that he and his party in the end.. Were just people trying to do right. Elisa reminds him that she's just a girl, now forced to deal with all of this thanks to him and his party's failure. Morvek then pushes Grimrid, placing blame on him as well before Morvek sees yet another vision. He sees through the eyes of a Tiefling, a member of Grimrid's party who was there when the situation happened. What was supposed to be a relatively normal mission for them became something far larger than they could imagine. During which Roast tries to interject, seeing the party fracturing, she tries to remind them of their mission. Elisa hears/feels Milton's feelings, how he wants to kill Grimrid as Elisa decides to take a step back. Morvek walks a smaller distance away. And Ivy falls to the ground, electricity crackling from her once more as she sees in her arms the Shaman that died, reliving the moment when Roast tries to get Grimrid to say anything other than "you can kill me". 

She drags Grimrid to where Ivy is, telling him to say something when ZipZap floats in, miraculously saying the exact words Ivy needed to hear before floating away. Roast adds that Grimrid would not be capable of bringing the moon here, much less on purpose. Morvek overhears this, joining in and receiving his bit of emotional help as he comes to terms that he was just trying to be angry at someone for what has happened. Morvek does point out that Roast is not only good at socialising but at reading people as well, asking how she had acquired the skill. For a split second, Roast recalls her Father before fibbing that she learnt it to survive the castle. 

Morvek believes it and adds it to the list of things he needs to learn. Ivy, however, sees through the lie, silently offering Roast comfort as Morvek takes his leave to help Elisa. Morvek offers Elisa a chance to see the vision he had seen. Elisa's psionics pushes a memory of hers to Morvek's mind, the day her city were taken over by mind flayers. Meanwhile, Elisa sees the vision Morvek had seen, how the day started out simple before becoming something larger than anyone could have anticipated. How the party, despite their fear, tried their best to fight the moon. How Grimrid died, throwing Verena aside, wholeheartedly believing that he could take the blow and was instead left without a head. 

How his party continued the battle and their demise were only prevented when Verena, in her rage and desperation called out to any Demon lord who would fight the moon, Demogorgon answered, a deal was struck. 

Coming back to, Morvek comments that Grimrid had sacrificed himself to keep the battle going, whilst Elisa questions why he had to throw his party member instead of picking her up and running. Morvek shares the reasoning Roast had given, that Grimrid would not mean any harm, and that his proclivity for exaggeration might have meant he wasn't as directly involved as he says he is, with Morvek theorising that Grimrid was simply in the area when things happened. With Elisa feeling less upset, ZipZap floats by to gloat his newly acquired skill of manipulating people into doing what he wants. Elisa then approaches Grimrid, confronting him with the truth of his quick death and sharing that it was his friend who had summoned a Demon lord to deal with the situation.

# Back to Jarchigudai

Going back to Jarchigudai, the party realises that she seems to be hiding something. Jarchigudai does not like the Vonindod, believing it to be chaos, and that's it's mere existence invites conflict rather than the stability of the Warmongering. She also does not believe the Khan's son capable of being their next leader, instead trusting that with time, War Master Cinderhild will instead take her rightful place as heir and to do so, solving the food situation will help Cinderhild greatly. She offers the party a trade, solve the food issue peacefully and she would grant them two parts of the Vonindod. They then head off to parlay.

# Goldenfields

The party walks up to Goldenfields' Walls accompanied by Jarchigudai after Cinderhild sends them alongside her to negotiate Goldenfields' surrender, they encounter Molier, who has no intention to parlay and poorly attempts to trick and intimidate the party. 

Then Bernard shows up, allowing the party, and only them, into Goldenfields. Bernard panics, grateful that Elisa is here but doesn't know what to do next. As Elisa tries to convince Bernard to calm down and surrender to bide time, Ivy, Roast, and Morvek go speak to the Almiraj they had seen. It is Biscuit, a cute Almiraj whose master is looking through their eyes. Then, not too long after, Biscuit's master appears, Reidoth, someone from Grimrid's past, Sildar Hallwinter's supposed love interest, and Mayor of Goldenfields. 

The party tries to tell Reidoth that surrendering for now, laying in wait for ambush is a good idea but Reidoth does not budge, insisting that it is the people's right to decide. Roast conjures up a map of the Giant's camp, alongside their strength as she asks if Reidoth and her people have the power to fight them all. Reidoth mentions that this is half the enemy's previous strength. As they are in the midst of discussion, Vaghar shows up, attempting to be all cool though the party either ignore him or spend their time teasing him. But as they continue to discuss, Roast does mention it odd that the Fire Giants have yet to attack and take over Goldenfields despite appearing to have the strength to do so. 

Roast suggests that there might be a chance that the golems aren't as strong as they think, merely eyes to look out for danger, and the clay golems were early not as functional as they should have been. Elisa consults Zephyros who reluctantly suggests that mayhaps the Fire Giant camp is not as mighty as it appears to be, that they might not have the power to secure their victory, hence they have yet to attack and are entertaining parlay.

The party agrees to not let Goldenfields surrender, but seeing as they still need to settle the Fire Giant's food supply to acquire pieces of the Vonindod, they decide to instead speak with the Hill Giants.
