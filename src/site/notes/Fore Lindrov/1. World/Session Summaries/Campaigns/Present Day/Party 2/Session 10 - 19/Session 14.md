Having successfully fooled Ardor Mor into thinking leaving the Tower of Eternal Flame alone, the party travel to Karaz-Andor to finally give Nari the Scroll of Legend Lore that she has been desparately trying to find in order to be allowed back home with the Golden Order. During their travel, they find themselves plagued by endless cacophanies of whispers and unfathomable murmurs. They are ambushed in a 10-ft-wide passageway of the Underdark by 4 Gibbering Mouthers, the combat itself was not overly difficult, with swift castings of Spike Growth from Elisa and Brom enabling the party to rid themselves of the Gibbering Mouthers.  However after the combat, Brom and Amunkhet notice something very strange.. Off in the distance set against the hallway darkness was a shape. Unmoving and unnerving, Brom can't identify what it is after Elisa just grumpily goes back to sleep. He stares deep into the Darkness, unable to clearly see what that "thing" is. Amunkhet decidedly just says they should go back to sleep. However Brom wakes Aurelion and they both aren't able to shake their paranoia of the shape in the darkness. 

Aurelion eventually decides to toss a torch over to the shape, as the lit fire rolls across the ground over to the shape, slowly lighting it up they see the horror that has been stalking them. A giant Elk's Skull with twisted antlers that stretch from its body, a strange tendril uncannily moves out from behind the massive skull blocking the passageway and snuffs out the Torch. Brom and Aurelion immediately wake the rest of the party up and they take their leave, backing away slowly they  leave the passageway constantly checking their back to see if the eerie visage is chasing them down.

The party eventually find another location to Long Rest and finally arrive at Karaz-Andor where they meet Nari in the inn of Kaldur Doomfist. Who simply greets them with a rude grunt, they sit down to drink with Nari and learn that Holly's left the city to do "something to do with her mentor". Stating that she's felt a pulse and that whatever she's looking for doesn't reveal itself often. The party present Nari with the Scroll of Legend Lore, proccing a hug and great gratitude from the female Paladin, who offers to bring the party to The Golden Order, though she cites that it will take her a few weeks to prepare for the journey. The party then say they may take her up on her offer, and leave her to her work. They then head back home and meet up with Dave and the rest of their home. They request that the Fire Cult stone masons build a door within the old Treasure Room where the passage to the Underdark lies and Aurelion inquires about Dave's injuries, something that Dave comments it will take him some time to heal himself. 

Dave also reveals to them that Ynas, the Gith Elder wishes to create a psychic ward for their home. The party go to see him and he tells them that a great psychic well of power has been revealed to him in his meditations, should the party bring back 20lbs of rock from the immediate area of this source of psionic power he will be able to craft a powerful ward. The party agree and head off in the direction of the ward, being struck by powerful psionic energies and discovering a quivering Umber Hulk after arriving at their destination, they stumble upon an old Lizardman Tomb after taking quite a bit of damage from the psionic waves, with Elisa 's earworm luckily providing her with much resistance.

They use Drow Poison to open the Lizardman tomb, a society that Amunkhet remembers was the kingdom her sister was married off to. THe party head inside and face various trials and tests of Wit, Strength, Might and Dexterity. Finally arriving at the door they are beset by Drow ambushers, who look strangely expressionless and quiet. THe party swiftly take them down, only for the first downed Drow's skull to crack open, revealing an Intellect Devourer and the presence of something far more sinister in the chamber.

The party barely take down the Drow and head into the final door, with Elisa seeing the citizens of Solaris, bald, enthralled and enslaved, alongside Lux. Who has been allowed to hold on to his Personality in his enthrallment she runs to hug Lux. Asking him what happened, with him revealing that they have been integrated into the Colony serving their Mindflayer masters, that her mother is alive, that Elisa 's survival is.. an infringement upon their deal with Party 1. Elisa simply stays there, shocked and happy that her family, her city is alive, asking more questions about Solaris, Brom, Amunkhet and Aurelion recognise the danger that everyone is in and attempt to pull Elisa away, being stopped by the Thralls they call out to her.  Lux uses Dominate Monster on Elisa , charming her and desiring to bring her back to the colony. The rest of the party trigger a fight in an attempt to save their friend. The battle begins as Elisa begins to make use of all her druidic / warlock powers to do battle with the party.

Casting Sleet Storm to break the concentrations of many a spell and blasting at the party with Eldritch Blast Aurelion is forced to use the Dragon's Blood, casting Summon Construct at 9th level, formulating his spiritual defender of Order, Atlas. Atlas rushes forward and begins to pound into Lux with Brom being taken down by the varying thralls and Amunkhet being forced to abandon him to keep Aurelion and Atlas up.  However Brom 's ancient blood begins to wake as Izeelzee calls for him to awaken. For a moment, Brom 's bloodlusted form rises from the grave, gaining the full powers of his bloodline he enters a Blood Rage that ends in him immediately draining one of the thralls, killing them and nearly killing one of the children that have become enthralled. During this time Aurelion is taken down by the other Thralls and Atlas dissipitates as Amunkhet in a bid to save the party presses the Black Opal containing her brother Geb into a thrall in front of her finds time frozen around her. Her brother simply asks her if she trusts her, to which she answers yes. 

As Amunkhet throws the Opal at the thrall, his body twists and turns as his flesh sloughs from his bones, forming out of dark shadow is a black-skinned Balor. Wielding a thundering blade of Shadow and a wisy Whip of fire. The Balor strikes down one of the Thralls and rips another into 4 different pieces with his whip. It speaks purely in Abyssal as it looks at Amunkhet , she gives confused gestures and utters sputtering words in reaction to all of this, however the Balor simply steps out, and begins to fight the Mind Flayer thralls.

A charmed Elisa takes out the Potion of Dragon's Majesty and feeds it to Milton, who transforms into the beautiful visage of a fully grown White Dragon and drops a hail of Cold on the party, bringing Amunkhet down. The Balor proceeds to cast Dispel Magic at the 9th level as Lux attempts to psionically cancel it out, only for the Black Opal in Amunkhet 's hand as she stands back up to shimmer and pulse, countering a Psionic Counter.  Lux escapes with the rest of the Thralls and Elisa is picked up by the Balor who says to her in Common to stay down revealing its ability to speak the Common Tongues. It gets down on a knee towards Amunkhet and speaks in Abyssal, before she can respond it says something else in Abyssal and disappears. Elisa apologises to the party, healing and helping all of them up. They are left with 3 dead Thralls and 1 unconscious one that survived the battle. They decide to bury the dead, with Elisa saying a prayer calling For the One of The Stars to give her the strength to save the people of Solaris, with small cosmic flowers gfrowing out from within the graves to mark the dead bodies.

Both Brom and Elisa carve the Sun Symbol of Solaris and Elisa puts "In memory of the Solaris" on the headstones. The party then begin their travel back home and run into a Gargoyle, Elisa immediately runs to it and apologises to Iymrith, saying that the Potion of Dragon's Majesty of Dripnir. With Iymrith saying it is alright, asking if Milton flew as Dripnir and upon Elisa revealing that he did, she says that it is good that the White Dragon managed to spread her wings one last time. With Iymrith also revealing that Dripnir would've loved to hear Elisa tell her how pretty she was.

Iymrith then reveals that it may be time to wake the Dragon Warriors, revealing that Poisoncalm, a Half-Dragon sits in an ancient Dragon Tomb and that the party must wake him and also do battle with him to calm him down as the Dragon Warriors are rather eccentric. The party agree and travel to the dungeon where they drop down into a Maze, with Brom and Amunkhet nearly immediately reaching the end, they begin to play chess and Aurelion and Elisa are nearly brought down by the Maze Guardian and its traps, with Aurelion barely making it out. The party then head into the final chamber where Poisoncalm awakens, a Half-Dragon throwing out over-the-top performance gestures and declaring all of his attacks, he reveals his power, that of a Stand named Hierophant Green.

The party quickly defeat him as he lauds them as people with Great Fighting Spirit. He thanks the party for helping the Lady Iymrith, saying that she is a woman capable of calming the hearts of others. That people feel at ease around her. That it may sound awkward, but if he were to fall in love, he’d like it to be with someone like her, and that he would give his all for her, and he would always want to see her warm, happy smile. He quickly leaves the Dungeon and Iymrith gives the party 6 Potions of Supreme Healing and a Scroll of Teleport. However just as they are about to begin their journey home, they hear a desparate Sending from their friend Jimjar who calls out for help in Blingdenstone.

The party, unsure what to do decide to use the Scroll to try to teleport to Jimjar using the Gold Coin he gave Brom and Elisa and the party are brought to Blingdenstone, but suffer a mishap due to something stopping them from teleporting. They find themselves in FRONT of Blingdenstone rather than at Jimjar's location. Where they are led in by the Guards and encounter a plethora of deadly defenses set up by the Deep Gnomes of the great City. They meet Peebles again, who reveals that he is the Doctor and unofficial stand-in for their Lord, Belfry The Great. The party then head to the Inn where they meet Tappy Foamstrap, who is an excitable Deep Gnome in love with adventurer stories and gives Amunkhet a lifetime stay at the inn for giving her a painting of Urshu.

The party also meet 4 Adventurers, who reveal themselves to have been part of the original Expedition into Fore Lindrov during Party 1's time. Paldron the Half-Elf Fighter, Declan the Half-Orc Barbarian, Krista the Kalashtar Ranger and Arche a Changeling Sorcerer. It is revealed that Paldron is here to seek out riches and that Krista his "companion" is here to "find herself". With Declan here to seek out a magic blade to save his clan from an Ogre Tribe and Arche is a poor Changeling Orphan. She cares for both her sisters alone and that this job will finally give them enough money for her to stay home and take care of both of them. She's left them both in the care of a friend however she worries if she doesn't return soon something terrible may happen to them.

Amunkhet also notices that the party has raided Urshu Tombs, she aggressively questions Arche about her Urshu Chain, asking where the tomb is and the "Undead" she's killed. The fellow party, named "Fall Damage" also reveal that they've raided a dead city that sounds like Solaris. With them being forced to do battle with a Slime Elisa knows to be Briddihue and chasing him away. "Fall Damage" also can be seen sporting a Dragonscale Bow on Paldron, proving they also have been raiding dragon tombs. The party bid Fall Damage good night and they all go to bed, awakening the next day and preparing to explore Blingdenstone to see if they can help anybody. After Peebles revealed in their first meeting that the city is trying to expand and is plagued by Wererats, monsters in the deeper reaches of the caves and something called "The Pudding King".
