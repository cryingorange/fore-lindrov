Instead of waiting another day and potentially receiving more torture, the party decides to strike that very night. Plan A is for Tiny, Wystram, and Nyktos to sneak out, luring their guards to a corner to be knocked out whilst Elisa kept up the facade of the three being in the cell. From then they can safely get their items and sneak out. And Plan B was for Eigeiron and Blagothkus to rush in and fight their way out. 

  

With the goblins and their captors asleep, Wystram silently removes the dimensional shackles from Elisa, allowing her to cast invisibility on the rest of the party as they sneak out of the room to the corridor. However, Morti realises their presence and watches as they sneak out. He tries to alert the Ogre, though Tiny manages to warn Elisa in time for her to cast the minor illusion. The Ogre,seeing the illusion, shoves Morti aside and goes back to his rest. Meanwhile the party somewhat successfully sneaks to the hallway and Wystram gives Nyktos a crash course on how to properly stealth. She manages to do so and attempts to enact their plan of luring the guards to the corridor. This does not work as Morti is fully aware of the party’s actual locations.  
  
Morti begins waking the other guards and with things going south, Elisa casts eyebite on the Ice Troll, knocking them right out. The other Ice Troll assumes that Morti did something and begins chasing Morti, who heads towards the sleeping Hill Giants. Elisa fixes that issue, making the Ogre, Ice Trolls, and Morti fall asleep, though not before Morti wakes up one of the Hill Giants, Boob. The Goblins wake up in the midst of all that, finding out that the party proceeded with the plan without telling them. Meanwhile, the rest of the party improvise, with Tiny opening the door to Hvitserk and Ubbe’s room. They break in successfully, opening the chest in the room to find Hvitserk and Ubbe’s belongings. Nyktos successfully dispels the magic on Storvald’s door, her magic potent enough to crack the door open slightly. She moves in, checking the chest in the room for any signs of magical traps and finds none. Wystram heads to Elisa to offer a hand, he speaks to the Goblins, convincing them to help with the efforts as they head to where Nyktos is. Wystram also manages to calm Crimson who upon seeing the mess was about to bark at the noises and dangers around him. 

  

Wystram and Elisa attempt to convince the Hill Giants to join their side, though it doesn’t go quite well and the party all gather in Storvald’s room. Tiny opens the chest to find some items, a medium sized chest, and half of Lucatiel’s corpse. At the moment, the party has no time to grieve. They grab the necessities, finding their gold lighter than before as Storvald had taken 3874 gold from Elisa, 1069 gold from Nyktos, 11 gold from Wystram and 1 gold from Tiny.  
  
Vhagar takes the Blue Dragon Mask, wearing it as the mask connects and grows over the skeletal part of his face instantly. Tiny grabs his weapons and bag of holding, with Wystram who offers them a hand seeing as the elf had no need for weapons. Elisa grabs her stuff, noting the lightness of her coin pouch. Nyktos takes the multitude of bags, picking both Lucatiel and their things, not wanting to leave them behind. Wystram and Tiny hold the door, alongside Jack as Nyktos holds out the Rod of the Blood Ruler, summoning her iron, commanding him to protect her. The Goblins then ask the party of what the plan is, to which they respond that they would fight their way out of there. Not wanting to be involved, the Goblins simply hide.

  

At the door stands the Ogre, Ice Trolls, Morti, and the Hill Giants. The Ogre is the first to die, followed by Morti who gets knocked out by a rather annoyed Wystram. Tiny tries to convince the Hill Giants to fight alongside them, though that doesn’t quite work. What does work is when Vhagar issues the command to them, showing his strength and might that strikes greater fear into the hearts of the Hill Giants than the Frost Giants have. Afraid, they agree, turning on the Frost Giants and joining the party’s side. However, to end the Ice Trolls regeneration requires fire, that which Tiny delivers onto their enemies. One of the Ice Trolls burns to death whilst the other remains alive, their screams waking up one of the Frost Giants. The awoken Frost Giant doesn’t take the party as much of a threat, slowly making his way to the party as the Hill Giants throw rocks at him. As the Frost Giant gains a better understanding of the situation, he wakes up his allies and the true battle to get out of enslavement begins. Blagothkus and Eigeiron land, taking on some of the Frost Giants themselves. The three Hill Giants take on one Frost Giants, and Loog’s owlbears take on some of the Ogres. The Goblins rush out, dashing towards the door in hopes of escaping. 

  

Tiny rushes to Severin’s side, wanting to extract him out of there should they need to run away. They punch the wall hitting hard enough to create a pathway through to where Severin is. Using their strength, they unbend the chains that kept Severin trapped. Tiny hands Severin Hespera’s arrowhead, telling him to stay safe and hidden and to use Hespera as an escape if needed. Ashamed, Severin takes the arrowhead as Tiny rushes back to help his friends. 

  

Meanwhile, Wystram, Elisa, and Nyktos hold their ground. Wystram uses the spell gem of Firestorm to torch their enemies, dealing a decent amount of damage to all of them. Meanwhile, Elisa hexes the enemies and damages them greatly, weakening them to allow Nyktos Eblood magic to work better. Nyktos heals the three of them, buying themselves more time. The party works well together, swiftly taking down the Frost Giants. Wystram beats the Frost Giants to a pulp, roundhouse kicking them to their knees and uppercutting through their throats as he ends their lives. Nyktos kills one of the Frost Giants, concentrating her blood magic on the Giant as Jack appears in the wave of blood, snapping the Giant’s neck. Tiny shows up just as the last Frost Giant falls and provides the much needed fire that his party needs to kill the Ice Trolls, ending their lives. 

  

With that, the dust settles, the party has won. They’re free. Blagothkus and Eigeiron are worse for wear but alive, as are the owlbears. Though the same can’t be said for the Hill Giants who lost one of their men and for the Goblins who all lost their lives during combat. Tiny heads back to Severin to deliver the good news and offer a helping hand to get him out of his cell. Nyktos heads to the cage, asking for Blagothkus Ehelp to break the lock as she reunites with Crimson, the pup still shaking from all that has happened.  
  
Nyktos also takes the time to retrieve Lucatiel’s head from their cell. Elisa reunites with ZipZap, though in a slightly different way as she rushes to inform ZipZap that Storvald had stolen their money, the two rush to Storvald’s room in hopes of finding something worthy to steal. Wystram silently heads down to the room that holds the bodies of his friends, his family. Confirming their deaths, he discovers Tazin, the Drow wearing a cloak bearing a strange symbol that he has never seen before. Borne on his shadow tattoos, Tazin’s body bears the words, “Stop the Fire Ein Shadowspeak. Torn and partial journal entries on Annie & Ludwig reveal that they had discovered Tazin’s capture and were killed trying to save him.

  

They quickly regroup once more, as Blagothkus and Eigeiron ferry them all back up to their home. At Blagothkus Ehome, the party finds three familiar faces, ClipClop, Jumbo, and Vermilion. They are mostly safe from dangers, having been picked up by Blagothkus and his family.  
  

Though ClipClop does share with Elisa about Jumbo’s rat eating habits. When asked how Blagothkus and Eigeiron knew where the party and their horses were, they explained once more that the Oracle had told them. That they could feel through their souls that the ancient caste system had been broken, the ordning shattered. It is theorised that this might have been the All-Father bringing down divine judgement. That now some Giants who seek the glory of the gods run amok. With the Maelstrom silent, the Giants are free to do as they wish. The Oracle had told them of the plight the party was in, prophesied that the party would be the ones to speak to the Shell of the Sea, to enter the Court of Storm and root out the evil that lies within.  
  
That all of them, even Wystram, would play a vital part in all of this. Blagothkus explains that their journey takes them to the Hill Giant’s land where Chieftess Guh resides in Grudd Haug. He also shares of the history of Giants, that Storvald was rumoured to be the reincarnation of “Thrym E the Father of all Frost Giants. He warns the party to not seek out Storvald no longer, that fighting him would be no easy task but the party all have their own reasons for wanting Storvald dead.  
  
Nyktos affirms that the fight is the party’s decision, that they would not bring Blagothkus nor his family into it but an evil like Storvald can not be left wandering the lands. Though Blagothkus reiterates that it is the Oracle who sent them to the party, and that they would help however they can. With that, the party each heads to the guest rooms to rest. 

  

Tiny asks Blagothkus if he has any medical facilities to heal both Tiny and Severin. Unfortunately the Giant does not, though he does extend an offer to keep any of the party’s friends here, providing them with protection. Which Tiny takes and thanks them for allowing Severin to stay. Tiny also decides to be honest with Blagothkus, sharing that Severin is a dragon like they are. 

  

Nyktos brings Crimson to a room, spending her time comforting the restless pup. A knock on the door is heard as Tiny enters, holding their severed arm. They enter the room, and ask if Nyktos has any way to reattach his arm. The Cleric doesn’t quite know what to say, knowing that her powers don’t allow her to regrow or reattach limbs. Still, she approaches the matter cautiously, giving Tiny’s arm a look over as Vhagar waves her off, telling Tiny that the arm is gone. Trying to piece her words carefully, Nyktos brings up Jub, the Orc that lives in their home who had lost both his arms, insinuating that if there was a way for her to regrow arms, she would have already done so. But Tiny maintains that it’s different, he has his arm here right now, maybe she could do something. Not wanting to break his positive attitude, Nyktos says that she is looking for ways to resurrect someone, and that it’s possible they could find magic that grows back limbs. But she warns Tiny that others might use the lure of such magic to convince them to take a deal, and that Tiny should not fall for such tricks. Which Tiny responded that they aren’t one to fall for any deal, not convincing Nyktos as they both know that Tiny was the one who eagerly took two dark deals when they were in the Amber Temple. They share a knowing look and a chuckle before Tiny brings up another matter, that of Dame Aylin’s body. The gentle repose on her corpse would have worn off soon after they were captured, and her body was probably rotting in his bag of holding. When asked what they should do about it, Nyktos suggests that this sounds like it should be a party decision, casually pushing the responsibility to others. Tiny agrees and leaves the room. 

  

Nyktos then holds a ritual, conjuring both hers and Callahan’s likeness in blood. Callahan looks older, as if his blood is magically compelled to hasten his growth. Nyktos relays orders to her people. She first understands their current situation, the cult is growing with tragedies of the moon resulting in people seeking help and faith to cope with the horrors. Though as the cult grows, resources wear thin. Nyktos agrees with Callahan and their plans to take over some neighbouring towns under a different identity to allow the cult to gain some legitimacy and power whilst Nyktos finds connections to help the cult. Nyktos also warns the party of the Giant's ordning and of Storvald and his son, warning them to retreat should they come across him, that his blood is hers to spill. Callahan comments that the Heart shows signs of the ordning’s break as well, seemingly reacting to it. Nyktos praises Callahan for his efforts whilst she's been away and affirms their goal to continue the growth of the cult before the conversation comes to a close. 

  

Wystram also heads to a room, eager to begin his trance when an irritating knock at the door forces him out. Wystram opens the door to see Elisa and exasperatedly says that they agreed to leave conversations to the next day. But Elisa argues that they had mentioned a _short_ rest, not a long one. Unable to argue against Elisa, Wystram continues the conversation. The two introduce themselves to one another, and both affirm the other of their strengths in the previous combat. Before things were too nice and positive, Elisa decided to once more bring up the topic of Wystram’s dressing or lack thereof. Wystram states that he might have an allergic reaction to shirts and proceeds to flex as if that really showcases why he shouldn’t wear a shirt. Seeing as Wystram is new to the party, ZipZap introduces himself as Wystram’s master, with Elisa cheerfully agreeing that the castle they reside in was in fact leased to the Cloud Giants. Wystram patronises the two, believing their words to be delusions of grandeur. Wystram then asks if Lucatiel was their friend and if Lucatiel was a mushroom. ZipZap proceeds to make an argument about sentient mushrooms and rocks to prove his point. That is about when Tiny enters the room as Wystram’s hope for rest diminishes. The conversations start up once more as Wystram sneaks away to one of the spare rooms in a desperate attempt to get some much needed rest after narrowly escaping the Raven Queen’s embrace just hours before. Unfortunately, Tiny manages to successfully track Wystram and he spends some time speaking with Elisa, ZipZap, and Tiny before he is finally allowed to rest for the night.
