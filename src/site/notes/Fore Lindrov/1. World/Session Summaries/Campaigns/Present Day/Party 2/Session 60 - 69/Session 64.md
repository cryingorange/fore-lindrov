As the party returns to their cell, they spot another captive being released from his cell. The man resembles a Drow, with red eyes and white locks that adorn his grey skin. The ‘drow Estrangely wears no shirt nor shoes but seems undisturbed by the cold. Wystram sees the party in a bad shape and notices the remains of Lucatiel, offering his condolences and healing which Nyktos expresses her appreciation for whilst Tiny ignores, still processing the loss. Wystram greets Nyktos first, calling her sister before introducing himself. He then begins asking if she’s seen some people, describing the countenance of the four encased in ice below. Cautious, Nyktos asks what relation the four have to Wystram who affirms that they are family.  
  
Believing him, Nyktos shares of the fate the four below had met, expressing her condolences to him. But she assures him that they can get his friends Ebodies back. During their conversations, Wystram clarifies that he’s not a Drow but rather a Shadar Kai. Wystram then tries to speak with Elisa, who having been enslaved by Drow before simply ignores. Later on, Wystram hears a voice in his head asking him why he’s half naked. Confused by the disembodied voice, Wystram asks Nyktos if she knows anything about it, to which the cleric responds that it could be a ghost. The voice continues to probe Wystram about his lack of clothing and receives a confused response towards the topics of shirts.  
  
Though eventually, they all hear sounds of footsteps stomp out from the hallway as Storvald and his sons emerge, along with two frost salamanders. Storvald carries a cage with the dewinged and very injured Parsley, adorning his clothes are four masks which Wystram recognises, clenching his fist at the sight of them. Storvald lifts up a conch, blowing into it as it activates the teleportation circle, as they presumably head to the feywild to get the never melting ice. 

  

Wystram decides to test the limits with the Jarl gone. He provokes one of the guards who demands that Morti open the door. The Ogre drags out Wystram, beating him to a pulp for his behaviour before tossing him back. Keeping an eye out for things outside, Wystram notices a fog that surrounds both Crimson and Zipzap, pointing it out to Nyktos, though the party are unable to do much about it as ZipZap remains peacefully asleep.  
  
They do, however, notice Luug and his condition. His injuries fail to heal and he is exhausted to the brink of death, it would only take one small incident to end his life, though the party remains unable to do much. Tiny looks around the place, finding a pound of food and kindly offering it to Nyktos who is exhausted and in desperate need of water. Taking a look at the meat, she discerns that it belongs to something sentient before deciding to give it back to Tiny, appreciating the gesture but warning them that it might be human. Vehga eats it without care, prioritising survival above all else.  
  
Noticing Elisa’s unfriendly attitude towards Wystram, Nyktos speaks with her, realising that Elisa had also thought Wystram was a Drow and hence was not too nice. Nyktos clarifies that Wystram is a Shadar Kai and shares that his friends were the four they had found downstairs, hoping that Elisa would warm up to him sooner.

  

As night falls, Nyktos' need for water raises cause for concern and Tiny offers to help, using their fire breath to melt the ice enough to drink. Elisa suffers from the same issue and Wystram kindly offers his blood as a last choice.  
  

Elisa states that she’s usually the one offering blood, not the other way around before drinking. But the taste of blood is too much as they both pull away, unable to stomach it. Elisa tries talking to their night guard, Morti, attempting to convince him to tell them where their stuff is. Though what she realises is that he doesn’t remember quite how to think, and his fear of the giants is so great as he refuses to tell the party where their stuff is. 

  

The next morning, Tiny and Nyktos begin taking their rest in the morning. The door then opens as four pounds of food are tossed in. Nyktos and the goblins reach out for the food, with the first goblin grabbing two pounds and Nyktos taking one pound, the final pound goes to a goblin who then gets shoved by Elisa and has their food taken from them by a mage hand. The spellcasters get to eat as Tiny remains asleep.  
  

The rest begin to plan their attempt to escape with Wystram noticing a small gap in the door, sizable for a small creature like the goblin to slip out from. Before the party can plan further, the Frost Giants have all gathered about, bringing out coins, trinkets and other stuff as they begin trading. They then pull the prisoners out, selecting Tiny and a goblin to play a game. They place boulders into a bag and have the two hold it, adding more and more weights.  
  

Through determination and teamwork, both Tiny and Goblin survive, with Tiny selflessly taking much of the load off the Goblin and even bestowing the Goblin with a gift to aid their attempt. The game comes to an end as the last boulder is added and the Frost Giant that bet that no one would get crushed wins, rejoicing in their victory. The party are sent back to their cell and this game provides the party the chance to have a good working relationship with the goblins. 

  

The party begin sharing their plans with the goblins who agree with it, though the party conveniently leaves out their intentions to save Luug, the Hill Giant that captured the goblins to begin with. Meanwhile, Elisa speaks with ZipZap, learning that Jarl Storvald thinks of himself as the second coming of “Thrim Eand how he overheard something about “Miran and Nym will kill each other E He also shares how it was colder last night, that he dreamt of a mist that touched him, and also says that Jarl Storvald took the stuff, a ring.

  

That night, a similar mist finds the party. Nyktos and Wystram attempt to communicate with it. As they do, Nyktos notices that the Goblins are also awake and seem to have something planned, she warns Wystram about it. And eventually the two resort to waking Elisa up, who Nyktos tries to nudge gently before Wystram kicks Elisa awake, he does the same to Tiny as well.  
  
They then learn that the cloud is Eigeron and that someone called “The Oracle Esent them to help. He offers to take the party away right now, but they decline, stating that they had other friends to save, one of them being a dragon. Tiny shares their view, stating how mindless judging of one another isn’t the answer. This manages to persuade Eigeron and he agrees to let the party do as they wish, with him and his father providing an escape route for the party. As they are having this conversation, Weakcheek, the leader of the Goblins, slips out, a shiv in his hand as he goes to take care of some business. It’s clear to the party that he means to go kill Luug, but Elisa manages to stop him, convincing Weakcheek to come back and allow her to give him something that makes him more sneaky. As he returns, the Frost Giants emerge, his chance at revenge gone. 

  

The next day, food is given and the Giants are bored from having lost their bets the day before. They pull the party and the Goblins out, tying the Goblins up, their legs bound. Before they can start any games, Tiny calls out to the Giant, grabbing their attention and provoking them. In response, they punt Tiny across the room towards Tubbler, using Tubbler as a crash pad. They have their fun, tossing the party members and some using their abilities to make the Frost Giant seem weak as his fellow companions mock his strength. Tiny speaks up once more, offering the Giant help. Insulted, the Giant picks Tiny up, beating him up until he is just barely alive before leaving him alone. Wystram jokes that he and Tiny are similar, both making silly choices. 

  

That night, the party begin their plan, they find what they can to use as lockpicks as Wystram unlocks the picks and following Tiny’s plan, the party puts the cuffs back on designating who’s allowed to take them off. Elisa casts invisibility on Wystram who slides out of the gap in the door and makes his way to the Jarl’s room. Wystram finds the door locked, and goes to the other room, the door is unlocked but very heavy and difficult for him to open. The noise generated cautious Morti to check the hallway. Fortunately he does not find Wystram but does begin to check for him the cell where Wystram quickly sneaks back, returning just in time. With that information, the party has a new plan that they will enact within the next day or so.
