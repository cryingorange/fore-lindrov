# Rest

  As the party takes a short rest, Elisa has her familiar explore the rest of the space. The familiar finds a set of 5 healing potions, conveniently laid before it as if someone had left it there for them. It brings back the potions as Elisa relays what her familiar had seen. During this short rest, Roast notices something around Elisa, the gift to see through illusions also allows her to see the psionic energies around Elisa. Roast tries to make sense of what she sees, realising that Elisa has psionics and is more aberratic than she realised. She approaches Elisa, showing her a mirror as she brings up the conversation of the aberratic nature about her. 

Elisa comments that it has been long since she has seen herself, having lost her eyes not too long ago, and then was blue, then lost the blue, and now is this…void. Roast gently pries, asking if Elisa always had these powers when the younger confesses that she did not. Her powers manifested after her city was taken over by a bunch of mind flayers. Roast asks if Elisa intends to kill all the mind flayers as Elisa shares that she intends to save her people that were turned into mind flayers and to kill the rest who attacked her city. As Elisa opens up about saving her people cursed to be mind flayers and Roast learns that there is a way to save individuals cursed with aberratic natures. Roast shares about her home, the castle from whence she came and of a friend cursed by the moon. Elisa offers to help this friend as well, as Roast remarks that they would simply have to capture him first. Elisa inquires if this friend is fast, which Roast confirms, adding that this friend can also fly.  Roast gives Elisa a pat on the head, similar to what her mother did for her, commenting that Elisa is strong, that at least 5 groups would have been sent to fight her. In that moment, Elisa realises how much taller Roast is and swims up to meet her height. 

## Prank on Grimrid

As they share this moment, the two turn their attention to Grimrid, notably how he refuses to swim, instead wading through the waters at a very slow pace. The two agree that the best way for Grimrid to overcome his fears is through exposure therapy as Roast sneaks up behind Grimrid and lifts him up before the dwarf struggles back to the ground, telling them to not repeat the actions again. Though much to his displeasure, an agreement is made between Roast and Elisa to help Grimrid concur his fear. 

# Princess Serissa

Finishing their rest, the party carries on to the next room, walking through the illusory wall. They enter another room with statues depicting the royal family of the Giants. King Hekaton, the monarch, with a look of judgement, pride, and restrained fury. Queen Neri, a serene beauty dressed in silk, her long hair flows down the length of her body. There is a sense of calmness about her yet behind those gentle eyes holds cruelty. Her hand is placed over her heart, an act that Lu can tell is pretentious at best. 

Next, two of their daughters, Miran and Nym. Miran’s statue is posed like a warrior in combat, a look of defiance and hunger for power in her eyes. In comparison, Nym’s eyes are still, too still, a look of detached coldness adorns the princess Eface. A short distance away is what seems to be a void, at least for those unable to see through illusions. Elisa calls out, telling the individual that their presence is known. The woman dispels her illusion and the party is greeted by a lavender skinned Giant who takes much care as she approaches the party, as if she’s familiar with smallfolk and how to move to not harm anyone. Elisa recognises her, Princess Serissa, a friend of Elisa’s.

Lu jumps in, pointing out who Serissa is. Serissa however, is simply excited to meet people. She happily greets the party, welcoming them in as she shares that she had been sent by her father, the king, who saw destruction and thus hid her alongside the Giant treasures, for her to return when it is safe. Serissa invites the party to a game, one introduced to her by an old friend Angrboða. Recognising who Serissa is, Roast pulls out the Rune Hammer gifted to her by Angrboða, introducing herself as a friend. When Serissa asks about Angrboða, Roast tells the truth of Angrboða’s circumstances but dies about her death, telling the princess that it was painless. 

Serissa introduces the game to the party and they play a couple rounds, with the party borrowing Serissa’s money to finance their fun. During which, as the party chats, they learn more from one another. With Ivy briefly mentioning that their body wasn’t theirs. At the last round, Serissa suggests that the final winner would get a special prize. The final game concludes, with Elisa winning. It turns out Serissa intends to teach the party rune magic. 

She tailors the runes taught to the individual and what would best suit them or what she felt they needed. With Grimrid, she teaches him the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Runes/Turisaz\|Turisaz]] rune, offering him a meaningful reunion with Giant runes when he shares that he once traded his knowledge to help someone who meant a lot to him. For Elisa, she teaches [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Runes/Perto\|Perto]] and [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Runes/Ehwaz\|Ehwaz]], commending Elisa for her trickery yet reminding her that she should always allow herself the freedom to have such fun. For Ivy, (Rune Needs To Be Inserted). For Lu, he learns [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Runes/Wunjo\|Wunjo]] as a nod to his joyous self. For Roast, she is taught [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Runes/Jera\|Jera]]. 

# Stay With Serissa

They stay with Serissa for seven days, seeing as it would take Lu that long to learn his rune and for Elisa to learn her two runes. During which the party converse more, as they finally discuss Ivy’s situation. From Ivy, the party learns that she is not a treefolk but rather a spirit possessing the body of a treant who agreed to be possessed, one of Ivy’s last living circle members in fact. And seeing as Ivy had no name at the time, she took up the name of the treant. Serissa does voice that Ivy is also deserving of her own name. 

And Roast suggests that the party could help Ivy come up with names if she wouldn’t mind. Grimrid immediately starts spewing out Dwavern name after name. “Mossbeard E “Ironroot E and the like. Lu takes a similar approach, suggesting names that all tie to “plants Ein his native tongue. Elisa refrains from suggestions, sharing that she names her pets FlipFlap, ZipZap, ClipClop. And that she once named a person FriendFriend, and thus decided to never name a person ever again. Roast suggests the name Sylvie, meaning “from the forest Ein her native tongue whilst Serissa suggests “Lif Eand “Gaia Esharing that Gaia was the name of a hero, a protector, much like what Ivy is for her people. 

With seven days passed, Serissa is about to see the party out when she asks why they had been there to begin with. Elisa then realises that the thing the merfolk had sent them to capture might be the princess herself. Elisa comes clean about this assuring Serissa that they would not allow anyone to capture her and would simply help solve the issue the merfolk have on their own. Before the party can go help, they find themselves transported once more. 

# Solaris

## The Start Of The Attack

This time, they are in an impossibly large cavern that houses a large city. In the centre of town is a gargantuan creature of fire that Ivy feels similar to, some kind of primordial spirit. There are thick chains around the bird, forcibly strapping it down as its flame slowly dwindles down into nothing, its energy siphoned to the englyphed stone platform. Looking around the place, the people seem happy, though giant tentacles stretch out from the grounds. Grimrid is bumped into by some children, and pays no mind but notices a familiarity with the girl who knocked into him, taking his coin pouch as she did. 

Then standing before the phoenix is an orc wielding a primordial axe, the orc mutters a promise to free the firebird when the work is done, before capturing its power to use as his. Alongside the Orc is a very beautiful elf and a purple tiefling wizard that the party has met before. On the shoulder of the elf is the same weird bald bird Elisa had summoned before. And behind them, a wave of mind flayers who swiftly descend upon the city. Roast, realising what this scene is, rushes to behind Elisa, wanting to offer her the comfort of not having to witness the downfall of her people again. Elisa pushes the hand away, wanting to see, to know why they did it. 

As the wave of mind flayers arrive the party is overcome with psionics. Elisa is unaffected, her rage at the three that destroyed her city far outweighs the presence of some mind flayers. Ivy and Lu manage to break out of it, as Ivy picks up Roast and runs off, chasing after Elisa who is now depowered, in her old civilian self. Elisa pulls out her dagger, trying to run after the three who destroyed her city. The party enters to see the three kill important members of Elisa’s city, and how they did so repeatedly without saying anything, eerily silent. Before Elisa can attack the three, Lu holds onto her, wanting to protect Elisa as there is no telling what happens to them if they die in this space. 

## Sylaverys and Fool

Ivy steps in, lending a hand in preventing the three from causing further harm to the city as she restrains Sylvaerys who turns to shoot Ivy and with Fool’s help the bullet pierces through Ivy’s bone shield, leaving Ivy hurt but alive. That is, until Fool notices Ivy’s regeneration capabilities and casts a spell, rendering Ivy to dust.  As Ivy's physical body fades, her soul does not, splitting into two as she possesses both Lu and Elisa. She is able to peek into their mind, into the memories that some dare not speak of. 

She sees Lu and the memory of a young man who looks similar as Lu pleads with his son to not leave. With Elisa, Ivy finds an emotional spirit pondering, wondering why the three did what they did. The memory flashes to when Elisa's parents desperately tried to save her at the cost of their own lives. She feels the guilt of being the only one to survive this horrid incident. But at long last, Elisa can finally see what went wrong. Blessed with Ivy's powers, Elisa rushes ahead but is held back by Lu, who drags her far away.

## Elisa's Home

For Grimrid, when he comes to, he hears Elisa's screams but not from the direction the party ran to. He rushes to the sound, stopping right before the house where three mind flayers approach. He attacks them, putting up a fight to save Elisa's parents in this memory-like place. His efforts aren't for naught but there is only so much one dwarf can do against three mind flayers. Grimrid too falls as the Mind Flayers approach to consume his brain. 

# Serissa's Pregnancy

The memory fades into a different scene. The party sees Goetia speaking with Serissa, alluding that some people are in danger. Serissa asks if the Goetia has an item of the people he seeks. And just as she remarks that she can only cast the spell one more time, Goetia stops her. Opening a flaming portal, he pulls out a dwarf and a contract. The dwarf is confused, and before he can say much. Goetia tears apart the contract as the dwarf bursts into Hellfire, his soul a black sludge that Goetia hands to Serissa to use as a mere spell component. Serissa is horrified, refusing to do as Goetia says. Who instead turns to threatening Serissa, the snarls that he will turn her soul into ichor for use if she doesn't do as he commands.

The scene changes once more. Through shadows emerge from the portal, one carrying a primordial spirit and the other has an almost divine level of beauty. The two turn back to see Goetia still inside the Dread Realm, a place where all the souls of the dead are being consumed by the Eldritch Entity that is the Moon. Nyssa, the Primordial Spirit, and Telantes, the Angel, beckon for Goetia to hurry up and leave the horrid Dread Realm. He chooses not to, instead insisting that he could fix all of this, drawing up yet another contract. Nyssa, Telantes, and Serissa watch as Goetia attempts something, but it soon grows clear that he has wasted too much time and the tentacles reach for him slowly sealing his fate.

Despite his threat, Serissa's good nature gets the better of her as she reaches in to grab hold of Goetia in an attempt to save him, but this hope turns to horror, as she throws Goetia out, the tentacles grasp onto Serissa, pulling her into the portal and trapping her. The party tries to help Serissa, those who try to touch her can't, passing through the visions, unable to interfere with the past. Roast attacks the tentacle, able to interact with it but does nothing to help Serissa's plight. The portal closes and Serissa is gone.

# Tasha's Wish

Yet another change of scenery, as the party finds themselves inside of a hag's hut.  The hag lists the components for some spells, a Broken Promise, a Broken God, a Broken Rule and a Broken Heart for a Broken Witch and the previous group of Nyssa, Goetia and Telantes, but they are accomanied by a new individual, an autumn Eladrin (Lariet) steps forward to the party asking them to tell her of their friend Serissa. It seems that whatever the group was doing, they were trying to help save Serissa from the dread realm. With great difficulty, the Eladrin approaches a member of the group, speaking of fey rules and the power of names. 

She provides the broken rule, revealing her true name to save someone she does not seem to know. The blue one heads to the caution taking out an ominous Moon that she senses is true  divinity for casting it into the cauldron, providing the broken God. The primordial Spirit steps up, uttering to herself that she won't use Varik's soul, instead she rips her own primordial heart, greatly weakening it to use it as a component providing the Broken Heart. 

In this moment Ivy finds herself relating with this individual as she too sacrificed a portion of herself for her people. Lastly, the broken promise. As Goetia steps forward, and the air of self-sacrifice he remains indifferent. From within he pulls out the primordial fire, the one he had promised to set free, the Phoenix that he absorbed in Solaris. Lu and Ivy are able to see that much like the primordial spirit, Nyssa, he had options, that there were other promises to break. And yet, when it came to save a friend who was trapped because of him, he still chose himself. With the last broken thing offered, the concoction changes color and the witch drops her blood in with excitement. As the magic flows into the witch, Goetia demands for Tasha, the witch, to cast the Wish spell to get Serissa back. She does so and they leave. 

# Serissa's Return

 They return to Serissa's side who shares that in her time in the dread realm she kept hearing something about a gift. Then Goetia pulls his party aside, telling them that she is pregnant and that it's an aberration. They reveal this information to the princess as Goetia immediately steps forward claiming that the child is evil and that they should get rid of it. Serissa, however, immediately bonds with whatever is in her, referring to it as her child and her baby. Despite the more rational party members attempts to dissuade her, ultimately they respect their friend's choice. All except for Goetia, who as soon as Serissa turns her back, is ready to brandish his weapon, declaring that Serissa holds an abomination within her, implying that even against her will, they should cut out the baby and destroy it. He is held back by his party members and the scene changes once more.

# Serissa's Disappearance

The party views this memory through someone's eyes as they follow the pregnant Serissa's day. One way her attendants worry for her endlessly, but she finds escape with her friend, Bubbles. Then with a clap of lightning, they see her stumble out of her room, moaning in pain, amniotic fluid gushing down her legs. The day of Serissa's escape. With the help of a golden haired dwarf, the princess manages to leave.

Vision changes to a dark place, as Serissa lays against a small bed, losing too much blood. The room shakes and she screams. The more perceptive members of the party managed to find something, anything that could potentially hint at the princess' location. With that, the vision ends. 

# Back To The Present

Back in present time, the party learns from Milton that they've only been gone for a minute. They look to the second orb, knowing that it too would hold information. They take a moment to breathe and process what they had just gone through. Roast approaches Elisa, asking her what that Eldritch portal place was. When Elisa reveals that that is where dead souls are siphoned to, Roast asks how they can go there to end the Moon and its curse upon all who live in Fore Lindrov. 

Elisa reiterates that they would not be going the directly, that instead that might be some other way to defeat the Moon that lies in the castle. Roast nods, assuring that when the time comes to be at the castle, that since it is her home she will be that guide. 

Meanwhile Ivy approaches Lu, she explains that she felt his guilt and saw his last interaction with his son and asks if he's okay. Lu takes out two cups, pouring tea for himself and his son. He opens up about Min. About how he had wanted to charge forth, even though his son told him that it was a trap. Even then what laid before them was not at all what he had expected, yet the one to die with his son. Lu quit, gave up everything. 

Ivy gives Lu a pet, and he hugs her, taking a much needed moment to feel. Then composing himself he says that he's okay and he is simply an old man trying to be what his son would have been. Meanwhile as the rest of the party overhears this conversation, Grimrid is eagerly nodding along, cheering on his friend from the sidelines. 

# Second Vision

The party continues on to the second orb as yet another vision greets them. They find themselves in a room of giant make. Where the king of giants stands before them praying to his God. He ignores the party's presence, instead questioning why God has shown him these smallfolk. Annoyed with his rudeness, Roast shouts at him, telling him to reveal what he had asked his God. She gets picked up rather harshly by the king as Ivy reaches out, grabbing a hold of Roast's ankles. 

And whilst before the King, Roast hides her fear well, showing only a defiant look. Their conversation is interrupted as Nym, one of the King's other daughters rushes into the room, crying about something her mother had said to her, only to be cruelly dismissed by her father. The king reveals what he knows, the downfall of the Ordning, and how his daughter is the key to it all. His demands for the party to seek out his daughter were met with a blunt honesty that they already are. 

Having no one else to rely on but smallfolk, the king cooperates. He promises to leave items for the party to find, ones that would help them in their quest. Mentioning the Eldritch threat, Grimrid receives the [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/References/Runes/Ansuz\|Ansuz]] rune from the king. Lu mentions Storvald and the party can sense even the king knows not what to make of the Jarl. And when Lu mentions the Stone King, Hekaton tells him to leave the Stone King be. In exchange for all the help he has given, the king requests one thing for them to tell his daughter that he has had many regrets but she was never one of them and that he did not abandon her. 

Lu continues to ask the king about the Earthly edict, which he learns is somehow related to the stone King and his further questions only anger Hekaton. In the midst of all the shouting, the door pushes open once more as Serissa steps through, gently reminding her father to be calm. And her presence does calm him greatly, and just as she's about to leave he tells her that he will never abandon her. As their conversation with the King concludes, the vision changes once more.
