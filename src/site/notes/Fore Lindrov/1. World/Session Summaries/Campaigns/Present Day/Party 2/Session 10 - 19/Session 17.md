After being removed from Gallus EHome, the party make their journey back to Blingdenstone in a grim mood. With their primary concern being what to tell Nomi upon their return. After a short travel time, they arrive back at Blingdenstone and seek out Nomi. Who immediately asks how Gallus is, they successfully lie to her, telling her that Gallus is fine and that he has instructed the Gnomes of Blingdenstone to leave him be for the time being.

They also reveal that the source of the corruption of Earth Elementals is something called Ogremoch’s Bane, which they know the location of. Upon their return, the party also decides to take some downtime. Entering the inn, they find “Fall Damage E having drinks with one another with one of their members missing. Upon questioning, they realise that Declan has gone missing, however Krista and Paldron aren’t too concerned as he has a history of leaving to go on Hunts. However the party does realise that Arche is rather nervous regarding this prospect, unable to shutter her worry for what’s happening. 

As the party bid Fall Damage farewell, they head in for the night to rest. Amunkhet decides to kick the gross Eye of Vecna under her bed as she enters her room while Elisa feeds her fellow Solarian Citizen. The party then awakens the next day to look for Glyphic. Who unveils to them that he has found where the final Standing Stone is, they head towards the location however Amunkhet spots a rather strange set of marks on the ground, with Elisa tracking the marks down the path they are headed towards as the party quickly chases away a Ghost who warns them to run away, citing that the Drow are coming to kill them all.

The party then sneak inside the chamber, coming to the Old Library, they spot a Medusa, accompanied by Earth Elementals. She calls out for a little bug that she seems to be hunting, only for the party to spring an attack on them. As they do battle, the “bug Ethe Medusa was hunting is revealed to be Arche, who quickly reveals herself from Invisibility, continuously healing the party and blasting away at the Medusa. With Aurelion having summoned Atlas who quickly knocks the Medusa out.

Arche is slightly shaken by Aurelion having used Brom E soul from the Soul Lantern given to him by Ardor Mor. However he manages to calm her down after revealing that they are seeking a way to fix him, to heal him. As the party ties up the Medusa, Amunkhet attempts to parley with her. Asking for her name and who she serves, being careful not to reveal too much information. However, Kel grows rather aggressive, asking her if she would die for her mistress, causing the Medusa to shut down. 

With negotiations soured, the party argue amongst themselves about whether or not to kill the Medusa, with Vesper insisting that the Medusa needs to die, something that Kel agrees with. To the objection of Aurelion and Amunkhet . As the Medusa slowly attempts to slink away and break free from her bonds, Vesper quickly executes the Medusa. With Amunkhet and Aurelion sorely disappointed at this turn of events.

The party then speaks to Arche, who reveals that the rest of her party has gone missing, with their stuff packed up. She decided to delve into Rockblight alone in an attempt to find them. The party posits that they should stick together, saying that they are also headed deeper into Rockblight for a quest, saying that they will help her find her party. She agrees and the party encounter a large dungeon behind a secret door in the Temple of Callarduran Smoothhands where they met Shar. The party encounters horrific, petrified corpses and an encounter with a Stone Bull. The party swiftly make it through this obstacle and come face-to-face with a strange cloud that zips into the final chamber as they make their way towards it. Entering the final chamber they watch as the strange cloud awakens a group of Elementals that the party takes down, only for it to reform into a massive golem. Prompting the party to use Polymorph to beat down the large construct. The party then acquires the final standing stone to the awe of the Deep Gnomes of Blingdenstone.

Glyphic gifts the party with Diamonds and they search the rooms of Arche’s missing party members, turning up with nothing. Arche says she will continue the investigation on her own and tells the party she will seek them out should she find anything. The party does think of invading The Gem’s Eye on their own, but decide against it. Choosing instead to pursue Ogremoch’s Bane to give the Deep Gnomes their Earth Elementals back.

The party make their way down into the deep reaches of Rockblight where they free a Petrified Deep Gnome named Vort. The party beat their way into Neheedra’s Lair where they encounter 3 Medusa, with 2 of them managing to escape after completely Petrifying Vort again. The party reach into the final chamber where they are met with Neheedra, a Drider turned Medusa and her pet Behir, Zil’Trisst. Aurelion is swallowed by the Behir as Amunkhet is blasted by its bolts of Lightning. They also see Ogremoch’s Bane, a rage-filled Djinni living amongst the masses of Petrified Drow within Neheedra’s Lair. Neheedra tries to speak of peace with the party, stating that in return for their help to destroy Blingdenstone, she will allow them to live. The party rejects this notion even as they lay dying and beaten. With Vesper crying out for help, meeting with a large Orcish Creature who offers her power, in exchange for servitude. A notion that she rejects.

Amunkhet eventually realises that she has a hail mary, taking her Spell Book she threatens to destroy it and kill everyone as if breaking a Staff of Power. Neheedra reluctantly allows the party to go free, although the party lives, they lose out on helping the Deep Gnomes of Blingdenstone defeat the corrupting essence in their city and also the Genie that could fix Amunkhet . 

The party then returned back to Blingdenstone, taking a solemn Long Rest.
