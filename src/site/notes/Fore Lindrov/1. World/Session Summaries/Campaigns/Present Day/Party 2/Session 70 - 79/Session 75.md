## Back Home
With information gathered and decisions made, the party heads back home, a short respite after all they’ve been through. They watch as merchants gather near the entrance of their base, the once hidden space now a bustling hub. 

## Vesper & Dave

The party sees Vesper guiding the children around the market, the ex Sharrite cultists selling trinkets, Dave atop Ygritte exploring the markets. Wystram spots Dave’s injuries and walks over to speak with him, learning that Dave’s burns are not something simple magic can heal, Wystram tries anyway to confirm that is indeed the case. 

## Ront, Grud & Arche

The party also learns that Ront and Grud plan to make a Fighting Pit, saying that Pulliver promised that it would be built soon.. They also show off the sign they made for the marketplace, a prominent, colourful sign reading “Mctpls Ehung proudly on tall wooden beams, welcoming all who visit. They also see Arche shopping around, the Mind-Erased Changeling looking much happier than ever. 


## Kafra

Elisa suddenly senses a presence sneaking up on her, turning around at the last second to pull Kafra into a hug. The Copper Dragon reciprocates sharing that he’s missed having her around. When asked if he had taken any of her stuff, he shared that they have all been very busy, with the expansions and trade, Kafra has been too busy to pull his usual pranks. 


## Rosavalda, Viona & Duncan

Rosavalda and Thorn approach, with Rosavalda handing a black rose that she found in the garden to Elisa before leaving. The party also sees Viona at Eilif’s shop, learning how to carve runes from the Goliath, the woman seeming to take a liking to the mute girl. Elisa spots Duncan skiving off, resting atop a boulder eating food, but gets pushed off by Elisa’s psionics. The two banter but ultimately Duncan leaves, finding a new, quieter place to rest and laze around, avoiding work. 

## Yale, Ratatoskr, Kazarak and Houzuki

In the distance, the party sees Houzuki and Kazarak standing near Yale as he trains. A voice calls out, instructing the young boy. Ratatoskr has taken to sparring and training Yale, the young boy the party saved from the Werewolves of Barovia. Nyktos approaches, offering Lucatiel’s shield to Yale, attempting to dissuade the child’s fixation on revenge though it does not work. Nyktos also speaks with her sister, informing her of the new bastion she’s created. Ynas then approaches the party, warning them of the Underdark as he senses that there’s something wrong there. The party takes his warning, sharing that the Underdark is their next stop. 

## Minerva

Minerva speaks with the party as well, requesting for their help to provide tools of all sorts to help with her analysis, hoping that perhaps she may find something that could either help bring Godfrey back, or to help stop him. They provide all the tools they have on hand and for a moment Minerva looks more like her old Gnomish self, happier and surrounded by tools as she hobbles back to her workshop in the caves of their Home. 

## Preston

Preston has taken to creating works of wire art, setting up a little shop for himself. He asks Elisa of her knight status as the Knights of Radhantia were key collaborators in turning the Party’s Home into a busy, bustling settlement of trade and commerce, questioning the fact that the King of Radhantia, The Silver Hand, King of Kinds, Lord of All Men seems a little too okay to enlist blind children in his army, a stark reminder that Elisa is only 17 years old. 

When his and Elisa’s closeness is pointed out by the rests of the party, Elisa happily declares that Preston and her were both slaves together, they two recount the different people they met, from the strange Serial Killer Buppido, to Gili, to the drow werewolf turned cult fanatic Sarith, to the dwarf Eilith who had a message to pass to her father in Gauntlgrym, to playful miscreant Jimjar, who the party saved from Blingdenstone. 

Preston shares that his works are bought by people from nearby settlements for their children and appreciates the new life the party has brought to their home. Next is Pulliver, who finally has some help with admin and is now able to manage the space without stressing out every single second. 

## Briddihue, Mobi & Bear Bear

Lastly, they speak with Briddihue, the Oblex Elder Solarian Blacksmith from Elisa’s destroyed city, who she met again in Barovia, Mobi a young Oblex Spawn under the charge and ward of Briddihue and Bear Bear, the legless Owlbear the party saved from Barovia. Briddihue has his smithy set up and has received some metals from Nyktos. 

Artorias also returns Elisa her rusted blade, suggesting that it might be better to have a professional look at it. Mobi and Bear Bear are doing well, though Bear Bear pretends to be very hungry when Elisa asks him about his food situation. His puppy dog eyes work immensely well as Elisa relents all of the piranhas they caught to him. 

## Personal Errands
### Wystram

The party then splits up, each doing their own thing. Wystram goes shopping, in search of a bag but comes up empty. Instead they hear rumours of a land ruled by Vampires and rumours that the Knights of Radhantia are stretched thin. As he is shopping, Nyktos approaches, handing Wystram a bag of holding with an embroidered W on it, inside containing a set of thieves' tools and a note teasing Wystram to not break this set. 


### Elisa

Elisa heads back to her room, noticing something off with the Mind Flayer Thrall. She tries to reach into his mind but finds herself stuck, literally, as if the thought stands are being held back. As she forces her way through, tugging at a memory, attached with it is slime and as she looks through the memory with Detect Thoughts, the only words she hears are, “OBEY. EWhatever it is, it doesn’t seem like good news, with her expressing this to the party.


### Nyktos
Nyktos returns to her room as well, pouring out a pool of blood as she connects with the world and its history. She tries to uncover information on the Vonindod, casting Legend Lore and hearing the screams and yells of thousands of dragons that died by its hand. The Vonindod is no mere weapon but instead an 80 ft tall construct made of Adamantine, with ruby eyes and a gargantuan greatsword. 

As the war waged on, bits of the Vonindod fell off as it continued to pillage the lands of life. The conclusion of this war and the Vonindod’s usage being a sea of corpses, Giants and Dragons all alike. Additionally, she learns of its creation. How it took the Giants working together and as a creation of the Giants, it only listens to Giants, attacking any others on sight. Nyktos shares this with the rest of the party as they finally come to grips with the sheer danger that is the Vonindod. 


## Lucatiel’s Funeral
Finishing up their rest the party gathers at the graves to give a respectful farewell to their fallen friend Lucatiel. They bury Lucatiel’s mended corpse with four of his mushroom plushies, leaving him to his eternal rest. On a coin, Elisa carves “Lucatiel, Hero of the Mist. The Light in times of Despair. EAnd on a pebble she writes “Lucatiel, Number One Employee Eas a nod to his fine work when he was alive. Elisa has also lent Artorias a dagger from an old friend, one which Artorias promises to put to good use. 

## Eldritch Happenings in Travel

With matters settled, they head to the Underdark. Upon entering, Elisa senses something wrong, she realises that someone is watching, listening in on her telepathic conversations. Still they travel and eventually seek refuge in a good spot. Nyktos wakes up the next morning, noticing a pin hole in her wrist that is seemingly gone the next second. Periodically, Nyktos checks her wrist for that same hole but finds nothing. When asked by her friends she brushes it off, likening it to a figment of her imagination instead of the new dangers that lurk about the Underdark. 

One night, Elisa dreams of her home, the moment that Lux was taken, when her entire city was enthralled by Mind Flayers, all with the assistance of the Party 1, Sylvaerys, Goetia and Fool. Strangely enough, she finds herself as she was before she began her adventure in her dreams, one before she lost her eyes to a dark deal in Barovia. 

Before she knows it, she’s hurled, straying just shy of a maw as she enters a dark void. Thin snake-like pupil stares at her as she finds herself plunging towards it. A flash of purple light erupts, the sounds of her parents crying, then silence. Her vision zooms in on their fearful eyes as they plead for their daughter’s help. Elisa wakes up from her hellish nightmare.

Another night, as Wystram keeps watch, he sees a silhouette of a man, looking, no, staring at them. Concerned, he unceremoniously drops soil onto Nyktos, waking her up. She huffs, unhappy at her disrupted rest as Wystram points out the man. Nyktos is skeptical but suggests walking over to check if Wystram finds it such a big deal. 

When they walk over, they realise it’s a shadow, a possible trick of the light. Returning back to the tents, Elisa and Artorias Ebeds are moving. Yet when they wake up to look, they find nothing. They rest. The next day, Artorias summons an owl, only realising that it vanished once they turned in for the night. The party becomes very confused, unable to remember if the owl did actually exist. 

Wystram suggests keeping a diary, wanting to record what happens with his eldritch tattoo. They rest once more, and Nyktos wakes up to a horrible scratching noise coming from Wystram. She then realises that he’s been using a rock to carve into his arm. She tries to stop him with words but as he refuses to budge she blood bends him and eventually with his magic manages to snap out of it. 

They all try looking at the symbol but don't seem to know what it is. As the others panic, Nyktos is calm, eerily so. She’s giggly, not finding anything, even her own behaviour as anything amiss. The party tries to confront her, to figure out what is happening to their friend. Artorias requests for Nyktos to heal herself, hoping that the spell will fix the issue. Nyktos is reluctant but picks up a rock, about to use it to cut herself when she instead drags it down the length of her arm, creating quite a big gash. This surprises them as they stop her, disarming her of the dangerous item. 

Meanwhile, Wystram tries to uncover more of the symbol, having the others read it. Nyktos follows the symbol on the ground he’s pointed out and says that they are coming. Elisa soon realises that despite her lack of eyes.. She can SEE the symbol. Zipzap then creepily tells the party that “they Ecan see them, because the party saw the symbol, something that Nyktos, in a seeming delirium confirms, that “they Eare watching, and that soon, “they Ewill be coming here. Elisa tries to confront Nyktos as to how she can identify this symbol, as Elisa herself finds it awfully familiar, and yet unreadable and incomprehensible, this confrontation bears no fruit due to Nyktos Eincreased delirium and lack of cognition. Regardless, the party does agree that they should leave. Though just before they are able to, they freeze. All except Wystram, who now have his party members staring creepily at him. 

He cures Nyktos using Greater Restoration who is then able to cure the others as they begin running out of there. They do not rest, charging ahead, escaping whatever it is that Zipzap keeps saying is coming for them, that now sees them because the party could see them, by forcing their travel without sleep, the party eventually make their way to the Duergar’s home, Karaz Andor. 

## The Gates of Karaz-Andor

But before they reach the gates, the Duergar attack the party, believing them to be enemies of some kind, especially due to their distance form the gates. But the party manages their way through, avoiding ballistas and an ambush by Duergar Guards, Elisa charges her way to the gates, knowing that a friend of hers, one that the Duergar who ambushed the party revealed is now Captain of the Wall, Galnom Forgeripper would recognise her. As she reaches the gates, Elisa’s friend stops the attack and allows them entry, but not before telling them that the Underdark has changed, and something is coming. But before that, the gates are attacked by Fire Goliaths and Stone Giants, the party rushes to defend the city, with Galnom jumping down from the gates to stop the attack alongside them. They manage to defeat this vanguard, with the two Stone Giants escaping with about half of the Fire Goliaths that attacked the city gates.


## Elisa’s Deeds

Galnom goes to fetch the head Cleric and leader of Karaz-Andor, Murram Blackstone, and during their wait, the party is approached by various Duergar that Elisa recognises.  series of people approach Elisa, all those who had received her help from before. The innkeeper, who Elisa helped provide poison materials to from a cave that coincidentally, held Zuggtmoy, a fungal, poisonous Demon Lord of the Abyss, offers the party the chance to buy his alcohol and poisons, giving Elisa a potent poison of Demon’s Blood as thanks for helping him create such a powerful brew. 

Then a Duergar Elisa saved during the Fire Cult’s attack on Karaz-Andor’s mines, who she managed to save without him losing his legs as his legs had been crushed by rocks at the time, gives her a metallic arm drill that can be seared to an empty stump. 

## Meeting Murram Blackstone

The party meet with Murram, Head Cleric and leader of Karaz-Andor, he reveals that something has changed in the Underdark, whatever fragile peace the Underdark races had with one another has been shattered, even Mantol-Derith, the Trading Outpost where the Races could trade together and in peace was destroyed, and that the Duergar of Karaz-Andor are leaving, moving to the City of Blades, a city of Duergar named Gracklstugh. Murram also reveals that the cypher the party has refers to the Symbol of Laduguer, a Deity of the Duergar, and that the “Stone of the Broken Arrow Eis a location. learning some information on the cypher, that the broken arrow refers to the symbol of Laduguer, the deity of the Duergar. Murram reveals that telling them the meaning of the cypher is all he will do, anything more, such as the location of the Holy Site that is the “Stone of the Broken Arrow Ewill require payment of some kind, but before it can be discussed, the gate’s warhorns are blown.

## A Warband Comes

A great warband has descended on Karaz-Andor, one led by Fire Giants, within it are Fire Goliaths, Stone Giants, Goblins and Iron Golems. The party soon realise that the earlier attack was merely a vanguard, and Murram tells them that if they want the location of the Stone of the Broken Arrow, then break the siege for Duergar, as he prepares the city for war. Telling the party that the Duergar are a hardy race, that they were once slaves to Mind Flayers, and that they are no stranger to insurmountable odds, despite the party’s declaration that this is not a fight they can win.

The party head to the War Camp, where they are forced to pass a Fire Giant Guard’s trial before entering, the Fire Giant challenges them to combat, telling them they will not see the leader of the Warband if they do not defeat him, and that if anyone other than him escorts them into the camp, they will be killed on sight. The party easily defeat said Fire Giant and are escorted in, learning that this Warband consists of even Duergar as they meet with the Leader of the Warband, a Fire Giant who gently interacts with a much smaller youngling, the party soon learn that this is Duke Zalto, or rather Zalto Khan, as he reveals that the title Duke was forced upon him by King Hekaton as an act of tyranny, refusing Zalto’s cultural title. He wears a necklace with a Giant rune and adorning his armour is a symbol of a great flaming bird like that of a phoenix. 

## Zalto Khan

The Khan does not at first acknowledge the party, only gently playing with and interacting with what the party assume is his son, Zaltember, the meek boy is clearly easily frightened and extremely weak, and yet the Khan gently eases his son’s nerves. The very son that the party had heard was castrated by the Storm Giant King, Hekaton. 

The party eventually gets the Khan’s attention and are surprised by his respect, he offers them drink, and an announcement that he is soon to be Great Khan of Fore Lindrov, and also offers them protection, saying that envoys are to be treated with dignity and can rest easy, for they are under his direct protection. He firstly applauds the party’s bravery, saying that few men would be so daring as to meet him in his warcamp alone. The party is given appropriately sized cups, but instead of pouring for themselves, the Khan, despite his size shows his dextrous nature, a gentle touch that Nyktos is able to surmise is one practiced, which one could surmise was learned from trying to interact with his extremely frightenable son. The Khan reveals that he seeks to rule over all of Fore Lindrov, and that he structures his leadership through Meritocracy, that he rewards ability, not birth nor blood, even extending this to the party, citing that if the Duergar trusted them so much as to be their envoys, the party MUST be capable people. Though they all reject his offer, each for their own reasons, the Khan is surprisingly respectful of their decision, saying that perhaps one day they will change their minds. 

## Resolving The Siege

In the context of the siege on Karaz-Andor, the Khan reveals that his war requires soldiers and smiths, of which the Duergar are both. That he will take the city, and execute any who resist, but embrace those who bend the knee and accept his rulership. He states that war is not simply about conquering, but also a way to seek out the worthy for the worthy will rise. Zalto Khan views himself as not a tyrant but a strong leader, an enforcer that uplifts those who are strong in brains or brawn. He disapproves of the surrender to the God Emperor during the Thousand Year War between the Giants and Dragons. He plans to not simply to raise the Fire Giant but also to unite everyone under his empire. His ideology involves rewarding people based on their merits instead of their connections. He then asks the party for their opinions, revealing that he is a leader that listens, not just one who believes in domination.

Wystram speaks up first, expressing his opinion and pushing back against the Khan trying to uncover his true intentions. Elisa speaks next as the Khan lowers himself to better hear her. She thinks of his perspective as honourable but cannot compromise, changing the subject as she alludes to the dangers that lurk the Underdark. Artorias offers a different perspective, questioning the Khan’s need to take over as opposed to forming alliances. The Khan responds that an alliance implies equality, and the strength of his hoard knows no equal. 

He then goes on to share that he intends to pass the empire on to his son, which the party finds, but does not outright say is contradicting to what he had said about meritocracy. At this, it’s clear, the talks of empires, of fairness are but lies he tells himself. His true purpose is to secure his son’s future, to make up for the injustice the young boy faced, for the wounds that scar both his body and mind. Nyktos targets this, stating that the Khan’s actions for his son are admirable. Still, unable to give up the Duergar, she instead offers the party’s services. At that, the Khan requests for the party’s help to acquire a Behir, revealing that Giants moulded and crafted the Behirs as weapons of war against the Dragons, but the Fire Giants, upon the Steppes of Fore Lindrov were natural Falconers, also revealing his title of The Roc who will Lift the World that was given to him upon his birth, further highlighting to the party this belief that it is his destiny to conquer all of Fore Lindrov. The party agrees to this and leaves to bring the Behir back before the allotted time is up, with the Khan telling them to bring it to him by Nightfall, or he will take the city.
