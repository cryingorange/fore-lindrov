As the party sit and posit this new offer, Amunkhet, Elisa and Friend-Friend reject Alastair's generous gift. With only Brom accepting. The party meets Alastair in 3 hours, with Friend-Friend and Elisa going off to speak to and check on all of the people in the settlement. They learn that the tensions between the Trolls and Hobgoblins are truly getting high, and Amunkhet thinks on what to do with Ilivara and the things she needs for her mummification. The party then meet with Brom who appears without his armour, Alastair kills him and feeds Brom his blood after he digs out his own grave and imprints the symbol of his order on the wall. He is buried and the next day he rises as a Vampire, before sealing the grave Brom takes his pendant and tosses it into the grave. Representing his rebirth.

As the party is still grappling with this, a werebear bursts out of the time bubble and lands on the ground, dying. Friend-Friend casts Aura of Vitality and heals the bear as chaos erupts. Elisa asks Kleve to call Vizeran as she tells everyone else not to touch the werebear. She casts Detect Thoughts and begins to speak to Bjorn, learning his name and that he was with a group. Brom identifies that the wounds on Bjorn came from something big with large claws.

Vizeran appears and immediately Force Cages Bjorn, sealing him inside. He decides to cast Dominate Person, something that Amunkhet tries to counterspell, in his annoyance, Vizeran also counterspells Amunkhet and makes an annoyed threat to her. In the meantime Kleve is tense and stops Friend-Friend from approaching Vizeran as he makes standoffish comments at Alastair.

Who is being calmed down by Brom and Elisa. As Vizeran makes a threat to Amunkhet for trying to disrupt his work and questioning, with Friend-Friend originally glaring at Vizeran, with Kleve standing in front of him, attempting to hold him back as he becomes increasingly aggressive, even going so far as to try to strike Friend-Friend as he tries to make a move for Vizeran, which Friend-Friend responds with turning into an Ankylosaurus, roaring moving Kleve away, Vizeran immediately responds to this hostility by casting Feeblemind. Reducing Friend-Friend to 1 INT and 1 CHA with the flick of a finger. He continues to question Bjorn who appears unable to really speak, but he also notices that Elisa casted Detect Thoughts to get around this hurdle, something he is very impressed by. He questions Bjorn and deems it safe, although he still tells the party not to touch the time bubble. He teleports away annoyedly and makes a sarcastic comment about Friend-Friend acting like an idiot so he decided to *make* Friend-Friend an idiot. 

Alastair storms off after a discussion with the party to find out how to help Friend-Friend. The party then goes to Dave who reveals that Heal, Greater Restoration or Wish is the only way to reverse what's been done to him. The party knows they have a Heal Scroll but are afraid it might fizzle out. The party then decide to ask Alastair to bring them to the Tower with his new abilities and he agrees to do so. Bringing them to Vanifer she greets them and they cover for Vizeran, telling her about the time bubble and also saying that Friend-Friend became this way due to touching it. They ask she keep their presence under wraps and Elizar uses the scroll to heal Friend-Friend, but not before Elisa calms him down as he begins to have a panic attack.

The party then go to their rooms and rest, with Amunkhet and Elisa having a secret discussion in the room using minor illusion, afraid that Vizeran may be watching. The party then rests with Alastair and they return home, speaking to Bjorn it is learned that he is from Nightfell, and that he was separated from his group. Bjorn swiftly joins the party and promises to earn his keep if they let him stay. Pulliver stumbles in and says the Hobgoblins and Trolls are ready to go to war and the party go to speak to the trolls as Pulliver calms the hobgoblins down. They meet Stakuga who has gotten bigger and says the party is no longer "Da Strongest" and the Trolls don't want to do chores anymore. As the party increasingly becomes angrier and angrier, Stakuga says if they get him a big stick from a Bigg'un he will get his trolls to do chores. The party travels to the location and it is revealed that stuck inside this chamber is a stone giant, it is learned that this stone giant named Sinmara hid away during the fall of Fore Lindrov and waited for her Rockbound Godrick to find her, promising him a surprise when he did. As they open the final door, they see what has happened to Sinmara, her body a shallow husk. She is hollowed, despite their sacrifice to grind a diamond to dust for Greater Restoration, they realise she is already dead. With her final surprise to Godrick having been that she wanted a baby. Spending her days in isolation carving every bit of wall to the bone to prevent mind rot and creating baby cots and toys. Her final statue depicts her and Godrick with their featureless child that she left featureless intentionally to update once they did have their baby.

The party solemnly end Sinmara's life and lay her on the bed, casting mending to fix her Club that held carvings of motherhood and healing her wounds. They leave solemnly and return home, only to be met with crass indifference from Stakuga.

This triggers a fight and Elisa burns the trolls with a Wall of Fire, as the rest of the party attacks Stakuga, Brom climbs up on his neck and smashes into Stakuga's head, knocking him out. As the Wall of Fire clears, the trolls are met with Brom standing atop Stakuga's head, with Izeelzee whispering in his ear, Brom imposes his will on the Trolls and commands them all to Kneel. Awakening his Bloody End, the party then takes some time off as Friend-Friend speaks to Amunkhet about morality and who he wants to be as a person, grappling with how shit the world is. 

The party travels to Karaz-Andor with Brom learning more about his new powers and inability to enter residences. They also meet a Nothic named Wyx, who has been dreaming of the Cult of Eternal Flame. The party directs him and his party to the tower and moves on. The party then go to the mines after arriving at Karaz-Andor where they discover that they have been clearing the mines out. In the meantime Amunkhet restocks on painting supplies and salt and commissions a Canopic Jar for Asha. The party then rush to the Temple of Laduguer where the Cleric, Murram Blackstone asks the party to help him clear out the creatures in the mines so that they can carefully sweep through it to avoid the supposedly horrid beast, Untuk that the party left in the mines.
