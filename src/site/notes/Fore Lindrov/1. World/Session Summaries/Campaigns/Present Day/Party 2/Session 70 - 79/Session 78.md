## FlimFlam & Amnon
As they start to leave, FlimFlam turns around, following the party instead. FlimFlam reveals that they hear Wystram’s thoughts and desires to keep FlimFlam around, despite Wystram’s denial. Lu and Nyktos see through the facade and the shadow of a smile that Wystram tries to hide. Travelling along, the party learns that Amnon came from the city of Gauntlgrym, having left shortly after King Bruenor incited the Ancient Rage of the Dwarves, his parents rightfully predicting that something was wrong. Amnon talks about the fantastical nature of the city after some probing from the party, revealing that it held a Dragon. He also talked a little bit about what him and his parents were doing after leaving Gauntlgrym, that when they came upon the Brightmires of the Underdark, his parents said that they seemed strange, and others that they had met and spoken with expressed the same sentiments, causing his parents to remain as Nomads, never staying in one place for too long.

### Long Rest
The party finds a suitable spot to rest for the night in the Tiny Hut created by Lu. They discuss their plans, with some of them suggesting subterfuge as the best plan to enter the drow’s camp and leave mostly unharmed. Nyktos steps out, taking out the dead bodies of the aboleth cultist and skum that had attacked them. She analyses the priest’s and the skum’s hearts. The Aboleth priest bears a human heart, cursed in some way to become the monstrous form it was. 

For the Skum, they are deformed, their hearts changed by some sort of a disease. Using the blood from the bodies, she sacrifices them to Tenebrous, and performs the ritual to speak with Callahan. She informs him of her situation and tasks him with the job of acquiring any beneficial information from Myrantar. With that, the party rests. The next morning, Nyktos has the party distract Amnon whilst she communicates with Callahan. Before she leaves, the party inquires with Amnon of his hobbies. 

He shares with the party his desire to learn fire magic. Upon hearing his wish, Nyktos gets distracted and pulls out a stone with a rune on it that allows the user to cast create bonfire. Wystram sees this and voices his opinion, thinking it unwise for a child to wield such potentially dangerous magic. Understanding his concerns, Nyktos compromises, letting the child try one use of the stone, momentarily calming him and down distracting him from the stresses of his situation. 

Nyktos steps out, performing her ritual once again as she speaks with Callahan. He conveys that in drow Society males are considered second class citizens. The priestesses hold the most authority and in general cunningness and backstabbing are part and parcel of their lives. The different houses have different specifications, House Baenre is known for their clerical prowess and House Mizzrym for the connection with winged demons. Overall the best way to masquerade as one of the drow is to simply act as if they belong. 
## Drow Blockade
### Sneaking In
Armed with some level of information, the party sets out. The party eventually comes across a blockade marked with the symbol of House Baenre. Wystram offers to scout ahead, he scales the high rocks and finds the path much more difficult to traverse. He does not see much but manages to come back to the party relatively unharmed. Elisa sends Milton to take a look at the enemy's defenses. From which he sees a simple blockade with a singular tent, groups of demons, drow guards, as well as some individuals who had been captured by the drow. Amnon's parents are not amongst the captured, though Milton does recognize one of them, he resembles a dwarf from Gauntlgrym. 

The party regroups, backtracking to find a safe place to leave Amnon and FlimFlam, leaving them in the safety of the tiny hut conjured by Lu. They then discuss their plans. Ultimately they settle on disguising themselves as individuals from House Mizzrym, with Elisa disguised as the matron mother herself. Wystram disguises himself as a consort to the matron mother, Nyktos as the matron mother's priestess and Lu as a slave. 

Adding to their entourage, Elisa summons a draconic spirit, the aspect of Sonngrad, now made to wear a chain around her neck and temporarily treated like a pet and Nyktos summons a spider demon, much to the dragon’s chagrin. With the set up done, the party approaches the blockade. The party speaks with the individual in charge, a Priestess and her consort from House Xorlarrin. From the Priestess, the party are able to gather that House Baenre and Mizzrym are working together, with a blunder on House Mizzrym's end resulting in the loss of one of their strongholds. The party manages to play politics, gaining Elisa, Nyktos a private conversation with the Priestess in her tent. 
### Outside the Tent
Meanwhile, the consort speaks with Wystram, inquiring of his house and not so subtly bragging, wanting to be the better consort. Wystram doesn't back down, instead he throws back some insults, holding his ground against the prideful consort. From their conversation, the party learns of a siege planned for the retaking of Velkynvelve and of a location they call Xunbelaern. Meanwhile, Lu approaches the other slaves, speaking to the Azran who initially called out to Lu, asking for his help in their escape. Lu recognises Azran as a Dwarf from Gauntlgrym based on his visions while he was meditating and asks for Azran's trust, sharing that he knows who Azran is even if Azran doesn’t know Lu and that he is a friend of Elisa’s. 

From Azran, Lu learns that the drow have been sacrificing people, using them to summon demons to their aid. But Lu garners the attention of a drow guard who demands that Lu kiss his feet. In response, Lu pretends to kneel down and trip only to hit the guard and cause him to fall. During which, Lu manages to swipe the keys from the guard. At Lu's misbehavior, the guard is about to strike Lu when Wystram steps in, questioning if a mere guard dares lay a hand on the slave of the matron mother. 
### Inside the Tent
Inside, the Priestess turns to address the matron mother directly, and is unnerved when Elisa doesn't speak to her directly, instead using Nyktos as an intermediary. From the Priestess, the party gathers that it will be about 3-4 days before word comes from the Inquisitor as the Priestess criticises the wavering of High Priestess Shriala. 

The party keeps the Priestess talking, using her insecurities against herself as they continuously imply that there’s more information that she is not privy to. Using detect thoughts, Elisa learns that the Priestess resents her lowly position, cursing the fact that she has yet been promoted to an Inquisitor, and doubting if her sister had deliberately kept information from her, wondering if Lolth had given her sister a similar task. 

A part of her thoughts also reveals a deep gnome whom she had let pass through. Then, as the conversations come to a halt, the Priestess makes one last comment, suggesting that it may not have been the wisest choice to promote a drow from a branch family to the role of a high priestess, saying that escaping the assassination attempt from her mother might have been the High Priestesses only good fortune. 

The party uses this as a chance to criticise the priestess, claiming that for her bold assumptions and insults, that they would take whatever slaves the blockade currently has. At this moment, Lu drops the keys he had stolen, allowing the party to further criticise the blockade’s lack of security. 

And for the final straw, Elisa walks up close to the Priestess, commenting that she can see why they had promoted Inquisitor Taldra over the Priestess. Sufficiently insulted, the Priestess fails to hold back, attacking Elisa who is still disguised as the Matron Mother. This provides the party with the excuse to end the Priestess without fighting the entire camp, which they do so easily, knocking her out instead of killing her. Afterwhich, they have the remaining leave for Velkynvelve, telling them to await their arrival before beginning the siege. 
### Lu’s Honour
The remaining consort approaches, asking if he could join Mother Miz'ri. At first, Nyktos suggests a show, a display of strength between Wystram and the consort, giving Wystram the chance to beat up the man and end him. But the consort rejects the idea, not yet that desperate. 

The party discuss, agreeing that they would have use for his magic but Lu does not agree with the continued trickery towards the consort. Nyktos and Wystram push back, insisting that the man has responsibility for the lives killed, those ruthlessly sacrificed by the drow. Lu understands but believes that the man should be allowed an honourable end. Lu brings up the concept of Agni Kai, a challenge to the failure, used to resolve conflict, where the winner gets their way. In this case, the consort would win his freedom if he kills Lu and die if he doesn’t. The fight doesn’t start off well, with Lu already injured from the previous fight. 

But Wystram keeps a close eye, standing near, ready to protect Lu should the need arise. And as Wystram approaches, Lu calls out, telling Wystram not to dishonour him by joining the combat. Wystram doesn’t listen, he doesn’t join in but doesn’t leave either. Still Lu manages to paralyse the consort, taking him down quickly. Before the final blow, Lu offers the consort a choice, surrender or death. Ultimately the consort does not surrender, and Lu cuts through the man, ending his life. As the consort is killed, Wystram turns away, not wanting to watch as someone dies. 

The consort’s corpse is peaceful, strangely so. Nyktos has her spider demon retrieve the bodies of the captured, wanting to bury them somewhere far from the blockade. Meanwhile, Wystram and Lu talk about what just happened. Wystram isn’t happy with Lu’s actions, disagreeing with the bard’s idea of honour as he warns that he could have died. But Lu insists, stubbornly clinging onto the tradition he grew up with. Hearing their conversation reminds Nyktos of Beowulf, yet another hero who had sacrificed himself, neither aware of the weight that has on the living. 

She interjects Wystram and Lu’s argument, reminding the shadar kai that death is not the end. Her words cause Wystram to think of the resurrection ritual he had attained not too long ago, a ritual that could bring back his friends for good. And for the reckless hero, she tells him that his honour is not worth his death before walking away, not wanting to confront the upset she’s experiencing. Lu continues, stating that he has lived a long life, and it would be worthy to die for honour. At his claim, Nyktos only responds back with  E4 hours E alluding to the short time she managed to spend with her brother before he was cruelly taken away from her, the very short time compared to the decades they would otherwise easily have had. 
### Comforting The Captured
Meanwhile, Elisa speaks with the captives and Azran. Azran immediately expresses concern for Elisa and her eyes but she insists that it no longer hurts. As for how Azran survived being turned like all the other dwarves, he shared that he had left before things got bad, hearing that an orc whispers to the king. Azran shares his disturbance for the new dwarves who now take to killing on mass and slaving. Elisa also comforts the others, made up of merchants from Gauntlgrym and some from the Brightmires. The Brightmires had initially left with the Vizier but then her escort came to their settlement, telling them to run before he slit his own throat. With no choice the individuals ran, and ended up being caught by the drow. 

The party picks up the Drow Priestess Ebody, as Wystram carries her around, knocking her out when appropriate. The party meets back up with Amnon and FlimFlam before deciding to send them to their home, it would be safe for them rather than following the party around, especially when the party are about to enter a Drow camp. 

Amnon is hesitant, pleading the party and promising to hide as they had taught him, however, the party are not willing to let the kid follow them to such a dangerous place. Instead they try their best to console him, telling him of all the fun and nice things their home has. From markets to new friends to a chance to cast magic. That, coupled with the assurance that they would contact him as soon as they could, convinces the young boy to head to their home first. Before they send them off, Nyktos casts a sending to Eilif, alerting her of the people they were about to send through and that she’d like for her help with the child. Eilif is quick to agree and the individuals, alongside FlimFlam are sent to the party’s home where it is safe.
### Ghost Horde 
As the party follows the path, the more observant ones hear a sound, groaning of sorts. The party halts and Milton scouts ahead, this time finding a massive horde of ghosts and zombies. The party intends to send a distraction in the form of a summoned aberration. It manages to push the lever, allowing for the contraption to lower, but before it reaches the ground the aberration is killed by the swarms of undead. And as they do, the party gets spotted. They hold their ground, quickly killing one of the swarms of ghosts, only for it to appear before them once more. This reminds Nyktos of Anastrasia and her horde of ghosts that couldn’t die unless the binding ritual is broken. With an endless wave of zombies and an unconscious Drow priestess on their hands, the party might have to find some other way up the cliff.
