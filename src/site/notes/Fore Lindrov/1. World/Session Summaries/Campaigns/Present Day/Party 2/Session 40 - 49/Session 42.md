From Elisa, the party learns more of her experiences with Ardor Mor. In particular, they learn about the times that she successfully pleaded with him to follow another course of action. Though those moments came with heavy prices. Elisa also shares the time of partial blindness that Ardor Mor had inflicted on her and her then party members. When asked how that blindness came to an end, Elisa connects it to the moment that Vesper had attuned to her new eye, revealing information about the eye and her experiences with it to the rest of the party. The Sword of Kas reaches out to Rook, imparting feelings of curiosity about this eye. Upon confirming that it is the Eye of Vecna, Kas informs Rook of the potential dangers of the eye, how it can and already has begun to corrupt the one that has it and how that could spell danger for those around the eye. But returning back to the main topic at hand, the party ascertains that the tower could provide them with more information about Ardor Mor and begins searching for any bargaining chips that could help them in their future talks with Ardor Mor. They find what seems to be a book about farming but upon closer inspection and use of her new found gift, Ilenti reads the true contents of the book. The book reveals the presence of his lord to be here in what was Nightfell and that if it were to be approached, then many questions could be answered. Including, a question of Ardor Mor’s musing, of him wanting to seek his own purpose and the reason that he was bestowed with unlife. Wrapping up their investigations, Nyktos speaks with Vorun, apologising for the unfair treatment and the blood bending she had done to him. The teenager is surprisingly quick to forgive and the conversation moves on to Vorun’s near death experience with the wraiths. To mitigate that, Nyktos asks for Vorun’s hand, casting deathward on the kid to protect him from any similar situations. 

  

Meanwhile, Elisa tends to Pyrite who seems perplexed as to why others don’t try to do more. Elisa explains that Banecroft had locked up the land of Barovia, preventing any external help. Pyrite pushes asking what of the moon and why powerful figures such as Ardor Mor do nothing about it. To which Elisa shrugs, explaining Ardor Mor’s lack of concern as part of his personality. Pyrite laments that people in power do not do anything and Ilenti stealthily approaches, placing a hand on Pyrite’s shoulder. Using her shadow hand, Ilenti connects with Pyrite’s emotions, gently manipulating and removing the guilt that weighs heavy on his heart. Pyrite feels relief as he gains a new resolve for him and the party to take on the evil that plagues Fore Lindrov. 

  

With that, the party speaks with Gertruda, comforting the girl as best they can before bringing her back to Kislev. Tensions are still high at Kislev, with the people both worried for their safety and a select few, namely Hayden and the Wachter house staff glaring at Alastair and Beatrice. Baron Dimitri greets the party, though less pleased and more stressed out than ever as he sees them arriving with yet another new person. He asks about Gertruda’s arrival and to keep things simple, Nyktos merely shares that the child has run away from home. The Baron accepts before leading the party to his house for more private talk. With the Baron’s insistence and the party’s guilt, the party shares more information of the resistance, informing the Baron of Dame Aylin, Briddihue, Mobi, and Broomhilda. Elisa casts detect thoughts to learn that the Baron is scared, fearful for his family and only remaining child as he weighs his options between fighting or surrendering to Banecroft. In addition, the information of the resistance provides him with a potential bargaining chip to aid in his surrender should he choose to. The party are then told to leave as the Baron and Baroness have much to deliberate about. 

  

Taking a brief break, the party approaches the new merchant hunters in town. The hunters consist of three people; a woman, an older man, and a tall buff guy who seems to be a leader. They behave rather hostile towards the party despite having never met them. And as conversations continue, the old man suggests a bet, that if Elisa is able to hit two targets, she would win two quivers. But if she does not, then she would have to buy two quivers. During this exchange, Nyktos notices the woman, Zuleika glaring at their leader. She attempts to ask about it but it only leads to Zuleika closing up. As the bet commences, Elisa easily lands the first shot, but the second is harder as the old man flings it higher into the sky. But With a little help from Zipzap, Elisa successfully hits the second target as well. The leader, Kiru, flares up in anger, grabbing the older man and shouting at him. The party notices as the two other hunters bow their heads in submission, akin to animals giving way to their alpha. Elisa then offers to pay for one of the quivers, which does help lower tensions slightly. With that, the party leaves. Nyktos borrows an arrow from Elisa, casting identify and subtly comparing the feathers on the arrow to Rook's. Once she has confirmed her theory, she returns the arrow to the druid, not wanting to hold on to it. Meanwhile, Rook remains painfully oblivious to the origins of the feathers. 

  

The party then head to Broomhilda's and make a bold decision revealing to her Wendel's fate. This understandably has her distraught, confused with feelings of self blame and anger towards her mother. During which, Nyktos tries her best to comfort Broomhilda, assuring her that none of it is her fault. Meanwhile, Pyrite continues on with business, wanting the resistance leader to share a message with the rest, a request that falls on deaf ears. As the party leaves, Ilenti secretly sends a message to Broomhilda, telling her that she would be back later to help. Elisa heads off first, going to Briddihue's and informing him of the information they had shared with the Baron and his thoughts about it. The rest of the party catches up and they take the time to learn more about Solaris. Briddihue shares the blood sacrifices that they used to do, and how wonderful their city was. Elisa chimes in, reminiscing of her old friends, the Star Defenders as they had named themselves. Briddihue shares about the leader of the builder's guild, Galar Stonefist. Unfortunately, they lost her in genocide and to make matters worse, her family heirloom hammer was cruelly looted by one of the Moribund, Goetia. They also speak of a half bird man named Kurf, a paranoid individual who did not buy fully into the religion but made great potions. Briddihue also shares about leaders and how they came to be and of the various guilds that existed in their home. The history of Solaris was written in marble, the original three leaders, Ellipsis, Constellon and Asterim. They had landed on the mountain that became Solaris, founding and establishing the city. 

  

Wrapping their conversation with Briddihue, Pyrite approaches Rook to discuss Vorun. Pyrite learns that Rook intends to raise Vorun like a Dra’kar. Pyrite asks for permission to speak to Vorun about matters of the state, and offers to stay near Vorun in combat. Meanwhile, Ilenti takes her rest and sees a vision, short sporadic bursts with a voice saying “I am not afraid of you monster E She senses the presence of someone suffering greatly in the abbey. Snapping out of the vision, Ilenti whispers the information to Elisa, who also expresses concern for the screams from the abbey. Nyktos joins in the conversation as well, noticing the attempt at stealth. The party agrees to check out the abbey. 

  

Entering the space, they come face to face with a muscular blonde man, wielding a sword. He introduces himself as Beowulf, son of Ecgtheow. He claims to have the strength of 30 men that was gifted to him by the gods. However, the party does not recognise the names of the gods he mentions, “Odin Eand “Thor Eare not part of the pantheon the party knows of. He puts on a show, speaking of how he once defeated a demigod in a race, even when there was a bolder in his path, he simply crushed it and won the race. Pyrite asks Beowulf to prove himself and tosses one of his polished rocks at the man who easily catches and with little effort crushes the rock to dust. Rook attempts to do the same, only to find it extremely difficult, thus lending credit to Beowulf’s claims of himself. The party is confused though, wondering why the hero who was sent to clear the abbey a long time ago still stands, and why he seems to think it’s only a couple days for him. Nevertheless, the party continues forward with Beowulf, urging him to look in the well. When Beowulf seems stunned, the rest of the party, excluding Pyrite, look in the well too. They see their reflections, but not the others. Even Rook, who still had his helmet on, saw his face. They feel a deep chill as if something is hiding within their bones. Nyktos, Rook, and Elisa feel their bodies go limp. Just as Elisa is about to fall into the well, Pyrite grabs onto her, keeping her from falling. The other two fall in and Beowulf jumps in without a moment’s hesitation, he catches Nyktos, saving her from the fall as Ilenti grabs a hold of Rook. With everyone safe, Elisa and Pyrite let down some rope for Nyktos to climb back up. Meanwhile, Beowulf simply digs his fingers into the walls of the well and returns to the surface. 

  

The party proceeds into the abbey with Rook and Beowulf pushing the door open. The party notices Beowulf’s sword, lined with runes of religious and magical purposes but does not ask questions about it. Nyktos walks up to the altar, finding a book on the Morninglord, Lathander and a bag of diamond dust, which she pockets. The rest of the party look into the other rooms behind curtains to find small men in black caps that shout to them in Sylvan for them to die. The party springs into battle and as Ilenti casts hunger of hadar, black ichor and shadows reach out to attack the enemies. The party makes quick work of them and as they die, they melt into shadow stuff. Rook and Beowulf work together once more, moving the rocks that hide the stairs with the strength of 31 men. Rook and Ilenti then find pieces of a sword, whilst Elisa finds a book. She opens it to read the words “I’m sorry Beowulf Eon repeat but as she shows the book to others, the content changes to teachings of Lathander. Elisa is confused, confident about what she has seen. Nyktos takes the opportunity to share with Elisa that Beowulf is probably who Bunny was and also shares this with the rest of the party. As they make their way past the connecting walkway, the party sees signs of battle with shattered glass on the floor alongside strands of blonde hair and a small piece of a ripped headband akin to the one Beowulf is wearing. They enter a well lived room with a scarecrow wearing a green tunic and leather pants, sporting a red wig atop its head. There is also a chest that inscribes a riddle to its password. Pyrite reads the Sylvan inscription, and Nyktos answers the riddle, speaking Broomhilda’s name as the chest unlocks. In the chest are a variety of potions and a Raven’s eye. The party then make their way down where they meet an injured owlbear. Casting speak with animals, Elisa converse with the owlbear who stands down upon seeing Beowulf. The owlbear shares that Beowulf always comes here with others and asks if the party will disappear as well. He explains that people disappear into the hole and it’s always the same people, a light lady, a bowman, sword man, and loud man. That there is something in the hole that cries a lot. When asked about the portal, the owlbear shares that once Beowulf came down with a foxman with a book and that was when he broke the portal by cutting it into two. There was also another time when a man with pale skin, sharp teeth, and pulled back black hair emerged from the hole to fight Beowulf, breaking his sword in the process. They also learn that the owlbear has no name and promptly names him Bear Bear after some discussion. The party feeds Bear Bear some rations and dire boar meat as he shares about one time when a big skeleton guy with long fingernails was here. Elisa then has Bear Bear hide in a corner whilst she mends the portal. The portal activates, showing the party a massive land, with waterfalls, rivers, and a mountainous landscape with the dark black sky. Nyktos then hears the Lord of Blood in her mind, informing her that this portal leads them to the land of Gehenna, where Yugoloths come from. They unfortunately catch the attention of a Yugoloth as it approaches the portal, its hand reaching out for them as Rook breaks the portal in half, severing the connection. Leaving the portal for now, the party heads down the hole that Beowulf has been staring at this entire time.

  

They come face to face with a beast, but Beowulf seems to recognise the being, calling her “Markovia'' before disappearing. The beast calls out in deepspeech, saying that something is arriving, that there’s too much guilt. The party split their efforts, with the rest focused on fighting Markovia in an attempt to knock her out as Nyktos tries her hand at curing Markovia of whatever curse or madness she’s been afflicted with. But as she channels her magic, the first time, nothing happens. Instead, eyes begin to appear on the walls and the party feels the effects of the eldritch horror mutating their bodies. Soon, Markovia falls and is knocked out as the party decides what to do. Nyktos tries greater restoration a second time only to find that the body has no soul. With that information, the party has no choice but to end Markovia’s life. Pyrite readily offers to use his blade to end her life, and so does Ilenti, the two seemingly having the least apprehension about doing it. Nyktos offers as well, expressing that mayhaps a cleric of Lathander would prefer to die with light. Meanwhile Elisa and Rook notice as the walls begin to move, the eyes coalescing into one as time goes on. They raise the concern, and Nyktos mutters a quick prayer for Markovia before casting sacred flaming, allowing the flames to take what was left of her. As the exterior of the beast burns, the party is left with the corpse of a woman, and watches as her soul emerges. It’s Markovia, she introduces herself and how she had joined Beowulf. She does ask what’s happened to him but the party keeps what they know a secret, not wanting to burden Markovia. She then asks for the party’s help, requesting that they bury Rothgar’s and Thorn’s bodies. She then shares how Beowulf was drawn to Barovia, seeing it in his visions. But after they had destroyed the portal, Banecroft showed up, calling Beowulf the lesser child of chaos. And how Beowulf, wanting to save what was left of their party, had given himself up so that she could live. She goes on about how she wants to help Fore Lindrov and was given a saint’s body that could be used even after her death. Her bones could be made into weapons or buried to cleanse any ground. Nyktos agrees, understanding the use of one’s body for spell components. Ilenti then takes the opportunity to speak with Markovia, distracting her as the party proceeds to harvest Markovia’s bones. 

  

They then return back to Kislev, with both Markovia and Bear Bear in tow. Elisa settles Bear Bear down at Briddihue’s teaching him to not eat anyone, especially Mobi. Pyrite takes the chance to speak with Vorun, educating him that those with power are able to build up the world, for better or worse and that he should make rational, well-informed decisions. Following that, Rook speaks with Vorun as well, expressing his opinion. Rook emphasises that leading should not be about bullying others but to lead because you care. Ilenti returns to Broomhilda, holding up her words from before. Ilenti extends her hands out to hold Broomhilda’s assuring her that the party and herself would not let Broomhilda go through all of this alone. Although she cannot tell Broomhilda if she should or should not hate her mother, Ilenti understands what it feels like to be betrayed by someone she looked up to. Ilenti iterates that it is not Broomhilda’s fault, that she had no say in the decisions her mother made. And once more, using her powers, Ilenti absorbs part of the sadness and conflict that resides within Broomhilda, hopefully allowing her to better process her feelings about the matter. Whilst that is happening, Nyktos gathers the rest of the party to speak with them. She apologises to them, feeling responsible for a lot of what’s happened so far. Nyktos apologises to Rook, suggesting that he would not have died if she had not alerted Banecroft of their presence. The Celestial, however, maintains his sunny disposition, thanking Nyktos instead. Still, the shadar kai continues, stating that she’s done nothing but mess up ever since they entered Barovia and the party is quick to cut her off. Elisa comforts her by saying that everyone makes mistakes and in Nyktos Ecase she had no way of knowing what her actions would have led to. Regardless, Nyktos thanks the party for being there for her and for all of their help. She vows to help them too, promising to dedicate as much of her life for them as they have for her. Pyrite accepts, understanding how Nyktos would want to repay her presumed debts whilst Elisa and Rook insist that there is no need for debts between friends before relenting. Elisa says something that takes Nyktos by surprise, telling her that she is worth their efforts. The conversation then turns playful, with the party joking about Rook’s lack of gold. The atmosphere is nice, warm, and at that moment Nyktos feels closer to the party than she’s ever been. Nyktos then takes a moment to speak with Kaze, wanting to learn about Alara, though the sword does not provide much new information. 

  

Alastair and Beatrice then approach the party, with Alastair sharing that a spawn is to be controlled by their maker. Beatrice then brings up a difficult request, asking the party to promise to kill her should the time come. Naturally, the party does not agree, all voicing out that there must be another way. Ilenti, however, subtly messages Beatrice, assuring her that she would follow her wishes. Alastair chimes in, sharing that there is another way, that Beatrice could instead ascend into a true vampire and gain freedom from Banecroft’s control. When questioned why now, Alastair simply states that he respects her, a lie that only Nyktos notices. The party then prepares for Beatrice’s ascension. Pyrite performs a ceremony, and Rook pulls out one of his feathers, blessing Beatrice in this ritual. Nyktos speaks with Beatrice, assuring and affirming her that she deserves this and wishes from the bottom of her heart that Beatrice can be truly free once and for all, to be allowed to pursue her heart’s desire. At this, Beatrice looks over to Alastair. Channelling her blood magic, Nyktos helps Beatrice calm down by connecting their emotions, allowing a wave of calmness to wash over her. Next, Ilenti walks up to Beatrice, puffing out smoke that envelopes Beatrice as the thick darkness surrounds them. She casts a message, promising to help Beatrice should she ever require anyone to do anything distasteful and vile. Finally, Elisa uses her magic to grow black roses surrounding Alastair and Beatrice, creating a nicer environment for Beatrice’s ascension. Alastair slits his wrist, uttering the words that “In blood there is freedom, and in freedom there is eternal life. Where life has failed you, unlife shall not. EWith that, Beatrice ascends, forever freed from the shackles that once bound her. The party rejoices and celebrates before turning in for the night. 

  

During their rest, Ilenti approaches Vorun, inviting the tired boy out for a drink. The younger being notably excited at the prospect of being alone with Ilenti. Ilenti takes out the bottle of Dragoncrush wine and drinks straight from the bottle. Vorun follows suit, taking a swig from the bottle whilst trying to act tough. The rest of the party notice this interaction, with Elisa pointing it out to the rest who are mostly puzzled and amused by the situation. They keep a keen eye as Ilenti tells Vorun that he does not have to act tough, advice which the teenager immediately follows. Ilenti goes on, teaching Vorun that it is alright to let the world pass him by. The party, still watching this, begins to place bets on the two. Elisa suggests a bet on how many days would pass before Vorun risks his life for Ilenti and eggs Rook on to join the bet. Eventually, Elisa and Nyktos both bet that it would be the next day whilst Rook bets on two days later, with Pyrite abstaining from the bet. Vorun waits expectantly only to find a cigarette before him as Ilenti offers him a smoke, whilst the others watch, confused as to what Ilenti is doing. Vorun eventually leaves, turning in for the night and Nyktos walks over to speak with her sister. Nyktos apologises for sending Ilenti away to Banecroft, and causing her pain once more, though emphasises that she never regretted saving her sister but feels remorse as to how things ended up. But Ilenti shows compassion, understanding that her sister only did what she could to save her. Having gained her sister’s forgiveness, Nyktos tells Ilenti that she’s giving Vorun the wrong impression before heading to rest. The small warning, however, goes completely over Ilenti’s head. 

  

The next morning the party sets out to where Agatha should be. As they traverse through the marsh, vision blocked by thick fog, Nyktos hears a voice out in the distance, whispers calling for help. Without hesitation, Nyktos runs after the voice, which prompts Ilenti to run after her. Seeing the person he cares about the most rush off, Vorun chases after Ilenti only to be stopped by Pyrite who warns him against the dangers of running off alone. Nyktos rushes closer to the voice and sees a body leaning against a tree. She turns the corpse around only to see her face before it disappears and she’s left there alone, her party nowhere to be found. Ilenti initially manages to follow Nyktos before losing her as well. As Ilenti wanders alone, she encounters a bunch of insects that land on her and sting her. But being relatively unbothered, Ilenti casts Hunger of Hadar centred on herself, taking the damage whilst killing all the flies around her. Pyrite leads the search team, casting locate object twice to find both the sisters. As they continue on, Ilenti and Pyrite accidentally step into piles of hissing snakes. The former teleports to her sister’s side and the later cautiously retracks his foot, both proceeding on without much issue. 

  

They then arrive at a place where the fog is thinner and they meet two children similar to ratboy named “Otto Eand “Zygfrek Eplaying around the large pillars of stone. The children halt the party, asking them why they’re here and informing them that Mother isn’t around. Ilenti successfully fibs that the party has an appointment with Mother and is here to wait whilst Nyktos slips up in her lies, raising the suspicion of the children. The children test the party, asking if they knew Mother’s colour and with a detect thoughts, Elisa easily gathers the answer. The children mention the others being in the cottage before leaving the party to do as they wish. Elisa takes a look at the druid circles, reading and understanding that the stone holds great power before draining the power for herself. They then head towards the cottage, with Elisa phasing through the wall and Nyktos connecting herself to see and hear through Elisa’s senses, describing the sight to the rest of the party. Elisa enters to witness eight other children kept in cages, locked up in barely livable conditions all covered in their own grime. Hearing the words, the rest of the party are in shock and anger at Agatha. Rook breaks the lock to the door, allowing the rest of the party access to the kids. Even Vorun speaks up at the sight, clarifying that the party would not let Agatha live. Meanwhile, Elisa speaks to the children, learning their names; Ben, Tim, Mara, Arlu, Arnold, Mills, Fin, and Norman. The children behave like pets, eager to please and desperate for food, attention, and praise. Elisa feeds each of them a goodberry, satiating their hunger for the time being as the kids eagerly show off what they’re able to do. They detect Elisa’s thoughts, and share that they would be able to make people do what they want to, they could also slow people down, make them stop moving, or even make them friends. The children share that they were to be showcased to Mother’s friend, a sentence that only angers the party. Whilst Elisa and Nyktos finish up cleaning the children with prestidigitation, Rook, Ilenti and Vorun have already run off with Pyrite following to keep an eye on them. The three, filled with rage, storm to Agatha’s house, ready to barge in without care. As they approach, Pyrite casts detect magic, sensing an abjuration spell on the door. He tries to stop the three but to no avail and rushes back to the rest of the party, hoping that they can stop the three instead. He meets Elisa and Nyktos along the way, hurriedly explaining the situation to them. Not willing to take the risk, Nyktos dispels the magic at the door, removing the spell that protected Agatha’s home from them. Upon entering, the party sees an illusion of a younger Broomhilda dressed in a green dress as she tends to the home, tidying the place. The party enters the basement after Rook dispels the magic that kept it shut. Downstairs, they come face to face with cages containing the skeletons of children, of the children Agatha experimented on. In the middle of the room is a magic circle containing both mindflayer tadpoles and soul larvae. Zephyros explains the usefulness and rarity of soul larvae whilst Pyrite advises the party against reacting hastily. But the rest of them are too filled with rage to be level headed like he is. Ilenti fireballs the room, burning every single last soul larva to crisp. 

  

Elisa, Rook, Vorun, and Pyrite return back up to the surface, proceeding to open the chest for anything useful. Meanwhile, the sister’s are having a conversation in the basement. Ilenti muses if their mother is doing something similar as well. Nyktos shares that the thought has crossed her mind too, and expresses her lack of care for their mother before asking Ilenti how she feels about their mother. The younger sister hesitates, having more conflicting emotions when it comes to the topic of their mother. Seeing her sister struggle, Nyktos shares that to her, their mother had died when she left, and that if push came to shove, she’d be willing to end their mother’s life. Ending that conversation, the siblings head back to join the party who have triggered the trap on the chest. Though it does not faze them in the slightest. Opening the chest, they find many useful items which they loot without a second thought, not knowing how soon Agatha may return.
