Having killed the fishes, Lucatiel walks over to one of them, using the boon Psilofyr had granted to turn the corpse into a plant. The party heads back to where Psilofyr resided, digging into the beans to find him still there. As Lucatiel once more reluctantly melds with Psilofyr and their spores come into contact with the party, they feel an overwhelming sense of curiosity, as if they were feeling someone else’s emotions. The party is slightly weirded out by it, experiencing what it’s like to meld for the first time. They all hear Psilofyr’s voice as he addresses Lucatiel, criticising their magic and granting them a new form of magic instead. Lucatiel’s spore cap lights up, turning from blue to purple to green as the fey magics weave an armour around their body.  
  
As one final support, Psilofyr speeds up time for the party, allowing them to recover their magic and energy. The party are about to leave, continuing their quest to save Sandy when the big metal door piques Tiny’s interests. Tiny manages to pull the door open without much issue, walking through to find an array of blue oxen meat. Nyktos hears a voice and upon closer inspection hears a “Moo E the voice reveals itself to be the spirit of a blue oxen who was slaughtered to be food. The party manages to befriend the blue oxen who longed to travel and see the world. Tiny and the party agree to help the blue oxen, giving him the name “Mr Moo E As part of their deal, Tiny acquires Mr Moo’s thighbone that now contains his spirit. 

  

The party then heads down, reaching a storage facility of sorts where wine and fabrics are kept. Nyktos immediately heads to the fabrics, stealing an array of fabrics into her bag of holding. Meanwhile, Elisa and Lucatiel found a rat. Elisa converses with the rat, Vinnie, learning that there are seven Sandys and how the other rats ostracise Vinnie for being smaller than they are.  
  
Tiny heads to the massive bottles of alcohol, uncorking one as a wave of wine gushes down, flooding Vinnie’s home, nearly drowning Vinnie. Elisa saves Vinnie, and proceeds to reproach Tiny for putting Vinnie in danger and destroying his home. Lucatiel helps clean up the mess, freezing the wine back into its bottle whilst Tiny eagerly jumps in, licking the frozen wine. Elisa uses her telekinesis to cork the bottle, preventing Vinnie’s home from flooding once more. As an apology, Vinnie demands that Tiny kiss his hand before the rat forgives him. However, the noise they had made attracted the attention of the other rats and the caretaker. Battle breaks out, and the party deals with the rats easily enough. Nyktos blood bends the ogre, puppeteering him to kill his own rats, including the one that’s perched on his shoulder.  
  
With them dead, the party enters Sandy’s habitat to find a seven headed huge goose. They speak with Sandy who seems utterly uninterested in returning to their employer, Parsley’s side. The party puts on a ruse, painting a picture of a sad, pathetic looking Parsley bemoaning the loss of his great companion Sandy. The lies and flattery work, and with the party’s words that Parsley promises to do anything to get Sandy back, she’s convinced to follow the party back when they’re done. 

  

The party heads back upstairs, looking through the orbs that seem to depict the story of how the Fomorians came to be. Originally Giants with elven-like ears who had ambitious plans to invade the feywild. These Giants were pursuers of the arcane arts, all highly skilled in magic and carried themselves with the conceited behaviour one might associate with a high elf.  Though their plans fail, losing the war against the fey and as a result are cursed to take on a hideous form, their skins turning purple, their bodies deformed in horrid ways.  
  
Zephyros provides additional information that a being known as Karontor had whispered to the Giants, egging them on to take over the feywild. Having uncovered the history of the Fomorians, Elisa, Tiny, Zipzap, and Nyktos get side tracked, each going about their own thing when Lucatiel and their summon Club head forward to find an interesting looking door. Nyktos heads to steal some of the fancy carpet The Golden has whilst the others proceed to enjoy the comfortable chairs. Lucatiel manages to lure Nyktos to where they are, speaking of a large carpet that she one again attempts to take. Though not before a trap is set off and a loud explosion occurs in the hallway. Having taken some damage, they take a seat, resting as Elisa heads towards the door to study it. What Elisa learns is horrifying.  
  
The door is decorated with creatures from the far realms, accurately recreated upon the door. Meaning that whoever created this not only has been to the far realms and witnessed all these, they managed to process it too, turning what most would find incomprehensible into decorative carving on the door. Her interest has Nyktos taking a look as well, only for the Shadar Kai to feel a wave of unease wash over her, unable to get the image of the door out of her mind no matter how hard she desperately tries. Likewise for Tiny who also takes a look at the door.  
  
But Elisa says the password, letting the party through the door. They come across a room, its door flanked by two beholder like statues. Cautious, Nyktos summons Ruby, asking him to slip through the doors to take a peek. Ruby confirms that there are no dangers behind, though him going through informs the party of the trap the two beholder statues have. Through some effort, they realise that the eyes of the beholders would fit into the door, allowing them to unlock and disable the traps. The party explores the room, with Nyktos rushing to the corner to where a chest lies.  
  
She has Ruby attempt to unlock the lock, setting off the trap that causes Ruby to be feebleminded. Exploring the rest of the room, the party finds a letter addressed to the owner of the room. The letter is from Zaiga to their sister, thanking their sister for what they have done, revealing that their sister had lived in the far realms and somehow made her way back home.

  

As the party heads up, they find trapped civilians as well as a warforged bearing the Sparkhammer symbol that only Tiny recognises. The warforged requests for Tiny’s help to free him. Meanwhile the Slaadi launch into combat. Tiny rushes in, eager to help their fellow friend, with Nyktos doing the same to hold the bad guys off from attacking the innocents.  
  
The commission rouses the sleeping Fomorian, Zaiga who immediately attacks the party with a hunger of hadar, hoping to rush them out of their safe spot. Elisa deals with that quickly, breaking Zaiga’s concentration on the spell and saving her allies from any further damage. The others try to make quick work of the Slaadi, but still take some hits from them. During combat, Tiny invokes the wild magic surge, and as a result, Lucatiel ends up uncharacteristically confident with their new look. Just as the party thinks they’re winning, one of the trapped civilians turns into a Slaad.  
  
The warforged explains that they have all been infected and that they had potions of vitality that would cure them but they were taken. Blade, the warforged, rushes into a room alone with Zaiga, taking on the Fomorian alone, buying the party some time to heal the innocents. Lucatiel, who had retrieved the potions, signals to their allies that they have the cure so long as everyone could help hand them out.  
  
Misreading the signal, Tiny blurts out that Lucatiel is a genius and proceeds to pick up the small Myconid, throwing them in the direction of the cages. Fortunately Lucatiel does a flip mid air, landing as they slide to a stop before the cages. Lucatiel places one of the potions on the ground and dramatically extends their hand, offering a potion to the other, saving two of the remaining three civilians. Meanwhile, the last is cured by Nyktos who teleports before them, the party working together to free them from a horrible end. Tiny rushes to help his friend, showing up just in time as Blade is on their last legs. Working together, the party makes quick work of Zaiga, killing them once and for all.  
  
The survivors express their appreciation for the party, once more asking for their names and promising to tell stories of their good deeds. As usual, Elisa and Nyktos try to deny any names whilst Lucatiel and Tiny proudly proclaim themselves as the “Heroes of the Mist E Tiny speaks with Blade, learning Blade to be forged by Reggie, Tiny’s dad, though Blade has no recollection of such information, they two still bond over their commonalities. In Zaiga’s room, the party finds a journal with an entry that hints to what happened to The Golden, including their name, Ziantha. The party heads up once more, coming face to face with The Golden and her entourage.  
  
One of which is Lux, this time in his human form. Lux assures Elisa that all is alright and that things have changed. He tries to convince Elisa to return back to Solaris, promising that no one is a thrall any more. The party are sceptical, all reminding Elisa to be vigilant, recalling the visions that she saw when she connected with the Elder Brain. Elisa feels a rush of her other patron’s magic from the portal, connecting to it as The One Of The Stars speaks to Elisa, telling her to close the portal that Ziantha is forming. Elisa sees a flash of Solaris, understanding in the moment that Ziantha is channelling energy from Solaris, syphoning to the moon.  
  
As an added measure, Elisa activates her truesight, seeing Lux for what he really is now; a mind flayer. Elisa connects to Lucatiel, seeking their help which Lucatiel confidently responds with a wall of ice that separates the enemies from them. Lux teleports away the moment he is able to, escaping for now whilst the party is left to battle the rest. Elisa identifies the mindflayer to be a survivor from Solaris and the party works to keep him safe. 

  

Thanks to Lucatiel’s wall of ice, the enemies are forced into a suboptimal position. Coupled with the feebleminded Ruby rushing forth, the enemies are unable to reach the party, giving the party the upper hand to easily kill those they need to. With her allies now dead, The Golden is forced to take matters into her own hands. She begins to monologue, boasting of her prowess and intelligence to the party. She asks the party if they killed Zaiga, not even batting an eye when they confirm his death. Ziantha is cruel, unfeeling and psychotic as she brags about having been around beings like the moon and how she’s managed to survive and live in the Far Realms.  
  
Lucatiel responds with some quips, insulting Ziantha, claiming that wisdom has clearly been chasing her though perhaps it is far too fast for her. Ziantha answers the party’s queries, explaining that the mind flayers are simply a means to an end, and the Solarianss are fuel for what she has to do. And that her gifting the golden eggs are merely a boon she bestows upon the current leader as a show of The Golden’s might. Ziantha claims that *it* will arrive soon, cracked from the little egg. Elisa’s last question is to ask what’s in it for the mind flayers, which Ziantha affirms that the mind flayers have joined the moon simply as a desperate bid to survive.

  

Ziantha begins to speak in an incomprehensible language, both similar yet different to the ancient language Nyktos speaks. Where the ancient language is simply one that has been long forgotten with time, the language Ziantha speaks is foreign, unlike anything anyone has ever heard before. Though her attempts to cast the spell are thwarted by Elisa who uses her psionics to counterspell Ziantha, leaving her to try to wonder who broke her spell. Lucatiel steps up, pretending to be the one who did it, incurring Ziantha’s wrath.  
  
Though a being emerges from the portal, a horrifying creature with an immense amount of arms for limbs emerges through the portal. The sight of which breaks Crimson and Lucatiel’s mind. Crimson manages to pull through, able to process the truth of the Eldritch horror. Though when Crimson next speaks, rather than bark as he usually does, he simply opens his mouth as the horrid, incomprehensible language sounds out from the small pup. As for Lucatiel, their heart weakens, as they falter, such a reality could not possibly exist.

  

Still, despite their condition, Lucatiel puts on a brave face, toeing the line that is Ziantha’s wrath. Tiny, Blade, Lucatiel and Elisa channel their resources on Ziantha, with Elisa breaking The Golden’s concentration, revealing her mage slayer abilities. Nyktos disables the eldritch creature, slowing it down to render its many limbs useless as Ruby blocks it with his body whilst Lucatiel adds a wall of ice in front of it, providing additional obstacles for the monster.  
  
Seeing as her only ally has been walled up, Ziantha summons two Slaadi, targeting Elisa and Nyktos respectively. The party works hard to humiliate Ziantha, knocking her prone and insulting her at almost every turn. Wanting to take back some control, Ziantha reaches out towards Blade, attempting to pick him up when Lucatiel reaches up from the ground protecting Blade from any attacks, stating that they wouldn’t allow Ziantha to hurt anyone. Lucatiel stands up, pushing past the adversity and gathering the courage as they overcome the madness in their mind.  
  
Things begin to take a turn as Lucatiel’s determination inspires the rest of the party. Sensing this, Ziantha attempts yet another cowardly approach, teleporting and walking towards the unconscious Solarian mind flayer as she picks him up and threatens to rip a void from within him. Elisa tries her best to protect the Solarian, casting sanctuary on him but Ziantha pushes past the spell, determined to make the party pay for what they have done. It is then that Elisa hears the voice of Milton and immediately tells him not to sacrifice himself like last time. Though Milton assures her that this time it’s different, that Elisa’s god says it would be different. Listening to her god, Elisa opens the portal once more, ripping the very fabric of reality to do so.  
  
At Ziantha’s threats, Elisa gives in to her god, finding herself now within an eldritch sea in Ziantha’s mind. Elisa sees the vast knowledge Ziantha has, standing eye to eye with Ziantha as she remarks that they are not fuel. At that moment, Elisa’s patron offers her the chance to send Ziantha’s soul into the far realm under the direct attention of her god. Agreeing, Elisa grabs Ziantha by the hair, dragging the once proud Fomorian against the floor, stopping in an empty space.  
  
She reaches a hand out, ripping the fabric as she feels her patron’s embrace. Elisa comments that it’s now Ziantha’s turn to be fuel and chucks her into the portal, enjoying the fear in Ziantha’s eyes as she drifts off. From the rip Elisa feels a mother’s warmth, quietly commenting that she hopes she did good. Elisa carries the Solarian mind flayer to the void, asking for her god’s help to fix him.  
  
A part of a being begins to emerge from the portal, forming the same finger Elisa once saw in her city. Elisa reaches out to touch the woman’s hand as psionic energy floods her being, channelling to the Solarian as his skin begins to rot, sloughing off the mind flayer skin to reveal the person beneath it. At that moment, the hive mind realises that one of them is gone, and not dead. 

  
The rest of the party sees as Ziantha’s eyes go blank, falling to the ground. And the mind flayer she tried to hold hostage turns back into a human. They all turn to Elisa, who returns to her body and rushes up to the healed Solarian. She holds the Solarian tight, muttering that her goddess had helped fix him.  
  
The party rejoices with Elisa, as Nyktos affirms that although her goddess may have helped, the Solarian could only have been saved because of Elisa’s efforts. The two share a hug as emotions run high for the party. Elisa has successfully saved one of her people, who is sure to be the first of many.
