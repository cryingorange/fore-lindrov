The party collects the magic items on Fool, namely her spellbook and her Black Robe Of The Archmagi. Lucatiel is tempted to take the book, interested in learning the magics that are kept within. Though Elisa is insistent on burying the items, not wanting the powerful magics to fall into the wrong hands and not wanting to put a target on their backs should Fool realise they carry her belongings.  
  
The party agrees, splitting into two groups and each walking a distance away to bury the items. Elisa and Tiny walk 51ft North, digging a 30ft deep hole and dumping the spellbook down. Before the hole is covered up, Tiny urinates on the book to further insult the wizard. Meanwhile, Lucatiel and Nyktos walk 101ft South, creating a hole only 8ft down before leaving the evil robes there.  
  
Once returned, Elisa modifies Tiny’s memory to forget the exact location of the hole. They then proceed to collect the bodies of the Solarians, building a funeral pyre before holding a small ceremony for them. Elisa speaks to the Solarians, assuring them that they would all be returned to their Starry Mistress Eside soon as she vows to keep the memory of them alive. Graced by their goddess, purple flames emit from Elisa’s hand, lighting the pyre and putting the Solarians to rest.  
  

With that settled, the party wakes up the scout, Carver. The party learns not much from the scout, though does inform him of his fallen friends. WIth that the scout soon leaves, returning back to his camp. The party doesn’t follow, currently unable to mask their presence as some of them smell like maple syrup still. 

  

They return back to New Phandalin though are stopped along the way as a civilian rushes up to them begging for their help to stop a malicious giant that is killing their people. Leading the way, the man explains the Giant’s appearance and of how they sent the men of the village to attack the Giant.  
  

The party rushes to help, especially Tiny who is eager to kill a Giant. Though instead of a ruthless massacre, the party sees a Giant not holding any weapons and a mob of people who are trying to attack him. Beside the Giant lay not the corpses of his enemies but the unconscious bodies of those who had attacked the Giant, mercifully knocked out. Looking at the situation, Nyktos understands that the Giant is simply protecting himself, cautious of the humans who will not stop attacking him. She calls out to the people, urging them to leave the Giant alone.  
  
Meanwhile, Tiny rushes up, dealing a substantial amount of damage to the Giant who in response pulls out his club. The humans are terrified, painting a picture of the Giant’s actions as an aggressive act towards them. The humans rush towards the Giant, harassing the Giant until Nyktos is forced to put them to sleep. Elisa helps the Giant, turning Tiny into a turtle once more whilst Crimson grapples him. Just then, one of the villagers starts acting weird, an uncanny smile gracing their face as they turn into an eldritch creature before everyone.  
  
It begins attacking one of the other men, turning him into a similar creature. The party and remaining awake people freak out from the sight of it as the party works to stop this madness. In an attempt to save the others, one of the creatures is killed, whilst the other remains alive, knocked unconscious by Elisa.  
  
Nyktos cures the alive man first, relieved to know that the man could be saved. She goes to cure the other body as well, allowing him to be buried as a man rather than a creature. The frightened villagers begin dragging the body, their prejudice towards the Giant overwhelmed by the terrifying powers of the moon. 

  

The party speaks with the Giant, learning his name to be Godric. Godric shares that he is in search of someone precious to him, his mate. Nyktos and Lucatiel offer their help, though the Giant politely refuses, stating that this is something he must do on his own. Elisa is notably quiet during this conversation, offering simple well wishes to Godric. As thanks, the Giant warns the party of some malicious small folk, that two massive groups are currently headed towards the direction where New Phandalin is. 

  
Elisa, Nyktos, and Lucatiel all bid Godric farewell, whilst Tiny takes the opportunity to call Godric by a slur. Fortunately the Giant does not notice, though Nyktos does take note of Tiny’s rudeness to be addressed later. With Godric gone, Elisa explains her silence, sharing that she’s met Godric’s partner and alludes that his partner is no longer around, regretting that maybe if they hadn’t killed her, she would be alive and around today. She muses if she should have told him the truth.  
  

The party stands by Elisa’s decision, assuring her that she’s made the right choice then and now. That hope is something Godric needs. That they can tell him the truth some other time, when he is in the better place to hear it. 

  

The party heads back to New Phandalin, warning the King of the upcoming war. King Raula hastily names the party as the generals, telling them to ready the troops whilst he attends to other matters. The party does that, with Tiny and Elisa rallying the troops whilst Lucatiel and Nyktos prepare themselves for war.  
  

Tiny and Elisa both gave a good speech to encourage the people of New Phandalin, though those previously unwelcomed, namely the six individuals who had been denied entry were eager to leave. Though Nyktos uses what goodwill she had built up with them to get the six to stay and help.  
  
An hour passes and nothing has happened yet, Tiny has the citizens build a trench. Elisa and Nyktos head to find the king, catching him just in time as they see a horse and two guards with bags full of what seems to be money. They question the King who vehemently denies any accusations. Having enough of his nonsense, Nyktos slices the bag open, revealing a decent sum of coins. Raula, now properly insulted, is also blood bended by Nyktos as she forces the ring of desires off of his hand.  
  

The guards are confused, wanting to protect their king on one hand but are soon convinced that Raula cares not for them and that their efforts would be best placed on the city instead of this failure of a king. Tying Raula up and taking his crown, Nyktos and Elisa bring Raula to the cells, tossing him in the room alongside the scouts the city had tortured. Soon enough, The Perpeptuals arrive, sending a squire to deliver the message. The party learns from Regix of Raula’s evil deeds, of how Raula had stolen gold from the coffers of the King of Radhantia.

  

The party heads out to meet the squire, a 16 year old by the name of Bernard, squire to Sir Daron. Squire Bernard manages to hold his ground, despite the party’s efforts to mess with him. Squire Bernard insists on knowing the fates of the two captured scouts for parlay to be possible. Having no other choice, Elisa reveals that one of the scouts passed. This prompts Squire Bernard to issue another order, stating that the citizens of New Phandalin are to be sentenced to death, causing panic amongst the citizens. Nyktos tries to calm them down somewhat and the party head to their parlay, dragging Raula along with them. Approaching the place, the party is greeted by some thuggish looking knights.  
  
The main knight has armour that differs from the rest, alongside his pauldrons that are made of two right hands. He looks weirdly familiar, exactly like Sir Almaric minus the rotten flesh. Despite his thick armour, Sir Alfred smells distinctively of alcohol, even going as far to change his speech as he catches himself before speaking in a more informal way. 

  

Sir Alfred stops the party in their path, stating that he would not allow them to speak with the King. Backed into a corner the party has no choice but to fight the knights. The knights do not attack with killing intent and this all seems like a test that fortunately the party does pass. They are allowed in and meet the King, he dons a silver mask alongside white and gold robes. Interestingly one of his eyes is scarred.  
  
King Baldwin of Lupra questions what the party wants as they express their desire for the safety of New Phandalin’s citizens, offering up both the stolen gold and Raula for his punishment. King Baldwin questions the party, inquiring of their relationship to New Phandalin and are seemingly more intrigued as they share no connections to the town, simply adventurers who had business to do. King Baldwin invites the party to his tent, where it smells of medications and herbs. Using her medicinal knowledge, Nyktos realises that the King suffers from Leprosy and in theory should not be able to stand, much less move without extreme effort. 

  

King Baldwin asks the party for their motivations, wanting to learn what drives their seemingly altruistic actions. Lucatiel explains that they were sent on a mission by Psilofyr to help with the moon. Whilst Tiny shares about their desire to protect others. Nyktos comes from a place of understanding, knowing how it felt to be powerless and now that she has power, desires to use it for good.  
  
Likewise with Elisa, individuals who were wrongs who were wronged who now wish to help others. Accepting their answers, King Baldwin speaks of his father who taught him to be strong and fair. He shares that he knew of the Heroes of the Mist, that he seeks their aid. When asked about his comment of not living past 20, King Baldwin explains that he is from a small settlement in the South West where there is a cancer that haunts the lands and that he was only spared because he is bad meat.  
  
King Baldwin had built his Kingdom to ensure that no other would suffer, for what is a man but one who leaves the world better. Nyktos inquires more about the King’s illness, offering to try to cure him. Though her attempt to do so fails as the King is not just ill but rather his blood is cursed by the moon, a curse that would take a miracle to break. 

  

King Baldwin explains that in exchange for sparing the citizens of New Phandalin, he desires for the party, or however many of them that wish to, to take on the mantle of his left hand, to be his limbs and eyes, to help those that he is unable to. In exchange, he would also help with their artisan issue that they have at home.  
  
Tiny agrees, lured in by the promises of titles and land. Whilst Elisa and Nyktos agree as well, each for their own reasons. Lucatiel, the freedom loving fey does not desire to be knighted, enjoying their freewill. Before they are officially appointed, Nyktos also warns King Baldwin of her religion, sharing the misconceptions others might have of those from her practice, though King Baldwin iterates that it is not the circumstances but rather the acts and intentions that define a person, approving of her cult.  
  
Nyktos also asks of Sir Almaric, sharing that they had met him as he now is forced against his own will to serve the evil dracolich, Ardor Mor. Nyktos passes along the message that Knight Almaric believed and loved his King to this day, that Knight Almaric never once abandoned his King nor his fellow Knights, that he never ran. 

  

The party heads out to speak with Sir Alfred. Sir Alfred immediately chastises Tiny for not calling him by his title, explaining that to call one by their first name symbolises them to be enemies or friends, of which their relationship as friends is yet to be determined. The party shares King Baldwin’s orders with Sir Alfred, giving him the details that are required. Lucatiel speaks to Sir Alfred, sharing that Tiny would require etiquette lessons. The party also takes the time to ask of the previous left hand of King Baldwin, learning that Sir Almaric was indeed Sir Alfred’s twin. They share Sir Alfred’s fate and of his unwavering love and faith for the Kingdom. 

  

Having settled matters, the party rests before being appointed as recruits. As King Baldwin stands before the party, his hand out. The three take a knee before King Baldwin, Tiny being instructed to by Sir Alfred. Elisa takes the King’s hand, placing it to her forehead, Tiny takes the cue from Sir Alfred, grasping the hand as their three heads each place a kiss on the hand, lastly is Nyktos, using her blood magic to draw out some of her blood as she pledges an oath in blood to King Baldwin. With the ceremony over, the party are now initiates of the Silvered King’s army. The party collects their items as the people set off for the City of Lupra. 

  

During their journey, the party is approached by various individuals. Lucatiel shares their desire for inks and Nyktos and Elisa coincidentally happen to have what they need. After some talks the three manage to strike up a deal, a fixed payment of gold and the next two gold sharings of Lucatiel’s are to be given to the two instead. 

  

King Baldwin invites them in for a chat, wanting to learn of where they had come from. Tiny shares of their past, and how they now are in search of their father, wherever he might be. Nyktos shares of her life in the shadowfell, and of her brush with death, and shares that she now desires to help others however she can. Elisa speaks of the Moribund, the three supposedly good people that her city had extended a helping hand to only for them to be ruthlessly sacrificed to mind flayers for the three’s own gain. King Baldwin promises that when the time comes, that the knights would help in Elisa’s battle. Lucatiel shares of the quest that Psilofyr sent them on, prompting King Baldwin to state that mayhaps Lucatiel has yet to find their life’s mission and desires, though that he is sure they will soon. 

  

One of the other nights, Sir Daron seeks out the party. The loud and drunk man is accompanied by the familiar face of Squire Bernard Ryswell, which explains a lot of the young Squires lack of experience. Sir Daron, or Daron as he has insisted to the party invites them for a game of facts, bets, and drinks. Squire Bernard is unsure though he can’t do much to stop the Knight and instead sits by Lucatiel, attempting to write down what it means to be a knight and the rules of Knighthood, though all previous entries are scratched out as it seems Sir Daron does not follow a fixed rule.  
  
Lucatiel attempts to steal from the gold pile, though their attempt is spotted by Nyktos who stops them. Daron, Elisa, Tiny, and Nyktos all drink, sharing fun facts. They learn that Daron is from Grapeton, a wine manufacturing town, though Daron got bored of mashing grapes with his feet and simply left to become a Knight instead. Lucatiel takes the opportunity to share Elisa's nickname “Elisa the Eminent E happily snickering as they tell on her. The party has a good laugh, though the tables turn as Nyktos shares tales of Lucatiel the Handsome and how they fought a Giant many times their size, playing the magic mouth disc that carries the word of Lucatiel the Handsome. Eventually, Daron has too much to drink and forfeits the game, the three parties collect their winnings, giving the last remaining gold to Squire Bernard who has to lead Daron back to his quarters. 

  

They eventually arrive at the City of Lupra after six days and King Baldwin gives the party their first two tasks. The first is to make right the death of Scout Gale, to inform his family of his passing. King Baldwin gives the party authority to help the grieving family as they see fit, though to remember that others would know of what reparations if any are given. The second is that the custodians of the Tower of Knowledge require help, that some knights have been sent to the tower before though no news of their return. 

  

The party sets out, heading to the Argent District and seeking Madam Cynthia’s home. Lucatiel does point out to the party that they look more like adventurers than knights, though nothing much can be done for the time being so they head to Madam Cynthia’s home. As they explain their station, they are invited in.  
  
Nyktos, being the most normal looking of the party, is the one to break the news, gently explaining that Scout Gale had given his life protecting the people and his loved ones. They tell her of the letter he kept close to himself and of where his body would be interred. They also offer support under the King’s grace, offering financial aid to tide the family over the tough times and comforting her to be strong.  
  
With that settled, the party heads to the Tower of Knowledge, taking the long way around. It is three in the afternoon when the party arrives at the tower. The Custodians may them no mind as the party are left there awkwardly standing. Lucatiel rushes to the books, picking up the first one they see and eagerly reading the different culinary techniques. Tiny steps up this time, speaking to an individual as they explain who they are. Though their attire causes the Custodians to believe otherwise as they instead try to convince their way through. It somewhat works as the custodian brings in someone else. Still they manage to proceed on their mission, learning of three knights who had been sent out five days ago to a mining settlement nearby and have yet to return. 

  
Given the urgency of the matter, the party rushes out, not stopping to rest as they soon arrive near the settlement at one in the morning. They see a murder of ravens circling above them, taking a closer look, Nyktos realises that the ravens are somehow unaffiliated with The Raven Queen. The birds begin to disappear, poofing away and as Nyktos forms a connection with one of them, she sees that the raven has simply gone invisible. With not much lead, the party continues on.  
  
They reach the town, where the people are all outside despite the late hour. As they step in, Nyktos smells the scent of rotting eggs in the air. A remarkably tall lady stands in the centre of the town, welcoming the party in. The party explains that they are knights, searching for some others who might have passed by. The lady claims to have not seen any other knights, something which Nyktos spots as a lie. Nyktos moves to Elisa’s side, with Elisa forming the telepathic bond as the two converse. Nyktos shares about the smell and the lies, as Elisa looks at the woman who changes into Goetia for a second.  
  
They continue their conversation, the woman turning into Ilenti for Nyktos, prompting Elisa to turn on her truesight as she sees before her a chain devil. The party launches into battle, as waves of dwarves rush out from the houses attacking the party with hellfire. Tiny manages to deal a substantial amount of damage to the chain devil, drawing the attention of the dwarves as they focus their attacks on them, paralysing and baning them. Spirit guardians manage to deal a healthy amount of damage to some of the dwarves as the party splits up to deal with them. Elisa, recognising the dwarves from Gauntlgrym, requests the party to try not to kill them.  
  
Fortunately for the dwarves, the concentration on spirit guardians breaks and Nyktos teleports away after being badly damaged. Lucatiel finds themselves being chased by the dwarves managing to stand their ground for the most part whilst Elisa sends Milton to help Tiny fight the dwarves. After successfully knocking them out, Tiny ties up the dwarves by the hands. The party speaks with the townsfolk, freeing them from the chains that held them. The townsfolk express their thanks, informing the party that the three knights they came to seek were taken away by the dwarves, and that periodically a Dwarf sat upon a burning Griffon will come to take them away. The party sets out once more to find the knights, hoping that they still live.
