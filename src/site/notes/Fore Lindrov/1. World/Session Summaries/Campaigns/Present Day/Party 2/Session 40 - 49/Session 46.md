Inspecting the door, Elisa identifies the symbols, noting that they refer to planes whilst Pyrite senses the magic on the door. The door has some vein-like patterns, prompting Nyktos towards the bowl near the door. The blood cleric readily sacrifices a pint of blood, noting as it flows and starts to fill up the veins on the door. Seeing as a blood sacrifice is required, Elisa pulls out her bag of tricks, retrieving three animal companions, a rat, a giant boar, and a giant goat. Seeing this, Pyrite suggests that he could drink from one of the animals, allowing him to restore some much needed vitality. Elisa consents and Pyrite does so, consuming the blood of the rat as it poofs away upon death. Elisa and Nyktos apologise to the giant boar before slitting its throat, aiming to drain the boar’s blood as swiftly and painlessly as possible. They succeed, and the door opens as they make their way into the first place. Puddles of water litter the floor with the walls seemingly sweating water. Pyrite and Tiny both open the door and a strong wave of water gushes through, filling up the room. Fortunately for Pyrite and Tiny, they do not breathe, allowing them to bypass the concerns of drowning. Unfortunately for the rest, they do as a hurried scramble to solve the room’s puzzle begins. Nyktos rushes to the corpse before the door, checking up on it as it begins to come to life. The diseased bodies about the room attack the party, and combat ensues. The party manages to end their lives swiftly, but the room remains filled with water. Elisa reads the primordial inscription on the altar, “Only what was once alive, now frozen in bitter cold may awaken dormant powers of Ice, Eand relays the information to her party. Trying everything they can, the party eventually settles on placing one of the corpses on the altar, as Elisa freezes the body with her spell. This clears the puzzle and the next door opens, allowing the party to breathe once more. 

  

They encounter two doors next and upon entering one notice a water jug puzzle. Nyktos figures out the gist of the puzzle, suggesting to the party that they would have to utilise the 5L and 3L jugs to acquire 4L of water and place that in the statue’s hands. In a matter of seconds, Pyrite figures out the puzzle and the party works together to solve it swiftly, acquiring the first of two keys. They then face the second room, suspecting that the door behind is also filled with water like before. Elisa phases through the door first, making sure to hold her breath. What greets her is a squadron of Ixitxachitl, they reveal Demogorgon to be their master, and question what Elisa wants. Elisa responds politely, convincing the aberrations that she and her friends would have something they want. The party, concerned that Elisa has yet to return, opens the door, allowing the water and Ixitxachitls to spill into the other room. The leader of the Ixitxachitl, Head Priest Ripspittle notices the mark on Nyktos and points it out. The party attempts a polite approach but as that fails, Elisa cleverly falls back on the child of chaos story, labelling Nyktos as someone Demogorgon had sent to interrogate Auril.  
  
The story is convincing, though as the Ixitxachitl demand for more information, Nyktos begrudgingly steps up, assuming the role of the child of chaos. She raises a brow at the Ixitxachitl Priest, asking him how much longer he intends to question a child of chaos. Surprisingly, she pulls off the act well, her words striking fear into the Ixitxachitl as the Head Priest reluctantly hands off the key, sputtering some last words at the party. He hopes that they get lost within the Amber Temple, mocking them that the days they spend within would only be mere hours in the outside world, cursing at them to lose their mind like the other one did. The party moves on and enters the final chamber. The party comes face to face with a book emitting chains of flame that seem to perhaps keep Auril locked up. The room also houses a familiar but scattered pile of rocks. Pyrite stacks the rocks back up whilst Nyktos hunts for a note, desperate to find anything from her sister. What she finds, however, is a worrying note: “HE TOLD ME THE GOD OF SECRETS TOLD ME OH GODS HELP ME NYKTOS ILENTI HELP ME E The note rattles Nyktos but she has no choice but to press on.

  

Testing the waters, Nyktos asks Crimson to try controlling the flames. It does not work but as the flames burst out from the book, Crimson remains unharmed, the primordial flames unable to hurt the hell hound. Elisa tries next, successfully using thornwhip to pull the book out of place. As the book falls out, and with a swift drop, Elisa places the symbol she got from Vanifer into the book.  
  
The fire dissipates and the doors open. The temperature of the room drops as most of the party respectfully bow to the Queen of Cold and Frost. Except Tiny, who greets her with a “Hi, Auril, hi E This annoys the Frostmaiden, rightfully so, as Tiny finds himself beginning to freeze up. At this moment, Tiny realises the power of the goddess before him and starts to kneel, he feels the restriction from his frozen legs but continues nonetheless, breaking them as he falls to his robotic knees. Elisa and Pyrite prostrate themselves before the goddess, seeking her forgiveness and her aid against those that forcefully caged her in the Amber Temple. Pleased with the party, Auril reaches a finger out to Pyrite, granting him a spellwrought tattoo with the spell “Wish Eon it. Elisa and Pyrite then apologise to the goddess on Tiny’s behalf, pleading with the goddess to restore Tiny his legs. Fortunately for them, she does, telling them that “the cold winds are not without their respite Ebefore leaving. With Auril’s departure, the room reverts back to a comfortable temperature. The party rests for the night, taking a much needed breather. 

  

The next day, they set out to the next plane and end up in a room with bowls of crimson liquid and two statues made of the same material. One of them is of an alluring woman and the other, a familiar face to Kaze. It is Hayato, with his tornado-like hair and kimono. Nyktos investigates the statue, finding yet another note from Arala that explains the goal of the trial. Meanwhile, Elisa and Tiny approach the bowls of red liquid, the metallic taste in the air confirming the liquid to be blood. As they get closer, they sense the ability to manipulate the blood and promptly have fun doing so. When told the goal of the trial, Elisa begins to craft a statue of Zipzap, doing so spectacularly. Following suit, Tiny crafts a statue of himself, a majestic dragon spewing fire from his lips. With that, the first trial is completed and they move on to a second chamber with stone formations that all house a slot for a hand. 

  

Nyktos tries first, immense pain shooting up her arm as the blood is forced to her fingers. She pulls out quickly, feigning that it was painless but the party sees through her pretence. Tiny tries next, bravely holding out and successfully completing a part of the trial. Though not without cost, as the young kobold struggles to comprehend the pain he had just borne. Elisa suggests that he rest for a bit and he does so, his expression blank as he sits down, leaning against a wall. Pyrite tries next but hears a voice as soon as he places his hand in the stone. The voice growls at him, forbidding Pyrite from giving away his blood, their blood. Pyrite calls out to the voice, though it does not respond. He then pulls his hand out, confused by it all. Nyktos suggests that he rest with Tiny and Pyrite seems appreciative but offers to stand with them, wanting to help how he can. Meanwhile, Elisa has been diligently casting resistance on the party members attempting the trial. Nyktos goes up next, trying once more and easily succeeding with her Blood Aegis ability and both Elisa and Pyrite who cast spells to help ease the process. Riding the high of completing the stone, Nyktos offers to do it once more. The second time around is harder than before, her mind quickly reaching its limits as the pain surges through her arm. Still, with the support from her party and her desire to please her god, Nyktos barely manages to complete yet another stone. Through pain and delusional thought, Nyktos offers to do it a third time but Elisa and Pyrite are quick to stop her. Elisa insists that they are a party who helps one another, urging the cleric to rest. Coming back to her senses, Nyktos agrees and plops herself near Tiny to rest. As Nyktos leans against the walls of the area, Mini hops over to the Shadar kai, offering her comfort. Nyktos thanks the mechanical bird before checking up on Tiny, who is still nonverbal at the moment. Through their conversation, Nyktos thanks Tiny for his help and calls him brave for withstanding the immense pain. This seems to cheer the Kobold up and they both continue their rest wordlessly. 

  

Pyrite tries a second time, speaking with the being that resides within him and managing to ignore the beast’s demands as he pushes through with the trial. Pyrite continues to push himself to the limit, gritting his teeth as agonising pain courses through his arm. As this happens, the blade of his sword changes, transforming into a greatsword the size of a man as Pyrite continues to give his blood. Alas, the suffering grows too much to bear as he is forced to pull his hand out before completing the trial. Though the party is much more confused with the sword’s transformation. The party huddles close together, cautious and ready to defend themselves should anything happen. Thankfully, nothing else happens, Pyrite looks confused at his blade, explaining to the party the strange voice he heard just a second ago. With the party confirming all is well, they leave Pyrite to rest as well. Elisa completes the final stone without issue and the doors open. 

  

They enter the next chamber and the blood on the floor forms into the shape of a woman, she asks what the party is here for and scoffs at Nyktos Ereply, informing her that the Lord of Blood is not trapped, merely waiting. She offers the party a test to prove their might and as soon as they accept, rids them of their armour and sets creatures made of blood to attack them. The party swiftly cuts down the blood creatures with Tiny jumping up to attack the creatures mid air. With the final test completed, Lady Malikah allows the party through. Before them is the Lord of Blood, Tenebrous himself. Nyktos enters first, immediately lowering herself to a deep curtsy before her god, as Pyrite follows suit and kneels before the Lord of Blood. Elisa and Tiny bow, offering their respects though Tenebrous does not seem to like it as he forces the two to kneel as well. Nyktos asks her god for clarification, wondering why he had saved her all those years ago. He clarifies that Nyktos is his will, and Malikah is his hand. Nyktos Eblood is foul, stained by the blood of demons. That he had saved her to prepare her for a path of deeper veins; to be his “will E The one destined to lead the Children of Sanguine to be the arteries that spread his words and offerings of blood to all places. Tenebrous affirms that Nyktos Eis the Airmorragikós, the One Who Bleeds. And as his chosen, she is privy to the secrets, to learn of the secrets of Lichdom. Nyktos clarifies the cost of Lichdom with Tenebrous, confirming that it would require the consumption of souls to extend her own life. Lastly, he bestows her with the Rod of the Blood Ruler, an artefact that would cement her position as his chosen, giving her power over all those who worship the Lord of Blood, Tenebrous. Nyktos accepts the artefact and the responsibilities that come with wielding it. 

  

With that done, the party cautiously asks Tenebrous some questions. They learn how Tenebrous came to be in the Temple, that the Temple was meant to seal dark vestiges though he had managed to free himself. Nyktos asks about her mother, only to learn that she has taken over the temple with her other. All further questions are ignored and the party moves on to the next room. 

  

They enter a large area with statues and writings that indicate the silence should be preserved. The party investigates some of the rooms, finding a journal from someone named Bruni Ironbell  and an amulet bearing the Sparkhammer symbol. Tiny alerts the party to this, informing them of the connection between Bruni , Tiny, and his dad, Reggie. They continue on, looking for more information about Bruni as they come across a big armoured piece of machinery. Through the symbol Tiny got earlier, he controls the shield guardian and the party notices that sounds created by movement do not trigger any attacks. Tiny names the shield guardian, Largie and the party carries on. They then come across a room with what Pyrite identifies as illusion magic. But just as Elisa rushes forward to grab an item, a team of ghosts appear, attacking the party. The ghosts swiftly take control of Tiny and Nyktos, forcing them to work against their party as the rest begin casting protection from evil and good on themselves. The ghost controlling Nyktos forces her to attack Crimson with Kaze and the pup recoils in fear, worried he had done something wrong. At the sight of Nyktos Ehorrid expression, Pyrite steels himself to attack his party member, hoping that the ghost would leave Nyktos once she was unconscious. With Elisa’s help, the plan succeeds and they regain their ally. Though in her anger at the ghost, Nyktos forgets to keep quiet, her casting of Spirit Guardians breaks the silence as yet another creature appears to attack the party. The party makes quick work, eliminating all the ghosts for now. Nyktos takes the time to soothe Crimson, who seems to bounce back quickly after the incident. During the battle, Elisa also speaks to the ghost of Bruni who confirms that Reggie isn’t here. 

  

The party resumes their investigation of the place, but are soon met with yet another machinery, this time one that is hostile. Tiny and Crimson both find themselves charmed by the statue behind the machinery and are unable to stop their desire to stand near the statue. Combat ends swiftly as Nyktos wields Kaze, slashing the construct’s body as Elisa slides in, taking a hit from the construct but bouncing back up as the sanctum amulet is used, allowing Elisa to unleash an arrow on the construct, killing it once and for all. The party then turns their attention to their two charmed party members. Pyrite identifies the spell casted on the statue and covers the statue with his bedroll, preventing the spell from affecting others. They continue their search, finding books from two of three of the Wizards that built the Amber Temple. They learn that Exethanter had betrayed his friends after the death of his wife. They then head down to the crypt, letting both Alaric and Xerenthian out of their graves as they mock the party for their supposed stupidity. In exchange for Exethanter’s head and the promise to collapse the Amber Temple, the two snooty wizards promise to provide the password to their locked treasury, allowing the party to take all of the riches. The party don’t agree but make their way to Exethanter’s room and find an old, confused lich. Unable to harm Exethanter who seemed relatively nice, the party hears him out with Nyktos casting greater restoration on Exethanter, giving him his memory back. Exethanter shares information, explaining that they had killed Bruni  in order to keep the location of the Amber Temple a secret. And that the beings within the Amber Temple are less trapped since Exethanter took a dark gift, offering him immortality. During the conversation, Exethanter politely declines Pyrite’s repeated requests for him to follow the party down to the crypts. Lastly, Nyktos asks Exethanter if he’s met someone like herself before, trying to track Arala’s whereabouts. The Lich pauses for a second before answering that the way Nyktos intends to save Arala may not be correct. Most of the party’s anger and apprehension towards Exethanter subsides the more he cooperates, as he leads the party down to the God of Secrets, offering to translate his god’s words to them. 

  

Tiny is the first to offer his secrets, in exchange acquiring a blueprint that would aid in his quest. Nyktos goes up next, offering the secrets of Lichdom as taught to her by Tenebrous mere moments ago. In return, she learns that the method she intends to free Arala is insufficient. That Arala’s soul is not the correct vessel but has instead been exchanged with another, one who can no longer be slayed. For one to live, the other must die. The God of Secrets provides a spell gem of Soul Cage, a valuable item for Nyktos to get her sister back. Elisa is up next, providing a series of secrets from her home and people to her ability to save others from being consumed by the moon. She gains the following answers for her secrets; The exact location of several bases, Divine intervention, The exact current locations of Goetia, Sylvaerys, and Fool, The method to save Dave requiring an archduke’s soul, Anastasia’s big secret - that she would bound her life to her army and slaying her once she has completed the ritual would render her army dead as well as the location of her camp, and lastly, a item that would grant them access to Banecroft’s manor. Pyrite then offers his secret and learns that the Amber Temple is a conduit for teleportation. The party wraps up their questions for the God of Secrets, now armed with the information and tools they need to complete their quest.
