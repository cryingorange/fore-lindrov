The party stands in Amunkhet's room, taking in the horror of what they just did. They all express their concerns as Vesper stands there, feeling completely fine, thanking the party for their help. With that, the party returns to their rooms as Amunkhet watches over the Celestial for the night. The next morning, the party is greeted by the tense atmosphere in the tavern as deep gnomes, civilian or guard, all brandish weapons at their hips for the upcoming war. They speak with Tappy, who shares her concern and fear for what's to come. The party notices the absence of Arche, and Elisa goes around the tavern trying to find the Changeling but to no avail. Vesper casts sending to Arche but only hears the Changeling repeat her siblings' names, Aura and Luka, over and over again. Concerned with this, the party plans to follow the lead they last heard from Arche about the suspicious figures who enter the Ruby Temple at night. With some time to spare, the party helps out with the war efforts. Aurelion uses his Decanter of Endless Water to provide both the city and the temple with fresh water for all their needs. Vesper spends her time making holy water, and Elisa goes out foraging and brewing potions. Amunkhet spoke with the guards guarding the Gemstone eye in an attempt to gather some useful information for when the party eventually storms the place.

As night finally falls, the party heads outside of the Ruby Temple, as Vesper and Elisa, who turned into a spider, stealthily enter the Temple. The suspicious figures show up, and Elisa successfully climbs onto one of the Deep Gnomes, following them in. As the rest of the party follows in after the Deep Gnomes, their poor attempts at stealth lead to them quickly being found out by the Deep Gnomes, who, after a quick conversation, realize that the party is on their side. It is then revealed that the Deep Gnomes are the Burrow Wardens, a group of Barbarians whose sole purpose is to protect Blingdenstone. The party speaks with Master Jadger, a Deep Gnome who was a part of the Burrow Wardens and is the current instructor leading the new batch. He demands that the group prove their might by sparring with the Burrow Wardens, and the party emerges victorious. They then learn about Digger's Peace, supposedly gifted by Calladurhan Stonehands himself to the Burrow Wardens. The party is tasked to aid Trisk Adamantelpiece, Master Jadger's protege, in his trial to retrieve the war pick. The party then heads down to the caves where the trial takes place. They are first met by Burrow Wardens Dugnuk and Sabby, who demand a show of skill before the party may pass. Trisk rages and picks up a rather large boulder, showing off his strength and might. Aurelion shoots beams of eldritch blast at the wall, albeit unimpressively as he barely even damages it, leaving them very unimpressed. Amunkhet asks the Burrow Wardens for their description of Blingdenstone before its fall, and produces a beautiful painting of exactly what they mentioned.

Elisa shows them her masterfully brewed truth potion, which heavily impresses the two. Vesper attempts to make a show of force using thaumaturgy but fails and instead channels her divine powers to turn the undead, causing Dugnuk to cower in fear as Sabby permits the group to pass after Aurelion shows off a higher level spell, blasting the 2 ghosts. In the second room, the party meets Burrow Wardens Diggerrin and Jamjam, who seek to test the party's ability to hide. Aurelion cleverly hides in one of the empty crypts, using his powers to create the illusion of an opened lid. Amunkhet and Vesper both get into the bag of holding, whilst Elisa, holding said bag, wildshapes into a spider and easily hides in one of the crevices. Trisk simply uses his Svirfneblin camouflage to hide amongst the terrain. Having succeeded, the party then enters the final room, one that is much larger than the two prior. Before them stands a gigantic blue scale. Looking at the scale, the party watches as happy memories of their lives play before them. Elisa sees the memory of her home-city of Solaris, of her playing with her friends and getting into trouble. Amunkhet sees herself walking with a familiar Drow, one named Ankhta, in the sandy dunes of Urshu. Aurelion sees the day he graduated surrounded by friends and family. Finally, Vesper sees every iteration of her and her husband, their meeting, their children, and their lives.

As they stare fondly at the memories, a voice sounds out. Burrow Warden Belwar Dissengulp introduces himself, with the war pick known as Digger's Peace by his side. Now a ghost, he speaks of the blue scale, expressing that while it does show happy memories, not all memories are great. As the scale begins to show Blingdenstone's fall as the drow invades with Neheedra, their leader sporting a symbol of the house of Duskryn. Vesper questions Belwar about how long ago was the fall of Blingdenstone, with him saying that he can no longer remember. 

He asks that they prepare themselves, as they are about to fight for their lives. The scale begins to manifest the fall of Blingdenstone, with troops of adventurers ushering the citizens to safety, with one of them being Chrom. The echo of Neheedra spots the party as she sends her people to fight them. Battle ensues as the party manages to take down many of her men with a well-placed sickening radiance and spike growth from Aurelion and Elisa, respectively. Finishing up the battle, the party watches as the memory continues. A drow assassin attempts to take Neheedra down as the rest of the drow turn against one another. They watch as Neheedra learns of the fall of House Duskryn and the desperation in her remaining forces. She swears that if they bring powerful artifacts back to Menzoberranzan, they will surely be allowed back.

The drow begin their endless march through Rockblight, gathering magical items and spell gems to bring back. However, it appears Lolth has fully abandoned House Duskryn, shown through Neheedra's transformation into a Drider. As she loses her mind, she encases all of her army in stone. Then meeting Ogremoch's Bane, a crazed genie who seems to recognize Neheedra's insanity.

Once everything settles, Belwar shows up once more and offers Trisk the war pick they came for. Trisk looks to the party for approval before receiving the weapon, telling the party they remind him of a friend, Drizzt. With war coming closer, the party plans their best course of action to balance the odds of the war. After a long discussion, they come to the agreement that Ogremoch's Bane must first be dealt with to give the Deep Gnomes back their earth elementals and a better fighting chance. They head to Nomi for help to summon earth elementals, and with Glyphic's approval, they do so, setting a trap for the Genie. With their 5 earth elementals laying in wait, the party ambushes Ogremoch's Bane. Vesper tries to use her divine powers to cure the genie of his madness but when that fails, the party blasts and heavily injures the genie. Through a greater restoration, however, the party manages to cure the genie who then introduces himself as Usman. Amunkhet then explains what has transgressed, and with some encouragement from Elisa and the party, Usman agrees to help in the war as penance for the mess he made. Usman also offers a wish to the group as thanks for fixing him. As the group deliberates on how to use their wish, Elisa mutters that she wished for help. Hearing that, Usman agrees, and the party watches as a towering giant of a man shows up. The man introduces himself as Armstrong and upon hearing of the situation at hand, he instantly jumps to fight Neheedra and her faction. Armstrong also seems to know of Vesper's eye, questioning her intent of having it, but accepts when Vesper shows interest in protecting the eye from falling into the wrong hands. With that, the party then heads back to Blingdenstone, who has been made aware of the war. The party assures Dorbo and Senni that the citizens need not fight.

Utilizing the route from the Wererats' home, the party then sneaks into the Pudding Court. While invisible, they hear the slime guards trying to usher their Princess to safety, but when the Princess declares that she will bring fiery weapons to the field on the people of Blingdenstone, the party has no choice but to intervene. After a quick battle, the party rids her of her two guards as they watch the Princess cower in fear of Milton as she surrenders. As time is of the essence, Amunkhet swiftly takes off her helmet, explaining to the Princess that she has witnessed and knows what happens to people in her position when they come back seeking revenge. The Princess doesn't say anything but is allowed to leave. The party storms past the treasury into the throne room where the Pudding King and his son, Prince Livid, reside. The Prince is shocked to find his tutors here to kill his father. Unable to say much, the party attacks. Aurelion steps up bravely during the battle, his newfound courage allows him to deal a large amount of damage to the Pudding King as he incurs the Slime-Beholder's wrath and fear. With a swift flick of the dagger from Amunkhet, the Pudding King falls down, dead. Anguished by the death of his father, Prince Livid rushes to his father's side. Under the advice of his guards, the Prince escapes as they party negotiates for the slimes to surrender. As Commander Schloop waves a white flag, signaling the slimes' surrender, the Wererats argue against the slimes leaving for fear that they'd come back for revenge. The Burrow Wardens take the moment to announce their presence to the people of Blingdenstone, reaffirming that the war is indeed over. With a cue from Trisk, the party slinks away, readying themselves for their second battle.

They sneak into the Gemstone eye and are quickly met with some ghouls. After quickly dispatching the ghouls, the party finds a study. Amunkhet opens a skull chest that blasts the party with necrotic damage, and finds some useful potions and an array of powders. Elisa finds a hidden tunnel in the closet, and the party goes through to come face to face with the party they knew as Fall Damage, their bodies now dismembered and stitched together to form gruesome pieces. They hear screams emit from another room, the voices of Krista and Paldron shrieking, begging for mercy. Elisa casts detect thoughts and hears a little voice going “Food, Food. ECautious, the party enters the room to see a baby gazer. It screams once more before mimicking the party's words. Elisa proceeds to feed the gazer, and it seems rather tame for now. Other gazers show up, with a malicious intent as the party swiftly deals with them. During the battle, Elisa scoops up the baby gazer she fed, and it attacks their enemy, dealing the final blow. Elisa happily names the gazer Zipzap as the rest all come to terms with Elisa's new pet.

Entering another room, the party is met with the sight of their friend Arche, her body cut open with her organs taken out of her body still attached to her. It seems that she’s still alive. And by Arche’s side is Peebles, with a dried flesh book in hand. Aurelion shouts at Peebles, berating him as Peebles stands there confused, insisting that he was doing research, that he was close to figuring out the formulas in the book. Peebles, sensing danger, places a scalpel by Arche’s throat as he threatens the party to talk things out. Aurelion subtly casts a spell, though Peebles realizes when he casts silvery barbs. Peebles then slices Arche’s throat as she begins to bleed out, and he dislocates his shoulders and escapes via a tunnel.

Concerned with their bleeding-out friend, Elisa, Amunkhet, and Vesper rush to Arche’s aid with the Changeling stable. Aurelion, blinded with rage, starts to storm to follow Peebles, though Vesper manages to dissuade him, convincing him that they needed his help to put Arche back together.

Aurelion relents, commanding his summon Atlas to follow after Peebles but to not engage. Passing through the tunnel, Aurelion feels as his summon disappears. Using her medical knowledge, Amunkhet tries to see if she’s able to put Arche back together but to no avail. The party panics with them devising ways to fix Arche. Aurelion then finds himself alone, as a tall half-orc fiend shows up before him. When asked who he is, the half-orc simply replies that he’s a sinner. The half-orc offers Aurelion power for revenge and to fix Arche in exchange for his soul and something more. As Aurelion negotiates the terms of their agreement, the fiend belittles him, calling him a fool at every turn. Aurelion agrees to raise an army for the fiend, vowing to create new laws in the unlawful land of Fore Lindrov. WIth the contract signed, time resumes as the party now sees Aurelion looking different than before, the cogs symbolic of his power burn away and a distinct hellfire courses through him. He steps forward, touching Arche as the party watches the fire flow through and fixes Arche. Confused, the party questions Aurelion but are ignored as he heads on to find Peebles. The party finds Peebles beside Lord Belfrey, whom they know as Bucephalus, and another member that Amunkhet seems to have a grudge with. The party gets split into two as Elisa, alongside Zipzap and Milton, and Vesper try their best to fight Bucephalus whilst Amunkhet picks a battle with Serbes. Serbes is visibly annoyed as he puts Amunkhet in a wall of force whilst threatening to disintegrate Aurelion, who does not yield to the threats, as he continues to agitate Serbes. Done with the two, Serbes casts disintegrate on Aurelion.

And as the beam is about to strike Aurelion, Amunkhet pleads to Ardor Mor. The Dracolich agrees to help on the condition that the next time she sees her brother, Amunkhet will tell him that she begged for Ardor Mor's help. Amunkhet begrudgingly agrees, repeating the terms of their deal through gritted teeth. The wall of force surrounding Amunkhet breaks, confusing Serbes. Amunkhet feels a swelling in her stomach as she pukes out flies that form the shape of Ardor Mor, who commands Serbes to leave. He does so, teleporting himself, Peebles, and Bucephalus out of there. Ardor Mor has Amunkhet reveal to the party the condition that she agreed to and seeing as he's saved the city, he asks Elisa if the city is to be his and questions why it's so terrible. She relents that the city lacks the great leadership that Ardor Mor could provide, though he is unsatisfied with her answer as he probes into her mind. Ardor Mor shows the party exactly what happened to Elisa's home and how a party consisting of an Elf, Tiefling, and the Half-Orc that Aurelion made a deal with cruelly destroyed Elisa's home. Ardor Mor asks that as payment for saving Aurelion's life, he should kill Goetia, which Aurelion readily agrees. Ardor Mor concludes that the city is his, before leaving, teleporting away.

After Ardor Mor leaves, Aurelion walks up to Elisa, apologizing for his actions during the battles against Bucephalus and vowing to kill the three that destroyed Elisa's home. Amunkhet apologizes to the party as well for allowing her rage to nearly cost them their lives. They then hear screams once more, this time belonging to Arche as she wakes up. The party rushes to her as Vesper tries to calm Arche down with words, but Arche continues screaming, morphing into the members of Fall Damage. Concerned, the party thinks of ways they can help her. Aurelion suggests using his newly gained powers to form a contract with Arche to erase her memories. The party instantly denies, not wanting to damn Arche's soul to hell. Aurelion insists, asking the party what other ideas they have. Vesper shares that she could try to channel her divine powers, but only the next day. Though it seems that in her desperation, she finds herself able to cast a scry on her husband. She hears the sound of chains and the image of a large gate of sorts. She is given the power to cast Wish as the soul of her husband, Chrom, emerges from her, looking like a true angel. She alters all of Arche's memories to forget Fall Damage and her siblings, and teleports the party somewhere to safety, bringing the caged Jimjar, Elisa's villager, and an unconscious Arche as Aurelion vows to stop Goetia.
