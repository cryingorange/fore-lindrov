# The Dung Mephits

The party arrive at the Dung Dump outside of the settlement, wherein Grimrid is pranked by Dung Mephits, picking a fight with them and falling into the river multiple times from chasing after them.

As Grimrid cleans himself off, Elisa and the Mephits make a deal, with her soiling Grimrid in exchange for some information. With the Mephits revealing that they came into existence due to the Giants' Magic, gaining sentience from simply being in the vicinity of Heechingu.

# Reese's Task

 Roast and Ivy work together to toss the dung into the dung pile, remembering Reese's Warning that the dung must all be dumped in piles together, for dung has evil energies. In the meantime, Elisa heads over to the pool of dung at the far end of the heap, seeing thick tentacles that float in the disgusting pool, identifying that there are Otyughs, dung eating creatures that hide within the pool and dung heaps.

# The Otyughs

The Otyughs refuse to give up their special snack, revealing to the PCs that it is in fact a Human Corpse. Instead of fighting them, Ivy goes to catch multiple pounds of Fish as a Black Bear using her Wild Shape, while Grimrid manages to catch traditionally a pound and a half. In the meantime, Elisa reveals to Roast that there is a massive fish near the edge of the water, with Roast remembering some information she read about Noodling. As she dives down into the water, capturing the massive fish by baiting it into clasping onto her hand, she flings the fish 10s of feet into the air, with Elisa convincing the Otyughs to lift the body up and exchange it for the fish.

# Jace's Corpse & Ewan's Confrontation

The party confirms that this is in fact Jace's body, and based on the wound, it is extremely similar to the square headed hammer that Ewan wears on his belt. They bury the body somewhere safe and go to confront Ewan at his home.

The party head to Ewan's home, confronting him, with Grimrid dispersing the gathering crowd and gathering more of their thoughts regarding rebellion, trying to convince them not to act irrationally. After their dispersal, the party manage to force a confession out of Ewan. With him revealing that he's been using the grain to feed Spirits that supposedly saved him when he fell into the raging waters beneath the mill.

He insists that the Spirits will help save the settlers, and that he need only feed them to make them strong enough to beat back the Fire Giants. However he seems desperate to believe in this regardless of the evidence, fearing that he may have killed Jace and gotten his Father killed all for nothing.

The party lies to Ewan, telling them that they will seek out the spirits and help Ewan drive off the Fire Giants as he gives them a map that Jace had drawn out, whom had found out Ewan was stealing the grain, and followed it out to where it was generally being taken, before being killed by Ewan when confronted.

# The Spirits

The party follows the map north, where Elisa notices a fish following them along the stream and waters of the deeper part of the forest. It insults and calls Elisa and the party ugly creatures when she attempts to speak to it, and it escapes as Elisa grows aggressive, trying to capture it to cook it.

The party follows the river to a cave, where they find the corpse of a Disemboweled bear. With an investigation yielding sea urchin spines and poison coursing through its veins. Elisa sends Milton deeper into the cave, and he comes out.. Clearly upset by something.

As the party enters, they discover the corpse of a Bear Cub, it's body scattered with Sea Urchin Spikes. But Roast discovers that Poison is not it's cause of death. Elisa senses the strong emotions of the bear and pulls the party into the memories of the cub's last moments. 

They see the cub and its mum eating something for dinner, although the cub finds the fish odd tasting, it does not speak up for fear of angering its mother. Then, the water boils, as shapes rise from it. The mother tries to scare the things away whilst the cub wedges itself into a corner as taught by its parent. And then silence. The cub looks up to see its mother, body torn open as she draws her last breath, her gaze demanding that he stays still. But it matters not, the cub hears strange noises, some kind of clicking before it feels something wet on its head as something sharp ends its life. 

Grimrid activates Tremorsense, discovering that there are creatures in the deep pool at the far end of the cave. As the party silently creeps up towards it, the creatures make themselves known. Tritons. Their leader, Corlos, introduces himself and his people, all the while behaving the way Tritons do, arrogant and self important, declaring the party and the commoners idiotic, simpleton surface dwellers who know not the sacrifices that the Tritons have made to 'protect them' from the horrors of the depths. The Tritons reveal they are working with the Storm Giants, namely Imperator Uthor. They also reveal that there is a plan in place to eventually confront and kill Ythimir / Iymrith in the Court of the Storm Giants. The party learns that they steal the grain with intentions to starve the Fire Giants Earmy overtime, with no concern for the innocents that are currently paying the price. The party does not stand for the Triton’s behaviour and combat ensues.

As the situation worsens for the Tritons, they resort to dragging the party members underwater, using it to their advantage. Roast gets particularly annoyed by Corlos and what she presumes is his pet lobster, dragging the lobster back to shore to be consumed by the party. Meanwhile, the Tritons retreat, as the party lets them go, seeing as they are also in a bad situation. 

The party buries the bodies of the bear and its cub, putting them to rest before returning to the town wherein they made efforts to reignite hope in the townsfolk, encouraging them to lay in wait for the opportune moment to strike. 

 They head to Heechingu’s place, sharing with him the findings and the Triton corpses as Grimrid sows discord between the Fire and Storm Giants, Lord Heechingu asks the party to retrieve Ewan

Arriving at Ewan’s house, they confront him for his crimes, telling him the truth and knocking him out as they parade him and announced his crimes, continuing to push the idea of patience and waiting for the right moment to strike, convincing the Townsfolk to wait.

At the town square, Lord Heechingu gives a big speech of what happens to traitors, making a big display of Ewan’s punishment, as he is crucified and left to die. Roast approaches Jace’s wife to support her when she found the truth, and gave her the glove, telling her to stay strong for her child

In the background, the party keeps up their message of silent rebellion, convincing everyone in the settlement not to give up hope, but to wait for the correct moment to rebel against the Fire Giants.

Before leaving, Grimrid asks Roast to draw a map of the area they had buried Jace’s body for his spouse, giving it to Jace’s neighbour, should his wife ever seek out the information. 


# To Goldenfields

## Party Reflections

With that, the party leaves town, continuing on to goldenfields and continuing their escort of Dirrel and the 50 people of his settlement. During the 7th Day of their travel since their departure from their settlement, the camp is quiet, with the entire convoy processing the horror of Heechingu's punishment, and what may come should the Fire Giants fully take over.

The little girl Ivy has grown somewhat close to is.. Numb, unable to wrap her mind around the horror of the crucifixion, Ivy sits with the family, hugging all of them with her tail as they all sit there together in silence.

Roast finds herself at the highest point of the camp, a position where she can watch everyone. She thinks about the day, the moment where Grimrid pointed out that mayhaps it was Roast who lost hope for a second. She didn’t like that feeling. And her mind wanders to her father, how he would have chastised her for that weakness she had displayed, the same sort of hesitation that would have killed everyone. 

She strengthens herself to not entertain such idiocy and weaknesses no longer, to keep her mind focused on the goal. But as ever, her father goes on, hammering in a lesson long understood when Roast shouts at him to shut up. It works, as she summons the will power to stand up against her father.

Elisa tells tales of Baldwin to the commoners, who come to her asking her if she has seen him, felt him, especially since she claims she is a Knight of Radhantia. She humanises him in a way that encourages the people that his conviction is achievable, that Man will always find a way to survive, and that Man must work together, not only inspiring them with stories of his kindness and benevolence, she also reveals that the King is extremely young, yet so wizened.

On the 8th Night, Dirrel asks Grimrid about female dwarves which prompts Grimrid to wax about them for longer than is needed. Ivy continues carving her circle members, as the kid, Ayla, points out that there’s one that Ivy can’t get right. Before Ivy can respond much, the kid asks if Ivy is a plant, and what it is like to have a tail, and if she is the only special tree. Ayla tells Ivy that the rest of the children fear her, believing that she would eat them, but not Ayla.

## The Roast

On the 9th Night, the refugees reveal that they have meat available to cook, having taken some from the camp and ask if they could have a feast to lift spirits. The party agrees but are cautious about the dangers of doing so. That night, as they head to rest, carrion crawlers ambush their camp. But the party manages to protect the refugees. They are scared but still press on.

## New Phandalin

On the 10th day, they reach New Phandalin, but instead of safety, they are greeted with the sight of a mountain of corpses. Elisa runs up first, seeing the corpses, joined by Grimrid who sees it and rushes back to the others, telling them to head back. But the alarmed behaviour has gotten the rest curious as they walk to the ledge, seeing the same sight. Elisa rushes down, followed by Roast. Surrounded by the corpses, Elisa reaches out to her patron, learning that this incident happened 1-2 weeks ago, and Goldenfields is worse now but not as bad as the current place. Elisa then connects to another emotion strand, as the party witnesses the fall of New Phandalin. 

They saw the path of Sir Daron, how he was a drunkard, kicked awake by his Squire Bernard and summoned before Lupercal. The man everyone, including himself, thought of as incapable was given the important task to manage both New Phandalin and Goldenfields whilst Lupercal and the knights head to where their King has assigned them to. As well as tasked to keep an item safe, the a battery of the Vonindod, that could blow half the city up if in the hands of someone else. Lupercal mentions that he doesn't trust Elder Maxson, the Elder of the Custodes Legere in Lupra, citing Maxson's proclivity for the occult and his excitement at weaponising almost everything they find as the reason why Lupercal does not trust the Custodians with the Battery. 

Lupercal tells Daron that he sees through Daron's facade of drunken incompetence, and trusts him and tells him to be careful. The vision changes to Sir Daron standing over the walls of New Phandalin, taking in the responsibility that has been given to him. Then the chaos as stones are thrown at them. Then battle, the moment Sir Daron realises that the enemies outnumber them greatly. Daron turns to Bernard, telling him to run with the civilians, to head to Goldenfields. 

Then as Sir Daron stands before the knights awaiting his command, he realises that it feels nice to be respected, and realising that he could have gotten a wife and kids, as he regrets moments of his life, how he didn't get to say goodbye to his parents or Bernard, how he didn’t even teach Bernard how to hold a sword. But still he opens up the chest entrusted to him by Lupercal, drinking some great wine for the last time. He and his men fight into the middle of the enemy group, and that’s when Sir Daron sets off the core, sacrificing himself and his men, to buy the others some time. Even in his last moments, he bore no selfish thoughts, just focused on the logistics, on buying the others enough time to be safe.

With that, the party’s path is clear as they head out another 5 days to Goldenfields. 

# The Red Army

As Goldenfields begins to break through the horizon, the party is ambushed by centaurs from the Fire Giant’s army, who surprisingly do not wear the red khan clothes, their leader Xanth speaks to the party, convincing them to stand down, hoping for a solution of non violence. It is clear to the party that Xanth is not proud of his threats, and that a fight could put the lives of all the Commoners with them at risk. The party relents, agreeing as they learn that Xanth serves for their own reason, appearing to be individuals familiar with the concept of patience and biding until it is the right time. Ayla’s father also pleads with Ivy to protect his family at all cost.

Approaching the Fire Giant camp, Ivy notices something flying in the distance, some flying golems that the Fire Giant have under their command. Learning that they've basically created an entire network of unsleeping, unexhaustible flying scouts that have basically locked Goldenfields completely in.

The party also sees the line of Iron Golems that prepare and fire flaming rocks using Trebuchets outside of the massive Warcamp. As they enter, a few different groups catch the party's eye, a mercenary group of Necromancer, with their leader wearing an illfitting Urshuen Golden Mask and Urshuen wear. Elisa immediately notices that he has stolen the commissions that they made with the Blacksmith Darren, the Blue Dragon Scale from the scales Iymrith gave her and the Sea Serpent Shield. They also see Orik Thunderbeard, the one-armed Dwarf whom Elisa met in Gauntlgrym, now looking far more Devilish than Dwarf.
 
The party speaks to the Warmaster, whom Grimrid met many many years ago, now a stark difference from the spoilt brat whom wanted to keep Grimrid as a pet. Now a seasoned warrior, political powerhouse and inspiring leader of the Fire Giants. The party members recognise her armour and dual shields, belonging to that of her Mother, whom they saw dying at the hands of King Hekaton in the Giant Visions of Xunbelaern.

Grimrid does the bulk of the talking, subtly bringing up old manners of the Princess Eyouthful antics, who insists that she knows better now. The party tries to get the Princess to stop the attack on Goldenfields, but she tells them that if they wish to negotiate, they must first show their worth to her, that if they cannot even defeat her Forgemaster, then they have no right to request anything from the Red Horde. As she calls out to her Forgemaster, it is revealed to be Jarchigudai, Grimrid's old mentor from many many thousands of years ago.
