As the party prepares to spring their attack on the Drow attacking the lone warrior, having bid farewell to Myrrdin during their travel. The warrior deals with the monstrous Draegloth while the party takes down the Drow Assassins. Successfully knocking one unconscious, albeit with difficulty. Having burned all their resources in the fight. The party tie their prisoner up and the lone warrior, reveals her name to be Nari. A curly haired, freckled redhead. She offers the party various bits of bread and raisins, revealing that she has meat and fresh wood and accidentally revealing she is from a settlement and an order of Paladins. She also reveals that she is headed to Karaz-Andor to trade and offers to travel with the party, saying that the Duergar might be a little less welcoming to them than they are to her. In the meantime, Brom also loots the Drow, acquiring a few lockets of Lolth.

During their short rest, the Drow awakens and the party begins their interrogation. Asking him if he was sent to kill Nari and where the Drow reside. Using Detect Thoughts, Elisa sees that the Drow praise a Dark Goddess named Lolth. When questioned, she sees images of a spiritual apparition of the Goddess standing amongst other Drow priestesses that ritualistically sacrifice one of their own to their Goddess. She also realises that the Drow weren't sent to kill Nari but rather were simply seeking new slaves. Information that she chooses to withhold. Speaking to the Drow, they make a deal with him, that they will give him his life in exchange for his silence in regards to seeing them. He swiftly agrees and they hand him his poison Scimitar back, and he leaves.

The party then continue travelling and during travel, Elisa manages to coax out some information from her. Nari *accidentally* reveals that she is from the Golden Order of Marina, and reveals the story of Godwin and his 5 Children to the party. Stating that Marina was the 1st child and a noble warrior who died in the last death throes of Fore Lindrov, this information being confirmed by Amunkhet who was actually there during that time. The 2nd child, Godfrey was a tinkerer and someone who never used magic, the Twins, Makaila and Melania joined some kind of religious cult. Which was confirmed by Amunkhet to be the Fire and Wind Churches, which the party know have been destroyed. It is unusual however that the name of the final child was never revealed, and even Amunkhet did not know the child's name.

As the party arrive at the great city of Karaz-Andor, they see a familiar head pop up, Galnom Forgeripper. He tells the Guards at the gate to stand down and hobbles down from his post, brinign the party to his home to talk as Nari goes off into the city, bidding the party farewell. Inside, the party reveal to Galnom that they are here seeking trade. He tells the party that they will need ot prove themselves to be friends of the Duergar first, and try to catch the attention of a Cleric of Laduguer. Only then can there be a possible trade agreement. In the meantime, Untuk spends some time sharpening his axe, showing Galnom his technique, to which Galnom decides to do it for him. Sharpening Untuk's axe, giving it an extra (2d6) slashing damage on its following strike.

The party then head to the inn and Galnom returns to his post, it is there they meet Kaldur Doomfist. Who grumpily gives them room and board, however at a much higher price due to Untuk suggesting that Kaldur take credit. The party then decide to room together in order to keep their expenses lowered. The next day, Kaldur gives the party a quest, to acquire mushrooms for him that grow at the base of a black plant with a cone-shaped head.

The party venture through the Underdark, guided by Elisa, they encounter Skeletons blocking their path and swiftly take them down. Using the same tactic as before, with Untuk and Elisa working together to bring everyone across via jumping and teleporting, they eventually arrive at a Dwarvish Settlement where Milton tells Elisa that there are remnants of a battle between Drow and Dwarves. The party enter the shoddy settlement and find the Dwarves ready to execute a Drow Warrior. Her party having been killed by the Dwarves, although the Dwarves did also lose their fair share of people. The party entreat the party to allow the Drow to live. With Brom at the helm of the conversation, they manage to convince the Dwarves to stand down, saying that the information the Drow holds may be of use. The Dwarves however scoff at the notion of Untuk's suggestion that the party will eliminate all of the Drow for them to save them, but are placated once Untuk shares his smithing tools with them, the party spend 3 hours of downtime in the settlement while they question the Drow as the Dwarves are distracted with having fresh tools, they begin forging and improving their shoddy weapons with great enthusiasm.

Elisa, Brom and Amunkhet proceed to interrogate the Drow. Using *Detect Thoughts*, Elisa realises that the Female Drow are much more disciplined. With her being extremely effective at hiding her thoughts when Elisa reveals to her that she can hear the Drow's thoughts. However after some convincing, they manage to get her to reveal her name, Adira. Once again, Elisa realises that the Drow were simply here looking for slaves. Information that she again withholds from Untuk for fear of his reaction. Meanwhile, Amunkhet and Brom wish to be more diplomatic with her, wanting to have a possible ally within the Drow. They find out that her name is Adira, and that they will allow her to live if she helps get them to the cave they seek safely. She agrees to this after some pondering and the party begin discussing on a way to convince the Dwarves to let her go. Immediately Adira undoes her bindings and punches Brom in the face, saying that she'll meet them on the outskirts of the settlement. Brom pretends to become stunned from the strike and Adira gets away. Untuk sees this and begins charging towards where Adira was last seen, while Elisa just had her armour improved by a Dwarf. Having walked away annoyedly as she realised that Brom and Amunkhet wanted to deal with the Drow captive. She lazily walks out, drawing slight suspicion from the Dwarves but they do not push the matter. Believing that the party would recapture the Drow and bring her back. As Untuk rushes out of the settlement, with Amunkhet and Brom realising that he will just attack Adira without warning, they attempt to yell out to Untuk to stop, while Amunkhet casts Minor Illusion to create a bang to drown out the sound of Brom's shouting. Untuk hears this but elects to ignore Brom's requests, attempting to grab Adira in a rage as she waited patiently for the party outside the settlement. 

She just barely manages to dodge Untuk's 2 attempts to grab her and begins to retreat as she realises what's going on, thinking that the party lied and betrayed her. Amunkhet just barely catches up and together with Brom they're able to calm Untuk down and convince Adira that it was a misunderstanding. Citing that he didn't know of their plan. 

Untuk and Elisa are very clearly not happy with the deal made with Adira, with Untuk angrily telling her that he is keeping an eye on her.  Adira then begins leading the party through the Underdark. Swiftly stopping the party, identifying Ropers that loom ahead, she has them climb up a steep wall to the left and continue on. Soon the party arrive at the cave, where they are met with multiple Fungi that block the entrance. Adira quickly tells them that they need to destroy the Fungus all at once, as they will attack the party. Amunkhet casually blasts them away with Fireball and the party proceed into the cave. However they realise that Adira is staying behind, they suggest that she could accompany the party for a short while, a notion she rejects, requesting that she be armed while the party heals her. 

Brom and Amunkhet requests that Untuk give the scimitar he looted from the Drow to Adira so that she may survive the Underdark. He refuses, and the party instead gives her a dagger. She also mentions to them that the cave is dangerous, a place that even the Drow are told to stay away from. As Amunkhet arms Adira, she palms to her a small vial. What poison it is is unknown, not without a Herbalist to identify it. The party then head inside the cave, where they come face-to-face with a cave of fungus and plant matter. Elisa quickly casts Speak with Plants and talks to the moss that looms over the door. 

The door reveals its name to be Alkilith, remaining cryptic and vague about questions regarding creatures that enter and leave the cave. Alkilith opens the door for the party and they enter, they watch as Untuk disappears behind it as if having gone through a portal. Something that despite Elisa's constant probing yields nothing but cryptic replies. The party take a leap of faith and enter the door. Finding themselves in a maze of fungal like chambers and passageways. Elisa and Untuk find themselves with one of Elisa's Lions in a room of vines and plant matter. The plants attach themselves to armour made of organic biomatter and black chitin. The plants around them delicately cry for food, calling the triplet food. A notion Elisa politely rejects. As they approach the armour, it also talks, saying that it is trapped and in pain. That Elisa and Untuk should free it, however as Elisa approaches the armor, looking to touch it. The plant matter holding the armour in place begin screeching that the armor belongs to their mistress, the queen of mushrooms. They successfully drive both Elisa and Untuk out, who go off looking for the rest of their party. 

Meanwhile, Amunkhet finds herself trapped amongst vines, unable to get out. Brom finds himself alone with Elisa's Lion in the final chamber, standing face-to-face with a wall of fungal humanoids, assimilated into the wall of mycelium. Sat within it is a Skeletal woman of fungus and mushroom, and the mushrooms that the party needs. The Lion tries to roar at the wall and Brom swiftly grips his hand around its maw. Something that causes it to turn heel and run, disappearing into the maze. Brom elects to turn around and search for the rest of his party. However he runs into a group of roaming Infectors, who begin attacking him. As Brom takes a heavy amount of damage, he finds himself needing to retreat, with the Infectors close behind. He runs into a room full of chests and scrolls. Deciding to stand his ground he begins fighting. 

Meanwhile Amunkhet having finally gotten herself out. 

She roams the maze and finds herself in a room of Myconids, tending to mushrooms. She immediately turns around and walks out, finding herself in the Mushroom Queen's Room. She decides to commit the image to memory, and begins painting. In the meantime, Untuk and Elisa rummage through the cave, finding themselves in varying fungal chambers, back in the Armor room, to be entangled by vines, and then finally, they hear commotion up ahead. Brom is being beaten down by the infectors, and he is losing. They swiftly run up to him, with Elisa on Untuk's back, they begin blasting the Infectors, pushing them into the chamber with chests, only for the chests to morph and reveal themselves to be Mimics. Amunkhet finishes her drawing and begins to look for the party again, stumbling back into the Myconid Room, who realise that she is there, she immediately casts a spell, turning the ground to Difficult Terrain and running away, finding herself at the site of the battle. She immediately springs into action and assists the party. As they take down the mimics and the infectors, the party heal up and collect the scrolls. They leave the chamber, as Amunkhet reveals she is being chased. 

The party cycle back and forth through the maze, with Amunkhet being restrained by vines 4 more times, before the party finally stumbles again back into the armor room. Elisa refrains from casting Speak with Plants again as the last time she tried to command the Plants, the Mushroom Queen spoke to her telepathically. Threatening her. Elisa tells the party about the armor, saying that it is trapped and wants to be freed. The rest of the party elect to just leave it there, but Elisa's curiosity gets the better of her and she tries to touch the armour. Brom rightfully stands between her and the armor. Saying that it is dangerous, only for her to teleport behind him and poke it. The armor shakes and shimmers and it grasps onto Elisa and borrows its tendrils into her body. The party is sat, horrified at what has happened. They attempt to remove the armour but its foot long tendrils have dug right into Elisa's body. They elect to move on as she doesn't seem to be in any pain and finally find themselves in the Mycelium room, where the Queen of Fungus, Zuggtmoy resides. It is here that Elisa realises that the plants are in fact Shriekers, who let out a loud scream when disturbed. The party discuss their plans, deciding to try to go all in and get as many baskets of mushrooms as possible, giving Sethos, Amunkhet's summon, Ilythyrra's Dagger. He very carefully cuts out the mushrooms, and brings it to the party members who are sat at the exit. With Amunkhet and Elisa mounted onto Untuk's back. 

On the 4th attempts, Sethos disturbs a shrieker and a horde of half eaten humanoids bleed out of Zuggtmoy's walls and begins chasing the party. Immediately Untuk dashes out and manages to find his way out into the exit. However the party soon realise Brom has been left behind, Brom sprints through the various chambers, lost in the winding passageways and identical rooms. Just as he's about to become exhausted, he finally finds his way out and jumps out the exit, where the horde stops just on the line. With Sethos slowly maneuvering his way out of the cave with Ilythyrra's Dagger in hand. The party then head back, meeting a gargoyle that drops them a vial of Dragon's Blood, that when used, upcasts a spell by 5 levels. They safely make it back, giving Kaldur, the innkeeper his things. With the party also calming Untuk down as he originally wanted to confront Kaldur for sending them on such a dangerous quest. Kaldur gives the party 150gp, 4 Potions of Greater Healing and an Essence of Ether. They also realise that Kaldur is a Poison-maker, having stored the mushrooms in a basket next to his Poisoner's Kit. The party then notice Nari, sitting by a blonde-haired woman with green eyes sipping a cup of mead. The party are then introduced to Holly. Who tells them her mentor is out of town andd so she's taking the time to go on adventures. Untuk stares uncomfortably at both Nari and Holly,  causing them both to avert eye contact with him while talking to the party. As Untuk begins to boast about their adventures, speaking of how the party barely escaped death, Amunkhet then mentions the Mycelium woman in the walls, showing her painting, Holly immediately perks up, offering 1000gp for the painting and location. To which the party agrees. 

Brom however, grows suspicious and begins to somewhat interrogate Holly. Who remains cryptic about why she wants the picture and location so badly. Nari attempts to break the tension, by telling the party she's on a quest for one of the Clerics and if the party accompanies her and Holly they could put in a good word for them. The party agrees and they head off. Elisa, while having a conversation with Holly is able to probe that she's from the surface, coming up with strange names for her family members such as Oro, Jubi, Graman, Yeenie and her mentor, Natasha.

She cites that she dislikes her "family" and misses home. Something that Elisa relates to, and as soon as Holly asks about Elisa's own home, she recognises the sadness in Elisa's eyes, and quickly diverts the conversation to the cave that the party live in. Elisa reveals the various strange characters that live in the cave, information to which Holly is completely unfazed. She also gives the party 2 Potions of Speed.

As the party navigate through, avoiding all encounters thanks to Nari's familiarity with the Underdark and Elisa learning more about the new Symbiotic armour that has grafted itself to her body. The party run into an abandoned cart pulled by Umber Hulks. The party tries to be cautious but Holly simply walks forward, and the party is swiftly ambushed by a party of Drow. Who Holly very quickly threatens into submission. The party also notice Adira amongst the Drow, the Arachnomancer Captain states that the Library the party seek is a Drow Archive. She says that if the party clear it out for them they will allow access. Something that they all agree to, Holly mentions that they should split up and sweep the library to prevent the Drow from being able to lay an ambush. 

Scorch marks are found all over the Library, and toppled books that appear unburnt. Holly quickly casts Darkvision on Untuk and dispels 2 traps that she finds. Tellign the party to stay safe, they split up. The party enter the first chamber where Untuk and Elisa hear a heartbeat amongst the Debris. In the meantime, Amunkhet casts Detect Magic and inspects the blocked door to the next chamber, while Brom loots a corpse of a strange fire-dressed Humanoid in another end of the room. Finding a necklace with a strange symbol on it.

Untuk and Elisa realise that they have stumbled upon a Firey Orb in place of a heart in the split open body of a man in blackened armour. As the party try to figure out what to do, the bomb triggers and blasts the party. After recovering Untuk smashes through the debris after Elisa sends a rat into the next chamber, revealing 4 ambushers. Untuk retreats back through the hallway and the party funnel the 4 Razerblasts. Who all have their heart replaced with the same fiery bomb in their chests. The party dispatch the ambushers but not without taking heavy damage. In the battle, Elisa hears the men scream a name, "Imix" in Ignan, the language of Fire Elementals. They also find another Gargoyle, with Elisa asking for a potion, it miraculously drops a Potion with its eyes glowing Blue instead of Red.

They then find themselves in the upper levels where one room has been turned into a killbox by the cultists and another has been blocked by a Wall of Fire. Elisa attempst to dispel the Wall Twice, and the party head through. Finding themselves in a room of ice with flash frozen corpses, they meet a white haired man named Liperetus. Who scans each of their minds with Detect Thoughts to look through their memories to confirm they are not cultists. Elisa flusters Liperetus by asking if he is a Dragon, to which he answers, "Wait how do you know I'm a Dragon" to which Elisa reveals she didn't know he was  Dragon. The confusion causes Liperetus to fluster, and as he gathers himself. He gives the party a magic scroll of Ice Storm and also cites that they remind him of a group he's met before. He also reveals the Gargoyles belong to someone named Iymrith. He takes his leave and the party proceed to the next room. 

The party then proceed to the upper levels where they find dead Drow Priestesses, looting the various rooms. They also see a Plaque stating various tenants of Lolth. 

1. Men are lesser than animals, only Women may lead the way.
2. Carry out all of Lolth’s Tests without question.
3. Harm no sacred Spider of Lolth.
4. The Children of Lolth clothe themselves only in darkness, or none at all.

The party figure out that each subsequent chamber is a test of Lolth. The first door being only openable by a woman. In the 2nd chamber, the party hear an alluring voice telling them all to draw the blood of their allies without quesiton. To which Untuk immeidately strikes all the party members, opening the 2nd door. In the 3rd chamber, Amunkhet steps up to the door, only for a ball of spiders to burst out from her stomach they bite and sting at her body, the party then realise that eveyrtime they accidentally kill a spider. The testee takes a heavy amount of damage. Amunkhet goes down and is swiftly healed by her party members. 

Untuk decides to step up to take this trial, raging, he endures the horrific biting and poison of spiders gushing out of every orifice in his body. The party swiftly attempt to clear his orifices of spiders as he stands there, enduring the pain. Eventually, the spiders are cleared and the door opens. Soon the party find themselves in the final chamber. Realising that they have to remove their clothes or wear the dark garbs of the Priestesses. The party finally open the door, only to be met with creepy fiery children with clawed hands along with 2 Fire Elementals. Untuk rushes forward and slashes thruogh the Lava Children. And the party discovers they are *immune to metal*

With Amunkhet forced to use The Dragon's Blood they received from the Gargoyle, she summons Sethos at 9th level. Eventually the party is able to defeat the cult of fire, only to be surprised by the Drow. Who have betrayed them, along with a regretful Adina. Untuk resolves to kill her as he watches her and her Drow contingent attack the party, only to be interrupted by Nari and Holly. Holly goes berserk from grief as she relives her memory of losing her friends and withers away all of the Drow. Angrily disintegrating the Drow Captain, with Untuk attempting to kill Adina as Nari, Elisa and Brom are trying to calm Holly down.

Amunkhet jumps in the way of Untuk and is nearly cut down, she even takes the time to heal Adina to keep her alive. Realising what he's done Untuk stands down and Holly teleports everyone away as Adina, full of grief and regret leaves the party. With Amunkhet's final words to her that they could protect her. Holly teleports everyone out but Untuk, as an unwilling target is left behind. He sits in the tower, alone, thinking about what he's done. The party begin panicking and Nari, in her own panic as Holly teleports away again calls for help with Sending. A Custodes of the Golden Order, clad in thick plate and standing 10ft tall appears, simply grunting, he scans the mind of everybody, teleports to Untuk, knocks him out and teleports him back. Before telling Nari something and leaving. Nari then says she's no longer allowed to go home until she finds the Scroll of Legend Lore that the Order seeks.

The party head back to the Inn and have a long talk with Untuk regarding their ability to trust him, and if he should even be adventuring in the first place.
