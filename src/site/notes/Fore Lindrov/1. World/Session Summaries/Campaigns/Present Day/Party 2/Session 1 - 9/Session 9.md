As the party sits and thinks about their time as slaves, each of them are struck with nightmares of their past. Elisa is haunted in her dreams by the death of her city and family, accompanied by the echoing footsteps of a man clad in plate armour, leaving behind the yellow mucous of the horrid worm creatures she met in the City of the Zaratan in the beginning of her journey. Brom is shadowed by the selling of his oath, his betrayal of his order and the disappointment of his mentor. Coupled along with the death of the troop under his care and protection. Friend-Friend dreams of ripping flesh apart, his newfound undeath something that speaks to him in his dreams. While Amunkhet ponders in her room, she watches her new undead servant, having realised just what kind of a monster she's become. Allowing Slavery to change her. Sethos manifests and accompanies her, speaks to her about her responsibility and her greatness as a ruler.

As the next day comes, with Amunkhet noticing Elisa's refusal to talk to her. They explore the Tower of the Eternal Flame, with Brom going off to the library and meeting a cultist named Elisa, he picks up a book to learn how to speak Ignan. During this time he bonds with Elisa, learning more about how the Eternal Flame would cleanse the world, how Imix would cleanse everyone of their sins. He references his own sins and his own pain and Elisa brings him into one of their magical prayer rooms, where a magical flame of Imix spits and sputters within the prayer room. Elisa takes Brom's hands and asks him to close his eyes. Asking him to picture all of the horror and pain that he's been through. She then pulls his hands into the fire, and to Brom it feels as if the weight in his chest was being taken away, burned away in the flame of Imix.

As he opens his eyes, experiencing the effects of the Calm Emotions spell. Elisa reaches into the fire and pulls out an Amulet of the Cult of Eternal Flame and gifts it to Brom, a representation of his rebirth in the flame. Brom gains resistance to fire damage for as long as he is wearing the Amulet of the Cult of Eternal Flame. In the meantime, Amunkhet thinks about what to do with Iilvara, deciding to dispel her undead servitude and going to speak to Asha, the Drow priestess that was brought to the tower during the party's breakout out of Stronghold Levesari. Elisa goes off to get some buckets from Elizar while Friend-Friend gets food from him. Elisa spends some time taking a bath with magically created water and uses Milton to heat up the buckets of water to give herself a nice bath. 

Brom awakens from his room and goes to meet Amunkhet, the both of them head over to Asha's room and knock on the door. Noticing her behind it, they manage to persuade her to calmly open the door and drop the spell they detected she was readying. Asha is tense.. afraid. During the conversation with her Amunkhet and Brom attempt to question her, to find out what her intentions are. She reveals that she wants to leave, something that the pair of adventurers do not want to allow to happen. Asha scoffs at their fake righteousness, despite their suggestion that they are trying to be civil and kind hearted she tells them that in the end, she is still a prisoner who was forced to accompany her captors while they were burning the stronghold down. She had no choice in the matter. Brom and Amunkhet leave her in her room as they go outside to discuss what to do. Brom hears the evil whispers of Izeelzee, who tells him to dominate Asha, to torture her and break her will.

Something that Brom finds himself becoming more and more amicable to as time goes on. The party then decide to meet with Vanifer, with Elizar having been informed by Elisa during her bucket collection run that they wish to speak to Vanifer. Amunkhet also takes this time to gather the opinions of everyone who they brought from the Stronghold on what they think should be done about Asha, with everyone saying that she must die. The party also discover that the prisoners have taken up well in the Tower of the Eternal Flame, save for Preston. Who is panicking and wishes to leave despite the safety of the tower, terrified that he is in the base of cultists. Elisa manages to calm him down, offering him a chance to come to their settlement. Albeit having to do some further calming down after informing him that there are Hobgoblins, Gith, Vampires and Trolls in their settlement. The party also learn of Ront's intention to stick with the party, Topsy & Turvy and Buppido's desires to stay. Brom also takes the time to help Sarith with his issue with lycanthropy as he grapples with the fact that the murders he was framed for were in fact real. Sarith thanks Brom for removing the curse, and agrees to go with them to their settlement.

The party then make their way to the meeting room where they overhear Vanifer arguing with a Drow Wizard, accompanied by a Death Slaad. He insists that the Cult must attack the Drow city of Menzoberranzan, while Vanifer argues back that she has to take care of everyone and will not send anyone off to die needlessly. The party meet with Vanifer and she takes the time to check up on everyone, giving them all hugs. The other prisoners take their leave after thanking Vanifer for her hospitality and the party follow her to her room, where they see Gili. Who has had his mushroom burnt off by Vanifer and is hanging out among her ash paintings.

They also learn a bit more about Vanifer, who reveals to them her history as a consort for nobles as Elisa asks her if she knows that the people she cares for are Cultists. She reveals that the Cultists of the Eternal Flame are all drawn to fire and the tower, that their souls are riddled with chaos. She is here to guide them, to help them and care for them. She also reveals that she lost someone very close to her, and that Imix saved her from her life of being a consort and brought her here. She supposes to lead the cultists, a role that she is more than happy to take, to take care of the people around her. Amunkhet brings up what to do with Asha, which Vanifer immediately says that Asha is free to do what she wants to do and that Vanifer refuses to take anyone Prisoner. Friend-Friend also notices Vanifer's weakness and exhaustion. Having not healed from the breakout, he implores her to eat, and also learns that Vanifer has to go down into the basements to expand the food farms. The party then remember that the Cult has been looking rather emaciated, and that it seems most of the food has been going to the guests even though they have a shortage of food due to recruitment still happening during Vanifer's absence. 

The party agree to help Vanifer and are led down to the basements by Elizar, where they encounter an entire dungeon filled with corrupted Gargoyles, the same ones that they've been seeing outside in the Underdark. But these ones are corrupted by some kind of fiery energies. They destroy the Gargoyles and make their way down into a room, where an uncorrupted Gargoyle awaits. It is revealed the Gargoyles are owned by someone named Iymrith, who hid some Dragons away to keep the Dragon Kingdom alive after they fell into war when the God-King sealed himself in his castle. Iymrith reveals that Omaira, the Red Dragon left here went mad due to isolation, something that Iymrith deeply regrets.

Iymrith sorrowly asks the party to help end Omaira's misery, to correct the wrong that she has made. She gives the party a Scroll of Heal, Major Illusion and a few potions to help with the battle. The party enter Omaira's chamber and Polymorph both Milton and Brom into Giant Apes, beating down Omaira, Brom scales the massive columns in her lair and jumps onto her, pushing her down into the ground and holding her by the neck as the party blast away at her, narrowly escaping her Breath Attack while Milton beats away at her hardened scales. The Mad Dragon thrashes about trying to escape, but as soon as Friend-Friend moves behind her towards the Horde she was protecting, she goes Berserk, nearly breaking out of Brom's grapple. She begins to frantically claw away at the apes and tries to kill Friend-Friend, Omaira releases one final breath attack that Brom redirects into the ceiling with his hand still on her neck as Milton finishes her off, jumping onto her and crashing into her body, the dust clears, revealing the now dead Mad Dragon, Omaira. 

The party then go to her horde and as the coin falls away, they realise that Omaira was pregnant and had laid an egg during her Isolation. Iymrith shakily says that she didn't know as Vanifer rushes into the dungeon having heard all of the commotion. The mood is solemn as Vanifer realises what just happened, angry that the party didnt call her and saddened by the Dragon's death. Iymrith leaves the party with finding someone kind to raise the baby and bury Omaira with her horde before disappearing, the Party gives these wishes to Vanifer while keeping Iymrith's existence hidden, Vanifer takes the egg and seals Omaira's body in. The party take the time to take a Long Rest, beforehand Amunkhet also tells Elisa that she has released Iilvara and plans to mummify her as atonement for how cruel she became.

Elisa takes another bath as Brom, Friend-Friend and Amunkhet decide what to do with Asha. Brom leans towards torture as Friend-Friend leans towards casting Geas. Plans that Amunkhet is strongly against. The party decide to leave this for another day and go to do other things, with Friend-Friend exchanging notes with Elizar and Brom taking a bath as well. During this time Amunkhet speaks to Asha again, who seems resigned to death. But Amunkhet solemnly reveals that she does not want Asha to die, however Asha remind Amunkhet that the rest of the party seems to want her dead. She asks that Amunkhet give her Iilvara's Pifwafwi and Tentacle Rod so that she can survive out in the Underdark. Amunkhet appears desperate to find any reason to trust Asha, asking Asha to promise that she won't bring down the Drow on the Tower of Eternal Flame. Which Asha, despite lying, Amunkhet takes at face value. Amunkhet gets her the items, and Asha, armed with a new lease on life, climbs out the window, gripping the stone walls and ready to get back home, she jumps out and reaches out to grab onto the cave wall and a ray of force jets out from the top floor and disintegrates her in an instant. She turns into ash and is killed. 

Amunkhet, shocked, then angry storms up to where the force came from, with the party in tow they confront whoever was in the room. Banging on the door it is opened, revealing a Death Slaad and Vizeran, the Drow Mage behind it. He calls Amunkhet a fool and an idiot for trying to set Asha free. Which the party is extremely surprised at and slightly annoyed that Amunkhet tried to do this in secret. Vizeran reveals he is writing scrolls of Teleport for them, which is a waste of his time.

Saying he is only doing it as Vanifer deems it more important than saving the Drow. He reveals that he has tried to stop Lolth for centuries, saying the grip that she has on the Drow is more akin to hostage taking than benevolence. The only way to show the Drow a better way is to show them what Lolth is, a egomaniacal coward.  Amunkhet, still angry, says that Asha's death was not his choice to make, something that Vizeran scoffs at, once again calling her an idiot and makes a threat to disintegrate her should she endanger the tower again. Friend-Friend immediatley Wildshapes and is Paralyzed by Vizeran. He once again tells the party that he is doing what needs to be done and had it not been for him Asha would have killed them all. 

The party all hold their tongues, understanding their position they leave. However Elisa stays behind and tells him they think he was right, something he is impressed by. He tells her that should she want to help him stop the Drow to come find him as he has work, a way to save them all and show them a better way. The party then go back to their rooms and rest, having been given 2 stone masons and tools by Vanifer, they make their way home. Teleported back, with Jimjar choosing to leave the Tower instead now that the party no longer needs a guide through the Underdark. They each give him a gold piece to exchange if they meet again. The party rearrive back at home after a long hiatus but the celebrations are cut short as they realise a shimmer of magic is blocking he cave entrance. Vizeran arrives and tells everyone not to touch or interact with the shimmer of magic he reveals to be Time itself. He tells them it could kill them all and stations Kleve, his Death Slaad to keep watch. The party then get Ront and Preston settled in the settlement and are welcomed back by all of the settlers and kids except the Trolls. Elisa reconnects with Dave and checks up on him.

Friend-Friend is introduced to everyone and Alastair asks the party to meet him in his chambers, he reveals that his blood calls to him to somewhere in the Underdark and asks the party to accompany him. Moving with him in the Underdark they find The Crypt of the Von Carstein, clearing out the dungeon Alastair finds the Von Carstein Ring and Blood Drinker. Gaining new abilities he Teleports the party out back home. Where he tells them he owes them an Eternal Debt, one which he repays with Eternity, the offer of Vampirism.
