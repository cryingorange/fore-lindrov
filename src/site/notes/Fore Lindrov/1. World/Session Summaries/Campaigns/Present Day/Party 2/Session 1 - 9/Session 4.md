The party finally finds themselves in the final chamber of the Kruthik Hive. A chamber of lava and a statue of the Duergar God Laduguer. Sat at the base of his statue is an adamantine Morningstar. Galnom immediately draws his weapon as the party catch their breath, pointing it at the Mindflayer. He gives Hakon an ultimatum, to leave permanently or to die. Hakon agrees to leave, and just as he is leaving Ilythyrra and the party remind him that the Gith back at their home have a trepid history with Mindflayers as well. Hakon realises what this means and leaves the cave with his kinsman. 

Elisa then confronts Brom about what happened, demanding to know what was it that Brom did as she saw him die. He insists that there is no time for this, that it would be best to come to safe place before talking about any of this. As this is happening, the statue of Laduguer cracks and bursting from the rift laughing and cackling about something "working" is Myrddin, a Wizard donning academy robes and a book. He introduces himself and the party realise that he is a man obsessed with knowledge and little self-awareness. 

Elisa inspects the adamantine Morningstar, casting detect magic she sees a red aura about it. Brom asks Galnom if it would be okay for the party to take this Morningstar, to which he agrees. As Brom touches the Morningstar he hears his new Patron's voice in his head, "A Gift." and the mention of someone named Izeelzee. Elisa watches as the dark energies pulse through and conjoin with Brom's magical energies. The party also realise that Brom has changed, his sclera have become blcak and his pupils reddened. His armour and body bears the mark of his mortal wound to the Kruthik Hive Queen. Having removed his armour to show the party that he is fine. 

The party decide to drop it and move on, leaving the cave. They realise that Hakon took the Bag Of Holding as well as all their loot.

As the party make it back to their home base, the rest of the party, Untuk, Brom and Myrddin are introduced to the rest of the residents. With Alastair realising that Brom is an Oathbreaker. Untuk also notices that the Hobgoblin Warband has only 3 fighting males. And Myrddin wishing to have an interview with Alastair. The party then meet Dave, who tells them that there may be a way to restore him. Something that Elisa requests that the party help her with. Brom agrees, and so does Untuk along with Ilythyrra and Myrddin.

He tells the party of a strange feeling he's gotten out North. The party set off and for the first time, Untuk, Brom and Myrddin take in what is the surface of Fore Lindrov. A dead world occupied only by Stilts, dead trees and off in the distance a forboding castle with a Blood Red Moon above the tip of the castle. As the party begin moving through they fight off some of the local Fauna. Taking Long Rests within Leomund's Tiny Hut, the party then run into a pack of Gnolls. Everyone else hides away, hidden beneath a small plateu while Brom moves forward to confront the Gnoll Pack. Unbeknownst to Brom as he begins to make threats, successfully driving away the Gnolls he manifests some of his new powers. Devilish features form on his body, Elisa and the rest of the party rush out to confront him. Ilythyrra immediately puts a dagger to his back while Elisa and Untuk demand to know what is going on with him.

Brom still refuses to elaborate, citing that now is not the time yet again. Myrddin however, sees the signs citing that this is a classic case of some kind of Demonic Possession. Brom still refuses to elaborate, causing Elisa to cast *Detect Thoughts* delving into his mind against Brom's will. She comes to the horrific realisation that he gave himself up to some kind of creature, in exchange for the party's lives. She immediately steps back, horrified. While Brom continues onward in an attempt to keep the party on track.

Myrddin rushes forward, also casting Detect Thoughts  in order to confirm whether or not Brom's thoughts are his own, causing Izeelzee to finally lose his temper. The Pit Fiend manifests itself, strangling Myrrdin, the creature demands that Brom do the task he was given to do. And not to allow lesser beings impose their wills upon him. The party is utterly horrified by this realisation that a Pit Fiend resides in Brom's morningstar. Brom, although reluctantly, obeys the Pit Fiends orders to Command the party to approach and Kneel. Imposing his will upon them, the Pit Fiend reminds the party not to stand in the way of Brom's Master's Will and returns into the Morningstar.

The party silently move on, with Myrddin, Untuk and Elisa just utterly horrified with Brom and Ilythyrra unsure of what she feels. Untuk completely ignoring Brom in their Long Rests in Leomund's Tiny Hut. The party finally arrive at what they think is their destination, a great fortress. Discovering a journal, they realise that some kind of Angel named Solaris declared war on Devils in the North. Brom also finds a spear, refusing to use The Bloody End he straps it to his back while leaving the morningstar at his hip, tying his broken longsword to his chest.

Meanwhile Myrrdin immediately searches through the keep while Elisa is distracted by a small light that she sees in the Residentials. Myrrdin then sees a small radiant flame in the throne room and hears a noise. He is then attacked by a Bullete, who he swiftly engages in a fight while the rest of the party rush in and help him seeing him having used his Blade Song, with Mirror Image and Shadow Blade active. The tiny flame is then touched by Elisa and its energies give the party a vision of Sylvaerys, Goetia and Fool standing over a defeated Angel, the Half-Orc says something to the Solar and as a look of horror appears on his face he finds himself approached by a woman with black finger tips, who says that the party has found her
a fine replacement, and corrupts him into a Demon Lord. The Light then zips away to the East, where Elisa swiftly rushes out to follow. Along with Ilythyrra, the rest of the party follow her and manage to convince her to stand down. Reminding her that their mission is to help heal her friend Dave. She bites her tongue and reluctantly agrees. Brom also searches through the throne and finds a radiant gem with The party then head into the remains of what looks like a warzone, various bodies of Humanoids and Devils. Small balls of glowing light float amongst the remains, that attack upon Elisa touching one of them, as the party swiftly takes them down. Brom uses one of his new abilities to dominate one of these creatures. 

They question it, revealing to the party that Party 1 was here, snatching after the soul of a Solar. Elisa immediately walks to the site of that clash and discovers drag marks leading North. Untuk also finds these drag marks and leads the party onward. The party then arrive at the Devilish fortress where. Elisa brings down the gates and they enter, searching through the Residentials, the party find various journals depicting a Devil that regained its memories named Gunther. Who took to eating the bodies on the battlefield and somehow became corrupted. Elisa turns into a spider and discovers Gunther, hungry at the Keep. He is lured out by their magic and pounces on Brom. During the fight he transforms into some kind of corrupted sludge, killing Untuk before finally being taken down.

Elisa casts Revivify on Untuk only for her to realise that something else has a grasp on her soul, one by one each of the party members get dragged into this new realm, with only Myrddin escaping as the sheer force of being dragged in causes him to begin bleeding out, but not before he catches a glimpse of the horror holdin onto Untuk's Soul.

Untuk meets his old companion Dao, who asks that he follow the eyes and join the Warband.
However Untuk realises it is not real and the horror decides to simply take his soul by force. Elisa continues holding onto Untuk, refusing to let go, along with Brom, Ilythyrra and ElIsa. Throughout this entire ordeal, Ilythyrra attempts to perceive her surroundings as the horror teleports her within the realm in front of the apparition of the Moon that exists within, it causes her to go completely blind. As for Untuk, he thinks to himself, "Who are you?" And catches a glimpse of the horror's true form. Permanently going mad. 

Elisa then realises that The One Of The Stars, her patron was still around. She offers it blood, feeding it, and also seeing The Space Between, seemingly trapped somehow. The party then collectively try to leave, having sacrificed both Elisa's Nature's Mantle and Ilythyrra's Weapon of Warning to leave the area. The party then silently rest as the blinded Ilythyrra is guided around by Elisa. 

The traumatisesd party makes their way back home, only to be met with a great Golden City. Suspicious, they still enter, meeting Godmore a demigod, after being lead by Huntley to the him. The party then stand before Godmore, having introduced himself and have questions to ask while Untuk storms off to the Inn to begin drinking.
