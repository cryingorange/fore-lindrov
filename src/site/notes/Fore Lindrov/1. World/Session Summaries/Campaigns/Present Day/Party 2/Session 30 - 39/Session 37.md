Following their conversation with Broomhilda, the party return to their rooms to rest. Elisa heads down to the already closed bar, helping herself to some of the liquor before retreating to her room, locking the door. Ilenti and Nyktos make their way to the others Erooms, with the latter swiping cups from the bar. Ilenti knocks on Rook’s door, inviting the Undead Celestial to talks and drinks. Nyktos does the same for Elisa, using the promise of alcohol to encourage Elisa’s participation. They all make their way to the roof of the inn, drinking the ale Nyktos had and testing the green wine Rook found some time ago. Ilenti kickstarts the conversation, sharing about their home and how they had been treated. She goes on to her regrets in life, namely the death of their father, Elareo. Ilenti shares the argument they had before she eventually found Elareo’s body dead in the middle of nowhere. She then hands it over to Nyktos, letting the older Shadar Kai share about her departure. Nyktos speaks of the first time she discovered her powers, how a game between children had become something more, and in her defence, she found herself manipulating the blood of another child. Nyktos shared the names she got called after, “Freak, Monster, Blasphemy E She goes on, explaining how she tried to hide in the shadows after, when one fateful day out leads to her eventual death and further unlocking of her powers, thus concluding why she had to leave, before she hurt anyone she cared for. The party takes that in for a second, before Ilenti continues about what happened in Nyktos Eabsence, that Ilenti had a close friend called Merlara. Merlara was a social outcast like they were, and was often a target of bullying due to her illness and breathing problems. Ilenti explains that Merlara eventually pulled away from her to join the “cool kids E It was fine, until one day Ilenti shows up at their usual hangout spot and sees a box that wasn’t there before. And upon opening the box, Ilenti finds Merlara, dead. She surmises that the other kids must have thought it funny to lock her in there, not really caring for the consequences. At all of this Rook speaks up, mentioning the promise he made to Nyktos pass and how he’d continue to support them in spite of what’s happened or to happen. Ths sisters voice their hesitation, with Nyktos commenting that Merlara’s death isn’t where the story ends for them. She goes on to share about Adella, a sweet, kind young lady who ran a pet sanctuary of sorts, who gets horribly murdered by Houzuki’s father. Nyktos bitterly points out that death follows the sisters wherever they go. And at this, Elisa remarks that she’s had a similar fate as well and it’s all the more reason for them to travel together. The party shares their drinks and sorrows before resting for the night. 

  

The next day, the party is approached by two men who look rather similar to one another. The men comment that “Lady Wachter wishes to meet them Eand so they follow the Wachter brothers to their home. They are met by the sight of a house that hates itself. Following in, they come face to face with Lady Wachter and four glasses of high quality wine. Rook drinks the wine, noting its exceptional make as they all sit down to listen to the Lady, with Elisa and Ilenti sitting more defiantly than others. Lady Wachter questions the party’s intentions behind their obvious defiance of Banecroft and is assumed as the party replies with a slew of insults for the vampire. She entrusts the party with the investigation of Jovlin Vallakovich and his occasional night time escapades to the statues of Dahlia and Delilah. The party accepts the quest and then promptly leaves the Lady’s house. 

  

Upon leaving the house, a raven approaches the party with a note tied to its leg. Ilenti opens the note to see a letter from Urwin Martikov informing them of Bluto’s departure with yet another potential kidnapped child. The party rushes to the lake, just in time to see Bluto already in the middle of the lake. Ilenti and Nyktos appear first, dimension dooring right in front of the drunkard whilst Elisa, Zipzap, and Rook fly their way there. Ilenti manages to knock the sack out of Bluto’s hands and upon opening it finds Ellie, the young bard they had met before. Ellie comments that she had willingly allowed Bluto to sacrifice her in hopes of convincing him that it doesn’t work, thereby preventing him from kidnapping other children, though the party had interfered with her plans. From high above, Elisa shouts out to the party, warning them about something underneath the waters. The party aren’t sure what to do exactly but as Bluto mutters about needing fish to trade for booze, Ilenti offers Bluto an unlimited supply of booze in exchange for his fishing pole. Bluto agrees and does the ritual, completing Ilenti’s first hag deal. As Ilenti awkwardly waits for a couple of seconds before a clinking of bottles can be heard from Bluto’s bag, the man finds his unlimited supply and begins to drink merrily before the party follows him back to shore. They then embark on their own row boat this time and as they traverse through the lake, Nyktos hears noises from underneath and as she glances over the edge of the boat sees a giant eye before they are attacked by a sea serpent of some kind. Battle ensues and the creature gets blasted with ice before turning to stone from Zipzap’s magic, sinking to the bottom of the ocean. Ellie comments that it was a pity as she would have liked to cut the creature open but nevertheless, the party make their way to the other end of the shore where it is rumoured that a mad mage resides. Bringing them to shore, Ellie comments that she is somewhat of a romantic herself and will be taking a stroll around the lake before moving on. As a parting gift, she gives the party a folding boat, telling them the command word as well before boating off. 

  

The party continues on and are quickly met with a giant elk. The party fights the giant elk only for it to turn into a blonde haired girl with blue eyes. Elisa recognises Holly, commenting that she’s a friend before Holly disappears in a flash of magic. The party follows Holly up the hill, with Elisa, Zipzap, and Rook flying up, only to be met with a spell of reverse gravity that shunts them back down. Nyktos dispels the magic and they move on, entering a cave. They dispel and disable more traps before eventually coming face to face with Holly. Holly easily brings down most of the party, and Elisa, through the pain of her injuries casts greater restoration on Holly, succeeding. However, Elisa could tell that though Holly’s mind had been fixed, something was still wrong. She could feel her psionics activating, allowing her to bring the party and her into Holly’s mind. The party finds themselves in the residential levels of the Zaratan, awoken by Holly and her friends, Gwen and Sulire. Holly invites the party to her house, and as they travel there, the party begins to hear and see stuff. Nyktos hears faint mummers of a woman’s voice, Ilenti sees flashes of a mahogany door. As they approach Holly’s room, the party sees a closet with the same mahogany door Ilenti had seen. Holly offers the party soda, introducing most of them to the bubbly sweet drink. She and her friends ask the party what adventuring is like, lamenting on how boring life in the Zaratan was. The party, much to Holly’s dismay, speak truthfully about the adventuring life, how dangerous it can be and how peacefulness is sometimes nice. Choosing to disregard that, Holly invites the party along to their trip, where they intend to sneakily follow the expedition in hopes of getting some magical trinkets. The girls sneak out the window first, leaving the party alone in Holly’s room. Rook hears a woman’s voice calling him to open the door and more voices, “This isn’t real E  E..don’t remember E “remind E Ilenti tries opening the door, and upon taking damage sees the chains that surround the door, the rest of the party do the same and regardless of what they try, nothing seems to work. Eventually with Holly rushing them, the party has no choice but to follow the three girls and see how things pan out. They reach the intestines of the Zaratan and as the expedition group harvest white blood cell Oozes appear through the walls, attacking the people. The party swiftly executes the Oozes, saving the expedition group from death. One of the adults questions why Holly is there but eventually allows the girls to pick up one item for the party’s help. They bring the demonic book back to Holly’s room, where the girls eagerly read the information. Then, when the party tries to open the closet door once again, Holly appears to stop them, telling them that there’s nothing inside and no reason to open the door, that only she could anyway. The party try their hand to convince Holly, stating that part of being an adventurer was to be brave and face one’s fears, that she wouldn’t be doing it alone but with friends. Eventually, with Elisa’s hand on hers, Holly opens the door. The party finds themselves watching several of Holly’s memories. Of the times she and her friends try to summon demons, happy to finally be free of the boring, mundane life in Zaratan. Then as their summoning goes on, the door opens with Goetia, Fool, and Sylvaerys emerging. Sylvaerys tries to stop the ritual by shooting at one of the girls. They teleport them all to an academy, where their friends start attacking Holly and her friends. But despite their efforts, the ritual finishes and Tasha is summoned. In her anger at seeing her friends die, Holly asks Tashsa for power. The next scene they see is Holly finding out that her home was destroyed as she gives an anguished cry. They then see an undead orc demon, one that Nyktos finds familiar, tormenting Holly as the same three false heroes open the door, witness her suffering and promptly closes it, leaving her alone. Then back in the manor they see Banecroft but maddened with tentacles attacking as Houzuki leads the villagers to retreat from battle. The last sight they see is Holly taking on Banecroft herself. As the visions end, the party find themselves back in the cave with Holly who’s well this time. Elisa reunites with Holly, and gets to know the party as well. The party updates Holly on the current situation and she agrees to meet them at Kislev, keeping a low profile to prevent Banecroft from knowing that she’s back. 

  

The party agrees and heads back to Vallaki to spy on the Baron. They sneak their way past the guards. Rook, not needing to breathe, hides in the bag of holding whilst the rest hide in bushes and trees. They manage to hide from Jovlin and Izek, and the necklace on the Baron’s neck shines a green glow, activating some form of magic. As the Baron does what he does, Nyktos has the misfortune of hearing pieces of his utterings to the statues. Elisa wildshapes into a spider and approaches the statues, allowing her to see more than she’d liked, she then rushes to the party, choosing to  dig and hide in a pocket. Jovlin then leaves, where Nyktos happens to witness the residue left behind. The Cleric and Druid rush back to the inn, all too eager to drown themselves in alcohol to forget what they had witnessed. From contextual clues and their disgust, the rest of the party figure out what happened as well. Rook procures liquor and a keg of alcohol for the party whilst Zipzap goes on about the many ways with which they should hurt the Baron and chop off his bits, which does earn a laugh from the party. The party, with half of their members drunk, make their way to Lady Wachter’s house once more, half seeking out wine and half to deliver her the information. Elisa and Nyktos drink more wine as Ilenti delivers the information to the Lady. Lady Wachter explains that they would need the help of the witches of the bonegrinder, or “Blood Bitches Eas an intoxicated Nyktos so kindly put it. They’re sent on their way, with Lady Wachter handing the payment to the witches to Ilenti. The party returns to the inn where they spend the rest of the night drinking. The next day, they set out to the bonegrinder windmill where Morgantha is not happy to see both Elisa and Rook since her last run in with them. Ilenti and Nyktos explain the deal they wish to make and the witches request more in terms of payments, promising a reward if the party helps them out with their Undead issue. The party agrees and along the way saves a dire wolf from being attacked by undead. They befriend the wolf and it leads them to the source of the undead where they meet a headless knight by the name of Maximillus. The knight calls them names and believes them to be of ill intent thus attacking the party to protect his friend Jack. However, Rook shows his true Celestial form and the knight calms down, now understanding that they are good people. The party enters the ritual room to see Jack, a 60 year old man holding a stone figure of an orc with three severed heads in his hand that Nyktos once again finds oddly familiar. Rook uses his divine sense to know that the figure is desecrated and that it would protect him from being turned. The party learns of Jack’s life, how he once had someone important to him, Merna, who knew Agatha and Broomhilda, but she had since long passed. In his despair, all he could do was pray to the figurine and create the mass of undead to fight against Banecroft. The party talks him out of it, explaining that the undead could do more harm than good if control over them was lost. And as the party is about to bring Jack out of the place, the door swings open. A female figure throws the knight on the ground, stalking into the room uttering a proverb, “A warrior cast down from lances Epeak, her blade waits amongst ruined walls. EThe party knows this voice. It’s Houzuki, as she brandishes her magic and weapons against the party, now hellbent on killing them. She goes for Jack, trying to kill him but the party won’t let her. Though low levelled magics seemed to have no effect on her. The party tries to run but Ilenti ends up being charmed by Houzuki, unable to leave. The party rushes back in, beating the crap out of Houzuki and as Rook deals what should have been the finishing blow, Houzuki melts and transforms into something else. The party put all their efforts in attacking this new form of Houzuki, but even then she doesn’t die, only melting into a pile of goop before disappearing. The party lets Jack out of the bag, and agrees to send him to hide his existence, only letting Broomhilda know and trusting that she can keep him safe. From Jack, the party learns about Agatha and her deep love for her daughter, how Agatha had contracted Sylvaerys and her party to create a heartstone for Broomhilda, protecting her from disease. The party drop Jack off before setting out to the Bonegrinder once more, now that the undead are dealt with.
