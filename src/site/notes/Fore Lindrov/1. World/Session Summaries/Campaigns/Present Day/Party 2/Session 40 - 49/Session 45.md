As the party approaches the gates of the Amber Temple, ravenous Ropers reach out, attacking. Just then, Rook falls to the ground, seemingly weaker than before as Ilenti’s shadow arm dissipates and she points at the statue before fainting once more. With no time to process what’s happened, the party launches into combat. The Ropers aim for the weakened party members, successfully grabbing onto Ilenti when a loud caw is heard from the sky before a metal man lands before the party and his metallic oxpecker companion rests on Nyktos Ehead. The armoured figure swiftly attacks the Roper, freeing Ilenti from its grasp. Elisa takes on her starry form for the first time and Milton appears, confused by the change but quickly adapting as he enters the constellation that shines behind Elisa, taking on an archer’s position. The party ward off the Ropers, protecting their unconscious party member. Though combat comes to a halt as Elisa speaks with the Ropers, learning that they only desire a meal. The party hands over their rations, satisfying the Ropers. Nyktos rushes to her sister’s side. She notices that Ilenti seems to be in a stasis once more, the second time since she returned from Banecroft. Having no cure for her current condition, Nyktos reluctantly stuffs her into the bag of holding until they can cure her. 

  

Rook then falls to his knees, what little magic he had left fades. What is left is the shell of the Celestial, his innate abilities withered away, a paladin no more. The Celestial echo transforms into a portal, one that leads to the astral plane, to where Telantes is. Rook steels himself, knowing his mission. He reaches out to the party, approaching Nyktos first as he hands her the bag of holding and Efreeti chain claiming that he has no use for it. Nyktos thanks Rook for all that he’s done; for being the first to promise to protect her sister, for dealing with Kas to save Crimson, for getting Ilenti back, and for being a good friend. Rook pulls Nyktos in for a hug. He then pulls out the book of fine wine, alluding to their shared enjoyment of alcohol, saying that it's so she’d know what fine wines to pick when he’s back. Nyktos pulls out the painting of D3TH TR4P, depicting a band with Nyktos, Ilenti, and Telantes. The painting that Rook had once requested for is now his, as Nyktos tells him to show Telantes the drawing, knowing that she’d love it. Trying to alleviate the sad atmosphere, Nyktos suggests a bet for the road, jesting that Rook would be poorer when he comes back whilst Rook takes the opposing side. The two share another hug, wishing each other well. Rook then speaks to Pyrite next. The hobgoblin expresses his regret in being unable to follow and help Rook as he once promised, but Rook understands, cracking a joke about finding Pyrite’s bones and hitting him should he dare die. Pyrite hands Rook his signet ring, one of the remaining items he had to remember his kingdom by. In return, Rook hands him the thigh bone of Saint Markovia and the raven’s slumber pendant that holds Wendel. Meanwhile, Elisa speaks to Vorun, warning him of the dangers he is about to face if he follows Rook. But the child seems to have grown since the start of his journey, knowing that he has to do what must be done and that at the very least, he would not abandon his mentor to face it all alone. The teen resolves, stating that he had to return to claim his name from Banecroft as well, suggesting that Elisa could give him back his name if he returns after they’ve defeated Banecroft. Elisa gives him some final warnings to not make any deals and be cautious of who they meet before handing him a Superior potion of healing which Vorun thanks her with a hug that Elisa awkwardly reciprocates. Rook then speaks with Elisa, saying that he’ll miss her as the druid cautions Rook to not make any more deals and that they don’t know what dangers lie in the astral plane. Rook affirms that he will be back to help Elisa fight the three assholes and Elisa expresses that Rook will have a lot of time before they do. As a parting gift, Rook gifts Elisa the sovereign glue and soul coin he had and Elisa hands him two bits of chalk to draw butts all over the astral plane. When Rook offers to return the figurine of wondrous power, Elisa rejects telling him to keep it before reminding him to take care of Vorun and to ensure that the kid doesn’t put himself in danger to save Rook. In return, Rook asserts that he would train Vorun to be better than he ever was. Zipzap adds on to the conversation, commanding Rook to help expand his influence to the astral plane, that any allies his minion makes are all his minions. Zipzap hands Rook 100gp, funding the trip. With that, Rook, Vorun, and Fido leave for the astral plane with promises to return home soon. 

  

With Rook leaving, the armoured man from before steps up, introducing himself as Tiny. The party dives into questions, wondering where Tiny is from and why he’s here to how his armour works and his face screen situation. They learn that Tiny recently awoke near the mountains and based off what he knows, Pyrite discerns Tiny to be someone from the past like he was. They soon move on to the gate, with Elisa sharing what she learnt from the Roper; that a ghost dressed in Selunite robes enters the statues. The party tries to speak with the ghost but to no avail and attempts to scale the wall are met with hostility as the statues begin to attack them. They successfully destroy one of the statues as the ghost is forced to show themselves. What the party sees is a familiar sight, Belmont but he seems different. As shadows surround his being, he has been made into an Eidolon, converted to protect the gate by Banecroft. Nyktos calls out to the party, informing Tiny and Pyrite that Belmont is a friend. They soon manage to take down Belmont, and remove the curse placed upon him. In that moment, Belmont comes back, recalling all of his memories from before he had been cursed by Lucian. As expected of a loyal Selunite, Belmont begins to ask about the Temple and of Rook’s whereabouts. Nyktos quells his worries about the temple, sharing that Doru and Donavich are safe and help maintain the place. As well as that they didn’t have to use the other Selune statuette, allowing its powers to protect the place still. When asked how that is, Nyktos doesn’t share anything, though the subtle glance at what used to be her holy symbol tells Belmont all that he needs to know as he thanks her once more. Bringing up the topic of Selune again, the party share what’s happened, how they had met Telantes and how she had died, sacrificing herself so that Nyktos could have her sister. But Belmont understands, the sacrifice Telantes had made was in line with what Selune stood for. He offers to follow the party and differing opinions are raised. Pyrite shares of the dangers that are up ahead, wanting others to make informed decisions but also understanding that they party needed all the help they could get whilst Elisa and Nyktos didn’t want Belmont to sacrifice himself again. In the end, they settle for Belmont following the party but staying out of trouble if the party faces combat. 

  

They scale the first gate, making their way past easily before reaching a guard tower and the second gate. As they approach the gate, they feel a strong gravitational pull that makes it almost impossible to pass. Elisa peeks into the guard tower, spotting a statue of Faraine and a stack of rocks, no doubt left by Arala. Exiting, she awkwardly informs Nyktos that her mother and sister have likely been here before. Tiny, not knowing better, rejoices at that, claiming it to be a good thing but halting as he notices Nyktos Egrim expression. The party enters the tower, reading Arala’s note and the riddle left behind. Pyrite solves the riddle, answering “madness Eas the chest opens to reveal a rock with a left hand symbol on it. Casting Identify, Nyktos learns that the rock can cast antimagic field and allow the party to bypass the gate without issue. Not wanting to waste any time, they do just that, clearing the second gate at eleven in the morning.  
  
As they pass, they begin to hear music, sounds of a melancholic violin playing, inspiring the party. They first assume it to be Ellie, figuring that the bard must have someone wandered to the mountains but upon approaching come face to face with a woman in a white dress with gold trim, golden accessories complimenting her dress as she plays the beautiful music. They call out to her and she introduces herself as Ludmilla, one of Banecroft’s wives before asking for their names. Nyktos introduces herself as Red, whilst Pyrite attempts to follow suit, calling himself “Stone Ethough it’s rather obvious that he’s lying.  
  
His lies are made further evident when Tiny pipes in that Pyrite had called himself by another name before, Elisa reluctantly introduces herself before Nyktos takes the chance to switch the topic to Ludmilla’s music, genuinely praising it. This helps Ludmilla open up as she shares about her life and how Banecroft had saved her after she lost her family, how he taught her to play the violin and showed her so much love. The party has mixed feelings about the situation, understanding that Ludmilla is a victim of circumstance but that her love for Banecroft is too deep to be cured in an instant. Pyrite speaks with Ludmilla, learning more about the wives of Banecroft and of the bridge as he notices the strong magic that surrounds the bridge.  
  
From Ludmilla, the party learns about Anastrasia, the first wife of Banecroft’s and her views on how to best help Banecroft, as well as Volenta, the youngest wife who was raised in a cult as a sacrifice before Banecroft saved her and as a result is very attached to him. Pyrite tries to encourage Ludmilla to guide them across the bridge but she refuses, changing the topic to Nyktos Emusic. Nyktos plays a tune which Ludmilla easily plays along with, Tiny joins in as well, pulling out the pan flute he received from his friend. At the sound of the new instrument both Ludmilla and Nyktos adapt, changing their tunes to support that of Tiny’s allowing him to feel like the main star of the performance. But the party is running out of time and as Tiny distracts Ludmilla, Nyktos and Elisa exchange ideas on how to deal with the situation. Elisa attempts to charm Ludmilla, but it fails and combat ensues with Ludmilla running off the bridge only to be picked up by her pet Roc, Luna. The party tries their best to fight, holding off the Roc as Elisa and Zipzap attack Ludmilla with spells. For the most part, the party are unable to see Ludmilla thanks to Nyktos Esleet storm, protecting themselves from some of the harm Ludmilla could otherwise cause them. However, the Roc Luna throws Elisa off the bridge and Pyrite jumps off the bridge, following without hesitation as he grabs onto the Roc, attacking it mercilessly. At Tiny’s request, Nyktos drops the sleet storm and for the first time since combat began the party sees Ludmilla’s expression, now wrought with fear. Luna flies back to Ludmilla’s side, as the vampire stays near her pet, desperate to protect it despite the harm it causes her. Pyrite attempts to talk Ludmilla down, wanting her to give up, but she doesn’t, insisting that she loves Banecroft. But the Paladin raises a fair argument, asking Ludmilla who she thinks killed her entire village before saving her. Unfortunately the vampire is too blinded by love to see logic, standing her ground as she attempts to protect Luna. Seeing this madness, Tiny flies up to Pyrite, attacking him and attempting to stop him but to avail. As Pyrite speaks to Ludmilla once more, she begins to make sense of the words, coming to a conclusion of her own as she now understands what her masterpiece was missing; Sacrifice.

  

She begins to play, using both her and Luna’s souls to fuel the piece. This is evident to the party as they become aware that the moment the piece ends, both Luna and Ludmilla die. They try their best to stop it somehow and ultimately a hit from Tiny causes the music to stop. Tiny catches Ludmilla, bringing her back to the bridge where it is clear that she’s dead. Though Tiny doesn’t believe as he attempts to wake her up before the party has to break the truth to him. Pyrite casts gentle repose on Ludmilla’s corpse and Elisa helps to bury the body. Sheets of music fall and Nyktos picks them up, referencing them as she plays a sad tune whilst they lay Ludmilla to rest. With that, the party travels on. 

  

They come across a tall mountain and begin to scale it with Elisa turning into a brown bear, Nyktos holding onto her, Tiny flying up the mountain with his boots and Pyrite, Zipzap, and Crimson in the bag of holding. 6000ft up, they begin their break when Nyktos Egrip loosens due to the cold and she begins to fall. Tiny immediately reaches out, trying to catch the shadar kai but to no avail. Tiny proceeds to fall after Nyktos, still hoping to save her. In a bid to save herself, Nyktos pulls out Kaze, trying to use the blade to slow her fall, though that fails and even her divine intervention falls through as well and she’s quickly approaching the ground.  
  
Realising that she has no choice but to fall, Nyktos casts deathward on herself and tries to brace for the landing. Elisa drops her wildshape, throwing her staff of the woodlands down, hoping that it would sprout into a tree to catch her falling team members before wildshaping into a bird and flying down. Nyktos lands first, knocking the wind out of her lungs as her spell protects her from death. Elisa lands next, hopping over to pick up her staff when a loud thud is heard. They both turn to the direction of the sound, witnessing a man sized hole in the ground. Making their way over, they see Tiny, dead. The two are stunned, unsure what to do, or how to help him. Tiny sees a diamond symbol, and a lady with blue skin and frost before him. She cries out for him to free her before reviving Tiny. As he shoots back to life, Elisa and Nyktos are shocked and confused. He shares the vision he saw and Pyrite notices the mark on Tiny’s back. Zephyros takes a look at the mark, recognising it to be the symbol of the Goddess of Winter and Cold, Auril. The party surmises that the goddess might be trapped in the Amber Temple and hence saved Tiny so that they would save her. Now back at the bottom of the mountain once more, the party comes up with a new plan, using Marina’s godshard to travel up the mountain without issue this time. They reach the top at about 1230, continuing forth and navigating before they come across a Yeti that Elisa quickly turns into a turtle, burying it a bit in the snow before Nyktos calls out about the approaching avalanche and they begin running. Elisa finds little to no issue in the snow, running past everyone but maintaining close enough to help if needed. Pyrite clears the snow with no issue, swiftly jumping over pits. Tiny reaches out to save Nyktos once more, dragging her away from the avalanche. Nyktos casts haste on herself, dashing to the front of the party. As a giant snowball rolls towards her, she pulls out Kaze, slicing the snowball akin to a skilled swordswoman. Pyrite, in this efforts to save himself from the avalanche, falls into a pit. Though Elisa reacts quickly and helps pull him out of the pit with telekinesis, saving him from the avalanche. The party successfully escapes from the avalanche, travelling forth til seven thirty that evening when they come across something they haven’t seen in ages: dirt. 

  

They rush towards it, with Pyrite picking up rocks and Elisa grabbing handfuls of dirt. They cheer before continuing on to the Amber Temple. The party soon finds themselves in a discussion with the Yugoloths, Charon and the two Aracanaloths. Elisa offers up the cure to mindflayer thralldom. Pyrite offers up the Flametongue greatsword, 40 gp, a scroll of holy weapon. Nyktos offers up 3 diamonds worth 500 gp each and a spell scroll of harm before Pyrite and Nyktos realise that they’re making it too easy for the Yugoloths to continue taking more. Additionally, the party offers up an appropriate amount of what they find within the Amber Temple. The Yugoloths agree to the deal and Charon guides them across the lake of lava. They’re then dropped off before where there are 5 statues. Pyrite looks at the broken statue, noting that it looks like Sangzor. The other stautes resemble Auril, the Frostmaiden, a (insert description of statue 3), Demogorgon, and Tenebrous. Beside the large door is a stack of stones and another note from Arala giving one final word to her sisters. Nyktos prays at the stones, informing Arala that she’s here too. During the prayers she sees a vision of people wielding katanas, white feathers, and an angry man chained up and bleeding. Despite the late hour, the party has to press on, knowing that they have little time left before the siege begins.
