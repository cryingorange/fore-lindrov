In the church, Mary is escorted to one of the guest rooms to rest. Priest Belmont then thanks the party for their help in bringing Mary back and to connect him to his goddess Selûne. Rook prays before the statuette and statue of Selûne. During which Nyktos hears soft wails from the south eastern room of the church. After, Priest Belmont picks up the statuette of Selûne, which Nyktos recognises is a (+1) amulet of the devout. Belmont shares that a party had acquired the statue for him while Lucian still ruled Nightfell. He laments to himself something about Selûne “would will it E However, the cries from the southeastern room grow louder, the screams and pleas eventually cause Nyktos to head towards the room as Belmont suggests otherwise. The cleric questions Belmont, asking him who was inside and that she could hear them. Belmont relents, bringing the party down to a cellar like place and before he could explain further, two vampires jump out of the shadows and attack Ilenti and Nyktos, successfully grappling them as they launch for the siblings Enecks. During combat, Rook attempts to cure one of the vampire’s of their disease with his lay on hands though it seems to be more than a mere disease as the magic does nothing to stop the vampire. 

The party makes swift work of the vampires with Zipzap turning the two men, Doru and Donavich to stone, where he is very eager to suggest knocking them over to smash the statues to bits. The party does not and Belmont explains that the two men were cursed after their excursion to Victor’s castle. Doru was a guard from Vallaki while Donavich was a settler here in the village, they were both approached by a blonde haired, green eyed mage to assault Victor’s castle. Saying that Donavich loved nothing in this world but his son, and likely went along to try to keep the young boy safe. He further explains that the only way to cure the two of this pseudo-vampirism was to use the magic in an amulet of the devout, one for each man. Though he continues that the church will fall as soon as their last Selûne statuette loses all of its magic. He tells the party that there is another statuette to the north east and tasks them to bring it back. They are about to leave as an oddity sticks out to Nyktos, she notices a rat in the basement of the church, despite not having seen any in Fore Lindrov. Elisa uses speak with animals to communicate to the rat, bribing it with goodberries as it explains the basis of the spy network in Barovia. The rat also shares that Bunny is a nice individual, who enjoys playing with the mice and does not like to hurt. Elisa suggests that the rat betray Victor by not telling on their appearance though the rat immediately turns the idea down, seemingly too afraid to do so. With that, the party heads out on their mission to collect the remaining statuette. 

Out in the streets of the village of Barovia, the party comes across a middle aged woman with big curly hair, dressed in drab robes. Just as they see her, Nyktos hears a familiar voice in her head urging her to kill the woman, that she is a traitor who should be brutally slain. Nyktos pulls out her dagger, walking towards the lady as Ilenti and Rook hold her back. Ilenti questions her sister, only to receive slightly confused answers as Nyktos replies that the woman is a traitor and has betrayed her somehow and shares her immense desire to kill her. She continues forth and Rook disarms her, knocking her dagger out of her hands but it doesn't affect the cleric as she shoots a spell right at the woman. During this all, Elisa walks up to the woman speaking to her to distract her from the situation. The lady explains that she was if she sells meat pies that she and her sister make, asking if Elisa will buy some, she does. Zipzap consumes their newly bought pies and Zipzap ends up incapacitated in a dream. With the party failing to hold Nyktos back, the spell hits the lady and Elisa immediately uses her telekinesis on Nyktos, pushing the cleric behind some houses. Ilenti follows her sister wanting to calm her down. Having been hit by a spell, the pie seller turns around angry as she sees Elisa and Rook. She accuses them of attacking her which the two vehemently deny, instead lying that her pies were delicious. The lady forces them to buy more and eat the pies right there. The two comply, whilst Nyktos and by extension Ilenti are led further away with Elisa’s spell, the former screaming to be let go. Once far enough, Nyktos knows that it is a lost cause and relents. Ilenti speaks with her sister, telling her that her actions, her desire to kill isn’t like Nyktos, that she isn’t a monster like Ilenti is. The sudden change in tone brings Nyktos back to her senses and the two regroup with the rest. As they head back, Rook and Elisa heal Zipzap from his incapacitated state, where the beholder shared that he was having a great dream, with him and Elisa ruling the world as kings, the siblings as their administrators and Rook as his errand boy. When they return, Rook questions Nyktos, casually stating that it was very out of character of her to do so, that it was more of a thing Ilenti would do. Nyktos rebukes Rook’s words, telling him not to say such things, as the paladin apologises for his words.

With that, the party searches for a place to rest for the night, ending up at an inn called “Bildrif’s Mercantile and Inn E the party enter and upon speaking with the innkeeper soon realise that it is a place that severely overcharges for everything. Eventually the party is sort of kicked out of the place seeing as they asked too many questions and we're not happy with the prices. The party heads back to the Burgomaster’s mansion to inform them of the delay in their travel plans as they explained that they have a place that requires hallowing. Hayden is obviously unhappy with the delay and is somewhat angry at the party for not fully understanding the situation. At the party request, Hayden has no choice but to explain as the party is shown the marks on Ireena’s back, the same symbol that they had seen on Hozuki’s neck now covering her back as well. They learned that the mark has something to do with Banecroft’s weird attraction towards Ireena and why the wolves keep attacking her. Nyktos attempts to identify the mark and is instead thrown back, her mind filled with screeches of simian breast cries and she hears the words “Child of Chaos E similar to when Rook had tried to remove the curse on Hozuki. 

The party takes their rest and the following day head to the desolate church of Selûne, where right at the door they see a dead acolyte with his body torn in half and his guts exposed. Investigating the scene, Elisa notices that the body was not sliced in half but instead bludgeoned in half and finds footprints of a much bigger creature that heads into the church. Nyktos notices that the body is only a day old. Ilenti and Rook both try to pull the door open as they feel like something is stuck within it. Eventually the door opens and the other half of the acolyte falls out. Elisa and Nyktos both notice signs of someone much larger, pointing it out to the party. The party goes in and are greeted with the sight of a massacre, dozens of dead acolytes. Upon attempting their investigations of bodies and statues and whatnot, the dead bodies of the acolytes literature start to shake and rise as weird winged insect-like creatures pop out of the bodies launching the party into combat. During which the insect-like creatures are able to manipulate the dead bodies of the acolytes summoning other fiends. Eventually all of the Chasme and their summoned fiends are killed and the party heads into the library of the church. They managed to avoid all the traps with Ilenti disarming a couple and gaining some grenades. Scouring the books, Elisa, Zipzap, and Nyktos learn about a Celestial that wields a mace called Sharran. As the party heads in ready to face the Celestial, they instead come across Basatur, the Orthon that they had fought once before a few weeks ago. He explains that he came here as he heard the cries of a Demon, a powerful one, as well as to strip the place of any artifacts and kill any Demons he finds. He comments that the entirety of Barovia smells like a layer of the Abyss. He speaks of the eventual battle he will have with the party, and that it will be one to true death. The party states that they have similar intentions and the Orthon proposes an alliance of sorts, as he has incentive from the Duke, who previously had come to Nightfell before to destroy the realm, as it reeks of Abyssal energies. He allows the party to do as they wish and have the artefact for themselves before disappearing. The party heads in and sees Sharran, once a Celestial connected to the deity Selûne, now instead his wings torn off, blood covering his body. He prays crazily to the statuette of Selûne and cries in madness as he hears no response. As he turns around, the party notices that his eyes are weird, each eye doubled. Sharran responds crazed to the party’s arrival, claiming that they too were here to take Selûne from him like the acolytes did. He sees Rook, a fellow Celestial and beckons his brother to join him, to help him. But as Rook tries to speak, Sharran realises that he is alone and attacks the party. Left with no way to fix Sharran’s crazed mind, the party had no choice but to fight him and Rook deals the final blow, cutting down his body. What they did not expect was for Sharran to transform as heads burst out. Before the party, in Sharran’s place now stood a Hydra. Elisa uses her telekinesis to restrain the Hydra, aiding the party greatly. Continuing with battle, the party tries their best but the heads just keep growing back faster than they are able to cut them off. And Elisa’s telekinesis, though great, cannot hold the Hydra back forever as the party continues to run out of resources. With a suggestion from Ilenti, Elisa drops the bag of holding and retreats to where the Hydra can’t see her, maintaining her control of the Hydra. Rook dumps out all of the gunpowder in the bag of holding and Ilenti strums her heart causing a siege of firestorm that explodes all of the gunpowder, severely damaging the Hydra. As the dust dissipates, the party sees four heads remaining. They try their best to cut the remainder off but it is clear that they can't and the structure of the church begins to shake; a cave in is imminent. They all start to run out as rocks fall from the ceiling. Zipzap, Crimson, and Nyktos are trapped under the rocks, unable to escape. The situation does not seem ideal as Ilenti might have to choose who to save. Fortunately however, Crimson manages to break free of the rocks and runs to safety. Rook commands his horse in, picking up Crimson as they escape. Meanwhile, Ilenti escapes with Zipzap, teleporting to safety. Just as the situation seems really dire, Elisa uses her powers to lift the rocks off, freeing the shadar kai as they all escape by the skin of their teeth.

The party heads back to the church and back underground and realise that it is quiet. There are three bodies slumped over chairs, the situation looks dark. Using his divine sense, Rook knows that there are four undead in the room, with one right behind them. Victor Banecroft makes his presence known, holding the statuette of Selûne in his hand. But there's something very different about him, his demeanour is different from last time, his eyes crazed and doubled like that of Sharran’s. He looks at the sisters, calling them the children of chaos, a new moniker that they share with Ireena. He offers a ring to Rook and tells him that the ring has many purposes, alluding that it would help him hide his wings. With Rook’s acceptance, Banecroft reveals his true intentions for being here. He has the party choose between saving the father and son, curing them both of their curse but damning Belmont to this death, or to save Belmont and one of the two cursed men, leaving the other to remain forever cursed with vampirism. The party approaches the three men, the son, Doru, begs to be fed, his bloodlust uncontrollable. The father, Danovich, pleads for his son to be saved, not caring for his own life. And lastly, Belmont, true to his good nature, tells the party to kill him. That it would be his Goddess Ewill, in Selûne’s will that he should die to save them. He continues on, sharing that he's lived a very long life since he was cursed by Lucian with immortality. With the other statuette in Victor's hand, the party is not left with much of a choice. During this all Victor encourages them, telling them to show him the name they have made for themselves, to unleash their potential. Ilenti and Nyktos look at Victor, confused and concerned as to why he knows the exact wordings of their promises made past. Nyktos approaches Elisa, suggesting that Elisa should be the one to do it. At least then, Belmont would be able to go where he's supposed to go. Agreeing with the plan, Elisa approaches Belmont and asks him about his life but Belmont insists that there is no need to remember him, that he would be thankful if the party could keep the church up for as long as they can. Especially since he knows that it would soon fall without the magic of the statuette. He does however share that he once had a woman that he loved but doesn't share her name. He looks at Rook and trusts that he would carry out Selûne’s intentions. Nyktos approaches Banecroft, her hand stretched out for the statuette as she relents that the priest will die. Banecroft begins to laugh, proud and pleased with himself for having made the party suffer. He hands the statuette over and watches as Elisa puts her hand over Belmont’s chest. As she does, Belmont begins a prayer, as best as he can come up with given the situation. As her last words to him, Elisa prays for Selûne’s light to lead him to where he should be. Her eldritch blast shoots through him, killing the good priest once and for all. Banecroft is pleased with the priest’s death and laughs before disappearing. However, Elisa could sense that Belmont’s soul is confused, that there really isn't a realm where Selûne’s followers could go. Elisa is left with a couple of choices, to send Belmont to her God, to send him to the closest figure related to Selûne, to send him to Selûne’s sister, or to send them to the closest God-like figure in Barovia. Wanting to stay true to her word, Elisa picks the closest figure related to Selûne and feels as Belmont’s soul is redirected to Rook. As Belmont’s soul connects with Rook’s, the paladin’s eyes glow with a newfound radiant energy. With Belmont dead, Ilenti leaves the room.

With the Selûne statuette in hand, Nyktos approaches the father and son. Speaking at them, she expresses that Belmont died for them and that the least they could do was to honour his death by helping out at the church. Donavich asks for his son to be cured first which Nyktos does using the first statuette. She then approaches the father to cure him. Instead of pulling out the other statuette, Nyktos pulls out her own amulet of the devout, an amulet made of a pink gem in the centre and vines that hold it in place. Without a second's hesitation, she uses the magic within it to cure Donavich of his curse. The magic seeps out of the item; its once magical glow now gone. The remaining party speaks to the father and son, asking them about the Mage that they had travelled with. They learn that the Mage was about 16, dressed in dark robes with a purple trim and has blonde hair. Elisa seems to know the Mage, asking more questions to confirm her identity. However, they say that she was killed by Banecroft. Her body left where the mansion was at the time. Elisa thinks that her friend might have died, though the party tries to suggest that she might have gotten away. With that, Nyktos leaves, placing the remaining statuette of Selûne, still filled with magical divine power, back on the altar where the previous one stood, protecting the church from any ruin. Nyktos then goes around the church, collecting the incense, herbs, and oils that she would need to hallow the Death House. 

Exiting the church, the party comes across the pie seller once more. This time her bag has something, someone. Nyktos asks for Elisa’s help and using her mind reading powers Elisa realises that inside the bag is a child. And the moment she shares that with the sisters, they both respond violently. Nyktos uses her blood magic, trying to control the woman as she feels the woman try to do the same. Ilenti attacks the woman, incapacitating her with just one spell. They all go up to her, questioning her what is in her bag and demanding that she free the child which the woman does. Out of the bag now is a young boy, named Wyll, who shared that he was sold by his parents. The party brings Wyll back to his house and his parents greet him happily, relieved that their child was brought back. Upon questioning, the party learns that the pie seller’s name is Morgantha, that she is a blood witch who sells the pies that give the consumer very nice dreams, dreams that would take them away from the pain of Barovia. The party also learns of the debt that Wyll’s parents owe Morgantha, they offer to settle the debt, so long as the parents promise to no longer go into debt, or risk their child for some pies. The party approaches Morgantha who was lurking behind them and pays off the debt, telling her to leave the people alone. Meanwhile Ilenti reaches out to Aunty Greta and asks how she is able to recreate similar pies. When the party is done with Morgantha, Aunty Greta shows up along with Aerith, providing the party with much needed information. She shares that Morgantha comes from a blood coven and that the children that they take are sent away. She says that the blood witches serve another master, that following Morgantha is not the right path and that the party should instead seek a mother's love and a woman named Broomhilda. As well as that Ilenti should look for signs in blood and guts. As for how to create a dream pie, Aunty Greta says that it is easy enough to do without the bones of children, a fact that makes Rook extremely uncomfortable knowing that he ate children. Aunty Greta says that they would need a raven's eye, a rotten tree’s sap, and a hag’s eye. With that Aunty Greta and Aerith leave. The party chases Morgantha down to ask her a couple of questions. They try to inquire about a mother's love and surprisingly Morgantha responds almost terrified. She warns the party not to speak that aloud should they not want to die. Morgantha shares that to seek a mother's love is to garner protection from Lord Banecroft. With that, the party has no more questions though Ilenti struggles, unable to decide if she wants to kill Morgantha. Nyktos realises this and stops Ilenti not wanting Ilenti to become the monster that sees herself to be. Nyktos rushes Morgantha away, who comments that the Lord of Blood only takes before leaving. Nyktos gives Ilenti a hug telling her that she did good and that everything is fine. This brings the Ilenti back for now. 

The party spends the night doing what they need to and Nyktos hands Crimson over to Ilenti to take care of him for the night. Though the hellhound and the displacer kitten do not get along. The party does note however that Alastair and Beatrice seem to be closer now, more like equals. Alastair greets them, hoping that they had as good of company as he did. Noticeably, his words cause Beatrice to blush and fumble. Nyktos spends the next 24 hours in the basement of the Death House as she uses her own holy symbol for the first time in a while to hallow the place, preventing the entry of Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, and Undead. With that all done she returns to rest for the night but before leaving, Nyktos casts death ward on Ireena, telling her that it is a protection like spell instead.

The next day the party sets out and comes across gallows with blank gravestones of the people hung there. They come across a windmill and the most confusing of all things; running water. Hayden sees the confusion and shock of the party, explaining that this was also Banecroft’s doing, that he was somehow able to change the land from what it was Alastair confirms this, stating that it was different before. His comment raises an eyebrow from Hayden. Regardless, they continue on and see a bear hunkering over the small figure of a man. Ireena, who was driving the cart, immediately cracks the whips and rushes forward. In doing so, Ilenti, Elisa, and Zipzap fall off the cart. Ireena takes a piece of metal and throws it at the bear, drawing its attention away from the man. Rook rushes forward into battle, moving standing in front so as to draw attention away from Ireena and the civilians. Ireena somehow manages to teleport, mystically shadow stepping right before the injured man. Everyone and herself are surprised by her new powers but she immediately pulls out a medical kit to heal the man. 

Rook pulls out a torch trying to intimidate the bear and the wolves to leave them alone. It works initially, though the wolves lie in wait to pick out any individuals left alone. In a further attempt to protect Ireena, Nyktos teleports to her side and casts spirit guardians. She does so using her own holy symbol this time, her magic different. Where once the blood had sparkles of pink that made it both mystical yet beautiful, now in its place was just blood, dark and angry-like. She tells Ireena to stay by her side, hoping that at least that way Ireena could stay out of trouble. Though the bear progresses forward attacking Crimson and the civilian as Ireena interposes herself, taking the brunt of the damage herself. This triggers the death ward placed on her. Battles ensues and eventually Rook strikes down the frenzied bear. As he does, the figure morphs, the hair sinking back into skin as the bear turns into a man. 

A Werebear as Elisa knows from her previous travels. The man they saved introduces himself as Gordon from Vallaki as he begs for them to save his wife, Danica, saying that they were attacked by these random bears who had taken his wife away. Of course the party agrees much to the chagrin of Hayden who clearly does not approve any more detours. Remembering Aunty Greta's words, Ilenti begins to drag the body of the Werebear away. This sparks the curiosity of Ireena and Ilenti lies about bringing the man away to bury his body. Yet somehow it works and they pull the Werebear into the woods.

Ilenti proceeds to cut the man open, harvesting his organs as she realises that she could cast augury and figure out the direction of the Werebear’s camp using his organs. Elisa soon shows up realising her urge to cast detect thoughts she does so but it's not enough for her to see where the sentiment is. The voice in her mind speaks, telling her to consume the brain. And though unwilling, she proceeds to carve out the man's brain and cuts a sliver of it. From his position in the sky, Rook sees the confusing sight and lands down to question his party members. He proceeds to be extremely weirded out as Elisa licks the brain, and as that does nothing she eats the piece that she cut out. Witnessing this causes Rook to puke, the whole scene is made further weird with the fact that Ilenti is holding on to the man's organs as well. Despite all of that, the party has a direction to head to and does bury the Werebear’s body before they leave.
