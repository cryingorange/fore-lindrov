# Getting In

## Guh Cries

The party’s rest is interrupted by Guh’s cries. Elisa and Ivy wake up to investigate but as Elisa is unable to enter the space, they have Ivy head in to check first and then as a party approach Thonka and Hruk who are guarding the gates but ignoring Guh’s cries.

## Hruk's Game

Thonka recognises Elisa, calling her a trickster who gave him a dog that disappeared. Causing the two Hill Giants to be apprehensive about trusting strangers after a horned person warned them against doing so.

Their trust is easily gained through some games as Hruk suggests playing skullbashing. Grimrid is made to play the first round, immediately being knocked unconscious in the first round as Hruk’s appetite for fun has yet to be satiated. Roast volunteers to play as well, surviving the first round well before knocking Hruk out on the second round, joining him as well

## Thonka's Game

Thonka also wants to play a game, but not skullbashing but Goblin hucking. In the absence of a Goblin, he picks up Grimrid. Ivy and Elisa convince him to throw a bush instead, claiming that it is the same colour as a goblin and moves, therefore proving that plants are alive. Thonka agrees, throwing the bush at a rock, decimating the bush. Thonka is proud of his achievements, urging Ivy to take her turn which she does, throwing the bush far as Elisa moves the rock, with them proclaiming that the rock was shifted by the sheer force of Ivy’s throw. 

Thonka then suggests throwing Grimrid, which the party can no longer stop but instead they try their best to protect Grimrid from any harm, catching him with Telekinesis as he continues his slumber. Thonka then points at Roast, telling Ivy to throw her. Ivy wakes Roast up, informing her of the situation as Roast agrees to participate in the charade, dimension dooring to the rock as Elisa disintegrates it. Winning the game, the party are allowed entry to meet with Chieftess Guh but before they do, a white elk calf staggers through from the tall grass, looking for Ivy’s aid. 

# Elk & Gnolls

It vomits up black demon ichor, sharing how its mother had told it to look for Ivy. Recognising the demon ichor, they wake up Grimrid whose lycanthropy only views the calf as food. Unable to resist the urge, Grimrid lunges for the calf. Amidst trying to stop Grimrid, gnolls appear. The party manages to fight the gnolls, but during combat, the calf does transform into something monstrous but is made unconscious by Elisa. 

# Verena

Post combat, the elk calf does turn back into its former self, giving some of the party hope that the calf can be healed. Just as they finish fighting the gnolls, a grey skinned tiefling shows up, adorned in fine luxuries. Grimrid, in his half werebear form tries to speak to Verena but his words barely make sense. Elisa rushes to pawn Grimrid off to Verena, who rejects the offer. Looking at the elk, Verena recommends putting it down or giving it to her should they not want to do it themselves but Ivy does not let it go, adamant on protecting the elk. 

Verena does explain the recent demonic spread. Her attitude does annoy Elisa, which Verena takes the opportunity to push Elisa’s buttons, seeing how emotionally exhausted Elisa seems to be as Roast joins the party after butchering the corpses of 1 Flind, 4 Gnolls to be fed to her Mother.  Verena leads the party to Guh but on the way, Grimrid faints from exhaustion, prompting the party to take a short rest. After his short rest, Grimrid finds himself able to revert to his dwarven form, and the party head up to Guh's Feasting Hall to speak with her.

# Guh

From Guh, the party learns that Moog has started a coup, leading away the other she giants as they all blame Guh for being lazy, stupid, and not contributing to the farming efforts, not realising that Guh is unable to help, not unwilling. The party does suggest that the remaining male giants could farm but the rigid social rules of Hill Giants decree that female giants do all the work whilst male giants simply play and are too stupid to be capable of anything. Realising that the Hill Giants do not have any food, the party does not mention their original plan to Guh, instead wanting to speak with Moog first. They do tell Guh that they will speak with Moog, hoping to see if the relationship can be mended. 

# Leaving

## Verena's Help

Leaving the main hall of Grudd Haug, Verena floats over some useful items to the party and upon learning their tight timeline, gives them a scroll of teleport, handing it over to Elisa as recompense for pushing her buttons. When it is questioned why Verena can’t do anything, she does allude that her powers are too much for the delicate sensibilities and often come with a cost. But before she is able to explain much, the floor beneath her splits open as the tendrils of Demogorgon reach out, grasping, and dragging her down. 

Grimrid steps in to help, or tries to as his attack goes past the tendrils and makes contact with Verena instead. Verena summons the power of celestials and fiends, siphoning the powers of their battle to free her hand just enough for her to grab her dagger as she slices down her own arm, feeding her blood to close the portal. The party sees why Verena’s hands are tied. The party finally takes their long rest before heading out once more.

## The Elk

The party learns from the Elk that it's mother and it were separated by the Gnolls, but that before they were seperated, they were both seeking out something that its mother described as smelling of root and earth, and that this creature would protect them and heal the Elk Fawn's corruption.

# Leaving

Heading out, the party does find a series of corpses, all bearing a mark of two holes which Ivy realises are like the fangs of a snake. Following the scent track Ivy found, the party arrived at a fog covered place at 820pm.
