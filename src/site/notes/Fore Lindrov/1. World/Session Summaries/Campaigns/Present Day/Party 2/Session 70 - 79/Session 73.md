Windfall carries on, turning her attention to Elisa as she reveals that a friend of Elisa’s has been by recently. When pressed on which friend Windfall is referring to, Windfall alludes to a soul mangled Aurelion who she claims Elisa damned to Hell. Successfully distracting two of the party members, Windfall turns to Nyktos attempting to tempt and possibly guilt with the lure of resurrection magic, reminding her of her deceased brother and friend. Her words don’t affect Nyktos but do draw in the attention of Wystram. 

Wystram gives into the sin of greed, taking the opportunity to ask about the resurrection magics. Elisa continues to ask questions about Aurelion's visit. She learns that he sought out Windfall in an attempt to find a way out of Hell. Elisa also asks questions about Fool. And Windfall obliges, sharing that she knows both where Fool is and also why she can’t be killed. Windfall then turns back to the other two party members, warning Nyktos of her mark and the destruction it could bring. 

She lures Nyktos and Elisa in, suggesting that Vanifer hides things from them, and that her current actions could attract dangers they don’t intend to. She tells Nyktos that the mark would cause her to kill everyone in the tower. But when Nyktos attempts to gather more information about the one who controls the mark, Windfall remains ruthlessly coy, speaking in circles and providing no further information. Turning her attention back to Wystram, she warns him that his family goes against what his new mother stands for. 

And to sweeten the deal for Elisa, she offers information on Sylvaerys, sharing that the woman Elisa seeks to kill has dragons by her side. Upon further inquiry, Elisa learns that the dragons are extremely loyal to Sylvaerys, a mother to one, and a friend to the other. Steadfast companions who would protect Sylvaerys til their deaths. Elisa notices the distaste Windfall holds towards Goetia. Asking further, she learns that Windfall finds Goetia and Bel annoying leaders and that she’d much prefer Zariel. Windfall also reveals that Fool is the strongest, once killed, she’ll return and find what makes her enemy weak, killing them without decency of letting them know they died. In addition, Fool had a hand in freeing Zariel. 

With all the information and bait Windfall has set out, the party begins to cave. Wystram is the first to make a deal, learning the ritual to bring back his friends and the cost is not simple but less harsh than what is expected. In exchange for the knowledge, Wystram has to collect the blood of 25 devils, 3 greater fiends, and 1 archfiend. The catch, however, is that should Wystram fail to follow through with his side of the deal, any being brought back by the ritual would be damned to hell. At ZipZap’s insistence, Elisa signs a contract, earning the powers to banish Sylvaerys Edraconic allies and the power to cast Imprisonment on Sylvaerys, the condition being that she would be able to escape when ZipZap dies. 

In exchange, Elisa is to pick one of the Giant Lords, allowing Windfall to decide their outcome. Elisa chooses Duke Zalto, completing one of her contracts with Windfall as she breathes into the vial. For the other contract, Elisa is to retrieve the mask Sylvares owns and give it to Tiny. Next, Tiny finally has the chance to speak, bringing back the topics of Severin. He makes a contract, acquiring a way to fix Severin and his injuries in exchange they have to amass a payment of 500,000 gold. With the third contract done, Windfall lowers her walls against Nyktos, offering the cleric a genuine response to her questions. When Nyktos asks once more who controls the mark. Windfall shares that the being in question goes by many names, “Arth E “Dien E “Goetia E Not wanting to be controlled by Goetia, Nyktos accepts Windfall’s deal to remove the mark. She’s offered the same deal as Elisa, she picks Thane Kayalithica. 

With all the deals completed, Wystram suggests that they merely forget the debt and Windfall accepts. Finishing the game, Windfall drops the act. Going from the seemingly evil self to…a joyous one? She seemingly drops her evil persona, revealing that it was all an act. She explains her rationale to the party, admiring the differently coloured vial contracts from each of the characters. Elisa’s is purple and blue, Wystram’s is a dark grey, Tiny’s is red and blue, and lastly Nyktos Eis red and black. Windfall advises Tiny, informing them that though they carry the blood of their Queen, they would still need to prove themselves to the other dragons. She warns them that there will be many challenges and trials ahead, that the might of a dragon is more than just raw strength but also force of personality. With that, she lets the party through, forgiving their debt and inviting them to meet the Dragon Queen. Nyktos hangs back to speak with Windfall, complimenting the Tiefling’s ability to lie well. 

Moving to the next room, the party enters the hallways. Elisa and Tiny immediately find themselves drawn to a ruby gem in the wall. Elisa senses a great amount of Psionic powers from it that would greatly empower her. For Tiny, they feel a rush of inspiration, the ruby gem would be the core of their magnum opus. They both rush towards it with Elisa connecting to it first. Inside the gem is a dragon whose body is covered in rubies. The being can’t talk but communicates through emotions. Elisa tries her best to reassure the dragon, trying to convey her desire to help. But the being is hesitant to trust, unfamiliar with the concepts that Elisa shares. Elisa relents the gem to Tiny, informing the party with what she saw. Tiny tries to speak to the gem in Draconic but receives no response.

Entering the next room, the atmosphere changes from the hot hellish landscape to a cold icy room. Nyktos dons her winter coat and covers Crimson in his blanket. In the middle of the room appears an Ancient White Dragon. He demands to know why the party has entered the lair or Arauthator, the old White Death. Tiny speaks to Arauthator, telling him of their purposes of meeting the Queen. Arauthator scoffs at the party, mocking their attempts at bravery. All goes well until Tiny mentions the Will of the Talon, insinuating that his position alone should be enough for them to pass. But Arauthator is unconvinced. Tiny tries to salvage the situation, suggesting games of wits and strength in an attempt to fix the situation. Arauthator, having heard enough, is about to breathe his cold breath upon the party when Vhagar steps up demanding to be let through purely because he demands it so. He threatens the ancient dragon, earning Arauthator's respect as the party is allowed through. 

Finally, the party reaches their goal. They walk into the throne room, before them is a beautiful woman, with a crown atop her head and five spheres of white, blue, red, green, black orbiting around her. Tiamat walks up to Tiny, reaching out for them, telling them how proud she is of them. The party leaves, allowing the mother and son to have their private conversation. During which Tiamat expresses her joy at seeing her long lost son. She tells Tiny and Vhagar that they are her greatest exarch, destined to be King, Kaiser. Vhagar, excited to be right, demands for his mother to repeat those words before the party. Tiamat agrees, indulging in her son’s prideful behaviour, telling him that her followers are fickle, but that Tiny can easily prove himself to her. 

Meanwhile, the rest of the party head back to Arauthator. Using the ice in his lair, they create a new snack of cinnamon shaved ice, enjoying it whilst Tiny speaks to their mother. They offer Arauthator some, trying to befriend the dragon. Arauthator seems to enjoy the snack and soon Tiny returns to bring the party back in to discuss business. At Vhagar’s insistence, Tiamat repeats that they were supposed to be King, Kaiser. Though Elisa and Nyktos are quick to dismiss that, squabbling with Vhagar as they usually do. Tiamat is amused by this, seemingly happy for her son. She removes the blue mask from Vhagar, revealing not his skeletal face but a completely healed one. She explains that the masks are Vhagar’s birthright, theirs to hand out to their most loyal exarchs. 

Though as she speaks, Wystram is skeptical and decides to attempt to take a look behind the veil. And so he does. He sees chains, a being much greater than the visage of the woman before them. He sees Tiamat for what she truly is. And she notices. The might of the Dragon Queen is strong, more powerful than anything Wystram has ever seen. He’s shaken, rattled by the sight before him. And as he notices Nyktos and Elisa’s relaxed, casual behaviours he reminds them who they stand before. The two dismiss his concerns, insisting that Tiny’s mother has been kind so far. The two willing to see past Tiamat’s title and legends, instead choosing to trust what they see before them. But the truth rears its ugly head soon enough. As Tiny constantly brings up the topic of their father, openly expressing their support for his ways and admiration towards him. This irritates Tiamat as she lets out a surge of power, injuring the party and their companions. Elisa and Nyktos are forced to see Tiamat as she is, not a caring mother but a ruthless individual. Bringing matters back to business, the party explains the situation at Altland and Tiamat offers them a great help in the form of Arauthator to slay the undead army whilst the party deals with the rest. With business settled, Tiamat invites Tiny and Vhagar to complete a ritual that is only possible with their royal bloodline. And as the ritual is completed, the party gets sent directly back to the village. 

As an effect of Hell, only half of the party leaves unscathed. Tiny and Elisa find themselves afflicted with sins of pride and greed respectively. Their actions in Hell have altered themselves slightly though it is fixable with time and magic. 

They appear before the forest elves with Arauthator in his majestic dragon form. Vaelith and the elves are surprised, worried as a great Wyrm has entered their home. But before the party can act, a man dressed in plate armour appears. He calls out to Arauthator, ready to fight the ancient dragon just to protect the elves. Though confused, the party stops the situation and under Vhagar’s command, Arauthator transforms into a humanoid form. Vaelith is in awe, claiming that the party must be the ones foretold, destined to help the village especially with the great Wyrm by their side. He muses that the party might be able to read the sacred tablet that lies within the emerald mists. Vaelith walks over to Thalor, entrusting him with the responsibility to guide the party to the emerald mist. In response to Vaelith’s faith, Thalor can’t help but feel guilt. The party greets the armoured person. His armour bears a silver mask, no doubt a knight of Lupra. The man is Knight-Protector Artorias of the Custodes Legeres. He’s reasonably confused by most of the party, seeing as Elisa is now blue and Nyktos is in her true form. He does recognise Tiny and greets the party with respect to their appointment. Wystram is in disbelief. 

To think that the immature party members he’s been around hold high appointments, the left hand men to a King, is extremely unlikely. The party doesn’t brag, feeling no need to mention much of their appointment given that they are probably far from the Kingdom. But from Artorias they learn of the present situation at Lupra and the recent dangers they faced with the Hill Giants. Despite his careful language, Nyktos notices something odd and speaks to Artorias privately to learn that Artorias had been sent by Elder Maxim to ensure that they were carrying out their task. In the meantime, Artorias offers his blade, wanting to help the party as best he can. 

Elisa and Vhagar immediately take advantage of this, leaving Artorias to set up camp and do meager chores like cleaning the weapons they do not even use, or polishing a blade they had purposefully dipped in mud. Meanwhile, Nyktos heads to Thalor, wanting to speak to him but unable to communicate with him. Arauthator sees this and offers a hand as well as some advice, much like Myrantha, he suggests controlling potential threats before they fester into something greater. But Nyktos declines, fully aware of the dangers she’s putting both herself and her people in but not willing to ruthlessly kill nor force Thalor apart from his family. She’s willing to put up with his anger, hoping that one day he can understand their decision. 

Failing to convince Thalor once more, Nyktos returns to her party. She speaks with Wystram, fulfilling her promise of teaching him about the Raven Queen. And though Wystram’s intentions might have been to learn of the Matron’s beliefs, morals, and teachings. He instead is told that the Dark Lady cares little for her children. That the respect most Shadar Kai give is purely an appreciation for the life she has bequeathed them and nothing more. In addition, she despises the undead and may or may not like resurrection magics that toy with the natural end of life. 

At this, Wystram thinks of the deal he just made to bring back his dead friends. Noticing his concern, Nyktos points out that it is nearly impossible to know if the Raven Queen would punish him for his actions. Instead she recommends that he do as he wishes, advising him not to forsake his goals for someone who doesn’t truly care. This leaves Wystram with a lot to think about, having found someone he thought was a magnanimous individual, only to hear first hand from someone raised in the Shadar Kai society that his saviour could care less. 

Finishing up their discussions, the party decides to rest for the night, splitting themselves up to protect the elves from any possible dangers. Four hours into their rest, Myrantha, Wystram and Nyktos notice signs of movement around them. The elves end their trance, watching intently at the forest elves who have awoken. Confused, Nyktos tries to follow one of them. Then suddenly Thalor awakes, as does Vaelith and the other elves around them. Realising that something isn’t right Nyktos casts Thaumaturgy, her voice booming as she calls for her friends to wake up. They do, just in time as the elves begin attacking the party. During combat, Nyktos surmises that the elves' uncharacteristic behaviours might be a result of possession. 

Calling upon the power of her god, she manages to expel the ghosts from the elves. Elisa recognises them as the ghosts she had met in Blingdenstone, the place she and her then party had begrudgingly given to Ardor Mor. The ghosts, in moments of clarity, manage to call for help, begging for salvation. Elisa manages to offer one of them such freedom. And as her magic ends the ghost’s life, she pulls the soul away from its owner, sending the soul to its rightful place. During which she narrowly avoids being spotted, catching a glimpse of a mountain of dead flesh. And in the soul’s final moments, it thanks Elisa for granting them the freedom they longed for. 

The ghosts turn from attacking the party to attacking the forest elves, realising they could strongarm the party with them. It doesn’t succeed, seeing as the party continues killing the ghosts. But one of the ghosts manages to take hold of Nyktos, able to wield dangerous magic. The party work together, using their resources to defeat their friend, to prevent her from killing the elves with which she’s been protecting. And fortunately they do, ending the ghost before it is able to do much harm to the elves. 

The party then continues with their rest, healing up from the fight. This time, they are able to rest without any more interruptions. The next morning, the party heads to the Emerald mist with Thalor as their guide. They appear before Naergoth who informs the party that they are too late, that _she_ will find him and he will serve his master all the same. He mocks the party for their naivety and foolishness in believing that they could take on an entire army with their small numbers. At this, Arauthator reveals his true form, taking on the undead army, leaving Naergoth and his minions to the party. 

Combat ensues and Artorias gets to witness the power of the King’s left hand and the company they keep. Wystram summons a wall of ice, forcing the enemies down a specific path as Nyktos summons the blood from her forest, creating shapes of humanoids made of blood who attack her enemies in her stead. He watches as Elisa opens a gateway to the dark between the stars, restricting their enemies. 

Artorias holds his own well, focused on the weak spots of his enemies, exploiting them to end the battle quickly. With Elisa’s and FlipFlap’s teamwork, they have Naergoth on the ground, weak and close to death. In his final moments he curiously asks if they would let him live. Elisa does, allowing Naergoth to begin his escape. Nyktos has no comment on the account of having been true polymorphed into a black pudding. Artorias deflects to the King’s Left Hand. And just as Naergoth begins his escape, Wystram summons an ice storm, ending Naergoth’s life. Lastly the party is left with what remains of the Pudding King. With Elisa’s hunger of hadar and Nyktos Ehelp from Planty, they are able to disable the Pudding King’s eye rays, keeping the party safe as they end his life. With that, the party has saved the forest Elves from Naergoth though not his companions or the “her Ethat he was referring to.
