Upon escaping to safety, Elisa picks up Rook once again, shaking and flinging him up into the sky whilst repeating the words “I’ll see you soon Eat him. Though the Celestial swiftly unfurls his wings, catching himself long before he would have hit the ground. This display confuses the party and a conversation is had about it where Elisa helps the party learn of what had transpired during their escape from Banecroft. However, as the party reaches the Lunar River crossroads, the civilians they had saved begin to speak up. They point out the atrocity they had witnessed, of Pyrite draining the blood of innocents. Davit brings up the murder of Haliq, and Andre mentions the death of his wife and daughter. Heart heavy with guilt, Pyrite offers his life, allowing the friends and family of those he’s murdered to do as they wish. Hayden takes the opportunity to join the opposing side, casually bringing up the other vampires the party has in their midst. The party tries to argue that they have done nothing but help, though Erwin’s partner, Danica, readily suggests that the help given may have been a ruse. Seeing as the conversation was not going anywhere, Elisa suggests that the civilians allow the party to escort them to Kislev. Hayden then hands Andre a dagger, encouraging him to take the life of the one who took his family from him. But Rook interjects, not wanting more innocent blood to be shed. Gordon and Danica speak up too, vouching for the party, though their words fall on deaf ears. Eventually, the group consisting of Medelena, Amalthia, Davit, Molly, Misha, Andre, Hayden, Erwin, and his family, insist on going to Kislev themselves. The party has no choice but to agree and with that Broomhilda parts with the party, offering to lead the other group to safety. Basatur offers his help too and turns invisible before following the group. 

  

Upon reaching the gates of Kislev, the party comes across a group of skeletal undead as battle ensues. Through effective uses of spells, they make relatively quick work of the undead and are soon greeted by Baron Dimitri, the Lord of Kislev; they also meet Baroness Anna Kreznov and her son, Ilya Kreznov. Entering the settlement, the party feels a wash of holy power, like that of a perpetual Protection from Evil and Good spell in the area. From speaking with the Baroness, the party learns of how Kislev operates, with her and her husband rising to office by democratic voting of the people. The Lord and Lady are sure to emphasise that they place the safety of their citizens above all else before leaving the party to do their thing. Elisa, Ilenti, and Zipzap head off, speaking with the locals in town to learn more. They learn about a local, Beowulf who had saved people who were taken in the night but had gone missing many months ago. And ever since the beowulf’s disappearance, the people would hear screams from the abbey though none dare approach. Pyrite takes the chance to speak with Alastair to learn of his intentions when Banecroft is dealt with. To which Alastair affirms that he will take care of the land of Barovia, and how he once had a notion that both Vampires and humans could be equal but now with their recent experience, the notion is weaker than before. However, he does hold the opinion that though humans are lesser, that does not mean that they need to be brutalised. Pyrite and Alastair agree Banecroft to be the worst person to rule Barovia before Beatrice adds that it is vital to get rid of Banecroft first and the rest can wait. Alastair takes the chance to ask Pyrite about what happened during the festival and learns of Banecroft magically compelling Pyrite. When asked if there is a way to counter this compulsion, Alastair simply shares that a spawn is under the absolute control of their maker, the Vampire Lord saying that it is something all spawn must go through.

  

During their walk, Ilenti speaks with Elisa, asking the druid for a favour. Should there be a moment where Nyktos is about to die, if Elisa could instead kill Nyktos, allowing her to return back to their queen. The request prompts a strong reaction from Elisa who comforts Ilenti and promises to do her best to ensure that they are all safe. Ilenti also shares her desires to be better, to live up to the sacrifice that Telantes has made for her which Elisa promises to help with. Throughout this conversation, Ilenti can't help but feel a nagging sensation in the back of her mind that she deserves all this pain and suffering. 

  

Meanwhile, Rook speaks with his ward, Vorun. The latter repeatedly asks when he gets to wear armour and do magic and the like. When Rook asks the boy how he feels having recently lost his parents, the kid instead replies defiantly, seemingly happy with his parents Edeath. This earns him a hit from Rook and Nyktos who was busy reading near them promptly leaves, not wanting to be involved with this. Rook then continues on with his teachings, telling Vorun that magic can be dangerous in the wrong hands and that he has to train before he can wield it. Rook tells Vorun to go on a jog, which the kid reluctantly agrees to. Ilenti, Elisa, and Zipzap return just in time to see Vorun jogging and immediately rushes to his side, all the while questioning what Rook is doing. Vorun quickly collapses to the ground, asking Ilenti to pick him up and carry him. Rook reminds Vorun that in battle, his enemies would not stop just because he’s tired and that convinces him to keep going. Nyktos subtly suggests to Rook to offer Vorun water and the child continues to jog for as long as he is able to. Rook then summons Sir Smigginsworth and learns that the horse had died attempting to save the child, though it is unlikely that he survived. As the party rests for the night, Nyktos notices three graves at the Kreznov family’s cottage that look rather new. She heads closer to read them, learning about three other children of the Baron and Baroness, Nicolai, Yulia, and Stella who had recently passed. 

  

As they rest for the night, Ilenti finds herself dreaming of the vast expense of home. She then sees a woman with solid purple eyes that reminds her of the void at the dawn of time, the coldness of the night emanating from this woman. However, as the figure extends a hand out, Ilenti feels a surge of warmth that guides her towards this entity’s embrace. The figure speaks to her, coaxing her in with the safety that is her. The figure promises to allow Ilenti to forget all her pain, and though the shadar kai initially hesitates, she gives in to her tiredness as she collapses into the embrace. And as she does, it’s cathartic. The woman holds up her face, black eyes with a rim of purple staring at her. “For one to live, the other must die Ethe lady warns, before continuing on that pain is a tool that guides Ilenti to secrets. The lady lets their foreheads touch, and what flows through is a rush of all the pain that Ilenti has accumulated in her life before placing a hand on her back, taking away all of her pain before she wakes up feeling extremely well rested. 

  

At the same time, Pyrite has a dream of his own. He finds himself in the junction of a hallway with multiple doors that line the walls before finding a door with the symbol of his knighthood. He hears the familiar voice of his princess calling out, “To me goblins, to me E He hears sounds of rattling chains, the door stained with blood. Before him stands a monster, a beast with black matted fur and chains sticking out of it blocking his way. Pyrite asks the beast its identity and it responds with “I am you Ebefore warning him about someone’s arrival, that “she is danger, she is blood. Only I can protect you Ebefore startling awake. 

  

The party wakes up to sounds from outside the walls of Kislev and follow along when the Baron gestures for them. They see a stunning woman with ashen pale skin atop a skeletal horse as she introduces herself as Anastrasia Karelova, sent to kill everyone in Kislev by Lord Banecroft’s orders. Though the woman instead suggests a game for the Kislevites, every game won gives them a two day delay before they’ll attack. Baron Dimitri requests for the party’s aid as the representatives. With that, they begin the games. The first of which is an arm wrestling match that Rook steps up for. Not wanting to risk losing, Elisa takes the chance to use her psionics on Anastrasia, making her lose the battle. However, this power piques Anastrasia’s interests though she’s unable to figure out who it is that has done this. Next is a test of dexterity, where a contestant has to deal three instances of damage to win. Ilenti partakes in this game, swiftly and easily attacking her opponent thrice, securing the win. The last is a game of concentration, where the contestant simply has to keep their focus whilst taking attacks from their enemy. Pyrite takes on the challenge and manages to attack the enemy first, channelling his smite into his attacks, wounding the Wight. When it is the enemy’s turn however, the party can’t afford to take any risks and Nyktos successfully blood bends the Wight, causing the hit to miss Pyrite. Anastrasia notices the magic and attempts to break it by casting psychic lance on Nyktos, though Ilenti quickly counterspells the spell subtly, giving Anastrasia no additional information about herself. During the Wight’s turn, he breaks his concentration as per Nyktos Econtrol, securing the party their third and final win. With that, the party has successfully gained six days for Kislev to prepare for battle. The Baron offers gold to the party as thanks, which they refuse at first but are forced to accept due to the Baron’s insistence. Pyrite accepts the gold but also offers it back in return as the party’s thanks for allowing them to stay in Kislev. 

  

As the Baron leaves, his son does not follow. The young man stays back, speaking to the party about the upcoming war and how he believes that they should not back down. To his disappointment, the party is less optimistic than before, having witnessed Banecroft’s army. The party then head back inside where a young timid boy approaches them, informing them that Broomhilda wishes to speak with them. Following Mobi, the party arrives at Broomhilda’s home. Elisa is quick to recognise the man Broomhilda was speaking with as Briddihue, the blacksmith from her town. Elisa takes the time to catch up with Briddihue and Mobi, informing them about Goetia. Pyrite also learns about the Moribund and the horrible way in which they had destroyed Elisa’s hometown. Broomhilda also informs the party that something has occurred at Aylin’s tower, that undead have been attacking the place. And so they set out to check up on the tower. Along the way there, Ilenti speaks with Rook, teaching him on how to best raise Vorun. As they travel, Nyktos notices shadows dashing towards them and calls out to find a pack of wolves, their leader sporting a scar down both cheeks. Elisa casts speak with animals and converses with the wolves, intimidating them into leaving the party alone and it seems to succeed. Though not too long after, Nyktos realises that the wolves are behind them once more. Elisa speaks to the wolves once more and Ilenti tries her hand at scaring the wolves, though they seem to not be bothered in the least. Battle ensues and the party manages to scare the wolves into leaving.  
  

Arriving at the tower, the party solves the puzzle on how to enter quickly and investigate the area. Heading up, Rook and Pyrite rush forward with the former calling out for his sister when wraiths attack. One of the wraiths rushes to attack Vorun and easily does so. When the attack hits, Nyktos is quick to use her sanctum amulet to shield Vorun from death. Rook quickly returns to his ward’s side, smiting the wraith that hit Vorun to protect the child. As the battle goes on, the party notices the imagery of a beast of darkness appearing behind Pyrite when he uses his smites and that his hexblade’s curse is a mark. After combat ends, Elisa questions Pyrite about it, asking if he had done anything to his sword and if his smites felt odd, both of which the paladin denied. 

  

Continuing into the room, the party stumbles upon two chests and Vorun opens the more childish looking one to find Gertruda’s journal which is promptly taken from him as attempts to teach Vorun morals are made. Ilenti reads the journal and Zipzap takes a peek, immediately questioning the words used before the rest places the journal back. In the other chest, they find armour belonging to an “Isobel Thorm E The party doesn’t think much about it before exploring the rest of the room. Meanwhile, the party approaches the other door to find Gertruda hidden away inside her room. She tells the party that Aylin had gone to deal with other threats but had yet to return. Nyktos also takes the chance to mention Gertruda’s mother though the child seems too madly in love to care about her mother. With Elisa figuring out the riddle to the door, they party ascend upstairs to find a wolf, Wendel Wynmir to be specific. Wendel shares that Aylin was given to Agatha, who only works for Banecroft because she desperately wants to keep her daughter, Broomhilda, safe. He also shares that Agatha is the key that gives Banecroft his powers. He eventually asks the party to kill him, so that he does not head back to Banecroft to inform the lord of the information he had just given to the party. But the party does not agree. Ilenti asks Wendel of his regrets and learns that he wished to have had children with Broomhilda, and wished to see his brother marry someone he loves. Rook comes up with a plan to save Wendel by attuning him to their Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location, and placing him in the amulet that he places Fido in in order to prevent Banecroft from knowing Wendel’s whereabouts while they search for a cure. All that was left wasi to stop the antimagic field in the room. Pyrite attunes to the box creating the aforementioned effect and the party carries out their plan, faking Wendel’s death. Finally, Pyrite decides to toy with the box and clicks the right button, solving the puzzle. It summons someone, something, as a singular eye looks at Pyrite. Elisa immediately recognises the figure and drops to her knees. The uncharacteristic behaviour has the party on edge as they all quickly follow suit. With further questioning, Ardor Mor learns that Banecroft is in league with the moon and is the lord of Barovia with an immortal servant and a demon lord connections. In response, Ardor Mor grants Pyrite an army at no cost other than that Pyrite should keep the puzzle box with him and bring it to the goblins. Ardor Mor also questions Nyktos and Ilenti about the state of the land, which the latter shares that she knows not of what the land has but the former shares that the land has water and rain, something that is unseen elsewhere. When speaking of plans after Banecroft’s fall, Ardor Mor asks Pyrite if he would like to be the new lord of Barovia. Though Pyrite responds negatively and shares that a Vampire named Alucard will be the next lord of the lands.
