However, before the party can move on, Ilenti collapses. The party rushes to her side to check up on her. Despite the sudden collapse, they find that she is alright, unconscious as though she was put into stasis. Zephyros floats over, suggesting that Ilenti's new found powers may have taken a toll on her body, hence resulting in her needing rest. Unable to do anything at the moment, Nyktos reluctantly places her sister in the bag of holding before continuing on their journey. Rook and Pyrite share a talk about Pyrite's new condition and Pyrite resolves to control his vampirism. The party continues, exploring what was left of Argynvostholt, finding old graves before making their way down to the crypt. They eventually meet Lloyd in his dragon form who's too busy moulding flesh into the forms of his fallen friends. Using her earring of message, Elisa speaks to Lloyd, sharing her condolences for his friends and how she understands, emphasising that his friends would want for him to live. However, Lloyd refuses to listen, choosing instead to enact his plan, to act like a merciless beast in hopes that the party would wish to slay him. Most of the party refuses to engage in combat, instead trying their best to convince Lloyd to stand down. Except for Rook, who is the first to attack and continues doing so as the dragon strike his friends down. In the moment that Rook allows his attacks to waver, he hears the voice of the Sword of Kas in his mind, asking him if he's willing to let his friends die just because some guy feels sad. Not wanting to lose another party member, Rook continues to battle the dragon. Eventually, Nyktos pulls out Kaze, taking a swing at Lloyd. Just before the blade hits, Lloyd stops his pretences, accepting the death he has longed for. To his surprise, nothing hits. As Nyktos stops just right before, asking Lloyd if he has had enough. Lloyd is confused, questioning that isn't it an adventuring party's mission to slay monsters like him. Nyktos takes the moment to assure Lloyd that he isn't a monster and the party chimes in offering him hope. With Lloyd agreeing to join their cause, the party informs him of the dangers in Barovia and where to meet them next. When asked if he could blend in with civilians, Lloyd transforms into a human male, though his skin is heavily disfigured. Lloyd shares his admiration for Pyrite's optimism, sharing that the Hobgoblin might be better suited for heroics than he was as he bestows Pyrite with a gift of the metallic dragon. Lastly, before they leave, Nyktos offers her cloak of many fashions to Lloyd, allowing him to be more inconspicuous. Lloyd then requests some time to himself as the party leaves to bring Hayden back to Vallaki. 

  

They head to the inn, where Urwin and Hayden exchange awkward salutations before the latter heads up to where Broomhilda's room is. Upon seeing the seventeen year old, Hayden immediately begins lecturing her. The party shares about Lloyd and Broomhilda shares her plans to leave and wait for the party to the west of Vallaki. They agree and head back downstairs to drink, as they leave, they hear Hayden reprimanding Broomhilda once more, mentioning how she's the leader of the resistance and how bad it would be if she died. Back downstairs, the party sees Beatrice, Kashin, and Alastair. Pyrite introduces himself as a knight from his kingdom to which Alastair comments that the Goblin Kingdom has been gone for thousands of years though offers that they may have survived like Pyrite did. They then sit at the bar, enjoying their drinks for the night when guards of the city walk in, asking for Ilenti's whereabouts before telling the party to show up at the Baron's manor first thing the next morning. Nyktos heads to the residences of Herschel and Dalaina to check up on their families. She reluctantly wakes up Charlie, only to learn that Peter has been missing for two days, and in her place was nothing but a doll that Herschel now has. Nyktos promises to bring Peter back and rushes off to check on Dalaina's parents before heading back to the inn to gather the party. The party sets out to Blinsky's shop and Elisa sneaks in to scout the place out whilst the other three remain outside, trying their best to look unsuspicious. 

  

Entering the place and heading up the stairs, Elisa is met with a creepy looking nutcracker doll that stares at her. She makes her way to the second room before she hears sounds of the nutcracker trying to make its way towards her. Thoroughly creeped out by the dolls, she makes her way back out, informing the party of what she's seen. They attempt to make their way back in, with Rook disguising himself as Blinsky and Elisa following behind invisible. The guards are curious to see Blinsky returning back home once again and make small talk asking about the bag of holding he has. Rook confidently mimics Blinsky's voice and mannerisms, easily making his way past the guards. However, before he is able to enter the home, the real Blinsky notices the opened window, closing it and commanding the nutcracker to stand watch for the night. Seeing as their only means of entering has fallen through, the party has no choice but to leave it for the next day. During their long rest, Nyktos heads to Rook's room to ask for his input for Telantes' song. Rook shares a couple of words and Nyktos gently broaches the subject of how Rook sees Telantes and Selune. With Rook commenting that they are one and the same, Nyktos carefully mentions Telantes' reactions towards being called "Selune" in her final moments and how she did not seem to enjoy that. Nyktos shares that whilst in an ideal world she'd love to bring them both back, the world they live in far from ideal. She leaves Rook with one last question, "Would you be alright bringing back Telantes and not Selune?" As silence surrounds the two, Nyktos starts to leave, telling Rook that she knows it is not an easy question to answer before leaving him to ponder about it. As the door shuts, she hears the sounds of Rook's sniffles before leaving. Meanwhile, Pyrite speaks with Elisa as she shares about her backstory. Continuing from that, they play some card games and Elisa asks what it is like to be a knight and learns about the laws, virtue, and honour that comes with knighthood. She also learns about the different rankings, a concept that she hadn't known before. Pyrite shares about his life, how he didn't know his parents and was sent to be a miner before he one day found his sword. Looking at said sword, Elisa realises that the blade has writings in Giant and has Zephyros read them. The wizard studies the writings, sharing that they are directions to a specific area where "high in the mountains will you find the seat of the blood of the Desert of Doom. Nyktos then enters and is promptly invited to join their card game. She accepts and brings up the song she has planned for Telantes, asking the rest for their input. With their conversations wrapped up for the night, the party rests. 

  

The next day, the party enjoys breakfast before heading to the Baron's manor. Outside, they meet Dalaina who seems extremely bubbly and excited for the festival. When Nyktos attempts to ask Dalaina about Herschel, she seems too caught up in her own excitement to have noticed. The party sees Molly and Misha. They gently probe about the performance, and with detect thoughts Elisa learns that they feel bad for the party and scorn the Baron for the ending of the performance. Molly also expresses her concern for Herschel and informs the party of where he is. The party fulfils their obligation to meet with the Baron, catching bits of his berating of his wife before they enter. He urges the party to stay within the manor, allowing them access to the entire first floor of the place. The party then leaves to meet Herschel where the doll in his hand looks eerily similar to Nyktos. Herschel is about to leave the mansion to look for his daughter when the party urges him to stay and promises to bring her back. 

  

They storm off to Blinsky's once more, immediately accusing him of kidnapping and demanding to know where the child is. However, Blinsky refuses to drop the act and is quick to bring the guards into the situation. Thinking quick on his feet, Pyrite starts commenting on Blinsky's use of Nyktos' likeness for his dolls, convincing the guards that their dispute was of a business nature. Nyktos chimes in, remarking that it's weird and creepy to which the guards agree and the matter is left alone for now. Meanwhile, Elisa has once again snuck into the shop in the form of a spider and effortlessly hops into Blinsky's shoe, following him into his home and up to his attic where she sees a bunch of dolls that with white skin, white hair, and black clothing that resemble Nyktos and Ilenti. She is eventually led to the room where Peter is tied up and surrounded by dolls. Blinsky tells the young child about the party, how they came in looking to save her but that they couldn't. Confirming Peter's location, Elisa heads back out to join the party. The make their way in once more and battle commences with the nutcrackers attacking the party. The party makes quick work of all Blinsky's defences before heading up to the room where he has kept Peter. Blinsky shouts about wanting to eat souls, specifically the sisters' so that he'll become  the true child of chaos and mentions something about father. Fortunately, the party is able to swiftly take Blinsky down and bring Peter back to her mother. They head back to Herschel to inform him of the good news and when offered gold, the party refuses instead urging him to share about the performance. He gives some information, telling them that the performance shares about their highs and lows but leaves it at that. 

  

Herschel then leaves and the party takes the time to snoop around the Baron's mansion. Nyktos takes 10 bottles of the dragoncrush wine that the Baron has clearly been hogging before sneaking upstairs alongside the party. They first make their way into a room with Elisa phasing through the doors and unlocking the door for them. In the room, they find well made drawings of different landscapes and sceneries. At the bottom of each is signed "Izek". They then find the Baron's study, and proceed to steal books of all kinds as well as some of the writing materials. Next is the Baron's room where the party sees a painting of the Baron's head attached to a body that looked nothing like his. Entering further in, Elisa finds a 15ft tall mirror, a statue of Banecroft, and offerings for him. She beckons the party in which they use identify to learn that the mirror houses the soul of an assassin. As they pocket the mirror into the bag of holding, Zipzap pockets the golden offerings. Meanwhile, Nyktos makes her way to the smaller room in the corner and finds it unlocked. Inside the small room looks like a supply closet transformed into a bedroom that pales in comparison to the other rooms. A quick look at the closet informs that this is indeed the Baroness' room and Nyktos makes her way back to inform the party to stay clear of that room for fear that what little the poor woman has will be taken from her. They finally end up at Vorun's room, where a half baked teleportation circle sits on the floor. Nyktos ascertains that the circle would kill whoever tries to use it, but before the party could snoop any further, Vorun returns. They all attempt to hide but Rook and Pyrite are easily spotted by Vorun who freaks out finding two people in his bedroom. Eventually the rest of the party show themselves as well and Nyktos attempts to misdirect the teen from their trespassing by bringing up the teleportation circle. This prompts Vorun to speak of his hatred of his family and how he wishes to leave. The party attempts to dissuade him with Elisa slapping him in the face and telling him to be grateful that he even has parents. And Rook promptly offers the boy a chance out of his home, offering to bring him to their place instead before anyone can stop him. Naturally, Vorun is elated with the offer and readily accepts as both Elisa and Nyktos point out that only Rook had offered and thus he would be Rook's responsibility. Leaving the mansion, Nyktos finds the chance to suggest to Rook that it might not be that great of an idea to bring a naive teenager along as he could very easily die. However, Rook stands his ground and Nyktos leaves the choice up to the paladin. 

  

Soon, it is time for the festival to begin as the Baron gives his speech, inviting the guests of honour on stage. The party allows themselves to enjoy the festivities and chance upon a wood chopping competition. Pyrite and Rook notice a sulking Herschel and attempt to cheer him up by challenging him to a game. Bets are placed and Elisa and Nyktos enjoy some snacks as they watch. However, when it seems that Rook might be winning, Elisa casts healing word, allowing Rook's tree stump to be mended. Noticing this, the paladin proceeds to channel his magic into the axe, smiting the tree stump. Wanting to let Herschel win, Nyktos and Elisa heal Rook's tree stump further, even going as far as casting haste on Herschel to allow him to win. Zipzap sees that Herschel has good odds and promptly adds in a 500gp bet for him. Pyrite and Rook however, have different ideas as they both smite Rook's tree stump, with Pyrite pretending to accidentally chop it. Though no one is fooled by that as Herschel wins the competition, earning a skeleton plushie that he comments his daughter would love. 

  

The party continues on to a fortune telling tent labelled "Madam Esmeralda's Fortune Telling". Sensing a familiarity, Nyktos happily goes in with the party in tow. Just as before, there are just enough seats for everyone and the cloaked figure before them introduces herself as "Madam Esmeralda" and asks the party to take their seats. Nyktos curtsies, something she's never been seen doing, before taking her seat. Madam Esmeralda prompts the party to ask her any questions and offers the first fortune informing Nyktos that Ilenti is well though her fate is of a thread made of feathers no longer. 

  

Elisa asks about Bunny, to which they learn that Bunny is a lost soul, an axe wielder, someone named Beowulf, a wretched soul that is immortal. Elisa then asks about her family, if her parents are alive and is relieved to learn that they still live. Though when she questions if they have been turned to mind flayers, the Raven Queen offers no more. Elisa presses if she will save them, and the Raven Queen confirms that Elisa's quests of vengeance will provide her with more answers. Elisa mentions Goetia, asking of his whereabouts. The Raven Queen states that she already knows where he is, but to fear not as she has a friend that she doesn't know of behind his lines. That her fate and his are intertwined and his answers will lead her to the Fool. 

  

Rook asks a series of questions about Telantes. The first being, "how to bring her back". Madam Esmeralda then answers his question with one of her own, "How do you bring back what she represents?" The second being her location. Madam Esmeralda shares that Telantes is in the astral plane, but that is not an adventure for Rook to continue on. Finally, Rook asks about what he must do to honour Telantes, and the Raven Queen tells him to stop asking others and to instead ask himself.

  

Pyrite asks about strength, explaining that he seeks enough of it to protect those around him. The response that he receives is of the sunblade, that they wield a vestige of a broken woman and of how the sun can give life, a chance to protect. However, they would have to exchange the life they have in hand for the life of others. Pyrite asks if any of his people still live. He learns that to the North West of Fore Lindrov, high in the mountains where the stars once slept, and since taken by slimy tentacles, that Pyrite wil find his answers there. The Hobgoblin then shifts his question to something more recent, requesting for the location of Lady Wachter's body and receives assurance that he will see it soon. 

  

Nyktos asks about her mother, wondering how she ties into all this. She's told that her mother is two sides of one coin, that she both loves and hates, and the answers sought lay in the manor. Nyktos brings up her powers, wondering if they are from an evil source. To which she learns that she will receive more answers in the Amber Temple, beyond the gates of hell. This reminds the party of the visions that they saw in Argynvostholt. Still thinking about her mother, Nyktos asks how likely they are to have to kill Faraine, referring to her by name instead of relation. The Raven Queen muses about who Faraine is, and perhaps that is something for Nyktos to think about. 

  

The Raven Queen then informs that party of the powerful shards that they have, that they are vestiges of pain that will allow them to reach the castle. That there are more out there, one possessed by the destroyer of Salem, son of Godwin, Godfrey. And the other is to be found by using the one they already have. 

  

For the final question, Elisa asks if there is a cure for two of her friends, Dave and Iymrith. Unfortunately, the Raven Queen says that she has run out of answers and gestures for the party to leave. They do so and continue on to an art competition. 

  

At the competition, Izek stares at Elisa and Nyktos who are both taking part in the competition. Izek draws the beautiful scenery of Barovia. Nyktos draws a scene of D3TH TR4P's performance, with Ilenti and Nyktos in their usual roles and Telantes as their drummer. Despite the beautiful painting, the description and story falls short as the audience sees through Nyktos' poor attempts to use pity to gain the win. As for Zipzap and Elisa, they have a rather crude drawing of the first time they met.  
  
However, Zipzap's horrifying and gruesome description of the effigies he had witnessed captures the audiences' attention and secures his victory, winning him a skeleton plushie as well. In response to Zipzap and Elisa's win, Izek breaks his painting into two and storms off. 

  

The games are halted as a parade begins, what was cheers quickly turns to silence as the bodies of the Wachter family are dressed in clown outfits and paraded about with the Baron encouraging/demanding people to throw rotten fruit at them. The civilians have no choice but to comply and as Alastair comments on how disgusting this all is. As the bodies are brought back to a guarded location, Rook and Pyrite follow. Rook disguises himself as a guard, lying about wanting to laugh at the corpses to gain access to them. To accommodate the corpses, he pulls out the mirror they had taken and leaves it there. To not arouse any suspicion, Rook uses minor illusion to create laughter. He successfully leaves with the bodies, creeping out the guards in the process. 

  

For the last game, the party plays a game of Liar's dice with Urwin, they all put in 20gp each and have a fun time playing. Pyrite ends up victorious and ends up with a decent amount of gold. As thanks for the game, Nyktos takes out five of the dragonscrush alcohol that they had taken from the Baron's house and gifts them to Urwin, knowing that his business could use it. As the night comes to a close, the performance starts. It opens up with the tale of a tragic warrior and the fates they have in store. The warrior is dress in black wielding a sharp blade, leading along a chained man where they hope to find their parentage but instead of answers, the warrior throws herself off the ledge, refusing to give in. Next, it shows the party at the Death House, and how they have saved the siblings and hallowed the place. It moves on to their meeting with Ireena and Hayden before cruelly showing how they had lost Ireena. It then flashes to Vallaki and the many things they have done to help, from removing the blood witches at the windmill, to slaying the evil tree that had sprouted, and lastly saving the innocent people from the maddened angel Barcovius. Then the scene changes with Misha reemerging as Pyrite, showing off how he had killed an innocent flesh golem before showing how they right their wrongs. And lastly for all the good and bad they have done, their fate was to die. However, before the four performers could hang their necks in the noose, Elisa uses her telekinesis to shove the noose far away. Battles ensues as an odd amalgamation of flesh jumps onto stage with tendrils sticking out of its body and two that sprout from its head that flop down to its side like bunny ears. The party work hard to save all that they can. Nyktos focuses on saving Molly, Misha, Dalaina, and Herschel, shouting at them to head west. Herschel, however, does not. He runs past Rook who grabs a hold of him, trying to stop him from leaving. Seeing this, Nyktos calls out for Rook to let Herschel go, understanding that he would give anything to save his family. Pyrite calls out as well, reminding Rook to focus his efforts on saving those that want to be saved to better their odds. Rook turns his attention to Vorun, using his powers to allow the teenager to run off to safety. Elisa focuses on saving the Wachter house employees, and so does Pyrite. The two also help save Dalaina's elderly parents. As Banecroft stands there watching, he decides to make things more interesting as he controls Pyrite and has him kill Dalaina's mom first. He commands Pyrite to drain Dana and unable to withstand the command he does just that. He then commands Pyrite to kill Dalaina next, to free her from her suffering as she watches her mother die. Trying to save the young girl, Pyrite instead attacks Haliq, bringing him close to death's door. His next attack, however, strikes Dalaina, the once crying woman goes quiet as her body slumps down, dead. And with his final attack, Banecroft commands Pyrite to drain Haliq of all his blood, killing yet another innocent. In the midst of saving people, Rook finds himself in a one to one combat with the immortal Bunny who repeatedly attacks the paladin. Being too caught up with saving the lives before him, Rook does not notice his connection with Sir Smigginsworth fade. Elisa and Nyktos shout for Urwin and his family to run, not wanting them to die. Urwin's wife scoops up the children and proceeds to fly away, leaving Urwin behind to fend off the vampires. Having secured a handful of innocents in one location, and presently holding onto the bag of holding that contains her sister after Rook had given it to her, stating that it was now hers, Nyktos is left with no choice but to start running away. She heals Urwin once more, hoping that he'll survive whilst she stays with the rest of the people. Bunny heads to where Nyktos is and is ready to charge at them, still following his commands to kill them. Nyktos plants her feet into the ground, trying to hold Bunny in place. She feels the bones in her arms breaking in the process but manages to keep the villagers safe for now. During part of the combat, Molly was about to take a hit that no one could stop when Pyrite makes a deal with Banecroft to save her, he offers up the sunblade, securing Molly's life this time. Seeing as most of the innocents are headed west, Elisa conjures up a wall of thorns, diving the battle field and tells Pyrite to bring Nyktos back up and run. With not much choice, Nyktos listens to Elisa and runs but not before saying that she'd see her later. Pyrite follows suit and the two lead the civilians through town. Meanwhile, Rook and Elisa work hard to save the remainder that they can. They first save Urwin then Danica and Gordon. During which Banecroft takes pleasure in halting their attempts to save them by charming them. Rook attempts to distract Banecroft, turning the attention to him only to be met with an insult as Banecroft calls him a bug. Elisa summons her griffon and has Zipzap hop on, telling him that she will be right behind him. And just as they have successfully saved Danica, Gordon, and Urwin. Rook rips out the page for Dimension Door from Vorun's spellbook, he looks at Banecrofts and comments that he would be seeing him soon before tearing the page. To Rook's surprise, Banecroft counterspells the spell, trapping Elisa and Rook there. Banecroft smirks and says that now is here. Fortunately, Elisa had a backup plan as she drinks the potion of dragon's majesty that she got from Telantes and flies off with Rook. Meanwhile, Nyktos and Pyrite are running through the town only to come face to face with a wall of vampires awaiting them. Just as they are about to run out of options, they see explosions go off as the Blaze of Hellfire, Basatur himself throwing grenades as the waves of vampires, creating a safe path for them to flee. Catching up with the rest, Elisa swoops down, using her dragon breath to torch the vampires that threaten her friends. The party makes their way to where Broomhilda awaits with Hayden, Alastair, Beatrice, and Kashin, the party has successfully saved who they can, and they all live, for now.
