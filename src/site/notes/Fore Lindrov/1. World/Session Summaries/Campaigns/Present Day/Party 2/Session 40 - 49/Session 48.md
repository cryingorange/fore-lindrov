The party discusses with Ploopploopeen, planning their course of action for the next day. Nyktos raises a few concerns, mostly her armour and her arachnid form. Ploopploopeen rejoices at the sight, claiming that the Sea Mother has blessed her champion with the ability to shapeshift. Ploopploopeen provides the party with the rope they’ll need to tie themselves up and leaves the party to rest as conversations come to a close. Tiny begins working on their armour, crafting a tail befitting of a dragon like themself. Pyrite offers help but is waved off by Tiny who claims to have been crafting long before Pyrite was ever born but allows the Paladin to watch. 

Nyktos, Elisa and Zipzap all head out to the beach. Elisa and Zipzap face the ocean, with Elisa asking Zipzap to describe the scenery before them. Zipzap gives a brief description but slowly comes to the realisation that Elisa can’t see anymore, and worse yet, she can’t see him. Zipzap begins to panic, demanding that Elisa see right this instant. Sensing his panic, Nyktos tries to help, saying that they could one day heal Elisa’s eyes but that isn’t good enough for Zipzap as he begins pouting and throwing a tantrum. As he does, he begins to sprout yet another eyestalk, but this one is a little different than the others, it bears a white mark through it, similar to how Elisa’s eye was before. In that moment, Elisa finds herself being able to see through the eyestalk, and Zipzap rejoices, proud of himself for being that great. The two enjoy their time leaning against one another, staring at the ocean. 

  

With that settled, Nyktos approaches the statue of her mother, speaking at it. She expresses her confusion, ranting about how she came prepared to have to fight and even kill her mother, but instead finding her some Goddess of Kuo-Toa. She goes on a ramble, expressing her confusion and explaining how Ilenti is in yet another coma, stuck in a bag of holding. Nyktos scorns at the statue, commenting how she hopes that Faraine is being a better Sea Mother to the Kuo-Toa than she ever was with them. She laughs bitterly at herself for speaking to a statue before returning to Ploopploopeen’s home to rest. 

  

Pyrite then joins Elisa and Zipzap at the beach, asking for their thoughts on the ocean. Elisa, having never seen an ocean before, shows her appreciation for it but comments that waves are odd. Pyrite describes the waves, providing more insight about the ocean and how it doesn’t really end like a river would. They sidetrack as Pyrite explains how the ocean is salty and Elisa theorises that the Kuo-Toa’s bodily wastes might be the reason for the saltiness. Elisa then reveals her new ability to see through Zipzap’s eyestalk, explaining the limitations of her vision and Pyrite praises Zipzap, encouraging the beholder’s already inflated ego as he claims to be the greatest. When Pyrite comments how not being able to see his rotting self is a good thing, Elisa switches to Zipzap’s vision just to mess with him and remarks once again on his stink.  
  
Pyrite says that he’ll get his appearance fixed tomorrow with Nyktos Ehelp and tries to bathe in the ocean but it does not remove the smell. Elisa offers her cleansing stone but even magic fails to remove the pungent cloud that follows Pyrite. Left with no other choice, Pyrite acquires a rotting fish from the Kuo-Toa, intending to use it as an excuse for his odour. Alongside the rotting fish, Pyrite procures a fishing rod and begins to fish. During which, Elisa and Zipzap wander along the shoreline and find a pearl. They show it to Pyrite, who joins in on that but only manages to find a seashell. Disappointed, Pyrite returns back to fishing, hoping to get something better. Elisa and Zipzap continue and Zipzap finds yet another pearl, Zipzap hands the pearl to his minion, Pyrite, telling him that it’s alright to suck. 

  

The next day, the party finishes up their final preparations for the bait, giving up their stuff and removing themselves of their armour and weapons. Nyktos, being in her true form, gets extra tied up and the party happily injures one another to look the part of being captured. They wait till 5pm, when the weird day-night cycle finally switches. Apart from the party, there is one another sacrifice, a red dragonborn adorning many scars seems to have been captured by the Kuo-Toa. Ploopploopeen plays his part, surrendering the party to Bloppblippodd as Bloppblippodd gloats. From a chest, Bloppblippodd pulls out a baby raptor, ready to slice its throat when Tiny breaks out of their bindings, refusing to let the little one get hurt. Battle breaks out as the party rush to grab their items. The mysterious Red Dragonborn rushes in to help as well, using a stick as his weapon as he bravely attacks the Kuo-Toa. Zipzap and Crimson fly in on the golden canary, ready to help those they care about. The party makes quick work of Bloppblippodd as Nyktos controls the dead Bloppblippodd’s body, forcing them to attack their comrades. 

  

The followers of Leemooggoogoon are clearly in a losing situation, when all of a sudden the floor begins to rumble, tentacles sprout out from the ocean as a giant Kraken approaches. Leemooggoogoon’s followers rejoice, believing the Kraken to be the champion of their God but the reality sinks in as the Kraken helps no one, clearly here to bring destruction to the town. A tentacles grabs the stunned Nyktos, tossing her into the ocean as the impact knocks her unconscious as she begins to drown. Pyrite begrudgingly rushes in, grabbing a hold of the drowning shadar kai as he begins to drag her to shore.  
  
Crimson is visibly angry, the little pup rushing forward to spew fire at the gargantuan monstrosity. Elisa notices this and enables the pup, polymorphing him into a giant ape as Crimson punches the Kraken with his bare fists for hurting Nyktos. Tiny activates the dark powers bestowed to them by Tarr Haak the Five Headed Destroyer, their armour glowing as lava flows through them. Tiny rushes up to attack the Kraken, more than eager to fight the gargantuan thing. Even as they get swallowed by the creature, Tiny remains ecstatic. Pyrite sighs, not really wanting to fight the Kraken but having no choice as all his teammates rush towards it. Pyrite swings his giant blade at the Kraken, forcing it to puke out Tiny who whines at being let free.  
  
The Kraken grabs Tiny once more, ready to pull them under the water when the Red Dragonborn grabs onto it, his muscles tensing and the veins in his arm bursting from the sheer pressure of holding onto a gargantuan monstrosity by himself. He manages to keep the Kraken in place, allowing Pyrite to slice the Kraken in half as it clings onto its final bit of life. Nyktos takes the opportunity to showcase the prowess of Blibdoolpoolp, slamming her Rod of the Blood Ruler into the sand as the charm magics take effect. She exclaims that Leemooggoogoon’s champion has been felled by the champions of Blibdoolpoolp, proving the Sea Mother’s might. The Kuo-Toa, all charmed to view Nyktos as their trusted leader, begin to cheer for Blibdoolpoolp, the whole town converted back to her followers for now. Crimson, still in giant ape form, proceeds to beat the shit out of the Kraken and happily returns to Nyktos Eside when he’s done. Elisa walks up to the Kraken, pulling out her blade of screaming souls and stabbing it to consume its soul, feeding the atropal within. Pyrite then takes a drink from the Kraken, his undead self allows him to bypass the negative effects of the demon-stained Kraken blood as he feels himself grow stronger. The party begins to process the Kraken, harvesting it for parts. The Red Dragonborn approaches them, offering his help and introducing himself as “Dremnir E He explains that he was part of an expedition team into the black gate, that they were only supposed to be looting items but as they were travelling, they found that the landscape around them had changed. He was then kidnapped and sold at Lamoriax, a city filled with thousands of demons.  
  
He had managed to escape but that was short lived before he got captured again, this time by Kuo-Toa. Dremnir joins the party as they equip him to the best of their abilities. The party rests for a bit before setting out to kidnap a Lizardfolk, hoping to interrogate them to find the location of Ableeblerboop. Elisa, with Tiny’s help, looks for tracks as they come across a set of humanoid looking prints. Pressed for time, the party decides to ignore the tracks, continuing on until they find themselves in the thick brush of the jungle. An arrow flies out of nowhere, hitting Elisa. The party soon realises that fighting them is an injured Female Centaur, missing one of her legs and the other ill and diseased. Elisa approaches, threatening the Centaur to concede or die. However, Elisa realises that the Centaur seems pleased with Elisa’s words, almost as if she welcomes the death.  
  
As the party tries to converse with her, each trying their own way to convince the Centaur to stand down, she doesn’t. Dremnir stops right before he is able to kill her, telling the Centaur that this ends now. The Centaur, Hespera, offers the party a trade, information in exchange for her death. She shares that she used to be a part of a tribe, but ever since she lost her leg and was unable to keep up, so too went her honour. Pyrite attempts to talk Hespera out of this, not wanting for a life to be ended for what feels to him are trivial matters. But Hespera insists otherwise, flicking her ear at Pyrite twice, seems to be some form of vulgar gesture in her culture. Elisa and Nyktos understand that there’s no use convincing Hespera and take her up on her offer, requesting for the information they seek and wanting to know how Hespera would like her corpse to be. From Hespera, the party learns that the Lizardfolk are driven by cold calculation. Elisa uses her ability, sending Hespera’s soul to where it should be, which is right where they were. It’s odd, and Elisa mentions this to the party. They notice the arrow in Hespera’s hand looks different. Casting identify on it, Elisa learns the magical properties of the arrow and takes it, trading it for the arrow that Hespera shot at her first. 

  

The party continues forth and comes across a group of lizardfolk. Tiny tries to sneak up to the group but Pyrite calls out to them. The lizardfolk are happy to see the party, calling them food which angers Tiny. As battle breaks out, the lizardfolk act as Hespera said they would and soon realise that the party is more trouble than they’re worth. Their leader commands the others to flee, when Pyrite channels his divine powers, forcing most of the lizardfolk to stay and fight him. Elisa steps in, charming the lizardfolk’s leader with magic and slowly convincing him to stand down. Fortunately, with the party backing off, it works, and the lizardfolk provide them with answers. Unfortunately, the party realises that their plan to kidnap and interrogate a lizardfolk simply wouldn’t work and most of them let the lizardfolk go. At some point during this not quite combat, Pyrite and Nyktos start looking for rocks in the river, with Nyktos offering a round, almost perfectly circular rock to Pyrite. However, Tiny refuses to let the lizardfolk retreat, having taken great offence to being called “food E Elisa tries to convince Tiny otherwise but they stand their ground, insisting on making the lizardfolk regret insulting them. The rest of the party usher the lizardfolk away, pretending to dismiss Tiny’s behaviour as a bout of insanity. Eventually, Elisa polymorphs Tiny into a turtle and picks them up before walking off far away from the lizardfolk. After a 30 minute walk, Elisa sets Tiny down, only to have them start crawling back in the direction of the lizardfolk. The party shares a laugh, finding turtle Tiny’s behaviour endearing before turning them on their back and reverting them back to their original form. Tiny and Elisa address the lizardfolk situation once more, albeit calmer this time as Tiny voices their upset feelings towards being turned into a turtle. Elisa tells Tiny the reality that the lizardfolk won’t be the last people to call them meat and they can’t just eat everyone who calls them that. Though Tiny refutes, claiming that they can because they are a dragon and are the best. Still, Elisa apologises for turning Tiny into a turtle, and assured them that they would get to eat the lizardfolk who did capture Head Priest Ableeblerboop and that would feel even better. Tiny then calms down and echoes in agreement about eating the lizardfolk.

  

The party head back to Sloobludop and are greeted by Ploopploopeen. They explain the situation and Nyktos asks for an item of Head Priest Ableeblerboop’s so that they could use it to scry on her. What Ploopploopeen gives them is a dress, a human sized dress. The party is visibly confused having assumed that Ableeblerboop was a Kuo-Toa like the rest of them. But Ploopploopeen confirms their suspicions and comments on their ugly pink-eyed Head Priest. Those words catch Nyktos Eattention, looking at the dress as she finds it familiar. She turns to Ploopploopeen, confirming that their Head Priest had brown hair as well. When Ploopploopeen gives confirmation, Nyktos walks off with the dress, barely able to process what she just learnt. The party’s confusion continues, wondering how Nyktos would know of such information as they follow after the cleric. Ploopploopeen offers them more information, telling them of a bell that when rung could answer their question. The party takes the information and follows Nyktos where they get some answers. Nyktos pulls out a druidic symbol, one Elisa recognises as both she and Pyrite begin putting the pieces together. Ableeblerboop is Adella, Nyktos Efriend from her first trip to the material plane, the very same friend that Nyktos found dying in a pool of her own blood. Though that seems she’s not as dead as Nyktos thought she was if she’s here. Nyktos begins to spiral a bit, now more urgent to save Adella/Ableeblerboop than before. She insists on leaving immediately when the party shares about the bell but the party are in no shape to do so. They all chime in, offering reasonings and rationales for why they could wait a night. Their arguments make sense, with Tiny suggesting that Adella, being head priest, would be as strong as Nyktos, and the rest pointing out that they can only save others if they’re in good shape. Pyrite points out the potential pitfalls of rushing through things and Nyktos eventually relents, agreeing to rest but stating that they’d head out first thing in the morning. The party agrees and they all head to rest for the night. 

  

The next day, Nyktos is awake the earliest as usual, already preparing the materials she’d need to scry Adella. Fortunately, the scrying works as it projects the view to everyone, a dark cave like place, with multiple dinosaurs, and a statue of Hethrediah. Nyktos uses her blood magic to connect to Adella’s senses, looking and listening from her perspective, though all she hears is the mutters of the lizardfolk in draconic. Still, the scry tells her one thing, that Adella lives. With no other leads, the party sets out in search of the bell. Along the way, they come across a pair of dinosaurs, a larger female dinosaur resting and a much smaller male dinosaur attempting to court the larger one. Pyrite and Nyktos both wish to walk past the situation, not really interested in getting the attention of the larger dinosaur, whilst Elisa and Tiny are extremely intrigued.  
  
Though the situation does not look good for the male dinosaur as the female is extremely disinterested and is close to being annoyed by him, she might eat him if he annoys her enough. Elisa and Tiny sneak up to them, wanting to help however they can. Elisa casts dominate beast on the larger female dinosaur, succeeding and starts speaking to the female, trying to convince her that the male was a worthy mate.  
  
She fibs about the male dinosaur's achievements, proclaiming that the male bears no scars because he’s just that good at combat that he never takes a hit. Her words seem to start to convince the female. Elisa also speaks to the smaller dinosaur, assuring him that she was going to help wingwoman him. Tiny helps by rushing towards the larger dinosaur, pretending to be someone who wants to attack them. With Elisa’s prompt, the smaller dinosaur attacks Tiny, and Tiny sells the attack by launching themselves backwards, landing on the ground where Elisa moulds the Earth to create a bigger impact than it seems. Though the attack seems to lack in strength, Nyktos, now more intrigued and onboard with the plan, helps by casting toll the dead on Tiny, taking the opportunity to payback the time they hit her. Tiny spits out blood, adding to the younger dinosaur’s display of might.  
  
The older dinosaur is more impressed now, and gives the younger one a chance. The male dinosaur rushes to Elisa, thanking her for her help and pointing out a treasure to them as thanks before running after the female dinosaur. The party leaves, picking up the treasure along the way before finally reaching a cliff side. Nyktos approaches the bell and rings it, as it takes her life energy. And whomever the bell is meant to summon, it works, the party begins to hear singing, sea shanties sung as a ship appears before them. Aboard the ship are a crew of 100 people, and alongside the singing are screams from tens of Vargouille. Stairs are rolled out and the party boards the ship. There, they come face to face with a woman wearing a long trench coat and a pirate hat. Her skin is a cool sickly blue, her bright green eyes staring at the party.  
  
Her lips are a nice shade of light blue and her hand is partially skeletal, as it rests by her side near her gun. Her pistol-cutlass  bears a symbol of a skull with glowing green eyes. Captain Rozvankee exudes a dominating presence as she asks the party how they got here. Desperate for the Captain’s help, Nyktos tells Rozvankee of the gate, willing giving the information and location of it. The Captain seems pleased at the information and notes that adventurers do not typically survive in the Abyss. Nyktos shares the information that she seeks, and Captain Rozvankee is able to provide that, for a price. She shares that there’s a traveller in the Abyss who has a curio for Belcheresk, a Balor of the Abyss. She wants them to retrieve said curio and advises them that the traveller is 3 miles away and suggests they use their words rather than their blades. 

  

The party makes their way to the swamp and finds a tree with a tribal marking that leaks sap. Tiny and Elisa are the first to interact with the sap, and nothing seems to happen to them. Tiny spends multiple attempts to smear the sap on Pyrite as well but it doesn’t affect him either. Elisa casts speak with plants and talks to a tree to learn more about the sap. The tree tells her that the sap was made by frog people and also shares that they saw people with chains on their necks passing by, following a very big pig. Elisa thanks the tree and they continue on.  
  
They eventually come across yet another tree, but this time, they notice several Grung around them, prepared to attack. The party soon realises that the sap both poisons them and allows them to speak with the Grung. The red Grung seems to be the leader of the group and warns the party to leave for they are trespassing Gulch-Gill territory. Elisa asks about the pig leading slaves but the Grung refuse to disclose any information, claiming that it is only for their king to know. Elisa asks to see the king for the party to pay their respects. The party are once again stripped of their armour and weapons and restrained as they head to the Grung’s home. Nyktos requests for Elisa’s help the next time she’s able to speak with plants, wanting to apologise to Mr Squiggles for the number of times she’s had to unattune to him as of late. 

  
The party was brought before Kalanatun Gold-his-eyes who is very quick to accuse them of being assassins. The party tries to quell every worry that King has, and notice that his gauntlets seem to glow with magic everytime he says something paranoid. Eventually, the King accuses them of being assassins and sends his guards to kill them. The guards seem tired, but are unable to defy their King as they begrudgingly fight. Pyrite manages his way to the King and casts remove curse, freeing the King from the cursed gauntlets as his mind returns to him. He thanks the party for their help and the Grung all rejoice.  
  
The King happily provides the party with the knowledge they seek, giving them directions to where the traveller would be. The party continues on, and meet the traveller, he is dressed in a vest and a big top hat. He introduces himself as John Smith and is incredibly intrigued by the more humanoid looking people of the party. Most of the party are weary of John Smith, except for Pyrite, who is the most trusting for once. Pyrite engages in trade with John Smith, buying a suit of armour that John claims to be resistant to physical damages.  
  
Though it’s later revealed that John got that off a corpse that was bludgeoned to death. Elisa initially asks about rings and is about to buy one when she realises that it’s cursed, likely to be the case of the many other items John sells as well. The more the party speaks with John, the more they learn that he’s simply a fiend who happens to be a humanoid fanatic, his dream is to someday live amongst the humanoids, experiencing their mundane lives.  
  
As the party brings up the curio, John Smith is more than happy to tell them all about it, informing them of the properties of the item and how it can be used to aid one’s fertility so to speak. Nyktos needs the curio to save Adella and is willing to give up almost anything for it. She asks the others if they would be comfortable with her divulging information of the gate to John Smith, seeing as he’s a humanoid loving fiend who probably wouldn’t hurt humanoids. The others agree and Nyktos turns into a human before John Smith, her transformation gaining his attention. She shares that she has an item that allows her to do so but she’s unable to give that up, instead she offers him information about the gate that would allow him access to the material plane. John Smith eagerly accepts the trade, handing the curio to the party and excited about fulfilling his dreams. Though John Smith begins to muse about his Githyanki servants and what to do with them, they were originally promised their freedom once they brought him to the Balor. Nyktos suggests that John Smith hand them the Githyanki, fully intending to let them go, but the Githyanki immediately start cursing at the party and begin their attack. John Smith is ecstatic about the chance of a ship to ship combat and suggests that he’ll head back to his ship and let the party have a play fight.
