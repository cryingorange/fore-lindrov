With battle over, the Party begins packing up ready to tie up the loose ends that they have in Barovia, when all of a sudden a portal opens a distance away. A small figure is tossed out of the portal and it stands up, rushing head first into the crowd of people. Amongst the cheers and roars, the figure slowly realizes that the war they had come to fight was already over. The figure spots the party in the crowd, and using their telepathy speaks to the least threatening looking person of the party, Nyktos. At the sound of a stranger's voice in her head, Nyktos turns to Elisa, asking if she spoke in her head. Moments later, the figure arrives before the party, a small mushroomfolk. The Myconid introduces herself as Lucatiel. Elisa immediately theorizes that Lucatiel is an awoken mushroom. Though Lucatiel denies it vehemently insisting that she is a Myconid. The party largely does not believe Lucatiel's claims but placates her nonetheless. Lucatiel shares that she was sent by the Archfey Psilofyr, Lord of Spores, to help the party fight the moon. Elisa grows suspicious of Lucatiel, questioning if she is a spy of sorts. Elisa and Nyktos soon conclude that Lucatiel is likely a spy who does not know that she is a spy. But before the party can go too far off course, they are reminded of the Manor, and who they are to meet inside. The party gives Lucatiel a heads up before heading in.

  
  

Inside the manor is filled with tons of rising shadows, darkness envelops the place as the torches go out. It is only when Elisa calls out that Ardor Mor shows himself. The Master of Death, the Black Flame, the Ender of All looms over the party. Elisa bows, and Lucatiel follows suit, kneeling. Nyktos does a deep curtsy, Tiny who recalls what Nyktos had told them, curtseys as well. Ardor Mor enjoys this, commanding the party to gaze upon death itself. And as they do, he declares that the land is now his, along with all those who live in it. Hearing this, Lucatiel shuffles towards Tiny, grabbing the attention of Ardor Mor who utters a word in an ancient language as Lucatiel begins to writhe and scream from the agonizing pain that they are experiencing. Ardor Mor stares at Tiny, leaning in as his breath surrounds them, the pungent stench of death fills the space. Elisa speaks up, drawing Ardor Mor's attention away from her friends. "Oh Great Master," she pleads, "during our travels we've come across a place of great power and knowledge." This seems to pique his interest, and Elisa proceeds to describe the Ember Temple, attempting to convince Ardor Mor that the temple is much greater than Barovia is, especially since the place is in shambles having recovered from the war. Nyktos chimes in, sharing of the God of Secrets, any many books and knowledge that resides within his domain that was illegible to mere mortals like themselves, and would be much better suited for the magnificent Ardor Mor. Fortunately, Ardor Mor finds it interesting and claims the manor and Temple as his. Tiny then speaks up, proclaiming themselves to be a dragon like Ardor Mor, and asks how they can be as great as he is. To which the Master of Death utters a word, and Tiny falls dead, again. Ardor Mor turns to Nyktos, asking if she allows such an insect to claim to be a dragon. Nyktos stutters, rambling that Tiny was a child who did not know what they were saying and that they would not even be worthy of the ground that the Black Flame walks upon. Satisfied with the response, Ardor Mor turns his head to Lucatiel, warning them to not presume that they are allowed to move in the presence of greatness. Ardor Mor then dismisses the party, instructing them to bring Tiny's body along with them. They do so at once, with Elisa and Nyktos each dragging a leg as they pull down their now dead friend out of the manor. The manor vanishes as they step out. Nyktos pulls out a diamond looking at Elisa, who knows in agreement to cast the spell. Nyktos places the diamond on Tiny's chest and casts Revivify. Though instead of the Dread realm, the party finds themselves still in Barovia, and Tiny shoots back up to life. Elisa and Nyktos are extremely puzzled with a weird sense of deja vu. The two discuss the matter, with Elisa theorizing then maybe the moon doesn't collect every soul. The two suggest not try again, counting this one time as a fluke. 

  

The party then explains the situation to Alastair, who understands and claims that he will seal off the path to the Temple. They also inform him of another matter, of how Escher had helped the party, and the rewards that he wanted, namely a title and a manor. Alastair agrees to uphold the deal made. Nyktos speaks to Beatrice away from Alastair, asking what she wishes to be done with the House Windmir's symbol that was used in Hayden's attempt to kill her. Nyktos offers to destroy it if Beatrice wishes but Beatrice instead wishes to keep it, safe from being used again. The party continues to find Broomhilda, who is eager to get her husband back. When the party shares that they would have to go to Agatha's to get Wendel back, Beatrice is torn, unsure if she wishes to face her mother again. Nyktos tries to encourage Broomhilda, sharing that Broomhilda has something rare, a mother who loves her immensely. In her attempt to persuade Broomhilda, she fumbles, saying something that would instead turn Broomhilda away. Seeing this, Lucatiel helps, using her magic to rewind time and allowing Nyktos a second chance to make things right. It helps immensely as Broomhilda nods, agreeing to visit her mother. They show up at Agatha's and she hands a mirror to Elisa, fulfilling her end of the deal. Agatha then brings Wendel back, who unfortunately transforms back without clothing as most of them turn away, except for Tiny. Tiny offers Wendel the pants they had taken from Jovlin and the couple reunites after a long time. Agatha is awkward as the couple share a hug, wanting to be a part of the family but unsure of how to approach. Broomhilda turns to Nyktos for advice and she nods her head, encouraging Broomhilda to take baby steps towards mending her relationship with her mother. And Broomhilda does, allowing her mother to be part of the hug and saying that she would visit from time to time. 

  

The party then heads a distance away from Kislev and undoes the petrification on Volenta. They attempt to convince Volenta to stand down, trying to get her to care about Viona or persuading her that Banecroft never truly did care for her. But nothing worked. As Volenta insists on bringing back Banecroft, the party have no choice but to fight Volenta, though none of them wish to kill her. They eventually settle on letting Alastair handle her instead. Elisa turns Volenta into a turtle and the party rushes back to Alastair. Alastair decides that Volenta shall be executed for her crimes and as a show of the new times, her execution will be made public, to prove to all Barovians of the equality that Alastair spoke of. Elisa and Nyktos are mostly okay with this, understanding why Alastair came to such a decision. Though Tiny does not agree and speaks up, clarifying that they have no intention to undermine Alastair's authority, but seeing as they had already won, Tiny discourages Alastair's first act as leader to be one of violence. They question if Alastair wishes to rule with an iron fist, or be a graceful leader. Though Alastair questions what then he can do, if he keeps her alive, it would appear to the others that he is playing favourites by giving a vampire spawn a lighter sentence. Beatrice steps in, with the symbol of House Windmir in hand as she suggests using it against Volenta to keep watch on her. As the symbol would also affect other vampires, the party suggests working with House Windmir to detain Volenta, letting the partnership between humans and vampires be the show of the new age.  

  

With that, the party heads to speak with Holly and Lloyd. Holly expresses her joy at the ending of Banecroft and the party suggests she come visit sometime. Nyktos also offers to keep in touch with sendings and Holly hesitates, stating that it should be mostly fine, though her master might intercept the communication. The party are confident that Holly's master is likely to be someone good like Holly is. Holly doesn't share too much but says that her master can be temperamental. She casts a sending to check in with her master and gets news to return at once, sharing with the party that an underwater city was recently overturned and that water is starting to return to the surface now. The party is confused, though Holly can't share more information as she teleports back home. They speak with Lloyd next who asks about Pyrite's whereabouts. The party merely shares that the paladin has gone off, continuing to fight other evils out in the world. Lloyd nods in understanding, stating that he would stay in Barovia to continue to help people, as penance for his sins. Lastly, the party speaks with Theron. They try to end the vampire, though unsuccessfully as he manages to turn to mist and escape. Shamefully, the party has Elisa cast message to Alastair to inform him of Theron's escape and with that, they gather their friends and rush out of Barovia before they make any other matters worse. 

  

The party are prepared to walk home, though Lucatiel offers to teleport the party should they have a teleportation circle and they do. They arrive back at their settlement with a whole group of people, much to Pulliver's dismay. Tiny and Lucatiel meet Warchief Ront and Grud, who Tiny instantly feels a sense of camaraderie towards. The party shows Tiny and Lucatiel around the place with Lucatiel taking up residence in the farm alongside Sir Julius. Lucatiel constantly gets called a mushroom by everyone who meets her despite her insistence. Lucatiel senses a kindred spirit in the form of Pulliver, an overworked, underpaid, employee who suffers at the hand of paper work and logistics daily. To help alleviate the immense stress Pulliver has, Lucatiel dedicates her time, helping Pulliver with the management of the settlement. Meanwhile, Tiny has formed a group with Ront and Grud, the trio enjoying their paintings. They spend most of their time together, even helping Elisa gather the trees that she needs. The trio also build furniture for the settlement, providing everyone with cabinets in their rooms. As Tiny hangs out with Ront and Grud, they learn of their life stories. How Grud was part of a group and about to murdered by his then leader Brother Boog for breaking his club when the party stepped in to help. They learn of Grud's insecurities about his lack of strength and of how Ront has taught him that strength isn't everything but instead warchiefs are more important. Tiny also learns of a brand that marks Ront and a bit of his time as a slave.

  

Elisa spends most of her time making their home safer, imbuing it with her magics as she turns part of it into a druid grove, with awakened trees, spike growth, and wind wall to act as the first line of defense. Elisa also takes the time to speak with the person who holds the other godshard that connects with hers. She realises that the individual is Nyssa, a friend she had met on her travels a long time ago. Elisa breaks the news of Telantes' death to Nyssa who seems mostly unfazed, clarifying if Telantes had died saving someone and claiming that she tends to do that. Nyssa shares that she now lives in a volcano along some firenewts and other beings who enjoy the hot weather. She also offers some advice for Elisa, warning her to watch out for the cult of the crushing wave, who hold sharp swords and are really into the government. She also shares of another cult, the Cult of Black Earth who are far more chill than the water cult, simply some builders. Lastly, she shares of the fire cult and warns Elisa to also be careful because she and her party had killed a fire genie who was reserving his wish for the fire cult's leader. Elisa shares the information with the party, informing them of what Nyssa had warned her. During their break, Elisa heads to the farms, shifting around a corner of land to plant the flowers she had taken from the Temple of Minds. Lucatiel, who stays in the farm, asks Elisa what she is up to and is excited to learn about the surprise Elisa has planned. Elisa finishes up the surprise and tells Lucatiel to keep it a secret as they head towards the bedrooms. Along the way, they pass by Tiny, Ront, and Grud, and Lucatiel, unable to hold back, speaks of a surprise which immediately piques everyone's attention. Elisa chastises Lucatiel for her inability to keep secrets but manages to distract the trio by sharing her "surprise" of starting a business and asking the three to build a stall, which they eagerly rush off to do. Elisa and Lucatiel arrive at Nyktos' room and lead her to the farm. In the corner is a fenced off area of white lilies and mimosas, along a little sign "Neektoes' Garden". The elf is elated, touched by the gift she's received from her friend, she expresses her gratitude and brings Elisa to the next topic she wanted to speak about. With Elisa's help, they speak to Mr Squiggles who for the week gets fed extra to make up for the incidents that happened whilst they were in the Abyss. Elisa then speaks with Milton, and their conversation turns towards Elisa's inability to age. She clarifies that when she turns thirty, she would not actually look thirty? And as Milton confirms that, Elisa finds herself bummed at the prospects of never aging. 

  

Meanwhile, on one of their first few days back, Nyktos performs a ritual in her room, offering the heart of Banecroft to Tenebrous, who grants her more power in exchange. She also busies herself with management of her cult, and also teaching some children blood magics. Her free time is spent with her sibling Houzuki as well as sewing up clothes for the people in their settlement. When she's finally done, she presents her gifts to the party. Tiny gets four identical top hats, one for each of their heads, one for Biggie, and one for Largie. The hats are primarily black with a red ribbon and the sparkhammer symbol embroidered on the brim. Lucatiel gets a verdant green neck scarf with plant iconography embroidered. Watching the array of presents, Zipzap demands for his and receives a new cloak with a white exterior and blue lining that has patterns made to mimic a starry night sky, and when observed closely, one can see that Milton is one of the stars. Elisa receives a blue bracelet with a similar starry night pattern and three figures, Zipzap, FlipFlap, and Milton on it. Nyktos then pulls out a large neck scarf, white with the starry night blue trim and a golden star embroidered, similar to Zipzap's one of the stars also represents Milton. And lastly, she pulls out a very small sweater, made similar colours for Milton, though she is unsure if he can actually wear it. Elisa puts the sweater on Milton and he puts a thumbs up. 

  

Months into their break, the party also eventually heads to Vanifer's. Lucatiel has apprehensions about the fire cult's home, not enjoying how much fire there is. There they are greeted by Gili and Dill who eagerly shows them the thing they wanted to show them, which is just a pile of hay set ablaze as Dill watches it burn. The kids run off as soon as they show up, eager to go play some more. Vanifer greets them next, offering them each a hug and inviting the party to dinner before rushing off to her duties as cult leader. The party then head to the library where Zhae Tsung is. And upon seeing books, Lucatiel immediately picks up one of them, only to realise that it is in Ignan and immediately plops herself down to start casting comprehend languages. Though she gets stopped and dragged away as Nyktos informs her that they will be spending the rest of their day thereafter they are done with business. The party heads to where Zhae Tsung is who immediately begins speaking condescendingly once more, not so subtly asking the party what took them so long. They eventually leave, enjoying the rest of their day before heading to dinner. Dinner is comfortable, a nice break from their duties, a space where no titles exist, just friends and family. After dinner, Nyktos takes the opportunity to speak with Vanifer, informing her of her new duties as the blood cult's leader, both bonding over how hard it can be. Vanifer shares some advice, telling Nyktos to remember that her cult can't live without her, that they depend on her and she needs to keep them safe, that she has to keep a balance between what their god wants and what their people need. Nyktos shares the gifts of clothing she had prepared for Vanifer and Gili, allowing the fire cult's leader some respite, to be treated like a regular person for once in a long time. Nyktos tells Vanifer to keep in touch and promises to check in when she can. The party leaves for their home the next day, returning back to their break. 

  

A year passes by quickly, and Elisa has successfully set up the defenses in their home, setting the password to bypass the security measures as "butt" in Primordial. The party are still on break, each doing their own thing. Lucatiel is helping Pulliver with the paperwork, Tiny is hanging out with his buddies Ront and Grud, Nyktos is conducting her blood magic class when she hears heavy footsteps coming from the hallway before them. She immediately alerts the rest, standing in from of the children, ready to protect them from any harm when a Half Orc with a silver hammer and a bottle steps into their home. He speaks to Ront and Grud, saying that they're talking, now. Warchief Ront says that if he wishes to talk, he can speak with all the warchiefs, pointing out the party as the main leaders. The Half Orc agrees and the party leads him outside, wanting him far from the children. As they pass by, they see the Half Orc has knocked out all of the Hobgoblins, and the four trees Elisa had. The Half Orc finally states his intentions, claiming that he is here to take Ront and Grud away. Naturally the party opposes, each trying their own ways to get the Half Orc to share more information. Tiny repeatedly affirms that he will not let the Half Orc take Ront and Grud away without their consent. Words fail and soon the party finds themselves in a battle against the Half Orc. The Half Orc is able to summon his hammer to his side without a word and he hits hard, Tiny is the first to be knocked out, and with that Ront and Grud begin to bargain for their friends. They agree to go. Though the party still insists otherwise, telling them that they do not have to leave if they don't want to. The Half Orc offers them a deal, that in agreement for not taking Ront and Grud away, the party has to bring them the head of a specific water genasi. Oddly, the Half Orc speaks to his hammer, saying something along the lines of it not being him that kills the water genasi. Lucatiel tries to speak with the hammer but realises that it's not a sentient item. The party gets the description of the water genasi and asks if he is a bad guy, to which the Half Orc affirms that the water genasi is probably doing some bad shit right now so their conscience is clear. 

  

The party heads towards the directions provided and along the way comes across a settlement that is eerily quiet. A man rushes out of the house, looking at the party like they are his salvation as he begs the party to kill the giant creature up ahead who is lurking around their only water source. The party agrees to deal with the situation and head off. There they see the Giant in the distance and as they gather the Giant's attention, the Giant introduces himself as "Imperator Uthor". He demands the party submit themselves to a search, and Elisa and Nyktos are compliant, both removing their staffs and bags as Nyktos notes to Elisa that there are traces of more than one Giant here. Lucatiel follows suit as well but Tiny does not back down, refusing to be forced against their own will. Their conviction lands themselves in a battle with the Giant, who takes effort to only attack the Tiny at first. Tiny shouts out to Lucatiel, trying to convince her to their side by pointing out how quick the Giant was to turn to violence. Lucatiel, who had initially been following along with Elisa and Nyktos to stop Tiny, finds herself wavering. And in that moment Nyktos sees the Imperator write off Lucatiel, likely willing to kill her as well. Before they know it, Tiny turns into a three headed dragon, and flies up into the sky, accidentally hurting their friends who were around them in the heat of the battle. Elisa tries to restrain the Imperator, failing once and then tries it again on Tiny as she casts Eyebite, the burnt sealed skin where her eyes were tears open, a singular red eye appearing. Though the Imperator is victorious, and as Tiny takes a fatal hit, Nyktos has Crimson activate the sanctum amulet, blocking the first blow. Though the Imperator does not stop, continuing to hit Tiny even after they had transformed out of their dragon form. They hear a flash and a voice calls out for the Giant to halt. Before them is a female Giant with Lavender skin and ashen grey hair. She is dressed like royalty and is heavily pregnant. Uthor halts at her word, as her steps falter, Lucatiel releases the spell on Uthor, allowing him to help the pregnant Giant. Uthor introduces the woman as Queen Serissa, demanding that the party show her the respect she deserves. They comply, referring to her respectfully with titles though Serissa insists on being called by just her name alone. As Uthor fusses over Serissa, she makes a comment of being pregnant not disabled, to which Elisa quietly chimes in that she is disabled, gathering a chuckle from the Giant Queen. She asks Uthor where her attendant is and when Uthor states that her attendant must have been taken underwater, Serissa rushes to go help but is halted. Nyktos chimes in, offering the party's help, to which the Serissa is grateful for as she casts water breathing on the party, giving them some potions and they head off. Tiny makes a comment about doing this not for the Giant but because it's what they would have done. Tiny also takes the opportunity to ask their other head if the Dragon thing was them, to which they responded that they thought it was the other party. The two then conclude that it was probably Biggie and that they should work together more, it was good. Elisa provides Lucatiel with some healing, half joking about charging for healing. This causes the Myconid to panic slightly, realising that they are now indebted to Elisa. Just to be safe, Lucatiel checks in with Nyktos, asking her if her healing is charged too. This backfires slightly when Nyktos pretends to realise that healing can be charged and affirm that hers is too. 

  

As the party heads underwater, Nyktos uses her staff to cast Alter self, allowing herself to swim under water. When they enter the water, they see a pool of blood, which Nyktos uses to gain some insight. She learns that the creature is healthy, though currently under the effects of charm. Her blood magics also allows her to know the direction of the creature as they make their way towards where the giant is. Along the way the party encounters a strong current, about fifty feet long. Their options are either to swim around the current or face it head on, the party opts for the latter, deciding that time is of the essence to save the giant. Lucatiel plans to teleport across, offering to bring anyone the same size as them, which is only Crimson. Casting speak with animals, Lucatiel speaks to Crimson who sniffs the Myconid before proclaiming that he does not like vegetables and growls at Lucatiel. At Nyktos' word, Crimson states that he's only following Lucatiel because that's what his master says to do. With that, Lucatiel teleports across the current with Crimson without worry. Tiny takes the currents head on, swimming into it. Meanwhile, Nyktos and Elisa take similar approaches, conjuring a blood and water elemental respectively to get them across. Nyktos' elemental experiences some issues, about to be washed off when Lucatiel uses their powers to help save the two of them, before shouting to check if healing is still charged, which Nyktos assures is not. Tiny faces some issues, slipping as the currents bash into him. The party rushes to help, with Elisa casting a thorn whip to pull Tiny closer and Nyktos sending her elemental after to pull Tiny out. Though Tiny misses the elemental and is pushed further away. They eventually get Tiny out of the currents and carry on. They find themselves in a place where a cave entrance should be but isn't. With their detect magic, Tiny points out the illusion and abjuration magics surrounding what they think is the entrance. Nyktos spots something shiny, and tries to identify it but to no avail, the rest help and realise that it is a scale, which Nyktos' pockets because it's pretty. Tiny and Lucatiel are trying out the door, with Lucatiel protecting themselves in a Resilient Sphere whilst Elisa and Nyktos stand about twenty feet back. Two Sea Serpents rush out from under them, trying to eat the party. Tiny realises that the chains around the Serpents are the cause of their charm, he tries to break them succeeding on one whilst the rest of the party is hanging on by a thread. Fortunately, Tiny manages to break the second one as well, freeing Slyrax and Zafira from their enslavement. As a show of their gratitude, the two provide the party with some items. The party takes a short rest before making their way into what looks like the hideout for the Cult of the Crushing Wave. 

  
  

They pass through and eventually come face to face with the Water Genasi they have to kill, as well as a Genie. The party are hesitant to start a fight, speaking with the water genasi who provides them with more information on the Half Orc that barged into their home, painting him as the evil enforcer of a terrible cult leader. The party hesitates, slightly confused on who the true bad guy is. Though they do get to inquire about Aslaug, Serissa's attendant, who looks at the party with disgust. The water genasi even offers the party the chance to speak with the attendant, who is charmed to speak truthfully and utters how her blatant disgust of the party. The party are stuck, though most of them are still suspicious of the people before them. Elisa casts detect thoughts, trying to read the minds of their henchmen as Lucatiel and Nyktos continue to read the face of the water genasi. Elisa questions them, asking why they are dressed like the water cult people if they are not them and the henchmen are proud, praising their boss for every lie he comes up with. Nyktos spots a lie and immediately casts Sunbeam alongside Lucatiel who casts a spell that gets counterspelled. The party launches into battle, easily taking out the water cult members, they almost take down the genie as well, but he manages to escape before they can do anything. With that all done, Elisa heads to loot to corpses, finding a letter from someone with a very long name, Pisces. The letter outlines how there are to be no more slaves, but instead minimal wage workers and how they know that the water genasi was from the water cult and his status' have been removed. 

  

Nyktos and Lucatiel head to the chest, finding a thousand gold that they both agree to split 50-50 at Lucatiel's suggestion. Nyktos also takes the three tridents and shares it with Tiny, who asks what was inside the chest, and gets directed to Lucatiel. They then pester Lucatiel who gives them 100 gold and informs them that Nyktos has 500 gold. When Tiny confronts Nyktos about it, she insists that the 500 gold she had was from other sources and swims off whilst Elisa distracts Tiny with her talk of trade. Nyktos rushes to Lucatiel's side to inquire about her giving her up to Tiny so readily. Lucatiel cowers, pulling out 50 gold to appease Nyktos who graciously only takes 10 gold and Lucatiel expresses her gratitude, happy that Nyktos is so kind. The party frees the attendant and they all make their way back up to Serissa who is extremely grateful towards the party, the party ignores the disgusted look the attendant continues to give them. The topic of Serissa's pregnancy is brought up, and the party offers kind words, affirming that they believe Serissa will be a good mother. Uthor becomes uncomfortable at the mention of Serissa's pregnancy and is quick to usher her away. Serissa leaves the party with some rewards for their help in bringing her attendant back.

  

The party hits back to their settlement, giving Go'l the head of the water genasi that he had wanted. He looks at the head and his hammer, saying "He killed you so he deserved it," before taking it and leaving. Tiny tries to ask Ront and Grud about what Go'l had said about blood. And the two share that Go'l says that that's for the god chief to decide. 

  

Having encountered the water cult that her friend had told her about, Elisa speaks to Nyssa. Nyssa provide some exposition about Go'l and of what happened in Oceanus. The party learns of how he had lost his sister and the manipulation he had suffered. The party also shares about the Giant Queen they had met, and learns the circumstances of her pregnancy. When asked if Nyssa or Lariet could help be by Serissa's side, Nyssa shares that both her and her friend are busy. And that her friend was now busy being Queen. Nyssa also shares the functions of the god shards and how they have to gather all of them to eventually defeat the Moon. The topic of Nyssa's volcanic home comes up as she invites the party to come visit her someday but warns that it would be pretty hot. The tone shifts as they begin planning a potential future visit. They ask about the lava and if there are sharks in the lava, which unfortunately Nyssa says that there are. They also ask her about a budding Kingdom in the North and she affirms that she's heard of it, and that they have artisans there.
