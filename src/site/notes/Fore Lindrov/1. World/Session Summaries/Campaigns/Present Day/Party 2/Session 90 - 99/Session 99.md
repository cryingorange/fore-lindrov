# Dragon Cultists
## Undead Ambush
Stuck in the snow, the party manages their way out only to be attacked by an undead Frost Giant accompanied by more smaller undead. During combat, Ivy spots a creature under the rubble of a tent, prompting her Roast to try and pull the creature out, instead, their advances are interrupted by an owl pecking at the debris of the tent, whom creepily tells Roast, after casting Speak With Animals, to kill whatever is beneath the debris. 

## Carbon The Kobold
The party kills the Undead and reveals a small Black Kobold, who appears to be freezing to death, with injuries and a strange frost covering his body. Upon an assessment, they conclude that Carbon is suffering not only from Hypothermia, but rather some kind of Curse, the frost indiciating that Carbon is literally turning into Ice, with Elisa likening it to Sangzor's Salt-Petrification. Ivy views this as a Curse by Nature itself, and Freck and Roast see this as a form of Moon-Scorching, meanwhile Grimrid just concludes that Carbon is Cold. They also remember that the corpses also appeared like they were turned into Ice.

Just as the party wonders how they would escape the Blizzard that has overcome the area to warm Carbon up enough to speak to him, Freck pulls out a deck of cards that transforms into a warm shelter, providing the party with a much needed reprieve from the cold.

They manage to warm Carbon enough that he can speak, and he reveals that his friends were kidnapped by bears, big ones that took them away. Elisa realises that Carbon and friends are the allies Iymrith spoke of, getting directions from Carbon as the party travels to where Carbon's friends were. 

## Tribe Of The Bear
They fight the were polar bears who captured the dragon cultists, who claim themselves to be the Tribe Of The Bear, citing that all that in this land is for them to eat and consume. The party swiftly defeats these savage Werepolarbears, rescuing the freezing dragonborns, Verdantvainglory and Sulphice.

## Verdantvainglory & Sulphice
Verdantvainglory's habit of speaking to himself annoys Elisa whilst Ivy kindly helps untie the two people. Elisa does react more kindly to Sulphice's behaviour but does label the dragonborns as the dumber of their party. From the two, the party learns of the bad feeling the location brings, and that Sansuri's Castle is rumoured to be floating in the area. Albeit they've had little success in finding more information as there is megafauna here that constantly tries to kill them, although they *just* discovered some locals that carry the heraldry of a golden cross with brass borders, and surmise that if these people are intelligent enough to have Heraldry, then at the very least they'll be able to . They are provided directions to the place as well as words of caution to hurry as the castle may leave at any time.

# Uthgardt
## Preparation
The party opts to first return to the House of Cards, as Freck has to retrieve his house before they can leave, providing Verdant and Sulphice much comfort that they will not fall to the snow once more.
Before they leave, Roast hands Freck a spell scroll, complimenting him on his skills with the house.

## Journey

The party begins their journey, trudging through cold and lack of rest to quicken their pace. Eventually, they are forced to rest, too tired and weak to press on. Ivy offers Grimrid her bedroll as Roast gives in to fate, pushing herself as far as she can into the furthest wall of the cave as she can when Freck kindly lends her an extra he had. During their rest, only Elisa, Zipzap, and the Deer are able to find uninterrupted respite. As Ivy, Roast, Freck, and Grimrid find themselves in a nightmare.

The nightmare starts with an avalanche, which each of them trying to defeat the obstacle in some way or another. Grimrid chooses to take it headon. Ivy transforms into a bird but fails to fly. One by one the party fails to defeat the nightmare, instead receiving a warning that tells them to leave. Waking up, they realised that most of them have had this weird dream, chalking up to magics or something else that is affecting them so, all except for Elisa.

Soon the party arrives, first seeing signs of a dead Yeti and the head of a Frost Giant. Roast notices a shine in the eye, plucking it out because it looks pretty and has magic within it. Heading closer, Elisa pulls out her standard and is eventually met with the leader of the people, an Uthgardt named Lyanna, who does not believe that Elisa is a knight. She quizzes Elisa on the King name and eventually, the party are given permission to enter.
