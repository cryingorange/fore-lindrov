The heroes have their items taken from them, cuffed and forced into tiny rooms made of ice. Before being thrown in, Lucatiel manages to charm the guard, planting the idea of setting Lucatiel free into their mind. Having done that, Lucatiel rests, their small stature allows them more space in the cube. Unable to do much with their arms bound behind their back, Lucatiel rests on the ice. Though the individuals with more body parts are less fortunate than their small friend. Nyktos, Tiny, and Vaghar find themselves in extremely cramped situations. The dragon uses their breath, keeping themselves somewhat warm despite their surroundings. Meanwhile, the half-spider elf contorts her body as much as she is able to, barely keeping her limbs from touching the frigid walls around her.  
  
Meanwhile, Elisa, uses her psionics to reach out to friends. She calls out to Iymrith, informing her of the situation and seeking her help. Though Iymrith seems…preoccupied, the dragon weirdly does not take initiative to even cast a sending out to her captured friends. She tells Elisa to escape, informing her of where to escape to. Iymrith is busy, attending to something the party presently is unable to help with. The party’s first night in their prisons is uncomfortable, the cold icy beds, the lack of light and space. Still, they just about manage. 

  

The second day is not any better. Being trapped isn’t great, being trapped whilst being denied food and water, is worse. Surrounded with nothing but ice, the party has to make do. Tiny and Vaghar use their fire breath, slowly melting the ice as they lap up the waters. Lucatiel does something similar, channelling their magic to the best of their abilities to cast fire at the wall despite their limited range of movements. Elisa harnesses her powers, first communicating with ZipZap, learning some vital information from the outside where he and Crimson are kept.  
  
Though the information is limited, as ZipZap’s tendencies for rambling doesn’t lend well to the sending spell. Still, Elisa manages to learn where the party’s items are kept. She then channels her psionics to break off pieces of the walls, eating the ice as a means to get water. Nyktos tries a couple of things, attempting to reach out to her people, hoping that they would be able to help. But that is harder than it seems and she eventually resorts to licking the ice, barely managing to quench her thirst. 

  

The next night doesn't pass peacefully. As the party members hear Loog’s whimpers from just outside their prisons. He’s not doing too well, having broken both his arms and left to rot in his injured state. Their captors don’t take well to Loog’s whinings, as one of them stomps up to him, demanding he keep quiet. Though it’s hard to do so, Tiny calls out, trying to sooth their friend despite Vaghar’s disapproval. Nyktos sends her magic out beyond the walls, focusing on what little she could to artificially calm Loog down, lowering his heart rate and stopping his whimpers to satisfy their captors. Fortunately, it’s enough and they leave Loog alone for now. 

  

Time starts to blur, the party does what they can to live through each moment. And a new torture presents itself. Stuck helplessly behind ice, the party hears distinct cries. Screams. Severin, wailing from pain, horror, and who knows what else. Elisa learns from ZipZap that Jarl Storvald has emerged from one of the chambers with a pair of red wings. The party has a choice to make, ignore the screams or don’t. Elisa and Lucatiel ignore them, knowing that they can’t help, that should they want to escape, they would need every ounce of their strength. Nyktos tries, though even in her exhausted state, her perceptive nature persists. Tiny listens, not wanting Severin to suffer alone. 

  

Some time later, the doors to their cells open. The first light the party has seen for a while. Before them is Morti and Brother Boog. The necromancer is different from last they met, an empty, broken look in his eyes. He does what is required of him, unlocking the doors. The party also sees Loog, his condition much worse than before. His two owlbears remain by his side, but the Hill Giant is on the cusp of death. Reading the room, it’s also evident that the Hill Giants have joined the Frost Giants, most of them bearing injuries, likely having been beaten into submission.  
  
They are then led to a different room, bigger than before. Along the way, Brother Boog pushes Nyktos, causing her to fall and also causing Crimson to bark. The poor pup has been incredibly anxious, separated from his friends and for the first sight of them to be this. Crimson’s barks have a Frost Giant annoyed, as he picks up the cage housing Crimson and ZipZap and shakes it roughly, knocking the pup out from sheer blunt force trauma. And despite the anger, Nyktos can’t do anything. Powerless, unable to wield her blood magic to end the life of the Giant. Instead she links herself to the Giant, silently promising to kill him. Elisa takes the opportunity to cast an Arcane eye, letting it roam outside. 

  

Morti unlocks the giant door, whilst Brother Boog pushes it open for the party to enter. There, a group of Goblins rest. With the party’s arrival, they immediately pull what little food they have to themselves, not wanting to share with the people they dislike. But one of the Goblins takes pity, repaying the party for the kindness they had shown him by tossing scraps of food for the party. The party each heads to one corner to rest.  
  
Lucatiel finds a spot where they can’t be seen by the guards, subtly trying to melt parts of the ice as they speak to the Goblins. The Goblins aren’t particularly welcoming but slowly warm up to Lucatiel. Meanwhile, Elisa has her Arcane Eye look about, coming into a room where she finds a tortured Parsley, his wings torn off, his arms frozen solid. And to make matters worse, she finds frozen heads, seven to be exact, of Sandy. The Goose Hydra was repeatedly decapitated before Parsley’s very eyes. The once lively and confident fey, now reduced to not much.  
  
There’s one room of note that Elisa sends her Arcane Eye to spy, though before she can continue, Jarl Storvald emerges. Hvitserk & Ubbe return to the cave, carrying along a living Frost Salamander. Jarl Storvald praises them, as they mention something about bringing the Salamander rather than extra guards. 

  

Jarl Storvald takes his time to survey the party, his gaze lingering on Elisa for a moment more. Nyktos notices this, soon realising that Elisa is doing something, though unsure what exactly. The Jarl offers his ‘hospitality Eonce more, letting the party choose which meal they wish to hunt for  ELoog’s owlbears or the Frost Salamander. The party remains silent, each leaning towards an option in their mind.  
  
Lucatiel observes their party members, figuring out what they would pick and bravely speaks up, choosing the Salamander. The party are given four daggers to equip their hunt. The Frost Salamander speaks in Primordial, essentially saying that they do not intend to die by the party’s hand, that they will survive. And true to their word, the Frost Salamander fights well, constantly burrowing into the ground, hiding from the Tiny as they unleash a freezing breath on the party, knocking both Elisa and ZipZap down. With one of the party members close to death, the Salamander rushes up to Elisa, attacking her mercilessly and just before the last strike hits, Lucatiel calls out to Jarl Storvald, seeking his help. Which he gives, all too readily. Looking at the others in the room, Storvald has Lucatiel choose someone. Not knowing what exactly that could mean, the Myconid picks the safest options; Themselves.

  

Storvald nods, picking up a dagger and handing it to Elisa, telling her to kill Lucatiel, a life for a life. Obviously, the party doesn’t want that. And they somehow manage to delay that, as Storvald picks them up, confidently placing them on his armour, leaving his neck exposed to them. He brings the party to the room Elisa had wanted to look into. Along the way, Nyktos tells Lucatiel to not do that again, to not offer their life.

  

Severin lays in the room, alive but severely beaten and bruised, chains dug into his skin, his wings gone. Storvald leans towards the young dragon and asks him how he’d like to die. Severin is silent, a question like that is hard to answer. The party doesn't want Severin to die either. Elisa can see Severin thinking, reading his mind as she realises that he’s trying to make his death as entertaining as possible so that everyone else can still have their chance. She tries to delay his response, assuring him that they will find another way. And the party tries. Thinking up what they can, offering alternatives, attacking Storvald out of frustration and rage. But nothing works, if anything Storvald only grows bolder. 

  

Everything proves Storvald’s beliefs right. That despite what many have said, clerics who swore that gods would strike him down. No one has. And maybe, no one can. His arrogance only grows as he grabs onto Tiny, slicing off his arm. Then squeezing him as he breaks a leg. And before the party can do anything, Lucatiel offers themselves up again, ready to sacrifice themselves if it means that everyone else could gain another day. Storvald lets Tiny go, picking up Lucatiel instead, and tells Tiny to cut off Lucatiel’s head. Tiny does not respond, the responsibility is far too much for one to bear. And just as Storvald is about to do something, Nyktos calls out, offering to take on the guilt, claiming that they are one party.  
  
Storvald pretends to think, before dismissing the idea entirely, wanting only Tiny to be the one to do it. Elisa bargains, using what she knows Storvald is looking for, lying that the item he seeks is in Larryton. Storvald thanks her for the information, and affirms that he already knows the Ice Queen has what he seeks. With no more tricks up their sleeve, the party has no way to save anyone. Tiny calls out, steeling their nerves but before they can, Storvald plucks off Lucatiel’s head, ending their life just like that. Storvald drops the Myconid’s remains on the ground, seemingly bored of the whole affair. 

  

The party remains silent, slowly coming to terms with what has happened. Tiny grabs Lucatiel’s head, bringing their friend along with them. Nyktos pats Lucatiel, softly muttering that she told him not to. Having lost their friend, the party shamefully walks back to their prison, everyone witnessing their loss.
