As the party is finished helping Iymrith get food over to a small contingent of Dragons, they continue on their quest to help Vanifer find the lost child of fire. They find themselves in a small settlement where they meet Liam. The unofficial "Mayor" of the small village, Liam reveals that the settlement is under siege and threatened by a group of bandits. They are being forced to pay 15000gp or the bandits will attack the settlement. The party also learns that the settlement is set on a barren part of the Underdark where no food grows. They're only able to get by via trading gold for food and supplies from a Deep Gnome city called Blingdenstone. Liam takes the party to where the Bandits were last seen and takes his leave. It is there that the party meet The Fearsome Four, Garth, Petunia, Dill and Karick. 4 Kids that see themselves as adventures and whose leader, Garth, reveals that they know the location of the bandits, having followed them during their last visit.

They offer to help the party track the bandits but only if they help arm the Fearsome Four, something that the party begrudgingly agrees to. The party follow the kids to an old abandoned fort, where after swiftly defeating a group of Black Oozes through some Vampiric teleportation from Brom and a heavy Fireball from Aurelion , the party is able to quickly arm the Fearsome Four and head back to the settlement with weapons and many flasks of Oil. But it is discovered that the settlement was attacked by the bandits, someone having been disintegrated by a rotting eyestalk held by one of the Deep Gnomes. The kids, absolutely enraged, immediately lead the party to the hideout. Saying that the bandits have to be killed, something that worries the party. The party attempts to speak to the kids, telling them to stay calm and not to act rashly. Something they're unsure whether or not the kids will listen to.

Eventually the party find themselves at the bandit hideout and avoid an ambush by the Deep Gnomes thanks to Elisa giving Petunia Illythyrra's old Dagger of Warning. The party chase down the bandits through their trapped hideout and discover that 7 - 10 Deep Gnomes from the City of Blingdenstone are the culprits of the horrific attack. Having acquired a rotting Beholder's Eyestalk, the party manages to defeat the bandits before the Leader is able to get his Disintegration Ray working, alongside the kids helping to throw oil at the bandits.
However, during the interrogation where one of the Deep Gnomes reveals something cryptic about Blingdenstone, saying that those who live there are no true Gnomes and will lead everyone to ruin. Also stating that they were just trying to run from the Eye Monger and that they simply needed a head start to maybe start a new settlement. The party also learns of someone named, "Peebles" who is the supposed doctor of the Eye Monger. However just as the party is prepared to let them go, the bandits are set aflame by none other than Dill. Who is revealed to have a psychotic love for fire after having seen Aurelion use fireball.

The party morbidly take the kids back, retrieving the Dagger of Warning and revealing to Dill's parents the dangers he might pose should he not be brought to Vanifer. After some swift convincing from both Brom and Elisa , the parents finally relent and follow the party out. In the meantime, Liam rewards the party with 1000gp and also tells them that the settlement simply trades with Blingdenstone, and that the Deep Gnomes stumbled upon their settlement rather than them seeking them out, and they know nothing else other than the meeting point where they trade with one another. Brom tells Liam to be careful when dealing with Blingdenstone, saying there is something more happening around that city.

The party then take their leave and travel back to Vanifer in order to bring Dill back. However, on the way, the party ambushes a group of Ghouls. Now no longer sporting primitive bone armour and mindless zombies, these Ghouls are well-armed, organised and even have cavalry. The party manage to defeat the Vanguard as Elisa summons a bear from her Bag of Tricks to protect Dill and his family while they confront the Ghouls desecrating Amunkhet 's ancient tomb. Entering the Tomb, they realise that the focus of this dungeon seems to be that of kindness and forgiveness, with statues and imagery of Geb laden throughout the walls. Something that brings Amunkhet quite a bit of nostalgia.

The party then run into Vulcan, who despite his narrow-minded focus of Dragon Slaying is unusually concerned with the desecration of the Dead. Breaking through into the final chamber, the party are confronted by Captain Matrevosk, accompanied by Elemental Ghuls. Captain Matrevosk reveals he serves the White Pale, The Emperor of the Imperium. To which Elisa 's only response is, "Tell the Emperor to suck my dick." Thus triggering combat, with Vulcan immediately conjuring a Mummy exactly like Amunkhet. The party managed to defeat Matrevosk, acquiring his Longsword, a Chalice and a Magic Candle. Vulcan takes his leave and tells Amunkhet she is a good ruler. With Elisa going outside to confront him about the knowledge she learned regarding his relation to Amunkhet thanks to her Detect Thoughts. He simply acknowledges her concerns, but takes his leave anyway.

The party then head back to Vanifer where she takes in Dill and his family, with Vizeran annoyedly teleporting the party to Karaz Andor to head to Mantol-Derith after they tell him that they failed using the Teleport Scroll he gave them. Elisa immediately goes to meet up with Holly and Nari at the Inn with the rest of the party soon catching up. Elisa tells Holly to erase her memories of something and Amunkhet swiftly confronts her about why she's been acting strange. Holly decides not to get involved and takes Nari with her, with Elisa avoiding this conversation by immediately undertaking their quest to Mantol-Derith.

The party meet up with Galnom and a small convoy of Duergar headed to the Trading Outpost where, upon arrival, they discover that the Duergar and Deep Gnomes are at war. Something having been stolen from the Duergar, the Duergar have set a fullscale attack against the Deep Gnomes, killing any that they see indiscriminately. As Galnom and the rest of the convoy go to speak to those in the Duergar Enclave, the party manage to convince 2 Duergar to stand down from killing 2 Deep Gnomes, as well as rescuing one stuck under a destroyed stall in the market place named Peebles. Who the party is unable to really question due to the pressing matter of a war happening around them. They follow the other 2 Deep Gnomes to the Deep Gnome Enclave where they see a contingent of Enlarged Duergar attempting to smash down the door to the Deep Gnome Enclave. The party decide it might be best to speak to the Duergar Leadership rather than try to ask the soldiers to stand down and head back to the Duergar Enclave where they discover the Enclave's Leader, Ghuldur, and the Duergar who had his Black Opal stolen from him, Krimgol, torturing and interrogating a Deep Gnome Mage named Yantha.

The party manage to get Krimgol and Ghuldur to stand down and allow them to speak to Yantha, with the 2 Duergar relenting and Ghuldur calling his pet Xorn off of her. The party cut Yantha down and are able to discern that she is being magically compelled to lie. Aurelion casts Greater Restoration and Yantha is restored, saying that she acquired a Black Opal from Krimgol, who wanted it appraised and for some reason she told him it was worth nothing and gave it to her assistant Flink. Who, upon her casting Sending, learns he is trapped in the Drow Enclave. 

The party informs Krimgol and Ghuldur of this, and they agree to temporarily stop the assault, however they will continue to hold Yantha and be ready to slaughter the Deep Gnomes until the Opal is returned. The party then head to the Drow Enclave and spot Flink, hiding beneath a guard tower in the grove of the Drow Enclave. As Aurelion produces a distraction, talking and increasingly annoying the Driders that stand guard. The party tosses a rope to Flink and he refuses to climb. Eventually, Elisa is forced to communicate and convince him to come out of the Guard Tower as a spider. 

The party form a plan to have Amunkhet cast Disguise Self on herself as a Drow then the rest of the party polymorph themselves into spiders to hide inside of a jar to infiltrate the Enclave. The party manage to bring Flink out thanks to a distraction provided by the Animals in the Bag of Tricks from Elisa and tell Flink to wait for them as they enter the Enclave. They find a secret entrance at the base of the Statue of Lolth and Amunkhet manages to stave off a curious Drow after appearing slightly suspicious, thanks to her quick thinking of flexing her Matriarchal Authority against a curious Male.

Entering the secret room, they discover the informant and traitor is none other than Adira. Who informs them that their encounter with her made her want to make a difference with the Drow. She also reveals more about Vizeran, that he is from a destroyed minor house named the DeVirs. They give Adira the stone from Vizeran and she passes them a note t o give to Vizeran himself. The party then head over to the Brightmires after learning from Flink that the opal was stolen by a Drow who is now in the Brightmire Enclave.The party convince the maddened Flink, who seems unusually terrified of his mistress Yantha and believes she will kill him if he does not get her spell components and the opal to wait for them at the market stalls. The party heads to the Brightmire Enclave where Amunkhet feels a painful familiarity with a Black Opal around one of the Drows' necks. The party realise they've stumbled upon an alliance meeting, where it is revealed that a Vizier named Ghazrim DuLoc from The Order of the Bright is in charge, that something called "The White Order" exists within Fore Lindrov.

They also see that DuLoc controls a maimed Beholder, with Brom positing if Ghazrim could be the Eye Monger. However before they can do anything else, the crazed Drow attacks the Brightmires, with Amunkhet taking this opportunity to take the Opal, a fight is triggered. Ending in the Vizier psionically compelling both Amunkhet and Aurelion to stand down, and the Inquisitors, armed, emotionless gunmen taking down Elisa and Brom begrudgingly standing down.

DuLoc is able to immediately discern Amunkhet is undead, and apologises to a panicked Elisa after she was shot by the inquisitors. Brom bridling with Rage manages to resist all of the Vizier's attempts to magically overtake him and Amunkhet tells the Vizier that she represents the Duergar, sparking his interest in the opal, the party convinces him to allow them to keep it, and to tell the Duergar that it was thanks to the Brightmires that the Opal returned to their hands. 

The Brightmires then agree to an alliance with the Drow in exchange for magic and information and the party returns to the Duergar with the cursed gem, where is is revealed to Amunkhet that her brother, Geb, is trapped within. Amunkhet and the party tests the madness-inducing effects of the gem and Elisa is accidentally turned mad, wishing only to kill Party One and go on her quest of vengeance. The party hogtie her as an example to show the Duergar of the Opal's maddening effects and spend the next few days using Greater Restoration to heal the afflicted and tell the Duergar about the Brightmire's "Good Will". The party then head back and bid Galnom goodbye, travelling back to the Tower, their journey is cut short by horrible screaming and yelling coming from off in the distance. They find a settlement having been beset by "Flying Death" and wild fauna now trying to catch easy meals. The party managed to save quite a number of civilians, losing many others and barely managing to save a child as they heard thunderous chaos happening further down.

Aurelion, concerned for a child's safety tells them to stay in whatever house they were hiding in and not to come out. The party then head to the source of the chaos, helping injured settlers in another settlement who reveal that a Black Warrior fought the Flying Death. The party rushes forward on animals, becoming stunned by deafening blows of lightning coming from the distance. Eventually they discover the source of all the destruction, the Dracolich Ardor Mor, having captured and defeated Vulcan. Who turns out to be Amenhotep, the elder brother of Amunkhet who abdicated the throne of Urshu. 

Amunkhet springs into battle with the party, but they swiftly learn they are completely outmatched by the Dracolich. Ardor Mor kills Vulcan and buries his body after bringing Amunkhet to unconsciousness. The party surrender to him and are brought to his lair, where they drink a strange concoction, losing vision in their left eyes and gaining searing marks on their left index fingers. Amunkhet and Brom continue to defy Ardor Mor, with Brom losing an arm and Aurelion alongside a armless Brom managing to convince Amunkhet not to risk her life, but to remember that she must save her brother Geb from the gem as well as Amenhotep. Amunkhet 's obedience is then bound to the party's enchantments, with Ardor Mor saying that should she defy him, he will kill the entire party and parade her body to Vulcan.

As recompense for their early defiance, Ardor Mor has the party acquire an item for him, an Insignia of Claws, he gives the party no time for rest and they are forced to head to a Dungeon where they see signs of Vampires within an ancient Dragon Tomb. The party head inside, making their way past zombified Kobolds and Half-Dragons only to meet Felix Vrykolas of House Assamite. Brom goes into a rage, challenging the Vampire to a duel, becoming swiftly defeated, the party then are forced to give up many of their magic items, Elisa 's Scroll of Nature's Wrath and Ice Storm, as well as the Chalice Brom acquired from the Ghouls. Vrykolas accepts this trade and gives the party the Insignia, Brom attempts to curry some favour from Vrykolas, asking him if Clan Assamite is recruiting, with Vrykolas rebuffing him as "Useless". 

The party then go back to Ardor Mor, with him rewarding the party with a Ring of Mind Shielding and a Hat of Disguise. The party then head back to the Tower, where they realise that they don't want Ardor Mor watching them, they formulate a plan to have Amunkhet enter the Tower during the night, to which she meets Vizeran who, seeing her strange behaviour reveals there are Divination Wards in the Tower. She reveals that the party managed to come into the service of a Dracolich, something that Vizeran is thoroughly flabbergasted at their level of incompetence. He reveals that in order to prevent the Dracolich from investigating, they didn't need to hide the cult from him. Simply tell him the cult is full of crazed fire worshippers led by a 16 year old girl. It is extremely uninteresting to a Dracolich.
