# The Storm King

The party surprise Hekaton, who expresses his shock at finding Smallfolk on the island, attacking him and his Personal Bodyguard to save Iymrith. Hekaton conjures a great Thunderstorm above, bringing down acid rain and lightning down on the island as the PCs thus find themselves in an extremely challenging position, with Lu almost immediately struck down by the Storm King's Thunder.

Ivy tries to heal Lu, bringing him back from the brink of death.. However, something goes wrong and Lu's body begins to morph and twist, bark begins to replace his skin and his limbs begin to turn to roots. He begins to panic, looking to Ivy for guidance only to find the Tree Spirit equally confused as she begins to question her powers, unsure if this bark is a protection or a curse. 

Meanwhile, Roast manages to grab hold of Hekaton, conjuring fleshy tendrils to hold him back from finishing off the rest of her Party Members. Elisa, rising from the waters that she was pulled under by Uthara blasts a powerful psionic pulse of energy at the Storm Giant Royal Guard, ripping her apart, and Uthara, Hekaton's and Uthor's sister turns to her brother in fear, before being turned to ash by Elisa, horrifying the Storm King, and thus kickstarting his hatred and fear of Smallfolk.  

The Storm King escapes via casting Time Stop, having walked to Uthara’s corpse to mourn her, painfully unable to interact with the actual world lest the spell's effects fail. As the dust settles, Iymrith lets out a poignant cry before dragging her children away.

# Iymrith's Past

The scene cuts to Iymrith in the desert, having limply flown her way back to the area she lives in, and oddly running away from something. Upon closer inspection the PCs soon realise that she's being chased by massive Desert Wurms, seeing the corpses of her Children as easy carrion to consume. But Iymrith, rather than lose the headless bodies of her children, rips off large chunks of herself, her wings, her flesh to toss to these Wurms to enable her to escape. 

The scene cuts away to Iymrith in a stone chamber with three Crypts, which Elisa recognizes as the same Crypt that she defeated the risen corpse of one of Iymrith's children.

It cuts away to another scene of Iymrith in her lair, as she stares deeply at a bubbling cauldron of Purple Worm Poison.. Just as she prepares herself to drink it, she hears strange voices from a mirror connected to one of her many Gargoyles in the Underdark. 

A young girl playfully insisting to her party that the Gargoyles give items, cutely raising her arms up at the Gargoyles.. Reminding Iymrith of her playful children, Elisa reaches up, waiting for an item from Iymrith's Gargoyle. Iymrith sets down the poison, seemingly deciding against ending her own life, and gives a vial of her own blood to Elisa, a potent magical item. 

The scene then cuts again, to when Elisa and her newer party member Nyktos met Iymrith in her buried amphitheatre lair, and the regenerative Spell Gem that they gave her to restore her limbs to her.. However, the never healing scar left behind by the Storm King Hekaton on Iymrith's chest remains unchanged.  

# Return to Reality

## Lu's 'Death'

As they are about to carry on, Lu finds his tree condition worsening, panicking as he loses control of his limbs. The others try to help but none seem to be able to break him out of his condition. Amongst the horrid panic of everyone, attempts to break off the bark only hurts Lu further. At some point, Lu finds peace, realising that the panic would not behoove the party, he begins to accept his fate. Assuring them that he is alright as he fully transforms into a tree and dies. Assuring Ivy that it's not her fault as the bark consumes him completely. 

The party is then brought back to reality, with Lu completely fine, back into the room with the giant door and two orbs as they come back to their senses. Ivy begins to panic about her situation, unable to trust herself and her powers as she panics about the many she’s healed as she did with Lu. Except in all of those other cases, she never stayed long enough to witness the aftermath of her healing. She loses control, letting out a wave of energy that hurts the party as Roast approaches her friend, trying to bring Ivy back to reality. Ultimately Ivy does calm down but her cautiousness of her powers only grows.

## Orbs

Grimrid breaks open the two orbs that were used to send them back in time and witness the past, finding a Stormgirdle and Wulfgrim’s Axe. Grimrid finds himself instantly attuned to the axe, his bond with Wulfgrim helping in the connection to his weapon. 

# Giant Treasure Room

## The Drow

Having collected their items, the party continues on to the next room, disguised as a group of drow warriors. With Roast as their mouthpiece, the party is forced to interact with Xullana Mizzrym, who has snuck in to see her mother, the neglected child ever so eager to assert herself over others thanks to her Noble Birth, insults the entire party, even the very guards who defend her life. As Roast speaks back to Xullana, the Drow guards protecting Xullana give her looks to encourage her not to draw Xullana's ire, especially in light of her Noble Birth and of who her mother is.

Xullana even declares that she will take Zekerin, the low-born, other, young entourage of Veszika as her consort, revealing her twisted crush on the young boy. Elisa, tired of this farce casts [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/6th Level/Mass Suggestion\|Mass Suggestion]] on the children, forcing them to say that all drow deserve to die repeatedly and springs an ambush on the guards while they're confused, very swiftly defeating them. 

## Zafir Al-Harid

The party looks through the chests, one of which they suspect used to contain the energy cell they party now has and the other has a bottle of a fire person. The party guesses that it could be a potion of some kind, both Elisa and Roast wanting to open and try the contents, though Ivy prevents them from doing so. Eventually, Lu opens the bottle and is blasted backwards from an immense release of heat from the bottle. 

A genie exits the bottle, introducing himself as Zafir Al-Harid, now ascended to [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/NPCs/Zafir Al-Harid'akthar\|Zafir Al-Harid'akthar]] since his ascension into a True Genie, explaining that he is a follower of [[Fore Lindrov/1. World/Religions/Vataqatal\|Vataqatal]]. Grimrid recognises this Genie, pointing out that they had met before. But Zafir mentions he does not remember Grimrid, dismissing him as an unimportant, not outstanding individual. 

Grimrid then heads to the corner, not wanting to listen to the genie’s preaching. As the rest speak with Zafir, Elisa learns that he is belives suffering is the way of the world, and that slavery is a necessary evil that helps individuals grow through suffering. Disgusted, Elisa leaves and joins Grimrid. 

Zafir asks for the others to share their suffering, in exchange for a chance to get a wish. Lu shares of the most recent suffering he had gone through, the death of a close friend, where he chose his own morals over her life. An action that he’s regretted since. And bringing her back would still not alleviate his suffering, knowing that when push came to shove, he chose himself. 

Zafir grants Lu’s wish, bringing back Nyktos as the genie does not realise that he’s opened a connection to the dread realm. The party is hurt and as they return, Lu’s arm is gone, his price to pay for bringing Nyktos back to life. The party then shoos the genie away, resealing him in his Bottle and taking him with them, pressing onto the next room where they face Shriala and her minions. 

# Shriala Mizzrym

## Vicintra & Shriala's Deaths

During which, the drow are performing a sacrifice to Lolth, causing an aspect of the Goddess to manifest through the silken statue. Elisa hears Lolth’s words to Shriala, that through her, her most blessed servant will rise. Grimrid is also controlled by Lolth, forced to kill Vicintra just as Jorlan shows up to see his closest friend killed. In anguish, Jorlan picks up the Silver Wraith to start fighting the drow. The party is in a bad state and for a moment, Grimrid agonizes over not having his proper equipment, he hears Elimina’s influence once more, taunting him as he fights against his deal with Elmina as the gauntlets given to him by the Duergar transform, the once blocky stone now replaced by a refined gold with symbology of Grimrid’s old friends, Spoon, Derrick, Verena, and Tormund and Lu eventually lands the killing blow on Shriala with Elisa eating her soul and branding it with Laura’s name. 


# Lolth Comes

Jorlan rushes over to Vicintra’s corpse, dropping to one knee as he mourns the loss as Lolth begins to hover over Jorlan, her hands weaving web directly into his mind, whispering horrible things to him, that everything he touches will turn to ash, that there is only Lolth, and that she is the only person who truly loves him. 

Lu tries to approach but gets turned into a spider as the party tries to intervene, originally Lolth deemed them unworthy of attention, but once they do enough to draw her ire, she first threatens to unmake Ivy, telling Grimrid that his god is but a servant to fiends, and eventually, the party believing they cannot beat Lolth are forced to allow Jorlan to fall under Lolth's thumb, his first act as her champion being to kill both Zekerin and Xullana. Grimrid tells Lu that they must bear witness to the moment, to remember those they failed to save. And with that, Lolth leaves with Jorlan. 

# Heart of Heaven and Earth

The party picks themselves up, with Lu heading to the other room with items relating to the Hua Jia. On the wall are paintings, depicting the tale of a group of five animal-esque Giants, a Bull, Lion, Fox and Scorpion, each of them being brought to heel and becoming friends via a duel by one Monkey-esque Stone Giant. The final painting depicts the five of them atop a hand with a singular eye, signalling that whoever these individuals are, they know of the Eye of the Allfather where the Oracle is, the key to finding Serissa. 

Elisa ignores the paintings, gunning for the chest which when opened, the party finds a Stone Heart that smells of earth and clay. Written on the heart is a cypher. The stone heart speaks telepathically, as Lu entertains it the most. According to the heart, its "brothers and sisters", known as the Five Heavenly Treasures were scattered far and wide by the King Hekaton. The Heart of Heaven and Earth is also a magic item that one could attune to, so Lu does, gaining access to the heart’s abilities.

# Leaving Xunbelaern

The party then has to decide how to leave the drow camp and save all the remaining slaves. Grimrid suggests taking them, confident as ever in his abilities to destroy their enemies, despite his current state. Elisa joins in, also wanting to fight the drow. Roast is hesitant, unsure that their numbers would be strong enough to ensure no further casualties. Grimrid does suggest a smarter plan, luring in groups of drow at a time and taking them down. Elisa agrees, surprised by the sudden ingenuity. 

Meanwhile, Roast approaches Ivy, asking for her opinion on the situation, hoping that she too would see to reason. When it is noted that Ivy still has magic left, Roast asks if Ivy would help provide some healing and as Ivy shares her discomfort, Roast is confident that her body would not succumb to external influences as Lu’s did, confident in herself and Ivy’s magic. But Roast also respects Ivy’s discomfort, telling them that perhaps in combat if it comes to. 

Elisa and Grimrid continue their agreement to fight the drow, when Grimrid suggests to Elisa that Roast tell the drow to fight them both, so Elisa starts to pull out from their initial agreement. Before they come to a conclusion, Lu suggests speaking to the genie once more, approaching the bottle as he opens it. Zafir al Harid emerges, still unimpressed by the Ajami. Lu speaks to Zafir, trying to persuade him of the wrongness of the state of the world as he introduces the concept of the dread realm to him. 

To Zafir, the dread realm sounds nice. Though Lu insists that Zafir does not understand how bad it is. Which prompts Elisa to show Zafir how it is. Zafir is mostly unaffected, but Grimrid points out that Zafir measures the world through an archaic lens, egging him on to leave and explore the world. Lu adds to it reiterating the purposelessness of this suffering that leads to no growth. Roast cuts in, telling Zafir to either do something or to stop wasting their time. He does, relenting to help the party with the state of things as he grants their wish to bring all the slaves back to Zipzapolis. 

With that settled, the party takes the simpler route, disguising themselves as Shriala and her handmaidens, walking out of the camp alive.
