The party, worn and tired, make their way to the Inn & Ale district. They head into Maegera’s Heat, a tavern in Gauntlgrym. After procuring their rooms and depositing Amunkhet in hers, Elisa and Vesper head out to market’s in search of some ink for Aurelion. As it is rather late, they don’t see much but Elisa manages to buy 10gp worth of ink before the two head back to rest. The next day, Elisa casually swindles Aurelion as the rest watch him pay 100gp for an ounce of ink.

During breakfast, the party has a talk with Aurelion, sharing how they felt about Damien and their concern for Aurelion 's behaviour towards Daegra prior. They gain a better understanding of Aurelion 's previous place of work and how his city profiled members of society, segregating the monster races from the rest. The party makes their points, trying to help Aurelion recognise the inherent racism that he had been taught. Vesper brings up the point of Jub.How when he had shared that the MLA had offered him family and shelter, everyone was happy for him, wishing him well in his new journey. Except for Aurelion who spoke to Jub like he was joining the enemy. When probed on what other options Jub could've taken, Aurelion defends that Jub could have joined their home, or be sent elsewhere, since he would have been able to teleport Jub. Vesper asks why then did Aurelion not offer Jub the choice and he stays quiet for the rest of the meal, silently pondering.

With not much to do, and all still exhausted, the party heads out for a shopping trip, looking for their new fey friends, Ivy and Alvar. Elisaorders a custom sweater for Zipzap that would take a week for Ivy to complete and Vesper orders 12 children sized sweaters that would take 3 weeks to complete for the children back at home. Wandering to the Trader’s Rest, the party sees familiar symbols of the Brightmires as a Vizier beckons them over. They learn that she is Alicia Ravenheart , who desperately needs the help of adventurers to find her lost knights. The party is hesitant to accept, seeing as they were all still recovering from their previous battle. However, the Vizier offers to cover the cost for spell services needed to heal up and the party accepts the mission. They head out with Squire Pod alongside them, the young male’s passion and eagerness for adventure worrying the party. They come across the bodies of 16 goblins who have starved to death and with Elisa  E quick thinking, she manages to piece together and find a small loot corner where Goblins had in their hovel. There she finds a letter, written in Goblinoid, that Pod manages to read some of. The letter vaguely implies that the Goblins were in contact with the MLA somehow.

They then come across Durlin Hammerhock, a Dwarven ghost who seemingly lost his mind. The party learns of a metal robot that’s looking for a weapon, though are sceptical of  the eccentric ghost’s words. They also learn of the direction of the knights and head there quickly in hopes of finding them alive. Approaching the Brightmire knight’s camp, Amunkhet looks out for signs of life and notices tracks of the party seemingly running away from something. Soon enough, the party figures out what they might have been fleeing from as a hoard of Cloakers spring up on them.

They manage to defeat them, and with Pod’s help find one of the bodies of the missing knights, Frederic, alongside a letter he wrote about Orcs, Hobgoblins and Goblins possibly working together. Continuing forth, the party finds a door. The normal looking door puzzles the group but Elisa knocks on it and it swings open to reveal Acererak. The lich seemed to be conducting some experiments before the party interrupted. Pod upon meeting this talking undead is surprised and attempts to attack before being stopped by the party and Acererak. The party asks Acererak for the bodies of the knights and he agrees on the condition that they bring him a Wyvern’s egg. Not wanting to argue with Acererak, the party relents and heads to find one. In a surprisingly merciless fashion, they swiftly defeat the Wyverns, killing the mother and knocking out the father as they bring the egg back to Acererak. As expected by most, Acererak proceeds to whip up a meal using the Wyvern’s eggs. He also gives Elisa a magical dagger for her to feed the Atropal within her before vanishing. Pod then stirs as the party fibs that they defeated the lich and they bring the bodies back to Vizier Ravenheart. 

Just as they approach, Vesper notices that the woman speaking to the Vizier is in disguise. Her staring is noticed by all, especially the seemingly suspicious woman. They return the bodies to the Vizier, receiving thanks and the rewards for their mission. 
The party then has a quick discussion on the disguised woman Vesper saw with Aurelion quickly identifying her to be Goetia’s assistant.

The party takes the time to explore the city more before being summoned by Themberchaud via his imp. The dragon sends them out on a quest in search of treasure which the party proceeds to after a quick detour to the Iron Tabernacle where Targrim Drakeshield, the King’s advisor, tasks the party to search for their lost religious artefacts. Revealing to the party the history of Gauntlgrym and that of the Dwarve, a people beset by tragedy and suffering. Whose ancestral home has been taken, occupied and defiled by all manner of creature, including Illithids and Drow. The party then stumbles upon a group of freshly turned ghouls, and battle is swift. During the battle, Aurelion kills one of the ghoul children, watching as her face morphs into one of fear before her death. Amunkhet knocks out another, seemingly having some plans for her. Elisa and Vesper questions Amunkhet about it, asking her for concrete plans on how to deal with the child. Vesper notices Aurelion  E lack of contribution to the topic as he stands over the body of the ghoul child he killed, silent. She approaches him, assuring him that he had freed the child from a life of being an unwilling soldier, that her soul would finally be able to rest in peace. With that, they regroup with Amunkhet and Elisa as the party agrees once more that that ghoul child cannot be left alive. With a swift strike, Amunkhet ends the child’s life as the party continues on with their quest. In the next room, they meet Ifi, a ghoul child revealed to be a High Priestess of the Ghouls, the party’s existence. Although she seems rather nonchalant about the empire.

She does reveal that the Ghouls are all connected to the Emperor in a way, with even herself not being able to say or do anything that would endanger the life of the Emperor, or else she will be instantly killed. She suggests this has something to do with the Emperor’s status as the God of their Ghouls or perhaps something else. Ifi mentions that she is here seeking knowledge, knowledge that will allow her to bring back a friend. Despite the party’s offering for her to move in with them, she mentions that they lack the resources that she needs and politely declines. She leaves them with a warning that the other priests are here seeking out dragons for the Emperor, and to be careful, as the priests are less relaxed with their restraint in not infecting people with the Ghoul Virus. In the room of the Dragonkeep, the party intrudes as the ghoul priests are completing their ritual to resurrect the dragons. Aurelion successfully stops the ritual, resulting in only one of the dragons to be resurrected. The priest ghouls, alongside the dragon, prove to be a tough battle with the party split on opposite sides of the room. Still coming to terms with the ghoul child he murdered, Aurelion focuses on keeping the only other child he knows, Elisa, alive. Concerned with how they might defeat all of their enemies, Vesper takes a gamble and uses the Eye of Vecna, relieved that she’s not dead, she manages to disintegrate the high priestess, chains wrapping the ghoul, crushing it to dust.

Still, the battle is far from over as Amunkhet is once more dying, Vesper cornered by a headless dragon and ghoul priest. The other priest makes his way past Aurelion to Elisa, blocking her only chance of escape. In desperation, Aurelion renegotiates his contract; he'd die when 20 years was up, trading the remainder of his life to save his friends. This works and Goetia himself shows up to save the party from the undead creatures, scoffing and grunting as he does. His mere presence only enrages Elisa as she tries to stab and shoot at the fiend. Aurelion tries his best to stop those attacks from landing, knowing that they would all be dead if the party hurt Goetia. Even after all the enemies are dealt with Goetia doesn’t leave, stating that Elisa doesn’t seem to be done with him, almost daring her to kill him *if she could. Finally, an attack lands, one that Aurelion can’t stop. He finds himself negotiating once more, reducing his 20 years to 1 in an instant as he allows the fiend’s assistant to sign on his behalf. Aurelion reveals the fact, pleasing Elisa to stop her attacks this time and she does. Goetia leaves and the party, tired, press on with their mission.

They activate the gargoyle and Iymrith, the ancient blue dragon appears. Though she is heavily injured, large portions of her legs missing, her body scarred with a giant gash on her chest. She shares that the dragon remains belonged to her late sons, Lotrol, Ilmerith, and Korax who were mercilessly slain by King Hekathon and how he used their heads to make a throne. She offers the party rewards for their deeds and gifts Elisa with a boon, imparting her rage as she blesses Elisa with the knowledge of how to best strike down a spellcaster. Leaving Iymrith to her children, the party returns back to Themberchaud who rewards them with an Amulet of Devout, something he had gotten from a cleric he killed. Vesper uses her new holy symbol to scry on her family, though everything comes up empty.
