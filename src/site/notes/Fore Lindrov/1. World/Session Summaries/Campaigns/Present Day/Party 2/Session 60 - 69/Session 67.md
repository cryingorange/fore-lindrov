The party checks out the rest of the area, looking for any other magic items that they could use to pay John Smith. Rushing into the rooms, Wystram, Elisa and Nyktos find a series of items. Wystram shows a charm bearing the symbol that they had seen before. Identifying the charm, Elisa realises what it does and its relation to Goetia, taking it with the intention of using his powers against him. Nyktos finds nothing magical but does bring up the topic of what to give John Smith.  
  
Tiny reluctantly suggests giving the adamantine hammer they found and Elisa pretends to have found only regular smithing tools. Though her attempt at lying doesn’t fool Nyktos who gently presses and eventually Elisa admits to finding some magical armour as well. With John Smith’s payment secured, Nyktos heads to the lava pool where Crimson resides, drawing him out, noting a shiny item in his mouth. The item is a piece of mithral he had picked up in his little lava escapade. Crimson is considerably happier, regaining his spark. Seeing how the lava had helped, Nyktos takes a small amount of it, as Tiny helps cool the molten liquid down to a rock, allowing Crimson to have a small piece of what he likes with him.  
  
The party regroups and using the item John Smith gave them go through the slimy inky teleportation and reappear before John Smith’s cart. Tiny and Elisa offer the items up and John offers Tiny a Belt of Hill Giant Strength which Tiny eagerly accepts. 

  

They then head to speak with Criston as Elisa convinces him to follow her, citing that the grounds looked weird. Criston follows, bringing along another villager with him. Using telepathy, Elisa casts suggestion to coax the truth of the blood stained grounds out of Criston, who responds and shares the horrible Hill Giant attack on their land. Criston’s companion is surprised at Criston’s relaxed behaviour and as the spell ends Criston is confused but is led back to rest by his ally.  
  
Putting two and two together, the party figures that Criston had taken the deal after the loss of his people as a way to protect themselves. Seeing as they have nothing else to do, the party begins to leave. Tiny attunes to the belt they had gotten from John Smith and feel the slight improvement in strength and power. Though like most of John Smith’s items this one has a curse too. Tiny’s body begins to expand, bloating up until it becomes difficult for them to move. They fall to the ground, with Tiny immediately blaming their ailment on the belt whilst Vhagar disagrees, wanting more power no matter the cost. Tiny asks Nyktos for help, knowing of the cleric’s abilities to remove curses. However, having used a lot of her magic in the previous battle, Nyktos can’t help and not so secretly assures Vhagar that she would not remove their strength.  
  
Seeing the situation, Nyktos and Wystram leave to collect their horses and carts, leaving Tiny and Vhagar to argue with one another about their new predicament. Nyktos suggests Wystram ready Vermilion whilst she helps ClipClop and much to her disappointment Wystram dodges out of the way as Vermilion attempts to kick him. ZipZap returns to the cart, hovering above it as he rests, still poisoned from before. He demands Nyktos heal him but she doesn’t, using his own logic of him being  Eoo strong to be poisoned Eagainst him. Tiny demands to be on the cart as well but the party denies, citing that they are too big and heavy to safely be on the cart. But Elisa has a solution, pulling out a boar and a bear from her bag of tricks to push Tiny around. 

  

Along their way back to Grudd Haug, they hear a sound from the distance, one that Tiny recognises as a cry for help. Rushing in the direction that Tiny’s pointed out, the party finds a beached Orca crying out in pain as a mother Bear and her two cubs feast from the still alive Orca. Most of them are okay with the situation, attributing it to be the circle of life. But Tiny isn’t okay with this and steps in, earning them the ire of mother Bear who wishes to protect her cubs.  
  
With Elisa’s help, Tiny manages to push the Orca back into the waters where it is safe from being consumed. Elisa casts speak with animals, allowing her to converse with the mother Bear, they offer food for her and her cubs, successfully saving all four animals Elives for the time being. As the bears leave, Elisa turns to the Orca, who has been incessantly trying to get her attention.

  

Speaking to the Orca, they learn that her name is Bubbles, a friend of Serissa’s. Bubbles explains that each sister has a whale; Nym, the middle child, has a friend called Cipher and Mirran, the eldest, has a friend called Tempest. Bubbles was trying to find Serissa, who had been missing about the same time the party got to Golden Fields. Her need to find Serissa is further exacerbated by the fact that the court is in trouble, Serissa’s sisters have taken over as Queens but constantly debating with one another, unable to come to an agreement. Bubbles also shares about a Giant called Thimir who has snuck into their room in search of clues.  
  
When the party offers to visit Bubbles some day, Bubbles gives them directions to her room, warning them of the potential dangers along the way and informs them that the safest way to get to the Maelstrom is via the conch that all Giant Lords have. As a final gift for saving her life, Bubbles undoes the curse on Tiny, freeing them, she also gives the party some potions and magic to those who need it. The party bids Bubbles farewell, promising to help her find Serissa as well. 

  

They eventually return to Grudd Haug, meeting up with Hruk and Loog who are having a fun time playing with the owlbears. As Chieftess Guh demands for her meals, the Hill Giants stop their fun, now needing to complete the chores as most of the smallies who used to do them have been fed to the Chieftess. Hruk enlists the party’s help and their first task is to wrangle up some Giant sheep. Wystram strides in, grappling one of the sheeps as he drags it to the corner rather easily whilst he watches the rest complete their chore. Nyktos goes up next, summoning a hand of blood to pluck some grass from the ground, bringing it up to the sheep’s eyes as she lures it to the corner.  
  
Elisa helps by surveying the area, picking out the meanest looking sheep and instructing Tiny to pick that one, claiming it would be the best. The sheep is not easy to deal with, but Tiny is strong as he slams the sheep to the ground, knocking it out and dragging it to where Hruk and Loog can pick them up. During this, Wystram’s sheep has gotten close to him, liking his presence as it nuzzles against him, not knowing the cruel fate that awaits. 

  

 Their next task is to collect some boars, who are much more fearsome compared to the sheeps. The boars put up a fight, as evidenced by the fact that even Hruk and Loog have some trouble getting them, but Hruk is ready to help Loog, pushing the boars away from his friend’s side. Wystram bravely walks in, confident in his abilities as he approaches a boar, attempting to grab it. The boar does not give Wystram an easy time, fighting against the monk’s hands and almost knocking him down. Seeing as Wystram needs some help, Nyktos sends some magic his way, puppeteering the boar just as Wystram jumps on its back to subdue it.  
  

The sudden stop causes the boar to launch Wystram into the air but he lands somewhat safely realising that Nyktos could have just done that from the start. Elisa and Tiny work together once more, this time though all the boars are about the same level of hostility. Elisa diligently plays her role as manager, telling Tiny what they have to do as they grapple the boar, successfully overpowering the animal. Done with their task, Hruk leaves the party to explore the area as they begin processing the animals for Chieftess Guh’s meal. 

  

The party is drawn to the delicious smells from the kitchen. Tiny, Elisa, and Nyktos enter as Wystram stays behind, ready to strike from the shadows if needed. They enter to find a kobold, bound like one would a hog above an unlit roast. The chef is a blue skinned creature with black claws, yellow eyes and sharp teeth. He smiles, introducing himself as Chef Margos as he shares his life philosophy.  
  
Tiny questions the kobold’s presence and is unhappy when Chef Margos tells them that the Kobold is to be eaten. But Chef Margos continues, explaining the many delicious humanoids and sentient beings that he’s consumed. Naturally, the party is appalled at the existence of such a being. Elisa and Nyktos attempt to discern what or where Margos might have come from but the Chef says nothing more, instead sharing about his previous master and admitting to cannibalising the man who saved him.  
  
Using his chopped off arm, Tiny manages to haggle for the Kobold’s life who prostrate himself before Tiny and Vhagar, referring to them as a great dragon. Vhagar enjoys the attention whilst Tiny insists on being Kobold's friend. The Kobold rejects the notion, claiming to be Tiny’s servant instead. Elisa manages to convince the Kobold that ‘friend Eis merely a title that Tiny gives to their servants and it appeases the Kobold. Though the Kobold is immediately in for another shock as he sees Wystram, assuming the elf to be a drow and readily picks up a rock to kill Wystram.  
  
But Elisa diffuses the situation, lying to the Kobold that Wystram is one of Tiny’s slaves. The Kobold steps down for now, leading Tiny and the party to the rest who are locked up. The other Kobolds are likewise basking in the glory of being before a dragon and all react poorly to Wystram. Tiny promises to free them but has them wait as they find a safe place for the Kobolds to hide. 

  

The party notices gems in the walls and are curious as they approach. A Stone Giant emerges from the walls, with yellow, purple, green, blue gems on her fists. She begins musing to herself, curious as to why she’s dreamt up smallfolk. The Giant, Thane Kayalithica, seems to fully believe that she is in a dream despite the party’s efforts to tell her otherwise. Wystram, however, is able to play into her delusions to help the party. Wystram’s prompts causes Kayalithica to share her dream and how it began with a loud explosion, the shatter of the ordning as prophesied by her Stone Mate, the Ruler of the Stone Giants who saw the ordning as restrictive and stifling. Wanting to break the cosmic chains, her mate led the charge against the heavens but lost the war when she was captured by Hekaton. And in her rage she had the Stone Giants forget themselves, lose their identity and forsake their names until the day their Ruler is returned to them. When asked what she thinks of smallfolk, she confidently proclaims that her mate had taught them all to not judge smallfolk. 

  

But her words are filled with hypocrisy. For someone who claimed to have reached enlightenment, she was easily enraged when the topic of her mate came up. And for someone who claims to have forgotten her mate and his name, she boldly proclaims his behaviours and beliefs as if they were the truth. In her anger towards Hekaton and the Storm Giants, she brought up the tale of Countess Sansuri, a brave warrior known for her combative nature who had been insulted by King Hekaton when he insinuated that she was a harlot.  
  
The countess Ebrother, Thullen, defended his sister from the King’s slanderous words in less than polite terms. As a result, the King accused Thullen of treason and had Countess Sansuri lead the battle against her brother and all others the King deemed deserving of death. 

  

Vhagar drags Tiny aside, sharing their ‘reasoning Eas to why they believe that Kayalithica should die. Vhagar insists that Kayalithica would stop at no end to bring her mate back, and there’s no telling what he is actually like. If the Stone Ruler demands that they kill all smallfolk, Kayalithica would no doubt agree.  
  
This moment is their best chance at taking down Kayalithica, they’d be fools to not seize the opportunity. But Tiny rejects the notion, citing Reggie’s words that there is a spark of good in every person, that one only needs to find it. Vhagar’s words make sense, though it is obvious that they only provide reasoning and argue for some greater good to attempt to get the party aligned with their desires.  
  
And since no one gives in to Vhagar, they have no choice but to go along with the party. Tiny returns to Thane Kayalithica and offers to help find her Stone Mate. In return, Kayalithica offers the party’s help with the Hill Giants, even agreeing to not kill anyone.

  

With no gems to collect, the party explores the remaining area. They find beds and notice a rattling chest. Opening it, they find a Goblin named Yuk. Yuk is afraid, worried that the party is here to send him to be eaten but they assure that they are not. Yuk’s sleeping areas would be a safe place for the Kobolds to be whilst the party fought and eventually they manage to strike a deal, the Kobolds would be allowed to hide there if the party kills Chef Margos. The party agrees. 

  

They also find sacks containing items and dive in to look for stuff. They all find useful items, from wheels of cheese to legs of ham, except for Wystram. The bag he inspects falls over, as a loud buzzing cloud emerges from within rushing to sting Wystram before Tiny jumps in, breathing fire and killing the bees, saving Wystram. 

  

The party heads to the kitchen once more, this time, intent on ending the chef’s life. They rush in as Tiny sees their arm over the fire, smelling very good. They proceed to beat the crap out of the chef, ending his life before he could do much. With the chef dead, Nyktos and Wystram head to the chest, pocketing the magical bags of spices that they found.  
  
Meanwhile ZipZap is actively salivating over the cooked arm. But Tiny notices in time and is able to prevent their arm from being consumed by the greedy beholder, instead they incinerate the arm. Finishing up with looting, Nyktos heads to Margos Ecorpse, inspecting the body as she cuts out his heart, taking note of the differences in anatomy. In place of his heart, she suggests pouring in the potion of diminution where Margos Eheart used to be and when the potion takes effect, that is when the party will enact the rest of their plan. 

  

With that, the party delivers to Chieftess Guh her meal. As the chieftess lays her eyes upon the party, she assumes that they are her next feast but Loog is quick to step in to protect his friends, assuring that they are good people. Tiny takes the lead, speaking to Chieftess Guh and offering her the bag of bounty so she need not hunt any longer. Chieftess Guh takes immediate offence to this. The Hill Giant leader not taking well when someone else tries to rob her of her autonomy.  
  
She reacts negatively, shouting at the party as they’ve reminded her of the worst moments of her life, when the Storm Giants cruelly barged into her home and forced her and her people to serve them meals whilst they were starved. But the party manages to diffuse the situation, with Tiny apologising for their words. The party chips in, seeking the Chieftess Ehelp and eventually convincing her to enjoy her meal.  
  
She picks up Chef Margos Ebody and consumes him. As the potion takes effect Chieftess Guh shrinks, and the truth comes to light. Loog is utterly taken aback by the betrayal. The ones he played games with, the ones he suffered with, the ones who saved him, the ones who were meant to be his friends were in fact nothing more than liars. In anguish, he shouts at them, vowing to tell everyone of the party’s cruelty. The rest of the Hill Giants attack and combat begins. 

  

It’s not an easy fight, with many Hill Giants versus the party who have already used a good amount of their energy fighting devils, drows, and a fire giant earlier in the day. But they manage, with Elisa ,Nyktos, Wystram dealing a great amount of damage to Giants whilst Tiny and Milton knock them out. The party successfully saves most of the Hill Giants, merely knocking them unconscious. All except one, who died when Nyktos used a spell gem of synaptic static when both her and Crimson’s lives were at stake. But as combat comes to a close, Tiny stands over Chieftess Guh. And instead of killing her, they offer a hand.  
  
Tiny recites the final lesson that Reggie taught them  Ethe importance of forging one’s own path. No longer should the Hill Giants be shackled by the ordning, that they are now free to find their own way through. A new life beginning with peace. And the Hill Giants Echance to break the vicious cycle of the ordning, starts with Chieftess Guh. 

  
But Vhagar interrupts, wanting as they’ve always had to kill the Giant Lords. Vhagar fights, pushing against Tiny’s grandiose dreams of peace, reminding Tiny that they are one and the same. That all the bloodlust, death, and power that Vhagar seeks is also what Tiny yearns. And for a second, Tiny falters, unable to deny the connection between the two.  
  
Vhagar’s control has a physical effect on their bodies as blue scales begin to grow past Vhagar’s side of the body, a visual representation of their battle. But as Tiny’s conviction waivers, a voice, Reggie’s calls out, reminding Tiny of the morals Reggie had instilled. Tiny finds confidence, assured of the dragon they want to be. As Tiny stands by their decision, pushing away Vhagar’s ideology, their red scales grow platinum, the shining hue washes over their scales, taming the blue tones. The radiance of a metallic dragon glows around Tiny before it fades away, leaving its mark on one of Tiny’s scales that shines ever so slightly brighter than the others. And in that moment, peace won.
