Wrapping up her conversation with Nyktos, Telantes heads to Elisa's room for a conversation. She shares of her memories of a sister that she had once hurt. Telantes elaborates of visions that she sees with her and her sister, where her sister despises her for leaving her behind and for all of the hurt that she's caused. She recalls being killed in this vision, and to all of this Elisa suggests that Telantes tries contacting and finding her sister. Their conversation naturally leads to Telantes's lack of money and a few items that she holds. Elisa offers her a potion of speed, whilst Telantes gives her a red potion of Dragon's Majesty and a symbol of Nen. Meanwhile Nyktos, not wanting to be alone, heads over to Rook's room to find him praying. His aura is different from before, now sporting a blue hue instead. Nyktos apologises for interrupting but is welcomed in by the celestial. Rook asks about her well-being and Nyktos confesses about her self-hate and how she can't help but blame herself for what's happened. As a sign of thanks, Nyktos produces the symbol of Selune and gifts it to Rook, knowing of the great importance that Selune has in his life. The two indulge in drinking their sorrows away and conversations come to close they all head back to their rooms for a long night's rest. However in the midst of their attempt to slumber, a loud voice booms from outside their rooms. To hear someone introducing themselves as Pyrite, a knight from an order that most of the party have not heard of. Rook, not needing to sleep, is the first to open his door and the sight that greets him is a grey skinned Hobgoblin, standing at 170 cm tall, dressed in chainmail and holding a shield with an immensely wide grin. Rook inquires Pyrite and learns of his intentions to join the party. One by one, the others drowsily emerge from their rooms, with Elisa simply sticking her head out through the door asking the newcomer to kindly lower their volume as they wish to rest. Introductions are exchanged and the party finally continues to rest, this time without interruption. 
  

The next day, the party receives a letter from  Broomhilda, informing them of the increased security around town and that she would be unable to join them in their investigation of the haunted manor. The party burns the letter as requested, and decides to leave the haunted manor for the next day. Continuing with their plans, they head to Castle Darkrift. During the travel, Telantes asks Pyrite what his favourite thing is, sharing that her own was when she made castles and toys with her sister. They continue on with their walk before being stopped by four civilians. The men and women that halted the party glare in anger, their words laced with malice as they blame the party for Lady Wachter's death. They resent the party for taking everything away from them and try as best as they could to attack and harm the party. To no avail, naturally, as the party easily overpowers them. The party tries to talk them down, urging them to instead turn their ire towards the Baron and for them to not sully the name of Lady Wachter with their actions. The party also reveals the plan that Lady Wachter had for the employees, but nothing shakes their loyalty for their lady. With a casting of calm emotions, Telantes deescalates the fight for now, as the employees confess that they would find a better time to attack the party. But for now, Medelena, Amalthia, Haliq, and Davit return to the town, having failed their first attempt at killing the party. 
  

Was that matter dealt with for now, the party continues on to Castle Darkrift. As they leave once more, Telantes recalls the god shard that she has that allows her to summon a more efficient mode of transport. She summons the giant sea snake once more and the party quickly reaches Castle Darkrift. As they approach the castle, Nyktos sees a figure a distance away, shrouded with darkness and raven feathers, she curtsies before continuing on her path. Telantes sees a silhouette of a young child, a figure that she had seen before in her dreams and visions. Under her breath, Telantes mutters, asking her sister if she's here. They continue on towards the apple tree that Grace had told them about. As they approach, Elisa's psionic powers activate once more showing a sight of Grace and Lucian carving their names and a heart on the tree. The party also hears a cute scene of Lucian and Grace flirting with one another during their happier times. Continuing on into the castle, Pyrite sees clear signs of battle as himself and all of the party start to hear voices. Nyktos hears Ilenti's voice, calling out to her. Telantes hears the cries of her sister. Without a second thought, the two of them rushed to the source of the sound, swinging the door open. Nyktos sees Ilenti, body battered and bruised she screams at her sister for abandoning her once again. She expresses her hate for Nyktos, swinging at her older sister. Nyktos stands there and accepts the hit, not caring whatever harm she has to endure if it will make her sister feel better. Meanwhile, Telantes sees her sister, who said that Telantes had promised that she would always find her and yet instead seems to love everyone else but her. That she had given up everything for someone else, something else. As this happens, Elisa sees her mother and instantly holds onto Rook, informing him of such. Her mother reaches out to her pulling her in for a hug, and Elisa can feel the warmth of her skin and smell the familiar scent of her hair. This is real. Her mother greets her happily, saying that she's finally here. But as Elisa continues to deny what she sees, claiming that it's not real, her mother's demeanour changes. She starts questioning why Elisa hasn't killed the three people that wronged them, that's wronged their entire city. Her mother cries out about being eaten, and starts admonishing Elisa, calling her leech, that she should have died with them, that she doesn't know why she bothered saving her. Trying to protect Elisa from the sorrowsworn, Rook pulls Elisa close. Lastly, Pyrite sees Riki, the healer at Salem. Her bottom jaw hangs loose, her tongue flailing about, sputtering as she tries to speak. Riki reminds him of what happened, of how he had abandoned them, how he had run away, how the men of the village had to sacrifice their lives instead. Pyrite denounces the being before him, knowing that Riki would never say that. But the figure before him persists, insulting his honour, mocking him for being a knight that runs away. Not willing to hear another word of this, Pyrite swings. His weapon instead lands on who was right before him, Nyktos. As the only one without guilt, Rook sees the figure for what it actually is: a disgusting amalgamation of flesh and slime. Battle ensues and the party makes quick work of the monster being continued on. They bring all of the dead bodies and bury them a distance away from the castle. During the burial, Pyrite and Rook both speak a prayer for the fallen. As they continue on, we find themselves in a bedroom where a baby's cot remains. The cot bears a nameplate, "Alucard". Then, a spirit appears before them proclaiming to be the butler of the house, and that he would not allow anyone to trespass on the lands no longer. Elisa senses a strange familiarity towards this man, and instead of battle, speaks with him. Revealing themselves to be Grace's friend, the man calms down and introduces himself as Abraham. He shares about the three adventures that had betrayed his master, how they had been named as the "Moribund", a name meaning " approaching death". He also shares that Bunny was sent here with a woman, dressed in black clothes and pale skin, who currently stands at the top of the roof. As his final request, he asks the party for death, to allow him to die in a slightly dignified manner as opposed to the maddened mess that he would soon become. Pyrite is the first to agree, it was about to brandish his blade before being stopped by Rook. The party speaks of something confusing to him, talking about sending souls to the right place. But he puts aside his confusion and follows their lead as they all channel their connection for the individual divinities, helping Elisa send Abraham to where he's meant to be. Naturally, Pyrite is extremely confused but the party explains that it would be best to leave this conversation for a better time. They agree, and following the path that Abraham had told them they reach a room with a throne. Elisa's psionics activate once more, as they see a vision of Lucian sitting in his chair, dejected and tired as Abraham updates him on the status of the war. They continue down and see visions of the Moribund who had tricked Lucian. Nyktos notices a spider failing to creep up to them, and alerts the party about it. Elisa speaks to the spider, named Nala, and learns that Bunny had instructed her to eat all those who come through. The spider informs them the sword they seek is up and across, though there is another being with Bunny, a more angry lady. Elisa successfully convinces Nala to leave them be and they head up to the roof. 

  

As they approach, Nyktos recognises the energy, it is the same way that sorrowsworns are created in the Shadowfell. They hear the angry voices of the sorrowsworn, shouting at Nyktos, calling her an abandoner, saying that she was supposed to protect Ilenti. Nyktos apologises, and urges Ilenti to attack her and to leave the party alone. Understanding their dynamic, the party deals with the sorrowsworn, and Rook help us bring Nyktos closer to her sister. However as the sorrowsworn are out to get Nyktos, they focus their attention on Crimson, swiping at the innocent puppy, hell-bent to kill it. As the pup falls, Nyktos lets out an agonising scream. At this moment, Rook hears a voice. The voice offers him power and a chance to save Crimson. Naturally, Rook accepts and a sword flies out from Telantes' bag to Rook's hand as a powerful wave of energy surges, wiping out the sorrowsworn. Ilenti turns to the newcomer, her head splitting open as a third eye emerges and makes contact with Pyrite's head. In less than a second, Pyrite finds himself somewhere else, somewhere dark and unfamiliar. He senses a presence and as he perceives it well, he finds the figure right before him as its hand reaches out to him and upon touching him crushes his entire being. And he finds himself back, right where he was, the battle is still ongoing. Rook swings his new weapon at Ilenti, and as the blade makes contact it pulls blood. The blade growls with satisfaction, and without realising, Rook makes his next attack aiming right for Ilenti's heart. Nyktos screams out, reminding Rook that he promised to protect her. In shock, drops the blade, and pulls out his original sword only to have it shatter in his hand. Eventually, the battle comes to a close. And in those final moments, Ilenti, seems to come back to consciousness. She apologises before bringing her hand to her chest, and killing herself. The party immediately tries to heal Ilenti in any way that they can, but nothing is working, her wounds do not seem to heal. Nyktos does all that she can, reaching out to her God hoping to stop this, but it doesn't work. And in desperation, turns to Telantes, asking for the cleric to do something, anything that she can to save her sister. But before Nyktos can even finish her sentence, Telantes places a hand on the older Shadar Kai's shoulder and she begins to channel her powers. At that moment the party realises that the person before them isn't just a cleric, but rather a spark of true divinity, the very same power that brought Rook back. She is none other than Selune herself. As she uses all of the remainder of her power to heal Ilenti, her body begins to fade. Elisa mutters that she knows who Telantes' sister is. Rook rushes forward to hug Telantes, not wanting to lose her just right after he finds her. But as he tries, his arms pass through, and her body begins to fade even faster. Telantes apologises to Elisa for not being able to fulfil her promise to help save her family, she also apologises to Nyktos for not being able to see Ilenti's smile. Telantes asks that the party inform Nyssa and Lariet of her passing. And in response to Nyktos asking how she can help with her sister, Telantes says to tell her sister she's sorry for everything. Then finally to Rook, she reminds him to not let the blade control his actions. And before her last arm fades, she pulls the party in for one final hug, thanking them for letting her live her dream, and telling them to be good. She bids them farewell, calling Nyktos by her nickname “Nikki E and Nyktos replying in kind by saying “Bye Telantes Ebefore fading away. With that, the goddess Selune is no more. 

  

With Telantes' passing, Ilenti comes back to life, black shadows that surround her change her armour and coalesce to form her missing left arm, she is notably missing her trademark axe guitar. The sisters hug each other tight, now finally reunited. Rook walks away, still processing the loss. Nyktos approaches, apologising to the Paladin as she explains that she asked Telantes to save her sister. But Rook insists that it is not her fault, that Telantes would have done it either way. The party then proceeds to collect Houzuki's blade. And as they do, the blade speaks, they learn of Houzuki's real name, Ilvari, and the story of her life. As they begin to set up camp, Ilenti speaks to Elisa sharing about what she knows of Banecroft, their mother, and Houzuki. She does the same, sharing the information with Nyktos, who is unsurprised to learn that Houzuki is their sister. Ilenti also asks about Telantes, which Nyktos happily answers and shares Telantes' opening question of "What is your favourite thing." Nyktos shares that hers is Ilenti's smile and Ilenti shares that hers is Nyktos. Meanwhile, Rook and Pyrite speak, sharing about their warrior culture, discovering that they had both been raised to fight from a young age. Rook shares about his undead nature, revealing how Banecroft killed him and Selune saved him, giving him a second chance. He expresses his regret in not realising that she was right there before him. Pyrite comforts the Celestial, saying that he had fought alongside Selune with honour and now he has sworn to bring Selune back. Pyrite offers his support, promising that if there is a way, he will help Rook in his journey. As the conversation continues, Pyrite shares that he must find his kingdom, to which Rook readily offers his help. Pyrite also shares that he was directed here by Auntie Greta, that he has found his blade buried in a wall in a tunnel.

  

A team meeting is then called for, and the party addresses the "soul going to the right place" situation, and the vision that Elisa suspects Pyrite saw. They clarify what is going on with the moon, and what happens to souls when they die. Pyrite takes some time to process the information and then plants this blade down as he swears to formally join the party and to deal with the moon. The party awkwardly re-welcomes him to their group and they all rest for the night. As the rest, Elisa casts plant growth at the apple tree, trying to preserve what is left of it. Meanwhile, Rook finds the Sword of Kas near him, despite him having thrown it away before. 

  

As the party wakes up to find Rook with the blade, they question him about it, asking if he had gone to retrieve it. Rook insists that he has not and Nyktos asks Kaze to clarify if swords could move on their own. Kaze suggests that the blade might be cursed and Nyktos offers to remove the curse for Rook. She shares this information with the rest of the party as well, who all voice their concerns, except Pyrite who fully trusts Rook. The party discusses as Rook seems to converse with his blade, he eventually settles on rejecting the offer for curse removal, citing that the blade did not want it. The remainder of the party once again bring up their concerns, reminding Rook that he needs to not let the blade control him. They set out for the haunted mansion. 

  

As they attempt to enter Argynvostholt, a tall man, whom they had not noticed before, stops them. He introduces himself as Bishop Heahmund and goes on about being a sword of god, and how he tried to fight Ficklehorn, who lives in the mansion. Both the paladins seem all right with this random man, whilst the remainder remains sceptical about him. They enter the house, and upon entering one of the rooms sees four flesh golems, battle ensues. Before Ilenti makes her attack, she covers herself in darkness, not allowing her party members to see her before transforming. During the battle, the Bishop was of little to no use, whilst the party killed all of the flesh golems. They go through another room to find Hayden bleeding and bound, as he calls out that the bad man is behind them. At that moment, the party realises that the Bishop was lying to them. He reveals his true identity and attacks Nyktos before running away. When Ficklehorn appears once more, Ilenti takes the opportunity to attack him, this time unable to hide it as her shadow arm turns into a blade. The party make quick work of Ficklehorn before saving Hayden who is still upset with the party but begrudgingly accepts their help. Hayden leaves, heading outside to wait for the party. The party continues exploring the place, learning of the people who had been kept there, Kaede, Ficklehorn, and Esme and their gradual descent to madness.

  

As they continue exploring, an annoying presence shows up. He speaks about how mercy is wasted on broken things and Rook shows his true form, proclaiming that Selune guides his step. The rest of the party stays silent as Pyrite confronts the vampire. As usual, Banecroft enjoys the game, demanding an apology from Pyrite who accused him of actions with no proof to speak of. Ilenti tries to diffuse the situation, saying that Pyrite knows not of what he speaks about. And when Banecroft asks Pyrite to dance, he does not. Nyktos attempts to blood bend Pyrite to force him to dance, not wanting to have someone die again from Banecroft’s whims but it fails. With that, battle ensues. It does not go well for the party, with most of them having to retreat out of Banecroft's sickening radiance. The vampire continuously attacks Pyrite, draining him of his life force before holding him hostage. During combat, the party is forced to reveal the sunblade, allowing Banecroft to know that they have it. He offers the party a deal, Pyrite gets to live and they get to keep the sunblade, in exchange, he will raise Pyrite as a spawn. Rook takes the deal, not wanting to lose anyone again so soon. And with Banecroft gone, the party buries Pyrite, readying themselves for when he comes back. With an hour passed, Pyrite wakes up with an uncontrollable desire for blood. Ilenti immediately punches Pyrite, attempting to snap him out of it. Meanwhile Elisa, who was already prepared, offers a wrist. Pyrite tries to control his hunger, but alas, he could not as he grabs onto the wrist, sinking his teeth into her skin. When he satiates his hunger, he finds himself incredibly ashamed for his lack of control and apologises for it, though Elisa seems unfazed, insisting that it was alright. In anger at herself, Ilenti speaks calmly to Pyrite, informing that she is going to punch him and he will allow her to do it because he put her sister and friends in danger, and that she's not going to let anyone do that, not even herself. Taking the punch, the party now reunites, still having one last person to deal with at Argynvostholt.
