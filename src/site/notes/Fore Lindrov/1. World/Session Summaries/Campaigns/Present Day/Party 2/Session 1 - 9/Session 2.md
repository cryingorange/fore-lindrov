The party spend a week at the cave with Dave and the survivors of Zaratan. In the night a child dies, Elisa desperately tries to bring the child back but to no avail. Mortified by Hakon's lack of apparent care at the death of the child. With Ilythyrra saying nothing, the group then buries the child with Dave struggling out of the cave to attend it as well. Hakon then gets into an argument with Pulliver who asks that Hakon train the children to defend themselves. He refuses, saying that they are children and that he doesn't do anything for free. Pulliver storms off in a rage. Ilythyrra goes out in late hours to scout the area for danger. Elisa, Pulliver and Dave also discuss plans to deal with Party 1, deciding that they must hide and plan. Ilythyrra and Elisa also go out to look for herbs for poison. Elisa casts Find Plants and they discover an area of dying green that seems magical. Elisa takes a magical plant with her while Ilythyrra gathers the herbs she needs. Elisa then gives Dave the worm of Godfrey in a jar to Dave.

Dave then tells the party that they need a safe place to stay. Citing that a Beholder is nearby, and they have a tendency to create well-made cave structures. He tells the party the directions and they head off, with Elisa navigating they make their way there. But they see a large swarm of Stirges, like a dark shadow. The party go into sneak and walk over the hill to where the source of Stirges are. There they see a Vampiric Woman dressed in black plate armour crouched low, approaching the mountain that the party are also headed to. The party struggle with what to do but decide on following the woman. She arrives at a cave entrance and lies low in wait. 

After about 15 minutes 2 Trolls emerge from the cave mouth, and the woman springs her attack. The Stirges charge in as the woman prepares to attack, only for Ilythyrra to sneak up on her and say hi. THe Woman immediately attempts an attack.

As the party end the ambush, they swiftly move through the convoluted trap chamber. With Hakon falling into a pit once again, the party investigate every inch of the wall. With Hakon discovering a hidden entrance in the corner of the room.
