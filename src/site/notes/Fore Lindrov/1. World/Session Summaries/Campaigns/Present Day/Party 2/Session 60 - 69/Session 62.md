Having slain the follower of Tiamat, Tiny looks at the mask they have in their hand. It calls out to them, drawing them in as they find themselves in a different place. Before them is a figure, and as they step forward it becomes clearer. A woman with white hair and a pale face is dressed in a flowing black dress with red trim. Her eyes are a deep green that exudes much warmth and kindness. She extends her hands out to Tiny, her blue painted nails wrapping around as she pulls them into a hug.  
  
Everything feels nice, warm, and safe as even Tiny’s other head leans into her touch. The woman speaks, promising the answers to Tiny’s identity, assuring them that they are destined for great things. The vision fades to an earthy place of heat, a low growl in the distance. She tells them that “Tiny Eisn’t their true name, that they were given a different name when they hatched. But before they could remember, they were stolen, taken away from her side. The vision flashes once more, showing a red egg that shines bright. She says that they had a name befitting their destiny  EVhagar. She assures them that they will learn more, that this is the first of many steps. They are fang and fury, a force to be reckoned with. 

  

To the rest of the party, Tiny had been staring at the mask, gradually bringing it to their face before they snapped out of their vision. The party is quick to suggest against putting the mask on, warning that it would be dangerous. And just as Tiny is about to give in, their other half puts on the mask, claiming themselves to be Vhagar. The blue mask attaches to Vhagar’s face, grafting onto the skeletal half of their head. Tiny hangs their head, resigned as Vhagar takes more control.  
  
As Vhagar walks them away, sounds of metal clang, falling to the floor. The suit that once housed the two of them breaks, as a new draconic body forms, freeing them from their metal shell. Tiny mourns the loss whilst Vhagar doesn’t care, feeling no connection towards the suit or the one who built it. Lucatiel helps Tiny pick up the pieces, offering Tiny some comfort regarding the situation. Though as the items are all picked up, Vhagar nudges the party along, sarcastically claiming that they have people to help. The rest follow. 

  

The party eventually heads to rest with Nyktos casting the dream spell to contact King Baldwin. Entering the dream, she finds herself in a heavenly bright cathedral, the symbol of King Baldwin’s house prominently displayed upon one of the walls. King Baldwin sits in the front, a focused stare as his gaze is glued to the large vat before him. The vat houses a creepy creature. The King is angry but his dreams are interrupted as Nyktos makes her presence known.  
  
She steps forward, bowing to King Baldwin as she reports the incident that has happened at White Crest, telling him of the cerulean mask and of the follower of Tiamat that tried to steal it. The King tells them to stay until backup arrives, putting aside the matter of Golden Fields for a couple of days, offering the citizens of White Crest his protection. Having finished her report, Nyktos attempts to change the dream before she leaves, wanting to offer King Baldwin a moment of respite. The dream changes as a young girl, Milly appears. King Baldwin looks at the girl gently, his gaze carrying pity and regret. He reaches out to Milly, telling her that she must live longer than he does before the dream goes back to normal. 

  

Nyktos returns, waking Lucatiel up who wakes the rest up with their signature alarm noise. She informs the party of King Baldwin’s decisions and they all return to their rest. The next day, the party helps out as much as they can until a single figure approaches the city at fast speeds. It is Lupercal, he assures the party that he will take care of matters at White Crest whilst the city awaits the rest of the forces to arrive. His boisterous attitude drastically contrasts the city’s heavy mood, though he may just be the change the city needs to start healing. Handing the city off to his capable hands, the party heads towards Golden Fields. They spend two days travelling there.  
  
During which, Nyktos has successfully convinced her horse to stop biting her at every chance. She manages to feed him apples and even bestows him the name “Vermilion E She approaches Elisa to thank her for her advice, sharing that Vermilion has truly warmed up to her through the apples. 

  

On their second day of travel, Lucatiel gathers up the good rations and offers them to Vhagar, claiming it to be their birthday party. Short of any time to make a proper present, Nyktos offers Vhagar a golden ribbon. Vhagar scoffs at the party’s antics, still taking what is theirs. Meanwhile Tiny sulks, asking where his birthday present and party is.

  

The party approaches the gates of Golden Fields, hearing a disagreement between the guards and a familiar individual. Ken, alongside his fellow cultists are trying to enter Golden Fields much to the dismay of the guards. The party attempts to introduce themselves, with Elisa showing the standard given to her by the King as proof of their identity. They vouch for the fire enthusiasts but before they are able to enter, a scream rings through the skies, that of a woman’s voice as an unseen force punches the moon.  
  
Tiny and Vhagar sense that something terrible has happened, but weirdly enough for Tiny, they feel happy about it. As the moon changes another scream echoes, knocking Crimson, Vermilion, and Zipzap unconscious. Rocks are hurled at the gates of Golden Fields, as the party springs into action to stop the attacks. Nyktos summons a wall of blood, bringing the first boulder to a grinding halt.  
  
Tiny and Vhagar ride the momentum of the blood wave up, grabbing hold of the second boulder as they crush it with their bare hands. Elisa polymorphs into a giant ape, leaping as she catches the third boulder between her legs, crushing it as well. Lucatiel extends a hand, channelling their magic on the final boulder, just managing to slow the boulder’s descent.  
  
The party eventually finds themselves fighting a group of ogres and Hill Giants. A figure appears atop the walls of Golden Fields, a middle aged man dressed in purple robes with a golden trim, he leaps down, helping the party with his magic. Nyktos manages to blood bend most of the ogres, pitting the enemy’s team against themselves as they make quick work of the enemies. 

  

They are then allowed entry into Golden Fields where they speak to the burgomaster who begs the party for their help, sharing information that other places had been attacked by giants as well. The party naturally accepts, attempting to ease the burgomaster's worries. Having finished their conversation with him, they then have the chance to speak with the mysterious sorcerer who helped them out.  
  
The garnet wielding individual introduces himself as Severin. Upon seeing Tiny and Vhagar, he immediately claims that they are the one he sees in his dreams. His vague wording prompts Elisa, Lucatiel, and Nyktos to think the situation as either odd or weirdly romantic as the three gossip in the corner.  
  
Severin shares of his dreams, how he’s witnessed blood freezing and ice chains, a rain over the hills, and of his own future, where his teeth fall and his nails slough off. Lucatiel, in a particularly enthusiastic mood after seeing Ken, readily offers Severin to join the “Heroes of the Mist E Severin wants to accept the offer, though only with the entire party’s consent. Nyktos inquires of Severin’s magical capabilities and agrees after warning him of the dangers that tend to follow the party. As they travel, following the tracks left by the Hill Giants, Severin shares his dreams.  
  
That he’s had them since young and that his parents told him they would be useful. Severin is exceptionally silent about his family, as the mention of them since to bring up an old wound. He does share that he’s lived in Golden Fields all his life. Lucatiel probes about Severin’s age and receives a suspicious response as Severin claims to be young. Recognising the odd response, Nyktos likens Severin’s response to one of how an Elf who has lived for ages would respond.

  

They soon come across a town that is suspiciously quiet. As they approach, they sense an unnatural drop in temperature about the place. Getting closer, the townsfolk are weird. In place of what should be their heads are instead beacons of light. The townsfolk seem to have no reaction as the party approaches, ignoring their presence. The party splits into groups, wandering off to investigate. Elisa and Nyktos find a pile of boulders. Upon closer inspection, Nyktos notices that the boulders seem to be part of a stack, whilst Elisa spots an arm sticking out from under the rubble.  
  
Using the telekinesis, Elisa removes the boulders, revealing the remains of a crushed person. Though unlike the rest of the town, this individual looks normal, untouched by any curse. They inspect the body, realising that their arms had stress factures, signs of potential torture. With Elisa’s help, they stack the boulders up, forming a pillar that could not be formed by any human. A sinking feeling washes over the two as Nyktos theorises that the town may have been a dollhouse, play area of a Giant. 

  

Meanwhile, Tiny, Lucatiel, and Severin explore another section of town. They find the roof of a building caved in, alongside two imprints on the ground below. They surmise that a Giant had sat on the building, using it like a stool. Traversing further, they find poles situated about. The pole has a hook, with bits of rope left on it. Beside that is a boulder, similar to the ones that the others had found before.  
  
And just like the other one, they see limbs sticking out from under it. Without hesitation, Severin walks over to the boulder, exerting his strength as he hurls the boulder off the corpse. As he does, a charcoal scent lingers in the air. They put two and two together, realising that individuals were forced to hold onto the rope, keeping the boulder up from crushing the other person. Clear signs of the town being part of someone’s sick game. 

  

The party gathers, sharing what they had found with one another. They realise the townsfolk were all gathered in one spot, surrounding a storage house of sorts.  
  
Taking the potion of gaseous form they had from before, Nyktos suggests using it to check the situation out  whilst keeping a telepathic link from Elisa to keep an open line of communication. Sneaking in, Nyktos sees a man, his limbs completely frostbitten, barely alive. Beside him is one of the transformed townsfolk. Returning to the party, they are confused, unable to understand why the man was left alive. Coming up with a plan, the party decides that Tiny and Vhagar would act as a distraction, drawing the townsfolk away from the house. 

  

As they enact their plan, it doesn't go as they thought it would. The townsfolk deal more damage than expected and with no other choice, the party attacks. They make their way through, finally reaching the storage house. Lucatiel heals the man who wakes up. The man sees the female townsfolk by his side and cries, begging the party to end their agony. Fen shares that a tall man with skin as cold as ice had tortured the town. Fen also describes an individual with two heads, chains, wielding an axe with frozen tongues.  
  
Having shared all that he knows, he seeks the party's help to end his and his wife’s Diana's suffering. Lucatiel offers Fen a chance to allow them to seek a cure, though Fen's mind has been made up. As per his wishes, Elisa has her magic channel towards Diana, ending her unlife and sending her soul to its rightful place. With his wife freed, it is now Fen’s turn. He is justifiably afraid. Elisa casts detect thoughts, attempting to use her psionics to offer comfort and peace to his mind. Lucatiel shares tales of  books they have read and what they say of the afterlife.  
  
Nyktos affirms Lucatiel’s words, using her credibility as a cleric of the Sovereign of Life and an elf closely linked to the Goddess of Death. She assures him that she will hold a ritual to ensure that his soul is to be sent to the good place, that should he and his wife have fate, they will find each other again in the afterlife. Her words offer the man much comfort, as he wholly believes the cleric before him. Closing his eyes, Elisa’s magic takes over bringing him to the afterlife that awaits him.  
  
The party is left with the rest of town, the civilians still cursed with undeath. Though they are short on time and resources to deal with it right now. Nyktos tries to reach out to her deity, hoping that they could help but there remain no answers to her prayer. The party agrees to come back later to deal with the situation. Severin grabs a handful of dirt, placing it in a bag as he vows to return to the place and help the citizens. With that, the party moves on for now. 

  

They then come across a tower, loud cries emitting from within as the entrance to the tower is held shut by a boulder. Lucatiel has their giant summon, Bunion, push the boulder, though Bunion fails. As the party approaches the boulder, some Orcs step out from the shadows. The orcs warn the party to leave their quarry alone. They express their desire to kill whatever is inside for food.  
  
The party is uncomfortable with that idea and each finds their way to delay the situation. They first pretend that the boulder is too heavy to be moved, suggesting that some of them would head up and drop some rope down instead. Tiny and Nyktos head up the tower and instead of letting down the rope, they head in. They come face to face with a Hill Giant, crying inconsolably. Through the tears and shouts, they manage to figure out that the Hill Giant is upset as her husband, Hruk was taken away by Chieftess Guh and that her attempts to recover him have been met with nothing but harsh mockery from her fellow Hill Giants. Tiny and Nyktos try many different ways to convince the Hill Giant to accept their help, but things do not go well for them as the Hill Giant continues to cry. 

  

Outside, the situation grows tense with every passing minute. The Orcs, fuelled by the need to feed them and their families, are desperate to kill whatever lies within the tower. Lucatiel initially offers to give some of the party’s rations to the Orcs, though they refuse to accept the kind gesture, sceptical about it. Instead, Lucatiel sells the rations to them at market price, two silver per ration. The Orcs readily accept the deal, handing over the gold in exchange for the precious food. But the rations alone are insufficient to sustain their tribe, as the Orcs once more express their desire to kill the Giant. Fortunately, between Elisa and Lucatiel, they are able to calm the Orcs, offering Bunion up as food. The Orcs accept the deal and following Lucatiel’s orders, Bunion follows the Orcs back to their home. 

  

Having settled the situation, Elisa and Lucatiel enter the tower to find that the others have failed to handle the situation inside. Elisa steps up and calms the Giant with her words. The Giant, Moog finally agrees to let the party help her, but only if they prove to be strong. She first scoops a pile of food in various stages of decay before the party, saying that a huge appetite is a sign of strength. Without a single word exchanged, they all look to Tiny, almosting expecting that they would be the one to eat the food.  
  
Tiny is unwilling to do the challenge, not wanting to eat the rotten food. The rest all offer words of encouragement, with Severin stepping up, telling Tiny that this may be their destiny. He claims that this could be the hill that Tiny has to cross which convinces Tiny to do so. Tiny eats the food, managing to not hurl any bits of the rotten food, successfully showing the party’s strength. The next activity is Goblin hucking. The Goblin begs to be free, unable to scream in anywhere but his mind, though Elisa communicates that all will be well. Lucatiel and Elisa head to an appropriate distance away, ready to intercept the toss with a feather fall spell from Lucatiel.  
  
As the Hill Giant throws the Goblin, the party enact their plans as the Goblin falls safely to the ground. They return with the same Goblin as the party tries to assure him that all will be well again, submitting him to the fate of being thrown once more. Tiny has a plan to assure their victory, they ungag the Goblin, feeding him a potion of flying and telling him to fly a long distance away. The Hill Giant questions what the potion is but quickly accepts the excuse that the Goblin is thirsty.  
  
The Goblin panics, but has no say as he gets thrown once more. The Goblin finds themselves disoriented from the toss, heading in a downward trajectory as Nyktos blood bends the Goblin, setting them on a path. The Goblin moves forward, before taking a sharp right and flying out of view. This confuses the Hill Giant but the party smoothes it over by claiming that Tiny simply threw the Goblin so far. Elisa summons FlipFlap and chases after the Goblin to free him of his bounds and inform him that the potion would only last for an hour, warning him to not fall to his demise. 

  

With that, the Hill Giant is convinced of the party’s strength. She offers the party some of her rotten food, which they kindly toss aside without her knowing. She also informs them of the location of the Giant camp. The party eventually appears before the Giant’s camp, watching as three individuals huddle around a fire, trying to stay warm in this now cold environment. They first attempt the nice approach, though as that quickly fails, combat begins.  
  
They end the lives of two of the three, promising to set the final one free if they divulge some information. Before they can set him free, Vhagar grips their axe, slicing into him mercilessly. The violence is uncomfortable, ruthlessly harsh compared to the party’s usual actions. Nyktos and Elisa don’t take kindly to Vhagar’s actions, with Elisa warning him that such continued actions would lead to problems. Nyktos gets into an argument with Vhagar as well, but Vhagar walks off, finding it meaningless to argue with them. Seeing the whole situation, Lucatiel suggests for Nyktos to be nicer to Vhagar. 

  

The party finds the entrance that the Hill Giants use, a boulder blocking the door such that only a small creature could sneak in. Elisa and Nyktos then convince Lucatiel to do some reconnaissance, which the Myconid somewhat agrees to. Lucatiel finds an empty hallway, some ropes, and the other ice door mentioned by the people outside. Very confident in their stealth, Lucatiel proceeds to head down the steps, not waiting for their party to join. The rest do eventually enter, and as  Lucatiel continues down the giant steps, the others find interest in the ice door.  
  
Nyktos suggests that Tiny should lick the door, and Tiny attempts to open it, triggering a trap. A spell begins to take hold of Tiny and Vhagar as they begin to bleed uncontrollably. Recognising the spell that caused the disease, the party starts to realise just the level of threat they are dealing with once more. Nyktos cures Tiny and Vhagar as they try to open the door once more. They succeed and walk in to find four individuals. A cleric, fighter, wizard, and commoner are trapped in ice, their bodies posed like trophies kept for someone’s sick pleasure. It would appear that whoever toyed with the town from before resides within this place. 

  

The party melts the icy bounds, freeing the individuals as best they can. Nyktos looks at the cleric, trying to discern who their chosen god might have been, though all she is able to find is the shattered remains of what was a holy symbol. Lucatiel finds magical scrolls on the wizard, and Tiny finds a sword. They take what they can find, vowing to avenge the deaths of the victims, placing their bodies into Tiny’s bag of holding. 

  

They continue their way down and soon come across a Giant’s body, already long dead and decayed. Nyktos observes the body, identifying that they had died from a series of bludgeons from clubs. She looks at what remains, trying to find any identifiers but finds nothing and moves on.  
  
Meanwhile, Tiny and Elisa spot a sleeping ogre, his head resting atop a bag of what must be faeces given how bad it smells. The ogre is left alone and they move to check on the other rooms. Eventually, they all move on, heading towards the stairs. But before they do, a voice enters their minds, asking for food. Tendrils emerge from the piles of trash as Otyughs speak to the party. The party does not wish to fight the Otyugh’s seeing as they are knee deep in trash and other disgusting wastes. Tiny recalls the pillow the Ogre is using and mentions it, which the others agree would make good food for the Otyughs. They all also agree that Tiny should be the one to grab the bag, seeing as this is their journey of self discovery. Severin steps up, placing a hand on Tiny’s shoulder and telling him that this is the rain that falls on the hill, that it is Tiny’s destiny.  
  
Curious, Lucatiel telepathically asks Severin if they are telling the truth, learning that the garnet wielding mage has been conveniently using their prophetic dreams to avoid having to dirty their hands. Tiny agrees and Vhagar is unhappy but goes along with it. They sneak into the Ogre’s room, the rest staying far away as they watch. Tiny and Vhagar grip onto the sack, pulling it out in one smooth motion when the Ogre stirs, tossing over before going back to sleep. Relieved, they drag the sack to the Otyughs and are thus allowed to pass. 

  

The party heads past, coming to a sight of Hill Giants bashing their skulls into each other. They make their way through, speaking to the Giant in charge, Loog. Loog brags about being the best husband, and shares his fond admiration for Chieftess Guh and her roundness. The party asks Loog to bring them to where Hruk and Chieftess Guh would be, which he agrees to if the party beats the Giants at their own games.  
  
The first game to be played is skull bashing, where two individuals are to bash their skulls against one another until someone passes out. The party readily agrees and are led to the circle where the game is held. Representing the Giants is Tubbler. As for the party’s representative, they all take a step back, leaving Tiny and Vhagar at the front to participate in the game. Fortunately, Tiny and Vhagar manage to knock the Giant unconscious, earning them a win for the first game. 

  

The second game is Goblin splatting, an updated version to the Goblin hucking they had played before. Like before, Lucatiel disrupts Loog’s throw by feather falling the Goblin. Meanwhile the party have another trick up their sleeve, Tiny and Vhagar toss the Goblin up, and as the Goblin falls down, the Goblin gets saved and a wave of blood gushes on the floor though the timing is delayed compared to what should have happened. Loog is confused, not understanding how it happened. Though Lucatiel and Tiny step in with an explanation, claiming that they threw the Goblin so hard that the world took time to process. Completely buying into the narrative, Loog drags the party over to the next game, fighting. 

  

They are to have a full blown battle, not with the intention to kill but for fun. Loog claims that if the party wins, he’ll lead them to Chieftess Guh himself. Though Nyktos sees through his words, it’s clear that Loog is just happy to play games with smallfolk and will lead them to Chieftess Guh regardless of the outcome. The party has a fun time play-fighting, Tiny and Vhagar being the first out from the party’s side. They head to where the food is, Tiny scorching the meats whilst the two share a meal, Vhagar experiencing the world outside of their metal body for the first time. Meanwhile, Lucatiel desperately attempts to identify the magic circle, only to be picked up and hucked many times, even being spun around in circles by the Giants. As their game comes to a conclusion, Lucatiel manages to identify the circle as a teleportation circle. 

  

They all cheer at the completion of the game, making comments about butts and what not when unbeknownst to them, the circle begins glowing. Nyktos is the only one to notice but as her friends are too preoccupied with their cheers and that none of them even knows what the circle does, Nyktos grabs Crimson and hides just in case. Much to everyone’s horror, stepping out from the circle aren’t more Hill Giants or Chieftess Guh herself. Instead, Frost Giants step out, the true residents of the space, the creators of the ice trophies and of the cursed town. 

  

Zephyros recognises one of the individuals, Jarl Storvald. He urges Elisa to leave at once. Though the party and the Hill Giants are confused, still riding on the high of their games. Jarl Storvald curiously asks why they are in his home and cites the rules of hospitality, offering false kindness to them. The Hill Giants speak up, telling Jarl Storvald to be gone, that the ordning is over. Jarl Storvald takes control of the situation, killing one of the Hill Giants before grabbing Loog roughly and asking the party why they are here.  
  
When they reveal that they were here to see Chieftess Guh, Jarl Storvald expresses interest in knowing where she is as well. He holds onto Loog, asking him to reveal the location, Though being the best husband that he is, Loog refuses to divulge any information, claiming that he doesn’t know. Jarl Storvald breaks Loog’s arm, prompting Elisa and Lucatiel to urge Loog to tell the truth. But Loog refuses once more, earning him a second broken arm. Seeing as the situation is really bad, Lucatiel contemplates the situation, ready to teleport them out of danger but finds themself unable to spot Nyktos and Crimson.  
  

Not only that, the scroll would only allow Lucatiel to bring up to eight other willing creatures, the party alone has five other members. To make matters worse, more Frost Giants appear through the teleportation circle, including an individual with two heads and a weapon covered in frozen tongues. Jarl Storvald greets his two sons, Hvitserk & Ubbe, and the party finds themselves in a terrible situation.  
  
Taking advantage of her stealth, Nyktos tries to put Crimson into the bag of holding but Jarl Storvald realises, appearing before her and placing his hand between them as he picks Crimson up. This had Nyktos frozen, immediately begging Jarl Storvald for what he wanted, not willing to let Crimson get hurt. Jarl Storvald laughs, the desperation of their cries are music to his ears.

  

Severin looks at the party, telling Elisa to bring the party out of there. All too familiar with others sacrificing themselves for her, Elisa grabs a hold of Severin, wanting him to stop. Severin continues, his body already changing as red scales begin to cover his form, his face morphs into a snout as he grows. But the transformation stops too soon, revealing only a young dragon. Still this excites Jarl Storvald, as he proclaims that he hasn’t had a dragon to toy with in such a long time. He grabs ahold of Severin, expertly breaking him in, the Frost Giant is extremely familiar with torturing dragons.

  

The party has to make a choice; Fight, or Run. 

  

Tiny’s and Vhagar’s stance is clear, they will not let Severin get hurt. Elisa knows Tiny and Vhagar, promising to stay by her friend’s side. Utilising her connections, Elisa reaches out to Princess Serissa, asking for her help as she informs the Princess that they are about to die to some Frost Giants. Instead of the Princess Evoice, Elisa hears the horrid sounds of an eldritch monster. It would appear that the Princess is currently indisposed, busy having her child. Elisa’s expression drops, her hope lost. Tiny and Vhagar rush up to Jarl Storvald, driving their axe into him with all of their might. Jarl Storvald takes the hit, barely feeling anything from it. Nyktos knows where most of her party would stand. She contemplates using the soul coin and the ring of the desires, burning the soul within to get a chance to bring her friends out alive, not wanting Severin to sacrifice himself.  
  
Still she doesn’t, instead choosing to fight and die with her friends if it is what has to be done. She reaches a hand out, channelling her magic to summon Jack, hoping that her Iron could buy them enough time whilst they run. Ubbe reaches a hand out too, the rune on his hand sparks as he disrupts Nyktos Emagic, breaking her spell. With her only chance gone, Nyktos drops Crimson down.  
  
She utters a command to him, the one meant for him to run off even if she’s in danger. But Crimson is nothing if not loyal as the pup stays by her side, tail tucked between his feet as if he’s done something wrong. Lucatiel enacts their part of the plan, knowing full well that the teleport spell can only bring eight other people as they decide who to take. They try to cast the spell, but it fails. With there being as many Frost Giants as there is the party, they are overpowered, unable to do anything. 

  

Jarl Storvald has the party rounded up, captured for his games. As he does, he sees the standard given to them by King Baldwin. He looks at it curiously, wanting to know where it comes from and where this place could be. The party says nothing in response to his questions. Elisa psionically reaches out to ClipClop, telling him to lead the horses to run away, to stay safe until they can find them once more. With that, the Heroes of the Mist, the Left Hand of King Baldwin have lost. Captured by the sadistic Jarl Storvald, their fates now his to toy with.
