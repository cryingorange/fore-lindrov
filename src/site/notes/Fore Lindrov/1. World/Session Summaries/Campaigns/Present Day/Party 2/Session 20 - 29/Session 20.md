The party appears in front of their home.Vizeran shows up, asking them for information and updates regarding where Brom is, Vizeran also laments that Vanifer has something that she needs the party to help her with and that he himself will also have something for the party soon. The party carries on to greet their friends. Elisa eagerly introduces Zipzap to Beatrice and Kashin, asking them for advice on how to train Zipzap. Alastair then greets the party, inquiring upon their missing party member Brom, to which the party shares that Elisa holds his ashes and that his soul resides within the lantern Aurelion carries. Alastair suggests a funeral and perhaps liberating what's left of Brom's soul from the lamp before returning to his rest. 

In Dave's room, the party shares what they've learnt about Goetia and his party. With Dave's knowledge, the party has a clearer view of what they're up against, though that doesn't deter Elisa's or Aurelion's determination. During the conversation, Amunkhet busily works on mummifying a body, coming to a sad realisation herself as she does. The party continues, meeting Warchief Ront, Pulliver, and the children. Warchief Ront shows them the banner he's made of the Warchiefs and asks where Warchief Brom went. Aurelion fibs that Brom's gone to the sky camp now, Warchief Ront nods understandingly and promptly updates the banner to showcase all the existing Warchiefs sans Vesper. The party is also given a flag that they could bring into battle. Elisa gifts the children the music box she had, providing them with loads of entertainment. Finally entering their room, the party sets Arche down and all take a much needed rest.

The next day, the party takes on Alastair's advice and holds a small, respectable funeral for Brom, burying his ashes in the grave that he had made before. For the rest of the week, the party spends their time doing their own thing. Amunkhet continues to mummify the body, Vesper entertains the children, and Elisa builds a well for them that Aurelion fills with his decanter of endless water. Elisa attempts to train Zipzap, which does not go well, prompting Amunkhet to begrudgingly train Zipzap instead. During their final day of rest, Vesper finds Aurelion, who was busy writing a constitution that aligned with his morals and beliefs. She asks him about Ozul, seeking to understand who he is before revealing what the 'God Savras' told her, that finding Ozul would lead her to her husband. Their talk gets interrupted as a paper bird flies into the room. Aurelion reads the letter and instantly summons a party meeting informing them that they've been called to the city of Gauntlgrym by his friend and partner Damien.

The party travels forth, saying their goodbyes as they once more embark out to the lands of Fore Lindrov. As they travel, the party comes across the Stilts and learns from Elisa that they should leave the Stilts alone. Though that doesn’t happen as a bunch of robed figures appear, slashing and consuming the Stilts. The party rushes ahead to help save the Stilts and after a quick battle, manages to save most of them. Vesper learns that the robed figures are disgruntled wizards of sorts who found out that consuming Stilts gives them power. One of the remaining Stilts speaks with the party, expressing their gratitude and sharing some information with the party. The party learns that one of the Stilt lives in a Utopia somewhere, that there is a warband of dog demons nearby, and that there have been more travellers in caravans as of late. 

With that, the party moves on and comes across a settlement and something they have not seen in a long time, horses. The party sees some men near the horses, presumably their owners. Curious, the party approaches the camp, with Elisa speaking to the person in charge. Vesper sees the men and horses for what they are, ghouls and their undead steeds. Vesper calls for a party meeting to discreetly inform the rest of the true nature of the men and Amunkhet surmises that they are scouts.

Elisa spends the night casting plant growth to aid with the food resource of the settlement and during this the ghouls, still in their disguise, come up to speak with her. Amunkhet sees this and intervenes, trying to pry for information though the ghouls keep things close to their chest. The party continues their travels the next day and spot a wooden caravan ferrying a family of halflings being ambushed by a bunch of gnolls. They jump in to save the family and fortunately manage to do so. During the battle, the halflings drop their disguise and the party comes to meet a family of fairies, Alvar, Ivy, and their son Lilup. As Amunkhet realises that they hail from Winblehuet, the fey kingdom that resides on the surface. Alvar shares that they left their home and upon questioning reveals that the situation in Winblehuet has turned tyrannical, though asks the party not to focus on that. They also learn that they’re heading in the same direction and so the party tags along as they travel to Gauntlgrym. 

As they finally reach the Mithril gates of Gauntlgrym, Vesper recognises the gates as one that she’s seen before when scrying for her partner. The party meets Damien and starts to learn more about Aurelion  E friend, though the rest of the party aren’t as excited to meet him as Aurelion is as Damien starts to express his rather racist thoughts. They are then led to the King Bruenor Battlehammer who, alongside with his guards, seem to show clear distaste for those they consider “dark races E

Elisa with the use of detect thoughts, learns that the King’s guard seem to be on very high alert as they almost expect the party to hurt the King. The party also learns that the King and his advisor Targrim genuinely believe in their cause of protecting the Dwarves of Gauntlgrym, the King then tasks the party to collect treasure as proof of their capabilities, mentioning that the abandoned City Levels are dangerous.

In the old city of Gauntlgrym, the party sees the ruins and dead bodies of those who lived there. They hear sounds of crying as they make their way through the rubbles of a fallen house to find an Orc, surrounded by the dead bodies of his friends as he cries. Just as they are about to help him, some monsters appear and battle ensues. It takes awhile but they manage to finish them off. The Orc, introducing himself as Jub, desires to pay his blood debt by leading the party to the treasure, sharing that he knows of a safe route. And so eventually, the party is led to a hallway lined with effigies of dead goblins and other races. Vesper uses her eye of Vecna to see through the door, witnessing a Werewolf Tiefling and three goblins as they seem to plead to someone. With no other choice, the party heads in. They see the 16 goblins that the werewolf was trying to plead but a trapdoor opens as the 3 goblins and Werewolf fall through, the werewolf swearing vengeance on the party should they harm the goblins. Not wanting to fight, the party tries to negotiate, though the goblins insist that they can take them on, and share that their master was more terrifying and powerful than the party. They learn more of the master, a “death dragon Eas the goblins say and how he is very strong, with black skin and paws.

Amunkhet manages to subdue most of the goblins before heading down to speak with the werewolf. Vesper and Aurelion attempt to intimidate them but to no avail. Elisa then tries the nice route, speaking to the goblins and comforting them when they seem self deprecating. Somehow, it works and the party is allowed through. The werewolf introduces herself as Daegra and the party introduces themselves as well, though Aurelion receives a cold shoulder from Daegra who seems to dislike him. The party quickly learns that Daegra and her group are from the MLA, which explains her distaste for Aurelion. In defiance, Aurelion refuses to stand near Daegra, something the party has never seen him do. They thread on and come face to face with this…big statured Tabaxi male. He drowns on in a self indulgent monologue and expresses pride in killing the innocent goblins that offered him shelter and food. This angers the party as they all instantly attack the ignorant guy and quickly kill him. But that seems to only be the beginning as his chest rips open and the party watches as an Atropal is birthed, its mere presence seems to kill everyone. In a desperate attempt, Vesper once again reaches out for help and asks for the Atropal to be removed. Though she feels something sinister reaching out to take the Atropal for consumption. Conversely, she feels a magic reaching out from her eye, willing to stop and change this from happening. She allows it and instead, she casts wish with nothing happening as the battle continues. The party stands their ground, with Amunkhet risking her life.

And just as she’s about to fall, a Lich with horns appears. He introduces himself as someone with many names, “The Devourer E “Lord of Unlife E “Acererak the Eternal E He questions why the party was trying to kill the Atropal and then moves on to paralyse Amunkhet, calling her a replacement for the Tabaxi they killed. Elisa reaches out to her god, and does as her god says, offering herself up as she seems to happily accept. Aurelion and Vesper attempt to stop this, trying to talk Acererak out of it but fail. In annoyance, Acererak casts a spell on Aurelion, causing him to writhe in pain. After letting everyone say their piece, he continues on and does as he wishes. Once done, he offhandedly apologises to Aurelion, and modifies his memory to forget the pain. He also charms Daegra into revealing her hate for the party and her relationship with Ozul. She shares her reason for her hatred of humans, how they killed her family, her daughter and how they assaulted her after. The party understands and most respects Daegra’s hatred for them. Acererak then casts wish, modifying the memories of the goblins and Daegra. With that all done, the party takes the treasure that they were there for and teleports out to where Damien awaits using the spell gem that Acererak gave them.

They put Amunkhet and the treasure in carts before appearing once more before the King, who gives his approval of them and allows them to learn what the treasure is for. The King’s guard, Targrim leads them to the forge where they meet Themberchaud, the Wyrmsmith in charge of supplying the required heat the forges of Gauntlgrym need in order to work their Ore. The party then goes into the inn to take a rest.
