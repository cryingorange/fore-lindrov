# Session 31

Finishing up their business in the Temple of Shar, the party wakes up one of the clerics, allowing Elisa to speak with him. The cultist, initially closed off, mocks Elisa for her young age, criticising her lack of understanding of pain and suffering. Though softens when Elisa shares the downfall of her home. With further convincing Elisa, manages to convince the cultists to join their settlement, assuring them a new place and which they can shift their dedication for Shar to their fellow friends and new ones. During which, Nyktos receives a sending from Minerva, stating that she required the party to return as soon as possible because she needed fire. With the spell gems gifted by Iymrith, the party and the ex-cultists head back to the settlement where they are met by Kleve, Vizeran’s pet Death Slaad, munching on handfuls of nuts. The party settles their new friends in, handing over more responsibility to Pulliver, who seems both stressed and delighted that Eilif would be able to help with the expansion of the settlement. The party then heads to Minerva who is as usual, deep in her research on the strange biomass collected from the sight of old Zaratan. She sends the party to collect flame from a fire elemental, handing Elisa a spherical device to house the flame. The party is initially puzzled about where they could get a fire elemental from, though with some prompting from Minerva, they recall about Elisa’s friends in the fire cult. With that, the party plans to set out in a couple of days. 

As they return to their living quarters, Khafra pulls a prank on the party, pretending to freeze like a statue. His prank prompts Rook's concern as the paladin falls for the prank offering the wyrmling gold, food, and a massage. Elisa , however, realises the situation as she sees Khafra sneakily stealing more of Rook's gold. Casting detect thoughts confirms her suspicions, as she plays into the prank, prompting Rook to dedicate more time and efforts massaging the wyrmling. Nyktos mentions informing Rook of the Dread Realm to Elisa, who agrees to showing him the next day. Meanwhile, Ilenti works hard to merge her Ollamh Harp with rock music, getting increasingly close to figuring it out. Nyktos takes the time to educate the children on the rules of Fey through stories, telling them about an adventurer's time through the Fey Kingdom and the lessons they've learnt. Her creative tale captures the attention of the children, who now remember the rules of Fey by heart. During the night, Khafra picks his next potential prank victim and sneaks to the door of one of the Shadar Kai. Unfortunately for him, Nyktos notices the sounds outside her room and opens her door to see the wyrmling there. Having been found out, he blows a raspberry and turns around, heading back to sleep after an unsuccessful prank. 

As the next day rolls around, the party gathers to share the truth of the afterlife with Rook. Ilenti proceeds to retell her experiences with Rook, though the unclear story leads to more confusion than understanding. Left without a choice, Elisa and Nyktos attempt to gently explain what he's about to see. With everything set, Nyktos casts silence to prevent alarming the rest when Rook inevitably comes back screaming. As Elisa's finger comes in contact with Rook, he finds himself in a dark place. He calls out for the party, warning them to stop their joke. There is, however, no response. He then senses a presence behind him and upon turning around sees a giant eye right in front of him. As the dread realm experience continues, he comes across the creepy bone white figure with a bulbous head that Ilenti mentioned. He finds himself stuck, unable to move as his mind fails to comprehend the sight before him. The eyes on the creature's head open, filling up the holes. The creature then reaches out for him, attacking him with acid, burning half of his face off before slashing him down. Rook then finds himself brought back to reality, any screams and sounds muffled by the silence spell. Rook is alarmed to say, drawing out his weapons towards his party. The others are surprised by his actions, though it is clear that the spell is no longer required. Nyktos gives a countdown, dropping the silence around them. As she does, Khafra wraps his tail around Rook's arm, restricting the paladin from potentially attacking. The party tries to calm Rook down, urging him to put away his weapons and fortunately he does. Elisa reiterates that what he had witnessed was where souls went when they passed. Rook questions how to stop the eldritch horror and his shock seems to be settled for the most part. Nyktos also takes the time to inform Rook of their recent trade with a fey and warns the party of the rules of fey. With everything settled and Rook all caught up, the party rests and soon heads out for the fire cult. 

Along the way, they come across a group of humans, dressed in shabby clothing, their hands cuffed at the wrists. The party rushes forward to their aid with Rook at the front line, prepared to attack whoever they were running from. Soon, three Drows from House Mizzrym appear, warning the party to return them their property. Ilenti insults the Drows and battle commences. During which, the drow seem disinterested in fighting the party, instead focusing on the people. As the leader shoots his arrows, quickly killing off two of the slaves, Nyktos uses the Sanctum Amulet Eilif had gifted her to save the female slave’s life. The other, unfortunately, falls to the ground dead. The Drows cowardly antics annoys the party as Ilenti pulls out her Ollamh Harp, strumming it and in her anger less ideally than Aunty Greta would have liked. An energy surges through her veins, causing them to light up with a green hue as she casts fire storm, burning her enemies. She then senses something, an ability permitting her to use the Harp more than she normally could. She strums once more, causing the fires to further burn the enemies as her veins glow red this time. The drow stood no chance, and their leader soon fell as Nyktos opened up all of the wounds on his body, drawing out every last drop of his blood before dumping it on the ground before him. The party frees the humans, Arthur, Merra, Dunken, and Castor, from their bounds before leading the exhausted people to a safe location to rest for the night.

The next day, the party speaks with the slightly less exhausted people and invites them to the fire cult where they could stay and rest. They agree, and share more about where they came from, how the drow had released them to hunt for sport. Conversations are had and with another day's worth of travel, the party comes across a group of brightmire merchants who are fascinated with the dragon that they seem to have on their hands. They proceed to inquire about the dragon though the party fibs that he is instead a statue. As the merchant approaches to analyse the “statue E Elisa and Nyktos work hard to distract the others by bombarding them with questions of trade. Meanwhile, as the merchant gets close, Khafra scares the man, laughing at him. As Khafra’s identity gets questioned, the wyrmling pretends to be a statue who thinks himself a dragon, Ilenti joins in on the act, convincing the merchants. They then complete their transactions and head off. 

During the night, the party is visited by Aunty Greta who was initially enjoying Khafra’s jokes before they crossed the line. The shadar kai, having finished their trance, emerge from their resting area to speak with the Fey. Aunty Greta proposes a trade for the displacer beast milk as before, asking Ilenti what she has to offer this time. As she scans the group, her gaze falls to Rook and Khafra. She offers Rook some Giant meat to feed the wyrmling and the paladin asks Aunty Greta what she wants in exchange, accidentally agreeing to her offer. Now the paladin owes Aunty Greta whatever she wants. Aunty Greta, noticing the dead body on the horse, offers the party a deal in exchange for reviving the man. Nyktos attempts to reject, stating that the party did not have the means to pay for it whilst Elisa shares that they did not want the old lady to strain her magic for them. Despite all that, Aunty Greta insists that it is no big deal and Ilenti eventually accepts the offer, now the party as a whole owes her an IOU too. The Fey stands up, and resurrects the dead slaves, her eyes rolling into the back of her head, very contrary to what Elisa expected to have happened. As for Ilenti, Aunty Greta brings up the two times Ilenti played the Harp subpar and the one time she used it for more than it was meant to be used for, her price for doing so is to use the Harp’s magic daily. Ilenti agrees and offers a card trick in exchange for the milk. She starts off having them picking a card, proceeding with an elaborate spring flourish. She then pulls a card from behind Aerith’s ear, fascinating the child, though Aunty Greta quickly picks up on the card’s location. Seeing this, Ilenti starts to improvise, her quick thinking leading her through to the end of the card show as she ends it off in a flourish, tossing the cards up into the air, letting them fall around her majestically. Still the performance was magnificent and Aunty Greta hands over the milk and meat before expressing that it’s good that they know, and that she’ll see them in the castle. Afterwhich, Nyktos and Ilenti have a disagreement over taking Aunty Greta’s deal to revive Arthur. However, Ilenti disputes that they would end up owing Aunty Greta sooner or later hence her decision. The other two party members step in, trying to smooth things over with the two. They do eventually calm down, with Ilenti taking out her guitar, strumming a tune that their mother used to sing to them. Relenting, Nyktos sits down as well, taking out her bass to play the accompanying parts of the song as the sisters reconcile through music. 

The next morning, Merra, Dunken, and Castor awake to see Arthur alive and all healed up. The party pretends to not know the truth, claiming it to be a miracle. The stragglers, having experienced too much in the recent days, simply accept and move on. Arthur introduces himself, stating that he was from the Brightmires and that they could head back to them but Elisa convinces him to see the fire cult for himself first and if they wish they could return to the Brightmires later on. With that the party continues and eventually reaches a giant black stone spire, where a magma elemental stands guarding the entryway. With Elisa’s connections, the party is allowed in and a female red tiefling with blueish black hair and orange eyes soon appears, rushing to give Elisa a hug. Accompanying her is a small child with a mushroom growing out of his head, the child asks for where Amunkhet is, wanting to show her his new drawing. Elisa placates the child and the tiefling, Vanifer, suggests that the child show the party his drawing instead. From high above, the party’s entrance is observed by Vizeran, the drow male accompanied by an undead drow, Zaraen. The party and the 4 people they brought are shown to their rooms by an anxious and stuttering Elizar. The party rests for the time being and explores the tower. Elisa and Nyktos both head to the library, with the former looking to enhance Zipzap’s intelligence. Upon entering the library, Nyktos is greeted by a cleric named Elisa who invites her to join in their prayers. The shadar kai gently refuses before Elisa enters with Zipzap in tow, introducing herself to Elisa. They inquire about some books before splitting up. Elisa finds an interesting picture book to read to Zipzap whilst Zephyros offers to help Nyktos translate the books. With Zephyros Eaid, Nyktos learns of a legend of a witch who was loyal to the lord of blood, and as a show of her loyalty had sacrificed an entire city. She was then rewarded with the form of blood. Meanwhile as Ilenti is wandering around, she comes across a young child. She approaches the kid who promptly reveals his passion for arson and drags Ilenti along to go burn animals. The entire situation creeps Ilenti out but the bard manages to free the burning animal before it’s too late. Ilenti eventually meets up with the others at the library and has a couple of questions for Elisa about the fire cult. Elisa then shares what she knows and of her friend who joined the cult, stating that he gained a resistance to fire and is presently stuck in a lamp. The interesting topic sparks a conversation as Elisa shares about the life of her old friend Brom. 

Soon, the party is gathered for lunch and are met with a wide spread. It is then that Khafra meets a red wyrmling, Fie, who immediately starts insulting one another. Zipzap and Elisa eagerly dig into the meal, whilst Ilenti messes with her food, pushing all her vegetables to Nyktos and swapping their plates because hers ‘looked like a lantern E They enjoy their meal, speaking with Vanifer who asks where the others are. Elisa, in an attempt to keep the happy mood, suggests that that is a topic for later. Though later soon arrives as they head back to Vanifer’s room. Ilenti notes the various artworks under the table, deciphering the sadness and hope behind the pieces whilst Nyktos notes the multitude of candles lit by Vanifer’s bedside, offerings to lives lost. Elisa shares the news of what’s happened to Amunkhet, Aurelion, and Brom, devastating the tiefling. Elisa also invites Vanifer to their settlement when she is able to, explaining that some of them have graves there for her to visit. Ilenti speaks up, comforting Vanifer by encouraging her love of art, that even if the sites painted aren’t true, the emotions behind them are, and that those emotions can inspire someone else to have hope too. The party notices a slight shift in Vanifer’s eyes, the look of someone who’s made a decision, even if it is one that they do not wish to do. Before the party leaves, Vanifer asks for their help to find the fire elementals, stating that they have been gone for a couple of days and have yet to return. Naturally, the party accepts without hesitation and leaves immediately the next day. 

Along the way, they come across a Brightmire settlement that is under attack and swoop in to save most of them. Nyktos helps the settlement by gentle reposing the body of the fallen guard, allowing his body to be protected for a short while. The Brightmire’s knight, Gilman, seeing the party’s capabilities sends them on a quest to quell whoever sent the monsters to attack them. Before accepting their request, Nyktos clarifies Elisa’s feelings about the Brightmires, noting Elisa’s slight dislike of them. Elisa shares that she was shot by one of them with a gun, but relents that there have been nice Brightmires. Following the tracks, they end up surrounded by three Minotaurs who do not take kindly to anyone aligned with the Brightmires. Fortunately, an old acquaintance of Elisa’s shows up, Wyx the Nothic vouches for the party and leads them to the Minotaurs camp. There, they find the fire elementals, Flaris, Ignay, and Azura. They learn about the situation, understanding that the Brightmires have taken something that would essentially submit a fire elemental to enslavement. Whilst the Minotaur’s leader seemed adamant on killing everyone in the Brightmire’s settlement, civilians included, the party did not. They asked for a chance to settle things diplomatically and the leader, a Minotaur named Zegdar, insisted on a trial to prove their worth. Naturally, the party strikes him down with ease and with his approval, they set out. Nyktos takes the time to send word to Vanifer, hoping to ease her nerves slightly. 

As they arrive once more at the Brightmire’s settlement, they are met with a strange, creepy man. The man offers no name, speaking in a robotic manner, his perfect posture creating an unnerving effect. The party offers up limited information, sharing that the brazier could solve the issue of the attacks. By scourging up what they have, the party manages to cough up 8,000 gold worth of items to trade. As the deal is completed, Elisa asks if the inquisitor would attack the camp nonetheless. To which his response was to ask if the camp had magical items. She says no and the conversation ends there. The party rushes back to the camp, with Nyktos sending word ahead for them to start packing, lest the Brightmires changed their mind. 

With the brazier acquired and everything packed, the fire elementals provide the party with the flame they require before leaving for the fire cult’s home. Upon entering, the leading Minotaur rushes to Vanifer’s side, kneeling before her, claiming her to be the one in his dreams. The tiefling blushes redder than before, her cheeks hot from the embarrassment as speculative mummers of her marriage float around, most notably contributed by Elisa and Ilenti. The mummers prompt Dill to ask Vanifer the same, flustering the young leader as she vehemently denies. Elizar, in his efforts, helps lead the minotaurs away from their blushing leader. The party is led to the meeting room once more, this time though there’s a more serious atmosphere. With the notable change in the air, Vanifer shares with the party the vague idea of what she’s about to do. That they would soon have to leave for the surface. As she speaks, it is clear that she wants no part of this, that the young leader merely wishes to revel in her little corner of the world, surrounded by friends and family. WIth that said, the others are invited in and lunch commences. The party works hard to make their last lunch in a long time as joyful as they can, sharing happy memories, laughter, and joy. Throughout the meal, there are times when Vanifer takes a step back, cherishing the last time she’d have this. As lunch wraps up, the party is once more led to Vanifer’s room. They’re brought before a chest that houses a picture of a grey drow girl named Arale. Vanifer starts to explain her life thus far, being a consort from the age of 10 and how Imix had promised her a free world. As she shared, her expression shifts, the once soft and kind gaze of Vanifer now hardened, more akin to that of a cult leader. She shares the rhyme Imix had told her, of what she had ignored. Nyktos reaches out to her, reminding her that though she’s taken on the mantle of leader, that it doesn’t mean that all the small family moments she enjoys are gone. Vanifer asks the party if they will remember her, and they resoundingly agree. Vanifer hands Elisa a symbol of the fire cult, an item that allows them to teleport back to the place. After this, Ilenti has stepped out of the room, brooding at the balcony outside of Vanifer’s room as she smokes. Nyktos leaves not too long after, heading off to prepare something for Vanifer. Elisa stays, speaking with the tiefling and providing her with the emotional support she needs. The two share a tight hug, a luxury that Vanifer might not experience in a long time. 

As Vanifer steps out, her robes now neat and tidy, looking like a proper cult leader, Ilenti stops her. They speak, with Ilenti sharing how Vanifer’s actions and decisions have helped her with her own life choices. Vanifer pulls Ilenti in for an embrace, whispering “You got this Ein her ear. Ilenti pulls out a locket, explaining that it is something she’s had all her life, and that Vanifer is to return it back to her someday. Vanifer graciously accepts, putting the locket onto her necklace, the locket resting right by her heart. Inside the locket rests a note written by a much younger Ilenti to her older self, “Did you make it? Eand “Are you happy? E As their moment comes to an end and Vanifer steps away to address her people, Nyktos rushes in front of her, with a pair of gloves in hand. She offers them to Vanifer, a pair of dark leather fingerless gloves with the words “Remembrance Eand “Forever Ehastily stitched on with red thread. Vanifer puts them on as Nyktos reassures her that she’ll do great, and that they’ll gather together someday, because they owe her a concert. Vanifer goes in for a hug which Nyktos returns by pulling her in close. Ilenti joins in, wordlessly hugging them both. Just as Vanifer is about to address her people once more, Rook steps before her. Used to the procedure at this point she offers him her hands. He holds them, stating that he has not much to offer her, but instead offers her a prayer that may Drakkar protect her as well. They then separate as Vanifer turns to leave, this time with no one left to stop her. 

The party hears Vanifer’s speech to her party, her speech rallying the cultists, prompting cheers and cries as their valiant leader shares what they must do. During which, the party is in tears. The siblings share a moment, with Nyktos crying heavily at the injustice of forcing a young child into a leadership position when she clearly doesn’t want to. Nyktos begs her younger sister to not die, that she’s aware of Ilenti’s passion for saving others. The younger shadar kai doesn’t respond, instead diverting the conversation towards the topic of the world and what must be done. Elisa talks to Zipzap about the situation that Vanifer is in, saying that it was nice while it lasted and enjoying a peaceful existence with Gilie and Vanifer. Elisa lamenting that she would’ve also wanted to live a normal life, one away from her vengeance. She parallels her situation with Vanifer’s saying that nice things don’t last and that people have to do what they have to get done. With that, Vanifer’s decision to step up as the leader solidifies the party’s plan forward. 

The party leaves the fire cult, heading back to their settlement. They deliver the flame to Minerva, who continues on with her research but waits for a more opportune time to share her results. 

Elisa leads the party to the gravesite, pointing out the individual thombs to Ilenti who requests that Elisa shares more about her fallen friends. Through tears and pained smiles, Elisa shares about Gobby, the loyal and heroic Goblin who shared a great love for the party, loving them til his last moment when he sacrificed himself to save them. Then Aurelion, an unfortunate soul who was constantly pummelled with challenges in life, even in his death when he made a deal with a fiend to save the party, trading away his life in the process when he allowed a fiend to sign on his behalf. Next was Brom, the man turned Vampire turned fire cultist and finally lantern. Of how he had sacrificed his paladin oath when they first met. And lastly, Ilythyrra, a member from Elisa’s first party. A quiet and somewhat creepy individual who would sneak up behind someone to whisper to them at the weirdest of times. Elisa shares that she didn’t get to learn much about her, and how she wished she had more time to get to know Ilythyrra. In response to hearing all that, Ilenti carves a gold piece for each of the fallen. For Gobby, “A hero who love & was loved by all E Aurelion, “We still smile in your memory E For Brom, “Sacrificed everything to keep us safe & in safety we prosper E And for Ilythyrra, “Watching over us, even in the shadows that surrounds us E The gold coins are placed atop of each of the graves, pressed into the ground to make them less obvious. 

After the long week, the party rests for the night and heads out the next day to the surface, following the location that Vanifer had told them. They come across a badly injured man, chased by hell hounds. They swiftly eliminate and harvest the hounds before being accused by the man who thinks that they are here to kill him. After realising that they are not, he introduces himself as Zhae Tsung and shares how he lived in a safe haven before demons came and killed everyone. He describes their leader to be a large red brutish beast, with horns, a skull mask, and a crossbow. He asks for the party’s help and naturally they accept, beginning the next leg of their journey.
