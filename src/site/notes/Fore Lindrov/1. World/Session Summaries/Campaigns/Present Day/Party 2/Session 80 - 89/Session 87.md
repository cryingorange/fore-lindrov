# Long Rest

## Elisa

The party takes their long rest. Elisa heads to her room, realising the lack of buckets, that used to be fetched automatically for her by her friend Elizar. She then heads to the storage room to retrieve them. As she does, she realises just how much effort it took Elizar to ensure that she always had them. Back in her room, she realises she has no way to conjure water, more so now that Elizar is no longer around. 

Searching for some answers, Elisa contacts her goddess. She learns that Aurelion wants to save his friends. And when asking what he’s up to now, the response is that Gauntlgrym is protected, implying that the ancient Dwarven Magic there blocks all sight into the city. 

Elisa also inquires if Ardor Mor has more than one mask, the exact number of which blips between two and three. Lastly, Elisa asks if she had made the right choice giving Ardor Mor the mask, the only response she gets is an unknown. She shifts the empty buckets out of her room, and rests with Zipzap. 

## Ivy & Roast

Grimrid heads to the same floor as Elisa, finding a comfortable wall to rest against for his slumber. Meanwhile, Ivy and Roast are in the library as Roast reads up on Imix and the fire cult whilst Ivy takes out a chunk of her side, making it the same shape as the missing piece of the table as she attempts to fix it. The table looks fixed so long as no one touches it. This act of self mutilation catches Roast by surprise as she asks Ivy if it hurts. Ivy shrugs, claiming to be used to it and that her body would grow back. Unsure of what to say, Roast tears off another strip of scrap material, tying a bow on Ivy’s tail who swishes her tail happily at the bow. 

## Grimrid's Dream

Once Zhae Tsung leaves the library, Roast messes with his set up, shifting books, changing chairs, moving the table a centimetre off. As Grimrid sleeps, he has a dream of him running to a large circle crafted by dwarves of his clan, seeing the person who chased him out. Then suddenly their neck is ripped out, by Grimrid in his werebear form as he swallows a pound of the dwarven flesh. Then the dwarf turns into his father, Lu, Elisa, Verena, Roast, Serissa before he wakes up where he sat down. 

# Next Morning

## Ardor Mor

The next morning, Elisa shares the news with the party, that Ardor Mor has more than two, maybe three masks. She also explains the origins of the mask which then Grimrid realises he had seen one such mask first hand. Grimrid creates a crude drawing of the white mask out of chalk, resembling an artwork done by a child whilst Elisa produces the notes she had stolen from Ardor Mor, perfectly depicting the mask. Grimrid shares that he had encountered the mask long ago with his party, accompanied by two others. They had given the mask to their friend Vulcan to be kept in his family vaults in a desert far away. 

Elisa, knowing Vulcan, tries to confirm if the Vulcan Grimrid knows is the same one she does. Grimrid describes the Vulcan he knew, a dancer who led the group known as the Vulcan force, a very flashy duo. Elisa is confused as the current Vulcan she knows is much more edgy than the one Grimrid knew. Roast steps in, pretending to not understand the dance that Grimrid describes, asking him to perform it for them, further encouraged by Elisa who challenges if Grimrid can’t dance. Grimrid then recreates the Vulcan Force’s introduction perfectly, loudly proclaiming each party member’s title, almost revealing his own but managing to cover it up well enough that no one suspects a thing. 

## Next Quest

With that, the party heads to the library, in search of the letter they had left behind. The party does not manage to find it. Elisa asks Zhae Tsung if he took the letter which he does not respond to, she attempts to read his mind but finds no thoughts. The party does ask Zhae Tsung for the information he has, learning that the Fire Giant camp are searching for another piece of the Vonindod but this time on the surface near Goldenfields. Both Elisa and Ivy also notice how Zhae Tsung’s dog is eyeing Elisa, looking at her as if she was a threat. 

Elisa hears the dogs thoughts planning how to kill Elisa should he need to. Meanwhile, Roast has been ritual casting detect magic, hoping to find the letter with it, instead she realises that there is a spell casted on Zhae Tsung, Mindblank. She shares this information with the rest of the party, all cautious as to how Zhae Tsung, a non magic user, is able to have such a high levelled spell casted on him. 

## Vizeran's Help

The party leaves Zhae Tsung alone and heads to Vizeran’s room where they find him writing up a spell scroll. Before they can say why they are here, Vizeran hands them a teleportation circle scroll, already knowing that this is what they would want from him as he sarcastically laments that he's been reduced to nothing but a Scroll Maker. He even says that he's now assuming that Vanifer won't even help him with his goal of ridding the Drow of Lolth. However disguised behind his words is kindness, an attachment to Vanifer that even he himself did not expect.

In his room are also two statues, one of Shar, and the other of Tharizdun. The party shares their concerns regarding Zhae Tsung, which Vizeran seems nonplussed about, claiming to have already known that Zhae Tsung had been dabbling in the art of magic. They also require a Sending scroll from Vizeran who begrudgingly begins drafting one as the party heads out. 

All but Roast who stands over Vizeran’s shoulder, looking at the spell he’s crafting. When Roast expresses her desire to learn, Vizeran begins writing more detailed notes into the spell scroll, his not so subtle way of teaching. 

## Vrolda

Meanwhile, the others head off to different rooms. Grimrid heads to Vrolda’s room, pushing the door open to find a smithy. Vrolda is inside, smithing and the loud clangs deafen Grimrid. He does eventually manage to catch Vrolda’s attention, seeking Vrolda’s insight against the fire giants he had previously infiltrated. He also comments on the spears on the walls. As it turns out, the spears were crafted by Vrolda’s father for him, the same spears that Vrolda will now use to kill his people. 

Vrolda laments that he never felt at home with the Fire Giants, and has betrayed his father, his brothers for the Cult. And now that he has failed his Prophet, he no longer knows where he even belongs. He calls himself an honourless cur that belongs nowhere. 

Grimrid relates, having been exiled by his own family. Vrolda then remarks that they shall both live in shame. Vrolda also shares his detest for Assassination with Grimrid and gives Grimrid a block of Adamantine, an unspoken gesture where Vrolda believes that Grimrid will be able to redeem himself, and that Vrolda himself will be forever marred by his mistakes. 

## Ken

Grimrid then heads out and bumps into Ken. A man barely dressed with great muscles. The two communicate exclusively through muscle poses, a brotherly bond forged the moment the two meet as they begin flexing together, finding out their names, who they are, what they believe in, and forge a bond as close as brothers.

## Gillie

Elisa and Ivy then head to Vanifer’s room, Gillie greets them both, showing them his newest painting, one depicting the funeral. Gillie then turns to Ivy, commenting that he’s never met a tree before and shows her a painting of a tree he did. He also begins asking a series of questions, wondering how Ivy drinks water without drinking the dirt and if she has roots. Ivy is at a loss, unsure how to answer the questions. She looks to Elisa for help who at first lets her struggle for a bit before turning Gillie’s attention to Grimrid. 

## Vanifer

Both Elisa and Ivy head to find Vanifer, who is lighting a candle for Elizar. Vanifer asks Ivy about her statement from before, learning that Ivy’s circle had left to venture and never returned. Vanifer can’t help but feel guilt for the situation, for crying so horribly when Ivy has lost her entire circle. Ivy comforts Vanifer, stating that she believes they are still out there as the power she gave them did not return to her. Vanifer hopes that Ivy can find her family, the two share a hug and find mutual comfort as Ivy tells Vanifer to not think her problems small. 

Elisa then brings up the topic of Nyssa, learning that Vanifer sees visions of Nyssa but is unsure if she should follow her dreams and her destiny whilst Elisa reinforces that Vanifer should do as she thinks is right, believing that they can rewrite their destiny. Vanifer requests for the party to reach out to Nyssa, so that mayhaps they could get some help or clarity. When Elisa offers the plate of food to Vanifer, Ivy senses that Vanifer wishes they could share a meal together yet does not dare to ask. Ivy subtly reaches her tail out to Elisa, getting them to connect mentally as Ivy shares Vanifer’s wish with Elisa who invites Vanifer for a meal. Vanifer heads down first, happy to be able to do something like the old times. 

## Meal With Vanifer

As Ivy and Elisa head out, they see Grimrid and Ken posing, with Grimrid joining Ken in his shirtlessness. The two continue their poses as they approach Ivy and Elisa, with Ken introducing himself to Ivy who is flabbergasted at the insanity she is witnessing before her is rooted to the ground in shock. Elisa runs away, escaping Ivy’s grasp by turning to slime. 

When Ivy is finally able to escape from Ken, the party regroups in the dining hall. Gillie gives Grimrid a painting of him, a perfect box. During the meal, Grimrid regales tales of cousin Bolin and of the dwarves, telling Vanifer that a dwarf would be a fine addition to her group. Vanifer asks if Grimrid has ever met any Minotaurs which Grimrid affirms, speaking of Tormund. As Vanifer politely says that the Minotaurs would be very fond of dwarves. Grimrid also mentions his new “brother E Ken, which Vanifer comments is very naked. 

But Grimrid insists that muscles are to be displayed even as Elisa tries to point out that there is such a thing as too naked, but Grimrid denies, insisting that beauty is to be shared. Elisa then changes the topic to chocolate, which Vanifer then changes the topic to Aurelion, asking Elisa about him as she lies that he is out finding himself.  Meanwhile, Fie eyes Ivy curiously, commenting that she looks flammable, and stating that there isn’t much here to burn that moves. 

To Ivy, Vanifer asks if she’s from a forest having never met a tree before as well. Ivy shares that she didn’t do much in the forest, not being able to leave, but shares that it was cooling. For Roast, Vanifer has questions when Roast places her mother on the table, feeding her the food provided. Roast clarifies that the chicken is her mother. 

As more questions arise. Vanifer tries to ask Roast about her home, which Roast shares is dark and far. During which Roast tries to hide the fact that her mother hates the place, and dislikes Vanifer. But Gillie and Elisa are able to sense that. Gillie, also curious, walks over to Roast, patting her leg before returning back. Using his telepathy, he asks Roast if she is a chicken and if she came from an egg. Roast agrees to being a chicken and says that all forms come from eggs. But Gillie denies, saying that babies come from storks. But as storks lay eggs, Gillie believes that all people come from eggs. 

Gillie is then curious where Ivy is from, and Roast affirms that Ivy comes from a plant egg. Roast helps to spread the spores, as Ivy joins the telepathic communications, Gillie pesters her with questions, asking if she too came from an egg. They are interrupted when Wyx bursts in, loudly proclaiming that Zegdar lives and that his death shall not be pinned on Elisa. They all hear the moo as Zegdar loudly proclaims his declaration of dedication to Vanifer, causing her to rush out quickly. Just as the party leaves, Ivy feels a small fire bolt shot in her direction by her tail, as she rushes down to angrily shove Zegdar, lamenting that his foul words of "Planting his seed into Vanifer" should not be used on the young girl.

Zegdar apologises, saying he shall be more soft in his wooing of Vanifer, still further drawing Ivy's ire, although she holds back her anger, seeing that any physical harm could cause Zegdar's death.

# Zipzapolis

The party then heads back to Zipzapolis, where they are nearly covered in feathers and grease by prank traps set by Kafra and Duncan. They realise their home is remarkably quiet. Investigating the party finds all the occupants of their home tired. They head outside to find abandoned market stalls, and some exhausted people still standing guard whilst the children play in a carnival. Confused, the party tells the exhausted ones to head in, promising to ask the carnival to leave. Before the guards, Kazarak's Hobgoblins, Kazarak, Ront and Grud head inside, they reveal to the PCs that the Knights have been moved out, and Ser Daron advised them to hide in their cave.

They also see all of the Children, especially the Mongrelfolk children playing at the Carnival, supervised by the Angel Vesper.Kazarak reveals their cave entrance being hidden by Illusion Magic is thanks to Kafra, and also reveals that before the Knights left, they treated injured Knights coming in over the weeks, who had limbs crushed, all signs of Giants causing trouble. They also learn that both Duncan and Kafra have become good friends, creating a Jokes Emporium where they sell prank items to people.

The carnival master is a man called Mr Light who claims to know much and to have travelled far and wide. He calls Roast by her name, Ophelia and refers to Grimrid with his full name, saying things not many nor anyone should know. Behind Mr Light comes out his son, Mr Light Junior. Looking at his son, Elisa shares the details of her deal with Micheal, about “taking the boy and running", suspecting that this could be the boy, Mister Light tells the party to enjoy his carnival, vaguely mentioning that the Carnival will be gone soon, that they are about to be acquired by a big business. The party tries their hand at some games. 

Roast first tries archery, and loses to a child. Then alongside Ivy and Grimrid head to the wood cutting competition. Grimrid wins but with some trickery Roast claims the victory by pushing Grimrid out of the way as Grimrid breaks the wood, earning Roast a wolf pelt. Elisa and ZipZap head to the Goat Riding Joust, playfully insulting one another as they both vow to win. Eventually, Elisa wins, getting a little marionette. 

## Fortunes

### Grimrid's Fortune

The party then heads into the fortune tent. Mr Light offers the party each a boon in exchange for answering some questions. Grimrid volunteers first, answering the first question of his identity as champion of Moradin, Cousin of Bolin, Exile. He continues explaining that he lost a fight, his craft, and place in the family. When questioned by Mr Light if he is still a Dwarf. 

Grimrid affirms and says that he might not be a dwarf in all aspects but craft is not the only aspect. Next Mr Light asks for his purpose. The mood turns somber, as Grimrid shares his belief that his cousin Bolin would have been there at the end of days and his mistakes caused him those he cared for. He aims to see this journey to its end, not for glory but to see the story to its conclusion. Mr Light asks if Bolin pushes Grimrid to his purposes and if Grimrid could even live up to him. Grimrid does not know but desires to do no more that will disgrace his cousin’s name further. For his fortune, Mr Light sees that Grimrid will die and no longer live in the show of his cousin but rather that of failure. As the smell of sulphur and rot begins to fill the space, Grimrid is shown the present fate of his people, working for the devils, smithing works with no heart. 

As a red skin devil commands them to work. Mr Light tells Grimrid that all Dwarves are doomed for hell and not only will Grimrid fail, he will be lonely. Grimrid states that he will right this wrong, not believing that deals alone could remove the pride of their people. That Dwarves have overcome every manner of trial, as they will this one. The party offers comfort to Grimrid, Roast offers the translation of the Devil’s words to Grimrid, how their high king had made a deal and sold all the souls of dwarves. Elisa affirms that they will help fix what was done to the dwarves. 

### Roast's Fortune

Next up is Roast, sat before Mr Light, he asks her some basic questions. Roast reveals that she has a family, not just her mother but a brother and father too. That she ran away because her mother told her to. That her mother is warm, her brother kind, and her father strict. Mr Light then asks Roast about her happiest memory, or if she even knows one. Roast lies, wanting to believe that the time with her mother, those days running away was happy for at least she could pretend they were safe. But they weren’t. Then comes the final question, who made her who she is today. She stays silent, genuinely wondering if she could say anyone else without it being a lie. Then softly, she mutters that it was her father who made her who she is today. 

A figure begins emerging from the fog, one that Roast knows all too well as she instinctively scoots back. The party watches as Roast, frightened, shrinks back into her younger self. Roast’s father appears, his eyes straying straight towards Roast, as he hisses that his daughter and her brother have the greatest weight ever beset upon Mankind placed on their shoulders. Then a change when his eyes widen, his posture straightens, he loses his cool ever so slightly as he spits at Roast, referring to her as someone that killed her mother to come to this world and now expects him to join in this farce and call her daughter. 

Roast tries to stay something, barely stuttering out that she didn’t mean to when the illusions fade. And Mr Light reminds Roast of her unwanted existence. The party approaches Roast, with Elisa telling Roast that she is wanted, and loved and desired but Roast rejects that, pushing Elisa away as she tells her that Roast can’t be weak and that Elisa will die, torn limb from limb by aberrations because of that weakness. Ivy offers comfort as well. Then with Grimrid, Roast says that they will someday have to face her father. 

### Ivy's Fortune

Then Ivy heads on, answering that her happiest memory was when she was with her circle, roaming around the forest as Mr Light comments on poor little Isse, poor man. Ivy shares that she left her home when her circle didn’t return and her shaman came back hurt. But Mr Light interjects as hurt is a light way to put it, the truth is that her shaman returned on the verge of death, begging to be saved. Ivy doesn’t respond but feels guilt. Mr Light asks Ivy if she thinks it was her fault that the shaman died. Ivy claims that the shaman isn’t dead but Mr Light points out that without a body, and no way to speak or feel, could the shaman truly be considered alive? Trapped in a cage, with no choice. As Ivy insists that the shaman isn’t caged, that the lantern was to protect what was left of her, to hold her until she could find a way to put the shaman back. 

Mr Light asks who Ivy wishes to speak to if she could, asking Ivy if the fate that Ivy has given the shaman is what the shaman would have wanted. Ivy affirms that she would have wanted to ask the shaman if what she has done is what she wanted, and for what happened that caused the incident to begin with. Ivy answers that she now travels to find a way to find them back but also admits that this ability to travel, to leave, feels freeing. Mr Light mocks Ivy, all it took for her to leave was for all her friends to die and now the shaman hangs at the hip of a devil all because Ivy didn’t read before signing a contract. 

Mr Light muses that Ivy never cared about her Circle, that she saw them as creatures without their own lives. That in her selfishness and her inability to be alone, she trapped someone's soul in a lantern without even asking them if they wanted to and lost that soul to a Devil. He tells Ivy that it is time for her to wake up. He ends his speech at her by asking Ivy if she is selfish, which she agrees she is. 

For Ivy’s fortune, Mr Light first shows the past. He shows the forest Ivy hailed from, how her and her circle crossed a log together day after day. But as the time passed, the stream grew smaller and smaller. Until one day the circle left in search of a way to fix this. Then they never came back. And a little spirit comes out, searching for its friends. But nothing can be changed of the past, only the future as they now see a dark forest, hear the clanking of something as a wooden warforged creature steps out. 

Ivy recognises this as the body of an elemental creature now morphed into an unnatural shape and is somehow still alive. But what is most important is legacy, as a tree with ivory hung on its branches approaches, and it’s clear, trapped inside the tree is Ivy’s circle. Ivy denies it, screaming at the vision, morphing once more as the vision fades, with it's lasting words being, "Look upon me.. This is your Legacy Ivy." Roast steps in, telling Ivy that her legacy is in her name, rooted, strong, green. As the others interject with better words, assuring Ivy that she has control over the story, and can decide what paths to take, and most importantly, they will bring back the sun once more. 

### Elisa's Fortune

Lastly is Elisa. Mr Light asks if she is distracting herself with what she wants instead of what she needs. But Elisa states that her purpose is to avenge people and fix them. Mr Light asks how much will Elisa sacrifice to achieve what she wants, although Elisa denies, saying that she has already given so much and only killed those who deserved it when Mr Light reminds her of all the people in Blingdenstone. And all the people who will get in her way between her and the archduke. Like Orik, who now has no arm, no soul, no family. Were those all worthy sacrifices in her pursuit for vengeance? When he asks what will make Elisa give up, she affirms that she never will. Elisa admits to not knowing if she has ever been truly happy, but she will give almost anything except for ZipZap and Milton to save her people. 

She would die for them or give up her vengeance if it would return them back to normal. As for what made her who she is today, it is being thrown into a position she did not ask for. Elisa’s words are then tested, the elder brain chanting “become Eand Sylvaerys both in front of her, with Sylvaerys dying out before her eyes and the tentacle reaching out to her. Elisa grabs her blade, pulling it out as she stabs the tentacle. The brain dies by Elisa's hand and Sylvaerys bleeds out. 

Finally, Mister Light leaves the party with an overall fortune, "The King distracts himself with his trinket, locked away in his Workshop.. But slay The Princess and The King will leave his Workshop and turn the land red twice over."

## The Red Horde

Then the party exits the tents, only to be greeted by a group of centaurs serving the Fire Giants, known as The Red Khanate or The Red Horde. The Centaurs try to extort the party, even threatening the children but the party has none of it as combat ensues. 

The party slays their enemies quickly with Grimrid single handedly holding back two swarms of Centaurs, Ivy hurts the enemies a fair ton with her use of spell and so does Elisa as she hurls the boulders back at the enemies. 

The enemies are dealt with swiftly, as Roast suggests to Ivy that she should bring the trebuchet back to ZipZapolis. Roast takes the opportunity to feed her mother and brings back the rest of the items to ZipZapolis. Vesper also speaks to Elisa, pointing out the worsening situation and that whatever it is they are doing they need to hurry as time is not a luxury anyone has. She does also tell Elisa that she hopes Elisa is able to have her happy ending. Which Elisa denies, not knowing if that is even possible. 

Back in their home, Elisa digs out a grave for Laura, as Ivy plants a flower on top of the grave to make it less empty. Elisa also reaches out to Vizeran for his help with ZipZapolis and Kleve is sent whilst the party heads out to Goldenfields.
