# Attack
Just as they are wondering what to do, Roast takes out the womb like structure that Morvek turned into, shaking it as she does all orb like objects before it begins to leak a black ichor, prompting Roast to drop it on the ground as Morvek is birthed out from it. The party expresses their concerns to Morvek but check that he is fine before moving on.

Elisa uses gust of wind to clear some of the fog, as they trudge through. Ivy, Grimrid and Elisa wade through the brush, whilst Morvek and Roast take the water route instead. As they pass through, the place smells like home to Ivy, as she recalls a memory of her and the original Ivy. Shaman Ivy remarks that spring is coming, plucking a small cluster of grass and handing it to Ivy as she reaches out for it, she feels its life, feels like her. Ivy also notes that the vines do not deter the elk’s movement but they do deter Ivy’s. 

Roast leaps past another fog wall, greeted by five awakened trees who attack them on sight. During combat, a primordial shows up, it moves past the party for the elk. Morvek was about to land the killing blow on the primordial when Ivy steps in, telling Morvek not to as Morvek redirects his attack. Ivy connects with the Primordial and realises that the primordial was trying to protect the elk, not hurt it, linking to a fellow guardian of nature, Ivy’s leaves grow fuller and greener for a moment. 

# Zenora

Following the Primordial, the party is led to an inner part of the rest, unaffected by Giant magic, where the remaining forest animals huddle together, Ivy notices that their bodies all bear grafts, bits and pieces of bone-like grafts that remind them greatly of the Primordial's body, albeit it now has large chunks of it missing, and its eyes bleed a black ichor like demonic ichor. The Primordial summons a wolf to communicate in his stead as he is no longer able to speak, after giving much of himself to save the forest animals. 

He shares that he had attacked the party due to the corruption he sensed in Roast, Morvek, and Elisa. Zenora recognises Ivy. He tells them how a white stag had brought the original Ivy to them, who gave her heart to awaken from a slumber he fell into during The Fall. Ivy learns that her circle had ventured here, coming to the realisation that they were helping people as they travelled looking for a way to save their own Forest. With the original Ivy willingly giving up a piece of herself in order to save this one. He gifts to Ivy, saying that he no longer has use of it, as he is dying.

Within the briarwood heart, Grimrid feels a magic similar to giant magic but much older. Zenora remarks that Ivy is much weaker than she should be. Morvek and Roast examine the heart, trying to figure out, immediately thinking how they could extract what power they could from this piece of Primordial Magic whilst Elisa speaks to Ivy. 

Elisa tells Ivy that the heart likely would not have enough to sustain one being let alone two, and tells Ivy that no matter what choice she makes, Elisa will support it. Looking at the heart, Roast and Morvek find it to be incomprehensible, something that is alien to their already alien nature. 

As Roast tastes the magic, there is a point where it tries to take over her but she is able to resist, as does Morvek. Upon realising what it can do, Morvek begins to muse on how the item can be used. Roast does tell Ivy that the heart is hers, encouraging her to try to absorb it. Grimrid provides the warm divine sunlight (Guidance), Roast draws the giant rune, Jera, on Ivy’s palm, hoping to help the connection with nature, Morvek thinks to what he knows of Ivy, empowering her sense of self (flash of genius). 

Ivy thinks back to when she first created original Ivy, and also remembers the first person she had shared her power with, Ise. And last they spoke, Ise, with a graft across his chest, tells her that he will be coming back. As Ivy absorbs the heart, she feels whole for the first time in a long while. Ivy grows, vines longer and leaves fuller as a yellow flower sprouts, blossoming into a fruit. 

Zenora remarks that he is unable to teach Ivy as he has little time left but perhaps his spiritual twin could, though he has long since left with a human.Zenora shares that his twin left as he was the more curious of the two of the, and with the passing of time, his name is forgotten. Roast tries to understand if they can save Zenora in any way possible but there seems to be little to no chance. Grimrid asks for the last words Zenora has for his brother. Ivy steps in, taking Zenora’s place in the ritual as she gives a part of herself to the Elk, the elk glows green, a bone plate growing around its chest. 

The party bid their farewells and left. Continuing on, they come to a horrific lair of rot and gore, massive swarms of gnolls, giants, maw demons and possibly flying demons. They ponder how to sneak in when Ivy feels a surge of rot, the corruption seeping through into her due to being in such close proximity to the Demonic presence. She feels parts of her sense of self dying.. The same kind of death that has already begun taking Zenora.
