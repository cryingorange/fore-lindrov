Heading to the second floor of the Red Belvedere, the party is greeted by a handsome looking Shadar Kai who calls out for Nyktos, complimenting her appearance. He does so with Wystram as well, shapeshifting into a drow’s appearance. To which Myrantar, amused by the man’s acknowledgement, suggests that she could keep him. But the man continues on, reverting to his original form of a humanoid with the head of a White Tiger which Nyktos knows to be a Rakshasa. The Rakshasa introduces himself as “Uvasharr E one of the Pit Masters. Following Uvashar, the party plays their first game, learning the ropes of the place. As Uvashar finishes explaining, Nyktos is quick to point out that Elisa might need some help on account of her lack of eyes. Though Uvashar is unable to provide any 

Tiny finds themself winning an awful lot of money, more than they have ever had in their entire life. The rest of the party resorted to some tricks. Nyktos utilises the very helpful drinks available at the Red Belvedere, whilst Elisa benefits from her psionics. Wystram excuses himself for a moment and returns as if nothing had happened. Though Elisa realises that there is another person following Wystram since his washroom escapade. Elisa tries to speak to Wystram, though he pays her no mind and instead speaks with the rest like normal. Not realising anything wrong with their friend the games continue. Their efforts have them winning some money as well as Uvashar’s promise to put a good word in for the party to Windfall, increasing their odds of entering the more private rooms. 

Along the way to the next game, Tiny very generously offers some talons to Nyktos who had gambled all of hers away. Moving on to the next bit of fun, the party enters the Scarlet Colosseum, all excited for a little bit of combat. As they enter, a Red Abishai lands before them. Khai Kiroth, the Pit Master of the Scarlet Colosseum. He remarks being excited at the strong faces that have graced his Colosseum and invites the party to partake in the game, luring them in with the potential rewards. The party agrees, and eagerly take their places as Khai Kiroth excitedly comments on releasing the more unique creatures that he has. 

The first round has the party fighting three Maws of Yeenoghu, Giant looking creatures who have been maddened by Yeenoghu’s touch. Wystram spends most of the first round doing not much, using his illusory double from his spell earlier to mislead the Fiends and temporarily distract them from hurting his friends. 

Elisa deals the final blow to two of the Fiends, consuming their souls as she finishes them off, reducing them to ichor. The party readies themselves for the second round, as a Purple Worm bursts from the ground, attempting but failing to swallow the party. Wystram and Elisa are mostly safe from the danger whilst Tiny and Nyktos decide on a more hands on approach, each climbing onto the Purple Worm. Tiny soon finds themself swallowed by the Purple Worm, safe from its gaping maw but in more danger due to the digestive acids of the Worm. As the worm resurfaces, Nyktos unleashes a spell gem of Mental Prison on the poor Worm, creating an illusion of a blood wave that keeps the Worm trapped whilst the rest of her party deals with it, killing it rather quickly. 

For the third round, they are up against two constructs of Basatur. The party isn’t in a good shape, with most of their resources drained. Still they continue on, not willing to pay the fee to back out and also the need to impress the Pit Master should they wish to enter the private rooms. The third round begins and Nyktos unleashes a magic the Hells have never seen before. 

She hallows the ground, transforming the grounds into a flesh temple for Tenebrous. His avatar manifests over the colosseum as he overlooks the blood-soaked grounds of the arena. Her spell has the land changed, fey are no longer allowed to enter and constructs, fiend, and undead find themselves weakened upon entering the hallowed grounds. This provides the party with just enough to beat the two Hellfire spewing constructs of Basatur. Wystram soon finds himself surrounded by the constructs as his two allies push the enemies his way. Though before Wystram falls unconscious, he hears a bark from the audience as Crimson uses his amulet to prevent his friend from dying. 

During combat, Wystram uses the powers granted by his goddess to heal Tiny, sending a visage of a raven over as it envelopes Tiny in healing light. With the heal, Tiny is able to stand up against the enemy, cutting into their bodies as the five heads of Tiamat appear behind her son, her power within Tiny manifested as he brutally destroys his enemies. The party quickly turned the tides, ending the two constructs and winning their third battle. 

As the party turns to where they had left their allies, they see Myrantar wielding the dagger, holding onto the suspicious lady they saved from River Styx. In the woman’s hand is a ring, one that she has stolen from ZipZap who is shouting for the lady’s hands to be cut. Crimson jumps in to stop the matter, grabbing the knife from Myrantar and keeping it away from her grasp. As the party wraps up their second battle, they attend to the situation, stopping it before it gets out of hand. 

Elisa speaks to ZipZap, sharing that she would only share her winnings if ZipZap listens. That immediately has him quiet down, as he puts aside his anger for gold. Wystram attempts to convince Myrantar to not cause a scene, though Myrantar is not convinced. What does work however, is Nyktos calling out to Myrantar, reminding the drow that the woman is her charge to handle, thus any punishment would be Nyktos Eto oversee, not others. Myrantar relents but holds onto the thief, assuring that she does not escape before the party can deal with her. 

Before the party could return to their allies, Khai Kiroth lands before the party, admiring their combat as he expresses his interest in battle. He offers the party 1000 talons if they beat him. But Elisa demands for twice the amount and her persuasive nature convinces the Pit Master to fork out more money. 

Tiny cleverly suggests for a fair fight, seeing as the party have spent quite a bit of their resources whilst Kai’Karoth has not even a scratch on him. Khai Kiroth agrees, gesturing over to an imp who brings the party health potions and any other drinks they require. Wystram breaks his defiant streak, taking a health potion to heal his wounds. With the party healed, their final battle begins as Khai Kiroth summons two Pit Fiends to his side, evening out the battle ahead. With the hallow spell still in place, Khai Kiroth and his group suffer sufficient damages as he admits defeat and the party wins both their talons and his good graces. With Khai Kiroth promising to put in a good word, the party heads to the stands to deal with the thief. 

The woman pleads, begging for help as she did before. This time, Nyktos sees the woman for who she truly is. Greed fills the woman’s eyes, no remorse for her actions. Myrantar suggests cutting off the woman’s hands, quoting that it would be kinder than the death she’d face if they were in Menzoberranzan. 

But Wystram and Nyktos are against it, both agreeing that the woman should be punished, but that no blood needs to be shed over this. They manage to convince Myrantar, who agrees that they should not dirty their blade with the blood of a dog. Instead, Nyktos walks up to one of the Guards, informing them to throw the woman out. And with that, the unknown woman leaves the party, and the premises of the Red Belvedere. Nyktos walks up to Thalor, knowing that he’s no longer under Elisa’s spell and that he had just witnessed another way in which the party deals with people. She speaks to him, expressing her regret in her inability to protect him. But wishes to assure him that the outside world isn’t what he’s seen in Hell, that there are places much better and safer. 

That they only wish to help and protect people. Though Thalor’s eyes hold nothing but anger. He believes every word they say to be false, a trick, a lie to manipulate him. He believes the party are evil people. And all he says is that he will do what it takes to protect his people. The hate and anger in his voice has Nyktos speechless, concerned that they might have ruined him. With a heavy heart, Nyktos returns to the party to see that they are having a discussion and knows that they are occupied with other matters. She instead walks over to Crimson who sensing his friend’s unhappiness offers nudges and hugs, trying to comfort his friend in any way he can. 

Myrantar walks over, and in her own ways tries to comfort Nyktos. She comments that Nyktos should not let the opinions of unimportant male affect her. That a leader does what they have to do. She shows an almost unrelenting trust in what Nyktos does, and assures her that children should listen to their mothers as Myrantar glances at Wystram finishing her point. But Myrantar’s continuous reference to the Forest Elves as savages, Nyktos stops her, not wanting anyone else to insult her people. Myrantar concedes, finding nicer yet truthful names for the Elves. Still Nyktos tries to see Myrantar’s points, stating that she wasn’t letting Thalor’s words affect her but rather disappointed in herself and her inability to protect her people. A lesson to be learnt, a mistake to never repeat.

Meanwhile, Wystram rests on the benches of the Colosseum as Elisa calls him out for being tired. To which he argues that they did march through half of Avernus just to be here. Wystram comments on Elisa’s energetic disposition as she agrees that money making has been a hobby of hers after she realised that she could start charging her friends for services when she ran errands for them. Wystram points out that with the lack of economy, money doesn’t do quite much, though the others are just happy to hoard and Wystram is alright as long as they are happy. 

Tiny then takes the opportunity to bring up something close to their heart. They mention how the place is weird, and the others agree, pointing out the uncharacteristic behaviour Tiny exhibited earlier. Wystram makes a note to recall Tiny’s more heroic moments, mentioning how he was impressed with how Tiny had handled the situation with Guh. That moment spoke to Wystram’s poetic soul, watching a hero give his enemies some grace. Elisa brings up the topic of Reggie and his words that they heard from Blade, the joy that they seemed to give Tiny, not unfortunately lost as he no longer has the magic mouth stones. 

As the trio wrap up their conversation, Nyktos seeks out Elisa to speak in private. Through Elisa’s telepathy, Nyktos shares her concern for Thalor. She fears that Thalor might become vengeful, that he might view the party as an enemy, a danger to his family. And that one day, he may or may not come find them for his revenge. Though she understands that there is nothing they can do about it, she merely wishes for Elisa to be equally aware of a potential threat. Elisa understands, and remarks that maybe they’re both too soft. Maybe next time, they shouldn’t allow eager eyed people to follow them. The two agree and just before Nyktos is able to call for drinks to fix her solemn mood. 

Myrantar shows up with two glasses of Joker’s Sky, the drink she has continuously been ordering, much to Wystram’s displeasure. Intrigued by the blue drink, Nyktos tries it, finding it to be a nice sweet drink as Myrantar shares that it reminds her of the wine they have in Menzoberranzan. Myrantar also remarks that Wystram should know better when to bring women drinks. As they make their way to the next game, Wystram takes a moment to speak with Nyktos, asking if she’s alright. Nyktos doesn’t share the details of Thalor’s behaviour, not wanting to bring the mood down. Instead she changes the topic, mentioning Wystram’s sad Shadar Kai like behaviour as of late. Wystram is honest about his cluelessness. The Raven Queen has given him a second chance, when so many that he knew didn’t get them. He expresses his helplessness, unsure of how to even carry out his Goddess Ewill. 

Cautiously, Nyktos admits to not knowing much either, but offers to share what little she does, suggesting that it might give Wystram a direction. He accepts the offer, grateful to have some help. Nyktos takes the chance to thank Wystram, for speaking up when he did and preventing her from straying. She expresses that she was so caught up in not letting her fear stop her, that she had instead let her fear control her. Wystram, ever so humble, denies. Instead saying that he was only able to see that Nyktos had strayed because of her continuous efforts to be good. 

That he had only pointed out the facts, and the one to bring Nyktos back to the light was none other than herself. Nyktos scoffs at the humility shown, but still offers her sincere gratitude. She does also take the moment to point out Myrantar’s care for Wystram, though he might not quite see it yet. As the topic of the Forest gets brought up, Nyktos also offers that whilst she doesn’t know which one of them will speak to the Shadow Elves first, she promises that Wystram would also have a chance to speak with them, that they should know of their options before making a decision. 

With that, they head to the final game available. They are met by a Blue Abishai named Nyssa Otellion. The party is introduced to her game and invited to play against her. As they do, Elisa notices something odd. Every attempt that Elisa has made to beat Nyssa, is somehow foiled. Elisa tries to make sense of what she’s seeing, she excuses herself and comes back disguised as an old man as she tries to make sense of it all. With her having snuck around and examining how the Casino even accepts betting the clothes on your back to stay in the game. Nyktos notices that it’s odd, Nyssa seems to be calling her numbers before she’s even rolled. 

Using Crimson as a distraction, Nyktos casts Identify and is able to see that the necklace Nyssa has on casts a minor illusion without any components. Figuring out how Nyssa is cheating, Nyktos calls her out for it. Questioning if it’s even winning if Nyssa has to use cheats. Impressed that someone has figured out her trick, Nyssa agrees to play for real. Without the cheats, the party begins to win and soon earns a decent sum from Nyssa, enough for her to also put in a good word to Windfall. 

With that, the party is soon invited to the private gaming rooms. Windfall leads them to where Elisa had noticed earlier and she gives them each a mark, which she is quick to assure Nyktos and Tiny that it isn’t permanent. She also shares that without the mark, one would likely be reduced to ashes. As she leads the party in, they are offered one last drink at the bar. Though they all decline and continue on to where Windfall leads them. Before they begin discussing matters, Windfall brings the party their bill for every drink and food they have had within the Red Belvedere. The total is over 75 000 gold, much more than the party has to spare. 

And after dropping that bomb, she turns to Nyktos, asking her about the Demons attack of what they said. Not quite happy nor forthcoming with Windfall at the moment, Nyktos asks for payment, after all nothing is for free. Amused, Windfall offers to strike 10 000 gold off the bill, which Nyktos accepts. Nyktos shares that as their job being adventurers they met many sorts of people, one of which happened to be Demogorgon’s heir. And the party had convinced the heir to refuse their mother’s call, turning them to the side of good and reducing Demogorgon’s power. 

As such the Demons might have misplaced their anger and attributed their losses to the party. Windfall nods, adding a 50 000 gold charge to bill the party for damages to their property. Windfall is convinced and accepts, removing the charge and reducing the bill as promised. Meanwhile, the others are struggling with the bill. Wystram is still shaking in shock as he can’t help but make comments at Myrantar for her drinking habits. 

Next Windfall addresses Tiny and Nyktos whispers to Tiny that Windfall’s game might be how well one could bargain. That mayhaps if he could make a good enough point, she would be impressed enough. Tiny makes their attempt to leverage his invitation by his Mother and his royal blood, only to be swiftly ignored. 

Windfall does suggest that they instead play a game, offering the party the chance to earn their debt’s worth and maybe more. Wystram approaches Nyktos, knowing how much she has and asks for her help to pay off his and Myrantar’s portion. At this point Myrantar steps in as well, sheepishly apologising as she once more asks for their help. Nyktos agrees to help and asks only for one thing in exchange, that Myrantar doesn’t discredit people based on their gender alone. With the Drow finally dropping her mask in front of the party, Nyktos also quietly alludes that she is aware of the kindness Myrantar holds, and assures her that the party is a safe space.
