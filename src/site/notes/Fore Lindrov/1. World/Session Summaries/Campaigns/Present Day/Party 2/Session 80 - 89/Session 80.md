# Veszika & Vizeran

The party inquires more about Veszika’s son, trying to understand the relationship between the two. Veszika explains that Vizeran was raised as a mage but was found consorting with another god known as Tharizdun. Vizeran was thus banished from his home with Veszika’s last words to his son that he was an embarrassment and a disgrace. # Xunbelaern 
The party arrives at Xunbelaern where they manage to enter with Jorlan’s skillful manipulation of drow politics. Then, a female drow with a broken arm rushes out and as she does, the more insightful party members are able to tell that the guards and other lower-ranked drow greatly respect her. She immediately hugs Jorlan who is upset at her presence and chastises her for being at Xunbelaern, revealing that he had told her to contact House Baenre and not the High Priestess, Shriala Mizzrym. The female Drow, who the party come to learn is named Vicintra, reveals to Jorlan that she was worried that he'd be dead, that she couldn't just stay in Menzoberranzan while he was potentially either captured or dead in Velkynvelve. Jorlan then angrily storms off, leaving the party with Vicintra. 

Vicintra then speaks with the party, seemingly genuinely interested in who they are as people. The party panics at her question, each coming up with a false drow name, exposing most of their inability to speak Elvish. Despite that, Vicintra doesn’t treat them differently, if anything she extends a gentle hand to the party, speaking to them in common. She also asks the party to not judge Jorlan based on his prior actions, sharing that he used to be a captain and in doing so reveals her feelings towards him. She also reveals that both her and Jorlan have been best friends since they were kids, however some of the more insightful party members are able to identify that this isn't simple friendship, but rather love between the lower-born Vicintra Miliskeera and high-born Jorlan Duskryn.

Vicintra also asks the party of the house they hailed from and what their hobbies are. Half of the party is hesitant to share, whilst the other half is much more giving with information. Lu starts, sharing his love for tea and even asking the captain to pose so that he may draw a painting of her. She allows the painting to happen, accepting the piece created by Lu. Grimrid goes next, sharing his love for training. Vicintra then asks for a demonstration. Grimrid tries but his skills with weapons have been more focused in strength rather than dexterity and elegance, and it shows. 

Next is Elisa who vaguely alludes to her past by sprinkling lies in with truth and speaks of her hobbies in potions and poisons. Lastly is Nyktos who speaks of her seamstresing which Vicintra asks to touch the clothing she currently wore, commenting that it was of a good quality, urging Nyktos to be more proud of her work. Vicintra gives the party permission to rest for the day, allowing them to roam around freely. 

# Shriala Mizzrym’s Cruelty

As the PCs leave Vicintra's office, they are met with Shriala Mizzrym, the High Priestess and Overseer of Xunbalaern and Velkynvelve, her entourage dragging along a slave who pleads for her life. The party intervenes, with Lu speaking very much out of turn as he blatantly insults Shriala and her authority, triggering anger in one of Shriala’s handmaidens, demanding the prostration of Lu and the rest of the party. However Veszika steps in, prostrating himself for the party as he speaks of their efforts in saving the High Priestess' daughter, Xullana, something that Shriala notably pays no heed to, specifically staying on the party’s defiance. The party does try to convince Shriala to take on the slave as another favourite, a pet. But their words mean nothing to the High Priestess and the situation worsens, with a potential fight looming on the horizon.

Before the party can try anymore to save both themselves and the slave, a shot goes off. An arrow from Vicintra goes through the slave. Shriala takes this as an opportunity to make a threat towards Vicintra, citing that this act of killing a sacrifice coveted by a High Priestess has stripped Vicintra of whatever political protections she had from informing House Baenre of the loss of Velkynvelve, however through Vicintra’s sacrifice, the Priestess lets the party go, and as mercy for their deeds, the party is commanded to hang the slave’s corpse above the sacrificial circle and cut out her heart. Laura holds her tongue, watching in silent indignation at the torment that Shriala inflicts on everyone around her.

Facing the consequences of their actions, Nyktos and Grimrid head to the body, hoisting her up as Nyktos expertly cuts out her heart. The skill she had been using against her enemies now used to harm an innocent. She pockets the heart, wanting to provide the woman with a proper burial. Vicintra speaks with the party once more, urging them to not repeat their mistakes and tells them to rest and to ignore all other instructions unless given by the high Priestess or her entourage. Shriala and her entourage then lock down the remainder of the mines, heading down themselves.

# Battle With Nomi

Gathering all of that, the party enters the mines, seeing many, many hordes of undead. They pick a direction, heading right to the source where they meet Nomi right as she kills another innocent. It’s here they realise what horrors Nomi has been inflicting on the populace, dozens of slaves kept in cages, starved nearly to death before ritually being sacrificed, the horrid hunger that they felt raising their corpses of Ghouls. 

Elisa speaks with Nomi, apologising for the events at Blingdenstone, asking for how she could help. But Nomi is too far gone, she believes that there's nothing Elisa can do. But she tells the party about the agreement she’s made with Ardor Mor to at least alleviate the suffering of her people, that she could not bear what Ardor Mor has been doing to them, working them to death and raising their corpses to mine more gems for him, she says that she could not stand it after what he did to Tappy, and catches herself after saying that something was done to Glyphic. 

Nomi tells Elisa that she’s made her bed, and absolves Elisa of her guilt, saying that it wasn’t Elisa’s fault that Ardor Mor took them, and that she knows they lied to her about her friend Gallus because they wanted to protect her. Despite this, Elisa still struggles, saying that it was because she and her party weren’t strong enough, and that it was because of them that Ardor Mor even found Blingdenstone in the first place.

Nomi begs the party to leave, that she doesn’t want to kill them too, but the party cannot back down, and they poignantly engage in combat. 
The party realise that the crystal that keeps the Undead summoned by Nomi unkillable is warded with additional protections during their attempts to break it.

# Ardor Mor Comes

Guilt leads Nomi to a difficult decision, one where she gives up, she laments that doesn’t want to do this anymore, that she can’t do this anymore. This mere proclamation of disobedience causes the air itself to shift, rot begins to seep into the mines as a dreadful creature that form, a great, rotting dragon. Ardor Mor arrives, asking the name of the insects who would dare turn a servant of his against him, exercising his control over Nomi, reasserting her slavery and his absolute authority. 

Grimrid boldly introduces himself, insulting the Dracolich, saying that since time immemorial, the Dwarves have forged Wyrmslayers, promising to take on this tradition with Ardor Mor. Roast introduces herself with proper grammar, saying that the Dragon is not a friend. Lastly, Lu goes out of his way to insult Ardor Mor, vowing to destroy him, a provocation that Ardor Mor calls out, telling Lu to strike him down now, with which Lu attacks Ardor Mor immediately, with Grimrid assisting him. As the two uselessly land no significant blows on Ardor Mor, Grimrid manages to leave a small mark on his bones, in response, Ardor Mor kills Grimrid with but a Word as the Dwarf falls dead on the ground. Lu resolves himself, simply continuing to strike at Ardor Mor while Roast and Elisa quietly whisper to one another, trying to find out how they can best handle the situation while protecting Nomi and Laura.

They suggest that Laura move away from the area, but upon doing so Ardor Mor kills her, disintegrating her into dust, only emboldening Lu to attack him, Ardor Mor then does one final thing, he puppets Nyktos E taking full control of her body as a show of authority, and prepares to punish Nomi, forcing Elisa to deescalate the situation by offering Ardor Mor something he wants.. The Blue Dragon Mask, a touch of the Queen of Dragon’s divinity, divinity that Ardor Mor seeks to use. Elisa says that she’ll give Ardor Mor the mask if he frees Nomi. Something that Ardor Mor coyly agrees to, and when the Dracolich turns to leave, Nomi willingly follows, Ardor Mor knows that she has no choice, so long as he holds Blingdenstone, he holds Nomi. Before they leave, Nomi verbalises to the party that she wants them to save Blingdenstone, that she hopes one day they can defeat Ardor Mor, a notion that the Dragon very clearly finds amusing, and that this is a game he is more than willing to play.

# Cousin Bolin!
While this is happening, Grimrid finds himself in a place he’s never been before, a great Dwarven Feasting Hall of Stone and Wood. There he is greeted by his cousin, the cousin he consistently tells outlandish stories about, Bolin the Bold. The two Dwarves are elated to meet once more, with Bolin bringing Grimrid aside for drinks, an offer that Grimrid heartily accepts, the younger Dwarf eager to tell Bolin about the stories he’s told of his cousin’s great legacy. 

Bolin praises Grimrid’s bravery, as they rejoice over the scuff mark left on Ardor Mor’s scale. Bolin shares that Grimrid is destined for much more, that he holds a greater purpose and is the last remaining true Dwarf. Bolin tells Grimrid that using his excellent bartering skills, he has negotiated with the Allfather, Moradin himself to give Grimrid one last chance on the material plane, having traded Grimrid’s life for an exquisitely sharp rock collected from the great Mountains of Mount Celestia, a stone sharp enough it could pierce the heart of any problem, even those that are divine. 

Grimrid mistakenly misinterprets this statement by Cousin Bolin, believing that he needs to find a particular rock in the Material Plane to fulfill his purpose. Before he can even clarify or speak to his cousin, Bolin tosses Grimrid off the balcony of the Great Hall, sending him back down to his party. He awakens, gasping for air and muttering about a rock as the others are surprised to see him alive. Grimrid shares his experience though not all believe his tale. With Grimrid back, that leaves their other fallen friend.
