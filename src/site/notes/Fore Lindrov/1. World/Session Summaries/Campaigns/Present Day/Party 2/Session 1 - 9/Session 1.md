Elisa and Hakon find themselves wandering aimlessly through Fore Lindrov. Finally arriving at a settlement, they see a small crowd has gathered. Where a young woman, Ilythyrra is apparently unconscious in the middle of the city. She wears an eerie mask, she wakes up and awkwardly walk away from the middle of the city when an attack strikes the city. Umber Hulks and Ankhegs invade, infected by worm-like parasites. The party swiftly dispatches them, albeit with some difficulty. 

The town mayor Garfield, rewards the party and enlists them to investigate the source of these monsters. During the conversation Garfield seems to be having PTSD as he stares aimlessly at Ilythyrra and the large Goliath, Hakon. Elisa questions him regarding this and he admits that the people of Zaratan used to live inside the massive dead tortoise that sat to the North. Only for it to one day die and for tens of thousands of people to die. He reveals that there was a Half-Orc, Elf and Tiefling involved. Which immediately sparks Elisa's attention. Who demands to know their involvement. Garfield says it is too hard to talk about and requests that they speak to Pulliver in Zaratan-C. Garfield also reveals the layout of the Zaratan, Zaratan-A in charge of farming, B in charge of mining and C in charge of Hunting. Each governed by their own Mayors.

Garfield also mentions that the monster attacks are unusual as they had a previous party putrefy the intestines. The party agree to investigate and set off, having been gifted a cart by Garfield. Ilythyrra summons a phantom steed to pull the cart. The party arrive at the decomposing corpse of the Zaratan, Uuvash, where they investigate the intestines. Having discovered that the intestines were never putrified, and the remains of the scroll, a human arm and a Troll was found. The party move onward to the main mass of the intestines.

There, the party investigates the area. Ilythyrra sees that the intestines have been consumed by yellow mould and that they were never petrified. The party continues to investigate. Finally finding signs of what appears to be a large hole in the intestines, mouldy footprints trail off to the tail-end of the tortoise. Finally the party makes their way there, where they see a wall of mould. Ilythyrra sticks her hands into the mould and tries to find something. She brushes up against a humanoid shape but accidentally pops a pustule that releases noxious gas. Injuring both herself and Hakon, Elisa begins using mage hand to unearth this humanoid shape from the wall. With Hakon thinking it best to stick his hands inside and pull it out. Elisa investigates the shape, touching its flowery head. She is sent into a vision, seeing a creature clawing its way out of the intestines and running off, before taking over a cave. The party is then attacked by infected spiders, with a spider riding Deep Gnome appearing. They manage to very quickly dispatch these enemies, and continue onwards. They find themselves at a mountain with a natural ramp, with the cave in the vision on the right. 

The entire party attempts to sneak inside, sneaking in, Hakon and Elisa are immediately spotted by the beast that resides within. A froghemoth and another rider, Elisa immediately casts a spell. Obscuring the creatures as the party takes potshots at them. The Froghemoth bursts out from the fog while Ilythyrra dispatches the Steeder Rider. It grapples Hakon and swallows him. Then sets its sights on Elisa, who it downs with a lash of its tentacles, preparing to eat the her. Luckily Ilythyrra proves a good distraction, and as she fires arrows at it. Hakon bursts out from the Froghemoth's body, killing it. As the party prepare to rest, Ilythyrra sneaks over to a large hole in the cave, discovering more than 10 more of the infected beasts. The party quickly travel back to Zaratan-A by cart.

They inform Garfield about the source of the monsters, he asks if they would be commissioned as guards of the towns. They agree, he asks that the party make their way to Zaratan-B to begin commissioning stone walls so that the 3 Towns have a defensible position. The party then takes a Long Rest. Ilythyrra's list has a name, but she has not decided to move forward with whatever it is she needs to do. The next morning comes and the party goes by cart to Zaratan-B, where they meet Marr. The Elvish mayor of Zaratan-B. She is shocked to hear about the monster attacks, asking that the party help their mining operation to get stone. Seeing as how they need to get started on the stone walls ASAP. The party agrees but tragedy strikes, a Roc dives down from the sky. Attacking the Town, Elisa springs into action by teleporting civilians into buildings. While Ilythyrra retreats on her phantom steed while firing arrows at the Roc. Who promptly picks her up and begins clawing away at her as Hakon throws Javelins at the Roc. Eventually the party is able to drive the creature away. With Marr changing her commission to tracking the Roc.

The party gives chase, realising that they are headed to the same mountain as before. They scale the natural ramps, beat back a Deep Gnome ambush and finally arrive at a Flatbed. The home of the Deep Gnomes, the Roc Aguya and the Fire Elemental, Aithne. Their names burn into Ilythyrra’s list. 

The party stay behind as Ilythyrra makes her up, knocking out the Deep Gnomes who patrol the area before springing on Aithne. Elisa’s attempts to persuade her after hearing Aithne say that all in Fore Lindrov must die falls on deaf ears. Aithne and the Aguya do battle with the party as Elisa casts Spike Growth to hold Aithne down and Hakon duels her. Ilythyrra pounces on Aguya again, who subsequently grabs her and brings her down. With Elisa quickly healing her Ilythyrra takes aim at Aguya as Hakon and Elisa strike Aithne down. As she fades away she believes

And says that Goetia, Fool and Sylvaerys will do what needs to be done. Elisa uses her second blast to disintegrate Aithne as Aguya becomes enraged at the death of her caretaker. But Ilythyrra, swiftly brings the great Roc down and kills it before it even has a chance. 

The party discover that there are 7 Deep Gnomes living there, terrified. While Elisa offers them good berries the party is unable to communicate with them due to them only speaking Gnomish. They take Aguya’s head as proof of their kill and head back to Zaratan-B. Where Marr pays the party and tells them to head to Zaratan-C, where Pulliver says they have discovered something strange. The party head off, arriving there, Elisa marches off. The young girl demanding that people tell her where Pulliver is, she storms into his office. Fining a skinny man staring at a heart shaped locket. He turns and Elisa immediately mentions the Moribund. To which Pulliver goes into a long rant. While Ilythyrra and Hakon stand outside. Waiting for Elisa and Pulliver to calm down. Pulliver shares that the city housed the Moribund, providing them food and shelter and in return demons were st on them, monsters were sent to destroy them and according to Pulliver, that they killed the Zaratan. Elisa also mirrors the same sentiment. As they calm down, the rest of the party come in. Asking for information about the thing Zaratan-C found, Pulliver tells them to find Goliath, Henry and Jared. The party meet 3 young men, the towns' best trackers. Who introduce themselves and guide the party a few minutes away from the town. Where they see similarly mouldy, yellow footprints that they saw at the intestines. The party bids the trio farewell and the trio gift them with a Potion of Speed and Potion of Healing. As the party follows the tracks they come across an adventurer, clad in plate armour being attacked by Cultists that bear a tattoo on their backs. The party beat back the cultists and save the adventurer, named Hastings.

He suggests that they form an adventuring party, and everyone agrees. Hastings accompany the party to their destination where they find that the moldy footprints lead to a cave. Elisa casts Pass Without Trace and Ilythyrra passes through the first chamber, guarded by Umber Hulks into the trapped Hallway where she triggers a spear trap, gas trap and spots a hidden pit trap. The party follow suit after she scouts and Hakon accidentally trigger a spear trap. The party teleport over to the final chamber where they are met with a fleshy sack. A proto-gravemind, Hakon disturbs it and what falls out is a disgusting creature able to transform. As the Umber Hulks rush to the final chamber to help this beast, Elisa casts Spike Growth and Plantgrowth to hold them down. 

While the party deals with the massive creature, who transforms into a Hulking Tank, and smashes Hastings. Dealing massive amounts of damage, finally the party is able to bring them down and they are given a vision. They see the life of a citizen of Zaratan, walking through the courtyard a sudden explosion happens. The vision cuts and it shows Garfield announcing to the citizens that Sylvaerys, Fool and Arth were responsible. Then moving on to the execution of the monsters that destroyed the city, but the courtyard is attacked by the Moribund. The horrifying visage of Sylvaerys firing a shot, Arth taking Garfield hostage and Fool setting a terrorist free.

The vision then cuts to the moment of Uuvash's death, then to the collapse. Where thousands of people fell and died, swallowed by the endless abyss of Uuvash's body. With the person they are piggybacking on being infected by a worm of Godfrey. The vision changes to the person, transformed, gathering Biomass. Hearing Godfrey's commands in its head. The sentient consciousness of the citizen still somewhere in the creature's mind. They then cut to the moment Godfrey was awakened, when the Moribund returned to Uuvash, escaped the horde and left.

It then finally cuts to the current moment in time, showing that the person within the infected has resurfaced. Saying that it must stop Godfrey, it claws out of Uuvash. Brings the Fire Elemental to its side, and fuses into a sack of biomass to evolve. But then another voice, the voice of Godfrey. Who announces that the hive will go west to be undisturbed. But they are to gather while they move and the party come to horrible realisation that Godfrey is unleashing the horde on Zaratan. The party swiftly dispatch the Umber HUlks and rush back. Barely making it in time before the attack, seeing the hundreds and thousands of bodies the horde is throwing at the towns. Ilythyrra summons multiple Phantom Steeds. When they arrive at Zaratan-C the townsfolk have take up a defensive position in the middle of the courtyard. The party annoujnces that they can only take a few people to safety. Grabbing 11 Children and Pulliver. With Elisa having been able to convince him that they need a leader to rebuild before turning into a Horse. The party then mvoe to Zaratan-B where they take another, 3 Women and 3 Old People. The party then make a run for it as the horde descends on the towns. 

They notice one of the horde simply staring at them as Ilythyrra shoots it down. The party then find themselves being chased by the Horde. Elisa, realising that she has to turn human. Tosses Pulliver and the child on her back, and they just barely manage to grab on to the cart as Elisa transforms and casts Plant Growth to slow the Horde down as Hakon tries to cut down as many as he can with Ilythyrra only managing to take down a few. The party are then eventually met with a procession of Stalkers. Then make the decision to turn Southwest from Southeast as Elisa casts Plant Growth and Ilythyrra sets it all on fire to stave off the Horde. However a few of the Steeds were about to disappear, and thus. Hakon had to convince them all to jump onto the cart. With 5 of them being crushed beneath the cart

in the attempt to jump over. Hastings does not manage to keep the cart steady as it flips upward, Hakon makes a crazy leap of faith, going behind the cart and stopping it from flipping over by pulling it back down. Barely making it with his strength and jumping into the cart. But unfortunately, Pulliver manages to hold on when the cart flips but no the child. And as the child falls over, Hastings stops the cart in an attempt to not run over the kid. Not heeding the rest of the party. The remaining Stalkers, 3 of them rush the party and begin their attack. 2 Of them turn into Tanks while 1 of them remains in Stalker Form. Hastings gets down from the cart and begins combat, Hakon swiftly frees the children from the cart with Pulliver and tells them to run as soon as the monsters are distracted.

Elisa is drained of all her spell slots, Hakon is almost dead and Ilythyrra is worse for wear. The party prepare themselves for death, but just as the kids sneak away and death descends upon them. Hastings makes his sacrifice. He Second Winds himself, drops his weapon and barely holding on, grapples the 2 Pure Forms. Telling the party to run. The last remainaing Pure Form attempts to attack as Elisa, hearing Grace's voice, bursts out vines from her body and holds it down. Hakon hears his brother's voice for a moment and Ilythyrra sees a shorthaired man with glossy black eyes. All of them saying the same thing. "Run" 

Ilythyrra then realises her mark is glowing, and realises she has casted timestop. Hakon jumps on Elisa as she transforms into a horse and Ilythyrra runs away on a phantom steed. But not before, after being aided by Hakon. To send an arrow at Hastings to end his life painlessly when timestop ends. Elisa manages to track the kids and finds them. However they find themselves wandering the coldness of Fore Lindrov. Alone and beaten. The party encounter a Xorn, who demands the party's gold. The party unable to defend both themselves and the children

relent and give it all 520gp. They then meet a group of basilisks, who are swiftly led away by Ilythyrra. Who uses her steed to continue running, and making it back to the group of foot. Then finally they encounter teleporting a Draegloth and Meenlocks. Who Elisa uses her wildfire spirit to lead them away. Eventually the party see a Nightmare sitting in a cave that beckons them to come into the cave to seek shelter. Hakon is distrustful and wary, while Ilythyrra hangs back. Elisa casts speak with animals and speaks to the Nightmare who reveals that a barrier will protect them should they come inside. It says its master is inside. Hakon begrudgingly agrees and the entire group goes inside the cave where they are met with a man, horribly burned and bandages. Having nightmares abnout the Moribund, spoutting the name of Sylvaerys. The party then go to sleep, where Elisa casts Goodberry to feed everyone while Ilythyrra feeds them. Hakon and Elisa speak to the man, who reveals himself to be named Dave. Saying that he was burnt by the Moribund. Who not only have a Lich under their command, the ability to make deals with Mindflayers, but also an Ancient Red Dragon who is also a Druid. 

Hakon says vengeance is folly as he says Elisa will get herself killed, the both of them argue before Dave chimes in. Saying perhaps it would be best to play things smart. To create something rather than seek the Moribund out. Dave suggests they find a Beholder, as they craft intricate caves, he says he saw signs of one during his travels and perhaps, the party could clear its lair out and they could move in to begin building a life. Elisa does try to heal Dave but to no avail, asking him how do they help him. He says that the task is not something they are ready for, for now they must find a stronghold.

The party then receive a long downtime.
