Waking up from the dream, Tiny turns to their other self. For once they both agree, revering in the woman’s beauty. She who emits a warmth that feels like home. And before them, the axe is no longer the Fist of Bane, but rather a weapon made of Dragon Bone. The weapon flashes red, burning with the heat of a dragon. 

  

The next day, Elisa heads to where the dwarvish prisoners are held. Through reading their thoughts, she learns that they have all accepted their deaths. Elisa clarifies that she isn’t there to kill them, that no one would be. She offers them empathy, that their actions were inevitable. She learns that King Bruenor still resides in Gauntlgrym and senses their great loyalty for their King. Elisa tries to gain information about the deal the King had with Goetia, though the dwarves are unaware of the details of the deal. She gives them one final message, to tell the King that should there be a way to get them out of the deal, she will help. With that, the dwarves are released, free to return home. 

  

From Eigeron, the party learns of Giant history. There once was a Kingdom called “Ostoria E “Father’s Seat Ein Giant. That fell in the Thousand Year War against the ancient dragons. However, after centuries, the Giants reorganised into the late Kingodm of Siggheimr, where they were brought to heel by the God Emperor, Godwin, alongside the various huddles of Dragons.

  

The party is then dropped off an hour away from the city, returning at about eleven in the morning. They head in to meet with King Baldwin, informing him of the situations, returning the fallen knights. King Baldwin then speaks of their Knighting, sharing that the party would have a week to rest before the festivities, where his Leprae Corpus will be whole once more. 

  

Taking the week to rest, the party gets into different shenanigans. Elisa heads to the free market, setting up an attempt of a shop with Zipzap playing his flute to welcome in customers. Though the sight looks more like a poor blinded girl and her pet begging for money. Hearing the situation, Elisa leans into it, extending her hands out, pleading the civilians for coin.  
  
The general public takes pity on Elisa, handing her some of their hard earned coins. Tiny, who happens to chance upon this sight is genuinely puzzled. They express that Elisa is not at all blind, claiming that she has sight better than theirs. The people do not believe Tiny, all gesturing to Elisa’s sealed shut eyes. To add fuel to the fire, Nyktos transforms into a woman who bears similar features to Elisa.  
  
She rushes in, crying about how her younger sister Elisa has resorted to begging now that they have lost their parents. Elisa plays along, commenting on how they don’t have to starve for the upcoming days. A wealthy patron witnesses this touching moment and hands Elisa a total of 100 gold pieces, hoping to tide them over this tough time. 

  

Meanwhile as this is happening, Lucatiel is off in the Tower of Knowledge. She’s happily indulging in books, though the information is more for the general public. She inquires if there are books about aberrations and learns that whilst these books do exist, they are on a need to know basis, that only knights are able to access the archives for them. In her desire to quench her thirst for knowledge, Lucatiel returns to the Tower the next day, disguised as a knight.  
  
Though in a city mostly populated by humans, a small knight would be hard to miss. Lucatiel enters the Tower and confidently heads towards the upper levels. They are immediately stopped by a custodian who requests that Lucatiel wait whilst they get someone else over. As they head over to speak with Lucatiel, the nervous Myconid makes a run for it. The knights give chase but Lucatiel teleports out of sight, losing the guards. The next day, Lucatiel returns back to the Tower. She will settle for the books not locked behind knighthood, at least that’s her intention.  
  
Unfortunately, the recent attempt to break into the Tower has raised some security concerns, resulting in the Tower being closed down until after the knighting ceremony. With nothing else to do, Lucatiel heads to the local mercenary guild in search of some coins. Her first mission is simple enough; deliver a parcel. The streets are busy, given the upcoming ceremony and festivities.  
  
A figure bumps into Lucatiel, causing her to fall. She gets back up to find the parcel stolen from and the thief already long gone. Lucatiel reports the matters to the guard, waiting hours for her turn. When she finally gets to describe the thief, their description is generic enough that it could easily be anyone.  
  
Her statement is collected and she finally sends what remains of the parcel to its destination. Lucatiel does a great job helping him out, earning an extra silver and a new friend.

The party heads to the festivities where stores and activities line the streets. Elisa and Tiny head for the food stalls, appalled to find that candied apples cost a large sum. They both eventually settle for cheaper items. Listening as they walk, the party hears whispers of strange people with fiery robes about.  
  
They speculate it to be Ken and his newly recruited fire cultists before following the crowds to the story tent. The cost to enter is two gold per person. Nyktos pays for two entrances, ushering Lucatiel in before their more miserly team members can start their spiel. Elisa attempts to garner pity, using her seared eyes to negotiate for a lower price, Tiny joins in as well, attempting to help the situation. Though it does not work on the man who’s in no power to offer any form of discounts. Eventually, Tiny pays for them both and they too are allowed in. 

  

The story begins with an aged man sitting on a high chair. He speaks of a mystical dream, of adventure, mysteries, and heroics. Far far away in the land of whimsy lived a golden goose who laid golden eggs that were said to contain many treasures. From one of those very eggs hatched a cold mist. The mist travelled far and wide, coming across a young child picking mushrooms and truffles.  
  
Unbeknownst to the child, the mushrooms they picked were magical and thus the mist granted the child with powers, transforming the mushrooms into a blade of fungus. The mist prophesied the child to be a great hero, teaching them the ways of blade and pyromancy. And so the youngling grows from a child to a great Mushroom Knight. 

  

Continuing their journey, the mist drifts to a woman soaked in blood, and before her; a demon. It is said that the woman had caught the demon’s eye, suffering from his many attempts to woo her. She had rejected the demon at every turn and after five arduous days, she thought she finally rid herself of him.  
  
She had been out farming when the demon snuck into her home, consuming the woman’s mother and assuming her form. The woman was blissfully unaware until the demon sprung his attack, bragging of how he had killed her mother just to get to her. In a panic, the woman picks up the nearest item driving it right into the demon’s heart. She pulled back, ripping the monster’s heart from its body, ending his life. From then on she was said to be the Demon Slayer, Blood Mover as the Iron of the demo’s blood formed around her, creating armour and gifting her with great magical power.  
  

The mist then travels next to a cave, where chants and prayers echo throughout. A nun takes a vow of blindness, shutting her eyes for three days as she believes that in darkness, she would find wisdom. The mist, impressed with the nun’s dedication, grants her that with which she seeks. They fuse her eyelids shut, eternalizing her vow. For taking her sight, the mist bestows a gift to the nun - the power to open her eyes to burn the unrighteous. 

  

Finally, the mist roams to the mountains where a Gnomish artist lives. He creates a sculpture of metal and iron. But as he finishes the sculpture, he feels as if he has built nothing. He had spent so much of his time building that he had no family nor children by his side. Faced with the emptiness that was his life, the Gnome ends it all, leaving the world for good. The mist takes pity upon the sculpture, what once was supposed to be a masterpiece now cruelly abandoned. They breathe life into the sculpture, awakening the soul from within. But sculpture, with no guidance lives like a mere child, roaming the far lands in search of a father that he does not know he has already lost.
