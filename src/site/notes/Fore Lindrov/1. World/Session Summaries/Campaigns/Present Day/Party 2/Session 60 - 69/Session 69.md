Wrapping up the conversation with Ythimir, she requests that the party share no details to any others and leads them back to the stairwell as Ythimir leaves them to their rooms. Sensing his concern for his niece, Nyktos reveals the party’s intention to find Serissa, believing that Uthor would have valuable information to aid in their search.  
  
Uthor is…sceptical, but with no other choice, he puts some faith in the party. He justifies his initial prejudice however, sharing how the smallfolk had betrayed someone he cared dearly for. His sister, the late Queen Neri had trusted smallfolk, but her kindness was returned with her split blood. 

  

The exact reasons for the beginning of the thousand year war have been lost to time. But the later generations continued, fighting the seemingly never ending battle their ancestors waged. Amidst the chaos and battle existed a time when Giants worked with Smallfolk against the Dragons. A hundred years before the war ended, Queen Neri married the recently appointed King of the Giants, King Hekaton.  
  
He was crowned for his prowess in battle and for a time the two led their people as best they could. Nearing the end of the war was when Princess Mirran and Nym were born. But too busy were their parents with wars and politics that the needs of their children fell to the wayside. Mirran and Nym knew not of a parent’s love but instead found themselves in a competition, desperate to gain the affections of their parents.  
  
Then came the Emperor who brought godlike powers and ceased the war, creating the Empire through force. When it came to the Giant’s choice of extinction or survival, King Hekaton chose survival. Though many Giants protested, none could go against the word of their appointed leader. The decision to have the Giants bow to the God Emperor suppressed the Giant Lords true might, and soon brought about trouble for the Giants. As humans who grew complacent decided to fight the Giants, resulting in more Giant lives Elost. 

  

Under the God Emperor’s rule, the Giant’s land was renamed to the Kingdom of Siggheimr. But King Hekaton was unable to move past the times of war, constantly paranoid of Dragons and his worries further exacerbated as the bodies of Giants slain by Smallfolk surfaced. Queen Neri, on the other hand, knew that hate was difficult to break down. And though the Storm Giants, initially known for their good nature and prowess at helping had mostly been changed by the war, their kindness took a back seat amongst the horrors they had witnessed.  
  
Not letting fear take over, Queen Neri spoke with Smallfolk, raising Princess Serissa to hold love and kindness within her heart. The Queen believed in peace, and asked the Smallfolk to join in her efforts. That is about when Ythimir showed up, informing them of the attack that would soon take place on the floating castle in the sky. Uthor was sent to help with the situation and when he returned, Queen Neri was nowhere to be found. Looking for his Queen Neri, Uthor found his sister, her corpse left on the shores of the island where meetings with the smallfolk were held.  
  
He believed that the smallfolk did it, assassins like shadows murdered his sister in cold blood. With news of the Queen’s death, no one was able to calm the King’s rage. Uthor turned to Serissa for help and she managed to calm her father. He named Serissa the heir to the throne despite being the youngest daughter. Uthor claims that the King had foreseen the doom, fall, and return of the royal family, furthering his belief that Serissa is the key to it all. 

  

Uthor shares of Serissa’s initial plans, how she intended to unite the people and in fact most of the Storm Giant Lords were in agreement before the shattering and the disappearance of the Princess. The party goes through each of the Giant Lords with Uthor, learning more about them. According to Uthor, the Stone King’s location is lost, though their ancient alcoves may hold knowledge on his whereabouts. In his opinion, the Stone King is a freedom obsessed fool, eager to cause rebellion but never present to help with the aftermath of his actions.  
  
The Hill Giants are simple, in need of direction to prevent their rampant hunger from controlling them. Tiny disagrees but leaves the matter be after stating their disapproval. Uthor views Duke Zalto as a warmonger, one who would refuse to ever bend the knee. When asked of the behaviours of Duke Zalto’s children, Uthor shares that the Duke’s son, Zaltember is broken, castrated by King Hekaton in a bid to suppress the Fire Giant Lord by destroying his legacy and threatened to do worse to his spoilt brat of a daughter, Cinderhild. With regards to the Frost Giants, Storvald is an exception.  
  
The party warns Uthor of Storvald’s insane tendencies of his current whereabouts, warning that the Frost Giant might attack the Queens when he next returns. Uthor mentions that during the reignited war between the giants and dragons during the fall of Fore Lindrov, he suspected the raiding parties that attacked Storm Giant camps were in fact sent by Storvald. And lastly, the Cloud Giants. Countess Sansuri is a reasonable person but the King had made her an enemy, one would have no idea how she might respond to Princess Serissa.

  

With not much information to work on, Uthor suggests that the party seek out the Oracle, revealing that The Oracle is said to reside in a place called the Eye of the Allfather. When asked of his thoughts of The Shattering, Uthor believes that it is a punishment for their complacency. But amongst his passionate words, Nyktos and Tiny find illogical errors. Nyktos questions that if Annam desires peace, then why shatter the Ordening when Princess Serissa was on the precipice of achieving that?  
  
This breaks Uthor slightly, causing the Storm Giant to conclude that it must mean The Shattering was not to punish the Giant’s inaction, but rather their lack of faith and their lack of praise and tribute towards their Gods.

  
He even goes so far as to accuse Ythimir of having something to do with Serissa’s disappearance, that her clear manipulation of Mirran and Nym is clearly lined with malicious intent, telling the party to follow him the next morning to arrest her, a course of action that is swiftly stopped by the party, as they inform him that Ythimir has done nothing wrong, and that hasty action may result in more harm than it does good, and that they will keep an eye on her, and will inform him of her movements to give him the needed evidence and leverage to move against her.  
  
Before leaving them, Uthor tells the party to keep Mirran and Nym safe as well, citing that they are but children still vying for their parents affections, and that they are products of the time they were born in, and not evil by nature.

Wystram goes second, begrudgingly sharing his flaw  Ethe weakness that resides within himself. His allies, his family, the only ones he ever cared for are now dead, and he wasn’t there to help, wasn’t there to stop it from happening.

He tells Nyktos that Fear is the mind-killer, that it is the little-death that brings total obliteration, that she must face her fears and permit it to pass over and through her, and that when it has gone past, she will turn the inner eye to see its path, and where the fear has gone, there will be nothing and that only she shall remain. He tells her that she can, as with all creatures, even Celestials, do their best, and that will be enough.  
  
Holding oneself back for that fear that one might fail, that would be the tragedy of it. And lastly Elisa, the youngest, and yet the one that has been through the most. Before Elisa is able to share her flaw, Yonel jokingly warns her to not mention her blindness for he knows the truth.

Her desire for revenge fueling most of her life. Yonel alludes to this but doesn’t say it, instead forcing the young girl to face her own flaws.

That whilst everyone might be aware of it, Elisa has to take the step forward, to embrace the truth, even if it’s just for this moment, just to pass this test. Elisa gathers courage and admits the truth. Yonel tells Elisa that vengeance is like a rope wound too tight, held tight by Elisa herself. That forgiveness may be a path towards a future where Elisa can live in peace and safety. Something that Elisa doesn’t take to entertaining, fully but Yonel is satisfied with just having her hear of a different path.

The guardian deflects, stating that his connection with humanity helps him protect them. But Nyktos sees through the lies he tells himself and gently suggests he ponder upon it before leaving him be. 

  

The party enters the palace and watches as a lit path appears before them. Though tempted to stray and explore the palace in its entirety, they instead choose to follow the path of the first world as Yonel stated. Following the pathway, they see the story of the birth of dragons. How two great cosmic dragons intertwined, creating two eggs, one scaly and the other with a platinum glow.  
  
Each dragon would breathe life upon their corresponding eggs and set it off into the world. The two would continue this, creating a series of gold, red, silver, blue, bronze, green, copper, black, brass, and white eggs. Until the final one, this time, instead of two eggs, there’s only one. This one has no scales, looking almost like a runt of the litter. But the cosmic dragons do not forsake it.  
  

 Instead, they both breathe upon this singular egg as it shines in all Chromatic colours and Metallic platinum glosses. The draconic eggs would then grow up, the Metallic dragons growing fond of the arts and cultures, whilst the chromatics hunger for power and destruction. The Chromatics began butting heads with the Giants. And it continued so until a mistake was made; a Chromatic Dragon was killed.  
  

In response to losing one of her kind, the Chromatic Cosmic dragon began to morph and mutate, turning into a giant 5-headed monstrosity and sending a fleet of Red Dragons to attack the Giant castle in the sky, kickstarting the beginnings of war. The Metallics didn’t join in but it was hard to watch the anger and destruction from both sides. They tried to speak to their chromatic brethren to find a middle-ground, but were quickly cast aside. Instead, they were forced to join on the side of the Giants, shapeshifting into Giants and humanoid heroes, althoug their focus was mainly to inspire the Smallfolk to fight for themselves.  
  
During this war, Tiamat herself was battling against a bearded Giant God that one could assume was Annam, but with each strike, the fabric of the Universe itself was coming undone, and Tiamat’s other half, the ruler of the Metallic Dragons joined on the side of Annam and they both cast Tiamat down into a land of stone and fire, imprisoning her.

That his words are Shield and Solace, to right the wrongs of dragons past.

That they have all been instructed by Tiny’s father to take a step back, that it is time for man to inherit the world, not dragons, not gods, not other beings. Although to Wystram, most it sounds more like neglect, like the dragons have shifted responsibility to Tiny, trusting the youngling to handle everything they couldn’t.  
  
Naturally, Tiny asks for their father’s whereabouts, but no one knows. Tiny also questions what would happen if they were to reject the Will they have been trusted with. To which Sonngrad states that their decisions are their own, that this is the entire point, for the creatures in the material plane, the Humanoids to make their own decisions, but in doing what they have done thus far, they’ve already begun taking up the mantle.  
  
Nyktos brings the topic back to Sonngrad, asking how they expect Tiny to deal with it all, and asking how they expect them to not end up just like the dragons past. Hearing this, Tiny speaks up, confident in their own ability to prevent similar mistakes, stating that they are not like the dragons of old. But as they do so, Vhagar speaks up too, the rush of being a King’s son getting to Vhagar’s head. As Vhagar confidently accepts his position as prince, demanding for stuff. Nyktos reminds Tiny of the truth. That even if Tiny doesn’t desire power and greatness, half of them does. And even as Vhagar speaks up and treats people rudely with their new title, Tiny does nothing. And so if nothingness continues, then Vhagar will have their way.

Nyktos is the first to escape, placing Crimson in the bag of holding before teleporting out of the bramble. Wystram is next, slipping past the vines and bushes.  
  

Tiny follows suit, and lastly Elisa places ZipZap into the portable hole, teleporting out as well. The moment they’re out, Elisa summons FlipFlap and flies off scouting the area. Just before she leaves, Nyktos blood bonds with Elisa, keeping track of the separated member. Elisa comes across the corpse of a shadar kai, an axe sticks out his back.  
  
The others follow Elisa’s trail and find what she does. Instinctually, Nyktos and Wystram step forward, wanting to help their fallen brethren. But the Elf was already dead. As they inspect the area, the corpse begins coming back to life, like that of an undead. Wystram knocks the undead down and the party hears a scream in the distance. Rushing towards the sound, they find an Elven man, slowly being eaten alive by a Black Pudding.  
  
The party immediately rushes to help, as Wystram brings the elven man to safety, healing him of his wounds. The Elf begins to speak to Wystram, in a language he does not understand. The Elf makes gestures, and points towards Nyktos, not wanting to run away just yet. Deciding it best not to fight against their new friend, Wystram brings him to where the elf wants to be, by Nyktos Eside as they fight to protect the Elf.  
  
The Elf tries to speak to Nyktos but likewise she doesn’t understand, she replies in Elvish but it’s clear that the man does not speak it. Instead, they all manage to kill the undead and speak with the Elf. Strangely the Elf speaks Draconic, a language both Elisa and Tiny understand. The Elf rushes to Tiny, holding up a knife against Tiny’s neck, irritating Vhagar as the man shouts towards Nyktos and Wystram to run. 

  

But the party manages to talk him down, clearing up the situation a little as they assure him that neither Elisa nor Tiny are evil. Speaking to Thalor of the Sylvadrak , the party learns more about the place they are currently in. It seems Thalor and his people have no concept of the outside world, believing the entire world to consist of the entirety of the Misty Forest. Even believing that Steel Weapons are magically conjured by the mists, acting as gifts to the beings of the Forest.  
  
And that it seems most of what they have is provided by the mist. Their entire society seems to be built only out of elves, no humans or dragons. The party mostly lies, so as to protect Thalor’s sanity. They fib about where they are from, claiming to hail from really far north of the forest, so far that no one from Thalor’s would have ever been there. Thalor helps the party by leading them back to his village, where upon their entry the guards are ready to attack, finding the party, their clothes, and items weird. 

  
The party tries to offer help, wanting to heal the injured. But the stubborn elder, Vaelith of the Sylvadrak, denies their help. When asked where they get their weapons from, the party simply claims that the mist gave it to them, that they are very blessed by the Mists. Tiny offers aid, volunteering to build for the village. And just as the elder was about to accuse them for lying, Nyktos spins an even greater lie, claiming that each and every one of them has been blessed by the mist, that they have all been granted unique gifts from the mist with the intention to help others that live around the forest.  
  
The elder still does not let them help. Wystram makes a bold statement, offering to let them hold a knife to his neck, if it means that they'll allow him to heal their injured. And as Nyktos was about to literally lower herself, not caring for her image if it meant that injured people could be healed, her staff digs into the ground, Tenebrous not allowing his will to bow to a mere nobody. But with their words, they manage to convince the elder and are allowed to heal the injured. They also offer to help the village, suggesting that the elder could send them on a mission.  
  
The Elder questions why they’d do all of this for free and Wystram states that they require the location of a waterfall, a Waterfall that he immediately demands that the party reveal how they know of it. Wystram lies, saying that they were sent here to find this Waterfall in their area. This provides some reason but the elder is still not fully convinced and it seems the party will have to try harder to work with someone not as forthcoming as the people they usually meet.
