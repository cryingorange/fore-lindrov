Following Zhae Tsung, the party find themselves at a white fortress with a fist sized crack in the wall, worn and destroyed. Seeing as there is no other way to open the town’s drawbridge, Elisa wild shapes into an owl and heads into the town where she is met with the corpses of what used to be the townsfolk. 

The owl’s keep an eye out for any signs of life whilst Elisa draws the bridge down, they then alert her of some danger and Elisa cautiously approaches one of the houses. Peeping into the keyhole Elisa sees a golden eye staring back at her prompting her to scream as the door before her kicks open. From what little they shared, the party gathers that he is sent by his master, from Avernus, to kill everyone. Seeing as they are going nowhere with him, the party kills and harvests the fiend. Searching the houses, Rook finds a journal that depicts Zhae Tsung in a bad light, calling him a liar and a con man.
