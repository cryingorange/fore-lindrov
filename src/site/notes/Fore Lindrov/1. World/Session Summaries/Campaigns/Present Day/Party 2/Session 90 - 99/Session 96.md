The party speaks to Gleck, Nob, Nitch, the three not evil goblins, learning that a friend of theirs, Yogg, has been brought down by the Giants and is in need of saving.

The party rushes down to help, with Elisa sending in Milton to check first. Milton comes back horrified, so the party barges in to find what is essentially a Giant harem with a bugbear in the centre of it.

The party attacks, wanting to save the Goblin and they do with Ivy tapping into a power the party had never seen from her before, unleashing her anger upon their enemies, killing the she-giant in a horrid death. Grimrid and Elisa try to stop any escalation of the situation, with Roast attempting to help but Ivy sees that Roast does not understand nor believe that the violence should be stopped. Elisa fails to convince the Bugbear as he pushes past to kill Ivy. Ivy sees a vision, a future where the party is dead. And she sees an albino gnoll with a vorpal blade, pus, mucus, phlegm, commanding an army of a thousand gnolls. Combat continues and most of their enemies are made dead or unconscious.

(Zipzap is depressed)
The party tries to talk to Ivy, with Elisa recognising this beast of darkness situation and shares her concerns to Ivy.
The party then talks to Yogg, who used to be the leader of the goblins and is wizard. He speaks of magic as if it were a work of art and showcases the ability to pull spell slots out of thin air. Roast tries, managing to recreate it as figures of elves appear in the magic, one of each type.

The party also receives a rock covered in infernal, following the instructions on it that will apparently give them help with the gnolls. They summon Mozerella the imp. Mozeralla communicates via sign language and Elisa's telepathy that his master is a bone mage who lives to blow stuff up. The party also learns about a goblin king, with a fancy sword that often talks to himself. The party presses on, continuing down a hallway, when Roast notices a substance on the wall and Elisa spots something odd in the hallway. Roast accidentally triggers combat when she stabs the ooze. But the party manages to handle the situation, and ends up talking to one of the oozes who has some psionic-like ability. Elisa, Grimrid, and Roast allow the ooze to sample their memories and are told the following. Elisa feels scared and tastes like ooze do. 

Roast is apparently afraid and tastes like death. And Grimrid is prideful with a hint of sulphur.
Continuing on, the party reaches the room and sees Yuck, an old friend of Elisa's and her previous party who had given the goblin a spell gem of summon fiend and a +3 shortsword. 

The party learns that Yuck is possessed and the evil acts done are by the demon and not him. Meanwhile, Moog is doing all of this for Hruk, the unrequited love of her life.

The party has to try to stop a ritual, Moog, and the demon possessing Yuck
