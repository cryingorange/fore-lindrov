## Meeting Continued
### Artorias Ereturn
Artorias receives a sending from his superior, Elder Maxim, informing him to return to Lupra as more manpower is required there. Artorias shares this with the party, thanking them for the time they had spent working together. Likewise the party wishes Artorias all the best in his endeavours, as Wystram and Artorias share a handshake. Zalto Khan, upon hearing this, states that the party is still his guest and provides the Knight with protection to send him back to the surface, pairing him with a Stone Giant escort. With that, Artorias leaves, returning back to the kingdom of Radhantia to aid his King..
### Lu, the Painter
As one final gift, the Khan bestows the party with a stone statue. He claims that the statue is a man from a time long passed and that mayhaps the party could figure out how to bring the man back, succeeding where the Khan had failed, he mentions that the statue was supposedly a man of great competence Before The Fall, and that it was the Khan’s business to know of all such men in Fore Lindrov. 

The party approaches the statue, confused as to how anyone could still be alive after turning to stone for millennia. Elisa and Nyktos pull out their staff, gently nudging the statue with them, testing it before Elisa goes for a big swing, barely causing any damage to the statue. Nyktos notices as the statue shakes, warning her party to stand back just in case. The stone flakes away, breaking apart to reveal layers of stone and a final layer of paint that has sealed the man perfectly. The ink flows, eventually getting absorbed by the large scroll hung on the man’s back. The man greets the party and the Khan. He introduces himself as “Lü Ewhich the party proceeds to pronounce incorrectly. Lu thanks the party for freeing him and thanks the Khan for keeping him safe. He also tells the party that he knows them, having witnessed their lives in his dreams. The party is unsettled by this but lets it slide for now as they have more pressing matters on hand. They leave in search of the Behir that Khan requested. 
## Journey to the Behir
### Wystram’s Penance
Wystram senses the Raven Queen’s presence. For once she speaks to him, informing him that she knows what his penance will be. The cycle of life and death is in shambles at the moment, and Wystram, the supposed Champion of the Raven Queen seeks to disrupt it once more. She tells him that his castle is where his penance will be, that the sin of disturbing the balance of life and death is to carry out her will without her gifts. That he will only be free to make his own choices when the castle is free. And just as quickly as she bestowed his powers, she took them away, leaving him as he was, still bound by her shackles but free from her expectations. He turns to the party, informing them of the matter as he rips off his armour, now shirtless and back to his original self. Reverting back to his unarmed fighting days, he requests to rent the Wyrmreaver Gauntlets from Nyktos, offering to continue their agreement from before. Nyktos agrees, handing him the gauntlets as they continue on their travels, three of them still tired from not having slept the day before.
### Nyktos E“Other EBrain
As they traverse the lands, Nyktos takes the opportunity to speak with Elisa, whispering about the thing that resides within her head. Elisa takes a look, sensing two brains inside of Nyktos Eskull. Elisa connects with the second brain and sees a vision of a large brain and hears a voice telling her that she will obey. She doesn’t but manages to fight off the presence, standing her ground as it leaves Nyktos Ebrain alone. Nyktos senses the parasite leave her mind, feeling the remnants of it but for now, the cleric is safe. 
### Elisa’s Dreams
As the party continues, they pass a rock formation that resembles a maw. This reminds Elisa of the dreams she had, first hurled past a maw then into the pupil of a snake like creature, then a flash of purple light and sounds of her parent’s cries. Then continuing on, the party finds themselves squeezing through an increasingly small tunnel. They struggle but manage to work together to get each other free. Eventually, they make it out of the tunnel. The path before them leads to the location of the Behir but off in the distance is a purple glow. Elisa stops, recognising the familiar scene whilst the rest continue forth. It is only as Elisa proceeds to the direction of the light that the rest halt as well. 

Turning to Elisa, they learn from her that the places they have passed thus far have appeared in her dreams and in the purple glow was where her parents were. Wystram hears something as well, not scream’s of parents but rather the clop of hooves. As he attempts to reach out to the Raven Queen, he feels a sense of disappointment, as if she is displeased with what’s ahead. Wystram senses a celestial presence in the cave ahead as well, suggesting that they should check it out, needing a unicorn for his ritual. 

Nyktos and Lu listen out for the hooves, the former senses of hint of falsehood, of imitation in the sounds. Nyktos warns the party of the situation, reminding Elisa of the many times they had faced illusions that lure them in with what they desire most. They party still decides to enter, with Nyktos warning that they would pull Elisa out by force if required. Elisa agrees to that and the party ties a rope around Elisa and Wystram, leaving 100 ft of rope between the two of them. They enter, finding a purple metal lined space with two sphincter-like doors. The centre of the room shows a symbol with a mind flayer head. The party quickly surmises that they are about to enter a mind flayer base. 

They prepare themselves before entering, and the sphincter-like doors open. Entering the next room, the party finds a group of people trapped in cages. Elisa recognises these people to be the Brightmires and as they plead for help, the party obliges. Wystram heads to one of the locks, picking it with ease as Nyktos and Elisa break the locks, the former doing so with a potion of strength and the latter using magic. The Brightmires refuse to leave, stressing that there are more innocents inside that they wish to save. The party relents, allowing the Brightmires to stay behind them and insisting that they do not recklessly risk their lives. Exploring the Mind flayer base more, the party finds more cages, more weapons and more corpses. 

### Balhannoth
These ones have their scalps open, their brains missing. Entering the final room, the party finds what they are here for, Elisa’s parents. But the two are stuck in some kind of contraption, hitting against the pods, begging for Elisa’s help. Nyktos rushes ahead, attempting to use her strength to force one of the pods open. Despite her best efforts that should have been enough to break the pods, they remain shut. In response to the forceful attempt, gas begins to fill the pods as Elisa’s parents panic. Elisa rushes to help her family, studying the console beside the pods, trying her best to free her parents. But every button pressed and attempt made by herself and ZipZap only results in more concerning sounds with alarms blaring and lights flashing. Before Wystram and Lu can move to help, the Brightmires betray the party, attacking them all of a sudden. 

To make matters worse, a horrifying aberration with tentacles coming out of its mouth appears holding a unicorn. Wystram is forced to decide between the unicorn that could potentially bring back his family or to help Elisa with hers, as he has promised to. He ultimately decides to help Elisa first and together with Lu work towards holding back the monster and the Brightmires. As Elisa is desperately trying to save her parents, her father tells her that he is lost, to abandon them, and to save her mother instead. Something that Elisa pleads for them not to say, eventually, Elisa’s Father and Mother beg her to run if they turn, that they’ll be changed, that they’ll no longer be themselves, something that Elisa still refuses to acknowledge. 

The plan to hold back the Balhannoth and the Brightmires is disrupted as Wystram hears a voice from the other room, Carver’s. Carver instructs his men to kill “them E saying that they no longer serve any purpose. Wystram soon finds himself ensnared by the Aberration as it proceeds to consume his memories, his mind. His party members rush to help, and as Nyktos approaches the creature, she hears Adella’s voice in another room, pleading for help. The poor attempt to trick the already skeptical cleric fails as she paralyses the Aberration, freeing Wystram from its grasp. 

Upon studying the area, Nyktos realises the odd situation for what it is, a Balhannoth’s lair. She reveals this to the party, allowing Elisa to turn her attention towards the Balhannoth attacking it for its tricks. The party easily kills the Balhannoth as everything turns back to normal. Wystram notices something wiggling in the Balhannoth and so does Elisa, the party finds and harvests four mind flayer tadpoles. Elisa connects with the tadpoles, absorbing the psionic energies within them for herself. 
### Lu’s Past
Having used up much of their energy, the party takes a breather before heading on. As they rest, Lu once again brings up how he had seen the party before. This continuous mention of Lu knowing the party doesn’t provide much comfort as they finally breach the very awkward subject. There, they learn that Lu hails from the past, a millenia ago. He was a warrior in his time, one who fought as the world came crashing down. He had sought to fight the injustice and dangers that pressed upon the world’s borders and spent 20 years of his youth doing so. But alas he had realised that he couldn’t, at least not alone. 

Lu prayed to the earthly edict, taking a gamble as he risked everything he had, his son and his future, for a chance to defeat the evils of the world. His prayers were answered as he entered a meditative sleep. During which he dreamt, his spirit wandering the world for individuals that could help save the world he loves. And in his dreams he saw Elisa, long before she had taken on the mantle as her goddess Esole worshipper. The earthly edict had led him to her, the most likely to save the world. 

Then it led him to Nyktos, the lonely child in the shadowfell that longed for peace, a safe space she could call her own. And lastly, it led him to Wystram, the orphaned assassin, raised to be a cold killer yet now a harbinger of justice, one who seeks to right his wrongs. The party sympathises with Lu, some admiring his conviction. Afterall, the sacrifice he made was not an easy one, nor one many would even think of trying. 

Then Lu goes on, rambling about Elisa’s past with a slight lack of tact. Elisa suffers as Lu drowns on whilst Wystram and Nyktos remain quiet, hoping that it wouldn’t reach them. But alas, one of them is not that lucky, as Lu turns his attention to Nyktos. He begins monologuing about her life, a smidge presumptuously as she looks to her two friends for help, only to see them slinking off. Nyktos tries to entertain Lu, but as he goes, making assumptions based on what he’s seen of her life, she realises that Lu isn’t exactly giving any proper advice. He more so resembles someone who really loves the sound of his own voice. And soon Nyktos checks out from the conversation, allowing Lu to muse upon everything and nothing at the same time. 
### Bhaal’s Cultists 
Finally free from ramblings, the party comes across a group of creatures that reminds Elisa of Buppido. The Derro prevented the party from approaching, stating that the area and the Brightmire cart they had taken were theirs and theirs alone. The Derro also mention Buppido and a blood god they worship, giving Elisa and Nyktos the idea to trick these people into relenting their wares. Elisa takes advantage of her status as a “friend Eof Buppido, claiming that he had told her to come collect the goods whilst Nyktos leverages her status as the Blood God’s chosen, claiming that the Derro should submit to their leaders and hand everything over. But the Derro are not convinced, demanding more evidence for the party’s claims. 

Nyktos blood bends the leader of the Derro as Elisa summons a Bigby’s hand, squishing the Derros head off. Safe to say, the remaining Derro are properly scared, and give in, running away without their spoils. The party then investigates the cart, finding barrels, buckets, and a chest. Lu heads into the cave, spotting corpses and an Ankheg acid sack. Lu picks the sack up, bringing it back to the party without much thought. As he approaches, his party members are cautious with Nyktos suggesting that Lu leave the sack a distance away, not wanting it to burst near them. Lu agrees, and the party leaves that to be dealt with later. Behind the cart is a corpse, one made into an effigy. Nyktos observes the body, finding parts of it that move as she brings the party to help. Lu reaches out, pulling out a Blood Ooze of some sort that is weirdly not under Nyktos Econtrol. 

### Brightmire Cart

They bottle up the blood ooze and turn their attention back to the cart. Elisa and Wystram turn back to the barrel they were about to open. They proceed to pry open the barrel, triggering a magical trap that causes most of them to be hit with a wave of magic. The party is fine though more cautious now as they try once more, all failing to pry open the suspiciously heavy barrel. Giving up, Wystram places the barrel in his bag of holding with Lu’s help. They move on to the next barrel as Elisa focuses on the chest. After much deliberation, the remaining trio decide that Nyktos would open the barrel. And she does, with less effort than it has taken the still sealed barrel. Inside of this one she finds scrolls to create food and water, as well as some mundane items. Meanwhile, Elisa was attending to the chest. She notices a symbol of Solaris on the chest. She tries some ways to open it, and ultimately settled on using her psionics. Just as she does, the glyph of warning on the chest goes off, creating a radius of magical energy that harms the party. Lu dispels the magic and mourns the loss of the acid sack he had collected. But after, the party is able to open the chest proper and retrieve the loot within. 

### The Umber Hulks

The party continues into the cave with Wystram bringing Lu up the wall and interrupting the meal of two Umber hulks. The monsters attack and Wystram calls out to the two remaining party members, boldly proclaiming that he would send the creatures down to them. As such, the two hold back, awaiting. Something does fly off the ridge, something round and much smaller than an Umber hulk. Lu lands before Elisa and Nyktos, leaving Wystram now alone. Elisa and Lu both summon creatures to help Wystram, providing their friend with some much needed support as Nyktos scales the walls attacking from a distance. The party defeats the two Umber hulks easily enough and finds their next destination blocked by a boulder. Nyktos and Elisa take a back seat, allowing Wystram to do something for once. Wystram attempts to break the boulder but it does not budge. Pitying the sight, Elisa and Nyktos focus their magic, successfully blowing up the boulder and clearing their path. 
### Aberrations
As they push past, the party find themselves before some Brightmires who immediately recognises Elisa. The Brightmire approaches Elisa, calling her by name as he asks for her to leave. Elisa is confused, not recognising this particular Brightmire. Not to mention, the Brightmire is not at all surprised by Elisa’s new appearance. He tries to have the party leave but as they do not a battle breaks out. The party manages to deal with most of the trouble, leaving only the Giant left. The party readies themselves for one big attack. As Wystram runs up the length of the Giant’s arm striking it in the mouth, causing its kness to buckle as Lu creates a ball of paint around the Giant to keep its lungs empty of air and oxygen, while Elisa uses her Psionics to conjure great Tentacles that grab and twist the Giant’s arms, breaking them and holding them down as Nyktos strikes the Avatar with her blood staff, an avatar of blood being conjured behind it as it reaches and seeps through the back of the Giant, tearing its ribs and back apart as an ocean of blood pours from the Giant’s corpse, the Avatar grabs hold of its heart, and crushes it, absorbing the blood into itself.
