As the party dusts themselves off from the attack, they find themselves winding through the tunnels left behind by the Kruthik. Eventually making it to the end, with Elisa  and Illythyrra  hiding in the shadows of the Tunnel, Hakon  peeks out of the ending of the tunnel with a light in his hand, discovering a 200ft. drop the party then work on how to get down and hunt down the Kruthik. Unbeknownst to them, an Orc Paladin, Brom who was rifted into Fore Lindrov, having been there for a few days staying alive from the various Fauna of Fore Lindrov was watching them. They are the first humanoids he's encountered since coming to Fore Lindrov. He eventually pipes up, speaking to the party and decides to help them as he spotted the Kruthik running out of the tunnels towards their hive. He passes them rope and they scale their way down, with it ending in Elisa  falling down from the high end of the rope but managing to grab on to a Piton, Brom running to save her while Hakon  drops to the ground. Brom swiftly heals Hakon and he joins the party. 

The party then track the Kruthik down and run into a Duergar named Galnom Forgeripper. Who reveals that he is also on the hunt for Kruthik, seeking a source of Lava for his people. He tells the party that their existence confirms his suspicions on the location of the source of lava as Kruthik build their hives close to great sources of heat. He then leads them towards the nest, as the party move through the Underdark they discover an abandoned temple of Laduguer, the Dwarvish God of the Duergar. They blast their way through the entrance guardians, Iron Golems of Dwarven Warriors and enter the 1st chamber.

Hakon, unable to see in the dark, is led forward by Elisa's tiny fire spirit. Meanwhile Untuk, having just been rifted into Fore Lindrov, was taken by the Kruthik to their hive to be eaten. As he awakes from his unconsciousness, he finds himself in complete darkness and horrific sounds of chewing and tearing flesh. He groggily lights a torch and discovers that he is in the Kruthik's Feeding Chamber. The Kruthik in the chamber begin moving their way towards him to finish him off. 

As soon as activity is heard, Hakon  rushes in with his Maul lit. The party move in and swiftly crush the Kruthik in the chamber, Untuk introduces himself and he joins the party. The party then descend deeper into the chamber, they rush a chamber holding a few Kruthik babies that wandered in. Killing them, they discover that these are the remnants of an abandoned Mind Flayer chamber. As the party investigates, they find a Mindflayer, still alive in its capsule. However Hakon spots that it bears the marking of a Goliath and the necklace of the Thunderer. Galnom and Elisa , enraged, prepare to strike down the Mindflayer. Only to be stopped by Hakon, who frees him and frantically begins trying to speak to the Mindflayer. While all this is happening, Elisa attempts to blast down the mindflayer while Brom tries to calm her down, Untuk as well, seeing this happen watches as Galnom rushes in to kill the Mindflayer, he grapples him and stops Galnom. He cries out, saying that he was wrong about the party as Hakon realises this is a Goliath named Arild, not his brother. Arild does however say that he does remember Hakon's brother in Fore Lindrov but nothing else.

As he sets Arild down, Elisa attempts to make another blast and Galnom struggles to release himself. They both argue that the Mindflayer is an abomination that needs to be destroyed as Hakon insists that he will not give up his kin

Untuk tries in vain to convince both Galnom and Elisa to stand down after the Duergar reveals that the Mindflyaer enslaved his entire race for millenia before they managed to break out. As conflict begins to grow again, Illythyrra  realises something is coming. This breaks up the conflict as the party prepare for combat, Elisa  sends her Wildfire Spirit to blast anything that she sees, meanwhile the rest of the party prepare to spring an attack as soon as anything hostile enters the room. Only for Elisa's spirit to blast a Kruthik, and tell the party that they disappeared. Only for everyone to realise that they are coming through the walls.

The party then make the decision to cast Pass Without Trace and sneak out of the room. Betting on the fact that the Kruthik do not already know they are here, Elisa uses her Bag of Tricks to summon a Boat and 2 Giant Goats. Leaving them and her Wildfire Spirit behind to serve as a distraction for the Kruthik. The party successfully sneaks away and their distractions are consumed. The party then make their way back into the same chamber, Ilythyrra scouts out and explores several more chambers, finding a Hive Lord Cocoon, a Central Chamber where around 60 - 70 Kruthik are lurking and finally their Hatchery and a mud filled room that hold no sign of Kruthik. Before exploring, with Galnom and Elisa's insistence, the Mindflayer is tied to Ilythyrra's Phantom Steed to be escorted with them. With Galnom and Elisa being held off by the fact that the party promises they will deal with it later. 

Eventually the party settle on exploring the Mud Room. Elisa immediately feels Druidic energies in the room, and finds herself face-to-face with 2 Earth Elementals. Enraged and angry, the elementals cite that all humanoids are pure evil, using the fact that Party 1 destroyed all of the Elemental Titans that resided in Fore Lindrov for no reason.

The party desperately try to convince the Earth Elementals to stand down, however they only prove their point. As their purpose in coming to the hive was to destroy the creatures for attacking their settlement. Elisa struggles with the decision to attack the Elementals, and so does Untuk. With him desperately trying to find a peaceful solution to the entire conflict. As the Earth Elementals also prepare to purge the Mindflayer, Hakon  steps in to defend his kinsman and combat is triggered. Untuk, Elisa  and Galnom stay out of the fight. Hoping the Elementals kill the Mindflayer. However during the fight Hakon casts *Shatter* triggering the entire Horde. The Broodmother awakens, charging about 110 Kruthik Adults at the party. They retreat swiftly into the tunnels and are caught by the Broodmother with Elisa on the Shadow Steed with the Mindflayer and Galnom on Untuk.

Ilythyrra , unable to do anything runs past the Broodmother and makes her way out of the tunnel, Untuk, makes the decision to grab Brom, who struggles and fights not to be taken along with him and leave Hakon behind, wrenches Brom away and beings running out of the tunnels. Elisa  struggles, and finally comes to the realisation that she cannot save Hakon, leaving him behind she teleports away with the Mindflayer and leaves Hakon behind as he is blasted by Acid by the Broodmother. Brom as he watches this unfold, manifests an avatar of his spirit, his drive to protect. He rushes forward to Hakon at a supernatural speed, as the rest of the party stand gawk-eyed. He deflects a blow from the Broodmother, and as he prepares to strike it with his blade, his power snuffs out and his blade shatters. The Broodmother pierces his body with an attack and Brom and Hakon begin dying.

However, Brom sees a vision, a handsome being with red skin and a ruby encrusted wand. It offers him a deal, to save everyone. He must sacrifice his Oath, Brom pays the debt and comes into service of this being in exchange for destroying the Kruthik threat once and for all. The being then gives him his first task, to heal Dave. The burned man in Party 2's base, he shows Brom Dave's wretched life. How   and her party killed his family, left him in his empty home, and caused him to become a Warlock. 

Brom, ever suspicious of Fiends, stipulates that he will only have this power for as long as he decides. To which the being agrees. With the contract stating, Brom will only have his powers for as long as Brom Nightkiller decides. Turning him into an Oathbreaker Paladin. The party is teleported out, the Kruthik destroyed and they find themselves in the main chamber of the hive, empty.
