## Velkynvelve Rebellion

The party manages to evade the horde, luring it to the far end of the area whilst they teleport to the lift. Riding the lift up the cliff, the party find themselves face to face with a blue half dragon wearing a symbol that reminds the party of the dragon cult. The Blue half dragonborn is accompanied by a group of humans holding weapons uncomfortably. The Blue half dragon speaks, questioning the party’s strength and asking who their leader is whilst mostly addressing Wystram. The rest gesture to Wystram, allowing him to speak with the similarly dressed half dragon. The blue half dragon attacks the party, lighting crackles as he unleashes a wave of lighting energy at the party. He and his draconic spirit unleashes a flurry of attacks against the party, which Nyktos and Elisa attempt to stop by mentioning their connections with the dragon cult. Though Cyanwrath remains unphased, his desire to fight overcomes logic. Nyktos summons planty grappling Cyanwrath as Wystram does the same. Wystram repeatedly punches Cyanwrath, rendering him stunned as the combat comes to a close, the half dragon claiming to leave it for later. 

## Monk Kame & Grimrid
A group of Kuo Toa approaches the party, from within, a wise voice speaks. Lu recognises this voice, a Tortle by the name of Monk Kame leads the group of Kuo Toa, with one notable lucid looking apprentice, Shooshar. Lu is surprised to see his old friend and before he is able to process that, another shows up. A gruff sounding dwarf shuffles through the group to the front. Lu sees Grimrid, his old party member from before the fall. Lu approaches Grimrid, asking how his old friend had survived. Though Grimrid does not recognise Lu, and when Lu insists his identity, Grimrid scoffs, offended as he proclaims that his friend Lu was a great general and not the man before him. Lu does prove himself, mentioning the name of another friend they had travelled with. As soon as Lu does, Grimrid proceeds to retract his words, pretending as if he hadn’t proclaimed Lu as his friend and complimented him. The two reminisce of old times as Grimrid also asks what happened to a party they used to travel with, Verena. Lu is unsure of her outcome but does mention that she returned home soon after Grimrid had disappeared. When asked how Grimrid had survived the fall, he says that Moradin had a hand in bringing him back. Grimrid also inquires about any dwarven made weapons the party might have, to which Nyktos produces an iron war pick. Grimrid takes the weapon, testing it and promptly breaking it. He does promise to return a better crafted weapon to Nyktos but sighs at the poor quality of weapons made these days. 

## Neronvain 
Monk Kame suggests that he could use some assistance from Wystram, and he follows him, alongside the school of Kuo Toa. The party is led by Cyanwrath to meet with the leader of the rebellion. The party tries to clarify why Cyanwrath had called them villains earlier, which he shares is partly due to how the leader of the rebellion, the Wyrm Speaker refers to them, sharing that he is someone who sees villains at every route. The party puts two and two together, realising that the leader might just be the dragonborn they seek, Neronvain. As luck would have it, it is. Nyktos and Elisa put on the antimemorywiping devices they had taken from Neronvain’s room, allowing them to gain some of the very paranoid man’s trust. Neronvain rambles on about the Cabal and how they wish to poison the entire world, but despite the Cabal’s efforts they can never beat the power of friendship. Though he does allude that the worms were not a part of the Cabal’s plans, speaking of tiny worm-like creatures. In his efforts to save the different slaves, he has a plan to head to Xunbelaern but if he did Cyanwrath would follow, rendering Velkynvelve defenseless. The party offers to do that in his stead and Neronvain agrees, informing the party of the King of Quaggoths which they have an alliance with and dismisses the party. 
## Laura & Jorlan
They leave Neronvain and are visited by a human woman with dark brown hair, clothes dirty and bloody. She bears a scar across her forehead, a mark from Shriala Mizzrym. Laura is angry, having her world stolen by Shriala Mizzrym. She tells the party of the drow the rebellion has kept in prison, wanting them to kill him. In addition, she knows of the party’s plans to head to Xunbelaern and insists on following, even going as far as to state that she would leave either way. The party doesn’t agree yet but does head to the prison to see the drow Laura speaks of. The drow bears many scars and is heavily disfigured. The drow tells the party to kill him, expecting that to be the reason for their presence. But the party doesn’t and instead as the drow looks up, he recognises Elisa, a flash of emotion crossing his face. He introduces himself as the son of the Matron Mother of House Duskryn, claiming that if the party were to help him out, he would owe them a favour, one that House Duskryn with its great military might could help with. He also shares information about Shriala and Taldra. Shriala is a high priestess who ensures the slave supply as well as the rock and stone supply to Menzoberranzan. She is often accompanied by two handmaidens and enjoys referring to herself as the “chosen of Lolth E a term the high priestess enjoys using more than most would ever dare to. In addition, Shriala is vain, often looking into a hand mirror. Once when asked about the mirror by a consort, she responded, telling him that she got it from an old woman, before getting angry at his question and lashing out. She was also said to have a fondness of a particular slave, referring to Laura. As for Taldra, her position as Inquisitor grants her the same authority as a Matron Mother, her and her sister are referred to as “Black Widows Eas their consorts rarely survive. The party accepts the drow’s offer, agreeing to let him free in exchange for his help in sneaking them into Xunbelaern and a favour to be cashed in at a later date. 

## Anintra’s Fate
The party also discusses Anintra’s fate, with Elisa and Nyktos wanting to kill her. Whilst Grimrid and Lu disagree. Once more Lu wishes to fight with Anintra for her freedom, an act that both Elisa and Nyktos think is needlessly cruel. Nyktos pushes back, questioning Lu’s thought process, pointing out that it is cruel for him to set up a bet on someone’s life when he knows he has a great chance at winning when he blurts out that it’s not what his son died for. Nyktos does not relent, only asking if Lu would be okay with Nyktos killing Anintra should Lu fail. Lu agrees. And the matter is left alone for now, as they place Anintra in the prison, knowing that her fate of death would soon be met. 

With that, the party explores the rest of Velkynvelve. They find a hand mirror made of wood with a flower design. Elisa looks into the mirror and sees her family, everyone safe, none of the troubles or pains that they currently have. Nyktos approaches Elisa as she goes quiet and Elisa flips the mirror over, asking Nyktos what she sees. Nyktos sees herself, Houzuki, Beowulf, and Adella, all together, happy, like the night they had in the Abyss. Lu takes a look, seeing his son. And whilst they are all in their feels, Grimrid takes the mirror, peering within to see the thing his heart desires the most. His beautiful, glorious, most beloved, weapons. He sheds a tear, longing for them to back by his side. The party agrees that the mirror is dangerous, knowing full well how powerful it is to use one’s desires against themselves. They keep the mirror, but hide it wrapped up in layers of cloth. 

## Monk Kame & Shooshar
The party returns to Monk Kame, just in time to hear the tail end of his lesson. They then speak with him as he shared that he had turned into a Jade statue that might have flowed down the river, resulting in his arrival in the Underdark. Lu shares that it was Monk Kame who had taught him the technique. Lu and Monk Kame catch up, as Monk Kame shares his philosophy on noodles, puzzling most of the party. Nyktos and Elisa approach Shooshar, both incredibly confused at how this Kuo Toa seems enlightened compared to his other friends. As they speak with Shooshar, his logic isn’t perfect either, though definitely more lucid and calm compared to normal Kuo Toa, only informing the two that Kuo Toa are capable of calmness, they just have to go through some monkly lessons. Shooshar shares his dream to help enlighten all others, and the two suggest that he visit the Kuo Toa settlement in the Abyss, hoping he can help them. In addition, Wystram declares that he would stay back, helping protect the people in Grimrid’s stead and a chance to have a moment of peace without the party’s weirdness or the seemingly impossible situations they constantly find themselves in. He hands the gauntlets to Nyktos, as well as a vial of poison. 

## King Derendil
Last on the list is the Quaggoth King. They manage to pass through the Quaggoth citizens, pretending that they have brought offerings for the king. As they approach, Elisa recognises the Quaggoth, Prince Derendil, now King. The King is flanked by two drow myconids and an actual Myconid, named Stool. King Derendil expectedly waits for the party to bow, which Elisa and Lu do, Nyktos bypasses by claiming to royalty as well, and Grimrid simply refuses to bow, offending the King. The quaggoth claims to be an Elf who was transformed into a Quaggoth by a mad wizard, claiming he was a Prince of an Elvish Kingdom named Nelrinenvane, a name that none of the party members from the past have even heard of. Within his words, Nyktos and Lu can sense his insanity, realising that this lucid Quaggoth might just be a smidge unhinged. By King Derendil’s command, Stool bequeaths Grimrid with the ability to communicate telepathically as well. 

Grimrid is unable to control his thoughts, revealing most of his true feelings towards the party. The party also speaks with Stool, learning that he intends to regrow his colony and have established a symbiotic relationship with the Quaggoth. The party promises to bring corpses back to Stool and leave. 

## Journey to Xunbelaern
They retrieve the drow and Laura catches them leaving, demanding to follow as well. Grimrid tries to dissuade Laura, but fails. And the party does as much as they are able to equip Laura, handing her a Wand of Winter, Piwafwi of Fire Resistance, and Tentacle Rod. Using a scroll of Seeming, Lu makes the party look like drow. Grimrid inquiries of the Wyrmreaver Gauntlets, expressing his interest and approval for Giant smithing. He asks to borrow them which Nyktos is hesitant about given the outcome of the previous weapon Grimrid had borrowed. They test it first, lending Grimrid a magic stone with a Giant rune. When that doesn’t break, Nyktos lends out the Gauntlets. The drow does also recommend that the party leave their pets behind, seeing as a beholder and a hell hound is the opposite of their intent to blend in with the drow. 

Elisa speaks with ZipZap, who is very unhappy to be left behind but is persuaded when Elisa promises to bring back something for him. As for Crimson, he is unhappy about leaving Nyktos Eside. And she tries to cheer him up, letting him make everyone smell like brimstone and reminds him of the Kuo Toa and Wystram that he could play with. But every suggestion only makes him sadder. Nyktos does take out his bed, blanket, and ball for entertainment whilst she’s gone and also takes the sanctum amulet off his neck. She lends it to Grimrid, suggesting that he put it on his belt or hang it somewhere but Grimrid insists that such an item must be treated with the respect it deserves as he hangs it around his neck.  And with that, they head out. 

## Drow Children
Along the way, they come across something. Scorch marks all over the grounds, and a small creature holding onto something. They also find the body of a drow guard, killed by an arrow that delivered acid into his body. They find the reptilian creature holding an arm. With Elisa’s speak with animals they manage to trade for the arm. Using the blood within, Nyktos tries to find the child it belongs to. As she connects with the blood she senses the presence of the child, all around them. She goes silent, walking towards the first area, picking up what’s left of the child, collecting as many pieces of them that she can find before burning what’s left of the body to ashes, keeping it in a vial for a proper burial at a later time. 

The rest of the party examines the scorch marks, and finds a set of footprints to follow. As they do, they find more scorch marks, bodies. And Nyktos and Elisa feel a sense of familiarity, of a friend they once had who enjoyed hunting and bombs. They also feel the ground shake as Grimrid senses the presence of a gargantuan creature that slides under the ground. Still, the party follows the treks, and hears the scream of a child. They rush ahead and see a drow male, who upon seeing them, asks for their help, claiming that the younglings are ahead. Nyktos, Lu, and Grimrid all rush to help without question, but Elisa is held back a bit by Laura who has no mercy for any sort of drow. 

The rest of the party help in their own ways, whilst Elisa flies over to where the child is, seeing that it’s a young girl. She hesitates, knowing that young female drow grow up to become cruel, the teachings create slavers who hurt people because they can. Nyktos protects the Male Drow from the Bulettes whilst Grimrid and Lu rush to help the boy. They forget their disguise, acting very undrow like. Though Grimrid does remember one of the Elvish words he was taught, pointing at the defeated Bulette and calling it “iblith E As they speak with the drow male, they learn that he is Veszika, Vizeran’s father. 

They confirm with Veszika’s sharing that it was Basatur who was chasing after them, hunting for sport once more. Veszika, despite his mark of shame, was left alive to serve as a tutor to educate the young Zekerin Tirin and Xullanah Mizzrym, Shriala’s daughter. Laura immediately wants to kill the child for what her mother did. And the party tries to stop her, all convincing her by appealing to emotions. Though it is what Elisa says secretly to only Laura that gets through, she suggests that Laura kill the child before Shriala, giving her the pain that she had suffered. Laura backs down for now. 

## Laura’s Attack
As they leave, Lu speaks to Laura, telling her that a single drop of paint can ruin a masterpiece, that one has to choose their actions wisely for not all mistakes can be undone. To which Laura responds that she is already gone. The rumbling that Grimrid had been warning them about surfaces, a purple worm, with odd psychic energy, hungering for both psychic energies and flesh. In that moment, Elisa senses the ability to control this Purple Worm, and asks through Rary’s if Laura wishes to kill the child herself. A second’s pause, Laura pulls out her blade, jumping straight at the child.

Elisa tries to stop her, using her telekinesis, but she feels a shadowy hand reach out, interfering. Angered at the Goddess Eof Loss, Shar's constant interference, Elisa fights back, still able to control her magic before Shar dispels it. With Shar’s help Elisa feels Laura’s emotions, the pain, the suffering, the guilt of first failing her family, then now forcing another child into an impossible decision. And as she leapt to kill, a third regret, the realisation that for a moment she truly desired to kill a child. Elisa has a choice to make, to find a way to somehow save Laura. In her desperation, Elisa feels a connection, tendrils of cold warmth that reach out towards her. The comforting touch that wraps around her heart. She knows it’s supposed to hurt as her ribs crack, her body breaks, the rush of cold as her body temperature drops. 

Elisa finds herself in her real house, the vast space in the corner of darkness with the stars. For the first time, Elisa feels as though she can understand the different horrors before her. They are both unexplainable yet comprehensible, individuals yet thousands. She sees the small sliver of what the moon is, a baby. She knows what it is like to live in this eldritch space, able to put it into words that the mortal part of her brain can understand. Elisa and the One of the Stars are before each other, able to communicate by simply knowing. She doesn’t know for sure that it is her, but yet knows that it is the One of the Stars. They communicate without words, and in that moment Elisa is able to speak true deepspeech, casting Power Word: Stun on all present. Elisa floats in the air, her arms are not her arms as they split, cosmic colours shining through, waves of light as she interacts with reality itself. At the moment, she finds herself able to fix the situation. 

Elisa rewinds the situation, right before Laura makes the jump and speaks with her. She tells Laura that she knows what she is planning, and makes Laura feel the effects of the past event, the regret, the guilt, the mess. Laura falls to the ground, lost. But Elisa helps her up, telling her that there’s no need to be guilty. But Laura is lost, so deep in hurt yet unable to do anything with it. Elisa sees what truly happened to Laura, learns the lies she told herself so that she could cope. Elisa sees as Shriala forced Laura to watch the torture of her son. As the poor boy suffered from pain, worked nearly to death by the drow, starving in his mother’s arms, begging her not to let him die, telling her that he is afraid.

Then the pain that Laura puts herself through as she was worked until her body was broken. The drow who picks her up, brings her before the beautiful Shriala who heals her imperfectly, but lets her stay in a cozy room. Elisa sees the way Laura behaved from the start, a defiant person, her unbreaking spirit wins her the interest of Shriala. Elisa holds Laura’s hand, making her feel the empathy and understanding that Elisa has, she understands that it’s difficult and tough, that the pain takes a long time to pass. 

It’s about learning to manage the pain, as Elisa shows Laura her own past, her journey in learning to cope, that the point of it all is to find meaning in other things. Laura asks if she should even go to Xunbelaern. And for the first time, Elisa shows someone her doubt with regards to vengeance. She shows what Windfall told her, of the child that Sylvares cares for, and how she is unsure of how to feel about taking away someone else’s family. Laura doesn’t say anything but will simply follow the party. As one final thing, Elisa gives Laura the feeling that she is enough and accepted and there are other people who feel the same way. 
## Elisa’s Ascension
As time continues, the Purple Worm flees, like a prey before its predator. The other drow are led away with Laura, Elisa looks at her and is able to feel the connection, Laura’s pain is still there but she realises that killing Shriala brings no relief, that her desire to do so was simply a means to kill herself. With her blindsight, Nyktos is able to see Elisa for what she is. Nyktos simply smiles, telling Elisa that she did whatever she had to know, letting Elisa know that she is proud of her. Grimrid is confused, and with his tremorsense he doesn’t sense Elisa, yet he sees her standing right before him. 

Grimrid is unsettled, deeply so, as he tries to comprehend what is before him. Nyktos simply turns, unbothered as she tries to calm Grimrid down, assuring him that Elisa is alright and it is still her. Lu is simply very confused, having no other form of vision, he sees only the seeming form he casted. But he speaks with Elisa, catching a glimpse of what Elisa is and immediately checks if it is still her. When she affirms that it is, he calms down. Next is Grimrid, who tries to be gung ho in response to his unsettlement at Elisa, Elisa asks him if she could show him something, her tentativeness makes him look away but reach out, and Elisa shows him when the worm came, and she could connect with it, and the choices that led to the change, namely the situation that the whole party was in, and Elisa deep desire to help, and how many decision making processes she was making, and how long it took her to come to this conclusion of giving in and becoming a cosmic being, and Grimrid upon realising this, begins to sob at her sacrifice, realising how noble it is, and how unfair his reaction was to her, and he goes forward to hug her and say, "lass don't let anyone tell you you're a monster, and you are not alone.", Elisa then hugs him, and Grimrid comments that "Some say the night sky is beautiful" and Elisa asks is that a complement, and Grimrid awkwardly says Yes.
