Their employer attempts to have them prove their worth, asking the party how they intend to find Sandy, to which they suggest with divination magic. Pressed by their employer, Elisa casts scrying on the collar, only to find nothing, as if something or someone was protecting Sandy from being scried. Their employer takes the time to clarify that divination magics would not work to find Sandy and offers them a special ability to do so instead. He transforms Tiny into a colourful boar. Tiny squeals and screams, unhappy to have lost his draconic form.  
  
They are advised by their employer to hurry, with him hinting that the transformation could turn permanent should they take too long. Hearing this, Tiny races out of the cave, making a beeline, chasing down Sandy’s scent. The rest of the party follows and they find themselves pushing their limits, with the journey exhausting most of them. The party tries to convince Tiny to stop, which he does agree, though insists on travelling out the moment they are able to. The party comes to an agreement, resting for the night.  
  
During the night, Tiny manages to transform back to themselves for a moment, rejoicing before reverting back to their boar self. Nyktos is the only one to witness this, encouraging him to try again though she gets knocked on her back by the fey magics as vines lash out at her. They go back to rest, getting up early the next day to continue their track when they come across a boar.  
  
A normal looking one, unlike Tiny, severely injured. The party tries to heal the boar, though realises that the arrow stuck in its side was preventing its wounds from healing. Nyktos manages to pull out the arrow safely and Elisa heals the boar and they communicate with him, learning more about what happened as he pleads for help. Four figures sneak up to the party, unseen if not for Elisa. She senses their presence, warning the party of them as four tabaxis make themselves known. They explain that the party has interrupted their sacred hunt, a ritual amongst their people.  
  
Nyktos attempts to trick them, wanting to assume the identity as the manifestation of the lands, though she fails at the first instance, lacking the confidence that a leader should have. The party tries a different approach, bargaining with the Tabaxis instead who graciously allows them to fight for the boar. The party launches into combat, easily knocking out the tabaxis and freeing the boar. 

  

They then continue on, eventually reaching a spot where three skeletal bodies lie where Lucatiel finds a wizard’s spellbook and a riddle. The party moves to where they think the riddle hints at, all working together to solve the puzzle. Upon solving it, several ice creatures appear as well as a door.  
  
Elisa tries to enter but the two guards sound out, stopping her from going in. The party suggests that Lucatiel try entering, seeing as they are the one who solved it. And as Lucatiel steps in, they are allowed without issue. Nyktos tries next, being allowed in without issue as well but the Ice Toads growl at Crimson. The party are confused, trying to figure out other ways to go in but nothing else seems to work and they are left with no choice but to fight the ice creatures.  
  
Clearing that battle easily, the party go down the hatch, with Lucatiel, Tiny, Elisa, and Zipzap falling down the pit, with a “feather fall Efrom Lucatiel to slow their descent. They reach the end, met with a beautiful mirror with four ice flowers that adorn it. Curious, Nyktos and Tiny approach the mirror, looking into it as a large paw reaches out from it, they both step back, giving the creature the space they require.  
  
Dalamarus introduces themself, informing the party that only Lucatiel may pass. The party bargains and are offered a game in exchange to pass, having to answer the Sphinx’s three riddles. The first is a riddle of a gift, to answer which the party must produce the item with which the Sphinx seeks, placing it on the grass before them. Tiny is confident that the answer is gold, asking their teammates for some as they do not presently have access to their coin. Lucatiel, who initially claims that they would not partake in the games reluctantly throws a coin for the party, which Tiny uses to get the correct answer.  
  
Next is a riddle of worth, the party is to find the most valuable plane, poison, cure, and slot. The Sphinx directs them towards one of the entrances that opened up, though most of the party are suspicious and instead choose to follow the other path. Except for Lucatiel who goes through the first hallway and finds their darkvision taken from them, they begrudgingly head back to the room where the rest are to inform them before sulking in a corner. The rest look through the options, first looking at the plants, Elisa takes a good sniff of one of them and proceeds to have a sneezing fit. Nyktos tries to identify the condition, realising that the plant releases powders that causes one to sneeze endlessly.  
  
Tiny looks at another plant, identifying it successfully. The party now knows the identities of the three plants, one with pixie dust, one with the ability to knock someone out, and one that causes individuals to sneeze. They can’t decide which is the most valuable and leave the plants alone for now. They move on to the cures, looking at each of them. Elisa identifies a potion of vitality, Nyktos identifies an elixir of health and the last to be a supreme healing potion. The party is once again at a crossroad, all arguing the usefulness of each of the potions. They move on, heading to the corner of the room with three orbs. Lucatiel successfully identifies the varying levels of magic that each orb holds, easily pointing out the most powerful one.  
  
For the first time since entering the room, the party has a clear answer. Finally, they head to the poisons, making quick work of it as they head back to the other two selections. They eventually deduce that the potion of vitality would be the most valuable, given the amount of magic that must be spent to replicate its effects. As for the plants, they settle on the pixie dust, figuring that something from a different plane must be worth more. Satisfied with their answers, they return back to the Sphinx, presenting the four items before them. The Sphinx does their charade, craning their neck close to the party members, constantly asking for their confirmation, trying to trick them. But the party stands their ground and is rewarded. Lucatiel gets back their darkvision, whilst the other three are allowed to take an item of their choosing. Now to the final riddle of lies.  
  
The Sphinx tells them of a tale of a noble and how his jewelled brooch was stolen whilst he was watching a juggling performance. The party heads to a different room where they see a prison set up and the nobleman and supposed thief, locked behind bars and guarded by some individuals who look less like guards and more like adventurers. The party questions the two, finding some discrepancies in the girl’s testimony. Meanwhile, Lucatiel investigates the “guards Efinding personal artefacts and surmising that they were probably the adventurers that came in before they did.  
  
Still, the only way to know for sure who the liar is is to check the evidence chest and the party’s actions causes them to be in combat with the guards. Defeating the adventurers, the party open the chest to find the nobleman’s belongings and the girl’s bag of marbles, inside of which lies the jewelled brooch. They head to the girl, disappointed with her lies as she attempts to manipulate them to give the Sphinx the wrong answer. Knowing that she is an illusion, the party heads back to the main room, giving the Sphinx the right answer once more.  
  
The Sphinx offers the party a chance to leave, warning them of the dangers ahead should they continue, but the party doesn't have much choice but to press on. They give them three more questions, all of which the party answers with ease. Having completed the Sphinx’s games the party is allowed through, but not before the Sphinx warns them of the time dilation and grants them one last gift, giving Tiny back a humanoid form, turning them into a humanoid boar. 

  

And with that, the party heads through the mirror into a beautiful place with colourful plants. Lucatiel and Nyktos recognise this to be a part of the feywild, Winblehuet. They follow along and a plain amongst the woods, where the trees and plant life have been trampled. Tiny finds a tiny plant, barely alive and shows it to the party. Elisa immediately awakens the plant, who begins pleading for sunlight.  
  
The party has no means of providing sunlight so Nyktos uses the next best thing she knows to help. Wielding the Rod of the Blood Ruler, she speaks the ancient word as red energies swirl around the plant, healing it from its wounds. Planty walks towards Nyktos, climbing up on her hand and turning into a bracelet where it rests. The party moves on, following the cries for help as they find a pigeonfolk stuck in a tree. The party tries their best, with Tiny attempting to pull her out but failing. Eventually Elisa uses her telekinetic powers to pull the pigeonfolk out of the tree.  
  
The pigeonfolk comments that it was good that the party uprooted the plant before they pulled her out. The party are confused by her words, though the confusion fades as they hear the loud screech of a plant that frightens a herd of Giant Ox who all make a mad dash away from the party, now threatening to trample them. Elisa summons FlipFlap, easily bringing her and Zipzap out of danger. Tiny rushes up ahead, creating a distance between them and the herd. Lucatiel, not wanting his friends nor the pigeonfolk to be crushed, casts Sleet Storm at the herd, causing several of them to fall and and be trampled by their own kind. Nyktos casts haste on herself, grabbing Crimson as she brings them both to safety. 

  
Elisa then helps the pigeonfolk, putting her up on a tree where she would be safe. Lucatiel observes the surrounding cliffs, attempting to find a safe spot for themselves, though they get gently hurled by Elisa’s telekinesis. Meanwhile Nyktos attempts to help Tiny, telling them to just make a break and attempt to scale the mountains. Soon the party is safe and they regroup. The pigeonfolk introduces herself as Dolores Budnug, a Luma. She chastises Lucatiel for what they did, claiming that they did not have to kill the Oxen. She offers her gratitude, suggesting that the party take a break and when they do she summons a hearth, surprising the entire party as she begins to cook. She offers the party some cookies, which Tiny hungrily scoffs down only to get hit on the head by Dolores who attempts to teach him table manners.  
  
Her motherly nature seems to have an impact on Tiny, who thereafter begins to call her mom. Dolores also chastises Lucatiel once more when they thank her, commenting that they are a bad Myconid. Being the only other person who’s ever used that word, the party questions Dolores on what Lucatiel is, learning about the race of Myconid people and how similar yet different they are from mushrooms. She then offers the party her favourite dish, a fish stew made of nuts and also educates the party on the history of Winblehuet as well as the rules their present unofficial leader has set. 

  

Tiny leads the way, bringing the party to a cart pulled by an Ox that’s led by an Ogre mindflayer like creature. Without hesitation, Elisa rushes forward, followed by Nyktos and Tiny who are ready to back her up. Lucatiel warns Dolores to stay back, not wanting her to get hurt. When the mindflayer sees Elisa, he comments about the orders to bring her back. The party easily takes down the group, ending the mindflayer’s life. And Elisa walks over to the mindflayer, pulling out her dagger, stabbing its corpse mercilessly, letting out all her anger.  
  
Meanwhile, Lucatiel checks in with Psilofyr, melding for the first time in a while. She asks if the Summer Queen is working with the Mindflayers and learns that Lariet is indeed working against the moon, though she is presently indisposed. Psilofyr also takes a dig at Lucatiel, claiming that she would know more if she melded more often. Elisa looks at the mindflayer’s corpse, the tadpole within her mind reaching out to the corpse as she connects with it, drawing all of the powers it had. She is able to peek into the memories of the mindflayers, witnessing how the Mindflayers have entered the feywild, led by Lux in his mindflayer form.  
  
It seems that the mindflayers were helping in the delivery of this package to gain the favour of the one who sent it. Elisa finds herself now able to tap into the mindflayer’s goals, she finds herself floating in a space, surrounded by other citizens of Solaris, all of them seem to be attached to the Elder Brain with a cord, even the Elder Brain has a cord that reaches high above the skies towards what seems to be the Moon. All connected except for Elisa who remains hidden for now. Soon voices begin to call out, all in unison as they cry out for survival. 

  

They break into the package, finding a large golden egg. Which Dolores shares the history of, she explains that there’s an Archfey named The Golden who always gives a golden egg as tribute to the reigning monarch. She seems hesitant, advising them that this Golden individual is extremely powerful. Though the party has no choice and breaks into the egg to get something useful. They gather their supplies, and Dolores leads them towards where The Golden lives, bringing them to the foot of a giant beanstalk.
