# Driders

The party carries on to the next room, filled with web as Milton had described. Elisa casts levitate on herself, floating through the area without issue as Roast scales the ceiling. The party’s attempt to sneak in does not go unnoticed as the Driders accompanying the high priestess question them. The Party attempts subterfuge, but aren't able to come up with any convincing lies and left with no choice, they decidedly fight the Driders. 

During combat, Grimrid experiences a deva ju as his beard is once more exposed to a fireball set off by his ally. The gauntlets given by the Duergar also fuels Grimrid with Rage as he begins screaming through the telepathic bond, insisting on fighting one of the Driders alone. The party is somewhat split up, Grimrid in a corner, Ivy and Elisa handling one of the Driders at the back a

s Roast and Lu approach the leader of the Driders. Just as Roast successfully wades through the web, now before the leader of the Driders, she finds the leader kneeling, her neck exposed. The Drider gives up, readying herself for Roast’s blade. But Roast is confused, having never witnessed any act of surrender in her life. To witness a being, still full of life, yet chooses to relinquish it to the enemy is perplexing. Roast lowers her blade, trying to make sense of the mutters of the Drider but is unable to make out any words. Lu arrives shortly after, seeing this odd stillness in the midst of battle and explains to Roast that the Drider has given up, theorising there to be some deeper reason for this act. 

Roast speaks at the Drider, telling her that the battle is not yet over, the victor not yet decided, that she still holds the power to change the tides. Faced with the Drider’s lack of response, Roast is prepared to leave her alone. Lu respects Roast’s confusion, sparing the enemy for now as he hands Roast a pair of dimensional shackles. His offer to restrain the enemy is met with slight confusion and disapproval as Roast comments that the Drider is no livestock, not one for them to bound and most of all Roast sees no need in binding an enemy whose gaze will not even meet hers. They leave the Drider be for now, helping the rest of the party. As they finish up the others, Elisa notices the remaining Drider, ready to kill when she is halted. 

# Drider's Story

Though disapproving of the situation, she lets her party members listen to the Drider’s life story. The Drider asks for the party to kill her, and when Roasts suggests not, the Drider stands up, towering over the party, indicating that she would continue attacking them if they did not kill her, forcing their hand. But their previous interaction with the Drider convinces her to speak more in her final moments as she shares her life story with the party. The Drider was the matron mother of House Asmodella, a commonborn house. She had many children, including two sons. She claims to have loved all her children and did everything for them. Then, Lolth answered her prayers, calling her the chosen. She had found a chance to elevate her house’s standing by partaking in a ritual with one of the noble houses. 

Yet during this ritual, the sacrifice they had brought was none other than her son. She opposed using her child, angered that the other house had failed to inform her of this and dared proceed without permission. Then, a manifestation of their goddess, Lolth appeared before them. She thought Lolth would help end this, would stand by her chosen, yet Lolth branded her a heretic for interfering with the ritual. Her goddess cursed her into the Drider form that she is today. The Drider then learns that the other house did indeed inform her family. They informed her daughter who made the decision to sacrifice her brother. And this Drider was forced to watch her daughter kill her son. 

And even now she works for Shriala not by choice, but with the hope that it would benefit her children. Elisa steps in, not caring about the woman’s sob story, as she tells the Drider that betrayal and hurt is all they are capable of, and all they deserve. Lu tries to convince the Drider against death, attempting to relate to her plight of having lost a child. Yet in his efforts to sympathize with the Drider only angers her, as someone who hasn’t experienced her life claims to know better. 

Roast makes an honest attempt, stating to the woman before her who clearly understands the drow society, filled with betrayal. She tells the woman there are other drow mothers and children who suffer, that she could help put an end to this cycle, lest more of her are made. Their efforts don’t go through. Nothing much can be said to a woman who lost one of the many children she cherished. Lu tries once more, but fails to see the woman as anything more than a mirror of his old self. As his words anger her, she strikes, cutting into Lu’s flesh. Elisa strikes as well, already prepared for the Drider to make some kind of move as she kills the Drider once and for all. 

# The Party Talks

Lu is stunned, overwhelmed with emotions of failing to convince a parent who like him had to mourn the loss of a child. Grimrid approaches, ready to greet his friend with compassion but his attempt is shut down before it even begins. Grimrid gives Lu his space, respecting and understanding that his friend has had an experience requiring some grace. 

Still confused, Roast approaches Grimrid as she directs her questions both to and at him. She doesn’t comprehend why someone would give up, why this is even a possibility, why- no how could anyone choose that. Grimrid explains, sharing that sometimes life puts people through challenges that they cannot live with. He then points to Lu, saying that that man has been through much, yet doesn’t allow that to bog him down. He smiles still, in spite of what he has been through and that takes much courage. 

Roast looks at the Drider’s body, asking if they often face enemies who give up and what the typical next steps are. Grimrid shares that his only enemies are the moon and the like, not the type to give up. But he also states that in Dwavern fashion they’d give their fellow people a funeral, and whilst that is something they do not have the time for, mayhaps Roast could find something, do something that would help herself with these emotions. Roast, taking advice, approaches the corpse, closing the Drider’s eyes as she stares at the body before her. Then, in her mind, heart, soul, she feels a familiar call. 

Her mother reaches out, hungry, aching, starving. Roast looks at the corpse before her, not wanting to feed this one to her mother as she approaches another left in the corner, courtesy of Grimrid. She places her mother down as the flesh moves, slowly enveloping the Drider, consuming its flesh, leaving only bones. With her mother’s hunger satiated, Roast cleans up her mother, gently assuring the form that they will never hunger, not again. 

Meanwhile, Ivy and Elisa converse, with Ivy inquiring what sort of a goddess, a supposed protector of her people would force them to live such horrid lives. Elisa, provides great detail and insight of the drow culture, having had the cursed luck to be kidnapped by these people once. 

# The Vault Door

With that, the party continues forth to a room with a vault door. Lu takes a look at the door, and proclaims that it is a puzzle of sorts before solving it easily. The party enters the next room, an entrance that resembles the decor of the Maelstrom with two water fountains flowing through the walls and floor. In the centre of the room is a colossal door, made for Giants. Grimrid heads to one of the water streams, happy to have access to clean water as Elisa follows after him, ever ready to prank the dwarf. As Grimrid tastes some of the water, he feels the magic of a divination spell, though there is no response. It would seem that the waters for commune with Annam, the Giant God, are disconnected. 

Meanwhile, Roast approaches the orbs, sensing magic from it as she connects with it. She finds herself in water, not trapped and as she wills herself she returns to the room her friends are in. Roast draws the party’s attention to the orbs, insisting that they all connect to it. Just as before the party finds themselves in water, alone. They feel themselves sinking. Elisa notices that Milton isn’t with her and summons him once more. Instead of the fire spirit, she summons a bald vulture wearing a sweater vest. 

# Oceanus, Kurab

Then the next second, the party finds themselves before a pale blue skinned merman. The merman asks if the party has the guiding stone with them and just as they are about to deny, Roast finds the stone in her hand. The party is unsettled, except for Grimrid who seems accustomed to pretending that he knows all. Elisa, Roast, Ivy, and Lu discuss the oddities, remembering where they came from, yet now in this oceanic place, and all feeling stronger than before. Ivy is lost, having never explored the ocean in all her years and has surrendered herself to the currents, tumbling about during the conversation whilst the others help hold her sturdy. 

Elisa tries to talk to this bird, and receives no response, though she is able to control it, indicating that this bird is somehow her summon. Meanwhile, Grimrid has been speaking to the merman, gathering some information as the merman reiterates the party’s mission to help the merfolk in their battle against the Koalinths, a type of sea hobgoblin known for their terrible manners. The party are sent to Geyser’s trench, wherein lies a powerful magic that the party is to tame and bring back for the Merfolk’s use for protection. 

With that, the party sets off. Just as they are about to leave this place, they find themselves transported in an instant, closer towards Geyser’s trench. Confused, the party tries to make sense of their experience. Lu and Roast are able to surmise that this seems to be a magic projection off another body, of an old memory. Still, with no further clues, the party continues. Grimrid, in an effort to help Ivy who is unable to see in the dark, uses his magic to shine bright sunlight in the deep dark ocean. It takes only a second for the party to see a giant mouth moving closer to them, seeing as food has presented itself so well. 

The party fights the worm, managing to avoid being eaten as Elisa paralyses the monster, allowing the rest of the party to defeat it without issue. As they carry on, Elisa and Roast find themselves able to see through illusions as they reach an illusory door that they both hit their heads on before navigating the handle. They tell Grimrid to help, as Roast, Ivy, and Grimrid all open the Giant door, leading them into a room filled with statues. The statues depict the different types of Giants; Hill, Stone, Frost, Fire, Cloud, and Storm. Each statue showcases what the corresponding type of Giant is known for, with the storm Giant standing tallest and proudest. As the party takes their first step forward, two of the statues activate, powered by the same energy cell that Ivy has. The party has no choice but to fight the statues, they end up badly hurt but somehow alive.
