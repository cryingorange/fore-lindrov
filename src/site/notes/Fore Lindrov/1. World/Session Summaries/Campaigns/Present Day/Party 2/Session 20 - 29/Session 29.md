**__Session 29__**
The party makes their way back to Iymrith and Poisoncalm, who after a long massage from Daegra is finally back to normal. He thanks them for the milk and hands them a scroll of teleport. Before they leave, Elisa sends Iymrith a message bidding her farewell, the Dragon leaves one last parting gift to Elisa, a scroll alongside a note. With that, they teleport back to the entrance of their settlement. Elisa greets Kleve, checking up with him and soon the party enters to the sound of something unusual. Proceeding forth, they enter the Kids & Pulliver’s living quarters where a small but loud performance is held. Pulliver shares more information about the two performers Ilenti and Nyktos. The performance ends soon enough and the party returns back to their room where they are reunited with Arche and Minerva. Minerva shares some of her research and Arche comments on how she was abandoned by the party. The party houses Rook, Jub, and Ankhta as well. Vesper calls for a party meeting, explaining how she needs some time off from adventuring to clear her head and get better. In a similar vein, Ankhta expresses wanting some time off before she leaves to search for a way to bring Amunkhet back. 

The next couple of days go by peacefully but an odd rumble causes the room to shake as Elisa, Rook, Ilenti, and Nyktos help usher the people to safety. They make brief introductions as the siblings try introducing the concept of drums to the party though it doesn’t go well.

On another of their rest days, the party hears noises from the entrance and find Kleve fighting with a rapier wielding male cleric who seems nice at first as the party helps mitigate the situation. He introduces himself as Vendrin though seems oddly suspicious. His suspicious behaviour causes Nyktos to cast a spell, forcing him to obey their commands. Vendrin spills that he is from Larryton, a place built by the people Elisa wants to hunt down, under their control, he also reveals the directions and defences of Larryton. The party calls for Holly’s help, though before she’s able, Vizeran shows up, scoffing as he does before helping in modifying Vendrin’s memory and sending him on his merry way. Minerva seeks out the party, informing them that she needs a sample from the bloating corpse that they had all seen before. She expresses wanting to go along, as she would have to verify the sample. Reluctantly, the party now consisting of Elisa , Rook , Ilenti , Nyktos , and Arche brings Minerva along. They come across a dirt mound, one that Elisa recognises as the place where one of the children, Milton, was buried.

She mourns his death, as the party gives her some space. But suddenly, Minerva is nowhere to be found. Each one of them senses something from the cave up ahead, Ilenti hears the call of her mother, Nyktos feels an overwhelming sense of dread, the same that struck her when she met the mysterious man, Rook sees the symbol of his people, signifying a covert, while Elisa hears the voice of Fool, calling for the death and sale of a child. The party all rush in with reckless abandon, scouting out the place and finding the people and things that they seek. Elisa sees Fool stuck in shadows as she casts a Flaming Sphere, trying to kill Fool, Rook hears his coven, a Forger speaking of redemption as he chases the voices down while the siblings hear their mother begging a man to let a child go, Arche hears the voices of people she doesn’t recognise, and Minerva hears Godfrey, albeit having been captured by the sinister creature that lurks in this lair. The two Shadar Kai soon realise that it’s a Balhannoth, a creature that warps reality to portray what one yearns for before them, with Ilenti captured by the many tentacled aberration, the party engages it in combat and swiftly defeat it and as the cave returns to normal, Arche questions the voices she heard as Elisa tries her best to dissuade the changeling from finding out the truth. Arche drops the topic for now but doesn’t seem fully convinced by the party’s words. Rook expresses that he has to leave to rebuild his weapon, and the party bid him farewell, wishing that he survives his journey back to their home.

They then come across a cart, as Elisa recalls yet another death of her friend, Hastings, a Human Fighter that sacrificed his life to allow her and her old party to escape the zombie-like apocalypse chasing after them. The party soon realise that Elisa has psionic powers, as she manifests a psionic image of her horrid past, the rest of the group are shown exactly what happened when Hastings Epassed, holding onto and holding off two monstrous creatures chasing after the party and the group of children they were escorting after tossing his greatsword aside. With Elisa seemingly subconsciously unleashing a grouping of vines to keep the last one restrained. Continuing on, they’re met with bones of women, people whom Elisa and her party tried to save but couldn’t. As the pain sears through her brain, the scene unfolds before everyone’s eyes. The sight of the women jumping, and failing as their bodies get crushed by the cart. As Elisa and Nyktos speak, Ilenti notices a man getting close to the dead bodies, casting animate dead on them. She counterspells, stopping the man from desecrating the bodies. Battle ensues and the man brags, calling himself “Morti the Undying Eas the party shows him their strength. They convince him to leave the bodies be and he leaves, swearing vengeance on the party.

They approach the spot where Minerva said but are unable to find any samples for her research. Then a third headache hits, as the party watches yet another horrifying memory. The sight of the bloated corpse, and a massive wave of worms from it unleashing itself upon the world. Left with no choice, the party heads on to the old abandoned settlements. As they approach, a bunch of bandits make their presence known, demanding that the party relieve themselves of their possessions and making lewd suggestions. Elisa notices that the bandit leader has scavenged Hastings EGreatsword from the site of his death. The party, in no mood for the depravity of the bandits attack them, however as they start winning the battle, one of the bandits rushes in to release a monster they had on their hands. Unleashing a horrid worm infected corpse, that calls out for Minerva as it attacks both the bandits and the party, infecting and turning any creature that it kills. The pox ridden creature explodes in swarms of worms that cling and try to dig into the flesh and skin of others.Luckily, thanks to some quick thinking from the party and a Gryphon, the party manage to keep Minerva from being captured and successfully bring a sample back home.

The next day, Elisa receives a Sending from Iymrith, who requests for her assistance. Elisa hands a scroll of teleport to Ilenti who is cautious about the dragon scale-like substance she sees and makes a comment about shouting at her sister should they find themselves killed by a dragon. They appear at the amphitheatre and before the dragon in question. Iymrith is nice, greeting the newcomers before handing Elisa a Ruby-Weave Gem for her and her previous party’s help in healing her. She then asks for their assistance with procuring information to cure her magical wound. Taking a day to prepare, Elisa unattunes to Mr Squiggles, promising him some other food source and Nyktos attunes to the living armour after a rather morbid conversation about the holes in Elisa  E chest and what will happen to Nyktos once she attunes to the plant-like armour.

They head out to the village, finding 2 owlbears eating at the dead bodies. Elisa casts Speak With Animals and strikes an agreement with the owlbears, convincing them that the party is not here to steal their food. As the party try to investigate the cause of death of the entirely genocided settlement, after Elisa discovers from the owlbears that they came after, as well as the strange smell that the northern house was emitting, one of cold darkness. Ilenti notices familiar shadow magic around the space, hinting at what killed them. They make their way to the big house the owlbears pointed out to them and find a gruesome scene of a family tortured to death. As Ilenti tries to close the mother’s eyes, she finds it impossible, and reaches out to grab her as the Goliath shouts at them, claiming that they smelt like the people who killed her family. Elisa recognises the Goliath to be a revenant and tries talking to her but it doesn't work. Ilenti , in a panic, shouts about simply looking for her mom and this seemingly manages to placate the Revenant.

Albeit the calm does not last long as the dead bodies of the Revenant’s family begin to rise, as well as those outside the settlement. The Revenant, now revealed to be Eilif from the journal entries that spoke of a mysterious woman named Orvo that happened upon the Settlement and was well-versed in the Giant Runes that Eilif discovered and began to use to help the settlement, is forced to attack her now risen family. The party does not manage to spare her that trauma, with her being made to beat down her daughter into a paste.

The party also manages to hold off the shadow zombies long enough to allow one of the Owlbears to escape, albeit one other was not so lucky. As they eliminate the zombies and solemnly help Eilif bury the settlers and their bodies. The revenant entreats them to help her with her revenge, to kill Orvo. She tells them that Orvo had come to the village, starved and decrepit but unusually, she knew of the runes that Eilif was translating, with the both of them managing to discover what is supposedly a trove of Runes. However just before they departed, Orvo, with some kind of ungodly strength, killed everyone in the settlement, making sure to strike at each settler so as to prolong their pain, leaving Eilif for last as she was made to watch her family tortured, before having her own throat slit.
