With Ilenti’s new found divination powers and some convincing lies, Ilenti uses the werebear’s organs to lead the group forth to the werebears Ecave. Before the cave are some bears and effigies made of mud, stone, as well as some animal flesh. The party unsuccessfully sneaks up on the bears and battles ensue with them swiftly knocking out the bears. Nyktos and Rook investigate the effigies, with Rook finding out that they are more pagan than religious in nature and pointing them out to Elisa. Upon investigating, she realises that they are druidic in nature and are intended to intrigue a druidic spirit for good fortune for the hunting of food. They enter the cave and are met with several traps. They avoid the initial pit fall but Ilenti steps into a bear trap, the teeth of which sink into her flesh. Rook rushes forward to help as Beatrice steps in to remove the bear trap from Ilenti’s foot. Ilenti proceeds to pocket some of the bear traps. A similar situation happens to Nyktos as well, this time Alastair steps in to help, remarking about checking for traps when he himself steps into one as well. He uses his vampiric powers to avoid the trap, swiftly using his blade to disable the trap. Still, his actions raise a brow from the already suspicious Hayden. Alastair is quick to lie, fibbing that he had dodged the trap and disabled it just in case. Though Hayden doesn’t let up, still suspicious of Alastair. Nyktos joins in, complimenting Alastair on his quick reflexes to help sell the lie and it seems to be enough for now as Hayden drops the matter. Alastair helps Nyktos up in a gentlemanly manner, prompting snickers from Ilenti. The party presses forward, investigating the many rooms as Elisa and Rook come across many crates, mostly emptied, finding a wizards of wine insignia which Zephyros muses that it could simply be a winery made by some wizards. Elisa also finds a different insignia. As they press on, they find a druidic circle, which Elisa reads to be “In the circle of ancient runes, the paw that leaves no trace shall pass unbound through the veil. Nature's kin alone, with fur and claw, shall dance within the mystic embrace. EAs they attempt to figure that out, Nyktos and Ilenti search the other room, a well-lived living quarters and find a notebook that they are unable to read. Meanwhile, Elisa and Rook are attempting to figure out the druidic circle. Elisa first attempts by turning into a bear and stepping into the circle, where vines immediately rush out to attack her. She then casts past without a trace and they’ve dragged one of the unconscious bears in. They eventually figure out the conditions to enter the circle without being harmed, though it doesn’t help them much. The party eventually gathers together once more and Elisa reads the journal Ilenti found. They understand the werebears' situation of poor hunts that has led to their new ritualistic ways. 

  

Elisa uses the insignia she has to unlock one of the doors. As they look past, they find Danica, Gordon’s partner, tied up, her arms strung tightly with razor-wire thin rope, with Nyktos realising that her arms have already begun undergoing necrosis. She’s tied to an effigy that resembles an Elvish Woman. Before the leader of the Werebears is able to speak, the party immediately springs an attack. Zipzap is able to identify that the effigy is an effigy of Shar. The party find themselves in dire straits soon after, with the Werebears having taken down Nyktos , with her on the brink of death screaming in fear as she desperately tries to heal her limbs over and over, stopping them from being torn away from her as a Werebear mauls her. Ilenti as well, finds herself beneath the rotted breath of a Werebear, who rips her arm away in her own mauling after attempting to save her sister. Nyktos cries out for help, screaming “Not again Eas the Shadar-Kai’s cries of fear echo through the cave. Ireena tries to push off the Werebear, and with hope dwindling and Nyktos about to be finished.. Banecroft appears.  With his appearance, the battle stops and Ireena heals Nyktos whilst Ilenti is healed with a mote of darkness from the effigy. They all turn around to see Victor, unhappy with his arrival. Nyktos holds a weak hand out in front of Ireena. Victor speaks to Alastair, sharing about his new blade that is better than the one they built before together, and he reaches a hand out to Ireena, who pushes past the party and accepts, going with her to the manor. He mentions that her father would like to meet her, and that the family will be together again. He looks at Ilenti and Nyktos, stating that the name that they’ve made for themselves is not enough and the potential lesser than what it should have been, once more mentioning the words that he should not know about. Victor then turns to Beatrice, commenting that she’s a spawn of his that no longer serves him, that he could command her to kill herself. Beatrice is notably afraid and fortunately, the Dread Lord does not follow through with his threats for now. His last words to the party, or specifically the siblings are that they too will accept his invitation soon, as Ireena has, where they would meet their maker, meet who they are meant to be, Children of Chaos as he leads Ireena away, taking the Werebears with him as servants, telling them that they serve a weak Goddess that will not provide. The Werebear leader destroys the effigy as his people follow Banecroft while the party watches angrily. 

  
  

Hayden then immediately points his gun at Beatrice, shouting that she is a Vampire. With the party swiftly convincing him to stand down, as they need to tend to the dying Danica. The party then tend to Danica, Gordon’s wife, whose arms they reveal have already died, and that the only way to save her is to cut them off. Gordon pleads to the party to save her arms, saying that without them, it will be as good as killing her. Beatrice and Alastair share a glance and whisper before Alastair steps up and offers a way to keep both Danica’s life and arms. As he mentions bestowing her with the gift of vampirism, Hayden acts up once more, cursing and swearing at the party, demanding if they knew Alastair and Beatrice were Monsters. He vehemently rejects the notion of turning Danica into a Vampire, citing that her soul will be damned. Elisa , not having any patience for this uses telekinesis on Hayden, dragging him far away as he angrily shoots at the walls. Gordon readily accepts, and the party agrees, mostly concerned with the blood Danica would need to feed on when she awakes. They settle on feeding her bear blood for now, trusting that the couple can handle anything else later on. Rook voices that he would take watch of Danica for the night. Once that’s settled, the party heads out to speak with Hayden. He asks the party what they intend to do, if they intend for the people to be cattle once more and shares how the Vampires have lorded over them and made their lives hell. The party speaks up for their friends and Beatrice assures Hayden that they want nothing like that, that they merely wish to free the land, whilst Alastair is notably silent. Eventually Hayden gives up and Elisa lets the humiliated man down as he pulls himself up on his wheelchair. 

  

As things die down for the night, and they all heal up, the siblings check up on one another. Nyktos with her horribly scarred neck and Ilenti with her missing left arm. They both chalk their injuries up to “new aesthetic choices Eand Nyktos awkwardly broaches the subject of Ilenti’s self perception and how she refers to herself as a monster. In many words, Nyktos shares her love for her sister, constantly trying to reassure her that the voices in her head are telling her lies, that Ilenti is one of the kindest and most caring people she knows. Though she is unsure of how many or if her words actually convinced Ilenti. They continue resting for the time being, and as the next day approaches, Danica rises from where she was buried. Once her hunger is satiated, she thanks the party for their help and questions why they did. The party, confused, simply remarks that they did because she needed help. With that, Danica and Gordon lead the party to Vallaki.

  

Right outside the walled settlement, the party notices pikes with the heads of men and women. Atop their heads adorned hats similar to Hayden’s. The atmosphere is heavy, with Hayden clearly unhappy and uncomfortable with the display. The party is soon greeted by a black haired nobleman who wears a necklace with a purple gemstone, his right arm is missing and is replaced by a hook. By his side are two mastiffs and a boy, around 14 or 15 years old, tall, and his body heavily scarred and deformed, his head bald. The nobleman happily welcomes the party as the guests of the Festival Of Destiny and reveals he was already told of the party’s arrival, with him immediately trying to temper Hayden’s anger, saying that “The better side won. EHe introduces himself as Baron Jovlin Vallakovich. He leads the party around the city. They pass by a church, where a young male priest attends to the graveyards, notably sporting a pair of fangs. The priest is dressed in robes that look to be too big for him. 

  

They then continue, passing by the heavily guarded city. Elisa questions about the pikes they saw outside and the baron merely states that he put them up as per Lord Banecroft’s request. They pass by a pair of statues of two women. Alastair seems to recognise the women, muttering their names under his breath as they pass. The party is soon led to the Baron’s house, where a woman dressed in red noble’s clothes, the baron’s wife, introduces herself as Lydia. She goes on about the party’s good deeds, from how they had saved a father and son til their latest battle with the werebears. The Baron also introduces his son, calling him “Vic- Ebefore stopping and introducing him as Vorun. They are then led to a room where the performers and decorators of the festival reside. There, they see a young ordinary looking human girl, about 16 years of age, a man with thick hair on his arms and a beard, and lastly a set of two young elves with blonde hair and sharp eyes who resemble one another. The Baron then leaves them be. They get acquainted with the 4 people, and learn their names, Dalaina, Herschel, Molly, and Misha. Dalaina approaches the party first, thanking them for the existence of the festival and thus their hiring. Herschel expresses his confusion of being hired, seeing as he was not very good at such dextrous work, whilst Molly and Misha were the true experts at this. Upon further inquiry, they reveal that they were personally approached to be hired for the jobs. Something that struck as odd to the party. One by one the siblings make quick connections, realising why these exact people were hired. The four also shared that they’ve been told to stay where they are, that they couldn’t return to their family during their employment. The party learns of some places in the city, namely to avoid the Wachter house with Lady Fiona and her children, and of a toy store owned by a man named Blinsky. Dalaina asks the party for a favour, requesting their help to check up on her parents, Dana and Andre, as they are quite old and haven’t heard from her in weeks. Herschel requests for them to hand a doll he made to his daughter Peter, and to also check on his wife, Charlie. The party accepts their requests, denying any payment. Nyktos approaches the mother and daughter, asking if they too had someone, or if it was only the two of them. Molly shakes her head, sharing that it was just her and her daughter. With that, the party leaves and Beatrice takes a moment to thank them. She shares about her life before, how she had been a guard before she was turned into a vampire, ending her words with another grateful thanks for the party. Alastair then mentions the statues and the sisters he had spoken about before, Delilah and Dahlia. They recall the prophecies given, “Twins trapped together, bound by hard earth, violated by a hooked hand. EAlastair shares about the twins Einterests and history, with Dahlia being a sex-crazed maniac with a thing for naked slaves and Delilah being the more tempered of the two, but even he doubts if the party free the twins if they could hold their temper without slaughtering the entirety of Vallaki. Elisa then brings up the four decorators hired, how it had been suspicious that they were personally approached. Elisa mentions how Dalaina was similar to her and Nyktos tries to lead the party to the same conclusion that she had arrived at without actually saying it. However, it does not work and the shadar kai says point blank that the four hired workers were meant to represent them, a 16 year old, a bearded man, and two elves. Nyktos shares her theory that something bad would probably happen to the four at the festival. However, with not much information of exactly what, the party has no choice but to let that be for now as they split up with Alastair and Beatrice, each on their way to find more information. The party heads to the toy shop, looking for a slight change of pace. As they enter, Ilenti notices that the shopkeep seems shocked to see her. The man introduces himself as Blinsky, and his monkey pet as Piccolo. He offers Elisa a free toy. And Ilenti inquires about his works and if he took commissions, which Nyktos senses that he’s hiding something about not taking commissions. With continued conversations, Blinsky agrees to make a toy arm for Ilenti. Rook buys some toys for the kids back at home. As Blinsky leaves for a moment to pick up some toys, the party peers over his counter to see the toy that he was working on, a doll with white hair. He returns and is not happy with the party for looking at his work. Nyktos buys a ball for Crimson, and as Blinsky hands her the items, she realises that he has a very strong grip as he tells her to take good care of the toy. Elisa takes the opportunity to cast speak with animals to converse with Piccolo, making a series of monkey noises to communicate. Weirdly enough, Piccolo responds with monkey noises that Elisa is unable to understand despite her spell. She also tries to detect the monkey’s thoughts, able to hear it as it laughs at her for speaking to a monkey. Rook uses his divine sense and realises that both Blinsky and Piccolo are Fiends. They soon leave, with Blinsky chasing them out of his shop. Rook reveals what he learnt to the party and takes steps to identify a doll that he has bought and learns that it has some magic imbued within it, though unsure what exactly it is. 

  

The party heads to where Dalaina’s and Herschel’s homes are. They see a handsome man in the pillory and speak with him to learn of his crimes. He shares that he’s an abbot of Saint Andal's and that he has been approached to help a mute orphan boy named Izek by Vasili von Holtz. And how he, Barcovius, had turned deformed Izek, attached the flesh of others to the boy. Then Bunny came along, with the same deformities as Izek, he revealed that Bunny was completely immortal, and seemingly impervious to all damage. The Deva asks the party to seek out and check on the groundskeeper, Milivoj, who was put in charge of the Church after Barcovius Esentencing, offering his map in his old study in exchange.

  

He then laments that there is no hope, that the only option for all mortals of Barovia is to willfully allow themselves to be slaughtered, to give in to the despair so that their deaths are at the very least ones without struggle. When Elisa asked how curing Izek of his disability had ended up in this, Barcovius  explains that he simply wanted to make the boy stronger. Rook tries to speak with the Deva, talking down to him for his loss of hope, for his despair, lamenting if this is what it means to be a Celestial. However the Deva only responds with weeps of horror and calls for acceptance to be slaughtered. Ilenti does not like the propaganda the celestial spread and also tries her hand at stopping his nonsense, though he seems set in his ways. They eventually leave and arrive at Dalaina’s house. They are greeted by the loving couple who are happy to learn that their daughter is doing well. They ask for the party’s help to rid the rats that live in their basement. The party, of course, agrees and heads to the basement. They catch a couple of rats, with Rook, Poofy, and Nyktos each killing one of them before swarms of rats with glass dome brains approach. As the rats see Elisa, she hears a cacophony of voices in her head, saying that she’s like their master, but that she was incomplete and they had to kill her. The party kills off most of the rats, safe for one who they question. The rat reveals that a mother’s love made them, and that there was a little rat boy in the nest. Following the direction from where the psionic rats came from they are met with more rats and a young rat boy. The party disposes of all the rats, and are left with the scared child. They attempt to speak with him, though he is clearly afraid of them, seeing as they killed his friends. Zephyros suggests that the boy could be suffering from some sort of madness. And the party tries to heal the boy, fortunately a greater restoration cures the child, though he retains no memories of what just happened. Elisa offers the boy some goodberries, and that seems to help calm him down. The boy shares that he was led to a windmill by a group of women with sharp claws, informing the party of some of the fates of children the blood witches took. Nyktos hands the boy the doll from Blinksy’s and the child sits down, safe with the watch of Milton, Zipzap, and Crimson. They explore the rest of the underground area, approaching a ball that morphs into an eye as a voice speaks through asking who the party thinks they are and for them to identify themselves. The voice sounds angry, shouting at the party that they would not put her daughter in danger, that Broomhilda would not be put in danger. The party tries to speak to the voice, assuring them that they do not harm children but the voice does not listen. Nyktos tries to get a last word in, assuring Agatha that they do not want to. The voice does not care, and vanishes, the orb exploding. 

  

With that, the party brings the boy back upstairs, to the surprise of the elderly couple. The party is left with not much choice, but asks the couple where they could bring the child to. The party also takes the opportunity to learn more about the city from the elderly couple. Elisa asks them about the Wachter house, where they learn that Lady Fiona has two sons, Nicholai and Karl . They learn that Lady Fiona hates the Baron, that she has stated that she’d rather serve the devil than a mad man. And prior to Lord Banecroft taking over, the Wachter house used to be the Embermarks who were in charge of the livestock, that things changed for them when Lady Fiona’s husband, the face of the family, died in the war. They learn that the lady has a book club with members of the upper echelon, though not much else. Having gathered all the information they can, the party sets off to bring the boy to the church, hoping that it might be a good place for him to be for the time being.
