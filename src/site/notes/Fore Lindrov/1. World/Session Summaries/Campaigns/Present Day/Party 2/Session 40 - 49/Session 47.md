The party, with Exethanter’s guide, make their way to the treasury room to claim the vast array of treasures for payment to the Yugoloths. From the treasures, the party acquires a portable hole, allowing them to take all of the gold in the room. They then head to the Amber vaults next, with Pyrite and Exethanter staying outside the vaults to keep an eye out for danger. 

The rest enter the vault of Ghasts and Elisa is the first to touch one of the many sarcophagi. Elisa finds herself transported to a nicely decorated parlour with a futon in the middle. Atop the futon lies a woman with platinum hair, icy skin and noticeably heart shaped holes in the middle of her palms. The woman says nothing as Elisa greets her, instead she simply stares longingly at the druid, gesturing for her to sit beside her. Elisa does just that, asking the lady for her identity. To which the woman explains that she is the Evening Glory, her portfolio consisting of love, eternity and obsession. The Evening Glory claims to be love itself and when Elisa asks she confirms that she had spoken to Exethanter and granted him an eternal life to bring back the one he loves. The Evening Glory then offers Elisa a gift, a power to charm others if she kisses them, though accepting the gift would also mean that The Evening Glory would have to kiss Elisa as well. Decently traumatised by all the different weird encounters she’s been through, Elisa politely rejects, attempting to not offend The Evening Glory whilst running back to her friends. She shares this information with the rest, who are all equally uncomfortable and agree not to go back to that sarcophagus. Beside The Evening Glory’s sarcophagus is a dull, unlit one. The sarcophagus bears a small crack, and with a little investigating, Nyktos surmises that the crack would be enough for someone made of liquid, or blood to escape from. 

  

The party then enters the vault of Harkotha. This time, Nyktos transports before a great lava lake where a five headed Hydra stands before her. Nyktos tests the lava, throwing a rock into it and watching as the mineral melts into the magma. Hesitantly, she steps onto the lake, prepared for the scorching pain of melted flesh but it never arrives. The lava is difficult to wade through but certainly possible. Tarr Haak the Five Headed Destroyer offers a deal; to bestow any who crosses the lake of lava great strength. Nyktos calls out to the Hydra, and receives no response, as if she’s too small to be noticed. Relieved, Nyktos runs away from the Hydra. Upon her return, she shares what she has seen to the rest of the party and Tiny seems to be the most excited about the boon offered. Still wanting to browse their options, the party heads into the next one together. They find themselves in a space filled with webs, a sight that reminds Elisa of the many unpleasant experiences she’s had before. They see a woman, at least from waist up as her lower half is that of a giant spider. In her palm is an egg sack, as Drizlash offers the party a boon, the ability to traverse lands like a spider can, in addition to being able to perceive without sight. The party, though intrigued by the means to see without sight, are mostly uncomfortable with the idea of eating an egg sack filled with spiders to accept the deal. They politely retreat from Drizlash space, heading off in search of more boons. 

  

Exploring the vault of Maverus, the party ends up in a temple chamber filled with bones as two massive skeletal claws of a dragon reach out towards them. Before the party is Tarakamedes the Grave Wyrm, an undead dragon who offers them the ability to become a dragon in exchange for swearing vengeance on all true giants. As he sits atop the throne, accepting the deal, Tiny’s scales begin to shimmer, turning a brighter shade of red as his teeth sharpens, unlocking the draconic powers within. Tarakamedes rejoices, reminding Tiny to bring death upon their enemies, the giants. Tiny agrees and the party leaves before checking up on him. As Tiny expresses his distaste for the giants, Nyktos mentions Sigrid to test the extent of Tiny’s hatred. Tiny affirms his desire to kill Sigrid and Elisa quickly changes topics, sensing it better to think of other things. They then find themselves in a circular chamber of human teeth, as the bowl in the centre of the room shows visions in the mix of grey, white, and blue liquids. The entity known as Dahlver-Nar, The One of Many Teeth offers a gift in exchange for a tooth. The gift being the ability to breathe like a dragon would and immunity to fire. The power comes with decent drawbacks that hold the party off from taking the deal despite the huge benefit it provides. 

  

The vault of Thangob is next, and the party sees a dimmed sarcophagus with marks that suggest to the party that’s where Sangzor was held before he broke out. Following that, the party finds themselves in a room with a beautiful starry sky and a spherical object covered with red cloth in the centre. The spherical object is revealed to be an eye as Yrrga The Eye of Shadows offers them the gift of seeing without eyes, and to see through illusions and things hidden with magic. Elisa and Nyktos are intrigued, but they all leave to continue perusing the other boons. 

  

They then head to the vault of Shalx, and are transported to a stone chamber, with sculptures of snakes. In the middle of the room lies a bowl filled with bubbling purple liquid as a woman with grey green fungus for hair offers the party mastery over toxins. The ability to spread sweet diseases, and the strength to resist poisons. Next, the party found themselves before Zrin-Hala, a humanoid made of clouds who offers them power over the storms, such immense strength that their enemies would quake and fall before them. Not very interested in the deal, the party leaves for the last room. 

  

Before entering the last room, the party senses some people behind the door, they’re cautious about it being a trap but surmise that it’s probably others who have come looking through the vaults like themselves. They swing the door open and are met with three Nothics, a species familiar to Elisa but unfamiliar to the rest. The Nothics immediately start pulling secrets from the party, using their powers to learn information that the party would otherwise wish to keep to themselves. Nyktos and Tiny respond poorly to this, immediately demanding that the Nothics cease their actions. Though that only inspires them to peer into the minds of the fascinating strangers. For Nyktos, information of her friend, Adella and how Nyktos had used the last item she had of her friend’s are taken, the Nothic antagonising her when they ask how Adella would have felt about that. For Tiny, they extracted the truth of his being, his obliterated body. But Elisa mediates between the two groups, assuring her party members that the Nothics weren’t doing it on purpose, whilst politely requesting for the Nothics to stop. Fortunately, Elisa manages to calm the situation down and the Nothics leave. Though as the three aberrations leave, Pyrite receives a shock, worried that his party members may have turned into something else as he shouts into the vaults. Zipzap responds, silencing the paladin as he affirms that the party is doing alright. For the second last dark power, the party finds themselves in an inky black void, sounds of chains about as a chain link the size of a town slams down before them. The party is silent, unfamiliar with what this could be but the power offered is tempting to Elisa, the power to bind creatures of certain types to service, as well as the ability to imprison one creature. For the last boon, the party meets Doresain, The God and King of Ghouls. He offers the party immortality, an undying body that no longer has need to fear diseases; to become a Darakhul. But this last offer pales in comparison to the others. The party looks expectantly, waiting for more and when they realise that’s all he had they turn and leave. 

  

Having seen all of the offers, the party makes their decisions on what other deals they wish to make. Tiny heads over to Tarr Haak’s sarcophagus accepting his deal without a moment’s hesitation and as Tarr Haak’s claw touches Tiny’s metal suit, it bends and crushes. This reveals Tiny’s true self, a kobold who lost most of his limbs and torso. The party watches as Tiny sprouts a second head that’s half made of bones. But Elisa and Nyktos come to terms with things quickly. Tiny seems meek, not as loud as he once was having his secret revealed as he scrambled to put himself back together again. He awaits their judgement, mentally preparing himself when he asks for their thoughts. But instead he’s met with concern, not for his pre-existing situations but rather for the second head that he had just grown seconds ago. He assures that party that he’s alright before his other self begins to speak. The other Tiny is more direct, less friendly, and more prideful as he takes over half of Tiny’s display screen, adamant on being seen. Next, Elisa heads over to Yrrga looking into the giant eye as she takes The Eye of Shadows Eoffer. At that moment, the party sees Elisa’s eyes melt away. Tiny rushes up, shaking Elisa in hopes of snapping her out of it. And moments later she returns, Yrrga has fulfilled her deal, blessing Elisa with the ability to see without need for eyes. Elisa is fine, quickly adapting and eager to crack jokes about her lack of regular sight. She confidently heads to the chain themed room next, accepting a second deal as well as the rusted and gnarled chains wrap around her neck, digging into and poking through her skin but likewise granting her with the spells that would no doubt aid her in her quests. Lastly, Nyktos, having spent much time contemplating, heads over to Drizlash, taking the spider egg sac from her palm and consuming it, thus completing the deal. Nyktos Ebody begins to transform, legs breaking and changing into something much different as her lower half gets replaced by that of an arachnid's, her eyes growing and splitting into eight. She returns before her teammates, immediately checking if they’re alright with how weird she now looks, especially Elisa who seemed exceptionally uncomfortable around spiders. Fortunately the party is alright and Nyktos shows the party her new ability to walk on walls. Although, their celebrations come to a halt as the realisation sinks in; Nyktos effectively looks like a monstrous abomination and any reasonable person would probably go off running and screaming or worse yet actively trying to attack the party if Nyktos looks the way she does. Though seeing as they are within the same space as her Lord is, the cleric asks her God for help and receives a silvery black crown with red gems that look like rubies from a distance but in truth are gems made of blood. As she adorns the crown, Nyktos transforms into her elven self, but devoid of any scars that she’s picked up during her time adventuring. 

  

They three then exit the vaults, with Elisa using one of her staffs as a walking stick and Tiny by her side, pretending to help guide the seemingly blind young girl. Pyrite is taken aback by Elisa’s new appearance, immediately both confused and concerned as to what happened inside. Exethanter points the obvious out, suggesting that Elisa might have gone blind and Elisa leans into the joke, enjoying the successful prank. Pyrite’s attention is then turned to Tiny, his screen now showing two heads rather than just the one. The other Tiny is annoyed, voicing his displeasure at being stared at. Tiny reveals the deals he took, proud to now be a real dragon. Looking at the new appearances of his comrades, Pyrite turns to Nyktos relieved, she still looks the same, safe for the crown. Pyrite makes a comment about her being the reasonable one, prompting an awkward laugh from Nyktos as she takes a couple more steps back, preparing Pyrite for what he’s about to see. As she controls her form, her legs break once more, shifting and transforming into her new arachnid form. With all their deals revealed, Elisa confidently strides to the crypts. Elisa informs the two demeaning wizards that they had spared Exethanter, explaining that if they had been nicer, they might have gotten what they wanted. What was originally meant to be a talk quickly breaks down into battle as the wizards refuse to be polite for even a second. The party deals with them swiftly, taking out their feelings towards the two. With that all settled, the party infiltrates the rooms of the two dead wizards and rest for the night. The time dilation allows the party a second long rest whilst the world outside remains relatively the same, now 9.40pm of day 4. 

  

The party sets out once more the next day, all mentally prepared to enter Demogorgans Edemiplane when they instead find themselves in what they soon realise is Sangzor’s place. They barge in, witnessing a child hiding from demons as Nyktos Escar acts up. Elisa rushes to protect the child, creating walls that form a barrier between the demons and the child. The party rushes to attack, sparing no efforts to safeguard the child from any danger. As they murder the last demon, he swears vengeance on the party but they largely ignore him. Elisa drops the spell, and they meet a young girl ten to twelve years of age. The child introduces herself as Viona, sharing that she lives here with her family but snuck out. As the party introduces themselves, they learn that the child is Volenta’s, Banecroft’s youngest wives Esister. With Tiny bashing through the stones used to seal the entrance to their home with ease, the party enters the temporary housing area of Sangzor’s cultists. They make their way past the traps and find themselves in a room filled with organs and bodily fluids. They check in with Viona, who confirms that those are their food supplies, harvested from the bodies of their fellow cultists, killed by the waves of demons. Though slightly weirded out, the party moves on, bringing Viona back to her family. The party meets the Ma'iz, noting the horrid scars they bear, no doubt a wound sustained from their various offerings to Sangzor. Viona's mother, Sibil steps up to speak with the party, clarifying their intent. The party convinces the cultists of their intention to help, with Viona affirming that the party saved her. Sibil requests for the party’s help, wanting them to get rid of the demons constantly invading their home. The party agrees and quickly arrive at the room with a portal and a fiend who looks similar to Moulder. They try speaking with the fiend but to no avail as they are launched into combat. The demon that swore vengeance on the party enters through the portal, threatening to end them as Pyrite coolly reminds the demon that they’ve already killed him once. They deal with the demons swiftly, and return to the Ma'iz who graciously reward the party for their efforts. The Mais express their interest in helping the party and it is agreed that they would await for the party at the entrance of the Amber Temple. 

  

The party moves on, using the tongue key they got to open the door to their final destination. Oddly enough they find themselves in a grassy area of sorts, surrounded by trees. The party is confused, not expecting this of Demogorgon’s demiplane. They attempt to investigate the area, believing it to be an illusion and not the real deal. Though each and every one of their attempts seems to prove otherwise. Pyrite attempts to use his Divine Sense but a wave of nausea hits as he begins puking. The others are concerned but also confused and the confusion continues as a horse approaches them. The horse is a normal animal and as Elisa attempts to speak to it, it attacks her triggering combat. The party deals with the horse swiftly and before they know it a bunch of smaller dinosaurs, equally maddened, rush forward. The animals crave flesh as they run towards Zipzap and Crimson, perceiving them to be the weaker of the lot as they ruthlessly charge at the two. Zipzap is annoyed by the animals, proceeding to spend his highest level spell slot available to attack those who wronged him. Meanwhile Crimson holds his ground as Nyktos helps defend the pup. The dinosaurs are dealt with and the party continues wandering about the place. They eventually come across some signs of a battle, with Nyktos noting that the attacked individual is still alive, although a bit injured. Elisa begins tracking the bloodstains with Tiny’s help and soon the party comes across a long narrow bridge. Tiny walks the bridge first as Pyrite expresses his desire to be placed in the bag of holding. Tiny’s foot catches on the vine bridge occasionally but nothing too serious as he begins to make his way across the bridge. Nyktos follows next, transforming into her true form and carrying both Crimson and the bag of holding that Pyrite resides in. Meanwhile, Elisa realises that she has her figurine of wondrous power to be used and activates it, allowing both herself and Zipzap to pass the bridge without worries. As Nyktos crosses the bridge, magic takes hold and she follows the suggestion in her mind to walk off the bridge. Meanwhile, Tiny asks for Elisa’s help, wanting to be ferried by the canary as well. Tiny tries to grab onto the canary, succeeding only to have Elisa thorn whip him and allow him to fall down. Nyktos lands first, arms wrapped around Crimson, protecting him from any danger. She finds herself at the bottom of the chasm, some skeletons littered about and a very out of place tree in the middle of the rocky terrain. Nyktos lets Pyrite out of the bag of holding, lamenting that he probably wouldn’t enjoy their current situation. Pyrite sighs but moves to investigate the strange tree whilst Nyktos looks at the skeleton. She notices that the skeleton seems to be that of a lizard and he notices something wrong with the tree. But they’re interrupted as a loud thud is heard as Tiny lands on the ground. Fortunately, he’s alive this time as the tree comes to life. The stone walls grow eyes, reminding the party of something they have seen before. The battle with the tree ensues as the party suffer from the effects of the eldritch horror. The eldritch horror has party members teleporting to random locations amidst battle, confusing and disorientating their allies. In one such instance, Tiny attack hits Nyktos, delivering a heavy blow to the cleric. Other situations include turning into plants, and Pyrite’s new stinking scent and rotting appearance. The party deals with the tree and with combat over remembers the initial victim they were trying to rescue. Nyktos offers the party a trip in the bag of holding and they all except, save for Pyrite who vehemently refused after falling from the bag of holding twice whilst Nyktos held it. His frustration towards his immense bad luck since entering fuels him with the strength as he climbs up the wall without issue. 

  

The party finds the victim, a Kuo-toa, cornered by two bigger dinosaurs. Elisa speaks with the dinosaurs, threatening them to leave the Kuo-toa alone. Tiny tries to help with a show of strength as he walks over to a nearby tree to uproot it, as he tugs, he realises that the tree might have been too big and roars in an attempt to mitigate the awkwardness. Next, Nyktos, already in her arachnid form, pulls out the Rod of the Blood Ruler, slamming it down into the ground as she casts command, shouting at the dinosaurs to kneel. Pyrite helps channel his beast of darkness to scare off the dinosaurs. Fortunately it works as the first dinosaur runs off, the second is less lucky as the beast of darkness takes over Pyrite, forcing him to bite into the neck of the dinosaur, draining it off its blood and tossing it aside. The Kuo-toa, though now safe, is extremely wary of the very scary party. Elisa begins speaking to the Kuo-toa in Aquan, learning that his name is Sloopidoop from the town of Sloobludop. They try asking Sloopidoop about the area, and learn about the “Screaming Jungle Eas well as the dinosaurs with lizard people. Nyktos notices a familiar mark, the necklace of Sloopidoop bearing a similar symbol to her cursed mark. When she shows it to Sloopidoop, he is extremely happy, calling Nyktos the child of Blibdoolpoolp. He leads the party to Sloobludop, as they make their way past the village that’s been split into two. Sloobludop brings the party to Ploopploopeen, the Archpriest dressed in scale robes with a staff bearing the visage of a woman with elvish ears. The Archpriest excitedly greets Nyktos, knowing her name even before she’s introduced herself as he claims that the high priestess, Ableeblerboop, has foretold Nyktos Eappearance and how she was foretold to be the champion of the Sea Mother. Ploopploopeen explains the history of the Kuo-toa, how they were once one faction, but when High Priestess, Ableeblerboop was kidnapped, Bloppblippodd, Ploopploopeen’s fellow priest, started spreading rumours that they had seen a vision that the Deep Father, Leemooggoogoon was the true god. Ploopploopeen brings the party to his home, requesting for their help to act as bait to lure Bloppblippodd into a trap. The party agrees but wishes to rest for the night before enacting their plan.
