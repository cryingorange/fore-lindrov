Travelling back to Gauntlgrym, the party hears cries of a woman from afar. Despite their feelings, they rush to help and come face to face with a crying drow woman who is desperately trying to run away. As they approach, they notice that her eyes have been gouged out. From a distance they see a hooded figure catching up, a man with two scimitars slowly walking towards the poor lady. Upon questioning the man introduces himself as Kaz explains that he is a revenant and tells the party of the crimes that the drow had committed against his family, how they had ruthlessly killed everyone he loved in front of his very eyes. He shares that she is the last Drow amongst the troop that slaughtered his family during their enslavement. However, Amunkhet argues against her senseless killing despite Elisa and Vesper's indifference to her plight. 

Sparking a short fight between Elisa and Amunkhet, with Amunkhet casting a Wall of Force around Kaz as he strikes at Amunkhet in response to her trying to talk her down. Elisa reacts in this way by beginning to attack Amunkhet to get her to drop Concentration on the Wall Of Force, and during the abrupt interruption by Aurelion and his horror at Kaz causing everyone to attack each other, the Bagman releases itself from the Bag Of Holding, tearing open a rift into the Dread Realm of Fore Lindrov as the party is nearly killed by the creature. Kaz is killed as the Drow, Balaena Baenre, manages to escape. Amunkhet's life is lost and her body slides straight into the Dread Realm, with the party escaping. However Elisa observes that the Bagman is doing something, as if pulling away at the Dread Realm, bringing something into the material world. But she is promptly convinced by the remaining members of the party to retreat.

Back at Maegera's Heat, Vesper spots a familiar face, through the guise of her disguise, the Blind Drow Ankhta, someone that she had just seen hours prior in the Temple of Shar. After a very awkward conversation with the Drow in an attempt to discern her intentions, Ankhta reveals herself to be "Tasek". An alias that Amunkhet once used before, thus confirming her identity. However Vesper chooses to withhold information to allow the party to rest. The next day during breakfast, a member of the King's Guard comes into the inn, informing the party that the King has requested their presence as well as a replacement for their missing party member. 

Vesper reveals Ankhta's existence to the party, with Elisa barely able to contain her knowledge that Ankhta is a Drow. With Her and Aurelion very awkwardly introducing themselves to Amunkhet's partner. At the King's request the party appears before him and he asks for the help once more. They are sent to an MLA outpost to try and capture as many of the force as they can. Seeing as they would be in battle and have gained enough of the Dwarves Etrust, Vesper takes opportune time to reveal her true Celestial form to the King and his trusted guards. Surprisingly, the King seems indifferent to the presence of the Seraph before him and sends word to inform his men about it. With that, the party travels on as they meet with the rest of the troop and Damien. Questions are then raised about Amunkhet  E absence. Zipzap, still lacking his social cues bluntly states that she had passed, shocking those present, including their new party member. They take a moment of silence for their fallen friend as Vesper discreetly passes the signet ring to Ankhta.

The party meet the leader of the ambush group, Captain Torin. Who reveals to the party the plan-of-attack, a double-prong with the party led by Damien. Who heartily greets the party and Aurelion when he realises that the party were brought in on the mission as well. Unfortunately, due to the time sensitive mission that they have, questions are left for later as they press on. With Damien as their guide, the party travels through the underdark and comes across a settlement of Goblins revealed to be hunting Dwarves and various humanoids as part of their diet. An enraged Damien expresses his desire to exterminate all of the Goblins, with Vesper's sarcastic pointing out of his seeming bloodthirstiness for genocide. Ankhta manages to trick Damien into letting the Goblins go by pretending to permanently Charm them into not hunting humans anymore. However, using Detect Thoughts, Elisa knows that the Goblin themselves are pretending to be foolish, swearing revenge in their minds.

During a Short Rest, Damien attempts to talk to the party, asking their opinions on the MLA as well as revealing his empathy for the plight of the Dwarves and their long-term suffering as a race. However this conversation is cut short as Aurelion pulls Damien away to confront him about his racist tendencies, turning into a shouting match between the two. The rest of the party get to know Ankhta a little better, with her revealing that she is a little bit of a schemer, to Elisa's delight. As Aurelion and Damien's fight is about to end, Mordicael sends a cryptic message to the party, telling them not to hesitate. As well as revealing that Goetia's interests rest in the MLA winning in the upcoming battle.

The party arrives at the outpost only to find themselves being ambushed by the MLA, with Captain Torin and his men being held hostage. The party are immediately told to surrender, and in their hesitation, a Dwarf is killed. The party is convinced by the MLA to relent, however in that moment, Torin seemingly makes a deal, likely with Goetia, prompting him to be gifted with a hellfire Greataxe, his men break away during the chaos and the party manage to beat down all of the MLA members, quickly trapping the Werewolf Leader in a Wall of Force as she is forced to watch as her men are killed by the party and the DWarves. Ankhta charms the Werewolf and has her follow the troop back to Gauntlgrym. Elisa questions Captain Torin on his killing of the soldiers of the MLA, with Torin cryptically saying it is "his price to pay."

With that, they successfully complete their mission and head back to town to rest. Seeing as it is still early in the day, they head for a quick shopping trip where they meet Lady Ravenheart who discreetly requests for their help. Telling the party that a settlement has refused to join the Brightmires, however, Lady Ravenheart still wishes to impart them with supplies and assistance, asking the party to secretly bring a "lost" wagon of cargo to the settlement quickly, and to check up on them. The party arrive there only to find an attacked Settlement, alongside a strangely happy and comically optimistic Samurai, while imparting help and listening to what's happened, the party learn that the Samurai's name is Shizuka. He enthusiastically walks off to assist the settlement in saving those kidnapped by the marauding Orcs. Only for him to turn around and also tell the settler that he requests that they smile, to bring hope in a hopeless world. He trails the party on their journey to save the settlers, with Elisa learning that he is in fact, Prince Livid. The son of the Pudding King.

The party encounters a group of Orcs ambushed by Gnolls, who they swiftly take down. With Shizuka revealing that he is not Human, the Orcs dismiss the party and leave the area. Saying that they are preparing for something at their camp. Shizuka also eventually reveals his true identity. Telling the party that he met someone named "Master Kachi" who taught him the way of the blade and to bring light to a hopeless world. Shizuka then tells the party that the words left by Amunkhet truly affected him, and he left to journey on the surface of Fore Lindrov. Revealing that the Time Bubble outside causes the surface to move at a faster rate of time than the Underdark. Stating that he has been travelling for five years, and that he's met a large town, a utopia of sorts named Larryton somewhere in Fore Lindrov, a utopia filled with various races that live together, including strange Hippomen, as well as revealing that the place has an Archangel, a Lich and guns. Run entirely by a Purple Tiefling there.

He also reveals that his Sister has not taken well to Amunkhet's words, swearing vengeance upon Blingdenstone, with plans to retake it and continue her father's dream of creating a slime Kingdom. Ankhta then takes this time to also reveal her true identity to the party and attune to the Ring of Mind Shielding, meeting her lost partner Amunkhet after Vesper reveals that the mummy's soul is left in the ring. The party makes their way to where the Orcs reside and find themselves interrupting a funeral. From the Orcs' words they learn that it is their tradition to have guardians that would protect those that have passed. The guardians in question are human sacrifices, the very people that they have kidnapped from the settlement, with the Warchief revealing their people were killed by Humans. They also see a MLA Werewolf presiding over this procession.

Better ensues once more and the party finds themselves in another precarious situation.  Vesper is trapped, unable to move as she is forced to take hit after hit and though the enemies are in no good shape either, Vesper isn't that lucky as the last strike lands. And just before she dies she casts a sending out to the world, to her world. She informs her husband of the unlucky situation she’s found herself in, and for the last time confesses just how much she loves them and how she wishes that things could be better. She receives the response, the most of the words are muffled. She hears one word, “here Ebut she's unable to do anything more.

Just before the last strike hits, Ankhta hears a voice, it's her lover. And she's about to do what she always does: save other people. Ankhta begs for her not to do this, pleading that for once she could be enough, that after centuries of living life without her, that they can finally be together. And as  Amunkhet hesitates she hears a familiar voice, “And be with my Pharaoh you shall Eas Sethos throws himself out to take the hit, forfeiting his soul and being obliterated completely. Vesper, Ankhta and Amunkhet watch in horror as his soul is completely destroyed, while Aurelion is bleeding out behind a building within the Orcish Warcamp. 

Vesper hears an ominous voice asking if she is angry.. She gives in to the voice, and manifests as an Aspect of Vecna and liquifies the rest of the Orcish Warcamp. Shizuka quickly finishes up his part of combat as he frees the settlers, the party move in awkward silence as they return the settlers back to the settlement, and Shizuka volunteers to protect them, as the assistance given by Alicia and the party has convinced them to join the Brightmires.

The party return to Gauntlgrym as a clearly stressed Alicia sends out Knights from the outpost in Gauntlgrym to protect the village. Now back at Maegera’s Heat, the party is approached by a man clad in armour looking for “Tasek Eand delivers a letter to her, the letter tells her that the Dwarves have to be stopped, written in Elvish and in a very familiar handwriting. 

Meanwhile, Vesper treats the party and Rook to dinner as they get to know one another. Later that night, Vesper speaks with Elisa about the dread realm they had witnessed and learns of what happens to souls when they die in Fore Lindrov. Some concerns are also shared with regards to Amunkhet  E dead body having been left there but not much can be done at the moment. As the party returns to Aurelion's now empty room, Mordicael shares that Aurelion had given Xartaza free authority to sign contracts in his name. Goetia has already taken his soul, Mordicael says that thanks to Amunkhet's death and Aurelion's taking, their contract is now void. He disappears in sulphur and hellfire as the party reads Aurelion's letters. Each letter is personalised, with Aurelion giving them well-wishes and luck for their tribulations ahead.

The next morning, Rook joins the party, expressing his willingness to help people even without the need for coins. Not long after, the party is summoned by the King to the Iron Tabernacle where they witness the dwarves undergoing a ritual that channels holy energy to fuel them with immense rage. The King shares a location that is important to the MLA and sends the party to investigate the area and its purpose. The party accepts the mission and leaves to the outer city where they encounter a familiar troop of Drow. The same Drow seeking out Gauntlgrym. The Drow are seen tormenting a Goblin named "Gobby". The same one the party saved from Orik's murderous rampage. The Drow are tormenting him by shooting arrows at his feet, the party, horrified by this demand that the Drow stop. Their coy refusal sparks a conflict as the party does battle with the Drow, it ends with Rook calling one of their souls to his service for a day, revealing to the party a partial insight into his Warrior Religion, citing that he calls the Souls of the Dead to assist him in battle, and chooses to use the Souls of the Evil for Good.

Rook questions the Drow Soul, and it is revealed they have an outpost nearby that they have to report to. With House Baenre quite annoyed with the loss of Q''Xorlarrin. The party comes across a door that leads to the location the King told them about. There, Vesper uses her eye to see through the locked door, seeing mind flayers stuck in columns. Her ability to see through the door surprises Rook who immediately expresses concern of his face being seen. However Vesper has taken extra care not to look at him, and Rook slinks away, to avoid having his face seen by the Celestial.

The party navigates past that and when Elisa learns that there are mind flayers in the room, she rushes in, followed by the party. The room is however empty with no signs of mind flayers even when Elisa starts chipping away at the columns. Who is stopped by the party, for fear of awakening the Mind Flayers.  They continue searching the area, breaking into a room that seems to belong to a couple. Zipzap eagerly steals all of the shiny treasures that litter the room. Rook in the meantime manages to unlock one of the two chests in the room, finding a necklace and a leather bound diary. Upon reading the inscription of the necklace, Ankhta realises that they have broken into her friend’s home and urges the party to put everything they found back, much to Zipzap's chagrin.

Continuing into the space, the party comes face to face with a stout dwarf dressed in spiked armour with fangs and a serious stink. They attempt to settle it diplomatically, though words fail as the dwarf and his mind flayer minions continue to attack. With the Dwarf eventually truly trying to kill the party after Ankhta says that they were sent by the King to "check on the lovely couple that lives here". She reveals that not everyone agrees with his methods, and that he did not used to always be like this. He was kind, and sought to bring freedom to all people. But something happened to both he and Targrim when Targrim brought him back with Revivify. She asks the party for their help, emboldened by the familiar face of Ankhta, the party agrees to do this while they speak to Thibbledorf Pwent, the vampiric Dwarf. He tells the party that he found the Mind Flayers outside of a rift, and they seem strangely attached to him. However he holds no love for them, desiring to find excuses to get them killed. He does reveal that they do have their uses, and that a location holding MLA members was discovered by them, although something has had them riled up. His disgusting habits are also picked up by Elisa who is very entertained by his farting and refusal to ever bathe, much to Catti-Brie's chagrin. The party then heads out and encounters an old Dwarven Temple where the Dwarves are revealed to be experimenting on people. They did not destroy the Tadpole Brine Pool nor the Goblin that was being experimented upon by Zaraen Oblodra. However, through Calm Emotions and smart words, they convince Huck to allow Vesper to use Sacred Flame and X-Ray Vision to destroy the Tadpole. Thus ridding him of his affliction, but now, they must find a way to get the already rotting tadpole out of his brain.
