# Fighting Jarchigudai

## Morvek's Introduction

Cinderhild commands Jarchigudai to fight with 2 clay golems and 2 other golems, then proceeds to tell the party that they do not have enough challengers as pets do not count. This enrages ZipZap who is promptly placated by Elisa and promises him some toes in exchange. Grimrid calls out to the audience, asking if there are any brave ones willing to take on the challenge, none respond. However, a Duergar appears out of invisibility offering his help. Roast tells the party to be wary over [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Spells/Official/5th Level/Rary's Telepathic Bond\|Rary's Telepathic Bond]], but Elisa supports the addition of this Duergar, citing her experience with them, that not all of them are evil. The Duergar, Morvek, joins. And the party is made to introduce themselves. Grimrid does his usual spell of bragging, followed by Elisa who does not as Grimrid tries to hype her up. Then Roast, who plays up to the gravitas Grimrid tried to set, thinking it all a game when Elisa and Grimrid confirms that Grimrid is taking this very seriously. Ivy also does not play into the game, earning Elisa's approval and Morvek introduces himself as the "Heir of Runeforge". 


## Combat

Combat ensues as Morvek and Grimrid spend it trying to outdo one another, both landing substantial hits, both getting tossed by Jarchigudai. There are points where Grimrid hides his surprise at Morvek's capabilities but Morvek does not so much as glance at Grimrid when Grimrid manages to land a blow. Jarchigudai spends most of the fight being disappointed at Grimrid, seeing that he is still the same "we can take them" child from before. But combat does end with Jarchigudai yielding before Grimrid could land a blow that would have knocked her out.
Cinderhild comments on Jarchigudai's age when she loses, and as agreed entertains the party's request to hear them out. 

# Save Goldenfields

## Discussion

Unable to offer anything substantial for Cinderhild to relinquish Goldenfields, the party requests a task from her. They are given two options, convince Goldenfields to surrender without a fight, or force the Hill Giants to join them. The party requests for some time to discuss and are told that however long they take to deliberate will impact the time they have to complete their task.

The party begins to discuss their next course of action. They party mostly agree on asking Goldenfields to surrender for now, lying in wait before the right time to ambush. Elisa reveals herself to be friends of the Hill Giants and suggests against bringing them in. Grimrid agrees as two factions of Giants fighting would cause great danger and horrid impacts on the world. As the party struggles to decide, a voice slides in. 

## Mordicael

Mordicael greets the party, introducing himself to the newer members when Roast points out that they have met before. Mordicael produces a ruby rose out of thin air, bowing as he apologises for having forgotten a beauty like her. Despite the party's protest, Roast accepts the rose, and becomes permanently [[Fore Lindrov/2. Mechanics/Condition/Charmed\|Charmed]] after failing a saving throw. Ivy notices that Mordicael is no longer wearing the Soul Lantern at his hip, and angrily asks where it is, with Mordicael ignoring her request. As the party attempts to chase Mordicael (Michael) away, Roast is obviously quite enamoured by the devil, politely requesting to show his chivalry to the rest of the party to inform Ivy where the soul lantern he had taken from her went. Mordicael indulges, sharing that the Soul Lantern is somewhere else, with the rest of her family. Ivy attempts to distract Roast, speaking to her and holding onto her face before trying to reach for the rose which Roast does not allow. Grimrid and Elisa repeatedly shoo Mordicael away, to which he does agree to leave, unable to offer a proper deal. Before he does, he extends a hand out to Roast to bid her farewell and as she attempts to reach out for his hand, Grimrid and Ivy try to hold her back to no avail as Elisa attempts to hit Mordicael. Roast offers her hand and Mordicael plants a kiss on the back of it before leaving. Grimrid tries to clean the hand, but Roast moves it out of the way until Elisa soils it with magic.

# Party Interaction

The following two conversations happen. Morvek speaks to Elisa and Ivy, learning of Elisa's backstory where the mind flayers had taken her city and enthralled her people. Morvek is awkwardly realising that the horrible things that happened to Elisa are things that he relates to, and the horrible things happening to Elisa, reminded him of the horrible things that had happened to him.

Grimrid speaks to Roast, asking her why she too had reservations against Morvek. Roast reveals that back home, Morvek and his people are who hunted Roast and her mother, never saying anything or giving them a moment of peace. Joining the rest of the party, Roast brings up the topic of not trusting Morvek, attempting to force him to reveal if he is from the castle then shares that he and his people are hunters who chased after Roast and her mother in their home, and asking why they hunted them. 

Morvek realises what Roast is speaking of, vaguely alluding that his people hunt her kind out of fear. Roast is confused at this "her kind" statement, clarifying what Morvek means as she is clearly human. The others also realise in the moment that they do not know what Roast is and never did ask as it would not be polite to. The other party members help to mediate the situation, with Grimrid bringing Roast aside and recognising her fear, helping to alleviate it with reason, promising to hurt Morvek if he hunted her, and also reveals to her that humans do not have chickens for mothers before telling her to not view all Duergar as a monolith. 

Roast shares that she and her mother are humans, that mother's current state is simply a result of her growing older. Roast also begrudgingly agrees that Morvek does not behave as the other Duergar's do, then telling Grimrid that she would not see Morvek as a monolith but Morvek would have to do the same as well and telling Grimrid to tell Morvek that. Following Grimrid's lead, Roast walks behind, looking like a proud child who had gotten an adult to tell off another child. Grimrid puts on a very convincing voice, sounding real angry at Morvek for seeing people as monoliths before the act is quickly diminished by Roast whispering instructions and talking points to Grimrid who repeats them. 

Eventually, Roast does speak with Morvek, coming to a truce as she shares the party's intentions to return to the castle. She also asks for Morvek's help to stop his brethren from attacking them when they return to the castle, awkwardly offering her hand out for a handshake. The two shake on it as Roast declares that the party will have yet another guide in the castle town.
