When the party heads back to the Bonegrinder, their Orthon ally calls out to them, informing them of the man turned dire wolf, Wendel and of Lady Wachter's personal matters to the party before leaving. The party approaches the Bonegrinder and Morgantha answers the door, she hands Nyktos a scroll with the secrets of blood magic and to release her she hands over a glass ball with bone and blood in it. Upon receiving the glass ball, Elisa proceeds to shake it toying with the boon that would help them free the twins. Lastly before the party can leave, Morgantha muses that they shouldn't have sought a mother's love, that the party has done too much and thus shall die. They jump into battle and fight against the blood witch coven. Despite being worn out and drained from their battle with Houzuki, the party puts up a fight. The blood witches try their best, hiding in their bone grinder where the undead may not enter and those associated with the Lord of blood are disadvantaged. The party stands their ground , mostly staying outside of the bone grinder. They face wave after wave of blood magic, having almost every last drop of their blood pulled out from their bodies and left on the grounds around them. Ilenti rushes and without hesitation, attacking away at Morgantha whilst Elisa and Zipzap some and shadow spawns in the Bonegrinder, attacking the blood witches from a distance. As the battle continues, Morgantha curses out the party, she looks at Ilenti before her and calls out to her sisters, telling them to disintegrate Nyktos. Not taking kindly to the threat, the battle continues. With a well-placed hit, Zip Zap successfully petrifies Morgantha. One by one, the remaining sisters are forced to step out of the safety of their home. With the party focusing their attacks, one of the blood witches, Bella, falls. With one sister dead, the coven is no more. The remaining blood witch, Philomena, drops to her knees and starts begging for her life. Faced with her desperate please, the party relents, allowing her to live as long as she answers their questions. 

  

They learn about a secret temple in the mountains but not much more. It would appear that the blood witches know not the details of Bunny's immortality. Nyktos questions them about the Lord of blood and why or how they're considered traitors. She learns that the Lord of blood promised them immortality but didn't fulfil. She learns about his liking for the rejects and outcasts, much like herself. As they wrap up their interrogation and are about to let her free, Ilenti notices something weird, the blood witch's heart seems distended from her body and has a weird glow, it looks like she's about to blow. Being the only one to notice this, Ilenti uses her magic to pull the blood witch closer to her, saving everyone else from the explosion. As it all happens in a fraction of a second, a party watches, helpless as deep blood witch begins to blow up. Elisa reacts first and manages to teleport Ilenti closer to them, but not close enough. Seeing the explosion just mere centimetres away from her sister, Nyktos attempts to call out to her God for help, hoping they can somehow bring her sister to safety. A voice responds, and in desperation to save her sister, Nyktos agrees without thinking. As the explosion fades, the party doesn't see Ilenti. And Nyktos, knowing full well who she had sent her sister to, turns to the nearest tree and starts punching, cursing and swearing at her own mistake. Elisa is the first to voice out, questioning where Ilenti went. And as Nyktos responds grimly, remarking that there's only one person who keeps taking people away from her. In anger, Elisa heads over to the bone grinder, and blows it up. She turns back to see Nyktos, still punching and trying to release her anger. In an attempt to snap the Shadar Kai out of her rage, Elisa turns Nyktos around, shielding as a fist almost hits her. Nyktos vocalises what she had just done, confessing that she sent her sister, who she was supposed to protect, to a powerful and evil man. Elisa assures Nyktos, knowing full well that everything Nyktos does, she does to protect her sister. Rook chimes in as well, reaffirming that they would definitely bring Ilenti back. The party proceeds to make their plans, outlining what they would have to do in a very short amount of time. 

  

As they turn around, they come face to face with a blue lady. The lady in question immediately introduces herself as Telantes. Upon meeting her, Rook feels a weird sense of connection and familiarity to this stranger. Elisa and Nyktos however are very apprehensive of this random person who just showed up. But too tired, and still in pain from losing her sister, Nyktos simply turns around and heads to the direction of Vallaki. Telantes is quick to point this out and summons a water snake that's big enough for the entire party to ride to Vallaki. The party has their questions for this new person, however Rook feels extremely comfortable in their presence and begins to share about their entire life. Nyktos and Elisa find this rather odd seeing as the celestial had not been as forthcoming with them, they start speculating reasons for his sudden openness, chalking it up to an infatuation. 

  

The party returns to Lady Wachter's house, with Nyktos immediately requesting and downing their fine alcohol. The party suggests heisting the statues at night, though Lady Wachter says that it is to be done now. When the party states that they would need some rest, Lady Wachter pulls out a black disk with a deep purple border, one that the party immediately recognises. The disc heals the party, replenishing them of their rest. Nyktos remarks about the symbol but soon the party is told to leave. They stealthily makes their way into the baron's garden once more and as they use the item on the statues, instead of merely disabling the anti-magic field like Lady Wachter had said, the statues instead disappear. The party is both alarmed and confused, they make their way back to Lady Wachter's house. And after spending a bit of time making their way in they eventually manage to go to the second floor most of the party manages to make their way into Lady Wachter's bedroom before the staff can see them. However this is not the case for Rook and Crimson, Rook tries his best to light his way out of the situation but unfortunately the staff do not believe him. Before any of them can call for the guards, Telantes cast sleep, knocking the staff out for a minute. The party frantically drags the sleeping staff members into the room before having to knock them out. The curious sounds alert one of the other staff members nearby who calls out for her friends. At this point Elisa looks to ZipZap, asking him to mimic Lady Wachter's voice. He does so and manages to convince the staff member to leave the second floor alone. With the area secured the party looks around the room seeing the dead body of Lady Wachter's husband. His corpse preserved with a gentle repose spell as identified by Telantes. The party notices a hatch on the floor by the bed and attempts to pick up the lock, meanwhile Elisa has gone around the other rooms to find more things. The party takes what they want and make it a way down the hatch wherein they see Lady Wachter's sons. The sons are eager and willing to fight the party should they interfere with what they think is their only chance to regain nobility. Telantes speaks to the brothers, revealing their dad's corpse in their mom's room. Following the party back upstairs, the brothers face the truth and are stunned. Telantes turns the brothers around, allowing them to not face the father's corpse, whilst Nyktos tells them to rely on one another while they can. As they head back down once more, they walk through the illusory wall, to see Lady Wachter freeing the two petrified twins. Lady Wachter speaks of their maker, claiming her to be no one else but the lady of loss herself. The party attempts to regain Dahlia and Delilah on their side, with Nyktos desperately attempting to appeal with sisterhood. Despite their words, the twins do not care, they offer the party an ultimatum; serve as spawns or die. The sisters go on sharing their plans for the town and how they wish to turn everyone into vampire spawns. With no choice, the party proceeds to battle. Elisa pulls out a scroll and manages to cast sunbeam, allowing sunlight to fill the room and hurt the vampires. That, combined with the party's efforts allow them to bring down one of the sisters as Rook knocks her out. Seeing as she was going to die from the intense burns of the sunlight, Nyktos who had yet to hurt anyone in this battle, draws out her blade and attempts to stab the fallen sister. Telantes casts sanctuary on the fallen sister and Rook attempts to disarm Nyktos whilst talking her down. They succeed in stopping her first attempt but not her second. As she channels her blood magic, she looks to the surviving sister and apologises for her actions; she cruelly pulls out every last drop of blood from the unconscious sister. What had been intended to be a merciful kill, but instead in her anger became an act of cruelty. The remaining sister screams at the loss of the person she cares for the most. Seeing this all, Rook feels extremely conflicted, he asks for guidance and it is answered through the summoning of a celestial. Dame Aylin appears before them and upon seeing the vampires, she mercilessly hacks away at them, whilst monologuing at them. The remaining sister rushes up to Nyktos screaming at her once more as she pulls at her heart and says that the Ravenmarks damns the party. As for the second time in the day, another enemy before them proceeds to blow up. With her reaction, Dame Aylin rushes in front of the party, shielding them from the damage with nothing but her own body. The two twins are now dead. Telantes stares at the location of the twins, muttering under her breath that she just wished to help people. Nyktos approaches Telantes and tries to comfort her offering words and an awkward pat on the back that turns into a hug. To ease the situation, Telantes asks what Nyktos' favourite thing is. To which she responds is her sister's smile, one of the most beautiful things anyone will ever get to witness. Telantes remarks that she wishes to see it one day and in response Nyktos says that she will. Meanwhile  Rook speaks of Selune, and how he felt that she was by his side whilst Dame Aylin clarifies that the light emitting from Telantes was Selune’s light. Dame Aylin turns to Telantes, referring to her as Israphale. Elisa is quick to correct that she is Telantes. The lack of recognition from Telantes confuses Dame Aylin, as she attempts to heal her fellow celestial in hopes of removing any curses that might have been placed upon her. But to no avail as Telantes seems to be perfectly alright in spite of what Dame Aylin says. Regardless, Rook speaks with his sister who is eager to teach him more about their mother and her powers.

  

The party heads to the inn and secure drinks while they rest. As they all have their conversations, Rook learns a great deal about Selune and what she stands for. Meanwhile, Elisa and Nyktos are once again speculating about Rook's crush on Telantes. Telantes joins the two for drinks as they share and converse, the three soon come to realise their relations with one another. It seems that Telantes was friends with Lariet and Nyssa, friends of Nyktos and Elisa respectively. Their talks lead to Elisa sharing her backstory once more, informing Telantes of the great deeds and pain that Goetia, Sylvaerys, and Fool have caused as Telantes pretends to not know a single thing about these three people. Soon, the conversations come to a close and the party hears sounds from the outside. They rush out to see the dead body of Lady Wachter, hanging from her estate as her sons, who had clearly murdered their mother, step off the roof, killing themselves as well. The party rushes to the scene, unable to help the already dead family as the guards rush to clean up the scene. The Baron seems smug and happy, though there is an air of unease and an increase in the number of guards. The party retreats to their beds for the night. Telantes notices Nyktos making an effort to not stray far from another person and offers to share a room for the night, wherein they converse further and learn about the music she and her sister played.
