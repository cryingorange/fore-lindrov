The party are travelling through the Underdark. Reeling from the harrowing experiences they've had since their enslavement, a Drow guard approaches their wagon during transport, offering extra food for them beating a small weak man in the other transport. However Amunkhet notices that another guard tells the other transport the same thing. Eventually as they come to a halt, Friend-Friend is attacked by one of the other prisoners, who draws a dagger. The man is swiftly beaten down by Friend-Friend, who gets on top of him and wrenches the dagger away from him. Scaring him off, Friend-Friend telepathically tells the man that its smart of him to know when to back off. The party then discover that he Drow had made bets, to see who would win the fight.

In the night, the Drow allow the party to sleep outside in order to stave off their Exhaustion Levels. They are tied to the cart and go to sleep. The following day, the party is brought out to lunch. With the Drow jumping on the transports, and no feed given to the prisoners. They toss a crappy iron bar down to the players, and begin to wait for something. A Carrion Crawler attacks the prison transport and the Drow watch as the party fight for their lives, with Friend-Friend passing the bar to Brom who swiftly jumps onto the Carrion Crawler and climbs onto it, attacking and smashing into it while Elisa asks Gili to free her. During the fight she is freed by Gertrude, who displays an unusual amount of strength. 

Friend-Friend disallows the Crawler from moving into the backline, holding it back. Eventually the party manage to narrowly take it down before it kills anyone and the Drow gather everybody up and toss them into their Transports. But before that, Elisa notices Gili running over to Vanifer's transport and doing something.

Later in that night the party discover that Gili managed to get some spores on Vanifer. Communicating with her, she reveals that she was captured quite a long time ago and doesn't want to talk about her capture. Saying she lost a lot of friends that day, she also tells the party that Gili always never tells her that he's alright, asking that they take care of him. She also asks for their names, and tells them that she doesn't want to leave anybody behind. Saying that if they want to get out, they need to free her at the Prison so that everybody can be rescued. The party is brought to Stronghold Levesari, where they meet Iilvara. Accompanied by another slave transport, the party are gathered in front of a large pit with webbing around it with 3 other Duergar slaves captured by the Drow.

Iilvara demands for them to all kneel. But the 3 Duergar refuse to do so, looking at the body of the Duergar that had died during Transport. Iilvara brings forth her pet Ogre Spider, Arak'Thuu to consume the Dwarves. As Arak'Thuu drags the Duergar down into its lair, Shoor, Iilvara's new squeeze kicks the last one down into the pit to be consumed after using his Wand of Globs to cover him in adhesive.

The party are brought to the prison cells, where soon after they made a variety of characters. Prince Derendil, who insists he is a noble of Elvish Descent who was cursed by a wizard into turning into a Quaggoth. Buppido, a strange Derrow who the party can tell takes some kind of sadistic pleasure in "using his blade" in his own words. Ront, an Orc who the party swiftly become friends with, forming a tribe with him, one where he and all off them are considered "Warchief" after some talking to by Brom. Getrude, who suspiciously puts the party off for some reason despite her friendly demanour. Scag, a bully and overall dickhead, Sarith, a Drow that is here for murder, one that he says he was framed for and Topsy and Turvy and Preston, who they already met as the man who attacked them.

The party soon figure out the hierarchy of the Prison, with Scag being an overall bully for food, with Ront following closely behind to attempt to establish dominance. The party's initial meeting with Scag ended with a fight breaking out, with the Drow Guard finally stepping in and having them shut up. The party also meet Jimjar, a Deep Gnome with a penchant for betting. He bets with the party that Preston will have his food stolen by the next day. With Brom providing an alternative, instead of gold, Jimjar will owe them one favour. Something that he agrees to. The party then take their long rest and are taken off to work the next day. Amunkhet and Elisa are taken with Gertrude and Preston to clean Shoor's room. Where Elisa does quite a shoddy job, having her face dragged across the stone table by the Drow Guard escorting them and Amunkhet managing to step in to convince Elisa to concede to the Drow.

Saying that they will not be able to help anyone if theyr'e dead. Meanwhile, Friend-Friend and Brom are off mining, having collected a bunch of stone, Friend-Friend spots a small spider. He tries to subtley grab the spider as Brom provides a distraction by pretending that his pickaxe is stuck in one of the rocks. However the spider proves itself to be much more dextrous than Friend-Friend and it scurries off. The party then meet back up for Lunch in their cells where Scag once again tries to steal from Preston. The party intervene and drive Scag off from Preston. Only for Ront to follow up with another spout of bullying, and the party convince him that Preston is in fact, a tall Baby that requires protection.

After which the cleaning crew are told to clear up the Quaggoth Pen, with Elisa and Amunkhet made to clean their waste corner. Elisa sees a Rusty hammer left behind in the Quaggoth pen, to hide this fact, she gets on her knees with Amunkhet and scoops the waste into the bucket and stuffs the rusty hammer into the base of her living armour. Meanwhile, Scag and co. are left to work the mines again, with Scag walking up to Brom, Ront and Friend-Friend and taking their stones. A fight ensues and Scag jumps on Ront and begins to maul him while Friend-Friend steals stone from Scag's rocks. As Brom begins to use his pickaxe, the Drow guard fires a bolt into him and demands that they stop. Ront insists that he could've taken Scag but Brom convinces him to stand down. They head back to the cell and Brom finds himself having a crossbow bolt to use.

The party then also speak to Gertrude, and after some interrogation they manage to surmise and have her reveal that she is in fact, a Hag. They convince her to help them make a lockpick, passing the crossbow bolt over to her. The party also asks her for a few things, where to find clay and also a Twig. These questions make her realise that they are spellcasters and she also reveal that Topsy and Turvy are Wererats. In exchange for information about the Clay, they exchange information on a map that Gili had made during his time working as well. With Topsy & Turvy having become swift friends with the party. She asks them to offer her their names, which Friend-Friend immediately tells the rest of the party not to do so, as Fey can do horrid things with the True Names of other creatures.

The party then go rest for the night. With Amunkhet spotting Buppido creepily eyeing both Preston and Eldeth. The next day the entire party is reassigned to feed Arak'Thuu. With Ront immediately volunteering to go down into the pit to feed it as he is quote, "The Warchief". Elisa immediately switches places with him Knowing that he will get himself killed. She casts Speak With Animals on the way down out of the eye of the guards as the Drow hands her a Baby Umber Hulk. She speaks to Arak'Thuu, convincing it not to eat her while it kills and consumes the Baby Umber Hulk. They head back up and on the way back meet Shoor. Who they can all sense torments the party due to his own insecurity. He questions Ront with Friend-Friend feeding him certain answers, who stops doing so as he realises he's digging a bigger and bigger hole for Ront. Shoor then decides to cut off Ront's hands. With Brom managing to convince him to brand Ront, although in a twist, Shoor instead decides to brand Ront's chest, through his clothes and Ront passes out. The party then get brought back to their cells with an unconscious Ront. During this time they also notice that Gili is being let out by one of the guards thanks to some bribing done by Topsy & Turvy. However they realise that Gili has grown sick, with Elisa managing to convince it with Speak with Plants, casted prior to entering the prison as they were left unattended, the Mushroom that is attached to Gili's head to move somewhere else. But they soon realise this is for naught as the mushroom's base that has drilled into his skull would kill him anyway.

Topsy & Turvy also seem unusually clammy on that night, with Topsy saying that she cannot distract the guard for them. As the party wants to send Jimjar, who they have convinced to go in place of Gili and who has convinced the party to let him go alone, so that he can sneak past the guard and pretend to be Gili. Brom decides to do this, and Jimjar successfully sneaks out. The party are then met with a horrible realisation, Sarith, who is in the prison for murder, one that he says he was framed for as the victims were ripped apart is in fact a Werewolf. With Brom having managed to find a metal pipe prior to entering the Prison Cell, he manages to take down Sarith with multiple smites and is afflicted with Lycanthropy, a curse he quicklyh removes with Remove Curse after the escape. Iilvara storms back into the room, takes Sarith to be sacrificed the next day and also discovers Topsy & Turvy hiding under rags as they have also transformed. She gives Brom a choice, either she cut off his hands for him sneaking a weapon into his cell, or she ties him up outside. He chooses the latter and she instead inflicts this fate upon the unconscious Ront, who she realises is friends with Brom. The party then expedite their plans to escape, with Jorlan, the ex-squeeze of Iilvara and horribly disfigured asks that they go into his old room and steal poison to assassinate Shoor. The party agrees and he leaves the gates open. The party bring this plan to Jimjar, who returns having scouted out all the rooms and tells them where Vanifer is kept. The party tell Scag that the door is open and the prisoners spring an attack after Friend-Friend scouts out where the people set to be executed are and also he finds a key to Iilvara's room and a key to Vanifer's cell in the chest in Jorlan's Room. Friend-Friend and Elisa wild shape and head into Vanifer's cell, revealing themselves, they begin to unlock Vanifer's bindings and just as she is released, a band of guards arrives at the cell only for Vanifer to use Psychic Scream and kill them all. With Iilvara narrowly escaping she runs into Amunkhet and Brom who slowly unlocks her bindings, walking after an escaping Iilvara. Brom goes to meet up with the rest of the party, follow Friend-Friend to Iilvara while Vanifer and Elisa free the prisoners. 

As Elisa wants to kill Asha, the young priestess who begs for her life, Vanifer asks that she stop. Saying that there is no need to be so cruel to the Drow. They take Asha prisoner as well and go outside, with Elisa managing to exact vengeance on the Drow that humiliated her on their first work day, Eldritch Blasting her down into Arak'Thuu's pit while tormenting the Drow with ironic words. Meanwhile, Amunkhet counterspells, Friend-Friend, saying that Iilvara's life belongs to her. Friend-Friend sits down to watch Iilvara's death and Brom grabs all the party's items from the chest in her room. Amunkhet creates a plague, a cloud of poisonous gas released from her undead mouth and chokes Iilvara to death, she alos then revives her from the dead. With Friend-Friend now realising he does not need to breathe. The party then follow Vanifer off to her home. 

During their travel, a few off things are noticed about Vanifer, apparitions of a living flame seem to chase after her and the campfire she sets up seem to have a Tiefling dancing within it. They finally arrive at her home, a large spire with a Magma Elemental as the door. Entering it, they realise that Vanifer is in fact, the prophet of the Cult of Eternal Flame. They meet her right-hand man, Elizar, who arranges rooms for them and thanks them for saving Vanifer. They rest in their rooms, thinking about all that has transpired.
