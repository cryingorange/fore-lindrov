Following Kluki’s suggestion, the party tries their hand at ending the civil war amongst the Troglodytes. Elisa manages to convince S’slaat to be co-leaders with H’slaat and offers to bring them the drow’s heart for them to complete their leader ritual. After a lot of back and forth, mostly trying to prevent S’slaat from assassinating H’slaat, the party is ready to face the drow. Kluki enlists Elisa’s help with her summoning spell, and Elisa helps create a sentient giant mushroom. Kluki seems unnerved by the fungi but nonetheless, they continue. The party sends Milton in to scout and eventually come up with a plan of action. The execution however, was…less than ideal as Vesper is quickly knocked out and prepared to be spider food whilst Ankhta and Rook are having a hard time managing the front line alone. 

Still, the party perseveres and the opposing drow priestess offers the party a deal. Holding the very injured Rook hostage, she threatens to kill him should the rest fail to surrender as slaves. Ankhta and Elisa are uninterested in being slaves and Kluki, faced with the two helpless 16 year old looking members of the party, stands up against the drow. The red kobold threatens the drow, gathering nothing but amused laughter from them. But that all ends when the priestess is somehow killed and falls off her tower. The sudden death shocks all, the party included as they clarify with Kluki if she did that. The kobold seems beside herself as she mutters about needing to leave and someone being mad. Elisa and Ankhta follow Kluki’s orders and tie up the remaining drow whilst she mutters to herself before casting a healing spell on Vesper that grows back her missing arm.

The party regroups and starts harvesting the fallen drow priestess. As Elisa pulls out the essence of the drow, something reaches out to her, prompting a WIS save that she fails. Still, the harvest is successful and the party acquires both the essence and blood of the drow. Meanwhile, Rook is recovering from the incident, yet another brush with death just the next day after their encounter with the eldritch horror. As he is about to stand up, his weapon shatters, fragments of it lay by his feet. He falls to his knees in disbelief, staring at the broken weapon. As the party surrounds him, he shares his history. The Drakaar, he explains, are warriors that have sworn to never show their face, that their weapon is their religion, for his to have broken implies that he is no longer Drakaar. He vows to rebuild his weapon and now goes by the name of Rook. The Troglodytes show up, still looking like they want to kill one another but the party understands that it’s a moot point and allows them to take all the drow away. They rest up and proceed to look through the place, opening a trapped chest, and looking through their documents to learn of a Ziggurat nearby, they also acquire some glamerweaves and a symbol of House Baenre before heading down to find the worm. Elisa brings down the recently killed drow as offering for the worm and speaks with it. It inquires about the use of the milk and seems satisfied with Elisa’s answer, allowing the party to milk it. Done with their quest, the party returns up to rest for the night.

The next day, they head down once more to scout the Ziggurat and are met with the stench of blood. They take turns offering their blood and are eventually allowed in where they witness three skeletal remains. Vesper notices that the remains are old yet strangely fresh blood seeps out from within. They come across a carving of a bull head with a long blade and fiery whips. Still they press on and enter a room where an invisible eyeball stares at them, whilst a drow is tied up to a pillar, about to bleed dry. Vesper informs the party of the invisible eye and most are ready to head back up to rest, but Sahmu’el walks forward to help the drow when three others appear. They seem unwilling, like they are fighting against a force that is making them face the party. Battle ensues and during such, the eye darts from person to person, even taking control of Ankhta , forcing her to cast a spell to hurt her party. Ankhta does attempt to attack the eye but her spell doesn’t seem to work against it. They make quick work of the fight and are able to continue forth, down a flight of stairs lined with invisible eyeballs, staring at them as they walk past.

The party is then met with tubes of blood, figures inside as the blood in the main tube forms the shape of a woman. She speaks to them, introducing herself as Lady Malikah, a practitioner of the ancient art of blood magic. She explains that her form, or rather lack thereof, was a gift from the Lord of Blood himself, Lord Vroghorath (Secretly Tenebrous). Ankhta pulls from her vast knowledge and recognises Lady Malikah to be a cult leader from a time back. Lady Malikah shares that the party is offered a gift from Lord Vroghorath as reward for passing through his temple. Though initially suspicious, Elisa , Vesper , Rook , and Zipzap drink the blood, each gaining their own boon. With the rewards dealt, the party are then teleported back to the entrance of the Ziggurat.
