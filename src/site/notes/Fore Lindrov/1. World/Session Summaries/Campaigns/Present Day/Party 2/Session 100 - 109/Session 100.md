# Bremen
## The Owl
Entering the settlement, the party sees various townsfolk, all riddled with the same curse as Carbon had. Grimrid speaks to one of the townsfolk, an elderly grandmother as he attempts to ask her if it were possible to trade for some winter clothing. The grandmother somewhat agrees but is more preoccupied with the bad omen on her home than other matters. Grimrid offers to help, learning from the grandma that owls are seen as a sign of ill fortune, that they appear before death does. The others' spot this interaction between Grimrid and the villager, with Elisa immediately assuming that Grimrid has gotten himself a paramour. 

Grimrid informs the party of the owl, to which Roast is immediately cautious of, warning the party of the behaviour of the previous owl she encountered. Elisa, who did not have the nightmare the rest did, does not agree, instead insists on communicating with said owl. Elisa tries to bait the owl with food but the fish offered is ignored as it rolls down the roof, caught by Grimrid. 

The owl looks to Roast, who has casted speak with animals as it says "You are not welcome here" and flies off. At this point, the grandma could care less for the oddities that is the party and tells Grimrid that she will not be giving him her clothes before rushing back indoors. Grimrid clarifies that he never wanted them to begin with but the grandma is already gone before he can finish his words. Roast shares what she heard to the party, Elisa still not believing whilst Freck is skeptical, leaning more towards Elisa's point of view. Roast and Elisa agree that the owls might be more interested in rats than fish, noting that they might want to try capturing one for next time. 

## Left Offering
Grimrid does ponder on the clues they have amassed thus far, realising that this could be the work of a god of some sort, he suggests to the rest of the party to do some sort of prayer ritual, offering items that would have aided their journey across the snow would appease the god. Elisa doesn't buy into it, neither does Ivy who is used to being prayed to rather than be the prayer. Freck, Roast, and Grimrid move a small distance away from the town; Freck offering his jacket, Roast, 8 days worth of rations, and Grimrid, his blanket and a magical shortsword that he used to stab the blanket, which Freck follows as well with a borrowed dagger from Roast. Roast does ask Ivy for the word meaning "Frosted" in Primordial, referring to the entity they are praying to as the Frosted One. Grimrid gives a sincere prayer to the winter, leaving the items there as the villagers all stare, cautious but also displeased that the party's actions have led to more owls near their village.

## Elisa's Soup Kitchen
Meanwhile, Elisa has set up a small campfire, cooking fish for the villagers, drawing in a crowd who knows not of fishes. Elisa speaks to one of the villagers, Jory, who does share that water only returned recently and the winter has gotten worse in the past few months. Trying the fish, Jory does affirm that fish tastes better than Yeti. Seeing as it is a potential new source of food for the already starving villagers, Elisa devotes time to teach the villagers how to fish. She shows Freck the fishing rod she made, an exquisitely made rod (Nat 20) and helps him gather the materials for him to make another fishing rod. Freck understands the workings of the rod well, managing to make a very fine fishing rod (rolled Nat 20), as well. Freck then asks Elisa to present said rod to the villagers. Elisa rejects, wanting Freck to take ownership for his own craft. Freck does not want to, pushing back that it was Elisa's idea, insisting that she'd be the one to hand it to the villagers. A villager, overhearing this commotion, steps in and asks Freck if he had made the rod. Freck denies, giving credit to Elisa who immediately redirects credit to the rightful owner. The villager nods, telling Freck that he did good in a gruff manner before moving on.

## Grimrid's Hand of Friendship
Post prayer, Grimrid goes to speak with Lyanna, the leader of the villager and member of the Uthgardt, using ale as an excuse for a chat. He does ask Lyanna how they managed to capture the Yeti, complimenting their work as he learns from her that they managed to set a trap, bait it, and concentrate their efforts to stab it to death. Grimrid tells Lyanna that he thinks he likes her kind, shining bright with sunlight as he assumes his identity as Moradin's chosen, proclaiming that the Uthgardt to be friend of Dwarves, asking for them to be recognised and for the mountains to provide them with the protection and grace as they would a friend of the Dwarves [14 Persuasion]. With that, Grimrid then gifts the decanter of endless ale to the Uthgardt, knowing that they would have more use for it than he would.

## Party Fishing
Some of the party help with fishing. Roast gathers a net, wanting to try her hand at laying a net trap to get some fish, the efforts of which will only be seen the next day. [8 survival, no fish will be seen] Watching this, Ivy comments to Elisa that Roast is trying very hard to catch fish with a net and Elisa tries to convince Ivy to join in on the fishing, stating that a cold place might have different types of fish since the water is semi frozen it would be easier to swim in. Ivy disagrees with the logic. Freck and Elisa fish with rods, both reeling big catches that will provide the village food for weeks. Ivy turns into a bear, diving into the cold waters to fish. She heads down to the riverbed, finding a large Crab that she wrestles, overpowering it easily, bringing it out for the villagers to try yet another new meat. Roast follows suit, jumping into the water as the splash causes a bigger concern amongst the villagers as they assume that someone had fallen into the river. They drop what it is they were doing, gathering their men, ready to jump in to rescue when Roast surfaces with a small fish, proud of herself for catching one. The people rush her out, grabbing whatever they have and start patting her dry, all frantic like. Those who can set a fire, bringing Roast near it as they all worry for her, talking to her, hugging her, telling her not to sleep. They all behave as if her death is nigh. Roast tries to assure them that she is fine but the villagers do not listen, insisting that that is how hypothermia works, that one thinks themselves as well when in actuality they are far from it. Unable to do anything, Roast accepts the help of the villagers, enjoying the respite offered by these kindhearted folk. 

## The Uthgardt's Reasoning
Lyanna walks up to check on the commotion as well. After an hour, Roast is still the same, dryer than before but not fully as the villagers helping continue to fret. Understanding the importance of being dry, Roast uses her magic to extract the water from her clothes, surprising the villagers and Lyanna who asks if anyone could do that. Roast affirms that it is possible for anyone to acquire magic in a similar fashion as she has, beseeching the help of a being. Lyanna posits that it sounds akin to selling one's soul, which Roast denies but instead offers alternatives. Grimrid chimes in, suggesting magic akin to Ivy's; those who dwell within the forests of the world soon grow close to it, able to draw from their connection to nature. Lyanna is still hesitant, stating that her people have made it by through the sheer force of mankind, that she wishes not to change what the Uthgardt have always stood for. Roast suggests that Lyanna and her people continue as they have, that given time, a natural connection to nature would form, perhaps not immediately but something that the future generations would benefit from. Lyanna still dismisses the idea, wanting to instead focus on fishing. Freck steps in, telling Lyanna that she seems to do a lot of complaining and not a lot of doing, berating her for her words and lack of action. In return Lyanna refutes that Freck (and the party) who are all blessed with magic would not understand the pains of the village. 

Freck asks why the villagers don't simply leave, questioning their stubbornness to stay in a place that actively thins their numbers. Lyanna reiterates that this has been their home for centuries, that they will not allow anyone nor anything to force them out of their home before leaving. At the end of that conversation, Roast turns to Freck, lightheartedly asking him why he harbours such hate in his heart.

The party's position in the village is dubious with the three owls still there from before. The party speaks with Lyanna, gathering the information they were here for to begin with. They learn from Lyanna that there is a person who could help and the directions to this person. Roast brings up the Mammoths Lyanna had mentioned in her angry spiel, inquiring the nature of this creature. Lyanna teaches the party of the various monsters lurking about, warning them especially of the snow tigers who would sneak up and pounce on their prey. With that information the party is ready to head out. The mention of the owls is brought up once more, with Roast stating that she believes the owls would leave as soon as the party does. Elisa, however, still wants to try communicating with the owls, levitating up to them and speaking at them before they fly off once more. [5 pm party travels out of Bremen]

# To The Sea of Moving Ice
## Ragnavald
The party soon arrives at an alcove covered in snow. They mostly make their way down unharmed. The more perceptive of the party do realise a small mound of snow, as if someone was buried under it. The party soon comes face to face with a Winter Wolf, Roast casting speak with animals to speak with said wolf, trying to convince the wolf and the party to not harm one another before the entrance by which they came in is covered by a Frost Giant. The party's attention turns to the Giant, all ready to attack. Elisa and Grimrid recognise this individual but for different reasons. Grimrid sees an old friend, Ragnavald, exiled brother of the now Jarl Storvald who wanted nothing to do with his brother. In the past, Grimrid and his party had convinced Ragnavald to pursue a different path, that of an adventurer. 

Elisa sees Jarl Storvald, the one who stole her gold, tortured and killed her friends, amongst other things. Elisa unleashes her magic on the Frost Giant and his two pet winter wolves as Grimrid tries his best to calm everyone. Trusting Grimrid's words, Ivy jumps in front of the lighting unleashed by Elisa, taking some of the damage alongside Grimrid to stop Elisa from hurting the wrong people. Over all of the shoutings of the Frost Giant's identity as either Ragnavald or Storvald, Roast clarifies with the Frost Giant himself who clarifies that he is not Storvald, but instead Ragnavald, telling Elisa that he is nothing like his brother, for starters having one arm instead of two. The combat ceases, the misunderstanding dealt with as the party informs Ragnavald the purpose of their visit. 

Ragnavald refuses to help, and in an aggressive tone tells the party that they will fail, that it is dangerous out there. The party does not back down, insisting that Ragnavald need only point them in the direction. Which he does, vaguely pointing in a direction in a fit of angst. Roast immediately begins walking off in the direction, not caring for the dangers of the snow as Ragnavald has repeatedly mentioned, forcing Ragnavald's hand as he begrudgingly agrees, preparing his sled as he pulls the party to where they need to go. One of the Winter Wolves, Kald, walks up to the party, speaking to them in common. Kald asks the party if they are his pups, which Roast agrees to be. Kald asks Roast what is on her back, which Roast immediately reaches to grab, remembers that Mother cannot be pulled off since recent times and instead turns around, telling Kald that that is her Mother. Kald nods, saying that Mother is now his mate.

## The Sled Ride
During the sled ride, Grimrid talks to Ragnavald, with Ragnavald asking if they had met before and Grimrid affirms that they did, telling Ragnavald of their past experiences. Ragnavald tells Grimrid that when he woke up, all the trees were gone, something that Grimrid says is a result of the moon. Grimrid remarks that it is good to see a familiar face, Ragnavald says that there's nothing good about his face and Grimrid reminds Ragnavald that he is nothing like his brother. Ragnavald asks Ivy what she is, learning that she is a Primordial. And also asks Elisa the same, only to have ZipZap respond that Elisa is his best minion, whilst Elisa responds that she does not know what she is. Elisa tries to inquire about Ragnavald and his brother but Ragnavald does not responds, not wanting to talk about his brother. Then Ragnavald questions why Freck, a goblin, is travelling with the party. Freck says that the party might be assholes but they're his assholes and gives Ragnavald a hug. 

Freck does introduce the idea of creating an arm for Ragnavald but Ragnavald vehemently rejects, scoffing at the mere idea as he throws a blanket at him, commenting on Freck's lack of proper attire. As they travel, the party comes across a sky unlike anything they have ever seen, colours of green, blue, purple, and pink that paint the sky so wonderfully. Roast and Grimrid both wonder how the colours are formed, the former able to surmise that interactions required from the atmosphere to create such a phenomenon. Meanwhile Grimrid, does not know but begins to weave a tale of how the Dwarves were the ones to create the Aurora, with one such Dwarf who went up to the stars and coloured them in themselves. 

As chatter continues, Freck notes that it had been long since he had looked at the sky, and with prompting from the party shares how his people were enamoured by the moon and had lost their minds and proceeded to cast Freck out after the death of his mentor. So Freck left. When pressed on why he has chosen to follow the party's mission to save the world, Freck simply remarks that it is what is to be done, as if it was the next logical step forward but Roast points out that Freck could have simply found some place, rested and did nothing, just wait til someone else had solved the problem. Freck rejects the notion, feelings strongly against it and proving Roast's point right, that Freck hadn't simply stumbled upon the path but actively chose it.

## The Walrus
The party are led to an icy ocean of sorts and Ragnavald tells them to stay put whilst he retrieves his boat. Ise approaches the party, expressing her disapproval of them and her belief that they only bring trouble to Ragnavald. She does tell them to stop Ragnavald from helping people, that Ragnavald going out to sea is like a death sentence. The party does not necessarily confirm that they would stop him. But some of the party members are led away by Kald who says that he will teach his pups how to catch fish and sea serpents and how to dissect and remove their toxins safely. Roast and Freck are led to the shore where Kald uses his cold breath to freeze the surface, as Roast and Freck each use their own ways to do the same, learning how to be pups but to the dismay of some of the other party members.

After some time, Ragnavald has yet to return and the party hears a loud commotion. Rushing to the area, they find Ragnavald attempting to chase away a Walrus the same size as him who refuses to budge, standing between Ragnavald and his boat. Elisa taunts Ragnavald, teasing him for not being able to handle a mere Walrus. Ragnavald leaves to let the party handle it and Ivy casts speak with animals to learn of the Walrus' intentions. Unfortunately for Ivy, the Walrus has taken the boat as his mate and firmly believes that their calves will soon be born. Ivy begrudgingly shares this news to the party, bringing much entertainment to Elisa. Grimrid leaves to speak with Ragnavald, congratulating him on the new calves his boat would soon have, annoying the Frost Giant further.

Ivy also knows that a way to attain the boat would be to challenge the Walrus for it and much as she does not want to, she begrudgingly does with the support of her friends. The first two challenges Ivy wins with ease, as Elisa, Freck, and Roast use minor illusion to conjure the loudest cry and large tusks. The final challenge is a show of might, tusk to tusk. Ivy uses bones from one of her spells to craft makeshift tusks that she uses in this battle, losing one round to the Walrus before defeating him in two more as he swims off, ashamed. Ragnavald returns, cleaning his boat before they set out to sea. Before leaving, Ragnavald tells Ise and Kald to not wait for him, telling them to be safe, and jokingly says not to give his stuff to anybody. On the boat, Grimrid takes to the front of it, posted there with one leg up for the full three hours that the journey takes. 

Roast sits beside Ragnavald, helping him with rowing as Ragnavald has only one arm. Ragnavald comments that Roast doesn't eat much, telling her that she's too scrawny despite her protest as he tosses her some Walrus meat to eat. Ragnavald confronts Ivy on why she has brought the deer with her. When Ivy says that it is the deer's desire to follow, Ragnavald mutters for Ivy to keep the deer in the basket, not wanting to hear her whinge when it dies. Ragnavald then asks Elisa rhetorically if his brother had killed her friend, to which she affirms he did and they leave it at that. Ragnavald asks Freck what his instrument is, learning of the ratakastor as Freck begins to play a shanty which both Grimrid and Roast join in with. As sight of the faraway castle comes into view, the more observant ones point it out, directing Ragnavald to travel in that direction.

# Sea of Moving Ice
## Storvald's Courtesy
Soon, their peace is interrupted as cries sound out. The party remembers what Ise had instructed them. Some do comment, being honest with what Ise wanted but ultimately, the party prepares themselves for battle. Closing in, they see the victims of Frost Giants, crucified people with one survivor on the brink of death. The party handles themselves well, with Elisa's spells obstructing the enemies ability to fight, Grimrid successfully blinding and wounding the Giants heavily armed with the Bane of the First Men, Roast's wall of light that not only chased away the sea creatures, providing Ivy with safe waters but also leveraging Grimrid's attack to strike down one of the Giants, and Ivy, now fully embracing the terror open waters provide, grappling the enemies as she drags them down to their doom. Thanks to the villagers of Bremen, Roast is equipped with the spell to save the last survivor, drawing the water out of his clothes as Ragnavald helps the other corpses down. 

## Forni
The party speaks to Forni, learning that he does not know exactly where he is now, having been kidnapped from his village. The more observant party members notice a symbol of a snowflake on Forni's necklace and the lack of icy curse upon his person despite the obvious cold injuries from the Frost Giant's torture. They learn that Forni and his people pray to the snow, similar to how Grimrid had led earlier on, confirming Grimrid's suspicions of a winter entity that protects those that pray to them. With no way to return Forni to his home for the time being, the party will simply have to bring him along. Freck informs the party of the dragon scale sails used and the party harvests some of the material for future use. 

## The Funeral
They hold a small funeral for the other victims. Ragnavald attempts a speech, calling out to Thrym before bailing as he cannot remember the words, and simply apologises for them being dead. Implying that he hates Frost Giants and by extension himself, that if they'd never met "his kind", they'd still be alive. Grimrid takes over, more well versed in speeches than most. Ivy and Roast are able to tell that Ragnavald feels useless, unable to do anything of use in his own opinion. Roast approaches Ivy, seeking Ivy's advice on how to help the Frost Giant. The conversation shifts, with Ragnavald sharing how he came to lose his arm, and the worthlessness he had felt all his life. How he had tried to challenge his brother but only came out more broken each time, eventually being exiled. Silence follows, then some words of encouragement. Then Roast climbs up to Ragnavald's ear, whispering to him that the world seeks desperately to define who people are, and asks him to consider for a moment that these voices might speak untruths.

## The Greatship
Suddenly, Ragnavald calls for everyone to shut up, stopping the boat harshly as a larger ship appears, with sails made of white dragon scales with 20 Frost Giants on board. After they pass by, Ragnavald cusses loudly, annoyed to be put in this situation as he lets out his frustration on Forni who Roast protects, telling him that Ragnavald does not mean what he says. Meanwhile, Elisa, Ivy, and Freck notice a snow owl appear. It tells them to follow it. Calling to everyone's attention, Elisa suggests an alternate path that Ragnavald insists is a dead end. Elisa tells Roast that a friend has offered some help, which Roast does not question and immediately is ready to sail off but Ragnavald senses something off, holding onto the oar. Elisa tries to lie but Ragnavald sees through it, forcing Elisa to come clean. 

## Revel's End
When Ragnavald mentions the owl out loud, Roast is hesitant to agree but does so on the condition that if the owl turns out to be evil, Elisa would help Roast capture it. With no other choice, the party sets off to follow the owl. To appease Ragnavald, Freck hugs him once more, telling him that he will be his brother as Ragnavald rejects the kindness once more, commenting that Freck does not need him as a brother, that he would only kill him. Following the snow owl does lead them to a dead end but as the owl approaches the ice wall, the snowflake symbol shines, opening a path up, a tunnel through the ice that leads the party to their destination. Forni realises that the snow his people worship might be something greater than they thought and Elisa also realises exactly who this entity is. It is Auril, the goddess of ice and cold, who Elisa and her previous party had saved when Auril was caged by the Embertome. Grimrid sighs at the news of this, asking why Elisa had not shared this earlier when Grimrid brought up his theories only to be told that it had slipped her mind. Regardless, the party follows along the safe passage provided by the Winter Goddess, leading them straight to the castle.
